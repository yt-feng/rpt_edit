# Tough ad business outlook

Maintain Neutral; target price cut to USD8.10

# 1Q26 earnings beat; topline in line

Weibo's (WB) 1Q26 revenue increased $6\%$ y-y in USD terms to USD421mn, in line with both Bloomberg consensus and our estimates (Fig. 1). Its non-GAAP operating profit declined $7\%$ y-y to USD120mn, with margin (OPM) down 4.2pp y-y to $28.4\%$ . The OPM was 3.5pp above the Street consensus, due to a significant decline $(-75\%$ y-y) in general and administrative expenses on the back of the recovery of a bad debt.

# Outlook for ad business worsening

Management sounded positive about securing an ad budget from AI-related sectors. Also, ad spending from the auto sector is set to maintain solid growth in 2Q, as per management, as automakers continue to launch new models despite declining vehicle sales. However, WB has a cautious stance on ads budget from smartphone advertisers due to uncertainty around the rollout schedule for new models owing to pricing pressure. Additionally, ad spending from the online game sector remains weak due to fewer new titles being launched by big game operators. As such, we now forecast WB's revenue to grow $3\%$ y-y in USD terms in FY26F, revised down from $9\%$ y-y growth owing to a weaker retail environment.

# FY26E margin likely under pressure on weakening topline and increasing AI investment

WB pointed out that its AI initiative, including AI-powered content creation tools, AI-generated video features and AI-powered search function, are currently in rollout phase. While internal token consumption has seen a marked increase, management indicated that monetization of these AI initiatives remains in the early stage. The additional AI investment, coupled with growing headwinds for the top line, will likely lead to pressure on margins for WB. Thus, we forecast non-GAAP OP to decline 13% y-y in FY26F, alongside a 4.8pp contraction in OPM to 25%.

# Maintain Neutral rating, but lower TP to USD8.1

We trim FY26F/FY27F EPS by 16%/11% (Fig. 3), as: 1) we lower FY26F/FY27F revenue by 5% each on a weaker retail environment; and 2) higher operating expenses due to AI-related investments. We reaffirm our Neutral rating and lower TP to USD8.1 from USD9.7, based on 6x FY26F P/E (unchanged). WB currently trades at 5.8x FY26F P/E (non-GAAP EPS: USD1.36). We prefer Alibaba (BABA US, Buy) due to its more solid fundamentals and stronger standing in the China AI space versus peers.
