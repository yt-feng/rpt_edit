# Another big win for sac-TMT in upcoming ASCO

Encouraging PFS data and positive OS trend reported in PD-L1+ NSCLC setting; maintain Buy with a higher TP

# What is new? OptiTROP-Lung05 trial reported strong PFS and OS data

On 22 May before market open, Kelun Biotech announced that its sac-TMT, a TROP-2 ADC, would report OptiTROP-Lung05 outcome in the upcoming 2026 ASCO on 29 May (local time). The clinical trial enrolled 413 NSCLC (PD-L1+) patients and compares sac-TMT combo pembrolizumab versus pembrolizumab (1:1).

Analysis suggest: compared to pembrolizumab alone, sac-TMT combo pembrolizumab (1) improved progression-free survival (PFS) significantly (not reached vs 5.7m, HR=0.35, P<0.0001), (2) demonstrated a higher objective response rate (ORR) 70.2% vs 42% and (3) saw a positive overall survival (OS) trend (HR=0.55).

Furthermore, the PD-L1 TPS 1\~49% group showed a better PFS HR of 0.28 compared to the TPS>50% group of HR of 0.47, while the nsNSCLC cohort reported PFS HS of 0.28 versus sNSCLC's 0.44.

# Our take: a quite encouraging result; maintain Buy and lift TP to HKD544.42

We view this set of data as quite encouraging, as it is the first ADC reported PFS improvement in a phase III trial for PD-L1+ NSCLC patients. Compared to other regimens (ivonescimab, pembrolizumab combo chemo, for example) that reported an HR of around 0.50, this PFS HR set a quite high bar and may change the competitive landscape. Note: this drug is being priority-reviewed by the Chinese regulator for this indication based on this data.

Considering this positive updates, along with the earlier-announced Trofuse-05 outcome (see our report: Quick Note - Kelun Biotech (6990 HK) (Buy) The first global phase III clinical trial meet primary endpoints, ahead of schedule), we lift our FY35F revenue estimates for this drug in China / overseas market from CNY4.6bn/CNY6.5bn to CNY5.8bn/CNY10.5bn, respectively.

We thus lift our DCF-based TP (including an increase in terminal growth from 4% to 4.5%, on a higher sales outlook, and an unchanged WACC of 10.8%) from HKD438.30 to HKD544.42. The stock currently trades at 4.0x FY35F sales of CNY24bn.
