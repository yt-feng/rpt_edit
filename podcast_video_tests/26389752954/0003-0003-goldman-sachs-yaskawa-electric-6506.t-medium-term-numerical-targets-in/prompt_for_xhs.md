However, the FY2/30 OPM target of $15.5\%$ looks optimistic, considering that the business's historical peak OPMs at $13.7\%$ in 4Q2/23, and $11.7\%$ for a full year (FY2/23). The company cites increased added value from AI robotics, but the viability of the path to achieving this remains to be seen.

As in prior periods, we expect the MC business to outperform and the robot business to underperform targets, resulting in broader achievement of the medium-term plan target. We expect the operating profit target of ¥100 bn to be achieved ahead of schedule in FY2/29, driven by an acceleration in semiconductor- and AI-related demand from FY2/27, and the MC business to achieve an OPM of over 20%, while OPM in the robot business remains around 11%. We intend to re-assess the company's initiatives.

# FY2/30 capital efficiency/capital policy targets underwhelming, lower than previous long-term vision

The previous long-term vision, which ended in FY2/26, set return on capital targets including ROE of $15\%$ or higher and ROIC of $15\%$ or higher. New targets for FY2/30 ROE of $12\%$ or higher and ROIC of $11\%$ or higher, and $10\%$ and $9\%$ , respectively, for FY2/28, therefore appear lackluster. We expect earnings upside to become clear, particularly from FY2/27, and above targets to be achieved ahead of schedule. While we understand the company's growth-first stance, we would look for more extensive updates regarding its capital policy. We await further details from the management briefing on June 1.

# Our view: We remain bullish

While the medium-term earnings targets fall short of our own above-consensus estimates, they remain above consensus expectations, and the long-term OPM target is a positive surprise. We would look for more details on medium-term segment trends, particularly on how the company will control costs to increase margins, as well as comments on cash flow allocation and awareness of capital costs. We expect Yaskawa to benefit substantially from the tailwind of AI capex, driven primarily by ongoing buoyancy in semiconductors, and for the company to post new record-high profits. We believe it will generate operating profits of ¥70 bn in FY2/27, close to achieving the company's FY2/28 target ahead of schedule. We reiterate our Buy rating.
