# 1Q beat despite the high base Quick Note

# 1Q26: Solid beat driven by resilient PC titles and margin expansion

NetEase (NTES) delivered robust 1Q26 results, beating expectations across both the top and bottom lines. This outperformance was driven by the resilience of evergreen titles—led by the PC version of the Fantasy Westward Journey (FWJ) series and Where Winds Meet (WWM)—as well as significant gross margin (GM) expansion, which increased by 5.3pp y-y to 69.4%. We maintain our Buy rating for NTES, as the anticipated launch of the upcoming title Sea of Remnants could serve as a catalyst to re-accelerate gaming growth in the second half of the year.

# Revenue breakdown

Total net revenue rose 6% y-y to CNY 30.6bn, exceeding the Bloomberg consensus by 4%. The Online Game and Value-Added Services (VAS) segment was particularly strong, with revenue increasing by an impressive 7% y-y (3% above Nomura estimates). Game growth was primarily driven by legacy evergreen PC titles, specifically the PC version of the FWJ series, WWM (both domestic and overseas versions), and the PC version of Tianxia. Notably, management highlighted that peak concurrent users (PCU) for the PC version of the FWJ series reached a record high of 3.9mn in 1Q26.

Based on our calculations, cash revenue grew by an estimated 3.7% y-y, aligning with our forecasts. We view this growth as commendable given the high base of comparison from the prior year.

# Margin expansion and profitability

The blended GM reached 69.4%, marking a 5.3pp y-y increase and coming in 4.7pp above consensus. The gaming segment achieved a record-high GM in NTES's recent history of 74.8% (up 6.0pp y-y and significantly outperforming our 70% estimate). Management attributed this substantial margin improvement primarily to a favorable shift in the revenue mix, with higher-margin PC titles accounting for a larger share of revenue, along with lower revenue-sharing fees charged by app stores.

Non-GAAP operating profit increased 17% y-y to CNY13.3bn, surpassing our estimate by 12%. Consequently, the non-GAAP operating margin expanded by 3.9pp y-y to 43.4%, well ahead of our projected 39.8%, driven by the higher GM. Non-GAAP net profit remained flat y-y at CNY11.3bn, which nonetheless beat the consensus estimate by a sizable 13%.

# Pipeline and corporate updates

Regarding the pipeline, NetEase management indicated that its upcoming title, Sea of Remnants, is slated for a 3Q26 release. We anticipate an early 3Q26 launch (e.g., July) to maximize exposure during the peak summer gaming season.

Finally, the company did not provide an update on the timeline for converting its Hong Kong-listed shares from a secondary to a primary listing. Under prevailing HKEX rules, NetEase has a one-year window starting from February 2026 to complete this conversion.
