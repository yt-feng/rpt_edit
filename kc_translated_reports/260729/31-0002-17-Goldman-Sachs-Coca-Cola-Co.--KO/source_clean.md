# Coca-Cola Co. (KO)

Impressive volume-led Q2 beat & FY26 guidance raise, which could yet again prove conservative

KO

12m Price Target: \$86.00

Price: \$84.07

Upside: $2.3\%$

Our View - KO reported another impressive quarter - including organic revenue growth that outpaced our above-consensus estimates (+6%, vs our/cons ests of +5.0%/+3.5%). Importantly, it was a high-quality beat by KO, highlighted by strong concentrate sales/unit case volumes that were +4%/+5%, likely boosted from strong activations around the FIFA World Cup, which contributed to a portion of the company's volume growth in the quarter (+5%/+8% vol for Trademark Coca-Cola/Powerade) - and improved weather trends. Impressively, KO's 5% unit case volume growth in Q2 represented the strongest volume growth in 17 years (ex-Covid), per mgmt - as the company continued to gain value share. Also, we note concentrate sales were \~1pt behind unit case volumes (+4% for concentrate sales vs +5% for unit case vol) - which reflects some inventory unwind following six additional days in Q1, as broadly expected. Given topline leverage, gross margins were stronger than expected at 63.4% (\~+120bps y/y, vs our/cons ests of 62.5%/62.3%) - which ultimately translated to an EPS beat at \$0.97 (+11% y/y, vs our/cons ests of \$0.94/\$0.93). All in, while we think investors broadly expected strong Q2 results - KO's \~2.5pt/4pt top/bottom line beat (vs cons) likely caught many by surprise, and the stock is being rewarded today (+5%, vs +2% for the XLP).

While there's a lot to like about Q2, there are a few key considerations for 2H, including: (1) KO expects concentrate sales to lag unit case vols by \~1pt in Q3 (similar to Q2) - which reflects some inventory unwind following six additional days in Q1 - and the company expects FY26 concentrate sales to slightly lag unit cases following some inventory building late last year. Note, there are six fewer days in 4Q26 compared to 4Q25; (2) Comps become incrementally more difficult in 2H vs 1H - as KO is now lapping y/y concentrate sales growth of \~+2% (in 2H) vs \~flat in 1H - including challenging +4% concentrate growth in 4Q25 (coinciding with the aforementioned inventory build late last year); (3) Affordability initiatives in APAC, particularly in India & China, as KO remains focused on building a total beverage portfolio and driving incremental consumers into the KO ecosystem - which are likely to weigh on price/mix in the region near term. Further, there is also likely to be some negative geographic mix in APAC as India & China likely outgrow other developed markets - like Australia and Japan and Korea; (4) Slight underlying gross margin expansion going forward, albeit tempered in part by commodity pressures impacting tea and coffee (specifically in APAC); and (5) The pending sale of Coca-Cola Beverages Africa - which is expected to close towards the end of Q3 or in Q4 (per mgmt) - which should help drive some gross/op margin expansion. Ultimately, despite what remains a challenging/volatile operating environment - mgmt sounded confident in their ability to deliver on guidance this year - particularly given KO's strong share momentum (given broad-based share gains across KO's global beverage categories), additional pricing (albeit likely more moderate y/y and in line with inflation) and the many levers at the company's disposal (i.e., productivity) to offset other unforeseen headwinds. Bottom Line - We remain impressed by KO's top-line momentum and execution in a very challenging environment, as it further positions itself well for healthy, sustainable, l.t. growth. However, with the stock trading at a 31% premium to the market vs its 3-year avg historical premium of 9% and an \~8.5x premium relative to PEP on a forward P/E basis, above its l.t. historical average premium of \~1x, we continue to see a more favorable risk-reward elsewhere. Through this lens we maintain our Neutral rating.

Digging into the quarter, KO reported strong Q2 organic revenue growth of +6% y/y (above our/cons ests of +5.0%/+3.5%, and against a somewhat challenging +5.0% comp in 2Q25). The top-line beat was driven by concentrate sales/unit case volumes that were +4%/+5% (vs our +2.1%/+3.1% est) - while price/mix was up +2% y/y, below our/cons ests of +2.9%/+2.7%. By segment, North America organic revenue growth of +7% was a stand out (vs our/cons ests of +4.5%/+3.3%) - as KO benefited from easier comparisons and gained volume/value in the quarter - with concentrate sales of +3% and price/mix of +4%. Along these lines, we note that KO's duel approach with the FIFA World Cup and America's 250th anniversary gave the company a unique platform to leverage its growth flywheel in locally relevant ways across the country. With this, the company specifically called out strong volume growth for brands such as trademark Coca-Cola, fairlife, Powerade, Fresca, Gold Peak, Smartwater, and Simply. In EMEA, organic revenue growth was a bit softer than expected at +3% (vs our/cons ests of +5.0%/+3.9%) - dragged in part by geopolitical conflicts in the Middle East - while favorable weather supported healthy volume trends in many European markets. Latin America also performed well, with organic growth of +5% (vs our/cons ests of +7.0%/+5.0%) - driven by price/mix of +3% and concentrate sales +1% y/y - as improving trends in Brazil were partially offset by a difficult (albeit sequentially improved) environment in Mexico. Moving down the P&L, gross margins were stronger than expected at 63.4% (\~+120bps y/y, vs our/cons

ests of 62.5%/62.3%) - while SG&A as a % of sales was up \~+30bps y/y to 27.8% (vs our/cons ests of 27.0%/27.1%), resulting in adj operating margins that were up \~+85bps y/y to 35.6% (vs our/cons ests of 35.5%/35.1%). All in, this drove a strong Q2 EPS beat at \$0.97 (+11% y/y - vs our/cons ests of \$0.94/\$0.93).

Looking ahead, we expect KO's organic topline growth this year to be fairly balanced between price/mix & volumes (we model concentrate sales/unit case volume growth this year +2.9%/+3.7%) - which continues to compare favorably vs PEP (where we model FY26 vols of +0.9%). From a profitability standpoint, mgmt expects the overall cost impact of volatility in certain commodities and evolving global trade dynamics to be manageable this year. Furthermore, we note that KO will continue to invest in its brands moving forward, including on innovation that prioritizes bigger and bolder strategies (many of which were on display at NACS last year), in an effort to engage new customers and retain existing consumers, maintaining the company's bias to invest in growth. Ultimately, we think this decision to invest behind their brands is the right one to help drive healthy, sustainable, l.t. growth. That said, we expect mgmt will quickly pivot to balance investment spend that coincides with optimized profitability levels (i.e., delivering attractive EPS growth) - highlighting KO's flexible and nimble approach to its reinvestment philosophy. Given the Q2 beat, we raise our FY26/FY27 estimates by \$0.03/\$0.01 to \$3.30/\$3.51 and our 12-month price target by \$4, to \$86.

FY26 Guidance - KO raised its FY26 guidance - which we had expected. With this, the company now expects organic top line growth of \~+5% (vs prior of +4-5%, and vs our updated +5.3% est) - and KO now expects f/x neutral EPS growth of +7-8% (vs prior of +6-7%, and vs. our updated +8.5% est). With respect to f/x, mgmt's expectations are for a 1pt tailwind from FX on topline growth (vs prior of 1-2pt), and a 3pt tailwind to EPS growth (unchanged). KO also continues to expect a full-year tax rate of \~19.9% - which does not include the impact of the ongoing tax litigation with the US IRS. This implies FY26 EPS of \$3.27-\$3.30 (vs prior of \$3.24-\$3.27, and vs our updated \$3.30 est), and comparable EPS growth of +9-10% (vs prior of +8-9%, and vs our updated +9.8% est). We note that mgmt now expects a \~2-3pt topline (vs prior of a \~4pt) headwind from acquisitions & divestitures - though continues to expect a \~1pt bottom line headwind from acquisitions & divestitures. Elsewhere, KO's expects operating cash and free cash of \~\$14.6bn/\~\$12.4bn respectively (vs prior of \~\$14.4bn/\~\$12.2), vs our updated \$14.7bn/\$12.6bn est. KO issued f/x guidance for Q3 and expects a \~1pt f/x tailwind on revenues and a \~3pt f/x tailwind on comparable EPS - in addition to a \~1pt topline headwind from acquisitions & divestitures (minimal bottom line impact from acquisitions & divestitures). It's also important to note that 4Q26 has six fewer days compared to 4Q25.

By geography - Unit case vol growth was up $+5\%$ y/y in Q2, led by India, China, the US and Brazil. North America segment organic rev growth was up $+7\%$ , driven by concentrate sales which were $+3\%$ y/y, while price/mix was $+4\%$ . With respect to KO's category clusters: unit case vol growth in sparkling soft drinks were up $+4\%$ y/y in Q2 (trademark KO grew $+5\%$ ); sparkling flavors grew by $+4\%$ y/y in Q2, primarily driven by growth in Asia Pacific; juice, dairy and plant-based beverages grew by $+2\%$ in Q2, driven by growth in Asia Pacific and North America; and water, sports, coffee and tea was $+6\%$ y/y in Q2.

## Valuation

Our 12-month price target of \$86 (vs prior of \$82) is based on an equal-weighted P/E of 23.8x (vs prior of 23.0x) and EV/EBITDA of 19.0x (vs prior of 18.4x), both based on our updated Q5-Q8 estimates. We slightly raise our target multiples to reflect strong continued momentum, especially in the US. Upside risks to our estimates & price target include: an acceleration in U.S. CSD volumes, an abatement in FX headwinds & less pronounced competitive pressures. Downside risks to our estimates & price target include: headwinds from on-premise exposure, stepped up competitive pressures, a material slowdown in CSD volumes, stepped-up ESG pressures around packaging, sugar/soda taxes.

## Forward EV/EBITDA (absolute & relative)

KO is currently trading at an FY27E EV/EBITDA multiple of 21.3x, which is an $8\%$ premium to the company's 1-year historical average multiple of 19.8x and an $11\%$ premium to the company's 3-year historical average multiple of 19.2x

\- Our price target implies a Q5-Q8E EV/EBITDA multiple of 19.0x, which is an $11\%$ discount to the company's current FY27 EV/EBITDA multiple, a $4\%$ discount to its 1-year historical average multiple and a $1\%$ discount to its 3-year historical average multiple.

KO is currently trading at a $59\%$ premium relative to its Beverage peer group on a forward EV/EBITDA multiple basis. This compares to KO trading at a $52\%$ premium to the Beverage group on a 1-year historical average forward EV/EBITDA basis and a $44\%$ premium to the Beverage group on a 3-year historical average forward EV/EBITDA basis.

## Forward P/E (absolute & relative)

KO is currently trading at an FY27E P/E multiple of 24.1x, which is a $9\%$ premium to the company's 1-year historical average multiple of 22.2x and an $11\%$ premium to the company's 3-year historical average multiple of 21.7x.

Our price target implies a Q5-Q8E P/E multiple of 23.8x, which is a $1\%$ discount to the company's current FY27 P/E multiple, a $7\%$ premium to its 1-year historical average multiple and a $10\%$ premium to its 3-year historical average multiple.

KO is currently trading at a 35% premium relative to its Beverage peer group on a forward P/E multiple basis. This also compares to a 29% premium to the Beverage group on a 1-year historical average forward P/E basis and a 23% premium to the Beverage group on a 3-year historical average forward P/E basis.
