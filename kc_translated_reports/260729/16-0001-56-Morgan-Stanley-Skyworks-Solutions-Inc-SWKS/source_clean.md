Skyworks posted solid results, somewhat surprising in light of smartphone pressures, and sees potential for the Qorvo deal to close ahead of schedule. Suspension of dividend makes sense but will be disruptive.

## Key Takeaways

Smartphone business tracking despite memory headwinds, with somewhat lower Apple content headwinds vs. expectations & August pixel launch

\- Broad markets mixed, with strong auto & data center but weaker IoT

Qorvo deal could close this year; QRVO #s also solid pointing to significant accretion

\- Suspension of the dividend will disrupt the share price but provides a strong value opportunity

Headwinds keep us EW but we do see strong value opportunity for patient investors

Mobile demand remains stable, although it is still early to assess the ultimate impact of higher memory prices. Management continues to see lean channel inventories, a book-to-bill above one and no indications of an abnormal inventory build or material deterioration in end demand. September mobile revenue is expected to grow in the high teens sequentially, with Apple (covered by Erik Woodring) growing meaningfully faster than the overall mobile business during the seasonal launch ramp. Apple trends have improved versus the initial outlook, with the anticipated content related decline narrowing from 20-25% to the low teens, driven by better unit volumes and mix. Longer term, we continue to watch price inflation impacts. Apple and the premium tier may have greater price elasticity, but it will take time for a new cycle of price hikes to take effect. At this time Skyworks continues to broadly expect a seasonal 2H, with content flat on a blended basis consistent with prior guidance.

## Higher input costs remain the main near-term gross-margin headwind.

September gross margin is guided to 44%-45% versus approximately 45% in June, reflecting a higher mobile mix and cost inflation from gold, freight, expedite fees and tighter manufacturing capacity. The pressure is more pronounced in mobile, where pricing is fixed during the product cycle, limiting Skyworks' ability to offset rising costs and absorbing normal seasonal efficiency gains. Longer term, a richer mix of higher-margin broad-markets revenue, Qorvo's now above-50% gross margin and at least \$500 million of expected synergies support Skyworks' 50%-55% gross-margin target for the combined company.

Confidence in an earlier Qorvo close has increased, while Skyworks is eliminating its dividend and increasingly planning around the combined company in FY27. The Chinese regulatory review has advanced to Phase III with SAMR, which management described as the final stage of the process, and the company is working constructively with the two remaining jurisdictions. Skyworks is now optimistic that the transaction can close during calendar 2026 and is preparing for a potential close before the end of its fiscal year.

In preparation, Skyworks expects to raise approximately \$2 billion of debt in the near term and announced a significant change to its capital allocation framework.

## The decision to eliminate the dividend will cause some short term selling

pressure. The combined entity will have a significantly larger share count, which would raise the dividend price tag, in addition to the debt raise to fund the deal. It's doable if the company wants to orient the capital structure of the company to maintain it, but our sense is there is a clear preference to use the cash to buy back stock or for inorganic growth – with some frustration that the share price underappreciates the current deal and the high yield. That said, historical instances of dividend suspensions for stocks with material yields have seen material dislocation.

September quarter guide: Revenue was guided to \$1035mn (up 11% q/q and down 6% y/y), ahead of the Street at \$1026mn and our estimate of \$1013mn. Gross margin was 44.5%, in-line with the Street and our estimate. EPS guide of \$1.27, slightly below consensus and MSe of \$1.29 and \$1.30, respectively. Mobile was guided to grow high teens q/q, in-line with MSe at 16%. Broad Markets guided slightly up q/q, also in-line with MSe at 1%.

Changes to our estimates: For FY26 we are now modeling \$3.9bn/45.3%/\$5.04 vs. \$3.9bn/45.6%/\$5.04 prior. For FY27 we are now modeling \$4.0bn/45.7%/\$5.11 from \$4.1bn/46.4%/\$5.38.

Thoughts on the stock: Skyworks has executed well despite Apple related content headwinds, and the company's continued solid performance is encouraging. The smartphone environment appears to be holding up, but we are still somewhat cautious given the severity of the memory shortage - the smartphone market seems like one of the worst demand drivers in semis now.

That said, the stock seems too inexpensive to ignore, post accretion, especially if the stock remains weak on the dividend suspension. We can certainly see the view that there are better uses of cash than to support a dividend that only results in a high single digit multiple. We see value here for patient investors, but the business pressures keep us EW.

In terms of our PT, our CY27 earnings estimate declines modestly due to a more conservative gross-margin outlook. Applying the same 13.5x multiple to our revised EPS estimate of \$5.31 results in a new price target of \$72.
