# SkyNomad's Technology Launch on July 30 in Beijing
## Key Takeaways

Xiaomi announced today that its new EV brand ‘SkyNomad’ will host a technology launch event on July 30 in Beijing. The series is designed to enable more versatile in-car lifestyle scenarios and greater freedom of cabin space use.

Xiaomi aims to redefine space through intelligence and create a “living space” inside the vehicle. Xiaomi believes cabin space should not be static; it should adapt flexibly to users’ needs and preferences. Target users may spend two hours commuting each day, take short rests in the car, handle work on the go, travel with family on weekends, or use the vehicle as a second home while camping.

Under WLTC conditions, SkyNomad offers a maximum pure-electric range of up to 380 km and a minimum fuel consumption of 5.7 L/100 km in charge-sustaining mode.

By 1H26, SkyNomad had deployed 566 test vehicles, accumulated >4.28 million km of road testing, and covered >300 cities across China. Testing conditions ranged from -41°C to 53°C, with the highest altitude reaching 5,380 meters.
