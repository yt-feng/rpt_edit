## LVMH delivered 3% y-y organic revenue growth in 2Q26

LVMH Group (MC FP, Not rated) released its 1H26 results (on 27 July): total revenue declined $3\%$ y-y to EUR38.64bn, with organic growth of $2\%$ y-y. 1H26 operating profit declined by $4\%$ y-y to EUR8.69bn, with an operating margin of $22.5\%$ (down by 0.1ppt y-y), beating the Bloomberg consensus of $21.9\%$ . In 2Q26, total revenue was EUR19.52bn, representing organic growth of $3\%$ y-y, slightly beating the Bloomberg consensus of $2\%$ y-y growth. Excluding impacts from the Middle East conflict, 2Q26 revenue registered $4\%$ y-y organic growth. According to our June channel check (our report) with an expert that has deep knowledge of China's high-end luxury shopping malls, the key brands owned by LVMH witnessed stable sales momentum in 2Q26. This is consistent with LVMH's 2Q26 results. We believe there are significant structural growth room for China's luxury consumption; we like China Resources Land (1109 HK, Buy) and reiterate our preference for Laopu Gold (6181 HK, Buy). Key results summaries of LVMH are as below.

\- 2Q26 organic revenue growth by region: organic revenue growth in the US, Japan, Asia Pacific (ex-Japan), and Europe was $6\%$ , $14\%$ , $4\%$ , and flattish y-y in 2Q26, respectively, vs. $3\%$ , $-3\%$ , $7\%$ , and $-3\%$ in 1Q26.

\- 2Q26 revenue by segment: revenue for Wines & Spirits/ Fashion & Leather Goods/ Perfume & Cosmetics/ Watches & Jewelry/ Selective Retailing segments reached EUR1.32bn/ EUR8.90bn/ EUR1.88bn/ EUR2.78bn/ EUR4.36bn in 2Q26, with organic changes of +5%/ +1%/-1%/ +11%/ +6% y-y. The core Fashion & Leather Goods segment returned to positive growth, recovering from the downtrend in the past two years.

## Other results highlights in 1H26

\- According to LVMH management, Chinese customers' spending was flat y-y in 1H26, although their domestic spending willingness remained at historically high levels. At the same time, consumer demand was increasingly concentrated around specific shopping holidays/events.

\- LVMH noted that domestic demand in the US was strong in 2Q26, with accelerated spending from tourists, leading to a higher organic revenue growth in the US market compared to 1Q26's.

\- According to management, the drag from the Middle East conflict continued to weigh down the company's total organic revenue growth by c.1ppt in both 1Q26 and 2Q26.

\- Brands performance under the Fashion & Leather Goods segment: the segment recorded 1% y-y organic revenue growth in 2Q26 (excluding impacts from the Middle East market, organic growth was 2% y-y). By brand, Louis Vuitton's performance was in line with the segment's average performance. Dior was slightly above the segment's average performance (consistent with our channel check). Loro Piana and Rimowa continued to outperform, and Celine and Fendi showed sequential improvement q-q, according to the company. Spending on the Fashion & Leather Goods segment by European, Japanese, and Chinese customers remained roughly flat in 2Q26, while the spending of US customers achieved high-single-digit% y-y growth.
