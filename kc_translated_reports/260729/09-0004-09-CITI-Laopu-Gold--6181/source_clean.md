# Laopu Gold (6181.HK)
## CITI'S TAKE

Laopu's preliminary 1H26 results implied 2Q26 NP was about $30\%$ below Citi and market expectations. The severe earnings miss came from both topline and margin. Our 2026E-28E net profits, which are the seasonality-adjusted annualization of 2Q26, were lowered $17 - 22\%$ accordingly on weaker sales and margin assumptions. We also expect a significant cut in street numbers and hence negative share price reaction in the near term. After forecast reset, we believe a potentially sequential sales improvement in SKP Mall's Aug promotion to be a re-rating opportunity. Buy with lower TP of HK\$507.

Implied 2Q26 results — According to mgmt, 1Q26 revenue and NP came in at the low end of the guidance range at Rmb16.5bn and Rmb3.6bn, respectively, weighed down by weaker-than-expected sales in March due to a pullback in gold prices. This implies 2Q26 revenue of Rmb3.3bn-3.95bn. Deducting SBC expenses of >Rmb40m (all incurred in 1Q26 per mgmt), reported NP is projected to be Rmb4.26bn-4.31bn in 1H26. This implies 2Q26 reported NP of Rmb660m-710m, with NPM down qoq to 18%-20% from 1Q26's 21.8%. Mgmt said the sequential NPM contraction in 2Q26 was driven by a higher sales mix of discounted products, steeper discounting (including gold coin gifting promotions), a lower online sales mix, and operating deleverage. Inventory rose as of end-Jun 2026 compared to Dec 2025 due to increased procurement in 2Q26, while operating cash flow turned positive in 1H26.

2H26E outlook—Mgmt said 2Q and 3Q represent the low season in the gold jewelry industry, while 1Q and 4Q are peak seasons, typically generating sales \~2x that of the low season (vs. industry average of 1.5x). Such seasonality implies 2H26E sales could reach 3x of 2Q26 level. On top of that, mgmt expect more attractive product pricing via incremental discount and new products carrying lower price per gram to stimulate sales. 2Q26 new products were limited to "test the waters" and, given positive customer feedback, major product rollouts are planned for 2H26E. We expect GPM in 2H26E to be lower than 2Q26's >45% due to these new launches, which anchor 43% GPM and additional 5% discount from top-tier membership, which can now be stacked with store promotions. That said, we expect potentially better operating leverage to offset lower GPM in 2H26E vs. 2Q26.

Earnings and valuation — We cut 2026E-28E NP 17-22% on lower revenue and margin, expecting Rmb6.4bn/5.1bn/5.9bn in 26E/27E (normalized earnings)/28E. Our TP is lowered to HK\$507.0 from HK\$659.0, based on unchanged 15x 27E P/E. Trading at 11.7x P/E and 6.4% yield in 27E, valuation remains undemanding to us.

## Laopu Gold

## Company description

China jewelry retailer Laopu Gold has carved out a unique high-end product niche in “heritage gold,” combining modern designs and classic Chinese motifs, featuring at least two Chinese traditional handmade gold crafting techniques, often embedding diamonds and/or gemstones, with products sold in elegant boutiques styled like a traditional Chinese scholar’s study. Laopu’s key strengths include high ticket size (>Rmb50k on average), leading per-store sales (>Rmb500m), unique fixed-price model, superior profitability, and exquisite craftsmanship and product innovation in its “heritage gold” niche.

## Investment strategy

We rate Laopu Gold shares as Buy. As price-sensitive customers are leaving, we believe Laopu now has a higher proportion of loyal customers who care less about the price premium. Thus, we expect future sales to have lower correlation to gold prices and further downside risk from gold price decline to be limited, which should improve earnings predictability. Although the customer mix shift led to earnings hiccups in the short term, this is likely already priced in, and we believe it warrants distinct brand positioning with matching audience, providing a more robust, sustainable business longer term, in our view.

## Valuation

Our target price of HK\$507 is based on 15x 2027E P/E (normalized earnings) vs. global luxury peers' 22x 2026E P/E. We apply \~30% valuation discount for Laopu's to reflect its shorter brand history and track record period in the luxury industry. The implied 12x 2026E P/E is at the low end of global luxury peers.

## Risks

Key downside risks that could impede the shares from reaching our target price include: 1) gold price volatility; 2) intense competition; 3) evolving consumer tastes and preferences; 4) consumption trade-down amid China's soft economy; and 5) negative free cash flow amid expansion.
