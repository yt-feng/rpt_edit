# June 2026: Power Demand Growth Softened
## Key Takeaways

China power demand growth was $5.3\%$ YoY in 1H26, vs $5.7\%$ in 5M26 and $5.4\%$ in 4M26. Monthly demand growth slowed to $3.7\%$ YoY, vs $6.9\%$ in May.

The softer June data reflected $4.7\% / 5.6\% / -3.1\%$ growth in the secondary/tertiary/residential industries due to cool temperature, vs $6.0\% / 9.7\% / 7.5\%$ in May.

Renewable installations were 12.5GW solar and 13.6GW wind in June 2026, vs 8.7GW and 3.8GW in May 2026 and 14.4GW and 5.1GW in June 2025, respectively.

Thermal added 38.4GW in 1H26, vs 25.8GW in 1H25.

By industry, we highlight 1H26 China power demand:

• Primary industry increased 4.9% YoY, vs 5.6% in 5M26.

\- Secondary industry rose 5.1% YoY, stable vs 5.1% in 5M26. In particular, there was a 9.8% YoY increase in the high-tech and equipment manufacturing segments.

\- Tertiary industry maintained strong momentum of 8.0% growth YoY, vs 8.6% in 5M26, of which charging services and IT services (incl. AIDC) grew 56.9% and 44.0% YoY, respectively.

• Residential demand rose 3.1% YoY, down from 4.5% in 5M26.

In 1H26, total power generation rose $3.5\%$ YoY to 4,750bn kWh, with solar $+12.3\%$ YoY and wind $-1.9\%$ YoY. Wind and solar power generation accounted for $18.9\%$ of the mix (vs $16.7\%$ in 2025). Overall plant utilization (thermal, wind, solar, hydro, nuclear) was 1,392 hours, -113 hours YoY.
