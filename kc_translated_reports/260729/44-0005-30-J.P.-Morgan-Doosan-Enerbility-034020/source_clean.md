## Doosan Enerbility
## 2Q beat; Orders on track with strong GT orders; Doosan our top pick

Doosan Enerbility's (Doosan) 2Q OP beat expectations at W314bn vs. JPMe/BBGe (W283bn/W279bn), driven by a stronger-than-expected Bobcat contribution on resilient US construction equipment demand; parent OP was broadly in line at W97bn (slight miss vs. JPMe W105bn). Parent 2Q order intake was W4.3tn, keeping FY orders on track vs. W13.3tn guidance (53% run-rate), led by gas turbine-related wins (W5.0tn progress vs. W6.2tn FY guidance incl. combined-cycle gas EPC). 1H gas turbine orders totaled 12 gas/6 steam turbines and 5 LTSAs (long-term service agreements), with management citing tight supply and high-visibility negotiations for 2030-31 slots. Nuclear commentary was constructive (Korea 12th Basic Plan by year-end; US long-lead orders visibility), while SMR remains the key watch as the \~W1tn annual target needs to be secured in 2H. Maintain Overweight; top pick among nuclear EPC companies.

\- 2Q OP beat, driven by Bobcat; parent broadly in line, with gas turbines leading orders. Doosan reported 2Q OP of W314bn, ahead of JPMe/BBGe (W283bn/W279bn), mainly on a stronger-than-expected contribution from Doosan Bobcat amid resilient US construction equipment demand. Parent-level OP of W97bn was a slight miss vs. JPMe (W105bn). Parent 2Q order intake was W4.3tn, keeping the company on track vs. its W13.3tn full-year guidance (53% run-rate), driven by strong gas turbine-related wins (W5.0tn progress vs. W6.2tn annual guidance, including combined-cycle gas plant EPC). We see potential upside to full-year orders from incremental gas turbine wins in 2H, while we believe the key downside risk centers on SMR, where management's \~W1tn annual order target needs to be secured in 2H.

\- Gas turbine updates. Doosan secured orders for 12 gas turbines, 6 steam turbines, and 5 LTSAs (long-term service agreements) in 1H, while maintaining its cumulative targets of 71 units by 2030 and 110 units by 2034. Management noted high-visibility negotiations for 2030-31 delivery slots and expects supply tightness to persist for the next five years, supporting additional order momentum despite a tight production schedule.

\- Nuclear updates. In Korea, management expects the 12th Basic Plan to be announced by year-end, with potential upside tied to the Southwest mega project supporting semiconductor fab and datacenter buildouts. In the US, management sees visibility to receive long-lead material orders from Westinghouse projects this year. On SMRs, the company is targeting NuScale orders in 2H, while discussions with other SMR design companies continue around long-lead material supply and major sub-component orders.

\- Maintain Overweight and Dec-27 PT at W130,000. We revise our 2026-28 OP estimates up by $1 - 7\%$ , mainly reflecting an upward revision to Bobcat following the 2Q results. Doosan remains our top pick among nuclear EPC coverage, supported by its expanding orderbook and balanced portfolio across large-scale nuclear, SMRs, and gas turbines. We view Korea's US investment announcement as a key sector catalyst, and believe Doosan is positioned to benefit across scenarios (link to our sector update note).
