# Hong Kong Property | Asia Pacific
# JLL 2Q26 Rental Update: Recovery Continues
## Key Takeaways

(+) Office rental rose 1.7% QoQ in 2Q26 (YTD: +3.2%) while Central rental grew 3.3% QoQ (YTD: +7.3%).

(-) Office rental at HK East and Kowloon East rental still declined 0.8%/1.1% QoQ, respectively.

(+) Office vacancy improved 0.4ppt to 13.1% as of June, with net absorption of 492k sqf. Central vacancy fell 0.8ppt QoQ to 8.8%.

(=) Grade A Office capital value rebounded 0.8% QoQ in 2Q26 on rental gains, but still 50% off the peak in 3Q18. We expect positive trend from here.

(=) Prime retail rents -1.4% QoQ / -8.4% YoY in 2Q26, but better than -10% YoY in 1Q26. We expect decline to narrow.

Stock ideas - Among landlords, we prefer Swire Properties (OW; 0.5x P/B, 5.7% forward yield) for its office exposure, attractive valuation, and positive shareholder returns (dividend growth, and buyback). We also prefer Link REIT (OW; 0.7x P/B, 6.8% forward yield) as retail recovery is visible, new CEO is announced, non-core disposals are ongoing and buybacks were initiated.

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Link REIT (0823.HK)

Base case scenario value, derived from a target 12-month forward dividend yield of 5.65%.

The target forward dividend yield is composed of a 4.05% 10-year US Treasury yield (MSe by end-2026) and a spread of 1.6% (1SD above long-term average since 2011).

## Risks to Upside

■ Non-core asset disposals with unit buyback from the proceeds

■ Upcoming rates cut for further yield compression

■ Potential REIT inclusions in Stock Connect to boost fund flows

■ Possible value-accretive acquisitions

## Risks to Downside

■ Slower-than-expected Fed rate cut

■ Further deterioration in Hong Kong retail and office

■ Longer-than-expected transition in key management

## Swire Properties (1972.HK)

Base case scenario value, from sum-of-the-parts analysis.

HK - Office and retail: Gross cap rates of 4.75% and 5.75%, respectively.

China - Office and retail: Gross cap rates of 7.0% and 8.0%, respectively. Rents remain stable.

HK development properties: DCF, WACC of 8%.

30% NAV discount: 1SD above average since 2011.

## Risks to Upside

■ Faster-than-expected ramp up of new investment properties to grow recurring income base

■ More capital recycling to unlock asset value

■ Strong recovery in office and retail segments in Hong Kong

## Risks to Downside

■ Slow recovery in HK office and retail

■ Slowdown in China retail recovery

■ Difficulty in off-loading non-core assets
