# JPM
## Huaqin Co Ltd - A
## NP beat on solid execution and investment gains; datacenter acceleration to drive re-rating in 2H26

Huaqin pre-announced stronger-than-expected earnings in 2Q26, supported by continued share gains across segments and investment gains. While investors remain concerned about weakening demand in smartphones and PCs following price hikes, we believe these concerns are overstated, as Huaqin's share gains and improving mix should offset soft end-demand and drive earnings growth in 2026. More importantly, we expect the datacenter business to become the key earnings catalyst from 2H26, as rack-level AI programs move into volume production. Amid the ongoing transition toward rack-scale AI infrastructure in China, we believe Huaqin's full-stack capabilities across AI/general server and high-speed switches should position the company as a key beneficiary and support valuation re-rate. Reiterate OW with extended Jun-27 PT of Rmb112.

\- Solid core operations and investment gains drove the beat in 2Q26. Huaqin booked a net profit of Rmb1.9bn (mid-point) with 83%/81% yoy/qoq growth in 2Q26, beating JPMe by 65% on investment gains. Its recurring net profit came in at Rmb860mn (mid-point) with 15%/5% yoy/qoq growth. We attribute resilient recurring earnings to continued share gains across PCs, wearables and AIOT products, more than offsetting smartphone demand softness and supporting 9%/31% yoy/qoq revenue growth (mid-point) with stable GPM in 2Q26.

\- Accelerating datacenter growth and a richer product mix drive margin expansion. As rack-level AI programs transition into volume shipments, we expect Huaqin's datacenter revenue to accelerate meaningfully from 2H26, with revenue expected to grow by $>50\%$ yoy in 2026. Management remains constructive on AI infrastructure buildout in China and believes Huaqin is well positioned to secure a leading role in the supply chain, supported by its full-stack capabilities spanning AI/general servers and networking. More importantly, increasing shipments of rack-scale programs and high-speed networking should lift ASPs and margins, making datacenter an important earnings driver with $33\% / 38\%$ contribution to total GP in 2027/28.

\- Market concerns on consumer demand remain overdone; reiterate OW. Huqin's share price has underperformed the SW electronics index by 31ppt YTD, amid concerns over weaker smartphone and PC demand. We think these concerns are more than priced in, and that Huaqin, as the key beneficiary with full-stack capability in rack-level AI programs, deserves a re-rate from the current valuation of 16.6x 2027E PE. We raise our 2026-28 earnings forecasts to incorporate non-recurring items and a stronger outlook for datacenter growth, and extend our PT to Jun-27 at Rmb112, based on an unchanged 21x 12-month rolling forward PE. Reiterate OW.

# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

Huaqin Co Ltd - A (Overweight; Price Target: Rmb112.00)

## Investment Thesis

Huaqin is an original design manufacturer (ODM) with diversified exposure to datacenter, smartphones, NBs, pads, wearables, AIOT and auto products. We estimate potential shrinking volumes in smartphones given downstream demand weakness, but we believe earnings will be well supported by strong momentum in the datacenter, wearables and PC segments this year. Meanwhile, we expect auto electronics and robots to bring extra revenue and support long-term growth. We favour Huaqin for its sustainable growth outlook from a diversified business platform, and project a 32% earnings CAGR from 2025-27E. Maintain OW.

## Valuation

Our Jun-27 PT of Rmb112 is based on a 21x 12-month rolling forward P/E, on par with peers' average, based on JPM and Bloomberg Finance L.P. estimates.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Downside risks to our rating and price target include: (1) competition among ODMs that hurts growth/margins; (2) unexpected suspension or constraints on GPU supply; and (3) a share price correction after the lock-up period expires.
