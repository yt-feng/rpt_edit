# Mona L03 to drive volume growth acceleration with strong orders post launch; Buy

XPeng officially launched its new SUV model Mona L03 in Munich, Germany on Jul 16, with official entry price of Rmb123.8k (compared to its pre-sale entry price of Rmb143.8k and Mona M03 official entry price at Rmb119.8k). Mona L03 received 20k / 47k non-refundable orders in 7 mins / 1 hour post global launch (vs. 10k / 30k non-refundable orders for Mona M03 in first 52 mins / 48 hours in 2024). This is the second model under the Mona brand for XPeng, targeting to a broader market (i.e. SUV vs. sedan) in China, per the company. We note its first model Mona M03 launch was a major success for XPeng in Aug 2024, with 1) non-refundable orders of 10k/30k in first 52 minutes/48 hours, 2) average monthly delivery of 13k in first six months, stabilizing at 12k average monthly sales LTM.

The company highlighted its globalization strategy, including 1) all future models will be also launched overseas; for example, the company targets to enter 65 global markets in 2026, 2) VLA2.0 model targets for roll-out in Europe in 2027, 3) the company already started to organize robotaxi fleet overseas, 4) it aims to deliver its humanoid robotics overseas in 2027, and 5) the company plans to launch its flying cars in Europe in the future. The company indicates it plans to adopt the strategy of “In Europe For Europe” supported by Munich R&D center and Magna factory in Austria.
