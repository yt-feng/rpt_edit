# Luk Fook Holdings (0590.HK)

# 1QFY27 strong SSSG as expected, yet store closure ahead of GSe; Neutral


LF announced its 1QFY27 (Jun-quarter) operational update on Jul 16.

SSSG came largely in line with that during Apr-Jun 21 reported upon FY26 results with solid performance. HK&Macau outperformed with SSSG at $41\%$ (vs. Apr-Jun 21 SSSG at $>40\%$ ); ML China also saw fast SSSG of $>20\%$ in line with Apr-Jun 21 performance, with self-operated store SSSG at $16\%$ . Jun SSSG in ML China saw deceleration from a strong May according to management, which is presumably due to less discount offered/more disciplined trade-in, and demand shift to HK&Macau markets. By category, weight-based product sales outperformed, while fixed priced products also recorded DD% SSSG in the quarter at group level.

\- Store closures were more than expected - Net store closure in ML China totaled 150, higher than 71 net closures in the Mar quarter, compared to GSe of 150 net closures in 1HFY27, while there were 2 net openings in overseas markets.

Read across to Chow Tai Fook (CTF): CTF will report 1QFY27 operating results on Jul 23. Similar to Luk Fook, we expect to see solid SSSG in both ML and HK&Macau with the latter outperforming (Apr-May SSSG was at +19.7%/+40.6% in ML China/HK&Macau). By category, we expect to see outperformance from weight-based products. We believe the growth sustainability into 2QFY27 and company's strategy to drive fixed priced mix increase would be key to watch during the results, with company's guidance implying HSD% SSSG in ML China and slight mix increase in fixed based products.
