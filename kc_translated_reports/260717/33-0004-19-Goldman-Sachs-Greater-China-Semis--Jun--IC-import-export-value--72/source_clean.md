## Strong YoY growth of IC production/ import value in May-Jun

IC production volume: +22.9% YoY in May 2026 to 50bn units (vs. +22.1% YoY in Apr 2026, +11.5 YoY in May 2025). We remain positive on the China Semis trend, which is driven by generative AI, and ADAS / AD trend in China, and the ongoing local suppliers' market share expansion in the China market with increasing capex plans.

Semiconductor total revenues: The May 2026 data was up 87.2% YoY, or up 10.7% MoM to US\$32.0bn (vs. +78.3% YoY in Apr 2026, +14.1% YoY in May 2025), representing continued YoY growth in 2026.

## Taiwan semis revenue growth was up 49.2% YoY / 7.0% MoM in Jun 2026:
