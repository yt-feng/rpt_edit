# OptiTROP-Lung06 Met Primary Endpoint - A Major Global Relevant Clinical Validation for sac-TMT

The event: Subsidiary Kelun-Biotech announced the China Phase 3 study (OptiTROP-Lung06) of sac-TMT+Keytruda (vs. chemo+Keytruda) as first-line treatment in PD-L1 TPS<1% nsqNSCLC has met its primary endpoint of PFS as assessed by BICR at interim analysis, and a positive trend in OS was also observed. Safety profile is consistent with previous studies with no new safety signals. We also await full clinical data to be presented at ESMO 2026.

Our view: Recall that the China Phase 3 study (OptiTROP-Lung05) of sac-TMT + Keytruda in 1L PD-L1 TPS ≥1% pts had met PFS endpoint earlier, with data presented at ASCO 2026 (see our take here and here). We have previously highlighted outcomes of the OptiTROP-lung06 trial as one of the most relevant clinical milestones of sac-TMT in the global landscape, where partner Merck (covered by Terence Flynn) is running 17 global Ph3 trials. Our global team sees upside to its current 2035 risk-adjusted sac-TMT sales estimate of US\$4.1bn (55% PoS), vs. Visible Alpha consensus of US\$5.9bn (note).

Regulatory path in China and US: In China, company indicated that it plans to discuss with the NMPA for BLA filing soon. In the US, Merck may pursue a BLA filing in the first indication of 2L+ EC using the National Priority Voucher, which would unlock registration milestones and sales royalties to Kelun.
