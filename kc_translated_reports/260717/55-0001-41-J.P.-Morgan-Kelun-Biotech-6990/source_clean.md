## Kelun Biotech
## The first PD-1+sac-TMT Ph3 win over PD-1+chemo; raising PT on higher PoS

Amid the recent sharp re-rating of the China healthcare sector, Kelun Biotech has been one of the strongest performers and has rebounded 38% (vs. Hang Seng Innovative Drug Index +24%) from its early June low, reflecting improved investor sentiment toward China biotech/pharma with clear commercialization potential and increased confidence in China's globally competitive ADC pipeline. The stock gained another 8% yesterday (July 15, vs. HSIDI +4.6%) after the company announced the China Ph3 trial of OptiTROP-Lung06 met the primary endpoint of PFS (progression-free survival) with OS (overall survival) benefit trend. Recall OptiTROP-Lung06 compares Keytruda+sac-TMT against Keytruda +chemo in 1L PD-L1 negative non-sq NSCLC (non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer) patients. We believe this trial success carries more significance than the success of OptiTROP-Lung05 where Keytruda+sac-TMT beat Keytruda monotherapy in 1L NSCLC with high PD-L1 expression. This is not only because OptiTROP-Lung06 covers a much larger patient population than OptiTROP-Lung05, but also because the trial success is the first time an ADC+PD-1 combo demonstrates statistically significantly better PFS than Keytruda+chemo in a head-to-head Ph3 trial, which gives us more confidence that Keytruda+sac-TMT could be successful in many other indications as Keytruda+chemo is a standard of care (SoC) in those indications. In addition, the China NMPA (National Medical Products Administration) has granted Priority Review to the sNDA (supplemental new drug application) for sac-TMT in 1L triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). Recall the company announced Ph3 win of OptiTROP-Breast03 in 1L TNBC in May 2026 and then submitted this sNDA. We expect OptiTROP-Lung06 data could be presented at ESMO'26 in October and OptiTROP-Breast03 data could be presented at SABCS'26 in December. We raise our PT for Kelun Biotech to HK \$648 (from HK\$535) as we increase the probability of success (PoS) for several domestic and global Ph3 programs in our model, including sac-TMT in 1L TNBC and 1L NSCLC.

\- The adjustment reflects the substantially reduced clinical risk following multiple positive registrational studies in China and the increasing validation of the asset across tumor types. Importantly, sac-TMT has now generated a number of positive registrational Ph3 readouts, including in 1L TNBC, two 1L NSCLC studies and a global Ph3 trial in endometrial cancer (see our note here and here), strengthening our confidence that the asset is becoming increasingly de-risked. In addition, yesterday's news that Keytruda+sac-TMT beat Keytruda+chemo in 1L PD-L1-NSCLC in a China Ph3 study suggests the potential that Keytruda+sac-TMT could become a new SoC not just in 1L NSCLC but also many other key indications where Keytruda+chemo is SoC now. Moreover, China NMPA has granted Priority Review to the sNDA for sac-TMT in 1L TNBC, providing another important regulatory de-risking event. The Priority Review designation signals regulatory recognition of the therapy's potential clinical value and suggests an accelerated review timeline, further improving visibility on commercialization and reducing execution risk for one of Kelun's most valuable near-term assets. ## Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation

## Investment Thesis

Founded in 2016, Kelun Biotech has been a domestic leader in antibody drug conjugate (ADC) development in China. Currently, it has one ADC SKB264 (sacituzumab tirumotecan [sac-TMT]) in the China market, alongside eight ADCs in clinic and three non-ADC cancer drugs under new drug application (NDA) review in China. Leveraging its advanced OptiDC platform, which enhances the design and development of next-generation ADC, Kelun Biotech is able to drive efficient drug R&D. Its strong ADC capabilities have been recognized by Merck. Merck in-licensed ex-China rights for SKB264 and has launched 14 global Ph3 trials for SKB264, covering broader indications than competitors Dato-DXd and Trodelvy. Based on our analysis, we think SKB264 could potentially be the best-in-class TROP2 ADC globally. We estimate SKB264 China peak sales could reach >Rmb9bn and its ex-China peak sales could reach US\$6bn. We have an OW rating.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Key downside risks to our rating and price target include:

\- Regulatory Risks: Changes in regulatory policies or standards could introduce new compliance challenges, potentially increasing costs and affecting the company's ability to operate efficiently across different markets.

\- Geopolitical Risks: Geopolitical risks can arise from tensions, such as trade disputes, tariffs, or sanctions. These tensions could lead to increased regulatory scrutiny, restrictions on technology transfer, and challenges in cross-border collaboration. In addition, geopolitical risks can affect supply chains, as companies may face disruptions in the availability of raw materials or components sourced from regions experiencing conflict or political instability. Changes in government policies, such as shifts in foreign investment regulations or healthcare policies, could also impact business operations and profitability.

\- R&D Risks: Clinical trials can fail at any stage due to safety concerns, lack of efficacy, or unforeseen side effects. Such failures not only could result in sunk costs but could also damage the company's reputation and investor confidence. Moreover, the rapid pace of scientific advancement means that Kelun Biotech must continuously innovate to stay ahead, which could strain resources and require ongoing investment.

\- Competitive Landscape: The entry of new competitors or the development of superior treatments by rivals could erode Kelun Biotech's competitive position and impact its long-term growth prospects.

\- Financial Risks: Any unexpected financial setbacks, such as cost overruns or revenue shortfalls, could strain the company's financial health and limit its operational flexibility.

\- Intellectual Property Risks: Kelun Biotech may face legal disputes over patent infringements or challenges to the validity of its patents, which could result in costly litigation and potential loss of exclusivity. Furthermore, the expiration of key patents could open the door to generic competition, eroding market share and profitability. The company must also navigate varying IP laws across different jurisdictions, adding complexity to its global operations.
