# JPM
## US Monthly (June)
## Despite concerns about high gasoline prices, demand remains solid

Autodata reported a June US new vehicle sales SAAR of 16.67 million, higher than our forecast of 16.2 million. SAAR has remained above 16.0 million since March, which suggests that demand is solid despite concerns about the impact of high gasoline prices. Among Japanese automakers, Honda's sales remained strong and Toyota's market share (single-month basis) fell amid a cautious switchover to the new RAV4, while at mid-tier maker Subaru there were signs of a recovery in Outback sales. HEV penetration was $16.1\%$ in June, down slightly compared with May, although the strong trend was unchanged. Amid ongoing concerns about how Middle East risk will affect demand, we think confirmation of solid demand in the US will foster a certain level of reassurance in the market.

\- US SAAR continues to exceed 16.0 million amid lingering Middle East concerns: Based on Autodata's announcement, June new vehicle sales SAAR in the US was 16.67 million, up from 16.21 million in May. SAAR has remained above 16.0 million since March 2026, which suggests solid demand. On a single-month basis, June sales were 1.38 million units (+3.4% YoY on a daily selling rate (DSR) basis), comprising 0.24 million sedans (+1.2%) and 1.14 million light trucks (+3.9%). While there are concerns about how the rise in gasoline prices caused by tensions in the Middle East will affect demand in the US, we think the apparent absence of a meaningful decline in demand will be viewed as reassuring.

\- Sales performance of Japanese automakers: Figures 1-3 show the sales performance of Japanese automakers. Among the three majors, Honda's sales remained brisk (134k units, +12.2% YoY) and its market share (single-month basis) remained high at 9.7%. At Toyota (213k units, +5.7% YoY), market share declined slightly in June to 15.4% and sales continued to decline MoM amid the switchover to the new RAV4, indicating tepid momentum. Among mid-tier companies, Mazda's CX-5 sales continued to decline YoY, so we may need to keep an eye on the pace at which the new model ramps up. Please refer to Figures 4 and 5 for inventory and incentive details.

\- HEV growth continues: Total EV sales in June came to 313k units (+8.3% YoY). BEV sales fell sharply, to 73k units (-30.5%), but HEV sales continued to grow strongly, reaching 222k units (+37.0%). HEV sales by major automakers were as follows: Toyota (110k units, +23.6%), Honda (35k units, +15.9%), Hyundai (21k units, +67.0%), Kia (22k units, +175.2%). Toyota is waiting for the new RAV4 to make a full contribution, and we do not expect its sales momentum, including HEVs, to accelerate in earnest until October.
