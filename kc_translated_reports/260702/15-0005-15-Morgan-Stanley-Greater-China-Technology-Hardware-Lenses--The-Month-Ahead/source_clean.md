# Lenses: The Month Ahead – Catalyst Previews

We preview catalysts for Taiwanese optical companies Largan and Genius in early July.

## Key Takeaways

We expect Largan and Genius to report weak sales in June.

As a key client's new product launching is in 3Q26, demand in June is likely to see seasonal weakness while sequential recovery will kick off from July.

GlassBridge is an emerging technology approach with potential disruption risk to FAU and related components.

This adds volatility for companies with high option value from FAU components.

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Largan Precision (3008.TW)


## Risks to Upside

■ Better-than-expected high-end smartphone demand

■ Faster-than-expected penetration of 1G6P

■ Faster-than-expected penetration of periscopes

## Risks to Downside

■ Greater-than-expected competition, which would erode Largan's ASP and gross margin

■ Weaker-than-expected smartphone demand, especially for high-end models

■ Market share gains and more pricing pressure from Sunny

## Genius Electronic Optical Co. Ltd. (3406.TW)

Base case, residual income model, which is the same method we use for our Greater China Tech coverage. We apply a CoE of 8.5% (3% risk free, 1.0 beta and 5.5% ERP), a medium-term net profit CAGR of 4% and a terminal growth rate of 3%.

## Risks to Upside

■ More share gains in iPhones

■ Better-than-expected mixed reality demand from Apple

■ Better-than-expected VR/MR shipments

## Risks to Downside

■ Delays in Apple's mixed reality shipments

■ Share loss in iPhones

■ Execution/yield

■ Slower VR/MR shipments
