# June 2026: Adoption Rises to $20.6\%$

■ AI-related investment growth remains strong, particularly for semiconductors, where equity analysts expect global revenue to reach \$826bn (ann.) by the end of 2026. Consensus revenue forecasts for memory producers has been revised up by \$173bn (ann.) since the public release of ChatGPT in November 2022. AI-related investment in the US national accounts also continues to rise and now stands at \$425bn (1.3% of GDP) above its 2022 level. AI-related hardware shipments from South Korea rose to an all-time high of \$36bn/month USD in May and US AI hardware net imports remained elevated at \$52bn in April.

AI adoption by firms rose by 1.1pp to $20.6\%$ among US establishments (with adoption expected to rise to $23.9\%$ in the next 6 months) according to the Census Bureau's Business Trends and Outlook Survey (BTOS). Information, professional services, finance, and education firms continue to lead adoption. At a detailed subsector level, publishing and some finance firms report adoption levels at or exceeding $50\%$ , and beverage and tobacco manufacturing and transportation firms report the largest expected increase in adoption over the next six months. Establishments with over 250 employees continue to lead in adoption $(40.7\%)$ , but medium-sized firms with 100-250 employees have accelerated their pace of adoption in recent months. Other business surveys note that many firms are beginning to experiment with agentic AI systems.

AI's labor market impact remains visible but narrow. We continue to observe employment drags in specific industries where AI-use cases have been established such as marketing, graphic design, customer service, and some tech occupations. However, this is offset by construction job growth in data center-exposed categories, which is up 251k since 2022 (relative to broader construction employment trends) and around 9k per month (latest 3m trend). The tech share of total employment continues to decline relative to the pre-2022 trend. The correlation between AI adoption and broad labor market slack measures remains limited, but there are early signs of a negative relationship with job growth and hours growth. Corporate layoffs attributed to AI accelerated to 38.6k employees in May, continuing a notable acceleration since the start of the year. $24\%$ of Russell 3000 companies mentioned AI and labor-related keywords in 2026Q1 earnings calls.

We continue to observe large impacts on labor productivity in the limited areas where generative AI has been deployed. Academic studies imply a 23% average uplift to productivity, while company anecdotes imply slightly larger efficiency gains of around 34%. Industries with higher AI adoption rates are now showing a slight acceleration in productivity growth over the past year in official US data.
