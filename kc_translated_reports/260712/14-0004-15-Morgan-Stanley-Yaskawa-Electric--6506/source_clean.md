# F2/27 1Q Results: Brisk Orders Positive, but Margin Issues Remain Particularly in Robotics
## Reaction to earnings
## Key Takeaways

1Q results significantly missed prior expectations due mainly to production disruption and costs increases associated with the ERP system migration.

■ Motion Control: Orders increased steadily, but sales failed to grow YoY due to production disruption issues.

Robotics: Sharp growth in orders was positive, but the margin fell more than expected to $1.6\%$ due to the impact of ERP system migration/restructuring costs.

We expect steady recovery from 2Q as production normalizes, and view guidance as achievable, but results likely fell well short of elevated market expectations.

1Q results: 1Q OP was -19% YoY to ¥8.5bn, falling far short of our ¥15bn forecast and the ¥14.4bn consensus due mainly to the impact of production disruption and cost increases associated with the ERP system migration.

1Q orders: Consolidated orders were +28% YoY and +7% QoQ to ¥163.8bn. 1) AC servo: Orders continued to grow, at +65% YoY and +10% QoQ, driven mainly by semiconductor and data center-related applications, but this was slightly short of our assumption. 2) Inverters: Orders were +31% YoY, -3% QoQ, broadly in line with our assumptions. 3) Robotics: Orders recovered to +14% YoY and +37% QoQ, centering on general industrial and semiconductor-related applications, and beat our assumptions.

Full-year guidance remains unchanged.

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Yaskawa Electric (6506.T)

P/B of 3.5x on F2/28e BPS of ¥2,134.

We forecast F2/28 OP of ¥77bn. Referencing past valuations, P/B was 2.2x when ROE was 10.7%. We apply 60% premium to this theoretical multiple considering: 1) we see huge potential for robotics demand to grow amid automation demand and physical AI; 2) Servo orders are recovering well for semi-related applications, 3) and OP is expected to hit past peak thanks to volume increase.

## Risks to Upside

■ Stronger-than-expected recovery in private-sector capex

■ Faster-than-expected penetration of FA and robotics in EMs

## Risks to Downside

■ Capex trends in electronics- and autos-related fields; the speed of FA penetration in EMs

■ Forex (OP forex sensitivity is about ¥240mn for a ¥1/US\$ change, ¥220mn for a ¥1/€ change, ¥330mn for a ¥1/CNY change, and ¥250mn for a ¥1/KRW change)
