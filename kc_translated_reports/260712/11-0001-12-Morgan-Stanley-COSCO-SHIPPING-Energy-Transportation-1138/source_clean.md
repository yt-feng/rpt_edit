## Key Takeaways

\- Implied 2Q26 net profit was Rmb2.3bn, doubling YoY and +7% QoQ, vs. MSe Rmb2.2bn.

With CSE's affected vessels having safely exited SoH by late June, 3Q26 could see improved effective utilization if traffic normalizes.

FY26 earnings may see upside risk if tanker demand proves stronger or lasts longer than expected.

CSE announced 1H26 preliminary results on July 10, 2026. It expects 1H26 net profit of Rmb4.5bn, up 141% YoY on a restated basis. Recurring net profit is expected at Rmb4.4bn, up 148% YoY. Implied 2Q26 net profit of Rmb2,327mn (doubled YoY, +7% QoQ) is 6% above the MSe of Rmb2.2bn.

The minor QoQ improvement is due to effective-rate dilution from vessels trapped in the Gulf, and soft rates in int'l product oil and domestic oil shipments amid the geopolitical tension, we think.

Management cited robust tanker rates as the key earnings driver, with average TCEs on TD15 (West Africa–China) and TD22 (US Gulf–China) up 180%/170% YoY in 1H26.
