## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Haidilao International Holding Ltd (6862.HK)

Base case, target P/E of 20x 2026e earnings. Our target P/E factors in gradual macro recovery and lowflation, with the company to slowly expand its store network. Strong operating leverage and disciplined cost control drives a 2025-27e EPS CAGR of 27%. Our target P/E is slightly higher than 1SD below the average valuation since 2018. We see upside potential should macro recovery be faster and the store/brand expansion turn more proactive..

## Risks to Upside

■ Faster recovery in the macro economy

■ Better-than-expected demand recovery

■ Faster pace of new store openings

## Risks to Downside

■ Slower ramp-up in table turn recovery

■ Raw material cost inflation

■ Delays in new store openings

■ Greater-than-expected staff cost increase or overly aggressive cost controls that dampen service quality and, hence, medium-term growth outlook
