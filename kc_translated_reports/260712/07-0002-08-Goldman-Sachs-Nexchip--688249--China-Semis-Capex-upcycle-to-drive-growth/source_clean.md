# Nexchip (688249.SS)
# China Semis Capex upcycle to drive growth ahead; TP up to Rmb70, maintain Neutral on fair valuation

We remain positive on China Semi supply chain riding on rising advanced node capacity expansion and the generative AI trend to support China semis ecosystem development. We expect strong earnings growth ahead for Nexchip, supported by:

\- Capacity expansion: The company announced its capacity expansion plan on 40nm and 28nm chipset manufacturing (link), and the trial production is expected to begin in 4Q26, with a total capex budget of Rmb35.5bn. The company expects the new plant to reach full capacity in 2Q28, with a monthly capacity of 55k 12" wafers, covering diversified end products, such as AI smartphone, AI PC, smart automotive, etc. We raise Nexchip's capex and capacity in coming years, reflecting the contribution of the new plant, while the rising depreciation would weigh on the company's margin,

\- Price increase: The company announced that a $10\%$ price increase will be implemented starting Jun 2026 (link), offsetting the rising cost of raw materials, which is positive to the company's profitability.

We change the valuation methodology to 2030E discounted P/E (from near-term P/E previously) on better long-term visibility given customers' rising capex and increasing localization, which is same as our China Semi coverage. Maintain Neutral on fair valuation with TP up to Rm70.

Earnings revision: We raise our 2026-27E net incomes by $1\%$ / $5\%$ , mainly on higher revenues. Our 2026-27E revenues are revised up mainly on higher contribution from 40nm and 28nm business, supported by Nexchip's capacity expansion in coming years. Our 2026E GM is essentially unchanged, while 2027E GM is revised down, considering the rising depreciation cost from the new capacity weighing on margin. We introduce our 2028E estimates.
