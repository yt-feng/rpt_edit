# InnoScience (2577.HK)
# 800 VDC to drive GaN in AI data centers; initiate at Buy with TP at HK\$114

InnoScience is a leading GaN (Gallium Nitride) supplier for consumer electronics, automotive, data centers, industrials, and humanoid robotics. We expect the company's GaN revenues to achieve a 73% CAGR in 2025-28E, driven by: (1) rising GaN penetration in data center power supplies to fulfill the requirements of ultra-high power density and ultra-high efficiency; (2) expanding applications from fast chargers into automotive (on-board chargers, LiDAR), industrials, data centers, and humanoid robots, with mix upgrades driving higher dollar content; and (3) capacity expansion of GaN wafers, with total capacity expected to reach over 68k wpm (wafer per month) by end-2029E. Accordingly, we initiate InnoScience at Buy, with a 12m TP of HK\$114 (implying 22x 2027E P/S vs. 68% avg. revenue growth YoY in 2027E-28E).

Key debates center on GaN penetration rates and competition. Featuring high frequency, reduced power loss, and high cost-effectiveness, GaN devices are gradually expanding from fast chargers into automotive, industrial, and data center applications. With rising agentic workflows and accelerated computing, GaN devices can enhance system performance, efficiency, and density. InnoScience offers a comprehensive portfolio of high- and low-voltage products, spanning wafers, discrete devices, and modules to better meet customer needs. It also serves global leading customers across various segments (e.g., power semis, batteries, and smartphones), supporting its leading market position.

Catalysts: (1) 800 VDC ramp up with rising GaN adoption; (2) new capacity ramp up at the Suzhou sites; (3) margin expansion with larger scale; and (4) emerging new applications, such as humanoid robots.

## GaN to ride on growing AI data centers

Given its high-frequency and high-efficiency advantages, GaN is increasingly utilized in AI data center server power supplies, or chip-level power delivery, which is one of the key challenges in AI infrastructure. As the industry transitions toward 800 VDC rack-level power architectures, the demand for higher power density and efficiency has intensified, and we believe InnoScience's GaN devices are well positioned to enable higher switching frequencies to meet the 800V HVDC power density requirements.

InnoScience's comprehensive offerings to support data center clients: The company adopts the IDM model and has started mass production of GaN solutions from 15V to 1,200V, which enable full conversion from 800V to 100V and cover all conversion stages. InnoScience's products can be used in power conversion between 800V to 54V / 54V to 12V / 12V to 0.8V. Management highlighted that the company's solutions can reduce driver losses by $80\%$ and reduce switching losses by $50\%$ , supporting clients' overall decrease in power losses.

## Valuation

We derive our 12m TP of HK\$114 based on the 2030E discounted EV/EBITDA methodology to capture the company's long-term growth, in line with our GC Tech coverage. We apply a target EV/EBITDA multiple of 29.5x on 2030E EBITDA, and discount it back to 2027E with a COE of 9.5% (beta at 1.0, risk-free rate at 3.0%, and market risk premium at 6.5%). Our target EV/EBITDA multiple is derived from: (1) peers' forward-year EV/EBITDA vs. the sum of EBITDA YoY and EBITDA margin; (2) InnoScience's forward-year avg. EBITDA YoY (26% in 2030E-31E) and avg. EBITDA margin of 35% in 2030E-31E. Initiate at Buy.

Cross check: Our TP implies 2027E P/S of 22x, in line with the company's avg. P/S of 22x since Apr 2025 (listed in Jan 2025).

## Key risks

(1) Slower-than-expected GaN adoption: We expect to see rising penetration of GaN devices across multiple applications to bring better efficiency. If the adoption rate is slower than our expectations, there could be downside risks to our earnings estimates.

(2) Fiercer-than-expected pricing competition of GaN products: With mix upgrades toward high-end applications, we expect to see growing blended ASPs. If there are more entrants, leading to fiercer pricing competition, there could be downside risks to our earnings estimates.

(3) Longer-than-expected time to ramp up new GaN capacity: The company continues to expand new capacity in its GaN factories. If the new capacity ramp up is slower than expected or is being delayed, there could be downside risks to our earnings estimates.

## Investment Thesis - InnoScience

InnoScience is the leading GaN (Gallium Nitride) supplier and adopts the IDM model, targeting consumer electronics, automotive, data centers, industrials and humanoid robotics. We expect the company's GaN business to be driven by: (1) the continuous capacity expansion of GaN wafers; (2) expanding applications from fast chargers into automotive (on-board chargers, LiDAR), industrials, data centers, humanoid robots, with mix upgrades driving higher dollar content; and (3) incremental revenues from GaN for VDC rack power architectures to fulfill the requirements of ultra-high power density and ultra-high efficiency. We are Buy rated on InnoScience, and see current valuations as attractive.
