# 2Q26 Prelim: In-Line, but Strong Underlying Earnings
## Key Takeaways

Revenue growth accelerated 129% YoY on strong memory pricing (W171tr, 28% QoQ), with OP of W89.4tr (+1,810% YoY, +57% QoQ), in line with consensus.

■ Memory profits likely near W92tr led by strong commodity DRAM/NAND pricing while foundry/LSI losses reduced at around W2tr, in our view.

\- Mobile and appliances likely posted slight losses at around W1tr in total, whereas display posted W700bn profits on seasonal demand.

On employee bonus provisions, we believe it was roughly 10% of OP, reflecting total of 1H26 provisions in 2Q26.

Overall, the debate is not on this prelim (that's backward-looking), but we are on the right side of the memory cycle.

Samsung still is in the midst of a sharp profit recovery cycle. Up 165% YTD (vs. 91% KOSPI), the share price has performed well, but perhaps is not reflecting the full magnitude of what is ahead – a more upbeat outlook likely at the July 30 earnings call (10am KST). Modelling a 2Q26 DRAM ASP estimate of 56%, we see memory profits growing more than 1100% YoY in 2026, approaching the W412tr profit mark. The compute-to-power ratio favors Samsung's more advanced DRAM and logic base die nodes versus peers and imminent LTAs instil stability and improve earnings predictability.

Stock implications: 52% operating margin (with memory at 70%+) despite substantial bonus provisions reinforces our constructive view that optimism will extend that chase for performance throughout 2H26. Significant upside exists once we adjust to earnings growth in a period of unprecedented capacity constraints, and upside to capital returns may underpin yet further outperformance.
