# Jun Check-in & Corp Day Wrap: Improvement MoM, but still soft; Corp Day cites strong momentum on Value Retailers by F&B brands and caution on pricing with moderating cost pressure

We hosted 15+ Staples companies during our APAC Consumer & Leisure Corporate Day last week. Despite weak consumer sentiment/unfavorable weather in 2Q26, most companies noted a sequential MoM improvement in June compared to May, likely due to an easier comp base (June 2025 social retail/catering sales slowed to 4.8%/0.9%), health channel inventory, as well as Dragon Boat calendar shift, while weather remains as a drag to beverage/beer/pax mobility. Key takeaways from our Corp Day: 1) Brights spots with divergence in performance: Wanchen's store openings running ahead of expectations with relatively resilient SSSG trends. Mengniu/Tingyi/Yihai maintained their 1H/full-year sales outlooks with resilient core NP performance with CR Beer maintaining its outlook for the Beer segment. By contrast, Eastroc and Bud China/Chongqing/CR Bev/Weilong recalibrated 1H or 2Q expectations on weather drag/de-stocking/soft consumption sentiment, respectively. 2) Moderating Cost pressure: Majority of companies commented on moderating cost pressure with recent pull-back of PET/oil price. 1H26 GPM has remained at least resilient across most names, with some expansion at Tingyi/Eastroc (mainly cost lock-in tailwinds) and Yihai (price hikes in 2H25)/Yankershop (channel mix upgrade), and relatively stable for Mengniu/CR Bev/Weilong. Beverage names and Yihai generally see 2H26 cost pressure as more manageable as PET headwinds eases sequentially from June and efficiency gains continue to provide a buffer. Beer companies remain cautious and maintained full-year unit COGS outlooks mainly reflecting logistics/aluminum cost inflation. 3) F&B brands remain cautious on pricing actions, mainly on concerns on weak consumer sentiment/competition but focus is more on high-margin/ASP new product launches. CR Beer and CR Beverage had small-scale regional re-calibration of pricing for some specific SKUs but remain cautious on broader-space pricing adjustment; 4) Expense investment elevated but still manageable: Selling expense ratios likely rose modestly in 1H26 for beverage/beer names due to sports marketing (FIFA/World Cup, though largely within pre-set budgets)/accelerated fridge deployment (Eastroc)/O2O penetration (beer), while direct end-consumer promotion has been the key competition strategy especially for beverages (with

reallocation from channel rebates to manage expense level). For the full year, Mengniu/Yihai/Wanchen/Yankershop/CR Beer expect expense ratios to benefit from spending discipline/efficiency gains/op leverage, with beverage still subject to competition/fridge investment phasing in 2H26. 5) Channel and format expansion a more meaningful growth lever: Value retailers remain the fastest-growing channel. Tingyi is seeing high-DD% growth YTD in this channel, which now contributes HSD%/3% of noodle/beverage sales. CR Bev signed a partnership with Wanchen this year and is supplying differentiated SKUs. Yankershop/Weilong shared continued fast scaling in value retailer channels with continued SKU breadth expansion. 6) Enhancing shareholder return remains a key support: Yihai/Tingyi remain committed to maintaining a high payout ratio (at c.100%) and CR Beer/Mengniu are committed to steady growth in DPS yoy, while CR Beverage committed to a three-year 2026-28 dividend plan along with a share buyback and Eastroc is considering a L-T commitment on dividend payout along with share buyback as well. Weilong is considering a special dividend.

June Check-In: Our channel checks and Corp Day takeaways indicate sequential MoM improvement in June compared to the challenging May. In particular, beer trends showed sequential improvement from May but still challenging with Tsingtao seeing a sequential improvement to largely flattish volume yoy in June and CR Beer was relatively resilient, while Budweiser/Chongqing saw declines sequentially narrowed MoM. Dairy saw continued resilient performance with LSD% liquid milk sales growth for Mengniu/Yili, while Haitian saw better performance in June vs. May/April despite the company's heightened focus on a healthy channel inventory in end-2Q. Anjoy's sales growth recovered to 10%+ in June on a low base. Value retailers continued aggressive expansion with 1,000+\~1,500+ new openings monthly for leading brands in May/June, while per store daily GMV/SSSG were under impact of unfavorable weather, especially in the 2H of June. Guoquan June SSSG also declined by low teens% under high comp base/weather impact. Within Snacks brands, Chacha was the outperformer, showing acceleration in June, which we attribute to easier competition and company's proactive collaboration with value retailers. For beverages, however, some players saw MoM performance holding steady or slowing for June likely on still weak pax mobility: Nongfu's overall sales grew in the low-teens% driven by strong sugar-free tea (Oriental Leaf up 40%) despite a likely decline in water in May/June, while Eastroc saw a sequential slowdown in June with shipment pace control. Tingyi maintained LSD% growth in beverages mainly supported by Tea with direct end-consumer promotions, likely gaining share from UPC.

Into July and 3Q26, we highlight: 1) A continued low base for on-trade channels (2025 Jul/Aug/Sep catering retail sales at $1.1\% / 2.1\% / 0.9\%$ yoy) and even lower base for Spirits and snacks; 2) Weather conditions and consumption sentiment remain critical swing factors for beverages/beer during peak seasons; 3) Raw material cost trend sequentially eases though pricing power remains limited (PET price retreated to Rmb7.2k/t recently from Rmb9.6k/t peak in early May); Konjac/Sunflower seeds are trending lower as are snacks; 4) Value retailers store expansion in 3Q and stabilization of per-store GMV/SSSG trends remain key.

Key stock ideas (Buys): We continue to highlight F&B value retailers (Busy Ming/Wanchen) with accelerating store openings and L-T substantial room for further penetration and category expansion, dairy (Mengniu) on steady demand recovery. We continue to like Haitian-H (on CL)/Yihai on solid execution of share gain and dividend support. We upgrade Chacha (link) from Sell to Buy, given its recovering pricing power from easier competition coupled with cost tailwinds. We also reiterate our constructive view on Moutai/CR Beer/Yankershop.

## Jun Check-in: Sequential improvement MoM though still soft; Dairy more resilient; Value Retailers further accelerating store openings in May

## June trends by subsector based on channel checks:

Beer volumes showed sequential improvement. Tsingtao's volume was largely flattish in June vs. HSD% decline in May/Apr, CR Beer continues to outperform peers with slightly faster MoM growth, and Budweiser's volume decline remained under pressure but narrowed vs. low teens% decline in Apr/May. Chongqing's volume decline also sequentially narrowed vs. May.

\- Beverages sell-through trends remained mixed: Nongfu's overall sales grew in the low-teens% in both May/June, while bottled water sales slowed down to a decline in June though sugar-free tea sustained stellar momentum at 40% yoy. Tingyi saw stable trends with LSD% growth for both noodles and beverages in May/June mainly supported by Tea. June largely maintained the trend in May. UPC beverage sales remained soft at LSD% declines in May/June. Eastroc experienced a sequential slowdown in June compared to May, while CR Beverage 2Q performance was soft with water down SD% and other beverages down DD%.

Liquid milk demand continued its gradual recovery with both Mengniu and Yili recording LSD% positive sales growth in June, sequentially improved vs. May, likely on Dragon Boat Festival calendar shift.

Condiments: Haitian sales growth moderated in 2Q26 but with June/May trend slightly better than Apr; while Jonjee experienced a MoM slowdown in May/June vs. April due to higher comp base. Yihai's third-party sales growth remains solid at HSD\~10% yoy (vs. May at HSD% growth).

\- Frozen food: Anjoy up 10\~15% in June on a low base, despite improving vs. HSD% in May.

■ Value retailers maintained a rapid store opening pace, with Wanchen opening no less than 4.7k gross stores/4.1k net openings in 1H26, and maintaining healthy SSSG. Busy Ming also maintained solid store open pace, with positive SSSG as well.

Snack brands saw generally soft trends in June, with Weilong under more pressure but ChaCha's growth accelerated.
