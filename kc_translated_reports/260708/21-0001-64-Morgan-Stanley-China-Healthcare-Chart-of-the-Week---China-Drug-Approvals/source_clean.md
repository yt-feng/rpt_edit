# Chart of the Week – China Drug Approvals in 1H26 and 2025
## Key Takeaways

The NMPA approved 58 and 120 novel drugs in 1H26 and 2025, respectively. Of those approved in 1H26, 31 are small molecules and 27 are biologics.

These include izalontamab brengitecan (recurrent/metastatic NPC), satricabtagene autoleucel (CLDN18.2+/HER2-G/GEJA), and retlirafusp (PD-L1+ G/GEJC).


In 2025, oncology topped the number of approved indications, with breast cancer seeing 9 launches, e.g., PI3Kα and AKT inhibitors, TROP2/HER2-targeted ADCs.

China novel drug approvals crossed the triple-digit mark for the first time in 2025, with domestic developer approvals outnumbering MNCs': Of the 120 novel drugs approved in 2025, 63 originated from Chinese developers (vs. 57 from MNCs). In our view, this speaks to China's continual rise as a biopharma R&D powerhouse and a supportive regulatory environment that expedites drug development and trial starts. The help of China's vast CDMO industry was also instrumental in achieving technological breakthroughs. In 2025, China-originated assets covered multiple modalities with blockbuster potential: 1) mazdutide (Innovent), a dual GCG/GLP-1 agonist for weight loss and T2D; 2) trastuzumab rezetecan (Hengrui), a novel HER-2 targeting ADC for NSCLC and HER2+ breast cancer; 3) recaticimab (Hengrui), a novel PCSK9 inhibitor for hypercholesterolemia and mixed dyslipidemia. One of four key planks of our Top Pick thesis for WuXi AppTec-A (China's top CDMO) is an accelerating approval trend for small molecules leading to more commercial contracts and higher propensity to outsource by MNCs and biotechs (note).
