# An out-licensing deal with AstraZeneca Quick Note

On 8 July (HKT) before market open, Sino Biopharmaceutical (SBP) announced that it has entered into an exclusive licensing agreement with AstraZeneca (AZN LN, Not rated) for TQC3721, a proprietary inhaled PDE3/4 dual inhibitor.

According to the announcement, SBP will grant AstraZeneca an exclusive license to develop, manufacture, and commercialize TQC3721 outside Greater China. AstraZeneca will additionally obtain a global exclusive license for certain development plans of TQC3721.

SBP will obtain upfront payment of USD200mn, total potential milestones of up to USD 1.9 bn and, up to double-digit percentage of sales royalties.

SBP has two ongoing clinical trials for TQC3721 in China: (1) phase III for COPD (nebulized suspension), and the phase IIb study demonstrated est-in-class potential (data presented at ERS 2025, Amsterdam; and (2) phase II for COPD (DPI).

Our take: positive for the company and sector as well

This is SBP's second out-licensing deal with a MNC in 2026. In our view, the company's R&D capability is a testimony to the company's dedication and it suggests it is catching up with other leading Chinese big pharmas in terms of deal pace.

This deal also, to some extent, would help ease investors' concern about the slowing down of China molecule out-licensing trend, thus helping the current sector rebounding momentum.

We maintain our Buy rating and TP of HKD8.67 unchanged. The company will hold update call this afternoon on 2pm(HKT).
