# Weekly Coal Update: Ongoing Safety Inspections Across Shanxi Continued to Curb Coking Coal Supply
## Key Takeaways

Thermal coal prices trended down WoW, while coking coal prices stayed resilient.

Thermal coal prices trended down: QHD 5500 inched up 0.1% WoW to Rmb727/t as of July 3. BSPI dipped 0.3% WoW to Rmb712/t. CCI 5500 slipped 0.6% WoW to Rmb851/t. Mine-mouth prices for Shanxi Datong 5800 declined 3.4% WoW to Rmb700/t.

Seaborne prices increased slightly: NEWC prices inched up 0.8% WoW.

Domestic coking coal prices stayed resilient: Liulin No. 4 mine-mouth prices remained stable WoW at Rmb865/t. FOR prices unchanged at Rmb2,040/t. QLD prices edged up 0.4% WoW to US\$242/t.

Chart of the Week – Shanxi sees mixed coking coal mine stoppages and restarts: Ongoing safety inspections across Shanxi continued to curb coking coal supply, with frequent production stoppages and restarts at mines. Most mines are operating at 30-70% of capacity due to safety checks, previous overproduction and other operational issues, keeping regional supply low, according to a survey by Sxcoal. As of July 3, a total of 54 coking coal mines across five cities – Changzhi, Taiyuan, Jinzhong, Luliang and Linfen – were idled, with a combined capacity of 72.15 million tonnes per annum (mtpa), Sxcoal data showed. The number of halted mines was flat from a day ago but down 65 from May 25. Suspended capacity fell 0.3 mtpa day on day and 62.35 mtpa from May 25.
