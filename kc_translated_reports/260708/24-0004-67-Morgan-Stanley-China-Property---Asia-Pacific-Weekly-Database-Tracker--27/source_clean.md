# Weekly Database Tracker #27
## Key Takeaways

Weekly primary registered unit sales in 50 cities increased 22% YoY (vs. -11% YoY in the previous week) for the week ended July 5, bringing YTD sales to -11% YoY: Tier 1 city sales declined 6% (vs. -19% YoY). Tier 2 city sales rose 29% YoY (vs. -13% YoY). Tier 3 city sales increased 22% YoY (vs. +10% YoY).

Weekly secondary registered unit sales in 10 cities increased 4% YoY (vs. -1% YoY in the previous week), bringing YTD sales to +5% YoY: Tier 1 city weekly secondary unit sales rose 14% YoY (vs. flat). Tier 2 city weekly secondary unit sales dropped 3% YoY (vs. -0.4% YoY).

Total sell-through rate was 68% (vs. 53% in the previous week): Tier 1 cities recorded a sell-through rate of 90% (vs. 58%). Tier 2 cities recorded a sell-through rate of 68% (vs. 53%).

Centraline six-city secondary asking price tracking index was 16.7% (vs. 17.0% in the previous week), and the property agent index in Tier 1 cities was 54.7 (vs. 51.6 in the previous week).
