# Hy3 Released: Significant Gains in Agent Capabilities and Product Experience
## Key Takeaways

Tencent released an open-sourced Hy3 on July 6, retaining Hy3 Preview MoE architecture (295B parameters, 21B active, 256k-token context).

■ Meaningful gains on productivity tasks such as software development, office productivity, financial modeling, fronted design and game production.

Cost-effective practical approach: Tencent Cloud API pricing: Rmb 1.0/4.0 million input/output tokens, Rmb 0.25/million for cache-hit input.


Hy3's capabilities continue to improve across Tencent's product ecosystem. Tencent's product matrix in turn provides HY with extensive and authentic real-world feedback for optimization, while the model's improved capabilities in turn benefit all integrated products, creating a virtuous cycle of transferable and generalizable improvement.

\- WorkBuddy task resolution rate increased from 72% to 90%, with average completion time cut by 34%.

\- In Yuanbao Agent's benchmark evaluation, Hy3 surpassed GLM 5.1 and many other domestic models in comprehensive office and life service agent scenarios.

\- Broader Ecosystem: Hy3 also delivers improvements across IMA, Mavis Agent, WeChat Reading, WeGame AI gaming assistant and code generation capabilities.
