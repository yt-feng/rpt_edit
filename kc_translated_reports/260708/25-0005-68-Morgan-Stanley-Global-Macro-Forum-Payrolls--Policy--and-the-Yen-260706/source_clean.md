## Key Takeaways

\- US economics perspective: June employment data were softer-than-expected with downward revisions to prior months. We think lower oil prices, remarks from Chairman Warsh that inflation risks have come down, and less robust hiring mean the Fed will stay on hold in July, and for the remainder of the year. Financial conditions are moderately more restrictive than pre-conflict levels.

\- US treasury market views: Current market pricing implies tightening vs the easing in the probability-weighted path envisioned by our economists. According to the proxy funds rate of the San Francisco Fed, the rate rise across US Treasuries, US credit, and US Mortgage-Backed Securities effectively hiked rates. We think this 100bp of effective rate hikes looks overdone.

\- US equity market views: The broadening out story is re-emerging as oil prices fall and take pressure off Fed to raise rates, and it could be aided by a peak in momentum leadership—semis. We like hyperscalers over semis. Our Favorite broadening beneficiaries include consumer discretionary goods, transports and biotech as lower oil leads to lower rates.

\- Perspective on JPY: JPY has not particularly underperformed since previous FX intervention. The current USD/JPY level is fair relative to Fed pricing, risk sentiment, and terms of trades.
