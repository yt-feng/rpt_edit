## AI Series #55: Hy3 Official Version Release; Token Trends; Lock-Up Expiry

Highlights (1) Tencent released Hy3 with further enhancements (e.g. success rate, cost efficiencies, long horizon tasks). API price is competitive (Exhibit 4) to support more users and scenario; (2) Token Expenditure Index: The week of 28 Jun remains soft and behind May level; (3) OpenRouter: Token usage is stable vs prior week; (4) Lock-up expiry for Zhipu / MiniMax this week; (5) new measures aim to protect users from AI virtual intimate relationship.

In this report, we provide investors with over 50 data points on token consumption in OpenRouter, pricing for different models, model intelligence in Artificial Analysis, user trends by sub-sector and other industry data for analysis.

Hy3 official version has a number of enhancements following user feedback. Highlights of Hy3 vs preview version include (1) feedback received across over 50 business units and upgrade with better user experience. On input/output price, Hy3 is charged at a lower price at RMB1/RMB4 per million tokens vs RMB2/RMB8 for preview version. It aims to support more users and scenario; (2) further enhancements in post training such as Reinforcement Learning (RL) Exhibit 5. Better performance in long contexts vs large models which are 2-5x more in terms of parameters; (3) value creation in productivity scenario such as software development, office, financial, design and game development; (4) Blind test among 270 experts in real work scenario show Hy3 scores better than GLM-5.1 (e.g. frontend, data, storage, CI/CD); (4) further improvements in (a) higher task success rate vs preview version (90% vs prior 72%), (b) token efficiency: compared to GLM-5.2, savings in token consumption is achieved in word processing (47.4%) and PPT (49%); (c) reduced hallucination by 15 pp. Please refer to Exhibit 6 across different third party assessments.

Silicon Data LLM Token Expenditure Index: The week of 28 Jun showed continued softness. The index (Exhibit 2) is weighted by where usage is concentrated (e.g. expenditure goes up when demand shifts towards premium models). On the other hand, media reports (e.g. The Information, Economic Observer) highlight tech and internet companies set upper limit on token consumption among employees. According to our checks, the index ranged from 1.62 to 1.71 between 28 Jun to 4 Jul (vs 1.68 to 1.74 between 21 to 27 Jun), vs 2.04 on 31 May. Based on Artificial Analysis, China models DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, MiniMax and Zhipu are highly cost-efficient vs US models and API cost is a fraction vs US models (Exhibit 3), thanks to former's refined architecture such as MoE (Mixture of Experts), attention mechanism (Group-Query Attention GQA, sparse attention) and MLU (Model FLOPs Utilization).

OpenRouter: Token consumption remains stable vs prior week. Based on analysis of the week of 29 Jun (vs week of 22 Jun) (Exhibit 1), number of weekly tokens consumed remained stable at 46.7tn. In terms of ranking, DeepSeek V4 Flash ranked no 1, followed by Xiaomi MiMo-V2.5 and MiniMax M3.

DS-V4 and Seedance 2.5 will be released in Jul. As per our recent updates (link) and (link), DS-V4 will have new pricing strategies starting from 15 Jul with the doubling of input/output price during peak hours (9:00 to 12:00, 14:00 to 18:00) (Exhibit 7-9). We view highly cost-efficient model is key amid rising AI demand and supply constraints. On the other hand, video generation model Seedance 2.5 is expected to be released soon with upgrade in (a) 30-sec video generation; (b) up to 50 multimodal input assets; (c) upgrades in video editing and generation improve efficiencies and quality.

Authorities implement measures to regulate anthropomorphic services. The measures were released in April 2026 by Cyberspace Administration of China, National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Public Security and State Administration for Market Regulation. The measures will be implemented on 15 Jul and aim to (1) protect users from issues arising from virtual intimate relationship services (e.g. virtual relatives or virtual companions); (2) avoid over-dependence on AI and affect real interpersonal relationships. We have seen Doubao and Qwen will stop offering these features on 15 Jul and 10 Jul respectively.
