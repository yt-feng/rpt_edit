# Sony Group (6758): Investment thesis, valuation, and risks

## Sony Group (Overweight; Price Target: ¥5,700)

## Investment Thesis

In the entertainment space, we have a positive view of Sony Group's preeminent global platforms or IP in games, movies, music, and animation. Over the medium term, we expect the company to expand its game and animation platforms and generate synergies with entertainment IP.

## Valuation

Our price target of ¥5,700 through December 2026 is based on an SOTP model referencing EV/EBITDA multiples of comparable companies: G&NS at 18x, Music at 11x, Pictures/Film at 14x, ET&S at 7x, and I&SS at 10x. We then apply a 10% conglomerate discount to arrive at our price target. (The SOTP model is on the previous slide.)

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Upside Scenario to Target Price/Rating

\- Faster-than-expected penetration for PlayStation 5

\- Sharper-than-expected increase in image sensor profitability

\- Hit first-party content in games and movies

## Downside Scenario to Target Price/Rating

\- Disruptive pricing by rivals in semiconductors and games

\- Reversal of multicamera smartphone trend

\- Deterioration in semiconductor profitability accompanying sudden yen appreciation

# Mitsubishi Electric (6503): Investment thesis, valuation, and risks

Mitsubishi Electric (Overweight; Price Target: ¥7,000)

## Investment Thesis

In the short term, we expect increased demand for factory automation systems and optical semiconductors driven by growing data center investments, and in the medium term, we anticipate expanding demand for power supplies/equipment and IT cooling systems accompanying the shift to DC800V power supplies for data centers, as well as for sensors, radars, missiles, information processing, and satellite communications technologies related to new defense areas (drones, AI, electronic warfare, and satellite and space domain utilization). Capital allocation aimed at restructuring the business portfolio and increasing ROE also remains a focus.

## Valuation

Our end-December 2026 price target of ¥7,000 values the shares at a 10% conglomerate discount to the theoretical share price we derive using an SOTP model based on our FY2027 forecasts. Target multiples in our SOTP model are based on EV/EBITDA multiples of comparable companies.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

## Upside Scenario to Target Price/Rating

\- Further improvement in the factory automation and semiconductor device cycles

\- Stronger-than-expected demand and profitability in the air conditioning business

\- Order growth in infrastructure, defense, and data center-related businesses

## Downside Scenario to Target Price/Rating

\- Decline in factory automation system and semiconductor device demand owing to a slowdown in macro demand

\- Delays in pass-throughs of sharp material cost increases

\- Reductions of defense budgets and data center investment plans

## Hitachi (6501): Investment thesis, valuation, and risks

Hitachi (Overweight; Price Target: ¥6,000)

## Investment Thesis

Hitachi's Lumada digital solution platform is contributing not only to expanding its business to a broad range of industries, supported by the digital transformation of overall society and industry, but also to converting its business model to one that is based mainly on services. Hitachi also has a large number of growth opportunities related to the environment (power transmission, railway systems, industry). We look for continued share buybacks along with asset sales.

## Valuation

Our December 2026 price target of ¥6,000 is set 10% above the theoretical price derived from a SOTP valuation based on our FY2027 forecasts. Target multiples in our SOTP model are based on EV/EBITDA multiples of comparable companies. We apply this premium due to (1) Hitachi's business model that can leverage its strengths as a conglomerate, (2) high visibility for profit growth, and (3) management's commitment to improving capital efficiency. (The SOTP model is on the previous slide.)

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

## Upside Scenario to Target Price/Rating

\- Greater-than-expected expansion in demand and margin in the power transmission and distribution business

\- Industry segment growth due to increase in domestic capex

\- Increase in valuation premium due to higher-than-expected profitability driven by the expansion of Lumada

Downside Scenario to Target Price/Rating

\- Intensified price competition in the IT services market

\- Unprofitable major projects in the energy and railway areas

\- Conglomerate discount due to insufficient margin improvement at Lumada

## Panasonic HD (6752): Investment thesis, valuation, and risks

## Panasonic Holdings (Neutral; Price Target: ¥4,600)

## Investment Thesis

We focus on profitability improvement across the company through structural reforms and portfolio management, growth in data center-related businesses such as BBUs and substrate materials, securing a medium- to long-term competitive advantage in automotive batteries, the possibility of realizing the potential value of Blue Yonder, and measures to improve competitiveness in existing businesses.

## Valuation

Our December 2026 price target is ¥4,600. Based on our FY2027 estimates, we use our SoTP model that refers to the average multiples of industry peers (Bloomberg consensus basis) and apply a conglomerate discount of 20% for a theoretical price of ¥4,810. Our ¥4,600 price target is the midpoint between that ¥4,810 and ¥4,410, a SoTP-based theoretical value premised on no IRA subsidies. (The SOTP model is on the previous slide.)

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Upside Scenario to Target Price/Rating

\- Greater-than-expected sales growth in AI data center-related businesses such as BBUs

\- Increase in profitability of automotive batteries

\- Improved profitability of Blue Yonder

## Downside Scenario to Target Price/Rating

\- Decline in market share and profitability in AI data center-related businesses

\- Decline in profitability of automotive batteries

\- Delays to the recovery in Blue Yonder's profitability
