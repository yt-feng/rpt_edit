# ASEAN Internet
# Food delivery and e-commerce receipts continue to show healthy growth in May'26
## What did we learn from the May'26 ASEAN Food Delivery and E-commerce Receipts data?

Leveraging UBS Evidence Lab Food Delivery (> Access Dataset) and e-commerce receipts, we conclude that: 1) Grab's food delivery volume growth remained healthy at 21% YoY in May'26, though moderating from 26-28% in Mar/Apr'26, partly due to weakness in Indonesia; 2) Similarly, Shopee's e-commerce GMV growth also posted healthy +21% YoY GMV growth, moderating from 22/33% in Mar/Apr'26, due to seasonality; 3) Competitive intensity for both ASEAN food delivery and e-commerce sectors ticked up in May'26, with discounts trending up MoM. We attribute part of the slowdown to changing timing for Lebaran, but also see weak macro as a factor that needs to be closely monitored.

## Growth momentum remains healthy in May'26

- Food delivery: Grab's ASEAN "GMV weighted average" order volumes grew 21% YoY in May'26, healthy albeit moderating from 26-28% in Mar-Apr'26. This was partly due to continued weakness in Indonesia (-2.6% YoY orders) amid a weak macro backdrop. AOV was flat MoM (-0.3%) and slightly down (-1.1%) YoY, as Grab continues to push affordability focused products to drive growth. In comparing peers, Grab gained 69/136bps market share vs foodpanda in Philippines/Malaysia, although it lost some (17bps) share in Singapore.
- E-commerce: Shopee's ASEAN e-commerce GMV grew 20.6% YoY in May'26 - healthy but, similar to food delivery, moderating from 23/33% in Mar/Apr'26 as we move past Lebaran seasonality. Volumes grew at a healthy pace of 28% YoY, although AOV was down 5.6% YoY.

## Competitive intensity broadly inched up in May'26

- Food delivery: On a MoM basis, competitive intensity inched up slightly in May'26, with higher discounting and lower delivery fees. Grab's discounts as a % AOV increased 5-140bps MoM across ASEAN with the exception of Vietnam (-38bps) and Philippines (-390bps). foodpanda took the opposite approach of Grab by lowering discounts in Malaysia (-22bps) and Singapore (-200bps) while increasing in Philippines (+70bps). Delivery fees as a % AOV were down 5-105bps MoM across ASEAN for both Grab and foodpanda, except Vietnam (+8bps for Grab) and Malaysia (+23bps for foodpanda).
- E-commerce: Competitive intensity was up with both Shopee and seller funded promotions trending higher MoM in May'26. Shopee funded discounts were flat in Singapore but up 60-125bps MoM in Malaysia, Vietnam and Thailand. That said, it was down 30/73bps in Indonesia/Philippines. Meanwhile, seller funded discounts were up 10-95bps MoM across all ASEAN markets.

## Sector view: Maintain Buy on both Grab (PT US\$5.9) and Sea (PT US\$125)

Overall, receipts data continue to indicate robust growth and a generally healthy competitive environment, supporting our thesis that: 1) Grab's affordability initiatives to expand TAM continue to drive structural growth, reinforcing its position as the leading pan-ASEAN food delivery platform; 2) Shopee continues to outgrow the e-commerce market as its moat around logistics, scale and customer engagement via ShopeeVIP starts to differentiate it vs peers. Maintain Buy on Grab (PT US\$5.9) and Sea (PT US \$125).

## ASEAN Food Delivery & Rideshare Receipt Transaction Monitor (>Access Dataset)

This report leverages the following UBS Evidence Lab asset: ASEAN Food Delivery &Rideshare Receipt Transaction Monitor. UBS Evidence Lab uses email receipts from a leading provider of consumer insights data that monitors billions of online consumer transactions. Key benefits of this data compared to many other commonly used credit card and debit card transaction panels is that the receipt data provides cash transactions and a more granular view of the receipt composition, including various fees and taxes charged, promotions applied, and detailed food and beverage items users ordered. Each receipt collection is tested using a proprietary set of data quality checks, with only passing records used in the analysis. Data cleansing is applied at multiple levels to ensure both completeness and accuracy. We utilize statistical measures to detect anomalies within each collection. We also apply proprietary scrub functions to clean and normalize data types across service providers, ultimately creating a standard data model containing the entire data pool. Exceptions at any stage are alerted to our data steward team for review and resolution. Data is collected daily and aggregated weekly and monthly. We aggregated the data on service provider and market levels. Foreign Currency: order prices in local currency are all converted to USD. Receipt Composition Taxonomy: Cleansed receipt line items are taxonomized into standard a composition category using a proprietary mapping process. Order Normalization: To exclude the impact of outsized orders on the analysis while keeping all orders in the scope, orders are labelled as Trimmed Orders if the receipts are within confidence intervals. User Type: Orders are labelled as New User or Loyal User.

## Valuation Method and Risk Statement

Sea: Our price target is based on SOTP valuation. We benchmark price/sales, EV/GMV and EV/EBITDA from other gaming and e-commerce companies across Asia and LatAm to value Digital Entertainment and E-commerce segment. We believe the risks for SEA are: 1) regulatory risks, in particular related to data privacy, security, online content, and digital financial services; 2) macroeconomic risks, related to ASEAN and global economic outlook; 3) industry risks from competition from offline retailers and global internet giants, i.e. Amazon, Alibaba, Tencent, others; 4) relationship risks with Tencent as one of SEA's biggest shareholders and because SEA's games portfolio in ASEAN is highly reliant on Tencent's published games; and 5) execution risks arising from business complexities as the company continues to grow.

GoTo: Our price target is based on SOTP valuation. We benchmark EV/sales, EV/GMV and EV/EBITDA from other ride hailing, online food delivery, e-commerce, and fintech companies in the region (Asia EMs) as well as other peers in DMs. We believe the risks for GoTo are: 1) regulatory risks, in particular related to data privacy, security, online content, and digital financial services; 2) macroeconomic risks, related to ASEAN, Indonesian, and global economic outlook; 3) industry risks from competition from offline players, local champions, and global internet players, i.e. Grab, Sea, and others; and 4) execution risks arising from business complexities as the company continues to grow.

Grab: Our price target is based on SOTP valuation. We benchmark price/sales, EV/GMV and EV/EBITDA from other ride hailing, online food delivery, e-commerce, and fintech companies in the region (Asia EMs) as well as other peers in DMs. We believe the risks for Grab are: 1) regulatory risks, in particular related to data privacy, security, online content, and digital financial services; 2) macroeconomic risks, related to ASEAN and global economic outlook; 3) industry risks from competition from offline players, local champions, and global internet players, i.e. DeliveryHero and others; and 4) execution risks arising from business complexities as the company continues to grow.
