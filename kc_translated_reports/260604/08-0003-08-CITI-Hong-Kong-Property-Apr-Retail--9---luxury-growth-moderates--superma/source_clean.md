# Hong Kong Property
# CITI'S TAKE

HK April 2026 retail sales were +8.6%yoy, a solid performance but at milder pace relative to Mar (+13%) and 1Q26 (+12%). The luxury segment, proxied by “jewelry, watches & valuable gifts”, decelerated to +20%yoy (1Q: +28%), reflecting a fading favorable base effect and lower gold prices. Supermarket continued to show signs of stabilization at +3%yoy (1Q: +2%), partly supported by increased promotion by operators. Online retail maintained strong momentum at +31%yoy, now accounting for 10% of total retail sales value amid shifted purchasing habit.

May data supported by Golden Week tourism (+8%yoy); beware of upcoming higher comparable base — We estimate HK May retail sales could rise 5-8%yoy (to HK\$33-34bn), partly driven by the 8%yoy growth in visitor arrivals during the 5.1 holiday, which included +10%yoy increase in Chinese mainland visitors. Despite the tailwind from a weaker HKD FX against Rmb (5M26: -4%), we expect overall retail to post sequential deceleration in yoy growth, as the base for comparison strengthens given the recovery in market sentiment that kicked in from late-May to June 2025. Relative to our previous expectations, we have turned slightly constructive on non-discretionary retail, noting its spot rent has begun to stabilize thanks to the sales stabilization (e.g., supermarket).

Our thoughts on retail landlords — Link REIT: we have recently upgraded to Buy given spot rent stabilization and the company's pivot to a divestment-and-buyback model; see more at our report U/G to Buy: More Good News; 7.6% Total Return on Divest & Buyback. For luxury retail across HK and Chinese mainland, the investors' worry on SSSG deceleration on a higher base into 2H had been a primary cause behind share price corrections. That said, Hang Lung Properties' 6.5% yield may started to be viewed as attractive by some yield-seeking investors, and Wharf REIC's tenant sales at Harbour City have outperformed the broader market (though some investors flag reversion as an overhang given company guidance). Across the whole HK property space, our top picks are SHKP, CKA, Swire Properties and Link REIT.
