# More Clarity on Cross-Border Investment: A Turning Point for Hong Kong Housing Market?

China's State Council has today issued the Regulations on Outbound Investment, effective 1 July 2026, covering mainland enterprises, organisations, and individual residents. Notably, Article 32 explicitly extends regulatory oversight to Hong Kong-related transactions, signalling a clear intent to tighten supervision of cross-border capital flows. Meanwhile, Article 12 introduces stricter procedural requirements, mandating pre-approval or filing, ongoing information reporting, and comprehensive cross-border capital registration for outbound investments. On enforcement, Article 27 sets out stringent penalties. Illegal gains may be confiscated and fines of $0.1\% - 0.5\%$ of the investment amount imposed where investors: i) engage in prohibited outbound investment; ii) fail to complete required approval or filing procedures; or iii) obtain approvals through bribery, fraud, or other improper means. For non-compliance or failure to rectify, fines may increase to $0.5\% - 1.0\%$ , alongside potential restrictions on outbound investment activities for one to three years.
