## China Autos & Shared Mobility | Asia Pacific
# Weekly Orders – Robust new model lift, yet divergent sustainability across players
## Key Takeaways

Weekly order intake of EV majors continued to show divergent trends, with the upside still very much tethered to new vehicle debuts.
Overall, demand of incumbent models stayed lackluster, with muted seasonal strength.
Among key players, NIO and Aito outshined peers, thanks to ES9 and M9, respectively, while BYD, Leapmotor and Xiaomi registered steady WoW order inflows.
The sector reacted positively to the MoM recovery of May sales, supported by model launches and overseas exposure, making 2Q results likely beat guidance.
Some investors have speculated about potential additional policy stimulus, which we view as less likely.
