## China HDT
## CITI'S TAKE

According to CV World, May HDT wholesale volume in China was around 103k units (+16% YoY/-12% MoM), with MoM decline due to depressed LNG HDT sales amid narrowed diesel-LNG spread and inventory destocking by major OEMs. The HDT insurance retail number was around +20% YoY/-18% MoM to 75.7k units. NEV HDT volume surged by +90% YoY/+3% MoM to 28.7k units with monthly penetration approaching 40%. LNG HDT retail sales plunged by -2% YoY/-55% MoM to 13.8k units due to narrowed spread of diesel-LNG price gap. The export number was in-line at around 34k units (+28% YoY/-2% MoM).
