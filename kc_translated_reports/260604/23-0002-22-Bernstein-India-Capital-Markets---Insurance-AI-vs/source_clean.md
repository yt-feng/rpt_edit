# India Capital Markets & Insurance
# AI vs. Human - India Insurance: AI startups trying to disrupt online distribution - can they succeed?

AI startups have the potential to compete with incumbent financial product distribution businesses (detailed views here). Insurance needs more hand-holding than other products. PB Fin has a strong brand & an army of tele-advisors that service warm leads from the website, and close sales over a call (selection, documentation, claims). They have a dominant position in the insurance distribution market. We think startups will leverage AI voice-bots/chat-bots to try and break that dominance. At the same time, PB Fin has the opportunity to improve tele-advisor productivity & unit economics.

1. AI startups are coming - can they win? Imagine an AI-powered insurance expert who can 'talk' to customers about their insurance purchase like a human. Take the PB Fin tele-advisor away and replace it with an AI bot (voice/ chat). An AI-native experience where you can talk to an AI advisor and decide what insurance to buy. Zyra AI (private) is building this. We see more challengers rising, planning AI-powered call-centers (link). They may fail, but they will try, unlike the past.

2. AI vs. Human - unit economics: PB Fin pays \~INR 10.5/minute in salaries for customer talk-time (\~INR 50k/month, \~25 days/month, 8 hours/day, \~40% of advisor time spent talking to customers, Exhibit 3). AI bots can 'speak' with customers at \~INR 4-5/minute, using frontier models (lower with cheaper/open-source models).

Yes, both do not sound the same, and would have very meaningfully different conversion rates. The cost/minute favors AI, but the cost/sale or cost/revenue still tilts towards humans, for now. That may change as AI advances. AI-augmentation should be the base case for tele-advisors/call-center operations, may be even replacement.

3. Competition from voice-agents: AI-augmentation opportunity is very real, even today. Better AI models (more human-sounding, cheaper) could make a case for AI-replacement as well. Today, PB Fin wins because of its brand, and its trained tele-advisory force - a unique right-to-win. Rivals have not tried to build assisted-selling models since it is difficult to manage a large tele-advisory force, and the high cost of driving web traffic for a new entrant. AI voice-bots give challengers some help in navigating the first hurdle. We expect challengers to leverage AI to build new assisted-selling models.

Applications beyond online insurance distribution: The productivity boost for PB Fin is large due to the nature of the business, but any sales function with client engagement as a key metric can benefit from higher clients/employee, and thus drive better unit economics & margins. We share our detailed views here.

Wealth managers can empower relationship managers (RMs) with AI tools to manage more clients. More clients per RM is more AuM/RM, which in turn is more revenue and profits/RM. This expands the margin profile as well since the cost base does not grow in line with the revenue line in such a case.

Mutual fund distributors (MFDs), and MF sub-distributors like Prudent (not covered) can leverage AI to augment their technology platform driving higher client conversions, and better client level reporting and recommendations. Banks can enhance branch-level teller/RM productivity. Insurers can invest in improving agency channel sales productivity, and so on.

# INVESTMENT IMPLICATIONS

We think AI startups will bring competition to the financial services distribution space. Insurance is the toughest product to sell, and PB Fin has done well to build a large and efficient sales machinery. Erstwhile competitors did not succeed due to the lack of a strong brand and their unwillingness to invest in the tele-advisory setup (call-center sales). We think AI will allow challengers to compete on the assisted selling model where PB Fin has built an edge. At the same time, AI offers PB Fin the opportunity to materially improve its own unit economics, driving higher productivity for the tele-advisory force and thus lower the cost of the call-center operations. This is a timely opportunity as it grapples with a potential compression in take-rates from regulatory action. While the report focuses on online insurance sales, we think the productivity boost opportunity equally applies to agency insurance sales, wealth managers, mutual fund distributors, etc.

We rate PB Fin Outperform.
