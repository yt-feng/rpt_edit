# Hong Kong Property | Asia Pacific
## April Retail Sales Slowed
## Key Takeaways

(=) April retail sales rose 8.6% YoY to HK\$31.4bn, (Mar: +12.8%), below Bloomberg consensus of 13.7% but in line with MSe. 4M26 sales growth was at 11.3% YoY.
(+/-) Electrical (+21.9% YoY) & luxury (+19.8%) sales remained strong, but both slowed MoM. Positive wealth effects and active housing market continued to help.
(+/-) Supermarkets returned to growth (+3% YoY) but department stores were weak (-6.7%). Overall consumer durable goods grew 19.8% (Mar: +28.2%).
(+/-) Motor vehicles sales increased 46.1% YoY (Mar:+81%) on last-minute purchase due to expiry of 'OfO replacement scheme'. Fuel sales dropped on rising cost.
(-) But online retail sales jumped 30.6% YoY (4M26: +30.2%). Mass retail could stay vulnerable with further penetration of Chinese e-commerce, in our view.

(+) Mainland visitor arrivals surged 11.5% YoY on the May GW holidays with overall May visitation up 9.5%. Strengthening RMB should give mainland visitors more buying power.

Our view - Link REIT (OW) is seeing stabilizing spot rents in its HK retail portfolio despite relatively conservative guidance on rental reversion (FY27E: -8.% YoY). We expect (1) necessity trade sales to further stabilize while overall rentals could still decline until 1H28E; and (2) May retail sales growth to stay at high-single-digit YoY on revived inbound tourism and healthy economic growth.
