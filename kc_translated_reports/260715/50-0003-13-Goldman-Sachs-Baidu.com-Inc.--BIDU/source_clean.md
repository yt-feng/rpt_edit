# Baidu.com Inc. (BIDU)
# 2Q26 preview: Cloud momentum to sustain with improving margin; Elevated AI investment in 2H; Not Rated


We preview Baidu's 2Q26 results (likely to report in mid/late-Aug), with Baidu Core revenue declining $-4\%$ yoy to Rmb25.2bn, reflecting persistent pressure from traditional advertising, partially cushioned by continued robust growth in AI Cloud business. While topline growth remains challenged, we expect Baidu Core OP trend to largely stabilize at $-18\%$ yoy in 2Q26E (vs. $-19\%$ yoy in 1Q26) thanks to sequentially improving AI Cloud profitability and disciplined cost control. That said, we expect a milder-than-expected recovery of Baidu Core OP in 2H26E as company plans to accelerate its investment especially in AI model training this year vs. in 2025.

Into the print, we believe investor focus will be primarily on: 1) sustainability and outlook of AI Cloud growth and margin trajectory; 2) AI capex plan, cadence of investment in model training and management's thoughts on Baidu's positioning in AI model arms race; 3) updates on dividends and dual-primary listing; 4) progress on Baidu's announced spin-off of the Kunlunxin division; 5) Group level profitability inflection and FCF/use of capital.

What's changed: We revise down our 2026E-28E revenue estimates by $-4\% \sim -6\%$ on a combination of weaker-than-expected advertising recovery and other business, and cut our 2026E-28E net profit estimates by $-5\% \sim -14\%$ to reflect enhanced AI investments and foreign exchange losses in 2026E. We are Not Rated on BIDU/9888.HK.

Within it, we forecast GPU Cloud will still deliver triple-digit% yoy growth in 2Q26E, propelled by the strong downstream demand across different industries and Baidu's full-stack capability.

Beyond revenue growth, we see AI Cloud margin as benefiting from a more favorable business mix as high-margin GPU Cloud services increasingly account for a greater portion of total AI Cloud revenue, together with improving profitability in legacy project-based business. We believe the combination of strong AI Cloud infrastructure demand and improving business mix should continue supporting the sequential improvement of AI Cloud profitability in the following quarters of 2026E.

## 2. Advertising remains under pressure with no signs of a near-term inflection

In contrast to the AI Cloud segment, we expect Baidu's advertising business (GSe: -21% yoy in 2Q26E) to continue facing structural headwinds, on a combination of 1) traditional search advertising, which is undergoing gradual erosion from changing user behavior among the proliferation of AI tools, 2) still no breakthroughs from Baidu's AI search transition initiatives, and 3) a still challenging macro.

That said, we note that advertising now accounts for a progressively smaller share of Baidu Core revenue mix (<50% in 2026E vs. 60%+ in 2025), and its drag on Baidu Core revenue trajectory should gradually decrease over time.

## 3. Core OP to stabilize at -18% yoy in 2Q, but accelerating AI investments likely to weigh on FY26 OP recovery

We expect Baidu Core OP trend to sequentially stabilize at -18% yoy in 2Q26E (vs. -19% yoy in 1Q26), supported by improving Cloud margin and ongoing operating-efficiency initiatives.

However, company is stepping up investments in AI model training to catch up with leading domestic peers, and we expect model-training investments in 2026 to be meaningfully elevated yoy, driven primarily by increased GPU procurement and additional computing resources. As a result, we expect its impact on earnings to gradually build through 2H26E as AI-related spending ramps up, and therefore forecast Baidu Core OP to decline -3% yoy in 2026E.
