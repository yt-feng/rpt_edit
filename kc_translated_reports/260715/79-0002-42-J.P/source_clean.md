## China Home Appliance

## Weak June AVC print, noisy subsidy base, resilient leaders

## What's new?

According to AVC (All View Cloud) data, China home appliances (H/A) retail sales fell 19% YoY in Jun (Table 1), deteriorating from -9% in May despite an easier comp. Two-year stack was also weak at -4%, versus +20%/+1% in May/Apr. For 2Q26, retail sales fell 15% YoY, versus -10% in 1Q, while two-year stack growth improved to +5% from -1%. In other words, Jun exit momentum was poor, but the quarterly picture is more mixed — and these growth metrics are (very) noisy given the uneven base from the trade-in subsidy cycle.

The investor debate is therefore not about the exact growth rate, but what Jun really signals: a weather/base/timing blip, or more persistent post-subsidy demand weakness? We lean toward a cyclical interpretation, with weakness amplified by fading subsidy impact and poor consumption sentiment, rather than a structural demand break. Anyway, our constructive case on Midea/Haier (both OWs) does not rely on a China H/A upcycle, but on their B2B and overseas growth opportunities. Midea remains our top pick; see our recent in-depth report for details.

## Demand pictures in details

\- Overall demand: AVC data show Jun 2026 H/A retail sales declined $19\%$ YoY, deteriorating from $-9\%$ YoY in May despite an easier comp base. Two-year stack growth also weakened to $-4\%$ , versus $+20\%$ in May and $+1\%$ in Apr. Jun exit momentum was clearly weak, but we would not over-read one noisy month given the uneven base from the trade-in subsidy cycle.

\- By channel: The slowdown was broad-based across both online and offline. Jun online sales declined $16\%$ vs. $-7\%$ in May (or $-6\%$ vs. $+20\%$ in May, on a two-year stack), while offline sales declined $22\%$ vs. $-11\%$ in May (or $-2\%$ vs. $+21\%$ in May on a two-year stack). This suggests the weakness was not purely channel-specific, though online appears more exposed to fading subsidy impact and price-sensitive demand.

\- By category: AC was the biggest drag, given it is the single largest Jun category, accounting for $>40\%$ of Jun sales over the past five years. AC sales declined $28\%$ YoY, a 14ppt deterioration from $-14\%$ in May, and were down $6\%$ on a two-year stack basis, a 30ppt deceleration from $+23\%$ in May. Cooler/rainier weather didn't help, but weakness was not just a weather story: refrigerators, washing machines and kitchen appliances also saw 5-10ppt/20-30ppt deteriorations in YoY/two-year stack growth. Vacuum was the clear outlier with $13\%$ YoY growth, accelerating from $-8\%$ in May, and $43\%$ two-year stack growth, accelerating from $27\%$ in May, mostly volume driven.

\- By volume/pricing: This is the messiest part of the data — YoY says volume was the problem, but the base is not clean. AC sales volume declined 25% YoY, a 20ppt deterioration from May, while ASP declined 3% YoY, improving from -9% YoY in May. Refrigerator and washing machine sales volume declined 6-12% YoY, a \~10ppt deterioration from May, while ASP declined 5-6% YoY, similar to May. Taken literally, this looks like volume-led weakness; but given last year's subsidy-rush demand, we think that overstates the underlying demand deterioration. On a two-year stack basis, white goods sales volume was broadly flattish, moving up/down within the LSD range, while ASP was down MHSD. So the cleaner conclusion is: units are normalizing after subsidy pull-forward, not collapsing, but consumers are still trading down / buying cheaper mix across both online and offline channels. Vacuum was the exception, with sales volume up 7% YoY, a 14ppt improvement from May, and ASP up 5% YoY, a 6ppt improvement.

## Market share trends (online and offline combined)

\- Total H/A industry: Despite weak industry demand in Jun, share trends were not uniformly negative. Midea's market share improved to $26.3\%$ , from $25.2\%$ in 5M26, mainly due to seasonal strength in AC, where it remains the clear leader. Haier's total market share declined to $23.7\%$ , from $24.3\%$ in 5M26, but this was mainly a mix thing (i.e., heavy skew toward AC in June); category-level data show broad-based share gains for Haier across most segments.

\- AC: Midea's market share improved to $34.9\%$ , from $33.2\%$ in 5M26, retaining the No.1 position. Gree ranked No.2, though share declined to $25.4\%$ , from $29.7\%$ in 5M26. Haier's share increased to $14.3\%$ , from $13\%$ in 5M26, while Xiaomi's share rose to $6.9\%$ , from $5.4\%$ in 5M26, helped by the 6.18 online promotion period.

\- Refrigerators: Haier's market share increased to $43.7\%$ , from $40\%$ in 5M26, mainly due to share gains in online channels. Midea's market share declined to $20.4\%$ , versus $21.5\%$ in 5M26.

\- Washing machines: Haier's market share remained stable at $39.2\%$ , versus $39.2\%$ in 5M26, while Midea's share improved to $35.0\%$ , from $34.5\%$ in 5M26, mainly driven by gains in offline channels.

Vacuum: Ecovacs' market share increased to $31.0\%$ , from $29.1\%$ in 5M26, broadly in line with its usual seasonal strength.
