# Estun (002747.SZ)
## CITI'S TAKE

We raise 2026E/27E earnings by 70%/26% to reflect Estun's better GPM profile and asset disposal gains as well as investment income in 2026; we introduce 2028E. Estun is diversifying its customer base away from key customers such as BYD (1211.HK) to improve its GPM; however, we expect Estun to still book \~Rmb20mn in impairment loss due to deteriorating receivables quality of leading NEV customers. We also estimate non-operating income to reduce from \~Rmb100mn in 1Q26 to Rmb10mn in 2Q26E without significant asset revaluation gains in 2Q26E (Rmb70mn in 1Q26). As such, we project Estun's 2Q26E net profit to be Rmb26mn, 29% below Bloomberg consensus. As Estun's share price is up \~80% YTD, we think weaker-than-expected 2Q26 results could be a near-term negative catalyst. Maintain Sell and prefer Leader Drive (688017.SS) over Estun among high P/E automation and robotics names.

2Q26E preview – We forecast Estun's gross profit to grow by 34% YoY to Rmb473mn in 2Q26E on 15% YoY revenue growth and 4.5pp YoY GPM expansion, which would be driven by both cost reduction (i.e., procuring more reducers from low-cost suppliers) and customer diversification (i.e., penetrating Seres and Geely to diversify from BYD). However, our net profit estimate of Rmb26mn (up 538% YoY from Rmb6mn net loss) is 29% below consensus since we estimate Rmb20mn in impairment loss related to leading NEV clients and only Rmb10mn in investment income in 2Q26E (vs. Rmb100mn in 1Q26 due to Rmb70mn in revaluation gains on Nanjing Yigong, 南京工艺 – a ball screw and linear guide maker)

Acquisition of Estun Codroid – Initial impact from a potential acquisition of Estun Codriod would likely be negative as Codriod is loss-making. Potential synergy is uncertain given Codroid's early stage of physical AI / humanoid robot development.

TP change and valuation – Our TP of Rmb28 (from Rmb13.8) is based on 12.3x 2026E P/B (prev: 6.4x 2025 P/B), set at 2.0 SD above avg P/B. Although we apply a higher multiple to reflect Estun's improving fundamentals, we think that its rich valuations, at \~19x 2026E P/B and \~180x P/E, have overly priced in all the positives, exposing the stock to any potential negative catalysts, such as 2Q26 results.

## Estun

## Company description

Founded in 1993, Nanjing Estun Automation Company (Estun) is one of China's leading manufacturer of factory automation components and industrial robots, both of which are applied in a wide variety of industries including solar power, consumer electronics, EV, CNC, construction machinery and etc. In addition to developing the technologies itself, Estun also strengthens its core competence and technology edges by acquiring international automation companies, including German welding robot maker CLOOS (private).

## Investment strategy

We have a Sell rating on Estun as we are concerned that its rich valuations seem to have priced in all the positives, exposing the stock to any potential negative catalysts such as possibly weaker-than-expected 2Q26 results.

## Valuation

We use P/B as our valuation methodology due to Estun's fluctuating earnings. Our target price of Rmb28.0 is based on 12.3x 2026E P/B, set at 2.0 SD above average to reflect Estun's improving fundamentals.

## Risks

Key upside risks that could cause Estun shares to trade above our target price include: 1) stronger-than-expected industrial robot shipments or market share gains; 2) better-than-expected GPM due to lower raw material costs or alleviated price competition; and 3) favorable FX.
