# Solid 1H26 profit alert reaffirms upward trend
## ASIC and switch markets are the key growth drivers
## Action: Maintain Buy while raise TP to CNY159, implying 25.4% upside

Shengyi Electronics (SE) reported a positive profit alert on 13 July after market close. The company guided for CNY56.2bn-59.37bn of revenue (+49%-58% y-y) and CNY10.82bn-11.37bn of net profit (+104-114% y-y) in 1H26E owning to strong AI PCB demand, as well as new capacity contribution, according to the company. We think SE has started the shipment of its new AI PCB product for its key ASIC customer due to the new product ramp-up (i.e. Trainium 3), which should underpin continued ASP and margin uptick in 2H26F. Moreover, we think the company will benefit from the AI switch upgrade in both overseas and China markets. We raise FY26-28F revenue / earnings forecasts by 7-15% / 6.5%-11.1% (Fig. 1) to reflect the faster product ramp-up from key ASIC and switch customers. We maintain Buy rating and raise TP to CNY159, based on 35x (unchanged) FY27F EPS of CNY4.55, in line with the company's historical median P/E, and its FY26-28F earnings CAGR of 37%. The stock is currently trading at 27.9x FY27F EPS.

## AI server and switch adoption will drive PCB content upgrade, leading to better demand visibility

We think the ASIC chip and AI switch demand would continue to accelerate in 2H26 and 2027 in both overseas and China markets due to the strong upsurge of token consumption from agentic workloads. We expect the company's server sector to remain the biggest revenue driver, contributing to $66\%$ of total revenue in FY26F and $28\%$ revenue CAGR from FY26-28F. Thanks to the large ASIC customer's product upgrade, the company's top growth accelerated in 2Q26, and we think it will continue due to the strong Trainium 3 lifecycle. Due to the upstream material price shortage, PCB companies are facing increased pressure than the upstream CCL / material companies, in our view, but the improving product mix will likely help them to mitigate the margin impact.

## Customer concentration is a key risk, but diversification is underway

We believe the company has solid technology in HLC (high layer count) PCB and the telecom equipment / network switch markets, which may help it to diversify its customer portfolio. China's accelerated AI investment will likely help the company develop more domestic customers. We believe the revenue exposure to the key global ASIC customer would decline from $50\%$ in 2025 to $42\%$ in FY28F.

## Company profile

Shengyi Electronics is a global PCB manufacturer and is the subsidiary of Shengyi Technology. The company produces high-end multi-layer PCBs for sectors such as telecommunication, server, auto electronics.

## Valuation Methodology

Our TP of CNY159.00 is based on 35x 2027F EPS of CNY4.55, in line with company's historical median P/E, and the FY26-28F earnings CAGR of $37\%$ . The benchmark index is CSI 300.

Risks that may impede the achievement of the target price

Downside risks include: 1) lower-than-expected PCB demand in downstream sectors such as 5G, servers, auto electronics; 2) increasingly fierce market competition on high-end PCBs leading to margin pressure and 3) higher-than-expected raw material cost pressure.

## ESG

Shengyi Electronics shares its social responsibility by offering electronic components for telecom infrastructures. While the production of PCB products may have impacts on the environment.

## Earnings forecast revisions

We raise our FY26-28F revenue forecasts by 7.0-15.0% to reflect the technology upgrade from ASIC customers and capacity expansion. We slightly lower FY26-28F GPM by 0.1-0.8pp to reflect the cost pressure from upstream materials' price hike. As a result, our FY26-28F earnings forecast increase by 6.5-11.1%.
