## Japan Petrochemicals Industry to Accelerate Business Restructuring

Supply/demand for petrochemicals has worsened further as ethylene production in China has surged since 2020. Reconsolidation momentum in Japan's petchem industry is gaining steam. Industry is shifting business structures from a focus on basic chemicals (vulnerable to economic swings) to high-margin functional chemicals (relatively insulated from economic waves).

# Mitsubishi Chemical Group (4188): Await Growth Drivers Following Structural Reforms

The stock does not appear undervalued from a relative industry basis, so our rating is EW....

\- Commendable restructuring of domestic petrochemical business based on business environment assessment and portfolio management.

\- However, the revenue scale of semiconductor materials, which have growth expectations, is small, resulting in an earnings structure that lacks growth drivers.

\- In the petchem business, MMA-related market conditions and spreads are expected to continue to stagnate due to capacity expansion by Chinese companies and weak demand.

\- We look for the firm to create growth drivers by channeling the \~¥510bn from the pharma subsidiary sale into growth investments.
