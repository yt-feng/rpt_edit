# ExxonMobil Holdings (XOM)

On Tuesday, July $14^{\text{th}}$ , we hosted Mr. Darren Woods, Chairman and CEO of ExxonMobil, to discuss key strategic initiatives. Key takeaways included: (1) Permian growth and technological advancements, (2) exploration opportunities, (3) M&A strategy, and (4) downstream earnings outlook. We left the meeting incrementally positive on the company's Upstream portfolio where management continues to deploy key technologies to unlock value, particularly in the Permian. In addition, we are constructive on the company's refining exposure which we believe should support earnings. Looking ahead, we continue to monitor for updates on the Upstream production profile, inorganic growth opportunities, structural cost reductions and LNG operations. Across the Upstream portfolio, the company discussed operational execution, particularly in the Permian and Guyana, as well as ongoing exploration efforts. In the Permian, the company maintained its long-term production target, highlighting the key advantages from its proprietary technologies, including lightweight proppant which they continue to deploy. In Guyana, XOM highlighted strong operational execution as well as efforts to optimize reservoirs and unlock additional acreage under force majeure. In addition, management discussed additional opportunities across the portfolio, including in Trinidad, and is constructive on low-cost Mozambique and Papau projects. In regard to Venezuela, the company noted a stronger fiscal and regulatory backdrop is required for commercial investments, though noted potential for opportunities to leverage its technologies.

The company highlighted its role as a logical consolidator in the medium-term, noting its competitive advantages and proprietary technologies. Conversation was focused on technological advantages in the Lower 48, particularly the value of its light-weight proppant which continues to be deployed in the Permian. Further, the company sees re-fracking opportunities to optimize asset recovery, supported by a pipeline of stackable technologies. Management's framework around inorganic growth remains focused on driving operational and technological synergies to drive structural long-term value. More broadly, the company's capital allocation priorities are unchanged, where we note the company reaffirmed its commitment to maintaining a strong balance sheet while also investing in advantaged, high-return growth projects and steady buybacks through the cycle. We further note a focus on structural cost reductions where the company remains on track to deliver \$20 bn of cost savings by 2030.

## Valuation & Key Risks:

We are Neutral-rated on XOM, and our 12-month EV/DACF, FCF Yield, and P/E-based price target is \$157. Key risks relate to commodity prices, refining margins, and operational execution.
