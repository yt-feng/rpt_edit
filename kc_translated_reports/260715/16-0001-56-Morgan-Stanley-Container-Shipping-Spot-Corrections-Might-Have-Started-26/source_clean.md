# Spot Corrections Might Have Started

The Shanghai Containerized Freight Index (SCFI) was down 4.3% WoW for the week ended July 10, 2026. Rates on European, Mediterranean, US West, and US East routes changed -2.5%, -3.3%, -6.2%, and -2.0% WoW, respectively.

Our outlook for the SCFI on July 17, 2026 – Negative.

## • Trans-Pacific routes – negative.

\- July 1 GRI was fully erased on US West routes, while the spot correction on US East routes was milder.

• July 15 rates could continue to fall from the current level, per liners.

• US routes have likely seen a near-term peak already.

## - European routes and Mediterranean routes – negative.

• Increased capacity deployment in July versus June.

• Cargo owners are in a wait-and-see mode.

\- Most liners are cutting rates to boost loading for the second half of July.

• Maersk's return to Red Sea by its AE15 service has impacted market sentiment, per liners.
