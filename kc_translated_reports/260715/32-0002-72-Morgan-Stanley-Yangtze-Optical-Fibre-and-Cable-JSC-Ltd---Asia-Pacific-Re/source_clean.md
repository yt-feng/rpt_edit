YOFC-H has fallen \~50% since the peak in June, yet the earnings trajectory underpinned by the AI-driven optical fiber super-cycle is unchanged. We believe the de-rating has created a compelling entry point.

## Key Takeaways

YOFC's positive profit alert announced July 14 suggests 2Q26 earnings at Rmb1.9-2.5 bn, making our full-year estimate of Rmb7.5bn highly likely to be achieved.

We believe the share price correction has reflected concerns about supply risk; we think risk/reward has become more attractive.

\- Our price target remains HK\$230, and we upgrade to OW.

YOFC is a leader in next-generation innovation: In June 2026, YOFC announced the world's first successful field trial of a hollow-core fiber (HCF) WDM transmission system capable of 1.2Tb/s per wavelength over an ultra-long unrepeated span, setting a new world record for capacity-distance product. We think this positions YOFC on the frontier of next-generation optical technology.

## Supply risk has been well reflected after two large new project announcements:

In our previous report, we took a more conservative stance as we believed consensus was too positive on the stock, ignoring the supply risk. We highlighted that several overseas players are undertaking aggressive expansion. Although Chinese companies' expansion has been more conservative initially, historically high margins could attract newcomers, eventually leading to peer pressure and resulting into accelerated capacity expansion. In the past several weeks, we have observed two key new projects.

## Additional discussion

1. Hoshine Silicon's project in Xinjiang, with annual 3,200-ton optical fiber preform, officially passed filing approval on June 8, 2026.

2. Han's Laser has a project in Zhangjiagang, involving annual production capacity of 2,000 tons of optical fiber preforms.

We believe those new projects have raised investors' concerns about supply risk, which we consider a key fundamental reason for the recent de-rating.

In our report dated June 2, we highlighted the reasons why we did not upgrade the H-shares to OW. We believe historically high margin (41.5% in 1Q26, 47%-57% in 2026-28 vs. 19%-31% from 2015 to 2025) will encourage supply addition, which makes fiber prices more likely to decline in 2028. If so, the company is likely to see earnings decline, which is why we made such a forecast.

However, we think capacity expansion will take time while demand from the AI infrastructure boom is robust. If so, earnings growth momentum is unlikely to see any material changes in the next 6-12 months. We believe the robust earnings release will be the key re-rating catalyst in the coming quarters.

## Valuation range

Using historical valuation as the benchmark, we find that YOFC traded in a 5x-38x forward P/E range across the cycle from 2020 to 2025. We believe the significant share price rally since 4Q25 reflected investors' more bullish earnings expectations. With the recent pullback of \~50% from the June peak, the current forward P/E has declined from 34x to 20x, slightly above the historical average of 17x but below +1 STDEV level.

## Key catalysts

We expect the company to record continuous earnings improvement, serving as a key catalyst in 2H26. We also expect the introduction of new fiber products in AI data infrastructure, such as hollow-core fiber, to not only introduce positive revenue/earnings impact, but also trigger valuation re-rating.

## What could we be wrong?

If AI infrastructure demand is weaker-than-expected while new fiber capacity expansion is faster-than-expected, the industry cycle could change earlier than our expectation. Under such a scenario, the earnings recession cycle might start sometime in 2027, potentially capping share performance.
