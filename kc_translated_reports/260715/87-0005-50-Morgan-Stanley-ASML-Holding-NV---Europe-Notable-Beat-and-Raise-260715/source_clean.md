# ASML Holding NV | Europe
# Notable Beat and Raise
## Reaction to earnings

Company guides well ahead of expectations for Q3 and FY26 - AI driven demand in particular is driving sales/profit higher.

Guide is raised for FY26 (up c.34% yy to €44bn; MS/e €39.4bn) with Q3 guide of €11.5bn also ahead of cons (12% up on €10.3bn expected)

The company is seeing greater High NA readiness and points to Intel use at 18A - earlier than expected. Also announces CMD for June 10th 2027

ASML is to materially increase capacity for LNA EUV systems - sees c.65 in 2026, c.85 2027 (although c.90 talked to) and c.110 by 2028, much as we previewed.

■ Margins are better on stronger overall sales in Q2; guide of 55-57% in Q3 is well ahead of street/MS (c.52%); FY26 is guided to c.55% c.260bps up on cons.

What happened. ASML reported €9.3bn Q2 sales (c.6% above cons) and a gross margin of 54% (above cons and guide). Higher sales volumes and business mix (including IBM) are contributing to better margins. No mention of bookings as expected (same as last Q) and yet company talked of very strong intake continuing across H1 and customers looking to accelerate capacity expansion which is suggestive of strong sales momentum into FY27. We note the company talking to customer LTAs as signs of commitments to come much as we previewed. The company guided c.€11-12bn sales for Q3 with c.55-57% GM%; given opex guide (R&D c.€1.2bn and SG&A c.€400m) we think that implies an operating income of €4.8bn or margin of c.42% (cons c.37%).

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## ASML Holding NV (ASML.AS)

We value ASML at a P/E multiple of 35x, at the upper end of our usual cycle peak valuation range of 30-35x. This is in line with historical peaks, but we note upside potential from significant foundry capex as well as continued rapidly rising memory prices. Applying a 35x P/E multiple to our 52.19 FY28 EPSe gives us a PT €1830.

## Risks to Upside

■ Meaningful ramp-up in logic/foundry spend with nrs 2 and 3 returning to investment mode

■ Higher HBM/DRAM spend than expected

■ Servicing margins expanding

■ Tech sovereignty programmes crystallising

## Risks to Downside

■ Materially weaker end-market demand (foundry / DRAM especially)

■ Strong inflation cycle and Chinese weakness driving slowdown and more order push-outs

■ Absent High NA adoption (DRAM 2028+) inhibiting multiple
