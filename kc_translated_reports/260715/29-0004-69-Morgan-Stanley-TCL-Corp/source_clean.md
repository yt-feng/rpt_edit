## Reaction to earnings

We view its 2Q26 preliminary net profit as a beat, but maintain our preference for BOE over TCL within the A-share panel space, given BOE's scale advantage, higher exposure to displays, and faster progress in glass core substrates.

The semiconductor material business Zhonghuan Advanced achieved Rmb3bn of revenue (+8% QoQ/+10% YoY), with shipments up 17% YoY, supported by capacity expansion and optimized product mix amid improving demand.

## Our view:

\- We expect TV panel prices to start trending down from 3Q26, as below-seasonal TV panel order momentum in 2H26 might soften the bargaining power of panel makers, though supply side discipline among major panel makers should prevent significant panel pricing declines.

\- Among China panel makers, we still prefer BOE (000725.SZ; 1.8x P/B) over TCL (000100.SZ; 1.7x P/B), reflecting BOE's scale advantage, higher exposure to displays, and faster progress in glass core substrates.

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## TCL Corp. (000100.SZ)

Base case, P/B to capture the stock's cyclical nature and align with our methodology for other panel makers in Greater China. We apply 1.7x 2026e P/B vs. its five-year range of 1.1x to 3.8x P/B, which we view as justified by our ROE estimates of 8% in 2025 and 11% in 2026, respectively.

## Risks to Upside

■ Less-intense competition in the industry

■ Better improvement in AMOLED production yield

■ More capacity shutdowns by existing players

## Risks to Downside

■ Worse-than-expected industry-wide ASP

■ Lower production yield in G10.5 fab

■ More TFT-LCD capacity additions

## BOE Technology (000725.SZ)

Base case, P/B valuation, which we think better reflects BOE's intrinsic value in view of the industry's relatively cyclical nature and aligning with the methodology we use for other panel makers in Greater China. We view our target multiple of 2.5x 2026e P/B as reasonable given that we estimate ROE of $5 - 8\%$ over 2026-28 vs. BOE's average of 1.1x P/B in 2023-25 with ROE of $2 - 4\%$ during the same period.

## Risks to Upside

■ Better-than-expected industry-wide ASP

■ Higher yield in G10.5 fab

■ Faster-than-expected AMOLED ramp

■ Faster progress on advanced packaging business

## Risks to Downside

■ Worse-than-expected industry-wide ASP

■ Lower yield in G10.5 fab

■ Slower-than-expected AMOLED ramp

■ Slower progress on advanced packaging business
