# Zhejiang Sanhua Intelligent Controls | Asia Pacific
# 2Q26 Preview: Revenue Better than Feared
## Key Takeaways

We estimate Sanhua's 2Q26 revenue will grow both YoY and QoQ, despite declines in China's domestic EV and air-conditioner (AC) volumes on a YoY basis.

■ Strong EV export growth from China, double-digit YoY volume growth from a major US EV customer, and increasing module mix likely supported Sanhua's 2Q revenue.

Mgmt has reiterated its target to grow liquid cooling revenue by $>50\%$ YoY in 2026. Besides the primary (outdoor) side, it is also developing parts for the secondary (indoor) side.

\- FX losses likely remained a headwind in 2Q, as the RMB continued to appreciate against the USD.

We also expect higher copper and aluminum prices to have weighed on 2Q margins, although Sanhua has partially mitigated this impact through hedging.
