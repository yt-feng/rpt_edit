# 2Q26 to Be Better Than Feared

We see JDR as better than feared in 2Q26 despite high base and weak consumption. In 2H26, we expect JDR's revenue growth to pick up and greater margin improvement. New business investments could remain at current scale as narrowed loss in food delivery is offset by investment in Jingxi and Joybuy.

## 2Q26 preview:

\- JDR: We project revenue to be down 5% yoy: electronics and home appliances revenue declining in the low teens (%) yoy, which is better than market expectation; general merchandise revenue up in mid-single digits (%) yoy, 3P revenue up in high single digits (%) yoy. In June, yoy growth picked up sequentially against an easier comp and with recovery in online trade-in quota. We project JDR's OPM to be up 5bps yoy despite revenue decline, mainly driven by GPM improvement in the general merchandise category (same as in 1Q26)

\- Group: We project revenue to be down 3% yoy. We estimate that new business loss narrowed slightly qoq, from Rmb10.4bn in 1Q26 (food delivery loss at \~Rmb7bn) to Rmb9.8bn in 2Q26 (food delivery loss at \~Rmb6.5bn). Hence, we forecast group non-GAAP NP at Rmb8.4bn (+13% yoy)

2H26 outlook: We expect JDR's revenue growth to bottom in 2Q26 and start to recover in 2H26 thanks to easier comps; OPM should show greater improvement compared to 2Q26. We expect new business loss to be flattish in 2H26 (lower food delivery loss offset by higher Jingxi and overseas investments). Hence, we project non-GAAP NP at Rmb31.4bn.

Maintain price target at US\$27 and rating at UW: We fine-tuned our earnings estimates. Our unchanged price target implies 9x 2026e non-GAAP P/E, which we believe is fair against a 10% earnings CAGR over 2026-29.
