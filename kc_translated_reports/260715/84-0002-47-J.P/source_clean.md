The current leader, Victory Giant (2476 HK, covered by Cherry Liu) may see a temporary setback in share as it works out a slight technical issue and would likely see comparable share with a new supplier for the time being (hence, this suggests potential upside to our modest expectation for a newcomer's share in the upcoming cycle). So far there is no sign that such developments will delay the overall GPU server project timeline. Again, we don't view the issue as serious in nature. Meanwhile, we also note that Unimicron is already experiencing very tight supply for its PCB/HDI even without the improving share, due to its shifting of resources to other fast-growing areas such as optical transceiver mSAP boards.
