## Hermès: 1H26 organic sales in line; EBIT 2% above

Hermès reported 2Q26/ 1H26 results before market open today, with Group OSG of +6.7% arriving in-line with consensus expectations. This represents a slight acceleration from 1Q26, driven primarily by France, Japan and the Middle East. EBIT margins came in at 41.1%, +70bps ahead of expectations, driven by higher GM%. FX was a -1.9pts drag this quarter. Currency fluctuations represented a €360m negative impact on revenue for 1H26, and €70m for 2Q26.

2Q26 is a slight incremental improvement relative to 1Q26, but should be taken with no drama, given the de-rating suffered by Hermès in recent months. We expect more to write home about, as Hermès ups the ante on the high-end and accelerates newness (see Hermès: Stretching upwards).

By division, Leather Goods delivered +10.2% OSG (vs. cons at +10.8%); RTW +3.6% (vs. +3.4%); Silks & Textiles +12.2% (vs. +7.4%); Other Sectors +4% (vs. +4.1%); Watches +4.4% (vs. -0.4%); Perfumes -9.5% (vs. -2.9%).

By region, APAC ex. JP saw +2.5% CFx growth (vs. cons at +3.3%), France and Europe came in at +6.2% and +8.3% vs. consensus at +3.3% and +8.7% respectively, the Americas +13.7% vs. +14.1%, Japan +12.3% vs. +10.6%, and Other regions -2.4% vs. -7.4%.

The conference call will take place at 0900 CET | 0800 UKT (webinar links in English | French). We rate Hermès Outperform, PT €2,150.00.

## RISKS

## Hermes International

On the downside:

1. Failure to convincingly innovate could push Hermès in a “classic corner”, a possible risk if the aesthetics pendulum swings away from “quiet luxury”.

2. Significant reliance on precious skins could at one point come again in focus, as consumers become more interested in animal welfare –

a move away from crocodile with higher volumes in less expensive raw materials could puncture the rarity effect of most iconic products.

3. Legal challenges to Hermes commercial policies seem a long shot (see the case in California) – if successful, though, they could force Hermes to increase handbag prices. This could translate to lower growth for other product categories, benefiting today from iconic products pull.
