# Beijing Kingsoft Office Software Inc | Asia Pacific 2Q26 Growth Solid Amid AI Disruption Concern
## Key Takeaways

1H26 revenue expected to grow 21-28%, implying 2Q26 revenue growing 18-33% to Rmb1.6-1.8bn. Our 2Q estimate (+19%) lies in the lower end.

We believe strong revenue growth primarily reflects momentum in the WPS 2C product and the enterprise collaboration product (WPS 365).

Excluding investment gains, SBC, and related tax effects, 2Q26 adjusted profit grew 3-45% to Rmb420-594mn – a notably wide range.

The midpoint of the adjusted profit growth range is +24% YoY, broadly in line with revenue growth and suggesting stable margins.

We think the good top-line growth could ease some concerns around AI disruption, though we still see the stock trading at rich multiples of 63x/46x 26/27e P/E.
