# Core Strong Now; More Call Options "Soon"

META's core reach/engagement continue to expand... the moats and launch pad for more products to come. META also detailed new products that we see adding \$9+ to '28 EPS. META remains Top Pick as ests nudge higher; look for further product scaling in 2H:26, with META potentially at 14X '28 EPS.

META's core social market is strong, improving and the long term. Endterm: META's core leading social platform continues to become stronger, with 3.6bn daily active users across Family of Apps and 2bn DAUs on Instagram. Engagement trends and META's ability to serve more relevant organic and paid content are only improving. The \$64bn top end of 3Q revenue guide implies 26% y/y growth on an ex FX basis, which is an acceleration on a two-year stacked basis (51% in 3Q vs 48% in 2Q)... and was better than we expected. This reach, engagement and monetization moat is deepening too as META analyzes more of its data in more analytical methods (now with LLMs). This is META's multi-generational moat that we see driving cash flow for investment while also enabling it to scale new products over time.

... With New Products and Call Options Coming "Soon:" META's 2Q results and management commentary re-enforced the expansive call options across its core business (better engagement and monetization) and new products across consumer, enterprise, SMBs and developers we have sized in Meta Platforms Inc: 4 Products to Turn Meta Back into an "AI Winner" (2 Jun 2026). As shown below, in all, we estimate these call options could add as much as \$9 (or \~25%) to our new base case \$35 EPS. And while we didn't get specific timelines on when these products could scale or showcase themselves in a material way: 1) management referred to the timeline as "soon" 12 times on the call for when we can expect product updates/improvements and learnings; and 2) The Meta Connect developer conference is set for September 23/24. Soon could be as quickly as within the next 56 days... and we think some of the products (like subscription and messaging products on WhatsApp/Instagram) should continue to scale in 2H:26.
