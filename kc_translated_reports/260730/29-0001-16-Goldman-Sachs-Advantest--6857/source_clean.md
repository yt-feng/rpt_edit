1Q results, full-year guidance stronger than expected 1Q operating profits of ¥190 bn, reported after the July 29 close, beat our estimate of ¥152 bn and the Bloomberg consensus of a little under ¥160 bn. Advantest revised up FY3/27 operating profit guidance to ¥846 bn from initial guidance of ¥627.5 bn, well above our prior estimate of ¥751.4 bn and the Bloomberg consensus of a little under ¥710 bn. While trade statistics from various countries likely made the stock market aware of the possibility that 1Q progress could be slightly better than management's assumptions, expectations for a large beat were unlikely to have been high, as recent GPU test times have not changed substantially. As such, we believe this came as an overall positive surprise.

## Key points from the results briefing

(1) TAM outlook: Advantest raised its CY26 TAM outlook versus its April forecast for SoC testers by US\$1.9 bn, and for memory testers by US\$0.3 bn (both based on the midpoint of the range). The primary driver of the revision is chip volumes mainly for inference AI-oriented ASICs, CPUs, and DRAM tracking above expectations. The company noted that while CPU test times are on par with or slightly longer than for GPUs/ASICs in wafer testing, they are somewhat shorter in package testing.

(2) Market share: In SoC testers, Advantest's share is on an uptrend from $66\%$ in CY25. The gap versus other companies is widening, especially in the AI/HPC space where Advantest has a high share, and the company is looking to capture a market share of at least $70\%$ in SoC testers overall in the medium term. In memory testers, market share could decline slightly in CY26 (from $61\%$ in CY25) because of recovery in the NAND segment, where the company's presence is relatively weak, but management plans to leverage new product launches to expand its share going forward.

(3) Production capacity: With strong demand likely to continue (demand visibility from customers is around 18 months), Advantest expects to reach its SoC tester production capacity target of 5,000 units by end-FY3/27 sooner rather than later, and is proceeding with capacity expansion ahead of its April plan to expand to 10,000 units by end-2028 or early 2029. The company is also working to expand memory tester production capacity along similar lines to its SoC tester plans.

(4) Profit margins: 1Q gross margin reached a record high of 69.5%, partly from the product mix and inventory valuation gains/losses. While tailwinds for the product mix are likely to persist as strong demand for SoC testers is expected to continue going forward, management expects margins to decline into 2H, partly from the impact of higher procurement costs for items such as DRAM (the company said that it expects memory prices to continue to rise into FY3/28).

## Stay Neutral, but expect share price upside near term; focus on rate of change in earnings from FY3/28

We revise up our FY3/27-FY3/29 operating profit estimates by 20%/14%/14% in light of 1Q results, and we raise our 12-month target price to ¥40,000 from ¥35,000. With confirmation of earnings expansion significantly stronger than prior expectations, we believe Advantest's shares, which had corrected sharply amid a recent stock price correction in AI-related names, are likely to rebound sharply in the near term. However, while we expect volume-based demand growth for AI semiconductors and the upward trend in test content to continue, we believe it is unlikely that expectations for GPU/ASIC test times will rise further. Consequently, we currently view it as unlikely that the company's earnings growth rate from FY3/28 onward will stand out as exceptionally high within our coverage. We maintain our Neutral rating relative to coverage.

## Investment Thesis - Advantest

We are Neutral rated on Advantest as we believe the market has now fully recognized the story of expanding semiconductor tester demand, primarily for AI/HPC applications. While we expect volume-based demand growth for AI semiconductors (mainly GPUs and ASICs) and the uptrend in test content to continue, we believe it is unlikely that expectations for GPU/ASIC test times will rise further. Given that the company's earnings growth rate from FY3/28 onward is unlikely to stand out as exceptionally high within our coverage, and in view of the potential for further expansion in SG&A expenses (primarily R&D) to maintain and strengthen competitiveness going forward, we maintain our view that scope for further valuation expansion is limited.

## Price Target Risks and Methodology - Advantest

Valuation methodology: Our 12-month target price of ¥40,000 is based on the global SPE sector average EV/EBITDA of 18X (to which we add a 40% sector-relative premium) and our FY3/28E EBITDA estimates (implies FY3/28E P/E of 36X and P/B of 18X).

Key risks: Market share fluctuations, fluctuations in customer capex appetite, trends in tester demand for China, USD/JPY fluctuations.
