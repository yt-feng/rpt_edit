# Easing Concerns with Strong Results: Earnings Beat & Surprise Guidance Raise
## AlphaSignals Earnings Reaction

Bloom has now raised 2026 full year guidance two consecutive quarters in a row and offered positive updates on demand, pricing, customer/project diversification and protections, and scandium supply. This reinforces our confidence in the growth outlook and BE's ability to execute

Our takeaways on key investor debates: Continued confidence in scandium availability - we didn't hear new information but think this concern will diminish given confident messaging around supply chain not being a limiting factor at all on the growth outlook. On the risk of project delays (i.e. Project Jupiter in NM), the guidance raise should lower concerns around near-term financial impacts and the company offered multiple assurances (portfolios of other projects to deploy fuel cells, contractual obligations from customer/financing provider, diversification of customer backlog). On capacity expansions, no firm guidance as expected but continue to emphasize that manufacturing capacity will not be a constraint in delivering against the backlog and growth outlook. We think overall will be well received as incremental reassurance on these issues.

Strong 2Q beat, surprise 2026 guidance raise. Bloom notched its first-ever quarter of >\$1b in revenue in 2Q and achieved higher than expected profitability (Exhibit 1), driven by greater operating leverage from higher shipment levels. For the full year, Bloom raised its full year revenue target by \~\$450m at the midpoint (roughly \~100 MW of shipments, by our math), reaffirmed margin guidance (Exhibit 2), and profit guidance increased by \$175m (26%) as a result. This comes on the company's second beat and raise result for the year.

Addressing several investor concerns. Management reaffirmed it does not see any scandium supply issues and pointed to prior disclosures that stated the company had line of sight within its current supply chain to support 25 GW of annual output. We didn't hear new disclosures here but continued confidence in the availability of supply and highlighting that the company's supply chain overall is not a constraint on growth. The company also noted that no single project (i.e. Project Jupiter) represents risk to its raised guidance level for 2026. ## Other items from the call worth noting:

\- Backlog is growing faster than revenue, implying that Bloom's current equipment backlog would be at least \~\$8b as of the end of 2Q (we think likely well above this level).

\- Bloom fuel cells are now validated with all major hyperscalers and more than a dozen neoclouds, AI labs, and colocation data center operators.

\- Potentially positive indications on price trends: management noted that it would expect customers to be amenable to Bloom "capturing value" given the value it offers to data center projects.

\- As Bloom is expanding capacity, it is reserving some capacity to serve book-and-ship customers who need fuel cells on a short timeline, underpinning the time-to-power advantage.

Our model is slightly more 4Q weighted, in-line with management commentary and we are modeling positive OCF and FCF for the remainder of the year. We've kept our deployment forecasts largely unchanged, ramping from 1.8 GW in 2027 to 4.8 GW in 2028. We wait for incremental announcement of large data center customers and a potential commitment to expand manufacturing beyond 5 GW with a new facility as next catalysts to potentially revise our forecasts higher.

## OVERWEIGHT THESIS

We see BE as a key beneficiary of several key themes including: (i) time-to-power for large load customers, (ii) the growing "economic wedge" or value proposition of distributed energy (i.e., fuel cells for C&I customers), (iii) rising grid instability, and (iv) 30% fuel cell tax credit through the 2030s. In the near-term we see a strong growth outlook for its core fuel cell business, particularly for AI power demand, with longer-term tailwinds arising from the OBBB and its hydrogen electrolyzer business, supporting a robust multi-year growth and margin improvement outlook.
