# Read-across from Kering's 2Q26 results
## Kering's 2Q26 results: AeJ revenue decline narrows to -1% y-y

Kering Group (KER PA, Not rated) reported its 1H26 results (28 July): revenue declined by 3% y-y to EUR7.2bn, but increased by 1% y-y on a comparable basis; 1H26 operating profit was flat y-y at EUR921mn, with an operating margin of 12.8% (up by 0.4pp y-y), beating the Bloomberg consensus estimate of 12.3%. For 2Q26, Kering's revenue came in at EUR3.65bn, up 2% y-y on a comparable basis, beating the Bloomberg consensus estimate of EUR3.62bn. In particular, its key brand, Gucci, sustained the momentum of sales recovery, with the brand's revenue declining by 2% y-y on a comparable basis in 2Q26, vs the 8% y-y decline on a comparable basis in 1Q26. The company mentioned that for Gucci, the mainland China market remained challenging despite a gradual improvement in 2Q26. According to our June channel check with an expert with deep knowledge of China's high-end luxury shopping malls (our report), Gucci's sales decline narrowed in May vs April, but remained in a downtrend, which is consistent with the company's observation for Gucci in 2Q26. We believe China's luxury consumption trends exhibit significant structural growth potential; we like China Resources Land (1109 HK, Buy) and reiterate our preference for Laopu Gold (6181 HK, Buy). The following is a summary of Kering's 1H26 results:

\- 2Q26 retail revenue growth in North America, Japan, Asia Pacific (ex-Japan), and Western Europe reached +10%, +9%, -1%, and -1% y-y, respectively, compared with +9%, -3%, -4%, and -7%, respectively, in 1Q26. According to management, in Kering's AeJ business, market sentiment in mainland China remained sluggish in 2Q26, while the rest of Asia (especially South Korea) delivered encouraging performance.

\- 2Q26 revenue from direct retail (include e-commerce) was up by $2\%$ y-y, improving from the $2\%$ y-y decline in 1Q26; revenue from the wholesale channel was up by $3\%$ y-y in 2Q26, slowing down from the $+6\%$ y-y growth seen in 1Q26.

\- 2Q26 revenue for the Fashion & Leather Goods/Jewelry/Eyewear segments reached EUR2.95bn/EUR252mn/ EUR476mn in 2Q26, with comparable changes of flat/+18%/ +8% y-y. For the Fashion & Leather Goods segment, the company mentioned that its North America and Western Europe markets fared particularly well, supported by strong client demand as the new collections continued to gain traction. According to Kering, better retail execution and stronger product availability drove robust growth among both VIC and core clients, while mainland China and the Middle East businesses remained challenging.

## Some observations from Kering management on the China market in 2Q26

\- Kering's management mentioned that China remains a top priority; it is restructuring its organizational approach to integrate the overarching strategic vision of its global team with the localized insights and expertise of its regional teams. This adaptation aims to enhance relevance across key areas such as product development, communication, marketing, and retail, specifically tailored to the Chinese market.

\- Management noted that Chinese consumers' behavior is evolving; Chinese consumers have become more selective, more experience-driven, and shifting toward local preferences.
