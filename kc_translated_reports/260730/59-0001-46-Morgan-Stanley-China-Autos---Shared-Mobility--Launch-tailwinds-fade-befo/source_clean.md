# Launch tailwinds fade before next product cycle
## Key Takeaways

Post-launch digestion continued this week, with most OEMs seeing WoW orders normalize. The softer weekly print also translated into weaker MoM trends for July.

XPeng's MONA L03 and Li Auto's L6 were the only notable exceptions where recent launches drove positive MoM order growth.

The next catalysts hinge on key models including BYD's Great Han, Qin MAX and Geely's Cruiser 700.

Weekly order trends in July 20-26 based on channel feedback:

BYD (1211.HK/002594.SZ) 70.5-71k (-8% WoW, -20% MoM, -7% YoY).


Li Auto (2015.HK/LI.O) 9.2-9.4k (-49% WoW, +3% MoM, +18% YoY), with L-series accounting of 54% of orders, down WoW post L6 launch.

NIO (9866.HK/NIO.N) 11.5-12k (-3% WoW, -15% MoM, +121% YoY).

XPeng (9868.HK/XPEV.N) 9.9-10.1k (-76% WoW, +43% MoM, +13% YoY), normalizing from MONA L03 launch on 7/16.

Tesla China (covered by Andrew Percoco) 9.5-10k (-14% WoW, -5% MoM, -32% YoY).

HIMA 8.6-8.8k (-7% WoW, -43% MoM), including Aito 5.6-5.8k (-10% WoW, -41% MoM, -10% YoY).

Leapmotor 15.6-16k (-3% WoW, -19% MoM, -18% YoY).

Zeekr 4.7-4.9k (-8% WoW, -22% MoM, +96% YoY).

Xiaomi (1810.HK; covered by Andy Meng) 6-6.2k (-8% WoW, -14% MoM, -41% YoY), with the debut of two SkyNomad SUVs being next catalysts.
