# Upgrade to Buy: compelling CPO-driven valuation and earnings upcycle from 2028E
## Upgrade to Buy: Stronger long-term upside from CPO

We upgrade Largan to Buy from Neutral on strong revenue/earnings upside from 2028E, led by its potential share gain at FA/FAU for CPO switches. We raise Largan's 2026-28E earnings by 1-13%, and lift our PO to NT\$5,600 (25x 2028E P/E) from NT\$2,900 (16x 2H26-1H27E P/E). We use 2028E to capture CPO's volume take-off, and our 25x target multiple is at its historical peak P/E, matches with last round's earnings upcycle. Given CPO is still at an early stage while optical interconnect is a long-term upcycle, we note the market is willing to look beyond 2028. Thus, we use SOTP for a sanity check on CPO's value into 2030, and derive a value of NT\$5,641 per share by applying 30x P/E on CPO business (base case: 30% share at FA in Exhibit 9 and Exhibit 4).

## CPO to drive 5-10% EPS upside in 2028E, 60%+ in 2030E

We are more confident on Largan entering Nvidia's CPO supply chain. According to mgmt., Largan is engaging with more than one customer, progressing through certification (coming months), and capacity expansion (pilot line by 3Q26-end). Although Largan has no track record in datacom, we see edge is in high-precision optic alignment via automation and its partnership with CPO ecosystem (see FAU sector report). Our analysis shows 2028E EPS could see $5 - 10\%$ upside vs 2026E from FA, assuming $20 - 30\%$ share and $30 - 40\%$ net margin (Exhibit 7). Into 2030E, under a bear/base/bull case analysis, earnings could expand by $24\% / 63\% / 184\%$ vs 2028E, respectively (Exhibit 9).

## Resilient smartphone business with iPhone spec upgrade

Although the overall smartphone supply chain is under pressure amid memory price hikes, we view Largan as more resilient on iPhone's multi-year spec upgrade cycle and its leading position. We expect variable aperture on the Pro series to drive 30-50% main camera ASP upside in 2H26, while Largan should continue to benefit from rising MP or higher lens count in main/periscope cam into 2027-28.

## Company Description

Largan, established in 1987, is based in Tai Chung, Taiwan. The company is Taiwan's leading optical lens manufacturer for digital imaging products. These include digital cameras, mobile phone cameras, projectors, and MFPs.

## CPO-driven earnings upcycle from 2028E

We are more confident on Largan entering Nvidia's CPO supply chain, given more concrete project engagement. According to mgmt., Largan is engaging with more than one customer, progressing through certification (coming months), and capacity expansion (pilot line by 3Q26-end). Despite its limited track record in datacom, we see Largan's edge in its high-precision alignment via automation, which are the two key bottlenecks faced by the supply chain.

We note that earnings contribution should be limited in 2026-27E as CPO should see volume expansion only from 2028-29E. However, once it reaches a good scale, CPO could drive a compelling earnings upcycle for Largan, even stronger than the smartphone camera upgrade cycle in 2016-18. Based on our in-house estimates, Nvidia CPO switches are likely to see 14k/52k/190k units of shipment in 2026/27/28E. Our analysis shows that, if Largan only supplies FA, EPS in 2028E could see 5-10% upside vs the 2026E level, assuming 20-30% share and 30-40% net margin. If Largan is able to expand its offering to FAU with its own PMLA, EPS in 2028E could see 12-23% upside vs 2026E level, assuming 20-30% share and 40-50% net margin.

We highlight that investors tend to believe optical interconnection is a multi-year upcycle, with technology eventually migrating to NPO/CPO/OIO in the longer term. Thus, we believe the market is willing to look beyond 2028 to capture the potential contribution. Thus, we conduct a bear/base/bull case analysis for earnings upside potential into 2030E:

\- Base case: Largan still supplies FA with 30% market share, ASP is at US\$30 (for 1.6T equivalent OE), and net margin is around 40%. Under this scenario, earnings could see another 63% upside vs 2028E.

\- Bear case: Largan still supplies FA but market share is relatively low at 15%, ASP is at the same level as our base case and net margin is lower at 30% due to smaller scale. Under this scenario, earnings could see 24% upside vs 2028E.

\- Bull case: Largan expands its offering to FAU with its in-house PMLA and ASP expands to US\$60. If Largan is able to hold a strong market share at 35% and a solid net margin of 50% (large scale, good yield), earnings could expand by 184% vs 2028E.

## Price objective basis & risk

## Largan Precision (LGANF)

Our PO of NT\$5,600 is based on 25x 2028E P/E. 25x is at Largan's historical peak P/E during last earnings upcycle. We believe CPO take-off from 2028 will drive another new multi-year earnings upcycle for Largan, which justifying our valuation multiple.

Downside risks are: 1) weaker than expected end-demand, which leads to pressured topline and margin on lower utilization rate, 2) more intense competition from Greater China competitors including Sunny Optical, Genius and AAC, 3) slower than expected spec upgrade, and 4) slower than expected progress at CPO.

Upside risks are: 1) better than expected end demand and faster than expected spec upgrade, 2) higher than expected ASP and market on competitors' inferior execution, 3) eased competition from key peers, and 4) faster than expected progress at CPO.
