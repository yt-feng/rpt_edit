# JPM
## Apple
## Approval to Leverage Qwen AI in China for Apple Intelligence to Help Overcome Price Elasticity with iPhone 18

In a significant positive for Apple ahead of the launch of the iPhone 18 series of devices later this year, Bloomberg reports indicate that Alibaba's Qwen AI has been approved by local regulators to be leveraged by Apple as part of its Apple Intelligence offering across all devices including iPhones. With the approval resolving Apple's go-to-market for Apple Intelligence in a key geographic region which has contributed recently to its share gains, we expect the development to be a positive on multiple counts:

1. Further improve Apple's competitive position relative to Android OEMs in China, with the combination of AI-led features as well as pricing (with our base case expectations for iPhone price increases later this year to be more modest than those enacted by Android OEMs) helping Apple continue to gain share.

2. The launch of the AI-led features (i.e. Apple Intelligence) in China will provide consumers an incentive to upgrade from older iPhones, which is important against the backdrop of price increases likely to be enacted with the launch of iPhone 18. As highlighted in our prior report on July 7th (link), iPhone volumes have over a longer-time frame had limited correlation with pricing, and rather have been influenced by features and differentiation offered on the device, and we see the launch of Apple Intelligence in China as a key feature to help consumer willingness to upgrade despite the price increases, limiting challenges relative to price elasticity on the volume outlook.
