## Key Trends, Catalysts, Risks

## Key Trends

\- Anti-involution – Shifting competitive landscape from price competition to value-driven differentiation

\- Tech – Advancements toward AI-enabled software-defined vehicles

\- Globalization – Chinese OEMs and their complete supply chain are accelerating overseas expansion

• New model cycle kicking in from mid-July

## Key Risks

\- Implementation of L3/L4 autonomous driving regulations and licensing frameworks

## Key Catalysts

\- Subdued domestic demand and intensifying industry price competition

• Falling YoY comparison base

• Emerging upside risk to the overseas sales • Autonomous driving regulatory challenges • Geopolitical uncertainties and potential disruptions to overseas sales
