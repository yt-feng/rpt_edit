# China CSSC Holdings - A

## Positive profit alert reinforces our earnings thesis; recent pullback offers an attractive entry point

China CSSC Holdings shares have corrected 9% since 13 July, underperforming the CSI300's 1% decline, despite exceptionally strong June operating data and a positive profit alert released on 14 July. We believe the weakness reflects profit-taking and sector rotation rather than deteriorating fundamentals, presenting an attractive accumulation opportunity. The company now trades at just 13x/11x/8x 2026-28E P/E and 0.5x price-to-orderbook, despite entering the strongest phase of its earnings cycle. Management guided 2Q26 NPAT of Rmb4.4-6.6bn and 1H26 NPAT of Rmb9.2-11.0bn, implying 2Q26/1H26 NPAT growth of 82-176%/144-191% YoY on the restated merger base, or 178-322%/212-273% YoY versus the originally reported base (pre CSSC-CSIC merger). While the guidance range brackets our estimates, it reinforces our view that higher-priced contracts secured during 2023-25 are increasingly flowing through deliveries, supporting sustained margin expansion and earnings growth. Reiterate Overweight.

\- Positive profit alert reinforces our earnings thesis; recent pullback offers an attractive entry point. Relative to our forecasts, the low end of management's guidance is $11\%$ below our 2Q26 estimate and $6\%$ below our 1H26 estimate, while the high end is $35\%$ and $13\%$ above, respectively, leaving our forecasts comfortably within the guided range. We continue to expect higher-priced contract deliveries, improved vessel mix and productivity gains to more than offset the Rmb appreciation headwind already embedded in our forecasts.

\- Delivery mix supports near-term earnings. As CSSC recognizes revenue upon vessel delivery, the delivery mix is the primary driver of reported earnings. 1H26 deliveries increased 22% YoY by CGT and included 4 LNG carriers, 9 LPG carriers and 4 VLCCs, compared with just 4 LNG/LPG carriers combined in 1H25. The increasing contribution from LNG carriers, LPG carriers and VLCCs – among the industry’s highest-ASP and highest-margin vessel classes – should support both ASPs and margins as these vessels flow through revenue recognition.

\- Orderbook quality continues to improve. 1H26 new order intake doubled YoY by CGT, led by 12 post-Panamax containerships for COSCO Shipping, 15 LNG carriers, 16 LPG carriers (Jiangnan) and 23 VLCCs (Dalian/Beihai). The continued shift towards premium vessel classes reinforces backlog quality and provides multi-year earnings visibility as these contracts progressively enter the delivery schedule.

\- Blue-chip customers continue to place repeat orders. Recent commercial wins further reinforce CSSC's customer quality. ADNOC awarded four 175,000cbm LNG carriers to Jiangnan Shipyard, while China Merchants Energy Shipping (CMES) ordered five Aframax tankers from Dalian Shipbuilding, extending its long-standing relationship following earlier VLCC orders. Together with the 12 post-Panamax containerships ordered by COSCO Shipping during 1H26, these orders demonstrate CSSC's ability to secure repeat business from leading international and Chinese shipowners

# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

China CSSC Holdings - A (Overweight; Price Target: Rmb45.00)

## Investment Thesis

Maintain Overweight. Following the merger of the former “South Ship” (CSSC) and “North Ship” (CSIC) groups, China CSSC Holdings has become the world’s largest listed shipbuilder and the flagship listed platform of China’s state-owned shipbuilding industry. We forecast 26% a NPAT CAGR during 2026-28E as delivery-based revenue recognition delays – but does not reduce – the earnings contribution from higher-priced contracts secured during 2023-25, supporting gross margin expansion from 12.1% in 2025 to 19.8% by 2028E. Combined with productivity improvements, SOE integration and strong cash generation, we believe CSSC is entering the strongest phase of its earnings cycle.

## Valuation

We value CSSC using a forward P/E methodology that reflects its long-cycle earnings profile and delayed earnings recognition. At our Rmb45 price target, CSSC trades at only 14.4x 2027E P/E, 1.7x P/B, 0.8x PEG and around a 2% dividend yield, below the 16.3x average for listed global shipbuilders despite stronger earnings growth. CSSC also maintains a Rmb129bn net cash position, equivalent to 48% of market cap, while our valuation excludes upside from Hudong-Zhonghua, further SOE reform and additional productivity improvements.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Risks to our investment thesis include a slower-than-expected margin recovery, an earlier downturn in the shipbuilding cycle, weaker SOE integration benefits, expansion of sanctions or geopolitical restrictions affecting valuation, sustained Rmb appreciation and delays to further SOE reform, including the potential Hudong-Zhonghua injection.
