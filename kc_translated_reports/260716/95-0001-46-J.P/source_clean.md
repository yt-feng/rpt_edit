# China Renewables

## Fundamental opportunities into 2Q print

We conducted a 2Q preview on select China Renewable names. Deye has pre-announced 2Q with earnings up 85% YoY (mid-point). Yangtze Power and Orient Cables should post solid prints as well. On the other hand, we expect Goldwind/Arctech/Sungrow/Longyuan to post un-inspiring prints, on base effect, time lag in passing through higher costs and Middle East shipment disruptions. For the solar silicon value chain, we expect continual losses into 2Q for most as anti-involution policies have yet to reverse the industry cycle. Having said this, we see share price volatility as an opportunity to add to those companies with strong fundamentals into 2H. Our key OWs are Orient Cables, GW-H, Deye and Sungrow.

Renewable power producers: We forecast Yangtze Power to register low-mid single digit% YoY earnings growth in 2Q, in-line with generation growth. We forecast Longyuan to register a \~30% YoY earnings decline in 2Q, due to pressure on wind/solar power tariffs and a rise in curtailment rates. We expect CGN Power to register flat YoY earnings growth in 2Q, with improvement in generation offset by a softer power price.

Upstream wind: We forecast Goldwind to register -10% to +5% 2Q earnings growth on a YoY basis. 2Q25 was a high base for wind turbine shipments due to project timing. Orient Cables should see accelerating off-shore wind development. Together with a low base in 2Q25, we expect YoY growth in earnings in 2Q26.

Select ESS and solar names: Sungrow: We forecast Sungrow to register mid-high teens% 2Q earnings decline on a YoY basis. Per our industry research, 2Q utility-scale ESS revenue recognition is mostly based on 1Q shipments, where ASPs were set in 3Q25. However, on the cost side, battery prices were high in 1Q26. The long lead time from order to delivery would mean 2Q margins are probably under pressure. A rise in electronic component prices also weighed down on margins. This should be offset by shipment growth. Deye: Deye pre-announced that its 2Q earnings should be in the range of Rmb1.48bn to Rmb1.54bn, +85% YoY, +27% QoQ (mid-point). We estimate the strong earnings growth in 2Q should be attributed to (1) 30% qoq growth in solar inverter shipments, (2) 50% qoq growth in ESS inverter shipments, (3) 10% qoq growth in micro inverter shipments, (4) 60% qoq growth in ESS battery pack shipments. Arctech: We see risk for Arctech to register losses in 2Q on shipment disruption. Arctech should have shipped key structures for the tracker to the Middle East project from its production base there. However, key motor drive components may endure shipment delays, resulting in delays in revenue being recognized. Momentum should improve in 3Q.

Looking beyond 2Q print: We see 1) China's push on off-shore wind; 2) market share gains for Chinese wind turbines in EM; 3) rising DG ESS installaton in EM as a structural thesis, benefitting Orient Cables, GW-H and Deye. Post recent correction on geopolitical risk concerns, Sungrow's share price has not priced in the AIDC ESS/SST optionality, we see a higher risk/reward. For renewable power operators, we have OW ratings on Yangtze Power and CGN Power. LONGi and Tongwei remain our key UWs in an unfavorable industry cycle.
