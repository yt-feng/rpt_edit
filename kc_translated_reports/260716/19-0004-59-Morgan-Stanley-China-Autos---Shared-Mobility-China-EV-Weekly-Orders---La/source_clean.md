# China Autos & Shared Mobility | Asia Pacific
# China EV Weekly Orders - Launch Lull Stalled Momentum
## Key Takeaways

The summer off-season is now firmly in effect with almost every marque printing negative WoW. The WoW drop was also attributable to a pre-launch air pocket.

BYD (Seal 08), Zeekr (9X 5-seat, launched 7(8) and NIO (ES8 5-seat, launched 7/9) are decelerating from recent launch pulses rather than losing momentum.

We saw pockets with resilience held including Denza, XPeng's MONA and Leapmotor's A-series.

Genuine soft spots are the names without an imminent catalyst, e.g. HIMA, which fell more meaningfully.

Upcoming launches in 2H of July (e.g. '26 Li Auto L6, XPeng MONA L03, Geely's Battleship 700/e5 facelift) should set up an August product-cycle reboot.
