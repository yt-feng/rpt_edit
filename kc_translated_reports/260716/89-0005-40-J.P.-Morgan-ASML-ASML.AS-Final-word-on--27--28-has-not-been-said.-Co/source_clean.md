Final word on ‘27, ‘28 has not been said. Co. can raise estimates again. Reiterate OW rating.

Investors are trading ASML as if it is 2025 or before, when ASML gave order guidance. The guidance from the co. over the last 2 quarters has indicated that the co. is not giving the sort of guidance it gave in the past, when full year guidance was always met and not beaten but the momentum was provided by the orders. Now, the company is raising guidance every quarter to provide momentum. Thus, there is every reason to believe that the FY27 guidance will be raised in the current market environment. In fact, the co. itself indicated in the conference call that the EUV capacity guidance in '27 was not a ceiling. Based on the demand trends and the capacity guidance from the co., the stock is now trading as if the industry is going into a downturn when there are no such indications in the market. ASML remains our top pick in our space, we remain OW and the stock is on the AFL.

\- Market needs to recognize that the way ASML guides has changed. There is every likelihood that the company will raise FY27 capacity indications again: ASML's FY26 guidance and indications of EUV and Immersion DUV capacity in FY27, FY28 were strong. The only datapoint that was not as strong as the market would have liked was EUV capacity in FY27 where the co. has indicated 85 tools. Since ASML stopped reporting orders, they have raised guidance every quarter. In the absence of orders, the philosophy has shifted to raising guidance every quarter. On the conference call, they in fact said 'at this stage, given the conversations we have, the 85 is a nice representation of the balance between what customers are asking of us and what, at this stage, we've been asking ourselves in the supply chain to do. But if more is needed, you know, just as we've done it in the past couple of quarters, we're going to roll up our sleeves and see if more can be done.' - thus the 85 EUV tools is not a ceiling. Thus, we do not see the guidance as negative and expect very large consensus upgrades on this print. JPM estimates for '27/'28 were 26.5%/23.8% ahead of consensus ahead of this print and we would expect that consensus should move to where we were or potentially ahead (JPMe goes ahead of our pre-report estimates as indicated below).

\- ASML is trading as if the end-market is in a downturn: ASML's interquartile P/E range is 23x to 29.8x on Y+2 EPS. The stock is currently trading at 21.9x our '28 EPS, so below the lower bound of the inter-quartile range, when there is no down-cycle in sight. We expect that even if the consensus does not move to JPMe '28 EPS of €70.7, it will move substantially higher than the current Y+2 consensus of €49.9. So the stock is going to look really cheap when the upgrades come through over the next week. Market is understandably skittish about semis given the stellar sector performance in Q2; however, in our opinion, ASML will out-perform semis in Q3 given the earnings upgrades that are likely post this print.

\- Raising our estimates and PT to €2,100. We are raising our estimates driven by higher company guidance, indications of strong capacity expansion plans and an inflection in gross margin. Our FY26 and FY28 revenue estimates are 13% and 6% higher than before while we have lowered our FY27 revenue estimates by 1% in line with ASML's capacity indications for EUV and immersion. This drives our EPS estimates 26%/1%/10% higher for FY26/FY27/FY28 and thus our Jun-28 PT based on 29.6x our FY28 EPS estimates is now €2,100 (previously €1,900).

## Our Takeaways

## Bullish tone and raise in the guidance is a function of a strong setup

ASML has indicated that aggressive capacity adds in leading edge nodes (including 5nm and below as well as 1b and 1c in memory) has taken capex higher in both logic and DRAM. Long term agreements (LTAs) at customers provide more visibility, which increases customer confidence to spend capex and add capacity. Besides the capacity the needed, ASML also commented on higher litho intensity with increasing number of immersion and EUV layers in memory. Customers are believed to continue to add more capacity in FY27 and FY28.

## Indicated capacity is not a hard ceiling and the focus should be on revenue capacity rather than number of boxes

ASML has indicated capacity for 65 EUV tools this year, 85 in 2027 and 110 in 2028. Interestingly, on the call they were not willing to say that 85 was peak 2027 capacity. ASML has indicated that 85 tools for 2027 is a good representation of customer needs and capabilities at this time of this report, but this could evolve. Messaging has changed with the company willing to comment on capacity increases, signaling strong demand.

Importantly, while the number of tools capacity is indicated to increase 30% YoY in 2027 and 2028, the revenue growth potential from this is meaningfully higher as higher productivity and ASP per tool need to be factored in. ASML has indicated that the impact from higher productivity tools in the mix would be equivalent to another 10% increase in capacity and thus if revenue capture is to be proportional to capacity provided to the customer, it is thus implied is that EUV revenue growth should be theoretically equivalent to 45% YoY.

## More favorable pricing environment

The co. gave a new msg - they see potential to use the current short-supplied market to make pricing changes that they would not be able to do in a normal market. That change will take time, but the co. plans to continue to implement value-based pricing, which will enable it to raise prices on next-generation tools as customers are capturing significantly more value in the current environment. On the F tool in particular, we see much more potential for the company to change the pricing dynamic.

## Gross margin indications were also positive

On the much stronger than expected gross margin guidance for the year the company said this was driven by 1) more EUV and immersion 2) better priced EUV tools (D tools fading out and F tools coming in later) 3) IBM revenue higher and with a better mix – both upgrades and higher EUV services revenue 4) higher volume (fixed cost coverage). ASML indicated that these factors will remain in FY27 and beyond, though they did not specifically guide FY27 margins. Thus 2H26 sees an inflection point in ASML gross margins

## Adjusting estimates

We are adjusting our estimates following the 2Q26 print and the company's indications on FY26 and beyond. For 3Q26, ASML has guided for revenue of €11.5bn at the midpoint, gross profit margin of 56% at the midpoint and Opex at €1.6bn. For FY26,

ASML has raised its revenue guidance to €44bn at the midpoint (€43 to €45bn). In terms of capacity, ASML has guided for EUV and immersion capacity to ramp 30% in FY27 and FY28 which implies EUV capacity for \~85/110 tools and immersion capacity for \~169/210 tools in FY27/FY28. We highlight that EUV tools shipped will have higher throughput in aggregate and thus revenue increase for EUV and immersion will be higher than 30% YoY, supported by higher ASPs. ASML has guided for DUV + metrology & inspection to grow 25% in FY26 and IBM strength should continue in Q3 (\~€2.9bn guided in revenue) and Q4. ASML has indicated EUV is close to fully booked for FY27 and the co. has strong orders for FY28 which is why it is planning to outlay the indicated higher capacity in that year. Higher ASPs, strength in IBM business and higher volume should support continued margin expansion. Specifically, the changes we have made are:

\- On Revenue, we are raising our FY26 estimates by 13% to reflect the company's guidance of €44bn revenue at the midpoint. For FY27, our revenue estimate is 1% below our previous estimate given the company's indication of \~85 EUV capacity next year (which we take to not be a ceiling) and \~170 immersion tools which is below our previous number but partially offset by higher ASP estimates. For FY28, our revenue estimates is 6% higher than previously given the strength of customer capacity expansion plans;

\- On Gross Profit, our FY26/FY27/FY28 estimates are 20%/3%/10% higher than previously driven by higher revenue estimates (except for FY27) and better profitability from i) more EUV and immersion tools ii) better priced EUV tools with mix including more F units over time iii) higher volume (fixed cost coverage);

\- On EBIT, our FY26/FY27/FY28 estimates are 27%/1%/11% higher than previously driven by higher gross profit estimates and disciplined opex driven by higher SG&A and R&D team efficiency;

\- Overall, our EPS estimates for FY26/FY27/FY28 are 26%/1%/10% higher than previously.

# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

ASML (Overweight; Price Target: €2,100.00)

## Investment Thesis

ASML is the sole supplier of EUV tools and its market share in the lithography segment should exceed 80-89% (seen in the last decade) driven by higher EUV ASP. The shift to High-NA that should start in '27 should help litho intensity improve as should greater EUV adoption in DRAM. The industry environment has changed significantly in recent quarters with a significant increase in DRAM prices. ASML, with its exposure to all the major memory companies, should be a significant beneficiary of a memory up-cycle.

## Valuation

Our ASML target price is €2,100 (\$2,400) which is equivalent to 29.6x our '28 EPS. ASML has historically traded at 28-38x forward earnings in the last five years (interquartile range) and at an average multiple of 33x. Our multiple is thus at the lower end of ASML's trading range indicating that the target price is earnings and not multiple driven, and to reflect that we are factoring in that this is an up-cycle price target.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

We believe the key risks that could prevent the stock from achieving our price target are: 1) export restrictions on tools to key geographies, such as China; and 2) an economic downturn, which would jeopardise our earnings estimates and PT. Further, lower-than-expected EUV adoption could imply downside to our estimates. Continuing increase in interest rates would also be negative to the multiple used and, thus, negative to our target price.
