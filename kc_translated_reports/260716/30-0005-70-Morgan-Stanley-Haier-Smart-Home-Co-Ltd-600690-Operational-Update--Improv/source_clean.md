# Haier Smart Home Co Ltd | Asia Pacific
# Operational Update: Improvement in 2Q26; China's Trend in 3Q26 Key to Watch
## Key Takeaways

Recovery in 2Q26: expect largely flat sales yoy in China and low-single-digit sales growth overseas with narrowed decline on bottom line vs. 1Q26.

In China, AC showed positive growth; fridges and washing machines narrowed their declines in 2Q26.

US sales decline narrowed in 2Q26; exposure to Chinese production is planned to be reduced to a single-digit share of US sales by 2027 from \~10% currently.

Solid growth momentum continues in EU, R&D speeding up for launching more localized products in the EU to capture growth opportunities.

■ Management maintains the target of mid-single-digit growth for both sales and net profit in 2026. Payout ratio to be 58% in 2026 and 60% in 2027-28.

## China:

\- Residential AC: double-digit growth and commercial AC mid-single-digit growth in 2Q26, led by new SKU launches with attractive prices and channel restocking for summer peak season and before price increases.

\- Fridges and washing machines: decline narrowed to mid-single digits in 2Q26, with new SKU launches and benefit from subsidy rollout.

\- Competition remains intense but the focus is shifting from pricing towards cost management, according to the company.

## EU:

\- High-single-digit sales growth in 2Q26, with double-digit growth from HVAC YTD (20% of EU white goods sales).

\- OPM target of $2\%$ for 2026 vs. break-even level in 2025.

## US:

\- Top-line decline in 2Q26 narrowed to mid-single digits.

\- \~US\$10-20mn tariff reduction per quarter after production shift to outside of China with new factory in Thailand and US local production.

\- Committed US\$3bn investment in the next five years in the US for renovation of local facilities and R&D capabilities.

• Targets flat OPM yoy in 2026 and 20-30bps improvement p.a. afterwards.

Emerging Markets: Overall top-line growth remained solid: double digits in 2Q26.
