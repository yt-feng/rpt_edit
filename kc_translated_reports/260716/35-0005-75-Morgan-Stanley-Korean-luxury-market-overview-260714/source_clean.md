\- South Korea is unique because the department store - not the luxury street - has become the centre of the luxury ecosystem.

\- Department stores are in effect malls as stores are operated under a concession model.

\- Department stores flagship locations combine dozens of directly operated boutiques with concierge services, VIP lounges, private dining, cultural programming, valet parking and sophisticated clienteling systems.

\- Luxury brands operate very few stand-alone high-street stores (generally only one or two flagships for the entire country).

\- The share of duty-free (downtown and airport stores) has dramatically shrunk over the past decade.
