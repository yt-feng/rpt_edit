# Preliminary 2Q26 earnings better than expected

Earnings remain supported in 3Q26: Coal prices corrected recently in response to the lower daily burn rate at power plants owing to the rainy weather in multiple regions and higher inventory level at ports. As the plum rain season comes to an end, the peak in daily consumption and restocking demand from power plants, together with the still constrained supply in major coal producing provinces, would continue to provide support to thermal coal prices, leading to solid earnings for Shenhua.
