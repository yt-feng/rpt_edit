# Preliminary 2Q26 slightly below expectations due to revenue recognition; strong 3Q26 outlook
## Key Takeaways

It is anticipated that 3Q26 net profit growth will accelerate rapidly, driven by strong optical fiber demand, higher pricing, and improving product mix.

ZTT completed the delivery of a lower-priced order for China Mobile in 1H26. The new prices in 2H26 will increase substantially.

The spot price of G657 fiber increased slightly MoM in June but remained flat MoM for G652.D fiber.

\- Optical fiber remains a seller's market. ZTT has been selective in customer acceptance and continues to require prepayment for orders.

ZTT expects 1H26 net profit to grow 50-60% yoy, to Rmb2.35bn-2.51bn, implying 2Q26 net profit of Rmb1.43bn-1.59bn, up 52-69% yoy or 56-73% QoQ. The 2Q26 net profit is close to the low end of our estimate range of Rmb1.5-2.0bn. We think the main reason is the order delivery and revenue recognition schedule. According to ZTT, key business segments performed as follows in 2Q26:

\- Optical fiber: Some exported specialty optical fiber products have not yet been recognized as revenue and therefore remain recorded as inventory.

\- Power transmission: >15% yoy revenue and earnings growth in line.

\- Marine systems: Moderate growth, given delivery of Sanshan Island 500kV AC and DC submarine cable with higher margin but lower revenue compared to Jiangsu projects being recognized in 2Q25. The two EU orders will be booked in 2H26 with a total order size >Rmb2bn.

Given the strong demand environment and full production utilization, the company has become more selective on customer allocation. The current sales strategy emphasizes customer selection and advance payment (prepayment) before delivery, helping improve cash flow and optimize order quality.
