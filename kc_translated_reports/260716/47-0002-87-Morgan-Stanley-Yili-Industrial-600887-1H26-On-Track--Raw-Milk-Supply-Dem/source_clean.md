# 1H26 On Track; Raw Milk Supply-Demand Improving
## Key Takeaways

We hosted an investor group call with the company. 1H26 performance was in line with guidance, and management maintained its 2026 MSD revenue growth target.

Liquid milk was flat yoy in 2Q26 due to seasonality, which was in line with management's plan. Channel inventory remains healthy.

■ Management expects raw milk price to remain broadly stable YoY in 2026, with a modest recovery in 2H26. Promotional pressure should also ease as costs rise.

■ Potential tax changes remain under review and may affect certain previously tax-preferential categories. Management expects no material impact on core profit.

■ Capital allocation: The company reiterated a dividend payout of no less than 75% during 2025-27.

## Updates by segment:

\- UHT milk: Positive growth yoy in 1H26, with Satine outperforming basic UHT milk. Growth was volume-led with stable pricing. Milk beverages stayed solid and yogurt decline narrowed.

\- Fresh milk: Strong growth continued in 1H26, though still loss-making. Yili aims to lead long term through its brand portfolio, cold-chain and broader channels. Price competition should ease as raw milk prices recover.

\- Milk powder and others: IMF is on track for MSD-HSD sales growth and 1-2ppt market share gains. Adult nutrition grew double digits %, supported by gifting and functional products. Ice cream and cheese maintained solid growth in 1H26.

\- Raw milk: Supply and demand are nearing balance. Better upstream profitability and lower promotions should benefit leading players with stronger supply-chain and cost control.

\- Overseas: DD growth and margin improvement continued in 1H26, including its dairy ingredients business in New Zealand, ice-cream and milk beverages in Southeast Asia market, and Ausnutria overseas business.
