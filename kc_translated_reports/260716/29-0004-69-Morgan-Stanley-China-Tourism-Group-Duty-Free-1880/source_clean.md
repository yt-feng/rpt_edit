## Key Takeaways

The $2\%$ revenue decline and $20\%$ operating profit decline in 2Q26 should not be a surprise as Hainan duty-free sales growth has decelerated visibly since April

Still, the $8.5\%$ reported OPM in 2Q26, -1.9ppt yoy, is not impressive; especially as GPM for the main business actually expanded 1.44ppt yoy.

We believe FX losses can explain part of the OPM compression, but we await interim results in late Aug for more detail on the operating deleverage.

The YoY comp for the Hainan DF market is low for 3Q, yet recent trends from related travel industries (e.g. hotels and transportation) are mixed.

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## China Tourism Group Duty Free (1880.HK)

We ascribe a 15% discount to our A-share valuation. This implies a 2026e P/E of 27x vs. 32x for the A-share (1SD above average since 2017). This H-share discount is similar to its average since the H-share listing in mid-2022.

## Risks to Upside

■ Favorable policy outcome for Hainan FTZ and downtown duty-free

■ Improving consumer spending (especially in beauty products) and consumption upgrade (more non-beauty luxury products)

## Risks to Downside

■ Overall economic slowdown and pressure on disposable income

■ Price competition among various retail sales channels

■ Competitive intensify if the government further opens the duty-free market (both Hainan and mainland)

## China Tourism Group Duty Free (601888.SS)

We apply a target 2026e P/E of 32x, 1SD above the average since 2017.
