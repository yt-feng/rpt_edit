## National Essential Medicines List (2026 edition) released

Updated after eight years, the latest EML includes more innovative drugs, which augurs well for volume growth

What is new? More than 100 medicines have been added to the EML

On 9 July (noon HKT), the National Health Commission of the People's Republic of China (NHC), National Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine (NATCM) and National Administration of Disease Control and Prevention (NADCP) jointly released the national Essential Medicines List (2026 edition, 2026 EML).


China Health Care & Pharmaceuticals


The EML was last updated eight years ago in 2018, and the 2026 edition of the EML will come into effect from 1 September 2026.

The total number of medicines in the 2026 EML has expanded by 109 to 794, which comprises 476 chemical drugs and biologics (68 more) and 318 Chinese medicines (48 more). Therapeutic areas such as diabetes, hypertension, COPD, paediatric illness are given priority, resulting in addition of several medicines.

Our take: The newly updated list will expand usage of included medicines Encouragingly, we note that several novel drugs (such as GLP-1 RA, monoclonal antibodies for oncology and autoimmune diseases and targeted therapy drugs) have been added, providing more choices for low-tier medical institutions.

In our view, EML inclusion should raise the usage volume of the drugs highlighted below:

Hengrui (600076 CH/1276 HK, Buy): loversol
