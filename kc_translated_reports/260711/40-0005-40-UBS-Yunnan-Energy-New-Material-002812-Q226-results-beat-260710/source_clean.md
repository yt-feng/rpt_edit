# Yunnan Energy New Material Q226 results beat
## Q: How did the results compare vs expectations?

A: The company released its H126 preliminary results, reporting recurring net profit attributable to shareholders of Rmb760\~930m. The midpoint implies Q226 net profit of Rmb610m (+115% QoQ), ahead of our and market expectations.

## Q: What were the most noteworthy areas in the results?

A: In Q226, the company shipped 4.1bn sqm of separator products (+58% YoY, +17% QoQ), better than the previous guidance of 3.8\~4.0bn sqm. We estimate reported unit net profit at Rmb0.15/sqm in Q2, a marginal improvement from Rmb0.13/sqm in Q126.

## Q: Has the company's outlook/guidance changed?

A: The company provided no new guidance. According to Zhuiguang Lithium Battery, price negotiations between mainstream separator manufacturers and leading downstream battery companies are nearing completion, with the market expecting a price hike of 7-8% (+Rmb0.05\~0.06).

## Q: How would we expect investors to react?

A: We expect investors to react positively.

## Company Description

Yunnan Energy New Materials is a China-based supplier primarily of lithium-ion battery separators, printing products and packaging products. The company mainly produces wet-processed film, including base and coated film, which contributed 81% of its total revenue and 54% of its total gross profit in 2024. Chairman Paul Xiaoming Lee and his family are the actual controllers of the company.

## Valuation Method and Risk Statement

We value Yunnan Energy on a P/BV methodology. We believe the risks inherent to the electric vehicle (EV) battery materials sector include, but are not limited to, the volatile nature of commodity prices and currencies, which may differ materially from expectations. The sector are also exposed to government EV regulations and global policies on climate change, which could significantly impact company/industry performance
