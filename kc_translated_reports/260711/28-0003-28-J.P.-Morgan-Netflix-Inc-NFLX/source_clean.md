## Netflix Inc

NFLX Trending & 2Q Preview, 7/10/26: Focus on Engagement, Content Quality, & Revenue Growth; Remain Overweight

Investor sentiment remains cautious heading into Netflix's 2Q earnings on Thursday, 7/16, as NFLX shares have traded down -30% since 1Q earnings vs. SPX +6%. Primary concerns include engagement growth, subscriber trends following recent price increases, content quality & investment, & the potential for further pursuit of M&A. Engagement time increased \~2% in 1Q26, similar to the growth in 2H25, & slightly ahead of the 1% growth in 1H25. NFLX will likely release its semi-annual engagement report concurrent with 2Q earnings. However, we do not believe engagement metrics provide a complete view of business trends and member satisfaction as NFLX's primary internal quality engagement metric hit an all-time high in 1Q, and we continue to view engagement growth as less correlated with revenue growth on a platform of 325M+ subs, & with additional lift from pricing & advertising. According to a WSJ article out after the close Thursday, NFLX may be considering adding live channels to its platform, similar to what it did with TF 1 in France, and bundling other streaming services, much like Amazon Prime Video does today. We'd consider these more extreme methods of increasing engagement, which likely won't be taken well by the Street. Still, we understand the concerns as Figure 2 suggests global "Cancel Netflix" search queries spiked in March'26 following US price increases, though they have since normalized. NFLX indicated at 1Q earnings that churn was in-line with expectations. Shifting to earnings, we believe investors expect NFLX to maintain 2026 Revenue guidance of \$50.7B-\$51.7B & operating margin guidance of 31.5%, while continuing healthy share repurchases. NFLX's 2026 revenue growth guide of +12%-14% (+11%-13% FXN) is supported by membership growth, pricing, & advertising. NFLX remains bullish on organic growth upside, w/NFLX still <45% penetrated of the \~800M+ global CTV HHs ex-China & Russia, and the company is on a path toward 1B viewers over time as it becomes global TV. We estimate US price increases will drive \~\$1.7B+ of incremental annualized revenue off the 2025 base & contribute \~250bps of growth in 2026, w/further upside from int'l price increases. Ad revenue will roughly double again in 2026 to \~\$3B. We project 2026 Revenue growth of +13% FXN Y/Y, including Subscribers +8% & FXN ARM +4%, though we recognize that FX rates have become less favorable, w/incremental headwind to reported revenue of 100bps+ in 3Q & 50bps+ for full year 2026. An offset could come from the 49ers vs. Rams NFL game on 9/10 from Melbourne, Australia, which should help both subs and ad revenue.

NFLX's 2026 operating margin guide of 31.5% reflects +150bps Y/Y expansion when adjusting for Brazil tax catch-up in 2025 & \~\$275M of deal-related expenses in 2026. The shape of 2026 operating income growth is a bit lumpy w/content timing leading to higher amortization in 1H & overall Y/Y GAAP expense growth of +16%. But 2H26 expense growth should decelerate materially—we project to +7% Y/Y—and price increases should help drive operating income growth above +30% Y/Y. NFLX will spend \~\$20B on cash content & expects a \~1.1x cash to amort ratio in 2026. NFLX will not realize the full benefit of reduced M&A costs

## Market Share Analysis & Industry

\- Nielsen's “The Gauge” estimates that streaming’s share of overall US Total Usage of Television (TUT) increased +380bps Y/Y in April to 47.6%, ahead of Cable (21.6%) and Broadcast (19.9%). NFLX’s share increased +30bps Y/Y to 7.8% of TUT, below YouTube (13.4%), but above Disney+ (5.0%) and Prime Video (4.2%), among other services.

\- Take: Streaming is approaching \~50% of US TV time & continues to capture share from Cable & Broadcast. We remain bullish on the secular shift toward OTT video streaming & believe that NFLX has strong leadership within a rationalizing streaming industry. We're positive on Netflix's potential to become global TV as it deepens penetration of the \~800M+ global CTV HHs.

# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

Netflix Inc (Overweight; Price Target: \$118.00)


## Valuation

Our December 2026 PT of \$118 is based on \~30x 2027E GAAP EPS of \$3.92. This is a premium to mega-cap tech peers trading at \~22x 2027E GAAP EPS on average, which we think is justified by NFLX's similar top-line growth & faster bottom-line growth.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Risks to downside include: 1) Advertising efforts do not drive subscriber & revenue upside; 2) Core subscriber growth slows, limiting NFLX's ability to unlock its TAM; 3) NFLX does not efficiently balance top line with growth investments, limiting operating margin expansion & FCF; 4) Video streaming is a competitive industry with low switching costs—key competitors include Disney+ (including Hulu/ESPN+ bundle), Prime Video, Max, Apple TV+, etc.; & 5) Less traditional valuation support vs. large cap tech peers with lack of visibility into near-term subscriber trends.
