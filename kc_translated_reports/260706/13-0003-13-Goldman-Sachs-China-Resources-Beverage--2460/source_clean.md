# China Resources Beverage (2460.HK): APAC Consumer & Leisure Corporate Day 2026 Takeaways: Active channel destocking in

Bottom line: We hosted CR Beverage's mgmt team at our APAC Consumer & Leisure Corporate Day event on Jul 3. Mgmt characterized 1H26 as a transition period for both water/beverage, prioritizing channel destocking, price discipline and launching new growth drivers (i.e. new barrel water, small pack 210ml, family channel establishment and low tier penetration for key water business). Water sales is expected to decline SD% in 1H26 due to active channel destocking, but mgmt emphasized that sell-out remained more resilient, with channel inventory down c.30% and online/offline price gaps largely normalized. Beverage sales is expected to decline more due to channel destocking/adjustment. For the full year of 2026, the company has largely completed the organizational reform, and maintained the target of LSD% sales growth for water with more disciplined investment, while the beverage business remains under transition with focus on destocking/promotion. Looking into 2H26, the focus will shift to stronger execution, including new channels development, operating efficiency and disciplined marketing investment, while NP will be lapping a much easier comp base vs. 1H26. On pricing, Mgmt also reiterated no plan for pricing adjustments for bottled water (the previously reported pricing adjustment to 2.08L for just for a small region to eliminate regional difference), and beverage remains in an investment year. On industry, the company also noted industry volume weakness for the bottled water industry in 5M26, likely due to cannibalization from low-sugar/no sugar beverages in the industry. On costs, 1H26 GPM is expected to remain broadly flat with PET lock-in benefit in 5M26 (Jun seeing some negative impact with higher PET price but moderated in July) and the company is still watching the PET price trend into 2H26. Shareholder return: Mgmt reiterated confidence in medium-term earnings growth and committed Rmb0.37 dividend per share for 2026-28 (implying 5.5% yield) with announced HK\$530mn share buyback (c.2.8% of current market cap, with repurchased shares to be canceled), reinforcing its commitment to shareholder returns. The company is trading at 15.4x/14.6x 2026E/2027E P/Es.

## Key Takeaways

## By segment update

Water sales is expected to decline by SD% yoy in 1H26, with channel destocking (channel inventory down 30% yoy in some regions) weighed on shipments. Mgmt noted that underlying retail demand remained more resilient vs. shipment. The company is also expanding its Water Plus portfolio to regain consumption occasions. Family water has become a strategic growth initiative, supported by dedicated teams and expansion into seven pilot cities. Therefore, mgmt is still targeting bottled water sales volume to achieve LSD% growth for FY26.

\- Beverages is likely to remain in an adjustment period to better prepare for future growth. Channel inventory also went down by 30%+ for some regions in 1H26. Following broad new product launches last year, the company will mainly focus on Zhiben Qinrun this year, concentrating resources behind fewer core brands. The Rmb1-for-one-more-bottle consumer promotion has improved sell-through in 1H26 TD but also added related promotion exp.

## Channel update

\- Channel optimization remained the key focus in 1H26. The company tightened online price mgmt, significantly narrowing online/offline price gaps while proactively reducing exposure to channels that disrupted pricing.

Mgmt highlighted three incremental growth drivers for 2H26, incl 1) Lower-tier cities, particularly in northern China and mature markets, remain key white-space opportunities. 2) Family water channel is entering the roll-out phase following initial investments. 3) Emerging channels like Value retailers are also considered as a new growth engine. The company recently signed an HQ-to-HQ agreement with Wanchen and is in discussions with other value retailer/KA channels. Dedicated 520ml water SKU (RSP at Rmb1.2 per bottle) will be supplied to value retailer chains to protect the existing price architecture on wholesale channels (mainly 555ml SKU). Mgmt also noted that online sales is also growing faster than offline but is margin dilutive.

## Margin/Cost outlook

PET became a headwind in 2Q26 (esp in May-Jun) with higher price. Mgmt expects 1H26 GPM to remain broadly flattish, supported by favorable locked-in costs in Jan-Apr, while FY26 margins will face pressure if PET remains around Rmb7k/ton on avg. The company continues to offset part of the inflation through procurement savings on other packaging materials and reiterated that FY26 selling expenses are broadly stable, with spending reallocated toward higher-return initiatives.

## Production capacity

Mgmt expects the UTR to improve in 2026 (vs. c.50% in 2025) driven by targeted LSD% bottled water volume recovery and ramp-up of new initiatives. The company also adjusted the unit size from 24 bottles water per case to 28 bottles per case (shifting ASP discount to higher unit numbers) to support utilization rate.

## Price Target Risks and Methodology - China Resources Beverage

We are Neutral rated on China Resources Beverage with a 12-month TP of HK\$8.2 based on a 16.5x 2027E P/E referring to the same target P/E as F&B players Tingyi/UPC, and discounted back to mid-2027E using 11.8% COE.

Key downside/upside risks: 1) More/less intensified competition than expected in the bottled water market; 2) Slower-/faster-than-expected development of the beverage business; 3) Less-/more-than-expected raw material price benefits; 4) Uncertainty over channel/distribution management; 5) Reputational risks/food security issues.

## GS Factor Profile

The GS Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows:

Growth is based on a stock's forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock's forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock's forward-looking P/E, P/B, price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile).

Financial Returns and Multiple use the GS analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics).

## M&A Rank

Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
