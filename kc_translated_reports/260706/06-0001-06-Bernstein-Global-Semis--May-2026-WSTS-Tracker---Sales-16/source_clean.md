# Global Semiconductors and Semiconductor Capital Equipment Global Semis: May 2026 WSTS Tracker - Sales 16.1% MoM, above typical (8.5% MoM) & +118.8% YoY on continued memory strength

On Friday the SIA published its monthly WSTS report, which tracks sales, units and ASPs across the semiconductor industry, and provides details by products, end markets, and geographies. The recent report includes data up to and including May 2026.

Total semiconductor sales grew 118.8% YoY in May after rising 106.4% in April. YoY strength was again dominated by memory with sales more than quadrupling at 325.7% YoY, driving \~\$200B in incremental revenue YTD and with memory pricing alone driving \~2/3 of overall industry growth since the beginning of the year. Excluding memory, sales were still up strongly at 34.0% YoY.

Total semiconductor sales MoM were above typical seasonality, up 16.1% versus the historical May average of +8.5% MoM. On a MoM basis as-reported, product group performance was generally worse to in line with seasonal patterns.

DSP (3.3% vs. typical -2.5%) and NAND (40.7% vs. typical 26.0%) were better than typical MoM patterns. Discretes (-4.9% vs. typical 2.7%), Optoelectronics (-10.8% vs. typical -6.3%), Sensors & Actuators (-0.8% vs. typical 2.4%), Analog Std Linear (-5.4% vs. typical -3.5%), Analog App Specific (-9.3% vs. typical -0.6%), and DRAM (27.7% vs. typical 41.9%) were worse than typical MoM patterns. Logic (3.9% vs. typical 4.1%), MPU (5.0% vs. typical 5.2%), and MCU (-2.7% vs. typical -2.2%) were in line with typical MoM patterns.

Total unit shipments were down 2.7% MoM, with ASPs up 19.3% MoM.

By geography, total sales increased YoY and MoM in all regions.

Application specific ICs sales were up (2.4%) MoM, in line with typical historical patterns (up 3.0% MoM). 1 end markets was better than typical MoM, while 5 were worse than typical: Wired Communication (12.7% vs. typical 6.9%), was above typical MoM patterns. Consumer (-1.7% vs. typical 2.5%), Computer & Peripherals (3.8% vs. typical 5.7%), Wireless Communication (-3.2% vs. typical 1.5%), Automotive (-4.7% vs. typical 0.5%), and Multipurpose and Others (-5.2% vs. typical -4.0%) were worse than typical MoM patterns.

Total semiconductor sales grew 118.8% YoY in May after rising 106.4% in April (Exhibit 1). YoY strength was again dominated by memory with sales more than quadrupling at 325.7% YoY, driving \~\$200B in incremental revenue YTD and with memory pricing alone driving \~2/3 of overall industry growth since the beginning of the year (Exhibit 3, Exhibit 4). Excluding memory, sales were still up strongly at 34.0% YoY (Exhibit 2). On a 3-month rolling basis sales were up 35.6%, much better than typical historical patterns of up MSD (5.0%) (Exhibit 5).

## By product category, we note the following:

Analog Standard Linear: Total Analog Standard Linear sales were down (-5.4%), worse than typical patterns (-3.5%). Shipments were down (-2.3%) with ASPs down (-3.2%). Product category changes MoM were as follows: Amplifiers/Comparators (-5.4% vs. typical -7.3%), Interface (-9.8% vs. typical +0.5%), Power Management (-3.6% vs. typical -2.1%), and Signal Conversion (-9.9% vs. typical -8.2%) (Exhibit 13).

Analog Application Specific: Total analog application specific sales were down (-9.3%) MoM, worse than typical patterns (-0.6%). Units were down (-6.4%) MoM, with ASPs down (-3.3%). End markets changes MoM were as follows: Consumer (-8.1% vs. typical -1.0%), Computer (2.5% vs. typical 4.0%), Communications (-12.6% vs. typical -1.3%), Automotive (-5.3% vs. typical -1.4%), and Industrial & Others (-19.6% vs. typical of -6.9%) (Exhibit 14).

MPU: MPU sales were up 5.0% MoM, in line with typical patterns (5.2%MoM). Units were up (3.6%) MoM with ASPs up (1.3%) MoM. On a 3-months rolling basis (e.g. March-May vs. December-February), MPU sales were up (6.5%), better than typical average of 0.4%. Sales increased 13.9% vs. 3 months ago and increased 30.8% YoY.

MCU: MCU sales were down (-2.7%) MoM, in line with historical averages of down 2.2%, with units up (1.8%) and ASP down (-4.4%). Product category changes MoM were as follows: Consumer (-5.4% vs. typical -6.7%), Computer (-7.2% vs. typical 12.8%), Handsets (89.0% vs. typical -11.4%), Wireless Communication-All Other (-1.6% vs typical -0.4%), Wired Communication (23.9% vs. typical 3.2%), Automotive (-5.9% vs. typical -0.1%), and Multi-purpose and others (-2.2% vs. typical 0.1%) (Exhibit 15).

Memory: Memory sales were up (31.7%) MoM, in line with historical average of up 32.7%. On a bit shipped basis, DRAM shipments were up 13.2% MoM and NAND was up 19.5% MoM. On a \$ per bit basis, DRAM ASPs rose 12.9%, while NAND ASPs rose 17.8%. (Exhibit 16, Exhibit 17).
