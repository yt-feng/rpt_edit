# KIOXIA Holdings (285A) | Japan
# Starts BiCS-10 Production

Kioxia has begun production of BiCS-10 at its K2 fab and has started sample shipments of 1Tb TLC products based on the new technology.

## Key Takeaways

The K2 had previously been producing BiCS-8 products, but the introduction of BiCS-10 will drive an expansion in production capacity.

Compared with BiCS-8, BiCS-10 delivers a 33% improvement in interface performance, a 59% increase in bit density, and enhanced power efficiency.

We expect the technology to play an important role in increasing KIOXIA's product mix for the data center and enterprise storage applications.

Kioxia and SanDisk announce start of BiCS-10 production: On July 3, KIOXIA announced that it had begun sample shipments of a 1Tb TLC product utilizing its BiCS-10 technology. In addition, KIOXIA and SanDisk (covered by our US semiconductor analyst Joseph Moore) announced that they had commenced BiCS-10 production at the K2 fab of the Kitakami Plant. The K2 fab began operations in September 2025 and had previously been producing BiCS-8. With the introduction of BiCS-10, the companies plan to further scale production volumes.

KIOXIA's BiCS-8 combines planar shrinkage, CMOS directly Bonded Array (CBA) technology, and increased layer counts to deliver competitive products. It expects 80% of its GB output to transition to BiCS-8 by the end of March 2027.

BiCS-10 increases interface performance from 3.6Gbps in BiCS-8 to 4.8Gbps, representing a 33% improvement. The technology is designed to address the requirements of next-generation SSDs supporting PCIe Gen 6 and Gen 7, where higher bandwidth is required for AI workloads. Relative to BiCS-8, BiCS-10 delivers a 59% increase in bit density, an 18% improvement in power efficiency during write operations, and a 30% improvement during read operations.

The company expects the 1Tb TLC product based on BiCS-10 to be deployed in high-performance SSDs, including the KIOXIA CM Series, which is designed as a high-throughput, low-latency SSD capable of supporting KV cache architectures and functioning effectively as part of the memory hierarchy.

Our take: We expect KIOXIA's expansion in the data center and enterprise storage market revenues in 2026-1H27, as well as continued reductions in GB cost, to be primarily driven by products based on BiCS-8 technology.

In the medium-term, the company aims to increase the proportion of revenue from data center and enterprise products from the current 30–40% to more than 60%. Achieving the target will likely depend on the successful commercialization of SSDs based on BiCS-10 technology. We will monitor the qualification for BiCS-10 products and the pace of BiCS-10 capacity expansion at the K2 fab.
