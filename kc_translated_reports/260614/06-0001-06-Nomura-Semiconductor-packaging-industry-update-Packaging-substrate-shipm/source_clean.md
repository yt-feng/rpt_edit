## Semiconductor packaging industry update
# Packaging substrate shipments up 20% y-y
## Packaging-related monthly statistics: April shipments down from March record high

According to the Current Survey of Production for April, released on 12 June, shipments of packaging substrates (rigid module substrates) rose 20% y-y to ¥23.3bn, down from ¥27.3bn in March. Shipments in terms of m $^{2}$ rose 1% y-y and the average price per m $^{2}$ rose 19% y-y, and only slightly m-m, to a record high of ¥1.226mn. Based on conditions in other supply chains, we think shipments of packaging for Rubin processors may have been temporarily sluggish because of the pushing back of server production. Shipments in April 2025, when Blackwell packages got into full swing, were high in m $^{2}$ terms. We think that shipments of Rubin packages will move into full swing in summer 2026 (June at the earliest and around August at the latest).

## Adoption of 3.5D packaging likely to pick up in 2028: Moves at TSMC, Intel, Google


One point to watch with regard to the adoption of 3.5D packaging is that it won't be easy for companies developing accelerators on TSMC's back-end process design platform (TSMC 3DFabric Alliance) to transition to EMIB-T, as 3.5D packaging includes key functions such as power supply and heat management. EMIB-T design will require companies to either rely on Intel's back-end process design platform or to build their own back-end semiconductor process supply chains. In power supply, we think vertical power supply or integrated voltage regulators (iVRs) would have to be developed jointly with partners, while in heat management, new cold plates with microchannel mechanisms optimized for cooling each chip would require the same. We expect collaboration among companies to pick up.
