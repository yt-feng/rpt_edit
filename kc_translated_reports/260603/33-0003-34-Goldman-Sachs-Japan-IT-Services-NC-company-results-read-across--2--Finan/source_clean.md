# Japan IT Services: NC company results read-across (2) Financial SI: Simplex, NSD, DTS

In this report, we consider the potential implications for our IT services sector coverage from visits we made to three Not Covered companies (we have no investment views on these companies): Simplex Holdings (consulting/financial SI), NSD (financial/megabank SI), and DTS (financial/megabank SI). Simplex

Holdings is seeing stronger-than-expected demand in consulting and for enterprise customers. The company announced FY3/27 operating profit guidance that exceeds its medium-term plan target, and has also raised its order outlook range. Simplex has begun to focus on AI-driven development, and as it is largely engaged in contract-based development, it sees large scope to capture man-hour reductions in its own profits. NSD is seeing strong orders, mainly from megabanks, but expects only a slight increase in operating profits in FY3/27 mainly due to increased upfront investment. DTS continues to face a challenging earnings environment due to the peaking of a large project for a megabank (a company-specific factor), and guides for only a slight increase in operating profits in FY3/27. In terms of read-across for our IT services coverage, we think strong consulting demand as well as demand from financial institutions, government agencies, and local governments will act as tailwinds for earnings at Fujitsu (Buy), NEC (Buy), and NOM Institute (Buy), which are strong in these areas. Below we outline the key points from company commentary. (Income statements shown in Exhibit 1/Exhibit 2/Exhibit 3/Exhibit 4)

# Simplex Holdings (4373.T) (financial SI/consulting)

(1) Guidance: FY3/27 operating profit guidance calls for ¥17.2 bn (+19% yoy), for the seventh consecutive year of double-digit (%) yoy profit growth. Demand for consulting and development/maintenance for enterprise clients is stronger than expected, and the company raised its medium-term plan target (from ¥15.0 bn previously). Although R&D investment in areas such as generative AI and Web3.0 is set to increase sharply (+¥1.9 bn yoy, with the burden weighted toward 1H), it expects continued high growth, with expansion across the board in consulting, financial, and enterprise businesses.

(2) Order environment: 4Q3/26 orders totaled ¥17.6 bn (+19% yoy), for the third consecutive quarter of double-digit (%) growth, with strength in consulting, SI, and operations. In addition to larger consulting projects, Simplex won two large SI projects (one in the services industry and one in the securities industry). (3) Consulting business: 4Q operating profits continued to grow sharply, rising +39% yoy, led by strength in the financial, telecom, public, and payments sectors. Simplex said this was not limited to consulting alone; cross-selling of SI projects has been successful, and synergies with other businesses are increasing. FY3/27 operating profit guidance (midpoint of range) calls for +34% yoy growth. Unlike overseas ops, competition for consultants in Japan remains fierce, but Simplex has been successful in recruiting thanks to its competitive compensation.
(4) Generative AI: In addition to applying it to overall operations, Simplex plans to focus on AI-driven development. The company looks for AI-driven development to start contributing to earnings from FY3/29, and if successful this could enable Simplex to aim for the upper end of its targeted FY3/30 operating profit range (¥25.0-¥30.0 bn). As it is largely engaged in contract-based development, it sees large scope to capture man-hour reductions from AI-driven development in its own profits.
The company also believes that even if pricing pressure from customers increases in the medium term, it can boost company-wide earnings by reallocating surplus production capacity generated from man-hour reductions to other projects (we share this view for the industry as a whole).

# NSD (9759.T) (financial/megabank SI)

(1) Guidance: FY3/27 operating profit guidance calls for ¥19.5 bn (+2% yoy), for only a slight increase in profits. While its view of a continued favorable demand environment is unchanged, the company assumes SG&A expenses will rise sharply this fiscal year (+22%/+¥2.35 bn yoy), mainly due to investment in developing AI solutions, advertising expenses, and costs for office space expansion. NSD said it is uncertain whether it will be able to spend the full amount, but expenses are expected to increase mainly from 2Q.
(2) Order environment: System development orders grew by +17% yoy in 4Q3/26, an acceleration from 3Q (+3% yoy). By industry, orders were strong from the financial, telecom, public, and automotive sectors. In the core financial business, a large project to upgrade the core system of the company's largest megabank customer is in an expansionary phase (the company expects the peak to be in 2H3/28), and inquiries for development projects from trust banks and life insurers are also strong. Overall, the order environment remains favorable.
(3) New medium-term plan: NSD targets operating profits of ¥23.4 bn (+7% CAGR) in FY3/29, the final year of the plan, reflecting continued steady growth. With the plan incorporating a certain amount of M&A activity, the company assumes its operating margin will remain flat versus FY3/27. It expects the favorable demand environment in system development to continue and plans to maintain current hiring levels. As the company has a high ratio of quasi-mandate contracts, which are based on monthly man-hour pricing, it plans to avoid pricing pressure from customers by increasing its upstream business.

# DTS (9682.T) (financial/megabank SI)

(1) Guidance: FY3/27 guidance calls for operating profits of ¥17 bn (+3% yoy). The outlook for growth is only modest mainly on the run-off of a large, high-margin project for a megabank (mainly impacting 1H) and an increase in upfront investments, mainly in AI and human resources (SG&A expenses: +13%/+¥1.7 bn yoy).

(2) Order environment: 4Q3/26 orders declined by -4% yoy, for the second consecutive quarter of negative growth. The effect of a fallback in mainly megabank orders continues, leading to lackluster momentum compared with competitors. However, the impact of the reactionary decline in megabank demand has largely run its course, and DTS says orders from the public and telecom sectors are recently showing signs of recovery. In the manufacturing sector, DTS is seeing orders grow for its proprietary mcframe (SCM) solutions, mainly for the automotive industry.

(3) Unprofitable projects: In 4Q3/26, unprofitable projects totaled around ¥0.7 bn, booked across multiple projects (mainly in the technology & solutions business). These were projects for new customers in new fields (such as the cloud), and a lack of expertise was a contributing factor. According to the company, provisions have been made for these projects through to go-live, and the projects are scheduled to finish during 1H3/27.
