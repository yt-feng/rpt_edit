# China Auto Manufacturers
# CITI'S TAKE

After including data for the below brands that have disclosed their May-26 NEV sales, we estimate that May-26 NEV sector wholesale volume is tracking at +12% MoM (+7% YoY), beating market expectations. We note the following brands' NEV sales outperformed vs. the sector in May: Huawei Harmony (+41% MoM), Nio (+28% MoM), BYD (+19% MoM), Leapmotor (+14% MoM), and GWM NEV (+13% MoM).

BYD (both domestic and export beat) – BYD reported May-26 NEV (PV+CV) sales of 383.5k units (+0% YoY/+19% MoM) and May-26 NEV PV sales of 377.0k units (+0% YoY/+20% MoM). 5M26 sales -20% YoY to 1.41mn units. May Denza +45% MoM to 16,303 units, Fangchengbao +4% MoM to 30,186 units, and Yangwang +8% MoM to 286 units. Overseas NEV sales volume logged 160,644 units in May (+19% MoM), with 5M26 overseas sales +65% YoY to 616,907 units. During the month, within NEV-PV, BEV logged -3% YoY/+27% MoM to 198.7k units, accounting for 52.7% of NEV-PV in May-26. 5M26 BEV-PV saw -18% YoY growth to 666.0k units. PHEV-PV sales were +3% YoY/+13% MoM at 178.3k units in May-26. 5M26 PHEV-PV saw -22% YoY growth to 714.1k units. BYD (1211.HK) – 4 new breakthroughs (Agentic-Al, 4nm Chips, L3/L4 Safety guarantee, ASP Optimization) + Peers' Cross-Read

Li Auto (In-line) – Li Auto booked 33,350 units of sales in May-26 (-18% YoY/-2% MoM), with 5M26 sales at -3% YoY to 162,577 units. Li Auto (LI/2015.HK) – 2Q Vehicle Margin \~10%; 2026 Priority Centered on L-Series Refresh

Xpeng (In-line) – May-26 deliveries booked 32,158 units (-4% YoY/+4% MoM). 5M26 sales saw -23% YoY to 125,851 units. Xpeng (XPEV.N/9868.HK) – 2Q GPM Flat QoQ; Humanoid Robot & Robotaxi Roadmap on Track

Nio (Beat) – May-26 deliveries logged 37,705 units, +62% YoY/+28% MoM. 5M26 sales saw +69% YoY to 150,526 units. Onvo delivered 12,029 units in May (+125% MoM). Firefly delivered 5,663 units in May (+14% MoM). NIO (NIO.N) – Expect 2Q Non-GAAP Profitability + Mgmt. Update Takeaways

Leapmotor (Beat) – May-26 deliveries reached 81,569 units (+81% YoY/+14% MoM), with 5M26 sales +52% YoY to 263,111 units. Leapmotor (9863.HK) – Expect 2Q Breakeven, Lower FY26 GPM to 14.5%

Zeekr – May-26 deliveries reached 34,377 units, +82% YoY/+8% MoM, with 5M26 sales +93% YoY to 143,201 units.
