# China Battery Materials

High-conviction call for lithium carbonate price to rally to Rmb250k/t, and Equities Will React

# CITI'S TAKE

Lithium and battery materials are out of the woods since 2H25 thanks to strong demand, echoing our last lithium report in early Nov-25 (see note). Considering demand continues gaining conviction ahead, together with on-and-off supply disruption factors, we remain bullish on lithium price and think it could test the level of Rmb250k/t during the year (possibly in Aug-Sep). With recent correction in Chinese lithium equities on concern of demand destruction if price stays high, we move up our preference on lithium names as we believe they will outperform the rest of the battery chain when the price rises. We raise the preference on lithium; our latest pecking order along the value chain is lithium > cathode > battery > electrolyte > separator > battery parts > anode (see here). Top picks are Ganfeng Lithium, CATL-A, Hunan Yuneng, EVE and CALB.

Reiterate our bullish view on lithium ASP during 2026—Even though lithium price retreated after touching Rmb200k/t in last two weeks, we still see possibility that lithium price could test another critical level of Rmb250k/t in the rest of the year, likely during Aug-Sep (traditional peak season of NEV plus another front-loading export window). Major battery makers should welcome new capacity onboard during Aug-Sep at the least to achieve its yearly output target, indicating a relatively material demand increase in that time. Fundamentals remain solid, which should support another round of momentum on the lithium price uplift, in our view.

Lithium names under our coverage — Stronger lithium prices reflect stronger demand, and this will be positive for equities sentiment along the battery chain. We maintain Buy on both Tianqi Lithium and Ganfeng Lithium, considering both lithium names should benefit from the lithium ASP uplift amid robust on-the-ground battery demand, thanks to their cost edge, scale effect, and other competitive factors. Of the two, Tianqi Lithium's share price is catching up QTD due to the undemanding valuation, and we estimate the trade should still open in the near term. However, we prefer Ganfeng Lithium on a longer time horizon, as it is witnessing double tailwinds of increasing equity volume in carbonate (incl Goulamina, Cauchari, PPG, and Mt Marion) and ASP uplift in the upcycle, while Tianqi should have few volume increments in the next 2-3 years.

# On-and-off supply disruption on the course of the year

Even though demand has been gaining conviction YTD, lithium becomes more volatile in trading as there are on and off supply disruptions during the course of the year, putting upside/downside risks on the dynamics. Here we list out the topical supply disruption factors and our read on the latest update.

- JXW (-ve to lithium price) - JXW has been suspended since Aug-25 due to the mining permit expiration, the inappropriate mining license as ceramic clay and environmental issues (i.e., river encroachment, tailings treatment, etc.). According to our channel checks with industry contacts & public government announcement, CATL has obtained proper mining permit in early-26 and is still pending approval for environmental license. It's difficult to arrive at a market consensus view on the clear resumption timeline as it depends on the government process instead of the corporate initiatives. Overall, it's a hovering negative alert to the lithium price before the finalized approval.
Lepidolite mines update (+ve to lithium price) – 4 of the remaining lepidolite mine might need to go through the same procedure (i.e., mining permit, environmental license, etc.) sooner or later during the year, indicating further downside risk to the lithium supply. According to our channel check with industry contacts, companies are expediting the lepidolite production YTD to pile up the lepidolite inventory for conversion later. That said, if the remaining lepidolite mines do need to suspend for a license update, the actual impact this year might be less than the on-paper figures (-8k tons LCE per month, taking up 7-8% lithium supply in China), but the impact in FY27 or years after depends on the approval timeline.

Australian mines update (-ve to lithium price) – Australian high-cost spodumene mines are gradually incentivized to restart and/or expand since 2H26 due to the attractive spodumene prices YTD. Below please find the summary on incremental spodumene incentivized from high spodumene price.

Zimbabwe (+ve to lithium price) – Zimbabwe resumes the spodumene export by end of Apr-26, after its \~3 months spodumene export restriction since Feb-26. We estimate the shipment should gradually arrive in China by end June / early July, easing off the tight spot market dynamics. On top of that, companies will be charged a 10% export rate out of revenue when exporting the spodumene in Zimbabwe, indicating cost inflation on spodumene/lithium conversion ahead.

# Ganfeng Lithium

# Company description

Jiangxi Ganfeng is one of the largest lithium producers in China. Established in 1996, the company went public in the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2010 and in the HKEx in 2018. With most of its operations in Jiangxi province, the company has \~116kt lithium carbonate and \~131kt lithium hydroxide capacity as of YE2025.

# Investment strategy

We rate Ganfeng Lithium as Buy. The company should benefit from the lithium ASP uplift amid robust on-the-ground battery demand, and improving cost competitiveness on increasing contribution from low-cost upstream resources (i.e. Goulamina, Mariana, and others). Its continual improvement in equity-owned lithium volume in the next 2-3 years (incl. Goulamina, PPG, Cauchari, and others) laid solid foundation for its long-term competitiveness. Also, its fast-developing battery business should provide further upside support to the headline NP in the upcoming years.

# Valuation

TP is set at Rmb93/sh for Ganfeng Lithium-A based on 3.2x 2026E P/B, at the 5-year historical average level since 2021, factoring in the tight S/D lithium dynamics set-up ahead. Implied 2026 P/E is 22x.

# Risks

Key downside risks that could impede Ganfeng Lithium A-share from reaching our target price include: (1) geopolitical risk on its overseas mining assets, (2) lower-than-expected lithium-ion battery demand, and (3) slower-than-expected ramp-up of Goulamina and Mariana.

# Tianqi Lithium

# Company description

As of YE2024, Tianqi Lithium had 1.62mntpa spodumene capacity and 91,800tpa lithium compound capacity. Tianqi has a 26.01% stake in Talison, which controls the Greenbushes spodumene (lithium ore) mine in Australia, the largest spodumene mine in the world with the highest grade of lithium ore (Li2O at 1.3%). This move has enabled Tianqi to secure low-cost mineral resources. Tianqi's products, including lithium carbonate and lithium hydroxide, are widely used in electric vehicle batteries, industrial grease, ceramics, and glass.

# Investment strategy

We rate Tianqi Lithium as Buy. The stock is one of the best positions to benefit from lithium upcycle, on back of its pure exposure to lithium space from upstream spodumene to downstream lithium carbonate. Lithium price uptick supports Tianqi with decent earnings recovery in 1Q26, and suggests solid financial performance on the course of the year. Also, Tianqi Lithium's undemanding valuation vs Ganfeng Lithium indicates attractiveness to investors in the near term.

# Valuation

We use a P/B multiple for better apple-to-apple comparison vs Ganfeng Lithium. Our TP is set at Rmb75.14/sh based on 2.5x 2026E P/B, the historical average level since 2021 to reflect the tight S/D lithium market dynamics ahead.

# Risks

Key downside risks that could impede Tianqi Lithium A-share from reaching our target price include: (1) weaker-than-expected lithium-ion battery demand, and (2) any supply disruption on the policy uncertainties.
