# ASCO 2026 Takeaways - Day 2
## Key Takeaways

Day 2 shifted towards broader front-line developments, especially RAS/ADC debates on regimen design, dose intensity, QoL and efficacy/tox trade-offs.
KRAS G12C mono still looks like cleaner front-line thesis; combo efficacy is real, but needs tighter PD-L1/co-mut/CNS selection, AE mgmt and dose continuity.
Mechanisms alone may not define G12C durability: ON/OFF read-through remains equivocal, while molecule-level PK, engagement, CNS and tox could vary greatly.
Non-G12C and pan-RAS keep the RAS map fluid; chemo-heavy paths, MAPK partners, lineage plasticity and derm/GI QoL tox remain constraints to watch in 1L.
China read: RAS implications for Abbisko/Genfleet/Jacobio; ADC debate favors Duality/Innovent/Akeso/Leads Bio on format depth, payload/linker & combo logic.

KRAS G12C NSCLC: Divarasib and Elisrasib data supported 1L mono/combo potential. The discussant was more confident on mono; combo efficacy is real, but use likely needs segment selectivity (PD-L1/co-mut/CNS) and proactive GI/liver AE management. The discussant saw a lower likelihood for chemo-only combos vs. +IO +/- chemo, given limited LT survival from chemo. She stressed "not all G12C inhibitors are created equal", with molecule differences and equivocal (ON) vs. (OFF) read-through, as some (OFF) agents are showing durable mono activity. Hepatotox/dose modification remains key.

Non-G12C/pan-RAS: 1L exploration continues across mechanisms. PDAC combos are mainly chemo-based, while future studies may clarify MAPK/other partners and lineage plasticity. Derm/GI tox are key given QoL impact.

ADCs: 1L use was advocated across UC, TNBC, and HER2+ BC, with focus on combo vs. sequencing for tolerable administration. Dual-payload/bispecific ADCs highlighted as next wave of innovation, esp. payload beyond topo/microtubule; linker stability, biomarker dependence and cross-resistance are things to watch for.
