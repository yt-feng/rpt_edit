# China Auto Manufacturers
# Bi-Monthly Duo NEV Pricing Trends (Discount & MSRP): End-May-26 Update
# CITI'S TAKE

In this bimonthly pricing trend report, we monitor the NEV-PV pricing of major OEMs based on data from Top 30 cities collected by Thinkercar. Discount is calculated as the difference between 4S shop retail price and the MSRP. In the second half of May-26, China NEV-PV sector weighted average retail discount was flat MoM to 7.6% (compared with 30-Apr-26). NEV-PV sector weighted average MSRP was +0.6% MoM to Rmb174,748.

# NEV brands

- Tesla: retail discount stands at $0.3\%$ (+0.0ppt MoM); MSRP at Rmb294,716 (flat MoM)
- Nio: retail discount stands at $2.8\%$ (+0.Oppt MoM); MSRP at Rmb320,799 (flat MoM)
- Li Auto: retail discount stands at $6.4\%$ (-0.1ppt MoM); MSRP at Rmb283,273 (+0.1% MoM)
- Leapmotor: retail discount stands at $8.5\%$ (+0.6ppt MoM); MSRP at Rmb123,975 (flat MoM)

# Traditional local brands (for NEV models only)

- BYD: retail discount stands at $7.5\%$ (-0.3ppt MoM); MSRP at Rmb133,150 (+0.4% MoM)
- Geely: retail discount stands at $12.9\%$ (+0.3ppt MoM); MSRP at Rmb109,578 (+2.3% MoM)
- Great Wall: retail discount stands at $7.1\%$ (-0.1ppt MoM); MSRP at Rmb264,957 (+3.9% MoM)
