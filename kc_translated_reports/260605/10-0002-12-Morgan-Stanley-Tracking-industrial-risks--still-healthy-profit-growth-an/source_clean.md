## China Financials | Asia Pacific
# Tracking industrial risks: still healthy profit growth and reduction of overcapacity risks despite slower headline macro numbers
## Key Takeaways

YTD manufacturing profit growth accelerated further to 20.4% yoy in April 2026, with single-month growth at 19.3% yoy.
- Strong export growth at 14.5% yoy and continued PPI recovery -- up 1.7% MoM and 2.8% YoY in April – have supported healthy manufacturing profit growth
Nearly 40% of sectors (by liability) showed profit growth improvement under our updated classification, including not only upstream sectors.
April YTD manufacturing FAI growth dropped to 1.2% from 4.1% yoy in March, from a high base last year.
Still rational industrial credit growth without loan volume growth push from regulator. Industrial firms' liability growth was 5.9% yoy, vs. 5.5% last month.

Profitability improvement continued despite slower headline macro growth numbers in April: We reclassified 31 manufacturing sub-sectors based on profit growth and FAI-IP gap trends at the end of 2025 and 2026 YTD and see that 37.2% of sectors (by liability) have improved profitability. Those sectors with improved profit trend are not limited only to upstream sectors – they also include downstream sectors like electronic devices. Overall gross margin improved 6bps yoy, helped by both PPI rebound and cost control. We think it will take some time for PPI recovery to translate into profitability rebound across different sectors. However, we expect healthy profit growth at manufacturing sectors to help industrial risk digestion at banks.

Continued overcapacity reduction as anti-involution remains a key area of policy focus in 2026: Despite some volatility in recent months' investment growth, we believe overcapacity reduction is still in progress among manufacturing sectors, with capex slowing down in 84.5% of sectors (by liability). We expect this and healthy manufacturing profit growth to help reduce industrial credit risks and support steady credit demand.
