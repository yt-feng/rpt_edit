# China housing prices continue to decline
## Key Takeaways

In May, secondary home listing prices fell 0.5% MoM (-11.3% YoY), slightly faster than April's 0.4% decline.
The trend continued to diverge among cities, with a broadly soft home price trend overall, although select Tier 1 cities may still see a mild uptrend.
Our China Property team maintains a prudent view and suggests waiting for more evidence of a market recovery to turn constructive.
