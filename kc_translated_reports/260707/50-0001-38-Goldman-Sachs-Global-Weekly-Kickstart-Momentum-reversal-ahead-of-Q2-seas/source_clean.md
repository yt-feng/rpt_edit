## Momentum reversal ahead of Q2 season

Global equities rose 2% last week, led by Europe, while Asia underperformed (Exhibit 1). Momentum sold off sharply as investors rotated from recent winners into laggards (Exhibit 31). Ahead of the Q2 reporting season, expectations are elevated, with strong EPS growth (Exhibit 7), positive EPS revisions (Exhibit 22), and positive sentiment (Exhibit 21).

## Macro data this week

US: ISM services index, minutes to the FOMC's June meeting, and speaking engagements with Fed officials including events with Governor Waller and Presidents Williams and Logan.

Europe: Germany factory orders and industrial production; Sweden flash inflation; Germany, France, Norway final inflation.

Japan: July 9th BOJ branch managers' meeting, May nominal wage growth, and June domestic CGPI.

AEJ: credit data in China; inflation data in China, Taiwan, Thailand and Philippines; GDP in Singapore and Taiwan exports, and a central bank meeting in Malaysia.

Pricing is as of the close of Friday, July 3.

## Investors prepare for Q2 season

The earnings season kicks off next week and will be critical in determining whether elevated valuations are justified. This is particularly important following last week's sharp momentum rotation, which saw investors move out of recent winners and into laggards.

■ Strong growth in the US. Consensus forecasts S&P 500 EPS growth of 22% in Q2, although the strength remains concentrated, with AI infrastructure companies expected to account for nearly 60% of total EPS growth. The median S&P 500 stock is expected to grow EPS by 9% y/y. The focus will be on whether AI spending is beginning to generate tangible returns.

■ Strong growth in Europe. Consensus forecasts STOXX 600 EPS growth of 11% in H1, although the strength remains concentrated in Energy. Excluding commodity producers, EPS growth falls to 6%, in line with the median stock. The focus will be on whether stronger manufacturing activity and a weaker euro can support earnings beyond the commodity sector. Our FX strategists revised their EUR/USD forecast to 1.12 from 1.20.
