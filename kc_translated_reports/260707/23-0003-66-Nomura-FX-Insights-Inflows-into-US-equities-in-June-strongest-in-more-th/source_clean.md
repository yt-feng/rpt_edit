# FX Insights
## Inflows into US equities in June strongest in more than a year

\- Foreign inflows into US-focused funds totaled USD2.1bn between 26 June and 2 July, with strong inflows into equity funds (USD3.8bn) being partly offset by outflows from bond funds (-USD1.7bn).

\- Foreign inflows into US-focused equity funds in June (USD13.8bn) were at their strongest since January 2025 (USD16.1bn) and momentum remains despite recent weakest in AI-related equities.

\- Inflows into EM-focused ETFs slowed to USD246mn between 26 June and 2 July, following inflows of USD1.8bn in the previous week.

\- Korea's retail investors net bought only USD21mn of US portfolio assets between 27 June and 3 July, down from USD49mn in the previous week.

\- Taiwan's investors net sold USD975mn of USD-denominated bond funds between 26 June and 2 July, after net buying of USD61mn in the previous week.

## Weekly update of high-frequency investor flows

\- Foreign inflows into US-focused equity ETFs and mutual funds[1] totaled USD3.8bn between 26 June and 2 July (Figure 3), following inflows of USD1.5bn in the previous week. In June, inflows into these funds totaled USD13.8bn, their highest since USD16.1bn in January 2025 (Figure 2).

\- Foreign outflows from US-focused bond ETFs and mutual funds $^{[2]}$ totaled USD1.7bn between 26 June and 2 July, following outflows of USD132mn in the previous week (Figure 5). In June, outflows from these funds totaled USD2.7bn, which is the highest monthly outflow since April 2025 (Figure 4).

\- Small foreign inflows into EM-focused ETFs $^{[3]}$ totaled USD246mn between 26 June and 2 July, following inflows of USD1.8bn in the previous week. In June, foreign investors net bought USD3.1bn of EM funds (mainly into EM equity funds of USD2.3bn), surpassing inflows of USD2.5bn in May 2026 (Figures 6 and 7).

\- Outflows from Taiwan's exchange-listed USD bond ETFs totaled USD975mn (26 June to 2 July), a shift from small inflows of USD61mn in the previous week (Figure 9). In June, Taiwanese investors net sold USD2.1bn of USD-denominated bonds, the highest outflows in over two years (Figure 8).
