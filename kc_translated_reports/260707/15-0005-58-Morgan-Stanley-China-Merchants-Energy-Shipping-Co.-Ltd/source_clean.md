Source: Company data, MS

## Key Takeaways

CMES 1H26 prelims were largely in line with MSe; the implied midpoint of 2Q26 net profit was Rmb4.2bn.

The significant QoQ improvement comes despite the SoH shutdown during 2Q, due mainly to CMES's tanker operational proficiency and a strong dry bulk market.

We remain constructive on tanker names as the up-cycle is playing out; 3Q26 could see further QoQ upside should the Strait re-open as expected.

We see potential upside to F26 earnings if elevated tanker spot rates and oil re-stocking demand prove stronger/last longer than expected.

CMES announced 1H26 preliminary results on July 5, 2026. It expects 1H26 net profit of Rmb6,600-7,300mn, up 214-248% YoY. Recurring net profit is expected to be in the range of Rmb6,540-7,240mn, up 244-281% YoY. The midpoint of implied 2Q26 net profit, Rmb4,187mn, is largely in line with MSe of Rmb4.2bn.

\- Tanker profit: 2Q26 profit contribution +50% QoQ, despite temporary oversupply pressure after disruption in the Strait of Hormuz.

\- Dry bulk: 2Q26 profit contribution +170% QoQ, benefiting from continued improvement in supply-demand dynamics and BDI recovery.

\- Containers and Ro-ro: Both showed steady recovery trends.

## Risks to Upside

■ More crude oil shipping demand thanks to Hormuz reopening, or removal of sanctions on Iranian oil exports.

■ Longer shipment distance for crude and dry bulks

■ Potential OPEC+ production increase

## Risks to Downside

■ Weaker global economy leading to lower crude demand

■ Lower-than-expected crude oil output from OPEC

■ Weaker than expected infrastructure demand in China
