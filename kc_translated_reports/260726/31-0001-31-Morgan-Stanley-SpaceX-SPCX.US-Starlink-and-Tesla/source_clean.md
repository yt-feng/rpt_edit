# Starlink and Tesla... Connective Tissue for Robot Swarm

Tesla Cybercab + Starlink integration is another sign of 'mission' overlap between robotics and space. SpaceX at 22x 2028 EV/EBIT (MSe) does not discount the opportunity. Reiterate OW.

What Happened? On the 2Q26 earnings call, Elon Musk confirmed that Starlink is being integrated into the Cybercab and will eventually roll out across all Tesla vehicles in markets where Starlink is active, framing it as essential to robotaxi operations given the persistent gaps in cellular coverage that could otherwise leave autonomous vehicles stranded in connectivity "dead zones."

## Significance to SpaceX Stock:

\- Robots impact SpaceX in 2 main ways: (1) accounts for the single largest cohort of Starlink ‘clients’ in our long-term forecasts; and (2) represents potentially the largest and most valuable end market within the Enterprise AI business.

\- By integrating Starlink, Cybercab gains a redundant, high-bandwidth connectivity solution that improves network availability and enables a consistent broadband experience for passengers.

\- Robots as a Service (RaaS) may represent a \$75tn long-term TAM on our calculations by 2050 (\$25tn original equipment with approx. \$50bn in downstream adjacent services).

\- We expect the robotaxi market to be an obvious and early adopter for mobile satellite connectivity. Even if primarily served by terrestrial 5G, we see the need for redundancy and resiliency in comms for critical network infrastructure including transport networks.

\- In our model, we estimate a peak Starlink penetration on AVs of 40% in 2040, or 83 million connected cars. We forecast Starlink revenues from autonomous vehicles to reach \$6bn by 2035 and \$31bn by 2040.

\- Beyond robotaxis, our Starlink forecasts include multiple other robotic client stratum including large and small drones/eVTOLs, industrial robots, humanoids, etc.
