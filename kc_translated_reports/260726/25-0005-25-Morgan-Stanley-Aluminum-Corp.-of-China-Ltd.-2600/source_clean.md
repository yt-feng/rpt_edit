# Aluminum Corp. of China Ltd. | Asia Pacific
# Baotou Plant Visit and Management Meeting
## Key Takeaways

Baotou plant has 1.46mnt aluminum capacity with 62.5% of the power is captive, made up of solar 200MW, wind 1900MW and 1720MW coal, with 4 hours of ESS.

Average power cost is below Rmb0.40/kWh, with renewable power cost at Rmb0.17/kWh.

45mnt remain a hard capacity cap. Company expects NDRC Document No. 45, effective August 1, to help control overproduction.

■ Enforcement could include retrospective checks comparing power consumption, approved capacity against actual output.

New Document No. 837 on overseas investment was effective July 1. The review process is more granular and may impact aluminum investment overseas.

The overproduction controls examine: Electricity consumption per tonne of aluminium produced; potline operating parameters and current efficiency; environmental compliance; the proportion and source of renewable power; and maintenance records and actual operating days. This retrospective traceability framework should make it more difficult for producers to conceal sustained production above approved capacity, according to management.

The overseas investment control will review:

\- Who is investing: including ultimate ownership and look-through analysis of the investor;

• What is being acquired or developed;

\- Where the investment is located: with country and geopolitical risk taken into account;

• How the investment is structured and financed;

\- Whether key technology is being exported: sensitive technologies may require approval.
