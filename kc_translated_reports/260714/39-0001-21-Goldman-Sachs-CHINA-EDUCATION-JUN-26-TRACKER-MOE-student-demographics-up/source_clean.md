# MOE student demographics updates; Learning tablet ASP hike a bright spot against pressured volume and seasonally softer tutoring app user engagement

We summarize key findings from the Jun 2026 updates of our China Education tracker, including AI education apps, AI learning tablets, mobile user engagement, tutoring licenses, overseas study trends, and East Buy.

K-12 tutoring TAM update: As per MOE data published last week, number of K-12 students declined -2% yoy in 2025 after peaking out in 2022, dragged by -4%/-10% yoy decline of primary school/pre-school students (vs. junior/senior secondary school students +2%/+4% yoy); we estimate that China's K-12 student count will decline at a -4% CAGR in 2025-30E. In Jun, the number of non-academic tutoring institutions further dropped -0.2% mom or -1% yoy to 117.1k.

AI learning tablets: In the seasonally strong month driven by 6.18 promotions, combined GMV of 6 major brands dropped -15% yoy (vs. -35% yoy in May) on major e-commerce platforms. TAL sold c.173k units for c.Rmb704mn GMV in Jun (GMV -22% yoy), with ASP expanding +26% yoy which may sustain into Jul post the launch of its premium T6 xPad series on June 30 priced at Rmb6.9-12k.

■ Mobile user engagement: In the seasonally weak month tutoring app user engagement softened broadly, while TAL Peiyou sustained positive MAU growth of +13% yoy (vs. negative yoy for EDU, Xueersi.com). That said, growth momentum of AI education apps sustained; Doubao Study DAU growth accelerated to +161% yoy as of Jun-end while Gauth maintained its global leadership with monthly billings +8% yoy to c.US\$0.7mn.

East Buy's GMV on Douyin grew +42% yoy/-13% mom to c.Rmb901mn in Jun (vs. +38% yoy in May), driven by new platforms' GMV +308% yoy (vs. -12% yoy decline for the original 3 platforms, Exhibit 27). East Buy app MAUs grew +32% yoy in Jun (vs. +38% yoy in May).

According to the 2025 National Education Development Statistics Report released by MoE in July, we highlight:

1. Number of K-12 students in China declined -2% yoy in 2025 (vs. -2% yoy in 2024) after peaking out in 2022, while we estimate that the number of K-12 students will decline at a -4% CAGR in 2025-30E.

2. Number of primary school students declined further at $-4\%$ yoy (vs. $-2\%$ yoy in 2024) with the number of new enrollments at primary school declined $-10\%$ yoy in 2025 (vs. $-14\%$ yoy in 2024), while the decline in number of pre-school students narrowed to $-10\%$ yoy (vs. $-12\%$ yoy in 2024). ## AI learning tablets: TAL tablet GMV -38% yoy in Jun; Strong ASP expansion yoy/qoq

■ GMV: Combined GMV of 6 major learning device brands dropped -15% yoy or increased +137% mom in Jun (vs. -35% yoy/+46% mom in May) on Douyin/Taobao/Tmall/JD. By brand, GMV of (+) Yuanfudao increased +33% yoy in Jun, while (-) iFlytek/TAL/Xiaodu/BBK/Zuoyebang dropped -5%/-22%/-56%/-62%/-29% yoy.

Specifically, TAL sold c.173k units for c.Rmb704mn GMV on major eCommerce platforms in Jun, with -38% yoy/+79% mom for shipments and -22% yoy/+98% mom for GMV (vs. -24% yoy/+85% mom for GMV in May). ASP of TAL learning tablets grew +26% yoy or +11% mom to c.Rmb4.1k in Jun (vs. -5% yoy/-8% mom in May).

New AI learning devices release: TAL launched its learning devices T6 series on June 30 including 3 models T6 Standard, T6 International, and T6 Max, priced at Rmb6.9k/8.7k/12k respectively. T6 series integrates a proprietary AI Diagnostics system with an AI Double-Teacher interactive classroom. We see the new products tap into the premium segment and could help drive ASP. Other recent new products include iFlytek's T90 series (starting at Rmb7.8k), BBK's S10 series (starting at Rmb4.9k), Youdao's X90 etc.

Price Target Risks and Methodology - New Oriental Education & Technology Our SOTP-based 12m target prices for EDU/9901.HK are US\$65/HK\$50, based on FY27E i) 13x EV/NOPAT on high-school tutoring; ii) 12x EV/NOPAT on other traditional business (including overseas test prep and consulting, college test prep, etc.) and new initiatives (including non-academic tutoring, learning solutions, tourism); iii) our target valuation of East Buy attributable to New Oriental; iv) US\$3.0bn net cash balance after excluding deferred revenue; and v) a 10% holdco discount. Key risks: 1) Industry TAM pressure from shrinking K12 demographics; 2) Pricing pressure due to competition and/or rising consumer discretionary attribute; 3) Further disruption to demand for overseas study; 4) AI disruption to learning scenarios; 5) Below-expected operating efficiency and margin improvement; 6) Regulatory changes in the education sector.

## Price Target Risks and Methodology - TAL Education Group

We are Buy rated. Our SOTP-based 12m target price for TAL is US\$14.7, based on mid-CY26E/27E 1) 13x target EV/NOPAT for high school tutoring; 2) 19x target EV/NOPAT for enrichment learning and others; 3) 13x target EV/NOPAT for content solutions assuming normalized OPM of 5%; 4) US2.9bn net cash after excluding deferred revenue; and 5) a 10% holdco discount. Key risks: 1) weaker-than-expected offline capacity expansion; 2) regulatory changes in the education sector; 3) unsatisfactory product launch for smart learning tablets; and 4) challenges in exploring overseas business.

## Price Target Risks and Methodology - East Buy

Our 12-month target price of HK\$12.4 is based on a 15x target P/E multiple applied to our FY27E EPS, benchmarking average P/E multiples of China e-commerce platforms, and China F&B and household product brands. Key upside risks: Stronger East Buy GMV growth, faster ramp-up of new sales channels, above-expected launch of private-label products, support from parent company, success of new business development, improved shareholder returns.
