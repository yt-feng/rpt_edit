# Raising TPs of SG Micro and Ruijie on growing AI data center; Ruijie to Neutral on fair valuation

We remain positive on the AI infrastructure upcycle, and identify SG Micro and Ruijie as well exposed to the trend. We updated our Global Server TAM in late Jun (report link), where estimates for AI servers implied AI chips were raised by 14% / 22% / 14% in 2026E-28E and China CSPs' AI capex was raised by 37% / 44% / 55% in 2026E-28E, reflecting our positive view on the ongoing AI infrastructure upcycle. SG Micro as a local leader in analog IC, expanding analog IC toward AI data centers (e.g. AI servers, optical modules); Ruijie as a local leader in whitebox switch, riding on China CSP's AI capex upcycle and growing Supernodes. With this, we raise our estimates and TPs for both SG Micro (to Rmb143 from Rmb96.1) and Ruijie (to Rmb120.5 from Rmb 83.4). We also downgrade Ruijie to Neutral (from Buy) on fair valuation; shares are trading near its historical average on 12m fwd P/E and TP implied PEG of 1.5x is within peers' range from 0.9x to 1.7x.

## Upside / downside risks:

\- Higher- / lower-than-expected China Cloud capex: China Cloud capex expansion is one of the main drivers for Ruijie's data center switch business. If the China Cloud capex is higher / lower than expected, it could result in upside / downside risks to our Ruijie forecasts.

\- Faster- / slower-than-expected product migration: We model the product migration towards switch with higher speed (e.g. 800G and above) to bring an ASP uptrend and drive Ruijie's revenue growth. If the product migration is faster / slower than expected, it could result in upside / downside risks to our revenue forecasts.

Milder- / fiercer-than-expected pricing competition: Data center switch is a fragmented market, and Ruijie is facing pricing competition from both local and global peers. If the pricing competition among data center switch suppliers is milder / fiercer than expected, it could result in upside / downside risks to our profitability forecasts.

Ruijie is a leading ICT equipment supplier in China, offering data center switch, SMB switch, access point, cyber security products, cloud desktop, etc. We expect Ruijie to leverage its accumulated experiences in white box data center switch and ride on China's Cloud capex expansion, and the rising market demand for high-end switch (high-speed, liquid-cooled, supporting LPO / CPO optical transceivers) which also supports the company's growth due to its higher ASP. The company's forward trading P/E is close to our 2027E target P/E, reflecting the positives are largely priced in. We have a Neutral rating on Ruijie.

Valuation: We derive our 12M TP of Rmb120.5 based on a target P/E multiple of 60x 2027E EPS. Our target P/E of 60x is derived from the correlation between P/E and EPS growth of Ruijie's peers, based on Ruijie's 2027E-28E EPS YoY growth.

Key risks: Higher / lower-than-expected China Cloud capex; Faster / slower-than-expected product migration; Milder / fiercer-than-expected pricing competition.
