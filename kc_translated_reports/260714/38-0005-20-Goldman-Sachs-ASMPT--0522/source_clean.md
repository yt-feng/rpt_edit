# ASMPT (0522.HK)
# TCB tool riding on AI infrastructure and advanced packaging trends; TP up to HK\$206; Neutral

We expect ASMPT to ride on the growing advanced packaging trend under the AI infrastructure upcycle, for which we updated our Global Server TAM in late Jun (report link) and raised: (1) AI servers implied AI chips shipments by 14% / 22% / 14% in 2026-28E, (2) US cloud service providers (CSPs) capex by 8% / 27% / 25% in 2026-28E, and (3) China CSP capex by 37% / 44% / 55% in 2026-28E. We expect the rising AI infrastructure end demand to drive ASMPT's TCB equipment and PCB SMT tool shipments. TSMC CoWoS and capex raise (report link) and ASMPT's announcements that it secured repeat order for 8 TCB tools for chip-to-wafer applications from a leading global IDM (link) also reaffirms our positive view on ASMPT's growth in coming years. We expect its net income to grow at +36% CAGR in 2026-28E.

We change our valuation methodology from 2027E P/E to 2030E discounted P/E to capture ASMPT's long-term growth, which aligns with our China Semi coverage. We raise our 12M TP to HK\$206 with both higher earnings and multiple, while maintain our Neutral rating, as the stock currently trades at 33x 2027E P/E, around our new TP-implied P/E at 36, reflecting the positives are largely in the price.

## Price target methodology and risks - ASMPT

Valuation methodology: We are Neutral-rated on ASMPT with a 12-month target price of HK\$206, which is based on 29.5x 2030E discounted P/E (discounted back to 2027E). Our target P/E is derived from correlation between peers P/E to the sum of NI YoY and OPM.

Key upside/downside risks: 1) customers' faster-/slower-than-expected adoption of Advanced Packaging tools; 2) stronger-/weaker-than-expected demand from automotive customers; and 3) stronger-/weaker-than-expected demand for traditional IC packaging and SMT equipment.
