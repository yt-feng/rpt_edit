# FX Positioning Indicates Investors Remained Net Long DXY

Our FX options positioning scores are now published daily on Bloomberg. A table of the tickers can be found in the Appendix.

## Key Takeaways

\- Options pricing data indicate that investors increased long NOK (vs. EUR) positions and increased short CHF positions.

In the futures market, investors increased long JPY positions, while they increased short CAD positions.

Overall, options data suggest that tactical investors are currently long DXY while being short EUR.

Our contrarian trading strategy would suggest long NOK/JPY offers value based on positioning score percentiles.
