## Strong inflows into US equity funds continue in July

\- Foreign inflows into US-focused funds totaled USD3.2bn between 3 and 9 July, owing to strong inflows into equity funds (USD3.2bn), while flows into bond funds stayed flat.

\- MTD July inflows into US-focused equity funds totaled USD4.4bn, tracking close to the USD4.9bn of inflows over a similar period in June.

\- Inflows into EM-focused ETFs remained anaemic, rising to USD514mn between 3 and 9 July from inflows of USD269mn in the previous week.

\- Korea's retail investors net sold USD84mn of US portfolio assets between 4 and 10 July, reversing from net buying of USD21mn in the previous week.

\- Taiwan's investors net sold USD253mn of USD-denominated bond funds between 3 and 9 July, following net selling of USD975mn in the previous week.

## Weekly update of high-frequency investor flows

\- Foreign inflows into US-focused equity ETFs and mutual funds $^{[1]}$ totaled USD3.2bn between 3 and 9 July (Figure 3), following inflows of USD3.6bn in the previous week. In MTD July, inflows into these funds totaled USD4.4bn, and compared with USD13.6bn in June 2026 (Figure 2).

\- Foreign inflows into US-focused bond ETFs and mutual funds[2] were largely flat at USD59mn between 3 and 9 July, following outflows of USD1.7bn in the previous week (Figure 5). In MTD July, inflows into funds totaled USD436mn, partly offsetting outflows of USD2.7bn in June 2026 (Figure 4).

\- Foreign inflows into EM-focused ETFs[3] increased to USD514mn between 3 and 9 July, following inflows of USD269mn in the previous week. In MTD July, foreign investors net bought USD1.2bn of EM funds, and compared with inflows of USD3.1bn in June 2026 (Figures 6 and 7).

\- Inflows into equity funds totaled USD307mn between 3 and 9 July, bringing MTD July inflows to USD874mn, and compared with USD2.3bn of inflows in June 2026.

Inflows into bond funds totaled USD208mn between 3 and 9 July, bringing MTD July inflows to USD368mn, and compared with USD771mn of inflows in June 2026.

\- Outflows from Taiwan's exchange-listed USD bond ETFs totaled USD253mn (3 to 9 July), slowing from outflows of USD975mn in the previous week (Figure 9). In MTD July, Taiwanese investors net sold USD253mn of USD-denominated bonds, and compared with USD2.1bn in selling in June 2026 (Figure 8).

\- Korean retail investors net sold only USD84mn of US portfolio assets between 4 and 10 July. This consisted of net selling of around USD58mn of US equities and USD26mn of US bonds (Figures 10 and 11). In MTD July, Korea's retail investors net bought USD228mn of US portfolio assets, and compared with USD466mn of net purchases in June 2026.
