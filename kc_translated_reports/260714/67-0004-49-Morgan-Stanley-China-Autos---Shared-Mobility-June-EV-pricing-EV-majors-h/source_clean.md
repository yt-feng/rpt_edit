# June EV pricing - EV majors held the line with new launches mitigating incumbent discounts
## Key Takeaways

Retail pricing remained broadly stable in June, with blended entry prices largely unchanged across major Chinese EV OEMs.

BYD was the most promotional, particularly on the Han series ahead of the Great Han launch.

New model introductions continued to shape pricing dynamics, lifting blended prices at Li Auto and NIO while prompting selective price adjustments at Geely.

We expect favorable mix of high-trim models will offset promotional push on incumbents, stabilizing overall MSRP in the off season.

BYD blended prices -0.4% MoM: Models were sold at 8-10% discounts to MSRP on average, with the Han series offering up to 20% discounts ahead of the Great Han launch.

Li Auto blended prices flat MoM: L9's discount narrowed to 4% following its facelift, while L6 pricing remained stable ahead of the rollout of the new L6 later this month.

XPeng blended prices -0.2% MoM: Pricing across all models remained broadly stable in June. Discounts to MSRP were generally 3-5% (around Rmb7k).

NIO blended prices +3.5% MoM: The launch of ES9 lifted the blended entry price, while discounts across other models remained broadly unchanged MoM. Discounts of most models were around 3%, while EC7/ET7 reached 12-13%. ES9 deliveries should provide further support for overall pricing in July.

Geely Galaxy blended prices flat MoM: Xingyuan and Panda Mini recorded around 7% retail price increases after the introduction of new trims.
