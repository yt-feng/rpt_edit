## JPM FX Positioning Monitor
## First evidence of yen short covering; USD/CNY downside demand ebbs

\- First signs of yen short-covering. Following Japanese officials' comments about potential GPIF portfolio adjustments, we observed +2.5-sigma net USD/JPY put demand on Friday (Figure 1). In the options space at least, that 1d USD/JPY downside figure screens as the largest such flow since August 2025, just before the resumption of Fed easing in September. While we are skeptical that this may ultimately reverse the structural JPY depreciation trend (see here), this nevertheless represents the first signs of yen-short deleveraging, and more positioning adjustments could follow. Figure 2 shows a distribution of USD/JPY put strikes transacted on Friday, which observed a median of 159.75 and the lower $10^{\text{th}}$ percentile at 156.0.

\- There is still a considerable yen short-base in the market, though it's not stretched. We had recently estimated overall USD/JPY length as high as +1.5-sigma when averaging across options and futures data, which has now fallen to +1.1-sigma (yen shorts in futures were reduced in the week prior to the GPIF news) (Figure 3). That still ranks in the shorter tier of global FX positioning across our metrics (Figure 4), albeit less than EUR & CHF at this juncture.
