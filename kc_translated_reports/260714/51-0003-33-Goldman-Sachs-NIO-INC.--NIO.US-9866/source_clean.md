# Industry-leading growth & premium margin profile with attractive valuation; upgrade to Buy from Neutral

We upgrade Nio to Buy with 12-month DCF-based target prices of US\$7.0/HK\$55, implying 46%/42% upside potential. Among our coverage, we expect Nio to not only deliver one of the fastest volume growth, but also a premium margin profile and strong profit/FCF turn-around in 2026E. This is supported by the company's successful turnaround with the launch of new ES8/ES9 (gaining No.1 position with 39% market share in the Rmb400k+ NEV segment), together with leading brand power (evidenced by top Net Promoter Score), strengthening its position in the premium NEV market, which we believe creates a higher competitive moat and is more difficult to displace by competitors. Looking ahead, we believe the company can apply similar strategy to its 5 series and 6 series models (priced between Rmb200k-400k) and rejuvenate the sales volume of these models in 2027E and beyond, further solidifying its positioning in the premium market and driving continued market share gain.

Despite the domestic NEV market growth of $-14\%$ yoy in 1H26, Nio expanded its volume by $+67\%$ yoy. For 2026E full year, we model volume/revenue growth of $+43\% / +60\%$ yoy, and non-GAAP net profit of Rmb1.6bn (vs. loss of Rmb12.4bn in 2025), we also expect free cash flow to meaningfully improve from Rmb-3.1bn in 2025 to Rmb12.1bn in 2026E. However, shares are down $-6\%$ YTD and $-32\%$ from its recent peak in Apr 2026, which is disconnected from the companies improving fundamentals, in our view. Compared with pure-NEV peers under our coverage, Nio is also trading at a $25\% / 29\%$ discount on 2026E/27E P/S and $17\%$ discount on 2027E P/E, which we see as attractive given its near term product momentum and strong growth. Catalysts include ramp-up for ES8 five-seater version delivery, profit improvement in earnings results.

Where are we different: Our 2026E-2028E earnings estimate are $30\%$ above Visible Alpha consensus (partly due to low base), mainly on higher revenue and lower opex as we believe the company's premium brand power could support more stabilized pricing and more efficient marketing spending going forward.

Estimates and TP changes: We raise our 2026E-2028E earnings estimates by 1%-9%, mainly on higher gross margin from strong sales of ES8/ES9. Accordingly, our 12-month DCF-based target prices increase by 6% to US\$7.0/HK\$55, implying 46%/42% upside potential. We upgrade Nio to Buy (from Neutral). Risks: lower sales volume, exacerbated price competition, higher cost inflation.

## (1) Successful turn-around with ES8/ES9

NIO has started to successfully refresh its product lineup since Sep 2025, highlighted by the launch and delivery of the All-New ES8 with LTM monthly sales volume of 10k+ as of June 2026, ranking No.1 among Rmb400k+ SUV segment. Following the launch of ES9 and L80 in May 2026, Nio delivered 191k units in 1H26 (+67% yoy, vs. industry NEV retail at -14%) and expanded its NEV retail market share to 3.6% in 1H26 (vs. 2.1% in 1H25). As of 1H26, the Nio brand has gained No.1 position with 39% market share in the Rmb400k+ segment.

We observe that Nio has shown consistent capability to launch more competitive models over the past four quarters, based on our product comparison framework, which identifies 4 key consumer purchasing factors, i.e. price, range, size, and autopilot. Based on our product comp analysis among Rmb400k+ top selling NEV SUVs/MPVs, we see Nio's ES8 and ES9 both rank top5 among the best-selling models (esp. ES8 ranking No.1), with competitive strengths in price and size (Exhibit 4).

## (3) Industry leading growth with premium margin profiles

Among our coverage, we expect Nio to not only deliver one of the fastest volume growth, but also a premium margin profile and strong profitability turn-around in 2026E.

Volume: we forecast Nio to achieve 43% volume growth in 2026E (vs. industry NEV domestic retail volume +1% yoy), mainly driven by competitive new model launches with timely delivery ramp-up. Beyond 2026E, we believe the company can continue to gain market share in the domestic market with 19%/11%% volume growth in 2027E/2028E vs. NEV industry growth at 3%/4%, as the company revamps 5 and 6 series models to rejuvenate their sales volume.

Vehicle gross margin: we expect Nio to deliver one of the highest vehicle gross margin at 17% in 2026E (vs. peer average 15%).

Starting from 2025, Nio has developed shared technology platform to reduce BOM cost. For example, it started to adopt in-house NX9031 chip on ET9 and more vehicle models since Apr 2025. It also unified BEV platform, smart driving system and 900V SiC platform across Nio, ONVO, and Firefly brands, which enabled to increase the commonality rate of components. Moreover, Nio enhanced supply chain management by conducting centralized procurement on battery, chips and steel to secure favorable pricing through long-term supply agreements.

Therefore, we believe the company is capable of enhancing its vehicle margin to around 20% for Nio brand, 15% for Onvo and 10% for Firefly. In particular, ES9/ES8 could generate 25%+/20%+ vehicle margin thanks to premium positioning and BOM cost optimization per our estimates (Exhibit 11).

Net profit and free cash flow: we model Nio to achieve non-GAAP net profit of Rmb1.6bn (vs. loss of Rmb12.4bn in 2025), we also expect free cash flow to meaningfully improve from Rmb-3.1bn in 2025 to Rmb12.1bn in 2026E.

Nio has been focusing on cost reduction through a series of cost control and efficiency improvement measures since 2025: (1) terminated projects that cannot generate

positive ROI (return on investment); (2) established vehicle model product line team to integrate R&D resources of the three brands; (3) improved operational efficiency via integrating functions of logistics, quality control and supply-chain with a c.20% total headcount reduction; (4) streamlined mid and back-end sales teams while maintaining front-line sales personnel.

We expect the company to achieve a more efficient SG&A and R&D expense profile post the above efforts, and model a meaningful decline in its SG&A expense to revenue ratio to 11.1% in 2026E (from 18.4% in 2025). At the same time, the company has effectively controlled its R&D expense, and we forecast 7.2% R&D expense to revenue ratio in 2026E (from 12.1% in 2025), lower than peers average at 8.6%.

## Key downside risks:

(1) Lower-than-expected sales volume: if Nio generates lower-than-expected sales volume due to weaker industry demand amidst weak domestic micro environment and lower government subsidy, or supply chain bottlenecks, revenue and gross margin could be lower than our projection;

(2) Exacerbated price competition: if industry price competition becomes worse-than-expected, Nio might need to cut prices or invest in higher promotion expense to ensure sales volume, leading to lower revenue, gross margin, and profitability pressure;

(3) Higher-than-expected cost inflation: if raw material cost grows higher than expected (e.g. lithium, memory), Nio's margin would be negatively impacted, resulting in lower profit.

## Investment Thesis

Nio is one of the first pure EV start-ups in China and has 3 brands in its portfolio: the Nio brand focuses on the premium BEV segment, the Onvo brand focuses on the mid-end family market, and the Firefly brand focuses on the family-oriented niche boutique market. Among our coverage, we expect Nio to not only deliver one of the fastest volume growth, but also a premium margin profile and strong profit/FCF turn-around in 2026E. This is supported by the company's successful turn-around with the launch of new ES8/ES9 (gaining No.1 position with $39\%$ market share in the Rmb400k+ NEV segment as of 1H26), together with leading brand power (evidenced by top Net Promoter Score), strengthening its position in the premium NEV market, which we believe creates a higher competitive moat and is more difficult to displace by competitors. Looking ahead, we believe the company can apply similar strategy to its 5 series and 6 series models (priced between Rmb200k-400k) and rejuvenate the sales volume of these models in 2027E and beyond, further solidifying its positioning in the premium market and driving continued market share gain. We are Buy rated (ADR/H share). Nio is trading at a discount (P/S) to the peer group, which we see as attractive given its near term product momentum and strong growth. Key risks include lower-than-expected sales volume, more-than-expected price cuts, higher-than-expected cost inflation.
