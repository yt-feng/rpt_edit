# META's 2Q26 earnings read through
## Quick Note

META (META US, Not rated) reported its 2Q26 earnings on 30 July 2026 after the US market close, and on the same day, management hosted an analyst briefing. Management mainly discussed its AI products and services development, measures on increasing monetization efficiency and improving engagement experiences. The company's FY26E capex guidance remained largely unchanged at USD130-145bn (previously USD125-145bn), while management did not provide much color on FY27E capex guidance. Regarding the monetization of computing power, management believes that the margin on selling Intelligence will continue to be significantly higher than that of selling Compute directly. We think this indicates the company's continued commitment to investing in large language models and Generative AI applications. We like global optical transceiver leader Zhongji Innolight (300308 CH, Buy), and major PCB / CCL suppliers such as Shengyi Tech (600183 CH, Buy).

## 2Q26 core business performance

\- META reported 28.0% y-y revenue growth for 2Q26, beating the Bloomberg consensus estimate by 1%, and delivered a 13.6% y-y decline in earnings, missing the consensus by 14%. For 3Q26E, management guided for USD61-64bn in total revenue, with foreign currency providing \~1% headwind to y-y growth.

\- Reality Labs segment revenue was USD431mn in 2Q26, up 16% y-y due to strong growth in AI Glasses revenue, partially offset by lower Quest Headsets sales.

\- Capex (purchase of PP&E) increased 82.1% y-y and 58.5% q-q in 2Q26 to USD30.1bn, while capex including principal payments for finance leases was USD31.1bn, driven by investments in servers, data centers, and network infrastructure.

\- Management expects 2026E capex including principal payments for finance leases to be in the range of USD130-145bn, narrowing from its prior estimate of USD125-145bn.

## AI development and monetization progress

\- Meta is developing new personal agents that will be the foundation for its next wave of products and revenue lines.

\- On a dollar basis, Meta's ads business is reporting faster y-y revenue growth than any other company's reported ad business, according to management.

\- Nine million small businesses on platforms are now using at least one of Meta's AI ad creative tools.

\- The company has launched Meta One, a new subscription offering that provides more tools and AI features across apps. As demand grows, management plans to offer a variety of different tiers and pricing options.

\- Meta also announced a new strategic venture with BlackRock (unlisted) to develop a new 1 gigawatt data center in El Paso, Texas.

\- Meta has deployed its first generative model into its ads retrieval system, and has seen notable improvements in ads performance.

\- Advantage+ end-to-end solutions reached over USD75bn ARR this quarter.
