# TEL 1Q27/3: Margin upside materializing sooner; Lifting PT to ¥79,300

In-line Q1. Revenue of ¥732.4bn (+2.9% QoQ) came below consensus as we previewed. Although, GPM at 46.8% and OPM at 28.9% were above consensus. OP came in at ¥211.4bn (+2.8% QoQ), in-line with consensus. By application, logic / foundry was a 10% miss; DRAM was 9% below, NAND was in-line. Raised Q2 guidance beat, with both topline 8% above and OP 6% above consensus.

CY27 WFE raised to \$190bn. TEL provided an improved market outlook of \$190bn (\$170bn prior) for 2027 WFE, citing AI as the major growth driver but this time with an additional mention of conventional DRAM and CPUs. TEL continues to see \$150bn for CY26. By application, company expects YoY growth of 15% for logic / foundry, >30% for DRAM, and 40% for NAND in CY26, and \~20% overall.

TEL expects to outperform the market — 1H sales is guided to be 37% YoY, and 2H is “expected to outgrow 1H significantly” — implying annual growth of above 37%. TEL continues to expect >50% growth from coater & developers, as well as etchers — for which they expect up to ¥1tn annual revenue — and advanced packaging, led by probers, to drive the outperformance. On capacity, CEO highlighted their robust supply chain management as well as their strong relationship with customers who can provide enough visibility for TEL to prepare well in advance, as their strength.

50%+ margins next year. Management confirmed that margin uplift from price hike will start to kick in from Q4, and that they will be able to target 50%+ GPM early next FY, ahead of investor expectations of FY29/3. There is further upside beyond 50%+ GM and 35%+ OPM. In terms of OP, the CEO said they will be able to target near ¥1tn this FY.
Outperform. Reflecting Q1 earnings and an improved outlook on next year's growth as well as margins, we lift our FY27/28 top line by 6% / 20% respectively, and bottom line by 12% / 43%. Lowering our valuation multiple to 28x (32x prior) P/E and rolling over a quarter, we raise our PT to ¥79,300, representing 52% implied upside. Reiterate Outperform.


Source: Bloomberg, Bernstein estimates and analysis.
