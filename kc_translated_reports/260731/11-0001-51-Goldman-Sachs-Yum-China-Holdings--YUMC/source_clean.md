# Yum China Holdings (YUMC)
# Earnings Review: Resilience amid tough macro; New initiatives progress encouraging; Buy (on CL)


The key takeaways from YUMC's solid 2Q26 results earnings call reaffirmed our positive view. Despite a fluid consumption backdrop, YUMC delivered SSSG improvement in 2Q26 and the initial tone for 3Q also shows confidence, ahead of investors' cautious view prior to the results given weak consumption sentiment, unfavorable weather conditions, and a high base last year. Aside from its solid execution track record, new formats/modules (i.e. K coffee Cafe, K Pro, Pizza Hut Burger Bar etc) have delivered solid performance and contributed to the parent stores, opening up new revenue streams; YUMC has further accelerated the new format roll-out. Mgmt comments on the rising chain ratio in the catering industry and disciplined promotion/pricing in the market are also encouraging. Looking ahead, we see solid catalysts from an easier base for margin in 2H on the back of less expense pressure from delivery mix increase. Separately, mgmt noted upside potential from stronger Pizza Hut growth and improved profitability following the acquisition (on track in August 2026, per mgmt). Remain Buy (on CL) with new TP at US\$59/HK\$459 (prior: US\$58/HK\$452).

Key highlights from the earnings call include: 1) Looking into 3Q26, management targets positive SSSG and noted that Jul performance has been broadly in line with their expectation; restaurant margin is expected to be stable or slightly up yoy with less headwind from delivery mix yoy increase and continuous cost optimization/efficiency gain, while OPM is expected to be stable or slightly improved yoy though there will be less benefit yoy from government subsidy (per mgmt, they are not including the PH acquisition impact, which they expect will bring 20-30bps OPM benefit in 3Q, in their guidance). Management reiterated confidence in meeting full year guidance without considering the impact from the PH brand acquisition (expected to close in Aug per mgmt), which they expect would have a slightly accretive margin impact this year.

## Key takeaways from the conference call

Recent trends: Mgmt noted that July trading was broadly in line with expectations despite temporary regional disruptions from extreme weather. Consumer demand remained resilient in coffee, light meals, innovative products and experiences. Pricing trends continued to stabilize, and competition among delivery platforms became more rational. Mgmt noted room for further consolidation by leading brands thanks to the ghost kitchen regulation and outperformance of chain brands vs nationwide retail sales growth.

## Outlook on FY26/2H26/3Q26/after PH brand deal/longer-term:

The company reaffirmed its 2026 full-year targets excluding the PH brand deal, including an SSSG of 0-2%, MSD-HSD% system sales growth, HSD% OP growth, DD% EPS growth, and slight improvement in both restaurant margin and OPM. The company also remains on track to reach 20k stores by year-end.

In 3Q26, Mgmt targets positive SSSG and transaction growth. Excluding the PH deal impact, mgmt expects restaurant margin to be stable to slightly positive yoy, as incremental rider-cost pressure moderates. OPM is expected to be broadly flat or slightly improved yoy, partly due to the non-recurrence of government subsidies recorded in 3Q25.

In 2H26, for PH, mgmt expects restaurant margin to show greater yoy improvement than in 1H26, driven by improved efficiency and moderating rider-cost pressure, broadly stable COGS and continued efficiency improvements. Mgmt maintained its FY26 PH COGS guidance of c.34% and the long-term target of \~31% (+/-1%).

Update on the PH brand deal: Mgmt noted the transaction remains on track to close in Aug. Per mgmt, savings from the 3% PH license fee are expected to add c.2.8ppt to PH restaurant margin (after VAT) and c.60bps to Yum China's margin. Mgmt expects the deal should contribute c.30-40bps to restaurant margin and OPM in 3Q26 and c.20-30bps for FY26 after deal costs, financing expenses and tax; they expect EPS impact to be slightly accretive in 2026 and MSD% accretive in 2027-28. Mgmt expects PH net openings to exceed 800 annually in both 2027 and 2028, versus the previous target of over 600.

## Pizza Hut brand acquisition:

Financing: The Company is on track to close the acquisition of the Pizza Hut brand in Mainland China in August and plans to secure around \$1.2 billion equivalent offshore bridge loan to finance the transaction, which carries \~2% interest rate. Per mgmt, longer-term financing options remain preliminary and may include syndicated loans, bonds, or convertible bonds. If convertible bonds are used, the group is committed to minimizing potential dilution, including capped calls with high premium conversion and net share settlement.

Strategical benefits: Mgmt expects savings in license fees will bring Pizza Hut's restaurant margin closer to KFC's level, enabling more new stores to meet the 2-to-3-year payback requirement. With the improved margin profile, mgmt uplifted the store expansion target. In longer term, mgmt expects the brand's ownership to provide greater operational flexibility, such as product and menu innovation.

## New initiatives' progress

KCoffee cafe: Store count reached 3,300 in 2Q26, adding \~700 from the prior quarter. Management aims to double sales yoy to Rmb 2bn in 2026 and continues to target 5k stores by end of 2027. KCoffee Cafe continued to deliver MSD% sales uplift to the parent KFC stores.

Kpro: Store count reached 450+ from 280 in the prior quarter, with \~20% sales uplift to the company store. Management raised targets to 800 store count (up from 600) by year-end. KPRO is delivering \~20% sales uplift to parent KFC stores, showing solid margin improvement, and expanding across Tier 1, Tier 2, and select lower-tier cities. Management expects KPRO sales to quadruple year over year in 2026 and exceed RMB 1bn in 2027.

KFC drive-thru and car-side pickup: The Company has >8,000 KFC drive-thru locations, but active member penetration is only c.3%, leaving significant room for adoption growth. Management targets RMB 1bn in sales from this channel in 2026.

■ PH burger bar: Pizza Hut Burger Bar has been well-received. Within roughly six months, it expanded to 200+ locations and generated DD% incremental sales uplift to parent Pizza Hut stores, with meaningful profit contribution (though initial COS is higher and margin lower than brand's average). Management plans to accelerate it to 500-600 locations by the end of 2026, equivalent to c.10% of the Pizza Hut store base, and targets more than RMB 1bn in sales this year, or c.5-6% of Pizza Hut sales. Management stated that burger sales appear incremental to the group, with the format offering customer choice and differentiated product attributes versus KFC.

■ PH Wow: Pizza Hut Wow stores continue to help the brand penetrate lower-tier cities with store count reaching >400.

Comments on SSSG and store expansion pace: Mgmt noted faster store expansion does not necessarily lead to lower SSSG, citing countermeasures including discovering untapped white space opportunities, strategic channels, prime areas in high tier cities, etc.

Pricing and competition: Mgmt observed continued stabilization in industry pricing, with more players willing to take pricing actions and delivery-platform competition becoming more rational. KFC avg. ticket declined 3% yoy to Rmb36, mainly due to incremental smaller orders from KCoffee, Kpro and other new customer occasions, while transactions grew 4%. PH avg. ticket declined 11% to Rmb68, approaching its Rmb60-70 target range under the mass-market strategy, while transactions increased 13%. Mgmt continues to prioritize traffic, value for money and incremental occasions over headline ticket growth.

Shareholder returns: The company reiterates its plan to return \$1.5bn to shareholders in 2026. Starting in 2027, the group reiterates that it will return c. 100% of annual free cash flow after dividends to non-controlling interests, implying \$900 million to over \$1 billion in 2027 and 2028, and to exceed \$1 billion in 2028. In addition, mgmt noted upside potential from stronger Pizza Hut growth and improved profitability following the acquisition.

## Price Target Risks and Methodology - Yum China Holdings

Valuation methodology: We are Buy rated on YUMC with 12-m SOTP-based target prices of US\$59/HK\$459 for the ADR/H-share. Our target multiples are 11x 2026E EV/EBITDA for KFC China and 7x for Pizza Hut China.

Key downside risks: 1) weaker-than-expected SSSG; 2) higher-than-expected commodity costs; 3) stronger-than-expected competition; and 4) lower-than-expected execution efficiency.
