## Zijin Gold International - H
## Allied Gold dust settled; organic ramp up to support growth until further acquisitions

Allied Gold and Zijin Gold announced the termination of Zijin Gold's acquisition of Allied Gold on 29 Jul (link), with Zijin Gold instead agreeing to subscribe for \~US\$295mn of Allied shares, representing an expected \~9.2% stake upon completion. We believe the risk of non-completion has already been largely reflected in the share price (underperforming gold peers by \~20% since May). Zijin Gold's valuation is inexpensive in our view even after removing Allied Gold's contribution and marking to market gold price at \~13x FY27E P/E. We therefore see the termination of the deal as an overhang removal. Excluding Allied, we still expect Zijin Gold's organic mine growth to support production growth from 59.2t in 2026, including Porgera, to 70-75t in 2028. We see increasing chance for further M&A with gold prices pulling back, and Zijin Gold is still intact to achieve its longer-term 100t production target in 2030. Maintain OW.

\- Termination of acquisition. Zijin Gold and Allied Gold announced that the agreement for Zijin Gold to acquire Allied Gold has been terminated, as both parties concluded there was no reasonable likelihood that the closing conditions would be fulfilled by the July 29, 2026 outside date or within any reasonable time thereafter. The companies cited broader external factors applicable to cross-border transactions of this scale. Concurrently, Zijin Gold agreed to subscribe for \~12.8mn Allied shares at C\$32.55/share, representing aggregate proceeds of \~US\$295mn, or \~C\$417mn, and implying a \~9.2% stake upon completion. Closing of the strategic investment is expected on or around August 10, 2026, subject to TSX and NYSE approvals, with customary participation and top-up rights.

\- Organic ramp up to reach 2028 target. Removing Allied Gold's four mines from our forecast, we now expect Zijin Gold's 2026–28E gold production to be 59t / 64t / 73t. As a result, we revise down our FY26–28E earnings forecasts by 12-32% and cut our Dec-27 PT to HK\$146, implying 16.5x FY27E P/E. Although we lower our earnings forecasts after removing Allied's contribution, we maintain OW and remain constructive on Zijin Gold's longer-term growth outlook. Organic ramp-up remains on track and valuation is still undemanding, in our view. Rate hike expectations and gold prices are stronger share price drivers until future M&A announcements as the company works toward its longer-term production targets.

## Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation

## Investment Thesis

Zijin Gold, a subsidiary of Zijin Mining, has established a strong growth platform through acquisitions and reserve expansion, achieving a 15% CAGR in gold production from 2023 to 2025. Operating nine mines across four continents, the company is expanding capacity at key sites, with gold production target of 70-75t in 2028. Zijin Gold's disciplined acquisition strategy drives improving capital efficiency, with ROLTA reaching 29% in 2025 (vs. 12% in 2024). With a favorable gold price outlook and robust growth pipeline, we forecast earnings to grow at a 33% CAGR from 2025 to 2028. We have an OW rating and Dec-2027 PT of HK\$146.

## Valuation

Our Dec 2027 price target of HK\$146 is based on a DCF valuation with a WACC of 8.5% and a terminal growth rate of 2.5%. Our PT implies a FY27E P/E multiple of 16.5x and a FY27E EV/EBITDA multiple of 9x.

# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

Zijin Gold International - H (Overweight; Price Target: HK\$146.00)

## Investment Thesis

Zijin Gold, a subsidiary of Zijin Mining, has established a strong growth platform through acquisitions and reserve expansion, achieving a 15% CAGR in gold production from 2023 to 2025. Operating nine mines across four continents, the company is expanding capacity at key sites, with gold production target of 70-75t in 2028. Zijin Gold's disciplined acquisition strategy drives improving capital efficiency, with ROLTA reaching 29% in 2025 (vs. 12% in 2024). With a favorable gold price outlook and robust growth pipeline, we forecast earnings to grow at a 33% CAGR from 2025 to 2028. We have an OW rating and Dec-2027 PT of HK\$146.

## Valuation

Our Dec 2027 price target of HK\$146 is based on a DCF valuation with a WACC of 8.5% and a terminal growth rate of 2.5%. Our PT implies a FY27E P/E multiple of 16.5x and a FY27E EV/EBITDA multiple of 9x.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Key downside risks to our rating and price target include: 1) gold price volatility; 2) operational risks arising from exposure to multiple foreign jurisdictions, project construction delays, and illegal mining activities at the Buriticá gold mine in Colombia; and 3) lower-than-expected realized prices for mineral products.
