# Equinix Inc. (EQIX)
# 2Q26 Review: Beat & raise with upgrade to L/T targets on expansion plans

EQIX's 2Q26 EBITDA of \$1.396 bn beat GS/consensus of \$1.365/\$1.362 bn with AFFO per share of \$11.78 beating GS/consensus of \$11.46/\$11.36. The beat was driven by revenue and margins with revenue of \$2.625 bn beating GS/consensus of \$2.606/\$2.591 bn on higher than-expected non-recurring revenue (recurring revenue missed) and a beat on EBITDA margins (53.2% v. GS/consensus of 52.4%/52.6%). Annualized bookings of \$424 mn fell between GS/consensus of \$454/\$404 mn.

EQIX raised its 2026E revenue, EBITDA, and AFFO per share guidance by about 1% and raised its 2027-29 outlook to imply 2029E AFFO per share of \$58 at the midpoint (v. consensus of \$56.47/\$56.20). The improved long-term outlook is underpinned by strong underlying trends and increased capacity expansion with \$5-\$7 bn annual capex (85% expansionary) up from \$3-\$4 bn prior. Importantly, EQIX continues to see 25% cash-on-cash returns after stabilization on these retail colocation datacenters and the company outlined credibility to execution including a 3GW pipeline with a timeline to power that aligns with its financial plan, as well as a land acquisition strategy that involves smaller parcels of land (60+MW) primarily in its top 25 markets.

EQIX is well positioned from growing retail colocation and interconnection demand that will likely be driven by growing enterprise AI and inference workloads, which we believe remains in its early stages of growth.

Key upside risks: (1) incremental supply tightness in the data center market; (2) stronger-than-expected demand dynamics for Equinix's metro-focused footprint; (3) Incremental chip and power efficiency innovation could increase demand for less dense infrastructure.

Key downside risks: (1) excess supply-side dynamics in the data center market; (2) weaker-than-expected demand dynamics from key customers; (3) obsolescence risk for existing data centers; (4) more intense price competition from outsourced data center providers.
