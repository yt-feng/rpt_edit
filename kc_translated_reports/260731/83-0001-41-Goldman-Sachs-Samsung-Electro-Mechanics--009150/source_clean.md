# Samsung Electro-Mechanics (009150.KS)

# Earnings Review: 2Q26 OP beat; Tight supply and strong AI demand continued tailwinds for strong MLCC and ABF earnings growth; Buy

009150.KS 12m Price Target: W2,250,000 Price: W879,000 Upside: 156.0%

Samsung Electro-Mechanics (SEMCO) reported 2Q26 revenue of W3.5tn and operating profit of W440bn, where OP was above vs. GSe of W418bn and Bloomberg consensus of W421bn. We believe the stronger than expected MLCC and camera module profit led to the beat. Despite the recent share price correction, we believe fundamentals for key businesses (MLCC and FC-BGA) remain solid and continue to expect tight supply and solid AI server demand to lead to strong earnings growth, with higher earnings visibility supported by global big tech companies' strong request for LTA. Reiterate Buy with an updated TP of W2.25mn.

## Key takeaways

\- Favorable MLCC S/D outlook and more positive tone on pricing: MLCC revenue showed strong growth (+19% qoq and +44% yoy) due to solid demand for AI DC (server/power/network) and auto (ADAS/xEV), and we believe OPM expanded meaningfully to high-teens% level driven by higher AI/auto mix. The company expects strong AI server MLCC demand growth (the company expects 1005 47μF product demand to increase by 7x next year) and tighter MLCC supply going forward; hence the company mentioned it will respond more actively on pricing, which we view as an incrementally more positive tone on pricing.

■ Management guides strong FC-BGA revenue growth in 2H: We believe FC-BGA revenue showed solid growth (GSe: +9% qoq and +60% yoy) on the back of strong AI server demand and new customer addition. For 2H26, management commented it expects strong FC-BGA revenue growth as the company expects demand for AI server to remain strong on the back of customers' new product launch and plans to strategically respond to pricing to reflect tightening supply condition and higher raw material

■ Multiple global CSPs and semiconductor companies seeking to sign LTA for MLCC and FC-BGA: SEMCO said it has signed around 10 LTAs on MLCC with global CSPs and semiconductor companies and is actively responding to additional LTA requests. We believe MLCC LTAs are typically 1-yr terms (as shown in this note) with fixed pricing which increases earnings visibility and provides room for upside if supply shortage persists for multiple years. For FC-BGA, the company said it is currently in LTA discussions with most key AI and datacenter players in the form of capex sponsorship and plans to expand capacity based on these agreements. As SEMCO FC-BGA UTR is expected to reach full level in 2H26, we expect timely capacity expansion to be critical to sustain solid FC-BGA revenue growth.

Management reiterated strong confidence on forward earnings growth: Management expects tighter supply, strong AI server demand, and increasing ASP trend to lead to strong earnings growth on both a qoq and yoy basis in 3Q26 and strong earnings trend to continue in 4Q26 and 2027. In-line with the management's guidance, we raise our 3Q26 OP expectation to W600bn (from W529bn) which represents $36\%$ qoq growth and $131\%$ yoy growth.

## Tweak earnings estimates and lower TP to W2.25mn; reiterate Buy rating

We tweak our 2026E/2027E/2028E EPS estimate from W19,853/W44,830/W73,583 to W19,924/W42,116/W70,172 mainly reflecting lower BGA and camera module margin estimates. We also refresh our target multiples (28.1x to 27.3x for Substrate Solution, 26.4x to 24.0x for Component Solution, and 11.5x to 10.0x for Module Solution) to reflect changes in peer valuation. As a result, we lower our 12m TP to W2.25mn (from W2.6mn) and reiterate Buy. Key downside risks include a stronger-than-expected industry MLCC supply increase and weaker-than-expected demand for AI server/auto/smartphones.

## Investment Thesis - Samsung Electro-Mechanics

Samsung Electro-Mechanics (SEMCO) is a leading global electronic component supplier with the second-largest market share in multi-layer ceramic capacitor (MLCC). We expect the 1) solid MLCC demand from AI server and auto and 2) strong FC-BGA growth driven by AI customer expansion will lead to solid OP yoy growth and are Buy rated on the name.
