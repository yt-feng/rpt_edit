## FCC targets new Chinese inverter imports; maintain OW as negative already priced in

On July 28 $^{th}$ , the FCC said it would block imports of new Chinese humanoid/quadruped robots and connected power inverters. While this is not entirely new, the scope (covering both inverter and ESS PCS products and both utility-scale and off-grid applications) trends towards the bearish side. In-line with market expectations, the ban would affect new models, where previously FCC approved models can still be sold to the U.S. We see negative earnings implications to be more mid-term. Another key development will be the legal interpretation on “remote connectivity”. Per Sungrow, its products do not have wireless communication capability and are connected to its customer control unit via a physical cable. Potentially, Sungrow could apply for a conditional approval to secure FCC authorizations for new models through an individualized risk assessment (via DoW/DHS) but the hurdles could be high, in our view.

We estimate the U.S. was $\sim 20 - 25\%$ of Sungrow's 2025 gross profit; a full U.S. exit could imply $\sim 20 - 25\%$ earnings downside (assuming Sungrow will rationalize its operations). After $\sim 25\%$ underperformance vs. SHCOMP since Jun30th, Sungrow trades at $\sim 12x$ FY27E P/E (or $\sim 15x$ excluding U.S. exposure) and a 26/27 ex-US ESS installation CAGR of $\sim 40\%$ , we see valuation as compensating for the risk and maintain OW. That said, this development also caps Sungrow's re-rating potential from the AIDC ESS theme. Within China ESS integrators, we also flag Deye as a cleaner EM DG ESS growth exposure.

\- Event summary: On July 28 $^{th}$ , the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) released that they would block imports of new Chinese humanoid and quadruped robots, as well as connected power inverters, which are devices that allow renewable energy sources and batteries to connect to the grid and to data center equipment (link).

\- Our analysis and conclusion: This is not entirely new to the market, as news reports have suggested a possible FCC rule being considered that could restrict new foreign inverter imports to the US on June 30 $^{th}$ (link to our report). That said, the scope (covering both inverter and ESS PCS, both grid-scale and behind-the-meter applications) is coming in towards the more stringent side of things and we take this as a negative (though not entirely unexpected outcome). An important element is whether “remote connectivity” is defined broadly enough to capture its new inverter models; if so, those models may no longer be sold in the U.S. We believe existing, already-authorized models are likely unaffected for now, which would push any earnings impact out by a few years.

\- How to apply for exemption: Per the FCC, there is a potential that the Department of War (DoW) or the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) may grant “Conditional Approval”. Inverter manufacturers may seek conditional approval through an individualized risk assessment, while DoW/DHS may request more information. Note that applications must be submitted by 1 $^{st}$ Jan 2028, with the required information including (1) corporate structure, i.e. legal name, jurisdiction of incorporation, ownership structure, board members and executive leadership, etc.; (2) manufacturing and supply chain disclosure, i.e. bill of materials, country of origin of every component, etc.; (3) US manufacturing and onshoring plan, i.e. a detailed, time-bound plan to establish US manufacturing so the device can qualify for FCC authorization, etc.

# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

Sungrow - A (Overweight; Price Target: Rmb212.00)

## Investment Thesis

Sungrow is the world's largest solar inverter producer (by production volume), with a market share of $\sim 30\%$ as of FY24. Sungrow has gained market share in recent years because of its cost advantage. Relative to other domestic producers, Sungrow has superior product quality and more established brand awareness, in our view. We rate Sungrow OW as we expect it to ride on strong ESS demand, on the back of rising order potential from both utilities and data centers.

## Valuation

Our Dec-26 PT of Rmb212 equates to a 12-month forward target P/E of 23.0x. We use SOTP valuations for its different segments. Our target multiple is -0.1 stdev vs. the historical average.
