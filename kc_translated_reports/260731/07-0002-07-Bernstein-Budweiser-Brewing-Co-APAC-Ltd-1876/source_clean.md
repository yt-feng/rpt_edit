# Bud APAC Q2: China remains in investment mode as management strive to stabilize the volume decline

Bud APAC's Q2 came in broadly in line down to cc EBITDA and beat expectations at the bottom line due to lower ETR, primarily. Q2 cc revenues were down 2.1%, cc was EBITDA -9.7%, and Normalized Net Income was up 3.3%. China disappointed across the P&L while Korea margins, in particular, materially exceeded expectations. We have cut our 2026 APAC West EBITDA growth estimate to -8.0% (from -4.5%), and have raised our APAC East cc EBITDA growth to +5.7% (from +2.3%), resulting in an overall 1.9% cut to our 2026 cc EBITDA est. With management flagging continued China on-trade weakness, elevated commercial investment requirements and raw material headwinds, we have also cut our 2027 cc EBITDA estimate by 6.3% to reflect this.

China volumes declined 10% in Q2, coming in materially worse than expected. While DD Instant Retail growth and strong Super Premium performance were a bright spot and mitigated the volume weakness somewhat, cc revenue was still -8.6%. Continued investment behind the in-home RTM expansion strategy and operational deleverage drove APAC West cc EBITDA down 16.5% in Q2. We expect to see a further 100bps of APAC West margin contraction in H2 and, although China comps get easier in H2, it's hard to see Bud China materially closing the trajectory gap vs peers.

## Investment Implications

APAC East EBITDA was a big beat at +26% yoy in Q2. Revenue was +9%, and pricing + operational leverage drove 413bps of margin expansion. We expect the Korea trajectory to normalize in H2 now that the 2025 price increase impact is full cycled.
We maintain our Market-Perform rating and cut our PT to HK\$7.00 from \$7.30 (model). We have cut our FY27 EPS by 6% to reflect the persistent China weak performance and raw material headwinds. We maintain our P/E multiple target at 17.5x.

## SEGMENTAL PERFORMANCE DETAILS

China volume declines deteriorated to $-9.7\%$ this Q from $-1.5\%$ in Q1. Bud China underperformed peers on top of the soft industry impacted by adverse weather, and on-premise channel still remains under pressure (China Beer June update). However, price mix became positive (+1.2%) in Q2 mainly driven by Super Premium strong momentum, and in-home momentum was

healthy with new packs launched for Corona. India continued market share gain and delivered DD revenue growth in Q2. Regional margins were down 381bps mainly due to China's operational deleverage and India's one-off commercial investment was also a drag. Government grants dropped to "close to zero" this time and will stay at the level according to the company.

Korea volumes increased by low-teens, cycling an easier comp due to shipment phasing ahead of the price increase in last April. Regional margins were up 413bps mainly driven by operational leverage and positive pack mix in in-home channel is also a tailwind.
