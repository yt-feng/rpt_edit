## Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation

## Investment Thesis

Infineon is exposed to multiple secular trends including AI power, software defined vehicles and ADAS where it is a market leader. In its reported results, Infineon has now indicated improving trends in its core automotive business ( $\sim 50\%$ of revenue). Infineon is also a major beneficiary of power semiconductor growth due to AI power, where it is a leading supplier. The strength in MOSFETs due to AI power demand is helping pricing for MOSFETs in non AI markets and this trend will be accretive to Infineon margins. Overall, with the cycle improving and with the company's strong AI power position, Infineon should see estimate upgrades over the next few quarters in our opinion.

## Valuation

Our Dec-27 price target of €96 is based on a 24x our FY28 EPS estimates. Infineon has traded between 15.6x and 23.0x its forward earnings in the past 10 years (inter-quartile range), with an average multiple of 19.8x. Given the company's leading position in AI power, which is an increasingly larger part of the business, and structural growth in power infrastructure (which should inflect towards the end of '27), the stock should trade at a premium to this historical range and thus we see 24x forward earnings as a fair multiple.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Key risks that could keep our rating and target price from being achieved include:

\- Macro: If the global economy slips into recession, the target would prove too optimistic.

\- Competitive pressures in a weak environment could mean weaker profitability than forecast.

\- Sudden shift from a currency tailwind to a headwind would impact margins and sales negatively, and vice versa.
