# Our Thoughts after Recent Industry Underperformance

The recent sharp decline in developer stocks exceeded our expectations, even though we have called for industry weakness. Current trough valuation may be appealing to long-term investors, but we see no near-term catalysts and expect the sluggish outlook to continue to weigh on industry performance.

## Key Takeaways

We attribute the $>30\%$ pullback in industry share prices since mid-May (vs. $-13\%$ for the HSI) to fast cooling in home sales and fund flows to AI-related sectors.

Current record-low P/Bs may suit value investment, but sales/earnings headwinds and limited policy upside may put further near-term pressure on the stocks.

We reiterate our cautious view on physical market recovery and expect slightly faster m-m home price declines in 3Q amid further slowdown in property sales.

We continue to favor quality plays with credible self-help alpha. Relatively, developers with robust mall rentals offer better risk-reward at current levels.

Near-term headwinds: We see 1) potential further weakening of property sales in 3Q (secondary home sales in major cities may turn negative y-y); and 2) potentially weaker-than-expected 1H26 results, with the majority of developers posting earnings declines/losses owing to reduced booking and margin compression (developers usually start to provide guidance in mid-July). Also, considering the strong sales rebound and milder decline in home prices in March-May, as well as the K-shaped macro backdrop, we foresee limited incremental housing policies in the July Politburo meeting.

Stay selective with quality alpha plays: Given the increasing uncertainty around sales recovery and potentially continued fund flow disruption, we suggest staying prudent and waiting for better entry points, despite industry P/B valuation (0.30x) has fallen back to historical trough levels. We continue to favor quality players with credible self-help alpha.

Relatively, we think CR Land (1109.HK, Top Pick), C&D (1908.HK), and Seazen (601155.SS/1030.HK) offer more alluring risk-reward at current valuation. They all have solid EPS outlooks and medium-term re-rating potential, even with no notable physical market recovery. In contrast, we stay bearish on Gemdale (600383.SS) and Vanke (000002.SS/2202.HK) given their depleted landbank.
