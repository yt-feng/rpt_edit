# May Revenue Wrap – AI Momentum Persists
## Key Takeaways

■ May revenue for Taiwan's tech companies grew 4% MoM and 39% YoY, reflecting strong AI demand offsetting the seasonal slowdown in consumer electronics.
Data center hardware and smartphone supply chain outpaced our expectations, while IC foundry fell short.
Taiwan's tech stock prices and TAIEX have both risen 11% since May 4.
We prefer stocks with healthy order visibility or leading positions: MediaTek, Macronix, Delta, Bizlink, Accton, Wiwynn, Yageo and Zhen Ding.
