## Autos Sector

China auto sales (May): Moment of truth for ICE autos this summer; NEV shift continues, despite reversal of price gap with ICE

China's passenger vehicle retail sales continued to fall in May, but exports hit a new record high for the third straight month, breaking above 800k units for the month. The shift to new energy vehicles (NEV) was evident in domestic demand, with the NEV penetration rate rising 160bp MoM to $63\%$ . In response to soaring export demand, major Chinese manufacturers are extending delivery dates and raising prices on some NEV models. On the other hand, popular internal combustion engine (ICE) models dropped out of the top sales rankings, despite deeper discounts. We assume that ICE auto sales will fall this summer because of seasonal factors, but with the number of used cars (mainly ICE autos) with zero recorded mileage rising again, declines in new vehicle prices and sales volumes could become more of a concern.

- Domestic NEV penetration accelerates, price gap with ICE autos reverses: China's May retail sales of passenger vehicles amounted to 1,528k units (–22% YoY), down YoY for the eighth consecutive month. Whereas retail sales were 579k units (–38% YoY) for ICE autos, demand for NEVs was solid at 849k units (–7% YoY), and the penetration rate rose from 61% in April to 63% in May. Major Chinese OEMs have hiked NEV prices, mainly reflecting intelligent vehicle systems, whereas ICE automakers have lowered prices substantially. In the entry segment at CNY100,000 (about ¥2.3 million) and below in particular, popular NEVs are more expensive than ICE autos, but consumers continue to shift to NEVs.
- ICE autos set to struggle this summer: Among companies that have disclosed May retail sales in China, Toyota Motor (102k units, -29% YoY) and Honda Motor (28k units, -49% YoY) both reported continued YoY declines. In seasonal terms, ICE auto sales momentum may fall through the summer, but we estimate that conditions for sales will be even tougher than usual this year. The number of used cars (mainly ICE autos) with zero recorded mileage is currently rising, and we see a risk that this will depress new vehicle prices and sales volume still further. As a knock-on effect, ICE resale values have fallen over the past few months, and the advantage over NEVs is diminishing.
- Surging export demand: Passenger vehicle exports reached an all-time high for the third consecutive month, at 809k units (+73% YoY), driven by NEVs at 435k units (+110% YoY), though we should not overlook the net increase in ICE autos at 374k units (+42% YoY). Production capacity for BYD and other popular makes is reaching its limits, and delivery times are lengthening both in China and overseas. Brazil and Australia have emerged as key destinations for Chinese automobile exports, in addition to Europe and ASEAN.
