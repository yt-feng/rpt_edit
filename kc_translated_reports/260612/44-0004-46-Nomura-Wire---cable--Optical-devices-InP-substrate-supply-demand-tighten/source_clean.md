# Wire & cable: Optical devices
# InP substrate supply-demand tightens further on buoyant demand
## Quick Note

Major optical device producer Lumentum Holdings held a briefing for investors on 9 June at which it discussed supply-demand conditions for indium phosphide (InP) substrates, a key input for optical devices. The company had previously said at its results briefing on 5 May that supply-demand for InP substrates was tight, but that long-term contracts should ensure stable procurement. However, demand for optical devices has been rising further since the results briefing in May, and it has now said that it will need to find alternative suppliers for InP substrates. We think demand for optical devices has been strengthening further recently.

Sumitomo Electric Industries [5802] (Buy) has the world's largest production capacity for InP substrates, and manufactures optical devices such as continuous-wave laser diodes (CW-LD) and electro-absorption modulator integrated laser (EML) chips, some of which it sells to third parties. We think that its advantages in terms of supply of core materials will enable it to increase production capacity for optical devices more flexibly than rivals such as Lumentum Holdings, and that it will also be able to benefit from high margins. We expect these advantages to increase further (see our 8 June 2026 report Sumitomo Electric Industries (5802 JP) (Buy) - Revising our forecasts: We raise our outlook for infocommunications segment further).

Lumentum Holdings has also mentioned that it intends to increase procurement from the UK as well as from Japanese suppliers. In December 2025, it signed a multi-year agreement to procure InP epitaxial wafers from UK company IQE, and we think it could further increase procurement from this supplier.
