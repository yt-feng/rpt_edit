# Weekly Database Tracker #24
## Key Takeaways

Weekly primary registered unit sales in 50 cities decreased 1% YoY (vs. +21% YoY in the previous week) for the week ended June 14, bringing YTD sales to -12% YoY: Tier 1 city sales decreased 9% (vs. +3% YoY). Tier 2 city sales increased 2% YoY (vs. +25% YoY). Tier 3 city sales decreased 4% YoY (vs. +27% YoY).

Weekly secondary registered unit sales in 10 cities increased 4% YoY (vs. +23% YoY in the previous week), bringing YTD sales to +6% YoY: Tier 1 city weekly secondary unit sales rose 3% YoY (vs. +34% YoY). Tier 2 city weekly secondary unit sales were up 3% YoY (vs. +17% YoY).

Total sell-through rate was 48% (vs. 100% in the previous week): Tier 1 cities recorded a sell-through rate of 45% (vs. 100%). Tier 2 cities recorded a sell-through rate of 51% (vs. nil).

Centraline six-city secondary asking price tracking index was 17.4% (vs. 17.5% in the previous week), and the property agent index in Tier 1 cities was 53.2 (vs. 54.4 in the previous week).
