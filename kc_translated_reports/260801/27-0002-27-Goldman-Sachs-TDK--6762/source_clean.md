# 1Q above expectations: Very positive, with results/outlook trending up in all segments; raise TP, reiterate Buy
## BUY

TDK's 1Q operating profits of ¥86.3 bn, announced after the July 31 market close, were well above our ¥68.8 bn estimate. The company maintained its full-year guidance of ¥295 bn (assuming ¥150/USD). Key points from the conference call are as follows. (1) Passive components: Operating profit rose from ¥11.4 bn in 4Q to ¥17.4 bn in 1Q. The company guides for 2Q sales growth of 2-5% qoq (assuming a constant qoq exchange rate). Strong demand from automotive and AI/data center applications is driving qoq profit growth. MLCCs and aluminum electrolytic capacitors are the main drivers. For inductors, the company expects growth from smartphone applications in 2Q, with future growth expected from AI/data center applications. (2) Sensor application products: OP rose from ¥1.5 bn to ¥7.8 bn. The company guides for 2Q sales growth of 3-6% qoq. In addition to a recovery in automotive demand, profit growth is expected to widen in 2Q due to seasonality in smartphone applications. (3) Magnetic application products: OP rose from ¥7.5 bn to ¥9.6 bn. The company guides for 2Q sales growth of 5-8% qoq. QoQ HDD head volumes were up 12% in 1Q and are expected to grow 10% in 2Q, while suspension volumes were up 8% in 1Q and are expected to grow 6% in 2Q. Management's view is that from 2Q onward, the effects of sales price hikes and rising front-end process utilization for HDD heads will contribute significantly to earnings. (4) Energy application products: OP rose from ¥41.6 bn to ¥69.4 bn (OPM of 17%). The company guides for 2Q sales growth of 9-12% qoq. Small rechargeable battery volumes are expected to increase 10% in 2Q, with earnings expanding due to seasonality and an increase in high-value-added products. (5) Overall view: For the full year, management appears particularly confident about rising profitability in HDD head-related business and earnings growth in passive components. ## Investment Thesis - TDK

TDK is a Japan-based company that operates a passive components business, which manufactures ceramic capacitors, aluminum electrolytic capacitors, and piezo materials/components; a sensor application products business, which supplies temperature/pressure, magnetic, and MEMS sensors; a magnetic application products business, which produces HDD heads/suspensions and magnets; and an energy application products business, which manufactures secondary electron and power supply products. We are Buy rated on TDK, as we expect balanced earnings contributions from growth in batteries, sensors, passive components, and magnetic applications. We see gradual revenue and profit growth in batteries, assuming no changes in material costs and selling prices. We forecast continued improvement in the sensor business, with signs that the business will remain consistently in the black. In passive components, we see automotive/industrial MLCCs driving growth, and in magnetic applications, we see nearline HDDs driving growth. We believe valuations are attractive considering the company's well-balanced business drivers and growth potential of the battery business.

## Price Target Risks and Methodology - TDK

Valuation methodology: We are Buy-rated on TDK with a 12-month price target of ¥4,900. Our target price is based on FY3/29E EV/GCI vs. CROCI/WACC, applying a 10% premium to our sector average EV/DACF multiple of 10X (implies FY3/28E P/E of 27X).

Key risks: Decline in smartphone production volume, higher input costs, and yen appreciation.
