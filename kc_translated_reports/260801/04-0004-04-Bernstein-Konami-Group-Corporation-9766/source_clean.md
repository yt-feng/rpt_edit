## Konami Q1: Football won, eFootball won too

Beating the referees. Konami's Q1 results were very strong, driven by World Cup engagement powering eFootball revenue to c. 78% growth this quarter, versus us modeling 69% growth. JPY130bn of group revenue (+33.6% year on year) was 8.0% and 5.9% ahead of us and street consensus (JPY120bn and JPY122bn). JPY45.3bn of operating profit was 63.3% higher than a year ago, and was 21.5% and 17.7% ahead of us and consensus (JPY37.3bn and JPY38.5bn). Within this, JPY43.9bn of Digital Entertainment operating profit beat our estimate and consensus by either side of 25%, while folks at Konami's smaller businesses spent the quarter watching the football.

Extra time - Q2 guidance strong. JPY52bn of aggregate eFootball revenue in Q1 was split across c. JPY43bn mobile and JPY9bn for console/PC. With July billings from Sensor Tower looking 24% higher than June levels, and 36% higher than the Q2 monthly average, management unsurprisingly guided for eFootball revenue in Q2 to be “at least flat” versus Q1. With MGS Master Collection Vol. 2 and Silent Hill: Townfall launching too, it should follow that Q2 group operating profit will be higher than Q1. Zooming out, we remain of the view that Konami will continue to grow solidly, driven by eFootball user and monetization growth, lower mobile platform fees, and single-title game progress.

The fight after the final whistle? We'd expect Konami's shares to react positively to these results. The World Cup has come and gone. But management struck a confident tone on the analyst call, pointing to ongoing market growth for eFootball, and strong retention of users acquired during the World Cup. Some of the discourse we'd heard approaching the quarter centred around a possible dull period as Konami pulls back on eFootball monetization later this year. But if we assume stocks trade on fundamentals 6-9 months ahead, investors selling Konami now risk making the same mistake as buying Konami in January. Also, do stocks dip when everyone's lining up to buy the dip?

## 78% EFOOTBALL GROWTH DRIVES Q1 BEAT, Q2 GUIDANCE STRONG

Konami's Q1 FY3/27 results were very strong, with World Cup engagement driving eFootball revenue growth. Group revenue of JPY130bn (+33.6% year on year) was 8% above our estimate (JPY120bn), and 5.9% higher than Street consensus on JPY122bn. Digital Entertainment revenue hit JPY100bn, 36.5% higher year on year, 7.1% and 6.2% ahead of our estimate (JPY93.5bn) and consensus (JPY94.2bn). JPY45.3bn of group operating profit was 63.3% higher year on year, and beat us and consensus (JPY37.3bn and JPY38.5bn) by 21.5% and 17.7%. JPY43.9bn of Digital Entertainment operating profit beat us and consensus (JPY34.6bn and JPY35.6bn) by 26.9% and 23.3%.

Breaking down the digital segment, mobile revenue (JPY65.4bn, 65% higher year on year) was higher than we'd modeled (JPY62.8bn), while console/PC revenues (JPY19.9bn, 42.1% higher year on year) was similarly above. JPY14bn of trading card revenue was 6.9% higher year on year. eFootball revenue this quarter was split JPY9bn and JPY43bn across console/PC and mobile.

A strong Q1 was expected for Konami, but JPY45.3bn operating profit was at the high end of what we'd heard from investors into the quarter, which should argue for a positive reaction. With eFootball billings continuing to rise in July, management pointed to exceptional retention of users acquired during the World Cup... and guided eFootball revenues to be “at least flat” in Q2 versus Q1. It should logically follow that Konami's Q2 operating profit will also be higher than Q1.

A period of slower eFootball monetisation is likely thereafter, in the second half of FY3/27E. The company pointed to new features aimed at newly acquired users in the upcoming summer update in August. On the single-title game front, Silent Hill Townfall orders were said to have been “very strong”, while Castlevania Belmont’s Curse was described as the first of many in a reboot for the series.

Our latest Konami price target reflects updated earnings estimates on a 25x FY+1 PE target multiple (previously 26x).
