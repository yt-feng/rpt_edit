# Amazon.com Inc. (AMZN)
# Q2'26 Review: AWS Revenue Growth & Operating Margins Support the RoIC Behind AI Investments; Overall Profit Margin Multi-Year Thesis Building in Momentum

AMZN's Q2'26 earnings report featured several key themes: 1) AWS's revenue growth rate (+37%), operating margin (+39%), and momentum in their chips and AI businesses (each with a \$25b annual revenue run rate) continue to demonstrate a clear signal of return on invested capital as tailwinds build around the company's competitive positioning relative to the AI secular growth theme across compute workloads, custom silicon and increasingly enabling AI adoption through choice (with mgmt. estimating AWS could reach a \$1tn annual run rate); 2) Capex (while increased from \$200b to \$220b for 2026 to reflect inflationary component pricing) continues to be framed by management as a signal of both strong visibility into the return profile and supportive of the company revenue backlog (now standing at \$496b +154% YoY) which will likely remain supportive of continued AWS revenue growth acceleration in the coming quarters; 3) the overall health of the Amazon Prime consumer remains solid/strong and growing initiatives around a wider array of supply (especially groceries and household essentials) and geographies (+80 new cities this quarter alone), faster delivery times and continued adoption of Alexa for Shopping (+350m customers LTM) are supportive of the company's relative positioning to be one of the strongest secular growth themes in our coverage universe away from AI; & 4) advertising revenue growth (+26% YoY) continues to be supported by both the core eCommerce operations and a growing mix from Prime Video (particular engagement with live sports – i.e., NBA), programmatic advertising etc. In total, this earnings report was fully supportive of the company's operating momentum, its current pace/cadence of investments and the ability medium/long-term to produce sustained RoIC against those investments (across multiple areas of the business).

## Q2'26 Positives & Negatives:

Positives: a) Q2 consolidated revenue came in above the high-end of the guide & GSe/FactSet Street estimates at \$200.6bn (+20% YoY) driven by strength in AWS (+37% YoY), Advertising (+26% YoY), North America, & Third Party Seller Services; b) Continued strength in the chips and AI businesses (each seeing triple digits YoY % growth rates and \$25b annual runrates) with revenue commitments for Graviton increasing nearly 3x QoQ and management expressing the potential for AWS to become \$1tn annual revenue business; c) 2Q AWS operating margins of +39% (above GSe/Street of \~34%) as incremental margins from additional AI and core workloads come in better than expected; & d) In retail, return on investments in quick commerce continues to materialize through traction with perishable offerings (monthly customers growing +50% since the start of the year), expansion in Amazon Now (added 80 new cities in 2Q), and Everyday Essentials growing meaningfully faster than other US categories.

Negatives: a) Q3'26 revenue and operating income guidance came below GSe/Street expectations by (3)%/(2)% and (9)%/(3)% respectively at the midpoint, though this includes \~80bps of headwind from FX and a 400bps impact from the Prime day timing shift; & b) Mgmt guided elevated FY26 capex to \$220bn (vs \$200bn previously guided) and noted heighten input costs (i.e., memory) as the main driver.

## Valuation: Maintain Buy Rating; Raise PT to \$375 (from \$335)

Our 12m PT of \$375 (from \$335 prior) is based on a sum-of-the-parts of EV/EBIT applied to North America and AWS segments, and EV/Sales applied to the International segment, all based on NTM+1 estimates. Specifically:

North America Segment: 16x EV/GAAP EBIT (unchanged from prior) or 0.65x EV/GAAP EBIT-to-growth (from 0.63x prior) applied to our NTM + 1 year North America segment estimates.

International Segment: 1.25x EV/Sales (unchanged from prior) or 0.13x EV/Sales-to-growth (from 0.12x prior) applied to our NTM + 1 year International segment estimates.

■ AWS Segment: 29.0x EV/GAAP EBIT (unchanged from prior) or 0.74x EV/GAAP EBIT-to-growth (from 0.80x prior) applied to our NTM + 1 year AWS segment estimates.
