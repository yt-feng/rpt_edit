## China: Developers' contract sales value worsened further in July

The geographic K-shape might further delay necessary policy actions to clean up the mess in the property sector.


Jing Wang - NIHK


Ting Lu - NIHK

\- Contract sales value of the top 100 developers fell $18.3\%$ y-o-y in July, worsening from a $9.6\%$ decline in June, taking its year-to-date growth to $-14.6\%$ y-o-y in July (2025: $-19.3\%$ ). Worsening sales value was likely due to rising downward pressure on home prices, especially in lower-tier cities, as top-tier cities showed clear signs of stabilization.

\- On city-level data, growth in new home sales by floor space in 20 major cities rose to 3.2% y-o-y in July from -6.7% in June, thanks to a low base, while existing home sales volume growth in 18 major cities eased to 9.4% from 12.3%. Positive growth for the 20 cities was led by tier-1 cities, where sales growth rose to 16.6% y-o-y in July from -2.2% in June, with largely flat sales in tier-2 cities and a steeper decline in lower-tier cites.

\- In the past, improving housing markets in top-tier cities might herald a broader recovery. However, this time might be different, as the AI boom siphons resources from legacy cities to smart cities, leading to a large divide. The mid-year Politburo meeting reiterated the need to stabilize housing markets, while the geographic K-shape has complicated policymaking and conventional policy measures are mostly exhausted.
