# 1H26 Preview: Continued Solid Momentum while VNB Growth a Key to Track

We expect Ping An to deliver solid 1H26 results, making its 0.77x 2027E P/B and 6.0% dividend yield more appealing amid market rotation.

## Key Takeaways

OPAT may deliver steady 8% growth, while NPAT could rise 29% in 1H, implying >50% y-y in 2Q26, helped by the equity market rally.

■ VNB may slow to 11% in 1H26, implying a 3% decline in 2Q26. Some banca disruption could temporarily lower growth...

\- … However, we continue to expect full-year 11% and to meet guidance, and will further track the VNB growth momentum.

Guidance on higher CSM balance and profit-making in the AM segment in 2026 is on track and in line.

Ping An will release 1H26 results on August 20 post market. We look for 8% OPAT growth and 29% NPAT growth in 1H26. AM and Life drive net profit growth. Net profit may not be as strong as some peers (>50% y-y in 1H26) due to higher exposure to high-dividend FVOCI stocks. However, we do see a relatively easier 2H26 base vs. peers.

Life VNB slowed to 11% in 1H26, implying a 3% decline in 2Q26. 3Q26 also faces a very high base, while Management maintains a double-digit 2026 growth target. A favorable low 4Q base (4Q25 was 3.2% of 2025 VNB) caused by assumption changes could help realize the guidance. P&C CoR improved 0.2ppt to 95.0% in 1H26, in line with peers, and the company expects a flattish full-year CoR (2025 at 96.8%) if cat losses stay controlled. Bank earnings are on track for positive growth, at 3% for both 1H26E and 2026E. Strong AM growth (up 103% y-y) was driven by securities, and property drag could be insignificant. Please refer to the segment breakdown (Exhibit 1) for details.

Net assets rose a decent 3.1% h-h on dividend payout and OCI stock correction. DPS rose a solid 5.6% y-y. The CSM balance could post a small 1% gain in both 1H26 (h-h) and 2026 (y-y). ## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Ping An Insurance Group Co of China Ltd (2318.HK)

Base case. We value Ping An's business using a three-stage DDM model, assuming 33%, 46%, and 70% dividend payouts in the three stages of 2026-28, 2029-34, and >2034, respectively, and corresponding dividend growth rates of 6%, 20%, and 2%. We assume a base-case cost of capital at 12%.

## Risks to Upside

■ Recovered OPAT growth, especially on AM segment

■ Faster-than-expected Life VNB growth with improved margin and banca expansion

■ Stable increased DPS

■ A-share rally

■ More value creation from its ecosystems and integrated finance.

## Risks to Downside

■ Volatile equity market and interest rate in China

■ Persistently low rate or elevated assets and property risks

■ Shareholders' sell-down overhang

■ Lower capital level and DPS
