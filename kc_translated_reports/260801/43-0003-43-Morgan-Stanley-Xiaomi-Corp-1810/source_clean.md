# Attractive Pricing of SkyNomad, Positive Volume Outlook
## Key Takeaways

SkyNomad's potential upbeat sales performance is a positive catalyst for stock performance.

Xiaomi hosted a technology launch event today for its new EV brand SkyNomad, which marks Xiaomi's first expansion into extended-range electric vehicles.

SkyNomad N90 Max: A flagship seven-seat extended-range SUV priced at Rmb299.9k.

SkyNomad N70 Max: A large five-seat all-wheel-drive extended-range SUV priced at Rmb259.9k

The official launch and full pricing are expected in September 2026.

We believe the "Pro" and standard versions of both models will set lower price points. We expect N70 series to cover a price range of Rmb200-250k and the N90 series to have a range of Rmb250-300k.

Xiaomi aims to redefine space through intelligence, with the belief that cabin space should not be static but should adapt flexibly to users' needs and preferences. For more details regarding such design, please refer to Xiaomi Corp: SkyNomad's Technology Launch on July 30 in Beijing (27 Jul 2026).

With innovative design and attractive selling prices, we believe SkyNomad is highly likely to achieve good sales performance, in our view.
