# China Steel and Iron Ore Weekly Update
## Key Takeaways

\- Apparent consumption of long products increased 1.2% WoW, while that of flat products slipped 2.8% WoW.

Weekly output dropped for long and flat products; inventory inched up at traders but decreased at mills. Utilization rates rose at electric arc furnaces (EAF).

Iron ore inventory slipped at mills: Operating rates grew along with daily output.

Iron ore shipments: Combined shipments from Australia and Brazil were up by 1.59 Mt WoW for the period 20th July to 26th July. Shipments from Australia were up by 0.54 Mt WoW. Shipments from Brazil were up by 1.06 Mt WoW.
