## PRADA Group

Our Take: Prada reported robust H1 26 results, with a 4% beat on Q2 sales and 6% beat on H1 EBIT BBG consensus. At sales level, Q2 revenue grew 18% ex-FX and +7% organic, a solid improvement vs +3% in Q1 and better than our expectations at +3%. The Prada brand was the engine of this acceleration, with retail sales bouncing back to +6% (JPMe: +2.5%), a fast improvement vs Q1 at flat (and not so different vs the highly celebrated improvement at Gucci, arguably though on a much tougher base and to positive territory), thanks to ongoing work on product newness and focus on full price sales. Miu Miu was in line, +3% in the quarter (+6% adjusting for the drag from the Middle East), a well managed normalisation in our view considering the still elevated base of comparison (+40% in Q2 25). Profitability was also better than feared, with margins down less than anticipated (EBIT margin -500bps to 17.2%, of which -350bps from the dilution of Versace, vs our estimate at -590bps), and confirming that, organically, gross margin improved slightly, including a positive contribution from full price sales. This in our view is a very healthy delivery from the Group, which continues to work hard on product newness, merchandising, activation/clienteling and retail excellence at all brands in the portfolio, delivering some of the most resilient performance in the leather goods category. At 13x P/E 27e this is clearly underappreciated by the market: we think this print should be well received and we remain Overweight.

\- Noteworthy Areas: 1) Group Q2 sales were better, +16% reported to €1.6bn, +18% ex-FX (JPMe: +13%) and +7% organic (JPMe: +3%); 2) Prada brand drove the acceleration, at +6% ex-FX in retail (JPMe: +3%), a nice improvement vs flat last quarter and ahead of the pace of the sequential improvement the sector saw in Q2 (at +3-4% on average). Pleasingly, management noted that growth was slightly positive in volume terms, with positive mix driving most of the improvement. This is testament in our view to the strong work the brand is doing on product and clienteling to nurture relationships with consumers across the pricing pyramid while stretching the pyramid higher (a rare phenomenon in leather goods these days with most brands busy recalibrating their pricing architectures). On clusters performance, the Group flagged “Chinese very positive in Q2, up double digit further improving vs Q1 (both locals and tourists), Europeans flattish/slightly improving, North Americans very positive demand and further accelerating, Japanese flattish”; 3) Miu Miu grew by 3%, in line with expectations, but actually by 6% excluding the Middle East drag. Importantly, the brand continued to grow on a base that was up 40% a year ago and +175% vs 2 years ago. This included a flat/-1% LFL (testament that the work on product and branding continue to support the desirability of the brand) and LSD% space contribution; 4) Regionally trends were consistent with the rest of the luxury reporting: Americas was very strong and accelerating at +19% organic, Apac also improved to +8% organic, including “very positive” performance in Korea, Japan (+8% organic) was supported by locals and tourists, while Europe wsa negatively impacted by tourist spend albeit recovering; 5) Profitability was healthy on an organic basis: H1 EBIT margin declined by 500bps on a reported basis, although including 350bps dilution from Versace consolidation and unfavourable phasing of marketing

We rate the stock Overweight.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

We believe that the key risks to our OW rating and target price include the following:

• Deterioration in the macro-economic environment

\- Sustained heavy investments to support top-line momentum

• Pressure on the core leather goods offer

\- Unforeseen changes in fashion trends that the Group does not manage to capture

\- Toughening competitive landscape (notably a faster-than-expected comeback from Gucci)

\- Forex

\- Disruption to tourism

• Execution risk at Versace
