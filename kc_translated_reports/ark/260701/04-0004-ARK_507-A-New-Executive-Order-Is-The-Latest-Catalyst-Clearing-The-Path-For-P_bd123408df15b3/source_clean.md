# 木头姐ARK：#507: A New Executive Order Is The Latest Catalyst Clearing The Path For Psychedelic Therapies, & More
## Feed摘要

On April 18, President Trump signed an Executive Order directing the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to issue Commissioner's National Priority Vouchers (CNPVs) to psychedelic drugs with Breakthrough Therapy designation, a mechanism that can compress FDA review timelines from ten months to one or two months. More importantly, the order is a clear signal that the White House, the Department of Health and Human Services, and the FDA are aligned in accelerating psychedelic drug approvals.

## 原始材料

On April 18, President Trump signed an Executive Order directing the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to issue Commissioner's National Priority Vouchers (CNPVs) to psychedelic drugs with Breakthrough Therapy designation, a mechanism that can compress FDA review timelines from ten months to one or two months. More importantly, the order is a clear signal that the White House, the Department of Health and Human Services, and the FDA are aligned in accelerating psychedelic drug approvals.
