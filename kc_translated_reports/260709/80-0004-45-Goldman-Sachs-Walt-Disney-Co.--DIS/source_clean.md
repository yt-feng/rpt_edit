# Walt Disney Co. (DIS)
# Orlando airport arrivals in May decrease $2 \%$ yoy

BOTTOM LINE: We refresh our Orlando, Florida tourism market indicators for the month of May 2026 (as part of our efforts to measure intra-quarter demand for Disney and Universal theme parks). This includes: (1) Orlando International Airport flight arrivals (source: Greater Orlando Aviation Authority); and (2) Orange County, Florida Tourist Development Tax receipts, which is a $6\%$ tax paid on hotel and short-term accommodations (source: Orange County, Florida Comptroller's office).

Key takeaways from our analysis include:

\- Orange County's Tourist Development Tax (TDT) collections rose 9% yoy to \$32.8 mn in May 2026, a moderation from the +14% yoy pace in April 2026, though notably marking the strongest May collections on record.

May 2026 Orlando airport arrivals declined $2\%$ yoy (per the Greater Orlando Aviation Authority), a deceleration from $+3\%$ yoy in April 2026. The yoy softness was driven by a $-3\%$ yoy decline in domestic deplanements, partially offset by a $+2\%$ yoy increase in international deplanements. That said, international visitation has now posted two consecutive months of growth following three months of relative weakness—consistent with management's commentary that international visitation to U.S. parks has cleared its pandemic-recovery overlap period and is no longer showing signs of further deterioration.

C2Q26 MCO passenger deplanements are tracking to $+1\%$ yoy, in line with GSe's estimate of $+1\%$ yoy growth in C2Q26 DIS-reported domestic attendance, assuming June trends approximate the average of April and May.

Rating, price target, key risks, investment thesis

Valuation. We are Buy rated on shares of Walt Disney Co. Our 12-month price target of \$163 is based on a sum-of-the-parts valuation methodology based on the following

assumptions: Parks and Experiences at 11X NTM+1Y EBITDA, Consumer Products at 15X NTM+1Y EBITDA, Linear Networks at 4X NTM+1Y EBITDA, Content Sales and Licensing at 20X NTM+1Y EBITDA, Sports at 6X NTM+1Y EBITDA, and DTC based on an EV/subscriber methodology with an implied EV/EBITDA of 21X.

Key downside risks: intensified cord-cutting, sports rights cost inflation, streaming competition, economic slowdown impacting consumer spending, weak theatrical slate, M&A, unfavorable regulatory or policy reforms, higher than expected interest rates and FX.

Investment Thesis Summary: Disney produces, distributes and licenses filmed entertainment content across its various brands (Walt Disney Studios, ESPN, ABC, Hulu) and is a leading destination theme park owner and operator in the US, Europe and Asia. Its long history of 1) generating genre defining film and television content via its iconic characters and studio brands (Disney Princesses, Mickey and Friends, Pixar), 2) operating industry leading destination parks with global demand, and 3) producing and distributing a global leading sport-focused entertainment brand have contributed to the company's established brand strength.

We are Buy rated on DIS, as we believe the company is a high-quality EPS compounder supported by 1) continued progression to scaled long term DTC profitability made possible by wholesale arrangements, bundled offerings, password sharing restrictions and other initiatives; 2) studio performance improvement (from a period of under earning) enabled by cost rationalization and organizational restructuring; 3) effective sports rights cost mitigation and cord-cutting headwind management via ESPN DTC flagship, live rights sub-licensing, portfolio curation, and corporate actions; and 4) robust theme park growth enabled by industry tailwinds and a \$60bn investment over the next 10 years.
