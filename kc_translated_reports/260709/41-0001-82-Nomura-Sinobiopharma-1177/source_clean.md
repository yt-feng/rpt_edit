# Two more on-market drugs obtained from GSK Quick Note

What is new? Further China commercial rights to two respiratory products obtained from GSK

On 8 July noon (HKT), Sinobiopharma (SBP) announced that it had expanded its collaboration agreement with GSK (GSK LN, Not rated), since these two struck a deal in May (see our report: Quick Note - Sinobiopharma (1177 HK) (Buy) Obtains the commercial rights to an HBV drug).

SBP would obtain the China commercial rights to two respiratory drugs, namely fluticasone furoate, umeclidinium, and vilanterol inhalation powder (FF/UMEC/VI, 全再乐, Trelegy Ellipta) and umeclidinium and vilanterol inhalation powder (UMEC/VI, 欧乐欣, Anoro Ellipta) from GSK.

Trelegy Ellipta is the first and only single-inhaler triple therapy (SITT) in China indicated for maintenance treatment of both asthma and COPD. Anoro Ellipta was approved in China in 2018 for long-term maintenance treatment of COPD. According to GSK, Trelegy Ellipta / Anoro Ellipta generated global sales of GBP3.0bn/GBP542mn (11%/-5% y-y) in FY25, and GBP646mn/GPB128mn (-4%/+1% y-y) in 1Q26.

According to Pharmacube, a data provider, sales of Trelegy Ellipta / Anoro Ellipta from sample hospitals in China were worth CNY819mn/CNY118mn +34%/11% y-y in 2025 and CNY218mn/CNY30mn (+6%/-3% y-y) in 1Q26.

Our take: a good replenishment of SBP's respiratory portfolio and deepening of the collaboration between the two parties

We remain positive on this collaboration for SBP, which has strong commercial capability in this therapeutic area in China, in our view. More importantly, this collaboration would further solidify the relationship between the two parties, paving way for further collaborations.

We maintain our TP of HKD8.67 and Buy rating on Sinobiopharma.
