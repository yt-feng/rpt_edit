# JPM
## Bayer
## 2Q'26 Preview: Expect a solid Quarter, with Guidance reiteration to underpin latest Vara Consensus

\- Pharmaceuticals: We forecast Pharma Sales of €4,381m (-1% at CER), c. 1% below BBG Consensus at €4,410m. We forecast the Pharma division sales declining by -1% at CER, together with -1% FX, reaching €4,381m in 2Q'26, with our forecast c. 1% below BBG Consensus at €4,410m. On key growth drivers, we forecast Nubeqa sales of €838m (+54% at CER) and Kerendia sales of €297m (+63% at CER) and we forecast continued erosion of Xarelto and Eylea to €356m (-45% at CER) and €648m (-24% at CER), respectively. For Pharma EBITDA (u/l) we forecast €1,038m, c. 2% ahead of BBG Consensus of €1,023m underpinned by our c. 50bps higher margin assumptions.

\- Crop Science: We forecast Crop Science Sales of €4,777m (+1% at CER), in line with BBG Consensus at €4,794m: We forecast Crop Science sales to grow +1% at CER, together with a -1% FX impact, reaching €4,777m in 2Q'26, in line with Consensus at €4,794m. We forecast y-o-y declines for Corn (-6% at CER) and Soy (-1% at CER) due to timing of sales being offset by strong growth in herbicides (9% at CER) and Cotton (67% at CER) due to volume recovery and delayed purchases falling into Q2. We forecast Glyphosate growing at 20% CER due to volume recovery and pricing gains (10% price; 10% volume). For Crop Science EBITDA (u/l), we forecast €714m (15.0% EBITDA margin), with our forecast in line with BBG Consensus at €715m.
