## Pfizer Inc.
## Anticipating Upside to Guidance As We Move Through the Year Although Pipeline Remains the Focus

Heading into PFE’s 2Q earnings (8/4), we are roughly in-line with the Street for the quarter and continue to see potential upside to guidance as we move through the year. On the quarter, we forecast sales of \$14.6bn (+\$130mm vs cons) and EPS of \$0.69 (+\$0.01). And for the year overall, we estimate sales of \$62.7bn (+\$860mm vs cons) and EPS of \$3.05 (+\$0.09) which leaves us modestly above PFE’s guidance (\$59.5-\$62.5bn, \$2.80 - \$3.00).

More broadly on the story, we continue to view PFE shares as inexpensive (\~9x 2027e EPS) with several assets in the company's pipeline that could make the story more interesting over time. However, we believe further derisking/data from these programs will be needed to shift investor sentiment. And here, most major data updates will come in 2027 and beyond. As a result, we remain on the sidelines with a Neutral rating and are decreasing our Dec-26 PT to \$28 (from \$30) following the recent Ph3 failure for Sigvotatug Vedotin in the 2L+ NSCLC setting.

# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

Pfizer Inc. (Neutral; Price Target: \$28.00)

## Investment Thesis

We continue to view PFE shares as inexpensive (\~9x 2027e EPS) with several assets in the company's pipeline that could make the story more interesting over time. However, we believe further derisking/data from these programs will be needed to shift investor sentiment. And here, while there are a handful of expected 2026 readouts, most major data updates will come in 2027 and beyond. As a result, we remain on the sidelines with a Neutral rating.

## Valuation

Our Dec 2026 price target of \$28 is based on our DCF methodology assuming a 0% terminal growth rate and an 8.5% WACC, on par with our assumptions for the overall pharma sector.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Risks to our Neutral rating on Pfizer include, to the downside, 1) failure to maintain operating margins and 2) potential for pipeline setbacks. Upside risks include 1) continued pipeline outperformance and 2) stronger-than-expected new product launch performance.
