# Property Data Monitor

Mainland China: Secondary listings in tier-1 cities further dropped; HK: high-frequency data softened

## Mainland China

\- Iceberg 10-city real-time secondary weekly sales (冰山指数实时二手成交) rose $14\%$ Y/Y (last week: $+18\%$ ). Tier-1 cities' weekly sales grew $15\%$ Y/Y (Table 1), with Shanghai outperforming $(+20\%$ Y/Y). Real-time sales generally lead the trend of official sales registrations by a few weeks.

\- Iceberg 10-city secondary listings (冰山指数二手挂牌量) were flat W/W. Tier-1 cities' listings dropped $0.4\%$ W/W, driven by $-2.2\%$ W/W in Shenzhen and $-0.4\%$ W/W in Shanghai (Table 2). The volume of secondary listings in tier-1 cities has been consistently coming down (down $3.5\%$ from the peak in March), and this is a key factor that will support continual secondary home price stabilization.

\- 60-city primary sales registrations (一手网签) rose $27\%$ Y/Y (last week: $-8\%$ Y/Y) (more), mostly due to the month-end loaded nature of sales.

\- 12-city secondary sales registrations (二手网签) rose $12\%$ Y/Y (Figure 7) (last week: $+1\%$ ). YTD, 12-city secondary sales have risen $5\%$ Y/Y (Shanghai: $+14\%$ ).

\- The Centraline tier-1 cities' secondary asking price index dropped from 17.0 to 16.6 (Figure 3).

\- The Centaline secondary manager confidence index rose from 52 to 55 (Figure 4), Shanghai saw the largest W/W increment from 48 last week to 52.

\- Southbound holdings rose 0.23% W/W (Table 7): C&D +1.44%; Greentown +0.65%.

\- Share price moves (Figure 17): The sector rose $4\%$ last week, outperforming the HSI $(+3\%)$ . The outperformer were Shimao $(+12\%)$ , Jinmao and A-Living (both $+9\%$ ). The underperformer was Sunac $(-6\%)$ .

• JPM top picks: COLI, CR Land, Jinmao and CR Mixc.

## Hong Kong SAR

\- The home price index rose 0.5% W/W (Figure 13). Wrapping up 1H26, home prices have risen 12%, already reaching our full-year target of 10-15%. Looking ahead to 2H26, we expect slower price growth of <5%. For more discussion, please see our report HK Residential Property: Home prices already hit our full-year target; momentum may slow in 2H.

\- Secondary transactions in the top 35 estates totaled 35 units, +6% W/W or -54% Y/Y (Figure 12).

\- The Centa-Salesman Index (CSI) (Figure 15) moderated from 67.3 last week to 64.5. A reading of $>50 =$ sentiment is positive and property prices are likely to rise.

\- The Centaline Valuation Index (CVI) (Figure 14) moderated from 84.3 last week to 81.6.

## Credit View (by Alvin Au)

\- The JACI China HY Property index rose a mild 0.16% last week (vs China HY: +0.03%), bringing YTD returns to 5.5%.

\- Vanke: Vanke announced last week that it has appointed Huang Yu (former deputy head of Shenzhen Financial Regulatory Bureau) as the new President, more than a year after the former President Zhu Jiusheng stepped down amidst the developer's liquidity crisis. We note that both Vanke's chairman and president at present were formerly part of the Shenzhen city government.

\- CK Hutchison: Moody's has reaffirmed an A2 rating on CK Hutchison ("CKHH") with a Stable outlook. It expects CKHH's balance sheet to improve significantly, as the disposal of UK Power Networks, UK Rails and 49% of VodafoneThree will likely generate sizable net cash proceeds of HK\$97bn (48% of group adjusted net debt). This will more than offset the associated reduction in earnings/cashflows (less than 10% of group total). We stay OW on CKHH '41s (76.9 offer, 5.4% ytm, SOT+96), as we appreciate its deleveraging with solid fundamentals and asset disposals, and the credit's decent valuation along the curve.

JPM top picks: LNGFOR '29s (84 offer, 9.9% ytm) and SHUION '29s (101.3 offer, 9.2% ytm).
