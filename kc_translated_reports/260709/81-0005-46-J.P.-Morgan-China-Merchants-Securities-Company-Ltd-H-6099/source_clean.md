# CMS Company Ltd - H

2Q26 profits up 160-198% y/y, a beat; but does this matter?

2Q26 result a strong beat. After market close on 7 July, CMS announced its preliminary 1H26 results (link). 2Q26 net profits rose 160-198% y/y to Rmb6.7-7.7bn, a record high and likely ahead of market expectation. That said, investors may question whether the beat still matters given that A/H share brokers' share prices were down by an average 3%/3% this week, after GTHT's strong profit alert (link). We argue CMS's results further validate our constructive view on the sector, and that recent share price weakness presents an opportunity to add—given the sector's undemanding valuations and potential upward revisions in consensus earnings following the profit alerts. We believe CICC, Citic Sec, and Guangfa may issue profit alerts in the coming weeks.


\- But why didn’t the market react positively to GTHT’s profit alert? A/H share brokers’ share prices have declined 3%/3% on average this week, despite GTHT’s strong pre-announcement last Friday (link). We attribute this to investors’ profit taking and concerns on sustainability of such a strong performance. Note that the CSI300 and STAR50 indices were down 4% and 9% in July MTD. Some investors may worry that strong 2Q results were disproportionately driven by investment income, and that the declining market indices could translate into softer operating performance in 3Q.

\- We see recent share price weakness as an opportunity to add: We view these concerns as overdone. First, we expect brokers' strong 2Q results to reflect a broad-based recovery across brokerage, investment banking, capital intermediary, and investment income. Second, both GTHT's and CMS's 1H26 profits are \~70% of Bloomberg consensus FY26 profits, suggesting the market is underestimating the strength of China brokers' earnings momentum. We see scope for consensus upgrades to drive a sector re-rating. Third, valuations remain undemanding: A- and H-share brokers trade at \~1.15x and \~0.74x forward P/B, respectively. We therefore view the pullback as an opportunity to add, particularly in high-quality brokers.

## Investment Thesis

We have an OW rating on CMS-H. Our analysis suggests CMS is gaining market share in the brokerage business and will benefit from mutual fund business growth. CXMT's listing on STAR board may bring upsides to CMS's investment income in 3Q26.

## Valuation

Our Dec-26 PT of HK\$17.20 is derived from a combination of DDM for FY26-28E dividends and terminal value based on ROE-g/COE-g methodology. We compound the time value to derive a fair value of Rmb15.0 for Dec-26 from the current fair value of Rmb13.9 and use spot CNY/HKD to convert to HKD for our H-share PT. Key assumptions include: a risk-free rate of 1.80%, an equity risk premium of 8.0%, a beta of 1.2x and a long-term growth rate of 4.5%.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Downside risks include: (1) risks related to contributions to the government stabilization fund (the CSFC) and the ban on hedging; (2) tighter regulatory supervision of the rapidly emerging domestic asset management businesses; and (3) long-term pressure on brokerage commission rates due to the development of online/mobile industry platforms and fragmented market conditions, combined with uncertainties from the potential deregulation of brokerage fee structures.

Upside risks include: (1) higher-than-expected brokerage commissions; (2) higher-than-expected income from new business; and (3) improving ADT.
