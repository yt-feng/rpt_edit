## Wanhua Chemical - A

## 2Q beat; we raise FY26E EPS to a 5-year high and upgrade to OW

Wanhua reported a strong 2Q prelim NP of Rmb6.1-6.7bn (+100-120% y/y, +68-80% q/q) - the $2^{\text{nd}}$ highest quarterly NP in its history. 1H26 represented 56-59% of FY26 Bloomberg consensus NP. Wanhua's share price has declined >20% since 13 April (vs SHCOMP flat) on the back of lower oil prices and a fall in MDI/TDI spreads. We believe weakness is priced in, and see potential for a recovery in margins. More importantly, we attribute a large part of the 2Q beat to Wanhua's newly started ethane cracker, which, similar to peers Satellite Chem and Petrochina, likely delivered very strong 2Q NP. Post earnings, we raise our FY26-27 NP estimates for Wanhua by 21%/10% to Rmb19bn and roll our PT to Jun-27 at Rmb90/shr. Upgrade to OW as current valuation appears attractive with the stock trading at 12x 1-yr forward P/E vs. FY25-27E EPS CAGR of 23%.

\- Wanhua 2Q NP: it's a beat. Wanhua's earlier issuance of a profit alert pointed to 1H26 NP of Rmb9.8bn-10.4bn (+60.5% to 69.85% y/y), representing 56%-59% of FY26 consensus at Rmb17.6bn. Implied 2Q NP of Rmb6.1bn-6.7bn marks 68%-80% sequential growth vs. 1Q NP at Rmb3.7bn. We attribute the earnings beat largely to the Rmb1,560/t and Rmb635/t q/q expansion in China MDI/TDI spread over the quarter, as Middle East escalation effectively shuts down 440ktpa/240ktpa of MDI/TDI capacity, accounting for 4%/6% of global capacity. 2Q was also boosted by strong earnings from its Yantai #1 ethylene cracker (1mtpa), which has successfully undergone feedstock conversion from oil to ethane and commenced full operation in Jan 2026. On 22 Jun post market close, Satellite Chemical (002648 CH, NC) also issued a profit alert with implied 2Q NP potentially doubling q/q, citing attractive ECC economics after Asia margin jumped 5x in Apr/May vs. Feb (JPM note) as PE price gained >30% q/q while US ethane price remained flattish.

\- Resilient ECC margin as Asia PE spot stays 15% above pre-conflict: While retreating oil/naphtha price (-40%/36% vs. Apr-May average) has reversed some of the 5x ECC margin jump in Apr vs. Feb level, we note that Asia PE price remains 15% higher vs. pre-conflict level on spot while ethane price has remained largely steady over the same period. In an \$70-80/bbl oil price environment, we expect ECC margins to continue to outperform NCC. Relative economics of ECC also presents an attractive arbitrage opportunity as Europe olefins/PE prices surged >\$3,000/t vs Asia PE prices of \$1,050/t, with easing freight rates potentially providing a further boost as the conflict de-escalates.

\- Gradual mid-LT recovery in MDI spread: As Middle Eastern capacity takes time to normalise, we note that MDI supply crunch will likely see limited relief into 3Q given maintenance peak. BASF has scheduled a 45-day turnaround in July, which likely is in association with the pre-commissioning work of its US capacity expansion, set for 3Q operation. On the pricing front, the earlier announcement of a price hike from Covestro (+\$0.22/lb) is effective 1 Jul, providing near-term support.

## Valuation

Our Jun-27 PT of Rmb90 is based on a 15x one-year forward P/E, in line with the historical five-year average.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Downside risks include: (1) further escalation in US-China trade tariffs; (2) a rise in global recession risks, resulting in a deterioration of chemical demand; and (3) a hike in tariffs on China MDI by European/US allies.
