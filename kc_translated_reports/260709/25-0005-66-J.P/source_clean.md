## Taiwan substrate
## Exiting BT an emerging trend

\- BT capacity conversion to ABF underway. Our industry supply chain checks indicate that multiple Asian suppliers are planning/reducing their BT exposures by transferring some production tools for BT, which is mostly utilized in consumer electronics and memory-related chips, to the production of ABF substrates. Driven by AI and size migrations, the demands for ABF core layers are rising rapidly, as seen by the recent announcements by Japanese and Austrian substrate makers. The capacity conversion from BT provides a timely capacity addition to ABF. We believe the results of conversion will be net-net positive for both rev and margin for substrate makers (ABF margin meaningfully higher). We believe some substrate makers could elect to exit the BT business completely in three years.

\- The implications. We expect some moderate disruptions in BT substrate supply for the leading handset SoC/OEMs, as some reshuffles in procurement are likely still needed. BT prices were up for 1-2 quarters after mid-2025, driven by tight supply (driven by rising memory demand plus limited supply growth, in our view). While we have yet to see another wave of widescale price increases, we don't rule out such a possibility. Meanwhile, we expect some overflow orders to be directed to Tier-2 Taiwan substrate makers, plus some smaller producers in Korea and Mainland China.
