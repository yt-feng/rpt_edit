# China Autos & Shared Mobility | Asia Pacific
# China EV Weekly Orders – Slow Summer, Selective Sparks
## Key Takeaways

July officially marks the start of the off-season, with overall order intake and store traffic expected to fall and consolidate.

A handful of automakers could buck the trend. For example, XPeng has seen a notable increase in showroom foot traffic thanks to the debut of MONA L03.

Overall vehicle sales are likely to remain seasonally sluggish until the next round of new product cycle kicks off in August.

Over the past week, BYD, Luxeed, Tesla and Geely Group (Galaxy/Zeekr) delivered standout order intake.

## Weekly order trends in June 29 to July 5 based on channel feedback:


Li Auto (2015.HK/LI.O) 7.3-7.5k (-36% WoW, -2% MoM, -23% YoY) with orders normalizing after L8 launch.


XPeng (9868.HK/XPEV.N) 6.7-6.9k (+2% WoW, -53% MoM, -77% YoY); all eyes are on the MONA L03 launch on July 16 after registering solid pre-orders over the debut weekend.


Tesla China (covered by Andrew Percoco) 11-11.2k (+16% WoW, +16% MoM, -35% YoY).

ZEEKR 6.4-6.6k (+12% WoW, -15% MoM, +83% YoY); sales of 5-seat 9X will launch on 7/8.

Xiaomi (1810.HK; covered by Andy Meng) 6.5-6.7k (-7 WoW, -28% MoM, -92% YoY).
