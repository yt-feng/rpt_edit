## Retail Pharmacies: Easing Regulatory Headwinds

## Muted SSSG:

\- April SSSG declined 1.0% YoY, while year-to-date SSSG was flat (up 0.3% YoY). Lingering factors such as regulatory spot checks, soft consumption and price cuts all played a part.

## Industry consolidation:

\- Net store closures in 2025 amounted to \~22,000, bringing the total number of pharmacies to 680,000 by year-end.

\- Commentaries still pointed to a further industry-wide 30% contraction by store numbers from peak (\~700,000) over three to five years, with closures concentrated in small- to mid-sized pharmacies and lower-tier cities.

\- Leading chains are back in expansion mode through M&A, franchising and self-operated (2026 target: DSL +3,000, Yifeng +2,000, LBX +2,100), with franchising as the primary source of growth given its asset-light profile and fast access to underpenetrated markets.

## Tightened regulatory environment:

\- Policy direction is shifting toward higher compliance standards and a more integrated role for pharmacies in China's medical system. Ongoing policy trends include: end-to-end drug traceability, integrated healthcare centre transition, whitelisting items for reimbursement through personal accounts, etc.

We expect earnings growth in 2026 to be driven by cost optimization through: 1) pruning down of low efficiency stores; and 2) AI initiatives to improve operational efficiency. Top line will depend on sales of TCMs, private labels and franchising.
