# China Tourism Group Duty Free Corp 2Q revenue likely to slip into YoY decline; market concerns linger on consumer downtrading
## Ongoing impact from consumer downtrading

We recently met CTG's management. Our key takeaways are: 1) 2Q revenue is likely to decline YoY; 2) gross margin and net margin are expected to improve YoY in 2Q; and 3) regarding overseas expansion, the company may enter Southeast Asia market under the DFS brand. We estimate 2Q net profit could grow by over $10\%$ YoY, with potential upside exceeding $20\%$ depending on the final net margin. The key market concerns remain: 1) ongoing consumer downtrading could continue to weigh on CTG's revenue. The sharp slowdown in Hainan duty-free sales growth in April-May versus 1Q suggests the continued reliance on policy support to offset this downtrend. 2) consensus revenue and earnings estimates for 2026-27 may face downside risks; and 3) profit growth driven primarily by margin recovery may raise questions about earnings sustainability. Furthermore, the magnitude of earnings growth in upcoming quarters will heavily depend on the scale of government subsidies.

## 2Q preview: revenue likely to decline YoY; but net profit may rise YoY

For 2Q, we expect revenue to decline by over 10% YoY, reflecting a weaker trend than 1Q. By segment: 1) Hainan, accounting for over 70% of total revenue, saw estimated growth of c10% YoY in 2Q, materially below the 28% YoY growth recorded in 1Q; 2) CTG Sunrise's online sales continued to decline YoY, and we do not expect it to flatten out within 2026; 3) At airports, despite double-digit YoY sales growth at Shanghai Airport T2+S2, overall airport duty-free sales remained YoY decline due to the loss of operating rights at Shanghai Airport T1+S1. On earnings: management noted that net margin may continue the improving trend seen in 1Q, supported by reduced discounting and sales mix optimisation. We expect 2Q net margin to rise YoY, which should bolster YoY net profit growth in 2Q.

## 3Q outlook: the magnitude of Hainan DFS growth depends on gov't subsidies

Our channel checks indicate the Hainan government increased subsidies for duty-free sales in July, including subsidies to airlines to boost flights volume to Hainan. We expect Hainan duty-free sales growth to recover to double digits in July-August. That said, returning to the 20%+ growth levels seen in 1Q may prove challenging and will heavily depend on the scale of government subsidies.

## Maintain Buy rating

We forecast a 2Q revenue decline of over 10% YoY, while net profit could still deliver double-digit YoY growth. Whether this earnings growth can exceed 20% will largely depend on net margin performance. We are planning to review our earnings forecasts.

## Company Description

China Tourism Group Duty Free (CTG Duty Free) is a leading duty-free operator in China; China Tourism Group is its parent company. It operates duty-free stores at entry/exit ports, Hainan offshore duty-free stores and downtown duty-free stores, as well as airport duty-free businesses in Shanghai and Beijing.

## Valuation Method and Risk Statement

We believe main downside risks to China's duty-free sector include: 1) continued economic sluggishness; 2) slower-than-expected growth in peak season tourist traffic; 3) bad weather conditions affecting travel; 4) earthquakes, air accidents, epidemics and other disasters. CTG Duty Free - A: We base our price target on DCF. We believe downside risks include: 1) declining tourist numbers in Sanya's tourism market; 2) China loosening policies that limit entry to the duty-free industry; 3) natural disasters or major epidemics and other force majeure events. We believe upside risks include: 1) faster-than-expected recovery of per capita spending; 2) the introduction of downtown duty-free store policy
