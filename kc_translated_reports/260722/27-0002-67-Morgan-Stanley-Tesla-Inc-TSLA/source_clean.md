# Rolling Through the Sunshine State

Tesla announced the launch of Robotaxi in Orlando and Tampa this morning. Tesla launched Robotaxi in two more Florida cities (reportedly including unsupervised Model Ys) on Tuesday, July 21st. Orlando and Tampa both were listed as "preparations underway" in the 1Q26 slide deck, along with Miami (launched), Phoenix (not yet launched), and Las Vegas (not yet launched).

Robotaxi is a key focus area for investors, and a meaningful update tomorrow is one of only a handful of catalysts that can drive shares higher (see our 2Q preview). We expect Tesla to launch in all of these metro areas by year-end and expect it to add additional cities to its list of planned robotaxi coverage with earnings tomorrow. In our report following the Miami launch, we mentioned sightings of presumed robotaxi test vehicles in New Orleans (not confirmed by the company), which was not one of the cities listed as being in the preparation stage in the last earnings presentation. While bears may point to a lack of growth in unsupervised fleet size, limited data transparency, or a small service area for Orlando and Tampa launches, we believe this news will be well received by the market after what had felt like a deceleration with the Robotaxi Tracker reflecting a decline in active vehicles in fleet since late April (although this data is not validated by the company). We are watching for an update tomorrow on Cybercab production and robotaxi rollout.

Early Signals from Austin. We wrote last month that our Alphawise data showed increased hiring in northern metro areas for 'AI Safety Operators' - a sign that Tesla is gearing up to expand robotaxi operations, along with FSD usage continuing to accelerate, reaching 10bn cumulative miles traveled in early May. We also built out a tracker normalizing NHTSA data from the Austin fleet, which reflected significant improvement in miles/accident since launch. With Tesla's approach to autonomy still heavily debated, investors are closely watching rate of change in monthly safety data. Tesla's ability to grow its robotaxi fleet while concurrently improving incident rates (miles between accident) is a key measure that we expect will dominate investors perception of "success" for its robotaxi business. For further reading, see: Our Guide to Robotaxi Safety Data: Early Signals from Austin.


Our expectations. We forecast 1,500 robotaxis (supervised + unsupervised) in the fleet by year-end, ramping to 30,000 by 2030. While the absolute number of robotaxis is likely immaterial to earnings this year, the rate of change in the rollout provides more clarity for investors who may be skeptical about Tesla's autonomous technology at scale. ## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Tesla Inc (TSLA.O)

Our PT of \$417 is comprised of 5 components: (1) \$47/share for core Tesla Auto business on \~8.5mm units in 2040, 9.7% exit EBIT margin (ex-FSD), 10.9% WACC, and 10x 2040 exit EBITDA multiple. (2) Network Services at \$146/share, 80% attach rate at \$240/month ARPU by 2040 (3) Tesla Mobility at \$125/share on DCF with \~5mn cars at \~\$1.33/mile by 2040. (4) Energy at \$40/share, and (5) Humanoids at \$60/share (50% probability discount).
