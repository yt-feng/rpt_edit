# Beijing Capital Int'l Airport | Asia Pacific
# 2Q26 Prelims: A Thin Profit; In-line

BCIA announced a preliminary net profit of Rmb10-20mn in 1H26. This implies 2Q26 net profit of Rmb1-11mn, versus Rmb9mn in 1Q26 and a net loss of Rmb39mn in 2Q25. BCIA attributed the earnings turnaround YoY to growth in non-domestic traffic.

## Our take – neutral:

\- BCIA remained profitable in 2Q26 despite total pax dropped $4\%$ QoQ and largely flat YoY, amid soft travel demand and higher airfares due to global energy shock.

\- We think the 2Q26 results were largely in line with MSe, with BCIA continuing to generate only a modest profit in 2026.
