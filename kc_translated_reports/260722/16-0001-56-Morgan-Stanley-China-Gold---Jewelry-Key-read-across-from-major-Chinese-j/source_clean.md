# Key read-across from major Chinese jewelry players' SSSG and mall trends
## Key Takeaways

Weight-based gold SSSG stayed healthy in June, but may have slowed sequentially. Luk Fook's 2Q China SSSG was $16\%$ vs. $>20\%$ on Apr 1-Jun 210.

Premium fixed-price gold demand YoY improved sequentially in June, as declines in China gold jewelry sales narrowed at key malls.

HK/Macau demand remained robust in June. Luk Fook's 2Q HK/Macau SSSG of $+41\%$ was in line with Apr 1-Jun 21.

Chow Tai Fook's 2Q revenue and profit trends should outperform Laopu, while Laopu's demand recovered sequentially.

\- Stable gold prices and RMB appreciation should provide a more supportive backdrop for Chow Tai Fook.

Following recently announced 2QCY26 (Apr-Jun) trends at Luk Fook (590.HKO, not covered) and Chow Sang Sang (116.HK, not covered), as well as shopping mall operators' (covered by Stephen Cheung) Jun-26 trends, our read-across are as follows:

Chow Tai Fook (1929.HK, OW): CTF will report 1QFY27 (Apr-Jun) operating data in late July. We expect the SSSG trend to be largely similar to Apr-May (China SSSG: +20%; HK/Macau: +41%). We do not expect CTF to revise F27 guidance at the 1QF27 briefing call, despite current gold price (\~US\$4K) being lower than its assumption (US\$4.3K), as guidance was only announced less than a month ago. In our view, the stable gold price environment is positive to demand for weight-based products and lower-tier cities. Rmb appreciation is positive to CTF's HK/Macau business and CTF financials (HKD is its reporting currency).

Laopu (6181.HK, OW): A weak 2Q trend is largely in the price, in our view. The sequential improvement among premium fixed price gold products is encouraging, although 3Q visibility remains low. We await management comments at the interim result briefing in late Aug (e.g., Jul-Aug sales trends, any revised strategy and dividend policy).

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Chow Tai Fook Jewellery Group Ltd (1929.HK)

We use a P/E-based valuation, consistent with the rest of our consumer discretionary coverage. Our price target is based on 18x F27e P/E, slightly above the average since 2016. This is to reflect a strategy change with slower network expansion but stronger same-store profitability. We await achievement of margin expansion beyond gold price hikes. We project a \~30% EPS CAGR over F25-27.

## Risks to Upside

■ Faster-than-expected network expansion and same-store sales growth in China/HK

■ Gold price rally driving margin expansion

■ Success in brand revitalization strategy for sustainable ROE enhancement

## Risks to Downside

■ Macro slowdown and declines in disposable income

■ Intense competition for gold products in mainland China

■ Gold price decline that drags operating margins

## Laopu Gold (6181.HK)

Base case scenario, P/E-based valuation methodology, consistent with the rest of our consumer discretionary coverage. We use 11x 2026e P/E, which implies \~0.35x PEG, lower than the average for consumer stocks.

We think this is reasonable considering gold price volatility and macro weakness. In addition, considering that growth is likely to decelerate in 2026 on a higher comp base, we believe a lower-than-peers PEG assumption is reasonable.

## Risks to Upside

■ More proactive consumption-related policy support

■ Faster-than-expected same store sales growth

■ Gold price rally

## Risks to Downside

■ Delay in consumption-related policy support

■ Weaker-than-expected demand trend due to sharp gold price correction

■ Sharp sales growth slowdown
