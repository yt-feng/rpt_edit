# Americas Healthcare: Generic Pharmaceuticals: 2Q EPS preview

Ahead of 2Q earnings (TEVA 7/29, AMRX 7/30, VTRS 8/6), we refresh our estimates for the latest script data and recent company commentary (representing overall incremental changes). While we expect broadly in-line reports across the group on the top- and bottom-line, we highlight the potential for an AMRX beat on stronger margins (with top-line risk also skewed to the upside), albeit positioning likely reflects this to a certain degree given the recent out-performance (+44% YTD). With reports, beyond updates on the key commercial products/dynamics, we are most focused on branded portfolio execution, pipeline progress, and capital allocation strategies.

From a broader outlook perspective, the group has continued to out-perform both the healthcare sector (+18% vs. XLV) and broader market (+12% vs. S&P 500) in recent months (Exhibit 9), driven, in our view, by both: (i) fundamentals (e.g., earnings and pipeline execution, alongside strategic and meaningful BD), as well as (ii) macro/rotational dynamics where the broadening-out (AI/momentum unwind) trade has been a tailwind for healthcare, particularly therapeutics - where generic pharma continues to screen well from a valuation perspective (we highlight VTRS; Exhibit 10). Looking ahead, while macro/fund flow dynamics will likely create volatility, we continue to expect the fundamental drivers of stock performance to be earnings growth and pipeline optionality - where we see a highly favorable set-up into a catalyst-rich 2H26+ for the group (Exhibit 8). In particular, we highlight the set-up for TEVA, where the recent pull-back in shares represents a compelling entry point.

VTRS (Neutral): Expect in-line quarter ahead of key pipeline updates in YE26+ VTRS shares have outperformed YTD (+30% vs. S&P 500; +36% vs. XLV) driven, in our view, by (i) an improving outlook for the underlying commercial business (supported by the longer-term guidance provided at the Investor Day earlier this year), (ii) clinical catalysts in 2H26+ (Exhibit 8), (iii) attractive risk-reward, given shares trade at a meaningful discount to the broader group, into the aforementioned catalysts, and (iv) macro dynamics (i.e., broadening-out trade as the AI/momentum trade has weakened - where VTRS screens well from a valuation perspective). We note generally positive expectations into 2Q earnings (alongside a pick-up in inbounds as the stock has outperformed), and continue to see positioning as mixed more broadly.

Ahead of the quarter, we make minimal changes to our estimates, which remain broadly in-line with consensus, and expect an overall in-line quarter. While commercial execution remains the key focus, where we look for an updated outlook on the commercial business, including both the generic and branded segment performances across geographies, and FY26 guidance, we will also be monitoring: (1) branded pipeline updates (e.g., timelines/outlook for fast-acting meloxicam, selatogrel, and cenerimod), (2) capital allocation commentary (e.g., appetite for business development vs. share repurchases, particularly in the context of recent deal activity for the group more broadly), (3) cadence of new product launches (NPLs) and how this is tracking vs. the FY guide (note the back-half weighted dynamic), (4) China dynamics and outlook (given the recent strong out-performance of that segment, which has emerged as an important growth driver), (5) update on the Nashik manufacturing facility (i.e., whether this is back at full capacity and implications for the FY guide), (6) F/X trends (given this was a significant tailwind in 1Q), (7) the enterprise-wide review (per management, we expect minimal updates here), and (8) update on the ongoing CFO search (per management, this is progressing well – with both internal and external candidates, but no specific timeline has been yet disclosed).

AMRX (Buy): Building commercial momentum as we monitor additional levers
AMRX shares have outperformed YTD (+34% vs. S&P 500; +40% vs. XLV), driven, in our view, by: (i) positive commentary at and following our June conference regarding the commercial business and pipeline - including commercial momentum for the generics segment (e.g., estrogen patch demand) and branded segment (Crexont and Brekiya), key upcoming launches (e.g., lanreotide and Iohexal), and the pipeline (new men's health specialty product and update to the PFE GLP-1 partnership to be announced in 2H26), (ii) gradual appreciation for the recently announced acquisition of Kashiv Biosicences (which vertically integrates the biosimilars segment), as well as (iii) macro dynamics (i.e., broadening-out trade as the AI/momentum trade has weakened - where AMRX likely

also screens well from a valuation/growth perspective). Overall, we continue to see a compelling diversified growth outlook driven by generics segment momentum, specialty product launches/pipeline, and an emerging obesity/GLP-1 franchise via the PFE partnership.

Ahead of the quarter, we make minimal changes to our estimates, which remain broadly in-line with consensus in FY26-27, but see the potential for a bottom-line beat in 2Q on stronger margins. We look for an updated outlook on the commercial business, including key specialty product launches (Crexont and Brekiya) and the generic segment dynamics (e.g., estrogen patch demand), and FY26 guidance, and will also be focused on: (1) key generic launches (lanreotide Q3 or earlier approval on track - AMRX expects limited competition over the next 2 years; additional dosages of lohexol recently approved with expectations to be the only generic on the market for the foreseeable future); (2) PFE-partnered obesity portfolio (management has expressed further conviction post the recent ADA updates and sees the partnership as a major growth lever not included in the long-term guidance; update on the partnership structure expected over the coming months); (3) key specialty launches (expect continued momentum for Crexont and Brekiya, where management has noted confidence in the upper-end of their respective long-term revenue targets; AMRX also expects to disclose a new men's health product over the near-term), as well as (4) Kashiv Biosciences integration progress (and updates on the biosimilars pipeline) and capital allocation strategy post the deal.

## Model changes, valuation, and risks

TEVA (Buy). We update for the 10Q, recently closed Emalex acquisition (\~\$700mn of IPR&D), and refresh our estimates based on the latest script data and recent company commentary, resulting in FY26/FY27 EBITDA estimates of \$4.3bn/\$5.7bn vs. \$5.0bn/\$5.7bn prior. Our 12-month price target of \$50 (unchanged) is based on a 100% EV/EBITDA valuation methodology with a target EV/EBITDA multiple of 12.3x (vs. 12.1x prior to reflect, in-part, the Emalex acquisition and positive IL-15 data in vitiligo - we do not include sales for either asset in our model), which we apply to NTM estimates 12-months out. Risks: (1) higher-than-expected price erosion in the generics business driven either by greater-than-expected price deflation in the US or emerging markets or failure to execute on new product launches within this segment; (2) commercial setbacks in the branded business, including weaker-than-expected growth for Austedo, Ajovy, or Uzedy; (3) clinical or regulatory setbacks, including negative clinical readouts, delays in clinical development programs, or failure/delays in regulatory approvals; and (4) litigation risks, including settlement agreements or the inability to launch certain products when expected due to patent litigation.

VTRS (Neutral): We refresh our estimates based on the latest company commentary, resulting in FY26/FY27/FY28 EBITDA estimates of \$4.4bn/\$4.6bn/\$4.7bn vs. \$4.5bn/\$4.6bn/\$4.8bn prior. Our 12-month price target of \$18 (vs. \$17 prior) is based on a 100% EV/EBITDA valuation methodology with a target EV/EBITDA multiple of 7.2x (vs. 7.0x prior, given consistent progress/clarity on core business growth), which we apply to NTM estimates 12-months out. Risks to our price target and rating: Downside risks include: (1) higher-than-expected price erosion (within legacy or branded); (2) clinical or regulatory setbacks, such as negative clinical readouts, delays in clinical development programs, or failure/delays in regulatory approvals; (4) extended or

additional manufacturing issues; and (5) litigation risk. Upside risks: (1) greater-than-expected growth in the generics or branded segments; (2) clinical or regulatory pipeline success, including positive clinical readouts or acceleration of clinical development programs; and (3) margin expansion.

AMRX (Buy). We refresh our estimates based on the latest company commentary, resulting in FY26/FY27/FY28 EBITDA estimates of \$755mn/\$845mn/\$924mn vs. \$759mn/\$845mn/\$924mn prior. Our 12-month price target of \$19 (vs. \$17 prior) is based on a 100% EV/EBITDA valuation methodology with a target EV/EBITDA multiple of 10x (vs. 9.5x prior, to reflect increasing confidence around the growth outlook), which we apply to NTM estimates 12-months out. Downside risks to our price target and rating, include: (1) higher-than-expected price erosion in the generics business driven either by greater-than-expected price deflation in the US or failure to execute on new product launches within this segment (e.g., biosimilars); (2) commercial setbacks in the branded business (e.g., weaker-than-expected growth for Crexont, Brekiya, or Unithroid); (3) clinical or regulatory setbacks (e.g., negative clinical readouts, delays in clinical development programs, or failure/delays in regulatory approvals); and (4) litigation (e.g., settlement agreements or the inability to launch certain products when expected due to patent litigation - both of which would impact future cash flows).
