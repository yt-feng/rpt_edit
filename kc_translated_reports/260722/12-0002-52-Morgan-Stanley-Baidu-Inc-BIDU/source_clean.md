# Baidu Files for Dual Primary Listing

We expect the earliest date for Southbound (SB) inclusion to be September 7; meanwhile, non-core asset spin-off should continue to support the share price. Incremental SB fund flows could range from US\$3bn to US\$6.8bn over the first 6-12 months after the inclusion, an estimated 7-15% ownership. EW.

This is another catalyst in addition to non-core asset spin-off/dividends: Baidu announced its plans to convert from secondary listing to dual primary listing on HKEX within 2026. (link). We think it should provide support to the share price with increasing fund flow.

Southbound inclusion timeline: Under current rules, a secondary-listed company with a WVR structure that completes its conversion to a primary listing by September 3, 2026, would be eligible for Southbound inclusion effective at the market opening on September 7, 2026. If this deadline is not met, the next inclusion would be effective at the market opening on March 8, 2027 for companies completing their primary conversion by March 4, 2027.

Case study on duration needed to complete a primary conversion: According to historical primary conversion cases, similar companies with WVR structure have taken roughly two to six months to complete the process after the initial announcement on HKEX. The process could be completed in \~1-1.5 months for companies that have already undertaken preparatory work and are familiar with the conversion requirements.

Expected Southbound flows of tech companies: Companies with a market cap of \~US\$40-45bn have seen SB inflow of US\$3bn-6.8bn over the first 6-12 months following inclusion, equivalent to roughly 7%-15% Southbound ownership over a one-year period. Historically, SB inflows have been front-loaded immediately following inclusion.

Our view: We think key market focus remains on non-core asset spin off, while fundamentals remain weak, with core ad business showing limited recovery and core OP facing downside risks from increasing AI investments in 2H. We expect the share price to get support prior to key events (non-core asset spin-off and Southbound inclusion) but it could still be under pressure over the medium/long term until revenue/earnings stabilize.
