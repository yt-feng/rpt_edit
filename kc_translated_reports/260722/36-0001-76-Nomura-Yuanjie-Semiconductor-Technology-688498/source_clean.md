# Yuanjie Semiconductor Technology 688498.SS 688498 CH
## Strong 1H26 profit alert reaffirms secular optical market trend Quick Note

Margin expansion driven by product upgrade in both telecom and datacom market; concerns about oversupply and price decline might be overdone

During the earnings call, management noted that the strong margin expansion in 2Q26E was driven by structural product upgrade in both the datacom and telecom markets. In the datacom laser chip segment, management noted the 70mW and 100mW CW lasers became the key drivers, and we believe the company is getting higher exposure to the 1.6T transceivers. Moreover, management noted that Yuanjie is developing the Ultra High Power (UHP) CW laser (300mW / 400mW), and it is confident that the company is poised to be one of the first pioneers to benefit from NPO / CPO demand. Despite the current small revenue contribution from EML, management noted the company's 100G EML capacity is expanding and shipments may ramp up quickly from 2H26E. To address market concerns about potentially more competitors and the unfavorable pricing competition, management noted that effective capacity expansion takes more than two years, and it expects pricing of core laser products to remain stable due to the undersupply.
