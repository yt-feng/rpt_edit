# Weekly Coal Update: Thermal Coal Prices Rebounded on Better Demand Outlook Driven by Summer Heats
## Key Takeaways

Thermal coal prices hiked: QHD 5500 inched up 0.1% WoW to Rmb722/t as of July 17. BSPI edged down 0.1% WoW to Rmb713/t. CCI 5500 hiked 2.6% WoW to Rmb821/t. Mine-mouth prices for Shanxi Datong 5800 rose 3.7% WoW to Rmb708/t.

Seaborne prices unchanged: NEWC prices were flat WoW.

Domestic coking coal prices trended down: Liulin No. 4 mine-mouth prices declined 1.7% WoW to Rmb850/t. FOR prices were flattish WoW at Rmb2,010/t. QLD prices dropped 2.1% WoW to US\$233/t.

Chart of the week – Thermal coal prices rebounded on better demand outlook driven by summer heat: China's domestic thermal coal market rebounded in the week ended July 17, with prices advancing across both production areas and northern ports. A widening cost inversion on shipments to port and continued output curbs at production areas under safety inspection lent further support to prices. Expanding high-temperature weather fronts accelerated the rise in thermal generation, with regions such as East China beginning to see modest restocking demand from both utilities and non-power industrial users, including metallurgical and chemical plants, according to Sxcoal.
