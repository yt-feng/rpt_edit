# Horizon 1H26 preliminary results: revenue miss, resilient margin

Horizon released preliminary results for 1H26, revenue missed by $10\%+$ , but margin resilience and disciplined Opex limited the earnings impact. Horizon's preliminary 1H26 revenue midpoint of RMB 2Bn (+30% YoY) missed our RMB 2.24Bn forecast by $10.6\%$ and Bloomberg consensus of RMB 2.36Bn by $15.1\%$ . The sales weakness mainly reflects the sharp decline in China passenger vehicle (PV) sales and slower-than-expected progress in securing new design wins. Gross margin remained resilient at a $63.1\%$ midpoint. Maintaining a $60\%+$ gross margin amid intensifying competition reinforces Horizon's pricing power underpinned by its software & hardware advantages. GAAP net income turned positive at RMB 3.5-4.0Bn, primarily due to a non-cash fair-value gain on convertible bonds following the stock price decline in 1H26. Non-GAAP recurring net loss of RMB 1.4-1.7Bn is close to our forecast. The broadly in-line recurring earnings, despite the material revenue miss, demonstrate disciplined Opex control.

The BYD cooperation should deliver more visible progress in 2H26. Horizon and BYD will soon launch the first GodEye B model powered by J6H, erasing the market concern that BYD's chip self-development would lead it to phase out Horizon's chips. The management expects Horizon's wallet share in GodEye C solution to rise rapidly to $90\%+$ in 2027, from just $40\%$ in 2025. Although it is still uncertain how much share Horizon can get in GodEye B, the achievement is still a very positive move. We still see Horizon as the only credible player to gain share from Nvidia within third party vendors, and in-house chip development still leave meaningful room for third party vendors, the BYD plan seems to playing out as we expected and definitely better than market expectation.

Downside risks look largely priced in, potentially limiting the scope for another sharp near-term de-rating. The correction in the past six months has already reflected weaker PV sales and potential competition from OEM in-house chip development. Management had also communicated the 1H26 pressure in advance, while guiding to a 2H26 recovery and maintaining its full-year shipment target. The shares edged up this afternoon following the preliminary results, suggesting limited incremental downside surprise from the revenue miss. We believe the current depressed valuation offers long-only investors a relatively wide safety margin. Maintain Outperform.


Source: Bloomberg, Bernstein estimates and analysis.
