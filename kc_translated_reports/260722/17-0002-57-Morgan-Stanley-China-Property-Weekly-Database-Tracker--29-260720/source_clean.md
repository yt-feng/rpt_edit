## Weekly Database Tracker #29
## Key Takeaways

Weekly primary registered unit sales in 50 cities increased 3% YoY (vs. +1% YoY in the previous week) for the week ended July 19, bringing YTD sales to -11% YoY: Tier 1 city sales decreased 24% (vs. +40% YoY). Tier 2 city sales increased 14% YoY (vs. +0% YoY). Tier 3 city sales increased 2% YoY (vs. -18% YoY).

Weekly secondary registered unit sales in 10 cities increased 6% YoY (vs. -3% YoY in the previous week), bringing YTD sales to +4% YoY: Tier 1 city weekly secondary unit sales rose 9% YoY (vs. +6% YoY). Tier 2 city weekly secondary unit sales rose 5% YoY (vs. -9% YoY).

Total sell-through rate was 26% (vs. 95% in the previous week): Tier 1 cities recorded a sell-through rate of 4% due to remote location project (vs. 90%). Tier 2 cities recorded a sell-through rate of 48% (vs. 100%).

Centaline six-city secondary asking price tracking index was 16.5% (vs. 16.3% in the previous week), and the property agent index in Tier 1 cities was 52.8 (vs. 51.8 in the previous week).
