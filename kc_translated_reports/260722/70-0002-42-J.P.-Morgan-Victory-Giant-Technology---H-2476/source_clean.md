## Victory Giant Technology - H
## Near-term headwinds, but capacity leadership remains the key differentiator; reiterate OW

Recent investor focus has centered on VGT's initial share allocation in Vera Rubin and the timeline of the Kyber project – although we believe investors have placed too much emphasis on such uncertainties while overlooking the key determinant of market share in the next two to three years. In our view, high-quality manufacturing capacity, not initial share allocation, will ultimately determine suppliers' wallet share for AI PCB projects. While Kyber remains under testing, with higher PCB complexity posing challenges, we believe stronger demand for NVDA's current generations and expanding share in Google's projects should partly offset the impact to VGT if Kyber is delayed. We lower our Jun-27 PT to HK\$500 to reflect these headwinds but believe the recent correction has more than priced in such risks, leaving an attractive risk-reward profile.

\- Initial Rubin allocation does not determine long-term market share. Our supply chain checks suggest that Vera Rubin PCB mass production is progressing slightly behind our previous expectation, while the overall rack rollout remains largely intact. Although VGT's initial share allocation appears lower than in the previous generation, we do not view this as a structural share loss. As production scales, supplier allocation should increasingly depend on capacity support. Given its continued capacity expansion, we expect VGT to regain share during larger-volume production in the coming quarters.

\- Kyber is still progressing toward production readiness. Our research shows that Kyber rack (NLV144) is currently under testing in multiple technology paths, as complex PCB architecture and higher performance targets have raised the bar across material selection, manufacturing processes and system-level assembly. Should Kyber commercialization take longer than expected, we think the earnings impact on VGT should be partly offset by stronger demand for NVDA's current generation and incremental revenue from Google's projects alongside share expansion.

\- Near-term headwinds are more than priced in. We cut our 2026-28E earnings forecasts by $5 - 13\%$ to reflect a slightly later-than-expected ramp of PCB projects scheduled for this year, as well as the potential impact of a later Kyber rollout. Accordingly, we lower our Jun-27 PT to HK\$500, based on 22x 12m rolling forward P/E (prior: 24x, with consideration of ramp-up uncertainties). Following the recent share price correction $(-51\%)$ from the peak on May 28, vs $-5\%$ for HSTECH), we believe the market has overreacted to these short-term headwinds. We view the pullback as an opportunity to accumulate and expect accelerating earnings growth in 2H26 to serve as a catalyst. Reiterate OW.

## Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation

## Investment Thesis

VGT-H is a global leading high-end PCB manufacturer with the No.1 market share in the AI and high-performance computing segments. We believe strong AI demand and continuous PCB content value growth, along with spec upgrades, will enable high-performance computing to keep driving global PCB market expansion. We consider VGT a key beneficiary amid the strong AI PCB cycle. Despite recent headwinds from new projects ramping up and Kyber uncertainties, we believe VGT's industry-leading technologies and accelerating capacity expansion should help to defend its wallet share and drive persistent earnings growth. We forecast an earnings CAGR of 73% over 2025-28 and expect accelerating earnings growth in 2H26 to serve as a share price catalyst. Maintain OW.

## Valuation

Our Jun-27 PT of HK\$500 is based on 22x 12-month rolling forward PE; this is \~15% below its A-share peers' 2027 average (largely on par with the average H/A discount YTD suggested by HSAHP Index) and in line with its global peers' average.
