Q2'26 Earnings Review: Management Team Focused on Compounded Revenue, Margin Trajectory & Capital Allocation; Investor Debates Likely to Persist

## Q2'26 Positives/Negatives

Positives: a) Q2 operating income and GAAP EPS above prior guidance and GSe/street estimates despite weaker revenue with management citing the timing of expenses; b) Positive commentary around growth initiatives including Advertising (reiterated \$3bn target for 2026), Live (expanded NFL agreement, 6 out of top 10 new member sign-up days over the past five years have come from Live), & Gaming (two most successful cloud game debuts in June, 3x growth in Netflix Playground); & c) A ramp up in shareholder returns with Q2 buybacks of \~\$4.7bn and \~\$27.1bn remaining on the existing authorization.

Negatives: a) Q3 Revenue guide below prior GSe/Street (and implying a sequential deceleration in YoY growth) with management citing choppier QoQ comps vs a back-half weighted; b) Q3 Operating Income guide below prior GSe/Street driven by elevated sales & marketing and technology & development to progress its advertising, games, and other key growth initiatives; & c) Stable (but continued soft) engagement trends with a reduced cadence of industry-focused engagement disclosures (now being provided annually instead of semi-annually) and a tilt towards more promotional activity (free trials, lower-priced first months).

## Valuation: Maintain Buy Rating; Lower PT to \$94 (from \$110)

Our \$94 (\$110 prior), 12-month price target is based on an equal blend of: (1) EV/GAAP EBITDA applied to our NTM + 1 year estimates and; (2) a modified DCF using EV/FCF-SBC multiple applied to our NTM + 4 years estimates discounted back 3 years. Specifically:

20.0x EV/GAAP EBITDA (from 22.5x prior) or 0.99x EV/GAAP EBITDA-to-growth (from 1.04x prior) applied to our NTM + 1 year estimates. We lower our absolute multiple to reflect reduced visibility into forward operating performance and apply a 20.0x multiple to maintain our prior \~1.0x multiple-to-growth ratio.

21.0x EV/FCF-SBC multiple (from 24.0x prior) or 1.06x EV/FCF-SBC-to-growth (from 1.07x prior) applied to our NTM + 4 years estimates discounted back 3 years at 12%. We lower our applied absolute multiple to reflect our lower confidence in NFLX's forward FCF trajectory and apply a 21.0x multiple to maintain our prior \~1.1x multiple-to-growth. The discount rate represents CAPM using the blended average of companies within our coverage universe consisting of: (1) 3% risk free rate (based on the normalized 10-year rate); (2) average beta of \~1.3; (3) equity risk premium of 7%.
