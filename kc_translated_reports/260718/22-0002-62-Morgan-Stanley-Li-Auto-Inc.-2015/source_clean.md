# New L6 – Same badge, next-level experience
## Key Takeaways

Li Auto launched the new L6 with a single trim priced at Rmb249.8k, which offers one of the most compelling value propositions in the five-seat SUV segment.

We believe the stronger feature set and price offering should broaden the L6's appeal to young families and reinforce its competitiveness in 5-seat SUVs.

While competing with Tesla Model Y, Xiaomi YU7 and ONVO L80 in the Rmb250-300k segment, we expect L6's run rate to surpass 10k units over the coming months.

L6 and L8/L9 should support sequential delivery growth from July onward, bringing Li Auto's overall deliveries back to YoY growth in 3Q26.

Li Auto launched the new L6 with a single trim priced at Rmb249.8k, matching the entry-level price of the 2025 model despite offering a refreshed exterior design and substantial hardware upgrades. Delivery will commence within a week. Key enhancements include a larger 51kWh battery that extends battery range from 212km to 300km, 5C fast charging that reduces charging time to 12 minutes, and the M100 chip (1,280 TOPS), all aiming to address shortcomings of the previous generation. The facelift also introduces the Snapdragon 8797 cockpit chip, a 29-inch 6K display with AR-HUD, new all-aluminium suspension system, bringing in-cabin and driving experience to the next level.

We expect the refreshed L6 to meaningfully improve sales momentum, with monthly deliveries recovering from an average of 5k units in 1H26 to 10k over the coming months. The stronger L6 contribution, together with L8/L9, should support sequential delivery growth from July onward, bringing Li Auto's overall deliveries back to YoY growth in 3Q26.

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Li Auto Inc. (2015.HK)

Probability-weighted DCF methodology. Derived from our ADR price target, and applying a HKD/USD exchange rate of 7.8.
