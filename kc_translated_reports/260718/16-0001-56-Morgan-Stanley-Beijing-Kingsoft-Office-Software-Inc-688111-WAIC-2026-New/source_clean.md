# Beijing Kingsoft Office Software Inc | Asia Pacific WAIC 2026 New Product Launch
## Key Takeaways

WPS 365 is shifting from an AI office collaboration tool to a one-stop office work platform, cutting into the enterprise management field.

We see KSO's full office + collaboration suite, existing cloud document base, and vertical focus in office scenarios as its key competitive factors.

With AI, the line between tools, agents, and enterprise management software is becoming vaguer.

At the World Artificial Intelligence Conference, Kingsoft Office (KSO) unveiled two products:

\- Lingxi Pro: An advanced office agent for individuals, with add-ins for WPS Office and Microsoft Office. It features project-oriented context management, high-quality task delivery, and professional office software operation.

\- WPS Comate (WPS 365 Upgrade): An enterprise AI workspace with an internal expert/skill/application library and modules for: (1) token auditing (WPS AI Hub), (2) document management (WPS AI Docs), (3) data management (WPS Data Hub), (4) software system integration (WPS API Hub), and (5) security (WPS Trust).
