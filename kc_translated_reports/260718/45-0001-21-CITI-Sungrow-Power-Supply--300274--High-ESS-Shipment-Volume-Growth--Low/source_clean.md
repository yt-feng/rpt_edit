# Sungrow Power Supply (300274.SZ)

High ESS Shipment Volume Growth; Low 2Q26E Margin Probably Reflected in >30% Share Price Drop in July

## CITI'S TAKE

We had due diligence with Sungrow today. The company's tone sounds positive, though not many numbers have been provided. Key points: (i) not very concerned about geopolitical risk, (ii) ESS shipment volumes have high growth; and (iii) new ESS products for AIDC attract much client interest. While another PRC ESS maker Sigenergy (6656 HK, not covered) guides its GP margin of ESS sales to drop 5ppts qoq in 2Q26E owing to increased battery unit cost, we assume less of a cut for Sungrow in 2Q26E (from 30% in 1Q26) for higher product quality. GP margin cut could cause relatively soft 2Q26E earnings for Sungrow but probably largely expected by investors in our view. Looking forward, the margin might be better in 3Q26E as lithium price peaked in mid May and dropped around 25% since then. ESS production cost is 60% from battery which has 20% production cost from lithium.

Comment on geo-political risk - Sungrow showed limited concern on geopolitical issues. It perceives not enough inverter supply if the US and Europe are not using Chinese products which could cause new project development delay. Utility scale projects in the US have majority of them using inverters from Chinese companies. Sungrow also thinks non-Chinese inverter related makers such as SMA Solar (S92 GY, not covered) and TE Connectivity Plc (TEL US, Buy), etc., are probably not competitive compared to Sungrow in terms of product quality. Sungrow reiterates its inverter products without remote access hence should be acceptable by US regulatory requirements.

High ESS shipment growth - ESS shipment volume growth remains robust year to date Sungrow is not concerned about weak domestic demand as suppliers of the domestic market are often not competitive for export which requires time to gain experience and ability. We assume global ESS demand to rise 50-60% yoy in 2026E and 30-40% yoy in 2027E if lithium price stays around current level of Rmb150,000/ton. Sungrow thinks ESS demand to be negatively affected if lithium market price rises to Rmb200,000/ton (from existing price Rmb140,000-150,000/ton) which would lower returns of new ESS projects. Sungrow's battery inventory period is around one month. Sungrow will start to use large battery of 684Ah, vs. currently using 314Ah type, for its ESS production in 1H27E conducive to cost savings. The use of Korean battery for its ESS sales to the US would probably not start until 2H27E.

Update of new product 10kV commercial solid-state transformer ('SST') for 800V high-voltage DC AI data centers - Sungrow signed orders with two domestic buyers when it launched its SST new product for AIDC on 9 July 2026. The company claimed the product has strong interest from such overseas companies as Google and NVIDIA (NVDA US, Buy). It believes more orders to be received from such domestic customers as Tencent (700 HK, Buy). The company assumes its new orders in 2H26E to be probably not a lot at initiation stage but primarily for sampling purpose. Sungrow believes HVDC is a temporary solution whereas 800V DC would be a sustainable solution.

Comment on 2Q26 performance - Sungrow said it had some hedging arrangement mitigating the impact of Rmb appreciation in 2Q26E. Its GP margin of ESS sales could fluctuate depending on battery unit prices and revenue mix by country and projects. We estimate its GP margin of ESS sales in 2Q26E probably at around $26 - 27\%$ , lower than the $30\%$ in 1Q26 but higher than the $24\%$ in 4Q25. We project its net profit to be around Rmb3.0bn in 2Q26E, which would be $-23\%$ yoy but $+31\%$ qoq. Based on investor feedback, we think market expectations of its 2Q26E results are probably not high in view of the increased battery unit cost in the quarter. Sungrow's over $30\%$ share price correction in the last one month, in our view, has reflected the low expectation for its 2Q26E earnings. Sungrow looks inexpensive at 11.6x 2027E PER and 3.0x PB with $29\%$ ROE.

## Sungrow Power Supply

## Valuation

Our target price for Sungrow shares of Rmb185.0 is based on a DCF valuation, which we believe is appropriate because it captures the long-term potential returns of the company. We factor in earnings forecasts up to 2035E and terminal growth of 4%. Our WACC for Sungrow is 10.2%, which assumes: 1) a risk-free rate of 5.2%; 2) a market risk premium of 6.8%; 3) an equity beta of 1.1x; 4) a cost-of-debt of 3.9%; 5) a target debt-to-capital ratio of 30%; and 6) a 25.0% corporate tax rate. At our DCF-based target price, Sungrow would trade at 20.2x 2027E PE and 5.3x PB.

## Risks

Key downside risks that could prevent Sungrow shares from reaching our target price include: (i) slower-than-expected solar installation that could accelerate Sungrow's PV inverter and EPC business growth; (ii) less-than-expected energy storage system demand from China and the overseas market; and (iii) intensification of overseas trade tensions that could lessen the exports of Sungrow's products.

## TE Connectivity Ltd.

(TEL.N; US\$201.34; 1; 15 Jul 26; 16:00)

## Valuation

Our \$230 target price is based on 18x PE applied to our FY27E EPS. Our peer group comprises connectors and semis companies. The peer group trades at a median NTM PE of \~33x, with a range from 110x to 9x. TE Connectivity has historically traded at a discount to peers, and we believe its customer segment and end-market concentration in transportation warrants a discount. We also note historically TEL has traded at a 0.8x PE relative to the SOX, though in upcycles this premium has expanded to 1.05x.

## Risks

Risks to the stock deviating from our target price include the following: 1) a further economic improvement/slowdown that impacts auto production; 2) better/worse restructuring implementation; 3) better/worse acquisition integration; 4) currency fluctuations; and 5) raw material price increases.

## Tencent Holdings

(0700.HK; HK\$484.0; 1; 16 Jul 26; 16:10)

## Valuation

Our target price of HK\$758 is based on a SOTP approach, which implies 22.8x 2026E and \~21.5x 2027E P/E. Our SOTP is based on the following: 1) Online games – We apply a 12x P/E to our 2027 estimated games net profit of Rmb134bn and yield a valuation of HK\$194 per share, or 26% of our target price; 2) Online advertising – We apply a 20x P/E to our 2027 estimated ads profit of Rmb84bn which yields a valuation of HK\$202 per share, or 27% of our target price; 3) Social network – While we attribute the non-gaming revs (community platform revs) as overall social network revs, given the WeChat, QQ, and other Tencent apps, we apply a 25x P/E to the estimated profit of Rmb62bn and yield a valuation of HK\$187 per share, or 25% of our target price; 4) Fintech – We apply a 15x P/E to our estimated profit of Rmb49bn and yield a valuation of HK\$88 per share, or 12% of our target price; 5) Cloud/business services – We apply a 5x P/S (average of China peers) to our 2027 estimated Business services revs of Rmb70bn, which yields a valuation of HK\$42 per share, or 6% of our target price; 6) Investment portfolio – We apply a 30% discount to the equity stake in the publicly listed investment portfolio which implies HK\$46/share.

## Risks

Downside risks that could prevent the Tencent shares from reaching our target price include: i) a faster-than-expected revenue slowdown in core PC games; ii) unsuccessful launches of new mobile games; iii) a decline of Honor of Kings ranking and momentum; iv) a further slowdown in China’s economy, which might negatively affect advertising demand; and v) a change in the regulatory environment.
