# China AI Foundation Model | Asia Pacific
## Moonshot K3: What's Our View
## Key Takeaways

K3 has received positive feedback globally, signaling an all-round catch-up of Chinese LLMs with US leaders in model size, performance, and pricing.

K3, the largest open-weight Chinese LLM so far, may suggest the scaling law still holds. Its high pricing is also positive to LLM market dynamics.

We do not view K3 as an overnight miracle but rather as the result of cumulative progress across China's AI model industry. GLM-5.2, released in June, was already outstanding.

We expect more Chinese LLMs with larger sizes, higher pricing, and better performance, with strong global competitiveness.

On July 17, Moonshot released K3, a 2.8T open-weight LLM specialized in long-horizon tasks, knowledge work, and reasoning, with full weights to be released before July 27. K3 uses original algorithm innovations—Kimi Delta Attention and Attention Residual—to improve compute efficiency. In Artificial Analysis ranking, K3 ranks no. 3 globally, only behind two top US LLMs. In Arena.ai's coding leaderboard, K3 ranks no.1 globally. Its API price is the highest among leading Chinese LLMs, narrowing the gap with US leaders. With 2.8T parameters, it is also the largest open-weight Chinese LLM so far.
