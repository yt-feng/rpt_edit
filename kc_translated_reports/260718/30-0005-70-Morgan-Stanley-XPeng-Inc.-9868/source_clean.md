# MONA L03 – The global launch that funds itself

LO3's aggressive China pricing should drive volume, while its simultaneous Europe launch lifts mix and margins. If supply ramps smoothly, we expect 15k+ monthly sales, with AI providing further upside. Stay OW.


The domestic launch defends share; the European launch pays. This is XPeng's first simultaneous China-Europe launch, rolling out across 65 countries rather than spreading expansion over 18 months in most peers' cases. As a result, overseas mix arrives alongside volume rather than lagging by a year. Looking only at China ASP and gross margin misses the point as a growing share of L03 units sold overseas at more than double the domestic price is margin accretive even as China pricing falls.

In Europe, for Europe: XPeng currently builds G6/G9 with Magna in Graz and plans to expand into sedans and compact/mid-size SUVs, and operates a Munich R&D centre with Bosch and BASF already in its supply chain and 150 dealers signed. XPeng's asset-light approach increasingly looks compelling amid local protectionism.

More than the car: VLA 2.0 comes to European next year. Robotaxi ambitions are also moving overseas; initial Guangzhou pilot programmes are underway while XPeng is engaging potential European partners. Of note, humanoids will arrive first in XPeng's own stores as shopping assistants and cashiers, then in European stores and malls in 2027. All three product lines sit on a shared physical AI foundation.

Our view: The near-term story is not only about volume but mix as Europe is carrying higher ASPs and margins. We look for a successful launch of L03, together with G9L/L05 later on, to anchor the core auto business while non-auto initiatives will drive valuation re-rating in 3Q. Stay OW.
