The results reinforce our positive view that Hygon is emerging as one of the primary beneficiaries of China's rapidly expanding AI computing demand...

## 2Q26 net profit 8% higher than our estimate:

\- 2Q26 revenue rose to Rmb4.9bn, up 21% QoQ, up 59% YoY, largely in line with our estimate. The company attributed the strong revenue growth to strong AI demand, including AI agents, localization drive, and product performance upgrade.

\- 2Q26 net profit was Rmb1.1bn, up 57% QoQ and up 55% YoY, 8% ahead of our estimate.

\- 2Q26 recurring profit was Rmb1.0bn, up 69% QoQ and 57% YoY, suggesting stronger underlying earnings momentum in 2Q26 than reported net profit alone.
