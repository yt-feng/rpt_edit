# 1H26 Preview: Weak Earnings amid Further Margin Squeeze

Developers could report another set of weaker-than-expected results for 1H26, with a 40-50% y-y EPS decline on average amid reduced revenue booking and prolonged deterioration in DP margins. Outlooks for 2H pre-sales, margin, and recurring income will be key.

## Key Takeaways

We expect declines of $>20\%$ in revenue and $>40\%$ in EPS y-y for our coverage amid reduced sales and $\sim 2$ ppt GPM compression in DP business.

DP margin could surprise to the downside with continued home price declines and still deep asset impairment, leading consensus for 2026-28 to drop.

Slow recovery in POE balance sheets given challenging property sales, but SOEs could show lower leverage because of less landbanking in 1H.

## We see weaker-than-expected 1H26 earnings (Exhibit 1):

\- 0-10% declines: CR Land (large disposal gain offsets DP GPM compression) and C&D (higher GPM offsets revenue decline).

\- 10-25% declines: COLI (stable revenue but lower GPM), Jinmao (less disposal gain), and Seazen A/H (reduced DP booking).

\- 50-100% declines: Poly, Longfor, CMSK, Yuexiu, and Greentown (all owing to notably lower DP GPM)

\- Deep losses: Vanke, Gemdale (owing to large DP losses and asset impairment).

\- Profit warnings: Poly, Longfor, Greentown, and Yuexiu, on top of announced players (Vanke, CMSK, and Gemdale).

What to watch next: 1) Pre-sales outlook – we expect most players to tone down full-year sales growth targets despite low bases in 2H. 2) Margin pressure and asset impairment risk owing to continued home price declines. 3) Recurring income outlook – strong mall retail sales in 2H25 present a high base. 4) Refinancing/interest-saving plans of POEs/semi-SOEs.

Stay selective: Considering potentially weaker property sales in 3Q and limited policy upside, we continue to suggest sticking with quality players with credible self-help alpha. We think CR Land (1109.HK), C&D (1908.HK), and Seazen (601155.SS/1030.HK) offer more attractive risk-reward at current valuations. We upgrade Jinmao (0817.HK) to OW for its improving pre-sales outlook (y-y may accelerate in 3Q); current valuation has largely priced in short-term earnings headwinds.

## We Upgrade Jinmao to OW

## Investment thesis

Above-peer sales growth in accelerating mode: Jinmao has notably outperformed peers in terms of contracted sales, growing 15% y-y in 2025 and 8% y-y in 1H26 (vs. -20% and -16% for the top 100 developers). Considering its abundant new launches in 3Q (11-12 brand new projects in top-tier cities vs. six in 1H), which will meet the low base, we expect its pre-sales growth to accelerate to 15-20% y-y in coming months, potentially leading to mid-teens (%) y-y for full-year 2026. This should be a good near-term catalyst for the stock.

Quality landbank in top-tier cities: Jinmao owns one of the largest landbanks in high-tier cities within our coverage, with \~35% of its unsold saleable resources in Tier 1 cities (mainly Shanghai and Beijing), and \~50% in Tier 2 cities. The company has remained proactive in land replenishment, adding \~Rmb30bn new saleable resources from 12 projects in 1H26, including \~40% in Tier 1 cities and \~60% in Tier 2 cities. We believe these quality projects, together with additional projects to be acquired in the next 6-12 months (management sees a better landbanking window in 2H amid a cooling physical market), could sustain Jinmao's above-peer contracted sales growth in 2027.

Well-recognized product quality: Jinmao has been famous for its good product quality among homebuyers, especially for its high-end to luxury housing projects. Considering that the majority of the homebuyers nowadays are buying for living upgrade, we believe the company's strong brand name could support stronger pricing power and higher sell-through rate. These could lead to above-peer profit margin and asset turnover, thus enhancing its ROE in the medium term.

Improving margin outlook: Legacy high-cost projects may still jhamper the margin and earnings recovery of Jinmao's development business in 2026-1H27. However, we believe the increasing contribution of the higher-margin projects in Tier 1 and top Tier 2 cities (high-teens to >20% GPM) acquired in the past two years could lift the development gross margin of the company to 15-17% from 2H27 onward (vs. 13% in 2025 and 14% in 2026E). This could support double-digit (%) earnings growth in 2028 together with strong sales booking.

We think the stock's current 0.4x P/B has priced in the short-term headwinds to earnings. For the longer term, the current share price implies \~6x our estimated normalized earnings and 6-7% dividend yield, which we think looks attractive given its SOE background, large exposure to Tier 1 cities, and recognized product quality.

## Valuation methodology

Our price target of HK\$1.79 is unchanged: This is our base case value, based on a 35% (unchanged) discount to our estimated NAV of HK\$2.75 (also unchanged). See Exhibit 4.

We arrive at this discount based on our developers' scorecard (Exhibit 5), which lists the key parameters that we believe are generally of greatest interest to investors in Chinese developers, including landbank (with a 8/10 score), execution (7), scale (7), growth (9), profitability (7), financing (10), and leverage (6). Jinmao's 35% target discount is in the middle of our range for developers.

Our HK\$2.75/share 2026 NAV estimate comprises:

\- HK\$2.15/share of development properties;

\- HK\$2.02/share of investment properties; and

• HK\$1.42/share of net debt.

Our NAV estimate values the existing landbank and properties based on a sum-of-the-parts methodology:

\- We use DCF to value the development properties, with a WACC of 8.2% (unchanged), in the middle of the range of our covered developers, given its relatively low funding costs.

\- We use a cap rate approach – cap rates of 5-8% on net operating income (NOI) – to value the investment properties.

Our bull case value of HK\$2.20 is based on a shallower NAV discount of 20% (unchanged).

Our bear case value of HK\$1.10 is based on a deeper NAV discount of 60% (unchanged).
