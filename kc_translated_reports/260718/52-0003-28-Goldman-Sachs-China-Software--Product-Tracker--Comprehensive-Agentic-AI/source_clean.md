# China Software: Product Tracker: Comprehensive Agentic AI tools driving token consumption; AI native applications in expansion
## China Software

We reviewed new software product launches from early 2026 to date (Exhibit 1-3), and found that momentum for AI agents and new AI features of software applications remains strong, supported by improving training/ inferencing efficiency leading to higher price-to-performance ratios, enhanced open-source models with more competitive performance, rising ToC (Consumer)/ ToB (Business) user adoption that drives the data flywheel, and a more visible path to commercialization.

\- Agentic AI to cover full work flow: The enhanced foundation models in China support agentic tasks (work assistant, call center, industry expert etc) and coding scenarios, along with rising demand from users and the inflection in token economics. The recent new AI agent launches focus on (1) desktop AI with comprehensive features, (2) AI/ digital employees that minimize repetitive workflows, (3) industry applications across construction/ cybersecurity/ image & video generation etc.

AI features to empower software: Recent new product launches focus on AI native products, including productivity tools with a more specific focus (e.g. WPS Notes, Foxit PDF Editor), entertainment tools targeting new areas (e.g. MVLand to generate visual content for music, AI drama generation) etc. Overall, we expect MAU to continue to scale across AI applications, and ToC/ ToB users will increasingly turn to Gen-AI products to interact with more embedded features.

AI monetization path: With cheaper tokens and expanding user cases, we believe a positive ROI for AI applications to end users will drive further adoption and spending. For software suppliers, we see the gradual migration from subscription fees toward usage-based token fees. However, annual/ quarterly fees still provide the core revenue to cover baseline costs.

Key highlights of new software launches

Meitu (1357.HK, Buy) Video AI solution in expansion: Besides video applications including Wink, Kaipai, Roboneo, the company has also released new products such as MVLAND and Artflo. Artflo is mainly used for inspiration management and conceptual visual creation to empower users' production. MVLAND is used to rapidly generate high quality visuals for music by leveraging multi-Agent collaboration, and management highlights the rising ARPU spending and strong demand from users. We also expect the new version of Kaipai with AI assistant and Roboneo with AI Short Drama features to drive user adoption.

Sensetime (0020.HK, Buy) AI Office products launched: Sensetime has released the open-source tool SenseNova U1 for understanding and generation, and also released its AI office skill suite SenseNova-Skills to better support its AI Office Raccoon, highlighting end-to-end automation of core workflows, including info graphic generation, PPT generation, data analysis, and deep research. We expect its rapid installation and user-configured integration to drive adoption.

Glodon (002410.SZ, Sell) construction AI agents: Glodon's industry AI solution focuses on (1) high-quality data, (2) high-value scenario, and (3) reliable AI model. The company has accumulated effective data assets, develops core AI products including PMLead, PMSmart etc., and established an AI engineering department to accelerate AI agents development. For construction cost, the company leverages AI to enhance products, and is expanding from property projects to new segments including industrial, energy etc. For construction management, the company is upgrading its platform solution and working with multiple clients on flagship projects, and then expanding to more clients.
