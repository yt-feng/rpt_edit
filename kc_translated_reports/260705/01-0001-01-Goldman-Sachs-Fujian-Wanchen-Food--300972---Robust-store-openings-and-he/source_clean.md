## Key Takeaways

## Mgmt decoded the growth path ahead in details:

## Per-Store GMV drivers:

Category expansion: new categories now account for c.5% of total GMV, with chilled/frozen food at c.3% and others (IP, daily essentials, etc.) at c.2%, which is expected to prudently grow with the mix.

☐ Private Label (PL) scaling: PL SKU count grew from 30+ in FY25 to more than double in 2026, aiming for full-category coverage by year-end (expanding from a beverage-centric portfolio now). At this stage, mgmt positions PL to deliver better customer value and product differentiation to drive traffic/sales rather than seeking GPM accretion.

☐ Sharper operational tracking: Mgmt shared that store expansion metrics are monitored closely and rigorously internally, focusing on per-store GMV (monthly/daily sales) and payback periods. Rent-to-sales ratios are strictly controlled by the company as well (through ie. three-party negotiation).

□ Membership operations: mgmt shared that registered members grew from 190mn in end-2026 to 200mn+ with faster growth vs. store expansion. Active members' purchase frequency remained stable at 3 times/month, with a stable monthly contribution of c.Rmb90 per member, while active members numbers also increased vs. end-2025.

■ New store format expansion testing: Wanchen is actively exploring new formats to drive long-term differentiation:

☐ Discounter supermarket: An iterative community discount format where ambient snacks and beverages contribute 90% of GMV, while other categories (chilled/frozen, IP, daily essentials) make up 10% in stores carrying these SKUs.

Full-category hard discounter (Huishengjia / 惠省嘉): \~10 stores opened in Guangdong since early 2026, currently testing product assortments and locations with PL focus.

☐ Convenience Store format: Currently, only one pilot store is operating in Changsha, but mgmt is testing the model of which the expansion runway remains vast (c.320k CVS stores in China).

☐ Fresh Snacks: Mgmt is monitoring this high-growth niche-category store format in the industry while remain cautious about the scalability due to high cold chain costs/spoilage ratio/primary location requirement. Meanwhile, the company is testing cold-chain products.

Logistics and fulfillment efficiency: The company is investing in more warehouses to support faster replenishment requirement as outlet network expands. Despite these new investments, mgmt expects the overall logistics expense ratio to decline yoy driven by improved fulfillment efficiency.

## Price Target Risks and Methodology - Fujian Wanchen Good

Valuation: Our 12m TP is Rmb319, based on a target 2027E P/E at 21.5x discounted back to end-2026E at an $8.2\%$ COE. The target P/E is at a HSD% valuation discount to Busy Ming, as we reference the average gap of DLTR's NTM P/E vs. DG's in the past two years, factoring in the relatively slower network scaling momentum and less favorable UE (lower per-store GMV but profits driven by high mix of smaller brands/white labels).

Key risks: 1) intensifying market competition and price sustainability; 2) franchise model risk and scaling complexity; 3) M&A risks; 4) supply chain, food safety and logistics.
