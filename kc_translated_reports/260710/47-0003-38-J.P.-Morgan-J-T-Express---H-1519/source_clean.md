# J&T Express - H

2Q26 update: Macro volatility no barrier to scale, quality, and accelerating global growth

J&T's 2Q26 operational update held today (8 July) marks a clear transition from ‘build-out + losses’ to ‘scale, productivity, and capital return’. J&T Express’s robust operational trends in the past one quarter, despite the macro headwinds, reinforce our positive stock view and initiation thesis that J&T’s platform-neutral, exportable operating model is scaling outside China. The group delivered 9.18B parcels (+24.2% Y/Y), with daily average volume surpassing 100MM for the first time – a milestone that underpins the company’s ability to drive further cost deflation and service consistency. Notably, ex-China parcels accounted for 32.3% of the total, highlighting the growing breadth of J&T’s global platform. The company’s new HK\$2B buyback plan, at current share price levels, equates to a c.2.4% buyback yield, providing a tangible technical floor for the stock as fundamentals inflect. Management reiterated that March guidance remains unchanged, signaling confidence in the durability of the volume and margin trends, despite ongoing macro and sector volatility.

Stock view: The share price is up c.14% over the past week (vs HSI up c6%) in anticipation of the strong quarterly print (with the date for today's operational update first released on 1 July), so today's muted response post our initiation is not a surprise – especially given the day's AI-driven rotations into HK's index-heavy names such as Alibaba amid fund rebalancing. YTD, the stock remains down 9% but is modestly ahead of HSCI (down 12%) on a relative basis. We stay fundamentally bullish: today's datapoints and briefing takeaways reinforce the re-rating case, with scale, cost/automation proof, and capital return all tracking ahead of plan. The next catalyst is whether these operating trends translate into earnings upgrades at the interim results, in our view. Maintain OW.

\- Southeast Asia remains the core engine, with growth increasingly driven by platform investment and rising logistics standards rather than just pricing. SEA parcels reached 2.75B in 2Q26 (+63.2% Y/Y), with daily average volume of 30.3MM. Management attributes this momentum to continued e-commerce penetration, sustained platform client investment in promotions and free shipping, and a deliberate push into non-platform parcels as product, service quality, and brand visibility improve. Importantly, platforms are raising their requirements for logistics partners, which is shifting competition towards scaled operators that can deliver on cost, coverage and reliability. We believe this dynamic supports J&T's positioning as a platform-neutral consolidator with a structural cost and network advantage, and helps mitigate the risk of yield erosion as delivery speeds converge across the industry.

\- China is acting as a stabilizer with upside, with share gains driven by network quality and customer mix rather than aggressive pricing. China parcels reached 6.21B in 2Q26 (+10.6% Y/Y), with daily average volume of 68.2MM. Management benchmarked industry growth at c.5% Y/Y for Jan-May 2026, positioning J&T as a share gainer through network upgrades, improved service quality, cost control, and technology enablement. The sector is shifting from pure growth to a focus on scale, quality, and efficiency, and J&T is seeing rising recognition from branded and higher-quality customers. The key for investors is whether these share gains can be sustained without sacrificing unit economics, especially as the competitive environment remains structurally intense.

\- Other Markets is now a credible incremental engine, with LATAM leading and five consecutive quarters of acceleration. Other Markets parcels reached 210MM in 2Q26 (+136.5% Y/Y), marking the fifth straight quarter of acceleration. Management highlighted LATAM as the strongest contributor, while Middle East growth was constrained by conflict. The TAM argument is compelling: higher GDP/capita and consumption, but structurally lower e-commerce penetration and parcels per capita vs China/SEA, provide a long runway for growth if execution holds. J&T is exporting its China/SEA playbook – automation, route optimization, last-mile efficiency – and deepening partnerships with Mercado Libre and Chinese platforms. New market entries (Colombia launched in 2Q, Peru and Central America under evaluation) and a preference for asset-light, compliant entry in the US and Europe further support the long-term growth story.

\- Buybacks, automation, and capex discipline are now central to the equity story, bridging fast growth to compounding quality. Management provided quantifiable automation outcomes, such as the Tangerang (Indonesia) project reducing processing time by 40% and boosting peak sorting efficiency by 150%. The company reiterated full-year capex guidance of US\$700-800MM, with investment priority across China, SEA, and Other Markets, and incremental focus on LATAM. The new HK\$2B buyback plan, at the current share price and share count, equates to a c.2.4% buyback yield – now a meaningful support for sentiment as the market increasingly demands tangible capital return alongside operational momentum.

## Investment Thesis

J&T Global Express is a leading platform-neutral, technology-driven 3PL operator – the market leader in Southeast Asia, a growing player in China, and an emerging challenger in high-growth New Markets (Brazil, Mexico, Middle East). The company's regional sponsor model combines entrepreneurial local execution with centralized technology, enabling rapid, capital-light expansion, while China-built automation and digital management systems are exported across regions, underpinning a structurally advantaged cost curve. Since its October 2023 IPO, J&T's share price has underperformed, reflecting sector price wars in China, initial loss-making status, and external shocks such as recent fuel price volatility. The market has also questioned the scalability of J&T's model outside China and the sustainability of margins in a maturing Southeast Asia, while New Markets were seen as too early-stage to move the needle. However, the business has now pivoted to profitability, is delivering industry-leading volume and margin growth, and is unlocking new synergies through its partnership with SF Holdings. Overweight on J&T.

## Valuation

Our Jun-27 P/E-based PT of HK\$13.0 is based on J&T's FY27 Non-IFRS EPS of US\$0.12, 13x target P/E (vs industry average of 11x, Bloomberg estimates, as of Jul 7, 2026) and USD/HKD conversion rate of 7.8.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Downside risks to our rating and price target include: 1) continued structural margin compression in China, if anti-involution weakens; 2) slowdown of SEA ecommerce GMV growth and increasing competition from competitors like SPX etc; 3) execution and profitability slippage in New Markets as heavy upfront investment and slower-than-expected localization of franchise models prolong the path to scale; and 4) geopolitical, regulatory, and trade-policy shocks could increase compliance costs and directly pressure cross-border parcel flows and demand.
