## Gold Mining 2Q26 Quarterly Preview
## Picking the bottom is tricky but miners are well positioned

Gold doubled from 4Q24-1Q26, driven by reserve diversification and debasement concerns rather than traditional drivers (real rates and the USD). The Middle East conflict proved to be the trigger for gold's historical macro drivers to re-emerge with higher energy-driven inflation, hawkish Fed, rising real yields, and a stronger USD triggering a \~25% correction. Near-term downside remains if US data remains robust & yields continue rising, but structural support remains from central bank buying, rising sovereign debt/de-dollarization; positioning has reset and the risk/reward is improving.

Gold miners (GDX) have corrected \~35% from the highs and face near-term headwinds from earnings downgrades, higher energy costs, and potential cost guidance pressure. However, compared with prior cycles, the sector is entering a downturn from a position of strength, with record margins, strong balance sheets (mostly net cash), robust free cash flow (at spot \~6x EV/EBITDA and 7% FCF yield), disciplined capital allocation, and capacity for buybacks and opportunistic M&A. If gold falls further, miners will likely follow, but in our view they are more resilient than in previous bear markets.

## 2Q26: Cost pressures evident but guidance likely to be largely unchanged

Consensus financial numbers need to come down, but largely due to 2Q gold price MTM. Energy cost pressures will be evident, but part offset by FX and lower royalties with limited risks to FY26 cost guidance.

\- Newmont 2Q operationally in-line with production on track but FCF more than halving q/q due to higher capex and working-capital headwinds, driving lower buybacks.

\- Barrick 2Q looks in-line with a modest q/q production uplift, supported by stable-to-improving NGM performance, though higher fuel-driven costs remain a key watchpoint; FY guidance appears achievable and Mali operations remain unaffected by regional unrest.

\- Agnico will again deliver consistent performance with slightly higher diesel-driven costs and weaker FCF from \~\$625m of investment outflows and seasonal capex. News of wall movement at Barnet pit at Canadian Malartic given 60-80koz potential cut this year.

\- Kinross 2Q mostly follows 1Q and in line with expectations, FCF impacted on prices and capex timing, with buyback cadence held up in 2Q.

\- Lundin Gold 2Q looks soft but expected (\~108koz) on lower grades and recoveries, with guidance increasingly looking 2H-weighted and toward the low end ahead of a busier 2H with various updates.

\- SSR 2Q looks weak with UBS at \~100koz vs 112koz consensus as CC&V and Marigold decline q/q; lower volumes should drive unit costs above guidance range with FY26 slightly stretched.

\- Wheaton revenue flat q/q driven by +10-13% GEO sales with full quarter of BHP Antamina, offset by higher interest expenses, depletion and lower prices (silver in particular).

\- Franco Nevada tracking in line with flat q/q despite higher energy contribution with guidance 2H weighting.

\- Royal Gold improve q/q with improved volume throughout year on sequencing and ramp up of various assets.

## Wheaton Precious: Buy rated, \$150/sh (Aug 6th)

Revenue broadly flat from higher sales. Relatively uneventful 2Q, with GEO production flat q/q and sales volumes up 10-13% following a seasonally weak 1Q. Silver sales should increase >20% from full quarter with BHP Antamina stream and strong Peñasquito deliveries, while gold sales are flat as stronger Salobo offset weakness at Constancia following Pampacancha depletion. EPS headwinds from \~\$30m of interest expense (\~\$0.06/sh) and higher depletion and COGS associated with the new Antamina stream. 45:55 production split through the year with guidance well on track given ramp ups account for <3% of guidance. Move to net debt position reflecting \~\$4.5bn cash outflow from stream acquisitions/financing (mainly Antamina \$3.4bn).
