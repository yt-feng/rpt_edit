# Greater China Technology Hardware | Asia Pacific
# Chart of the Day: China PC and Tablet Shipments in May 2026
## Key Takeaways

China's PC shipments decreased 14% y/y and tablet shipments declined 7% y/y in May.

China PC shipments -14% y/y in May-26: According to IDC, China's PC shipments in May-26 were 2.89mn units (+2% m/m, -14% y/y). YTD, China's PC shipments totaled 13.6mn (-8% y/y).

\- Lenovo: 1,114K units (+29% m/m, -6% y/y), 4.65mn (-1% y/y) YTD

• Huawei: 192K (-24% m/m, -39% y/y), 1.16mn (-26% y/y) YTD

• ASUS: 302K units (+20% m/m, -4% y/y), 1.19mn (flat y/y) YTD

• HP: 255K (flat m/m, -19% y/y), 1.09mn (-9% y/y) YTD

• Tongfang: 94K (+11% m/m, -12% y/y), 644K (+9% y/y) YTD

• Apple: 218K (-9% m/m, +45% y/y), 937K (+48% y/y) YTD

• Dell: 46K (-63% m/m, -39% y/y), 506K (-14% y/y) YTD

\- Acer: 27K (+60% m/m, -15% y/y), 98K (-21% y/y) YTD

China tablet shipments -7% y/y in May-26: According to IDC, China's tablet shipments in May-26 were 2.81mn units (+26% m/m, -7% y/y). YTD, China's tablet shipments totaled 13.2mn (-6% y/y).

• Huawei: 864K units (+27% m/m, +7% y/y), 4.23mn units (-4% y/y) YTD

• Apple: 648K units (+19% m/m, -13% y/y), 3.11mn (-6% y/y) YTD

• Xiaomi: 261K units (+31% m/m, -46% y/y), 1.32mn (-41% y/y) YTD

\- Lenovo: 303K units (+70% m/m, +42% y/y), 1.01mn (+25% y/y) YTD
