# Chengxin Lithium Group Co. Ltd. | Asia Pacific
# Strong Preliminary 1H26 Results; Raise PT


1H26 benefits from a high lithium price and its mid-stream conversion to volume growth. Valuation looks fair. Stay EW.

Strong preliminary 1H26 result: Chengxin expects 1H26 net profit to be Rmb1-1.2bn vs. a Rmb841mn loss in 1H25. 2Q26 net profit is implied to be Rmb536-736mn vs. Rmb464mn in 1Q26. Recurring het profit is even better: Rmb1.3-1.5bn in 1H26 and implied to be Rmb678-878mn in 2Q26 vs. Rmb622mn in 1Q26. We think the major recurring item is the lithium hedging loss realized in 1Q26. Management attributed the strong earnings preview YoY to much higher lithium prices and substantial growth in shipment volume from its Indonesia factory.

We raise our PT to Rmb42.00 and stay EW: We raise our ASP forecasts for Chengxin referencing our commodity team's latest price deck, and adjust SG&A and financial expense forecasts based on 1Q26 actuals. Our EPS estimates rise $54\%$ for 2026 and $49\%$ for 2027. We also raise our DCF-based PT to Rmb42.00, implying 19.5x 2026E P/E. We stay EW on the stock, as the valuation looks fair considering near-term dilution from its private placement (to be completed in 2026) and longer-term upstream volume growth from its Murong lithium mine (2028 onward).
