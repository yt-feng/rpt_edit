# Sustaining Positive Sales Trend

We hosted an investor group call with the company. YTD sales appear to be well on track with the full-year target, which was maintained. We remain OW on the stock.

## Key Takeaways

Mengniu saw \~high single digit sales growth in 5M26. It expects mid-high to high single digit sales growth for 1H26 if June sales is on track.

■ UHT milk recovered to positive growth YTD, and other key segments maintained strong growth momentum.

Raw milk prices are stable on a MoM basis, leading to more rational competition.

2026 target unchanged: mid-single digit top-line growth in 2026, with liquid milk recording low single digit growth. OPM is seen flat yoy.

Mengniu expects steady improvement in the dividend payment amount on healthy cash flow generation and lower capex going forward.

## UHT milk:

\- Positive growth in 2Q26, despite a slower pace vs. 1Q, primarily due to positive support from the CNY effect in 1Q yoy. Growth is seen mainly driven by volume, with relatively weak like-for-like pricing yoy on a higher base in 1H25 (2H25 had more promotions).

\- Better mix trends, with Milk Deluxe outperforming basic UHT milk.

\- Yogurt and milk beverage performances are stabilizing, and are seen flat or down slightly in 1H26.

## Fresh milk:

\- Mengniu sees growth acceleration to $25 - 30\%$ in 1H26. It targets double digit sales growth for 2026.

\- OPM is still low at the current stage, but could improve toward the group level when it scales up in the next 3-5 years.

## Milk powder:

\- Overall milk powder segment is expected to achieve over $30\%$ growth in 1H26.

\- Bellamy's has shown strong growth in cross-border sales to China with continued Southeast Asia market penetration. Domestic brands have solid growth.

\- Adult powder is growing faster than infant powder; quality and functional needs are the future focuses.

The tax change should not affect its operations, competitiveness and long-term strategy, in our view.
