# China International Capital Corporation (3908)

## 2Q26 results beat, expect consensus upgrades to drive re-rating

2Q26 result a strong beat. After market close on 8 July, CICC announced its preliminary 1H26 results (link). 2Q26 net profits rose 79-102% y/y to Rmb4.1-4.6bn, a record high and likely ahead of market expectation. More importantly, we believe the quality of CICC's result is stronger than that of CMS and GTHT (link, link), given CICC is less exposed to A share IPO co-investments and pre-IPO investments, which are more sensitive to market volatility. Thus, we believe that CICC's strong business momentum is more likely to be sustained into 2H26. Looking ahead, we expect consensus earnings upgrades to drive a re-rating, as 1H26 ROE of 14-15% is materially higher than BBG FY26 consensus of 9.8%. We stay OW and reiterate CICC-H as our sector top pick.

\- 2Q26 results a beat: After market close on 8 July, CICC issued an exchange announcement on its preliminary 1H26 results (link). According to the announcement, its net profit will reach Rmb7.7-8.2bn in 1H26, +78-90% y/y. This implies 2Q26 net profits of Rmb4.1-4.6bn, +79-102% y/y. The result is largely inline with JPMe but likely ahead of market expectations, as 1H26 net profits represent 52-55%/62-66% of the JPMe and BBG consensus FY26 net profits, respectively.

\- Result quality appears stronger than GTHT and CMS: GTHT and CMS reported their preliminary 1H26 results over the past week (link, link). In our view, their results were partly driven by investment gains from IPO co-investments and pre-IPO investments. As a result, investors may question the sustainability of their strong earnings, particularly given recent market weakness. By comparison, we believe CICC's strong performance was mainly driven by client demand-led businesses. Therefore, we view the quality of CICC's results as stronger than that of CMS and GTHT, and expect its strong momentum to be sustained into future reporting periods.

\- Consensus upgrades could drive re-rating: We estimate CICC's ROE reached 14–15% in 1H26, compared with 9.4% in 2025 and Bloomberg consensus FY26 ROE of 9.8%. This suggests room for consensus earnings upgrades, in our view. In our recent note, we expected CICC's ROE to reach 11.3% by 2027 under our base-case scenario and 13.5% under our blue-sky scenario (link). We now see upside risk to those assumptions. CICC-H is trading at 0.72x forward P/B, compared with 1.0–1.3x forward P/B during the 2020–21 bull market, while its 1H26 ROE has already returned to the 2020–21 level. We expect consensus earnings upgrades to drive a re-rating.

## Investment Thesis

We are positive on CICC for the following reasons: (1) CICC is uniquely positioned to benefit from the technology-sector growth cycle; (2) we view CICC as a key beneficiary of institutional business recovery; (3) the acquisition of two small brokers should be complementary to CICC's existing business, and support profits and EPS growth.

## Valuation

Our Jun-27 PT of HK\$28.50 is derived from a combination of DDM for FY26-28E dividends and terminal value based on ROE-g/COE-g methodology. We compound the time value to derive a fair value of Rmb24.8 for Jun-27 and use spot CNY/HKD to convert to HKD. Key assumptions include: a risk-free rate of 1.8%, an equity risk premium of 8.0%, a beta of 1.15x and a long-term growth rate of 4.5%.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Downside risks to our rating and price target include: (1) a material market correction that triggers government intervention, such as contributions to stabilization funds and the suspension of IPO reviews; and (2) stronger growth in capital-intensive businesses, which might lead to potential equity fundraising and result in dilution risks.
