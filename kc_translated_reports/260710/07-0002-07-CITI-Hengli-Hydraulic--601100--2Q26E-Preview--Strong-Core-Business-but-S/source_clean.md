# Hengli Hydraulic (601100.SS)

2Q26E Preview: Strong Core Business but Sizable FX Loss Again; Humanoid Robot in Progress

## CITI'S TAKE

While we expect Hengli to deliver 30% YoY revenue growth in 2Q26E driven by strong excavator component demand, we estimate its net profit to come in at Rmb888mn in 2Q26E, up by only 9% YoY (up 36% QoQ) and 2% ahead of Visible Alpha consensus due to \~Rmb200mn FX loss in 2Q26E (vs. \~Rmb26mn FX gain in 2Q25). Regarding the humanoid robot business, Hengli is currently supplying planetary roller screws to support its US client to produce \~100 units of humanoid robots per week, and based on our industry talk, the US client could ramp production to \~1k/\~19k in 3Q/4Q26 and further increase volumes to 200k in 2027. We therefore estimate Hengli's humanoid robot revenue contribution to jump from \~1% in 2026E to \~8% in 2027E. We recommend investors use the FX or fund-flow-led share price weakness to accumulate the stock. Reiterate Buy.

2Q26E GPM – We expect Hengli's GPM to stay flat YoY at 44.0% in 2Q26E as Mexico and ball screw & linear guide plants could negatively impact the overall GPM before the two factories scale up. Upside surprises, if any, could come from the ball screw and linear guide plant due to the spill-over effect on these products.

Compared to machinery OEM – While the downstream machinery OEMs' consolidation has come to an end, we think that the component makers' market share reshuffle is an ongoing event. We pointed out in 2024 that by establishing a JV with Eddie Precision (603638.SS), Kawasaki Heavy (7012.T) is gradually retreating from China's construction machinery component market, favorable to Hengli from the market share perspective (Figure 7).

Humanoid robot potential – By acquiring the linear motion R&D team from a leading European bearing firm before and producing planetary roller screws in China by its ball screw & linear guide veterans, Hengli is competitive enough to penetrate the leading US humanoid robot maker. Besides planetary roller screws, Hengli strives to win orders of micro motors and micro screws for dexterous hands.

Earnings revision – We slightly increase our 2027E earnings forecasts by 1% to reflect higher humanoid robot revenue. We reiterate Buy on Hengli for its strong core business and “humanoid robot optionality” (Figure 4 and 6).

## Hengli Hydraulic

## Company description

Jiangsu Hengli Hydraulic is a China-based company principally engaged in the research, development, manufacture and distribution of hydraulic parts & components. Hengli is China's No.1 hydraulic parts supplier. The company's products mainly include hydraulic cylinders, pumps and valves, hydraulic systems, and precision casting, among others. The company distributes its products within the domestic market and also to overseas markets. The company was listed on A-share market in October 2011.

## Investment strategy

We rate Hengli Buy as: 1) we expect not only sequential business recovery but also a bright 2026, driven by not only improving excavator component demand but also non-excavator component demand recovery as well as new order wins; and 2) we also expect its humanoid robot revenue exposure to lead to share price rerating.

## Valuation

Our target price for Hengli of Rmb160.0 is based on 50x 2027E P/E, which is equal to its average P/E + 0.5x SD, to reflect its strong core business and potential re-rating due to rising humanoid robot exposure.

## Risks

Key fundamental downside risks include: 1) weaker demand for excavator and non-excavator components; 2) weaker profitability of ball screw and Mexico plants due to weaker economies of production scale; and 3) lower-than-expected GPM on favorable product mix change. Any of these risk factors could cause the stock to deviate from our target price.
