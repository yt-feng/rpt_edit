# Scaling on track: record slurry month; photoresist ramping up Quick Note

The earnings inflection was broad-based across the semiconductor materials platform. i) CMP slurry and cleaning solution revenue rose 63% y-y, generating profit at scale of c.CNY47mn, with June monthly sales exceeding CNY40mn — a record high — and management guiding further margin gains in 2H26E. ii) CMP pad revenue grew 57% y-y, with June shipments surpassing 50k pieces for the first time, and the company has started building a third soft-pad line at Qianjiang, targeting glass-substrate and large-size (>2m diameter) pads. iii) In high-end wafer photoresist, Dinglong secured c.1,000 gallons of purchase orders in 1H26, has cumulatively developed 40+ SKUs with nearly 30 under customer validation — in-testing SKU count doubling y-y — and is adding BARC (bottom anti-reflective coating) and SOC (spin-on carbon) ancillaries. iv) Display materials revenue declined slightly on soft panel utilization, though PSPI (photosensitive polyimide) and TFE-INK (thin-film encapsulation ink) achieved first batch supply on China's first G8.6 AMOLED (active-matrix organic light-emitting diode) line. v) In addition, the revenue of newly-consolidated Haofei Materials (Unlisted) grew c.60% y-y over March-June and, with the parent, is intensively developing innovative lithium-battery functional materials addressing safety, thermal management, electrical performance and energy density with top domestic customers.

Semi renaissance thesis intact: slurry runway broadens and photoresist inflects
We believe the pre-announcement reinforces the structural growth thesis in our 21 May 2026 report: A guide to Semi renaissance in 2026-30F. First, we expect slurry wins to broaden the runway: qualifications in large-silicon-wafer fine slurry (12-inch single-crystal), ceria slurry at a leading domestic memory maker and TSV (through-silicon via) slurry at a leading advanced-packaging house unlock a domestic TAM (total addressable market) of over CNY1bn currently dominated by imports. Secondly, photoresist is inflecting: three ArF/KrF (argon fluoride/krypton fluoride) products are already in stable mass supply, and we expect 2026F to mark the first year of scaled volume supply, supported by a cumulative portfolio of 40+ high-end wafer photoresists plus BARC/SOC ancillary materials. With domestic localization rates at only \~15% for KrF and 2-3% for ArF, penetration headroom remains, in our view.

We maintain our Buy rating and TP of CNY104, which is based on 96x 2026F EPS, at +2.5x SD of its 5-year historical average P/E of 51x. The stock currently trades at 89x 2026F P/E.
