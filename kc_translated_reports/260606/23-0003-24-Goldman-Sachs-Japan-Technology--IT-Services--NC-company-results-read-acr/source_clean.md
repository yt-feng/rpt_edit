# Japan Technology: IT Services: NC company results read-across (5) (manufacturing/automotive): Systena, Argo Graphics, DIT

In this report, we consider the implications for our IT services sector coverage from visits we made to three Not Covered companies (we have no investment views on these companies) in the manufacturing/automotive system integrator space:

# Systena (in-car systems/SI), Argo Graphics (automotive

# CAD/semiconductor-related systems), and Digital Information Technologies

(DIT; in-car systems/SI). At Systena, the favorable business environment continues, centered on development projects for the mobility and financial sectors, but the company sees FY3/27 operating profits growing only modestly, due to an increase in share-based compensation costs from the issuance of new stock acquisition rights.

At Argo Graphics, FY3/27 operating profit guidance is for a yoy decline, due to investment curbs by major automotive customers and higher depreciation costs for a data center. At DIT, 3Q (Jan-Mar) operating profits declined for the second consecutive quarter, due to investment curbs by multiple automotive customers, and were below guidance.

# In terms of read-across, the declining investment appetite in the automotive sector is a slight negative for the overall IT services sector demand environment.

While there are no major system integrators with high sales exposure to the automotive sector, we will be closely watching trends at system integrators that serve a wide range of industries, such as Fujitsu (Buy) and NEC (Buy). Below we outline our key takeaways from company commentary (see Exhibit 1/Exhibit 2/Exhibit 3/Exhibit 4 for earnings tables).

# Systena (2317.T) (in-car systems/SI)

(1) Guidance: FY3/27 operating profit guidance is for modest growth to ¥16.0 bn (+4% yoy). The main reason for this is an increase in share-based compensation costs (+¥0.84 bn yoy) from the issuance of new stock acquisition rights. Excluding this impact and depreciation, the company forecasts that EBITDA will continue to grow by double digits (%) at +11% yoy. Systena expects the business solutions business to see a decline in sales and profits, due to the peaking out of the PC replacement cycle. However, excluding this business, it forecasts sales and profits to increase, with the favorable business environment for the core development business expected to continue (the company noted the impact from the situation in the Middle East is limited).

While Systena aims to grow all core businesses, it expects the digital integration business to drive growth as it continues to see strong inquiries for core system renewals and other projects. According to the company, this target does not factor in efficiency improvements from data-driven management or reallocation of human resources, which could be an upside factor for earnings.

# Argo Graphics (7595.T) (automotive/semiconductors)

(1) Guidance: FY3/27 operating profit guidance is ¥10.3 bn (-4% yoy), which would mark the first yoy decline in six years since the COVID-19 pandemic. Although investment by major semiconductor customers is showing signs of recovery, the company expects investment by major automotive customers to be curbed, and depreciation costs for the data center (DC) that began operations in Kitami City, Hokkaido, in April will increase. The company expects annual depreciation for the DC to be over ¥0.4 bn, with the expense to be incurred from 1Q (Apr-Jun). According to the company, a managed service that provides systems the company owns as fixed assets will start in 1Q, and depreciation for these systems will be incurred upfront.

(2) System products business (non-consolidated): FY3/26 sales were down -0.5% yoy. Within this, PLM sales were up +2% yoy (sluggish growth in automotive), High Performance Computing (HPC) sales were up +12% yoy (contribution from large projects), and IT sales were down -13% yoy (decline for semiconductor sector). For FY3/27, the company forecasts a slight increase in PLM sales, a decrease in HPC sales, and an increase in IT sales. According to the company, (a) in addition to weak earnings across the auto industry, IT investment is being curbed, due to customer-specific factors (strategic changes), making the outlook for the auto business still challenging; (b) on the other hand, multiple major customers are planning to expand investment in semiconductors (at their Hokkaido/Kyushu/Mie plants).

# Digital Information Technologies (3916.T) (in-car systems)

(1) 3Q6/26 (Jan-Mar): Operating profits declined for the second consecutive quarter, falling by -5% yoy to ¥0.88 bn. This was attributable to the drop-out of large-scale projects and moves to curb investment in the high-margin automotive business. Profits were below guidance, mainly in the embedded solutions and

product solutions businesses. The company left full-year guidance unchanged, implying 4Q operating profit guidance of ¥0.6 bn (+22% yoy) and the need to achieve a substantial increase in profits. DIT said that while achieving sales guidance would be difficult, it aims to achieve profit guidance by curbing strategic investments (development of security products, AI investment, internal system investment, etc.) and bonuses.

(2) Embedded solutions business (embedded systems): 3Q sales and gross profits turned down yoy by -1% and -5%, respectively. In addition to the pullback from large-scale projects booked in 3Q6/25, the investment appetite of major automotive customers is declining rapidly. Both the development and verification businesses are thus facing strong headwinds. According to the company, this trend is likely to continue into FY6/27, and it will need to be offset by the business solutions business (development and maintenance of business systems).
