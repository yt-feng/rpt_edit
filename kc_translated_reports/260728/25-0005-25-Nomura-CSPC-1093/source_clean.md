# Positive profit alert: drug sales good, recurring earnings not Quick Note

Results came in higher than our estimates, due to recognition of meaningful collaboration revenue

On 27 July after market close (HKT), CSPC announced a positive profit alert: management expects the company's net profit to shareholders to reach CNY5.9-6.2bn in 1H26E (vs CNY2.55bn in 1H25).

Management attributes this significant increase to: 1) recognition of c.CNY5.74bn of collaboration revenue from upfront payment on the deal with AstraZeneca (AZN LN, Not rated) in peptide drugs, and 2) a stable $c.10\%$ y-y growth in finished drugs sales in 1H26E.


## Recurring earnings seems soft, deserves further monitoring

Excluding the above big-ticket collaboration revenue (assuming a tax rate of 20%), earnings likely retreated 43%-5% y-y to c.CNY448mn-748mn, which we reckon might be due to: 1) higher R&D expenditure for progressing more late-stage clinical trials, and 2) drag from the sluggish bulk drug business.

We are positive to learn the company's finished drug sales came in higher than our estimates, while we suggest monitoring the reasons behind the downbeat recurring earnings performance once the final interim results are released.

We maintain our Buy rating and DCF-based TP of HKD10.31 (assuming a WACC of 9.7% and terminal growth rate of 3.5%). The stock currently trades at 15.8x FY26F fully diluted EPS of CNY0.47.
