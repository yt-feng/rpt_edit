# Week 30 Wrap - Transactions gains extended while market activities broadly remained stable
## Key highlights for the week:

Market performance: Transaction volumes extended gains in both primary/secondary markets at +12%/+2% wow in primary/secondary, concluding July at +7%/+3% yoy, respectively. Market activities likewise stayed stable with 1) new home searches activities improving +1.2% wow; 2) secondary subscription sales and visitations both flattened wow; 3) transaction prices saw modest improvements in monitored cities, echoed by more curtailed new listing supply (-5% mom and -16% yoy in July) as well as more positive price expectations from agents (improving for 2nd consecutive week).

## Key data points:

■ New homes sales volume was +12% wow and -2% yoy, while new home search activities improved 1.2% wow.

Secondary transactions were +2% wow and +10% yoy, with price appreciation expectations edging higher for agents.

July: Primary GFA sold on median was -8% mom and was +7% yoy; secondary GFA sold on median was -10% mom and +3% yoy.

YTD: Primary GFA sold on average was -14% yoy and was -16%/-39% vs. the 2024/2023 level; secondary GFA sold on average was +1% yoy and was +15%/+12% vs. the 2024/2023 level.

\- Inventory balance was -0.3% wow, with inventory months at 27.3 (vs. average 27.5 in June-26).

Valuation: Our covered stronger SOE developers saw share prices -3% wow on average, with Poly (600048.SS, Neutral) outperforming at -1% wow; meanwhile, other developers saw share prices on average -1% wow. Our offshore/onshore coverage now trades at an average 36%/32% discount to end-2026E NAV and at 0.5X/0.4X 2026E P/Bs.

■ Property sales in c.75 cities suggest top-100 developers' contract sales are likely to drop 6% yoy in July, vs. -13% in June.
