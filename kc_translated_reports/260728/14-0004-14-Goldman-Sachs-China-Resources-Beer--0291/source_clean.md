# China Resources Beer (0291.HK): 1H26 Preview: Positive beer volume/ASP growth out/pf industry but more cost pressure; spirits still

We look for Rmb24.5bn sales and Rmb5.3bn recurring NP in 1H26, up by 2%/down by 2% yoy, while we expect reported NP to decline 9% to Rmb5.29bn lapping the high base of land sales (c.Rmb827mn in 1H25). We expect 2.2% beer volume growth/ASP growth at c.0.6% yoy, supported by continued premiumisation and strong growth momentum by key SKUs (e.g. Heineken up c.20%)/sub-premium-and-above segment growth at MSD\~HSD%. That said, we expect flattish core beer OP in 1H26 at c. Rmb7.26bn mainly due to 1) Packaging material costs pressure (e.g. Aluminum price up by 19% yoy in 1H26 per our cost tracker offsetting barley cost lock-in benefits) leading to LSD% unit COGS increase (more pronounced in 2Q26); and 2) marketing investment with peak sports season and some admin expense due to headquarter moving in end-2025. Meanwhile, the spirits business is likely to remain a drag on group earnings in 1H26 under macro backdrop and still inventory destocking. Looking ahead, we believe beer premium mix improvement as well as a low base for ASP, and gradual recovery of spirits lapping on a low base should support earnings growth in 2H26/2027.

Beer - industry pressure easing with sequential volume recovery: We expect CRB's beer volume to grow by 2.2% in 1H26, outperforming the industry at 0.2% (by NBS) despite unfavorable weather in 2Q26. We also expect ASP to increase c.0.6% yoy, supported by resilient premium mix. Heineken maintained c.20%+ volume growth in 1H26, driven evenly by on-/off-trade and continued expansion beyond core markets into Jiangsu/Shanghai/Sichuan and Northeast China. Snow Draft returned to stable volume growth following packaging upgrades, Super X sustained SD% growth, while Lao Xue and Amstel continued to deliver strong DD% growth (see our consumer day takeaway). As a backdrop, we still expect on-trade demand recovery remain challenged in the industry, and the company is gaining market share to drive its premium portfolio, meanwhile it is also strengthening its Rmb8\~10 price band, led by Super X and regional brands, while maintaining agile channel execution and healthy inventory.

Spirits - earnings remain under pressure despite mild improvement in May-Jun: We expect the spirits business to remain a drag on group earnings in 1H26, with sales down $10\%$ , and greater EBIT loss yoy (GSE on -Rmb236mn in 1H26 vs. -Rmb152mn in 1H25) as consumer sentiment remains subdued and industry-wide destocking continues.
