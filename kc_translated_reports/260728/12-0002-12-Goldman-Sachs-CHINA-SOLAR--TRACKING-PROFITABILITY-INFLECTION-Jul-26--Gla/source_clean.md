# CHINA SOLAR: TRACKING PROFITABILITY INFLECTION
# Jul-26: Glass price increased while the remaining segments continued softness

Our China Solar Profitability Tracker follows monthly supply/demand and inventory dynamics by sub-sector, and spot prices/input costs implied cash GP & EBITDA margin trends for companies under our coverage.

## Key highlights in Jul MTD:

Glass price increased while the remaining segments continued softness: Throughout the month, pricing declined by an avg. 7% across the solar value chain. By segment, pricing trend is diverging with Glass price up by 6% MTD (due to improving inventory days to 50 by end-Jul from 57 days in Jun) while Wafer (-7% MTD on lower Poly price) and Cell (-11% MTD on lower Wafer price and continued silver price decline at -15% MTD) leading the price weakness. In terms of profitability, pricing change has led to 11pp/1pp margin improvement in Glass/Module and 3pp/5pp/5pp/8pp deterioration in Poly/Wafer/Cell/Film.

Tighter energy standard announced for Poly but likely muted impact to effective supply: On Jul 20, multiple authorities jointly announced mandatory energy standard for Poly production that came in tighter than the draft version with energy intensity cap for existing operating capacity (Tier 3 standard) lowered to 6.3kgce/kg (or 51KWh/kg) for Rod Poly vs. 6.4kgce/kg (or 52KWh/kg) in draft version, effective from Jan 2027. We see muted impact to effective Poly supply as most of operating capacity (2.6mn ton) can either meet or upgrade to meet the standard, and we expect UTR will stay at depressed levels for Poly at an avg. 30% in 2026E-2030E.

Glass inventory has showed initial positive inflection with a new round of supply cuts kicking in: In July, we're seeing a coordinated effort by leading players to cut supply by $10\%$ through cold repairs and inventory days is also showing positive inflection from 60 days in Jun to 50 days by end-Jul. Looking forward, we believe seasonal demand recovery will drive inventory days further down to 35 days in Sep, and $\sim 10\%$ glass price increase in 2H26.

## Jun-26 Global demand declined by 15% yoy to 37GW

Global Module demand (derived by the aggregate of China installation implied module demand, China export volume, and US AC-side installation implied module demand) increased by 33% mom and down by 15% yoy to 37GW in Jun 2026, sending 1H26 down by 44% yoy to 232GW, which is tracking below our FY26E forecast of -12% yoy.

## Specifically:

i) China installation in Jun declined by 13% yoy to 12.5GW (vs. -91% yoy to 8.7GW in May), sending 1H26 down by 66% yoy to 72GW, tracking below our FY26E forecast of -26% yoy.

ii) China Module export volume in Jun declined by 24% yoy to 17GW (vs. -38% yoy in May), sending 1H26 down by 6% yoy to 123GW (vs. -2% yoy in 5M26), according to China Customs and PVInfolink. Specifically, demand softened yoy across Middle East (-51% yoy), EU (-26% yoy), APAC (-9% yoy) while increased in Africa (+11% yoy).

## Price Target Risks and Methodology

Maxwell: We are Buy rated on Maxwell. Our 12m TP of Rmb278 is based on a 22X 2027E EV/EBITDA (90%/10% EBITDA mix of 15X/85X EV/EBITDA for core business/new application orders), discounted back to 2026E at an 8.3% CoE. Key risks: 1) Slower-than-expected new application demand could cool down the trial capex investment sentiment, and therefore lead to potential order cancellation risk to Maxwell; 2) tighter export control over solar equipment could meaningfully weaken Maxwell's earnings outlook; 3) slower-than-expected development in the semi industry.

Hangzhou First: We are Buy rated on Hangzhou First. Our 12m TP of Rmb20 for HZ First is based on a 17X 2027E EV/EBITDA discounted back to 2026E at a 10.7% CoE. Key risks: 1) Lower-than-expected solar installation leading to lower capacity utilization and lower solar film ASP; 2) slower-than-expected capacity expansion; 3) slower-than-expected development into high-end non-solar business (e.g. AI-PCB, solid state battery packaging, etc.); 4) More intensified raw material procurement (e.g. resin) due to supply disruption.

Longi: We are Buy rated on Longi. Our 12-month target price of Rmb14.2 is derived from a 2.3X 26E P/B based on its historical regression correlation of P/B and ROE. Key risks: 1) Stronger-than-expected rebound in Poly/Glass prices if demand beats expectations coupled with strong supply-side policy implementation; 2) slower-than-expected cost reduction technology adoption progress; 3) slower-than-expected BC development lowering mid-cycle margin inflection potential; 4) slower-than-expected ESS development.

Xinyi Solar: We are Buy rated on Xinyi Solar. Our 12-month target price for Xinyi Solar is HK\$2.8 and is derived from a 0.8X 26E P/B based on its historical regression correlation of P/B and ROE. Key risks: 1) Lower-than-expected solar glass ASP; 2) Higher-than-expected raw material and energy prices, 3) Slower-than-expected overseas capacity expansion.
