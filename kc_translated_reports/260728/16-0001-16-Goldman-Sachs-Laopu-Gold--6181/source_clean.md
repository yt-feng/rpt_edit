# Laopu Gold (6181.HK)

Profit warning: 2Q26 missed with bigger-than-expected sales pressure and operating deleverage

6181.HK 12m Price Target: HK\$560.00 Price: HK\$396.40 Upside: 41.3%

What happened: Laopu Gold announced a profit alert on Jul 27, expecting 1H26 revenue to be in a range at Rmb19.8bn-20.5bn (lower than GSe of Rmb21.0bn), while adj. net profit to be in a range of Rmb4.3bn-4.4bn (lower than GSe of Rmb4.8bn). Using mid-point of pre-announced 1Q26 operating results, this implies 2Q26 net profit range of Rmb0.56bn-0.61bn (or Rmb0.66bn-0.71bn if using lower-end for 1Q26), 40%-45% lower than GSe of Rmb1.0bn.

GS view: We expect to see negative share price reaction post the announcement, with 2Q26 sales and margin below market expectation. Using the mid-point of 1Q26 results announced earlier (i.e. reported revenue of Rmb16.5bn-17.5bn, net profit of Rmb3.6bn-3.8bn), the implied revenue in 2Q26 is at Rmb2.8bn-Rmb3.5bn (vs. GSe implied revenue 4.0bn), and implied net profit in 2Q26 is at Rmb0.56bn-Rmb0.61bn (vs. GSe implied net profit Rmb1.0bn); the implied net profit margin in 2Q26 is at $19\%$ at the mid-point, which we view is lower than expectation considering the GPM expansion post price hike (according to management's comment in May, GPM is able to reach $>45\%$ post price hike) and rental cost is largely variable, presumably due to 1) operating deleverage in staff and marketing/G&A expenses considering the company's investment in store upgrade and branding, 2) lower online sales (our online tracker suggests $>60\%$ yoy decline in Tmall in 2Q26) which is a margin accretive channel.

That said, we note the company has been testing and rolling out lower price amid gold price pull back (more details here) — following the initial test in Shanghai Xintiandi store, Laopu also allowed stack of membership discount and promotion discount (i.e. 5% more off than prior) in SKP recently, which brought notable improvement in traffic.
