## Flattish tourism traffic trend during the holiday

China tourism traffic performance was lackluster during the Dragon Boat holiday (19-21 June). According to the Ministry of Transportation (link), overall tourism traffic during the Dragon Boat holiday reached c.648mn, with average daily tourism traffic at c.216mn, showing a flattish trend y-y (even slower than the 3.5% and 8.2% y-y growth registered during the 2026 Labor Day holiday and Spring Festival holiday, respectively). We believe the lackluster Dragon Boat holiday tourism traffic was mainly due to: 1) unfavourable weather in South China, 2) increased travel fuel costs, and 3) continuation of weakness in the broader consumption space.

\- By travel modes, total railway tourism traffic increased $2.9\%$ y-y to c.49mn during the Dragon Boat holiday (was up $4.6\%$ y-y during the Labor Day holiday); overall self-driving tourism traffic turned to a downtrend, decreasing $2.1\%$ y-y (was up $2.6\%$ y-y during the Labor Day holiday); while air travel traffic resumed its growth trajectory and was up $0.6\%$ y-y (vs a decline of $5.7\%$ y-y during the Labor Day holiday).

\- The outbound travel trend recovered marginally. According to the National Immigration Administration, China's overall cross-border tourism traffic during the Dragon Boat holiday reached c.6.7mn, increasing by $12.9\%$ y-y (was up $3.5\%$ y-y during the Labor Day holiday).

\- According to the Ministry of Commerce (link), total foot traffic across the 78 key commercial areas nationwide increased by $4.0\%$ y-y and total sales of the same set of key commercial areas increased by $3.5\%$ y-y during 19-20 June, which showed weakened momentum compared with the Labor Day holiday (total foot traffic and total sales were up $5.0\%$ and $5.3\%$ y-y, respectively, during the Labor Day holiday).
