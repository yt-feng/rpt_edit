# China EV Weekly Orders: New models drive conversions; quarter-end promotions pull demand forward before July lull
## Key Takeaways

EV orders bifurcated further during the week, with new-model launches converting while brands lacking catalysts saw listless intake.

■ More aggressive quarter-end promotions pull orders forward into late June, inflating bookings ahead of a likely July air pocket.

BYD and Leapmotor launches/facelifts added conversion momentum to an otherwise incentive-driven market.

## Weekly order trends in June 15-21 based on channel feedback:


Geely Galaxy (0175.HK) 19-19.5k (+12% WoW, -6% MoM, -12% YoY).

Li Auto (2015.HK/LI.O) 6.5-6.7k (+16% WoW, -44% MoM, -28% YoY) with suppressed L-series sales ahead of '26 L8 launch today.


XPeng (9868.HK/XPEV.N) 7.2-7.4k (+6% WoW, -58% MoM, -18% YoY); all eyes are on the Mona L03 debut on July 2.


Aito 9.1-9.3k (+12% WoW, +63% MoM, +40% YoY) thanks to cheaper M6 variants with starting prices from Rmb229.8k.

ZEEKR 6.4-6.6k (+28% WoW, -22% MoM, +78% YoY).

Xiaomi (1810.HK; covered by Andy Meng) 7-7.2k (+8% WoW, -13% MoM, +75% YoY).
