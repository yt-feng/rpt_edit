## Lithium
## May China Export/Import Data

\- May lithium carbonate imports increased $78\%$ y/y to 37.6kt vs 21.1kt in the prior-year period. 2026 YTD net imports are 151.4kt or $54.5\%$ higher vs. the same period last year.

\- Lithium hydroxide exports from China in May decreased (36%) y/y to 3.5kt vs. 5.6kt in the prior-year period. 2026 YTD, China is a net importer of 10.5kt.

Chemicals: Specialty, Commodity, Agricultural, and Paper & Packaging

\- The lithium carbonate import price increased 15% m/m and 102% y/y in May: prices averaged \$18,993/t in May versus \$16,586/t in April and \$9,392/t in May 2025. See Table 6 for details.

\- The Chinese LiOH export price was 33% higher m/m, and 64% higher y/y in May: prices averaged \$19,856/t versus \$14,894/t in April. The average export price in May 2025 was \$12,093/t. See Table 5 for more details.

## Historical Information


Net lithium hydroxide exports in 2025 were 35.4kt or (69%) lower vs. 2024. Net exports for full-year 2024 of 113kt were (11%) lower versus 2023. FY2023 net exports were 126.2kt, or 40% higher y/y. Net exports in 2022 were 90.3kt, 20.3kt or 29% higher vs. net exports of 70kt in 2021. Full-year 2024 exports of 121kt were (7%) lower vs 2023 exports of 130.0kt. 2022 exports of 93.4kt were about 27% higher y/y vs. 2021 exports of 73.6kt for the year.
