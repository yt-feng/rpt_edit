## Key points for Electronic Chemicals


## Resonac > Sumitomo Bakelite > Tokyo Ohka Kogyo

• We think Resonac is likely to embark on capacity adds and further price hikes for CCLs.

\- Sumitomo Bakelite's business is strong at present; sales of granular encapsulants to Intel are increasing, and sample supplies of liquid encapsulants to TSMC and OSACs are starting up.

Tokyo Ohka Kogyo's lower growth rate than JSR may reflect lower MOR capacity.

## Key points regarding materials

• Very strong performance for FC-BGA CCLs.

• Semiconductor sealant materials doing well in Taiwan market.

• The China market is driving photoresists.

## Roundup

## Still bullish on Resonac

• 1Q CCL sales +60% YoY; of this, estimate GPU applications +100%.

• Estimate 1Q TIM sales +60% YoY; estimate NCF +140% due to 1H25 adjustment.

\- HD media: Inventory adjustment for Toshiba complete; inquiries from WDC/STX strengthening, with recovery expected from 2Q.

• Partial spin-off of Crasus Chemical (petrochemicals business) still on track.

\- Growing possibility of graphite electrodes business sell-off; non-recurring items potentially all out within F12/26.

## Approaching long entry point for Sumitomo Bakelite

• F3/27 sealant materials sales guidance +10% YoY, but start looks strong with Apr-May +25-30%.

\- Boost for firm's sealant materials if Intel foundries acquire work from Apple. Sumitomo Bakelite also due to acquire Kyocera's semiconductor sealant materials business in Oct.

\- Has also started shipments of liquid sealant material samples.

## Impact of high raw materials costs on TOK unclear

• 1Q OP topped consensus on strong photoresist sales.

• 2Q may be hit by high raw materials costs, but we expect upward revision to full-year plan at end-Jul.

• However, momentum looks slightly weak vs. JSR.
