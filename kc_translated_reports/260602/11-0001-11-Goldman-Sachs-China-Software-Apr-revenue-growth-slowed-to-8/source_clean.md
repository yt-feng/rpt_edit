# Software Monthly: Apr revenues down to $8.9\%$ YoY with MoM decline

# Apr 2026 China software quick read:

1. Revenue +8.9% YoY, leading to 4M26 at 10.9% YoY, slower than 1Q26 growth of 11.6% YoY: In April, the aggregate revenue of software companies registered in China was up +8.9% YoY to Rmb1.2trn (US\$163bn), resulting in 4M26 revenue growth of +10.9% YoY, while going down from 1Q26 of 11.6% YoY, per MIIT.

2. Net margin was 12.0%, leading to 4M26 at 11.4%, higher vs. 1Q26 of 11.2%: In April, the aggregate net income of software companies registered in China was Rmb141bn (US\$19.6bn), or net margin of 12.0% (vs. 9.0% in Mar 2026), resulting in 4M26 net margin of 11.4%, higher than 1Q26 net margin of 11.2%, per MIIT.

3. Overseas exposure up to 3.1%: In April, the aggregate revenue for non-China markets from software companies registered in China was US\$5.1bn, and overseas exposure was up to 3.1% (vs. 2.8% in Mar 2026).

4. IT services remain a key contributor: In 4M26, the aggregate revenue of software companies registered in China (Rmb4.7trn) was mainly generated from IT services (67% of the total), followed by software products at 22%, embedded system software at 9%, and security software and services at 2%, per MIIT.

# China security software / IT services market +5%/ +9% YoY in April 2026

China security software market revenue growth slowed in April: In Apr, the aggregate revenue of security software companies registered in China was up 5% YoY Rmb20.8bn (US\$2.9bn), and 4M26 revenue to Rmb87bn (US\$12.1bn).
China IT services market revenue +9% YoY in April: In April, the aggregate revenue of IT services companies registered in China was up 9% YoY to Rmb790bn (US\$110bn), and 4M26 revenue to Rmb3.1tn (US\$438bn), per MIIT.
■ Service outsourcing contract value increased in Mar 2026: In March, the aggregate contract value of service outsourcing was up to Rmb377bn, and 1Q26 contract value to Rmb590bn, per Ministry of Commerce.
