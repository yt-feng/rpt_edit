## Meta Platforms Inc

AI Outlook Flips as Muse Spark 1.1 Model Advances, with Signs of External AI Monetization & AI Product Cycle; Incrementally Positive, But Remain Neutral

Meta shares have traded up +23% off late June lows (vs. SPX +3%) driven by improved sentiment around META's AI strategy, the launch of external AI monetization, & signs of an AI product cycle. Muse Spark 1.1 comes with significant improvements across agentic & coding roughly a year after Meta's AI retrenchment via MSL. Continued model progression may push Meta closer to the frontier of personal superintelligence, tighten the competitive gap with Anthropic, OpenAI, & Google, & signal that Meta could start building more compelling AI products. Meta's Model API in public preview represents Meta's first form of external AI monetization, and lower relative API pricing (\~25% the cost vs. leading AI models) should enable Meta to accelerate enterprise monetization at attractive steady-state operating margins. Reuters reported that Meta plans to reach 7GW of compute in 2026 & double capacity to 14GW in 2027. We expect Meta to continue to work towards core AI products that can be leveraged across its base of \~4B users, but Meta has the flexibility to monetize improved models & significant infrastructure to generate better ROI across AI spend. We currently project Meta capex of \$142B in 2026 (+104%) & \$202B in 2027 (+42%), and we'd expect 2027 capex to move higher. Overall, we're encouraged by Meta's model progression and willingness to pivot toward external monetization to participate in large-scale AI economic capture which could drive earnings upside. To become more positive, we look for increasing signs of Meta using Muse Spark 1.1 & future models internally, greater developer & enterprise adoption, & potential LLM share shift. 2027 capex could certainly move higher, but that would be better received w/improving monetization. A rising share price is also likely to renew discussions around a potential equity raise on the heels of Google's in early June, but that could also be better received w/Meta on the offensive. We remain Neutral-rated & our December 2026 PT of \$725 is based on 21x 2027E GAAP EPS of \$34.19.

\- Monetizing Compute: Capturing Upside While Maintaining Flexibility. Building on our preliminary thoughts on Meta's potential cloud compute & monetization strategy, recent Bloomberg interview comments from Mark have provided greater clarity around how the business could fit into Meta's AI strategy. He suggested that Meta is using all of its compute capacity, but noted that capacity rental deals could prove highly accretive. # Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

Meta Platforms Inc (Neutral; Price Target: \$725.00)

## Investment Thesis

We believe Meta's virtual ownership of the social graph, strong competitive moat, and focus on the user experience position it to become an enduring blue-chip company built for the long term. Meta reach and engagement continue to drive network effects, and its targeting abilities provide significant value to advertisers. META is delivering significant AI-driven ad ranking & recommendation gains, supporting greater engagement, ROAS, & revenue growth. However, AI monetization remains less proven vs. Internet peers and heavy capex drives multi-year FCF declines. We look for updates across AI execution, model launches & efficacy, & agentic product rollout. Our December 2026 PT of \$725 is based on \~21x our 2027E GAAP EPS of \$34.19, further supported by our DCF model.

## Valuation

Our December 2026 PT of \$725 is based on \~21x our 2027E GAAP EPS of \$34.19, further supported by our DCF model. We apply a premium to the SPX as we have greater confidence in Meta's strong top-line growth and ongoing cost efficiencies. We use this valuation approach as we believe it appropriately balances Meta's valuation relative to its growth and industry peers. Our DCF results in an estimated value of \$725 per share and employs a \~14% WACC and a +10% terminal growth rate.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Upside risks include: 1) AI Ad initiatives drive engagement, revenue growth, & monetization; 2) Muse Spark & MSL model launches resonate and drive model efficacy, user growth, & engagement; 3) ROAS gains drive greater ad spend and market share gains, supporting revenue growth; & 4) Investments ease & cost discipline drives greater than expected GAAP EPS & FCF.

Downside risks include: 1) AI investments are heavy & monetization lags, which weighs on GAAP OI, GAAP EPS, & FCF; 2) slower than expected revenue growth limits AI monetization upside; 3) AI product & model launches do not resonate, weighing on engagement, revenue, & margins; 4) competition for online and mobile ad dollars from Google, TikTok, OpenAI, and other online advertising companies; & 5) regulatory litigations & charges.
