# China Free Liquidity vs. MSCI China YoY Change
## Key Takeaways

Free liquidity declined to a fresh low since May 2025, driven by muted M1 growth, elevated PPI, and stronger industrial output.

We expect it to gradually improve into year-end, as June likely marked the cyclical peak in PPI growth and in-budget bond issuance accelerates in 2H26.

\- Overall it will likely remain relatively tight, given lukewarm corporate borrowing desire under still the challenging macro backdrop.
