# New Dynamics and Implications
## Key Takeaways

■ New market dynamics are moving tanker shipping risk-reward toward positive territory.

Key catalysts we are watching: potential Middle East tension de-escalating or China crude import increase could act as potential positive catalysts.

## What's new:

\- Spot rates corrected in Atlantic markets: On July 21, TD15 (West Africa-China) reached US\$103k/day, while TD22 (US Gulf-China) was US\$105k/day, correcting from US\$189k/d and US\$155k/d level seen in late June.

\- Multiple tanker attacks seen in the Middle East region in the past month. In the Black Sea, damages are also reported amid attacks on tankers near the CPC terminal.

\- Tanker re-routing: In the Red Sea, several Yanbu-loaded tankers reversed north toward Suez following a Houthi warning against vessels calling at Saudi ports.

\- China crude imports remained slow in June: Imports fell 41.3% yoy to 29.27mn tons (or 7.12mn b/d), the lowest since Oct-16 and down 12% from May, extending the sequential decline since April.

## Our take:

\- We think VLCC TCE spot rates in the Atlantic market are reaching equilibrium with limited further downside.

\- Tanker re-routing could lead to longer voyages and consume effective capacity.

\- Vessel damages led by attacks could lead to a tighter supply when demand normalizes.
