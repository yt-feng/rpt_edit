# Kingboard Laminates Holdings (1888.HK)
# Read across from Citi Call with Nanya New Material
## CITI'S TAKE

We hosted a call with Nanya New Material (688519) Board Secretary after posting a 1H26 profit alert. Key takeaways were 1) expectation of continued increase in ASP for CCL and e-glass fabric during 2H26E, 2) the tight supply of e-glass fabric should continue till end-2027 as earliest, 3) Nanya GM will continue to expand significantly on sequential basis during 2H26 vs 1H26. We saw the call as a positive read through to the CCL industry including KBL and Shengyi Tech. KBL share price has dropped \~60% from all-time high this month which looks overdone at 8-9x PE which is even lower than historical mean low-teen PE. Reiterate Buy.

Strong net profit run rate during 1H26 preliminary results alert among CCL/PCB companies - Nanya reported 1H26E net profit to have surged by over 3.5X yoy to Rmb420-500m with mid-pt guidance implying $369\%$ yoy or $106\%$ qoq. Fig 1 exhibits the 1H26 preliminary results of CCL/PCB chain on China A-share stocks. Generally speaking, the quarterly profit rate should surge at highest degree at glass fabric plays (upstream), then CCL and lowest at PCB plays (downstream).

Implication of CCL ASP inflation during 2H26E - Nanya sees its ASP of CCL has almost doubled from Rmb160-170/sheet in end-2025 to Rmb300/sheet now on lead-free and halogen-free FR4 grade thanks to surging cost of upstream material e-glass fabric over the same period. See Fig 2. Mgmt expects the ASP of its CCL will continue to grow (likely Rmb360-400 at year end from mgmt) during 2H26 backed by 1) continued demand growth of non-AI applications like auto, smartphone etc though the growth is modest, 2) a notable CCL supply reduction on non-AI as the major CCL players like EMC, Shengyi Tech have been shifting from non-AI into AI applications.

Nanya expecting the sustainable tight supply of e-glass fabric till end-2027 as earliest- We thought the tight supply of e-glass fabric would be easing from mid-2027 for increasing looms (weaving machine from yarn into fabric). But Nanya mgmt shows a more aggressive view of expecting the tight supply to last at least end-2027 for strong demand outlook on AI-CCL applications. Nanya generates 70% of AI-CCL sales on M6-M8 grade with little vol for M9-10. The balance is M5 or below. Mgmt estimate AI-fabric accounting for \~20% of total capacity 3.6-3.7m sheets at this stage. Nanya will add 2m sheets with commencement in 2027 and bring uptown 6m sheets in 2028. Majority of which is set for AI-CCL.

## Buy


Implication of Nanya sequential GM expansion this year - Nanya sees a sustainable GM expansion on sequential basis from 1Q to 4Q26. We see this also applies to other CCL players like Shengyi Tech and KBL. They have set internal guidance not to take orders if GM below $25\%$ in 3Q, $20\%$ below in 2Q and $10\%$ below in 1Q.

Anticipation of KBL strong profit run rate in 2026E - We project 1H26E net profit to jump by >3X yoy to HK\$4.0bn. Some domestic investors expect KBL results as HK\$3.0bn which will likely miss our forecast. We see even if this is case, the key is quarterly profit rate would surge from HK\$1.0bn in 1Q26 to HK\$2-2.5bn per domestic investors view or to HK\$3.0bn in our model. The stronger profit run rate of KBL than pure CCL players like Shengyi Tech, Nanya is reasonable for stronger profit growth of upstream e-glass fabric. This is the power of vertical integration on KBL (self mfg of upstream) this year on phenomenal growth of fabric ASP vs other pure CCL players.

## Valuation

Our target price for KBL shares of HK\$130 is based on a \~28-29x P/E for 2027E, at 3SD over mean, which is even higher than its historical cycle peak between +1SD and +2SD, to reflect the growing % of its AI-fabric new business from 2H26E which should trigger re-rating along with further earnings upgrade possibility on e-glass fabric given higher run rate of price inflation in June. We employ +3SD reflecting recent kickoff of three kilns on AI-fabric from 2Q26. We think the premium is justified as we expect a continued GM expansion cycle during 2026E-28E along with potential earnings upgrades given KBL would expand its customer base for AI-fabric under the NVDA and ASIC ecosystem this year. We think a PE methodology appropriately captures the company's medium-term operating performance. Our target price translates to 0.3x PEG (regional comp around 0.6x) backed by strong three-year earnings CAGR of 99% (was 92%) thru 2028E. We think this appears undemanding compared with regional CCL comps.

## Risks

Upside/downside risks that could cause the stock to exceed/underperform our target price include: 1) faster/ slower-than-expected demand on upstream materials such as glass fabric and copper foil on AI-related products; 2) China macro growth performs faster/slower-than-expected; 3) stronger/weaker-than-expected stimulus policies in China; or 4) recovery/weakness in end-demand of electronic goods.
