# SAP (SAPG.DE)
# Resilient CCB and strong pipeline despite macro uncertainty; refining EBIT assumptions; reiterate Buy


We reiterate our Buy rating on SAP post 2Q26, which demonstrated another quarter of CCB resilience despite elevated macro uncertainty driven by Middle East tensions. Looking forward, we expect investor debate to be centred around the trajectory of CCB throughout FY26, with management reiterating its expectation of a slight deceleration through FY26. However, we believe the growth exhibited in 1H26, combined with a post-SAPPHIRE pipeline tracking better than expected, gives greater visibility into 2H cloud growth, which we view as broadly de-risked given the unchanged guidance. While we acknowledge the headwinds to operating profit from higher opex and dilutive impact from acquisitions, we still see potential for SAP to unlock structural cost efficiencies across its opex as SAP undergoes an evolving business model transition towards AI consumption. Overall, we continue to expect SAP to outperform vs peers driven by the resilience of its idiosyncratic product cycle and await any proof points on AI adoption and monetisation over the coming quarters following of launch of the Autonomous Enterprise and Business AI Platform at SAPPHIRE.

We update our estimates for FX, which represents an incremental headwind of c.30bps to revenues. We continue to forecast +22% organic yoy ex-fx FY26 ex CCB growth. We modestly raise our cloud revenues to 24.4% yoy ex-fx (vs. 24.0% previously) for FY26, while slightly lowering our FY26 EBIT growth forecast to 14.5% yoy ex-fx (vs. 14.8% previously), reflecting lower cloud gross margins and slightly higher opex, landing just below the midpoint of guidance. We also modestly lower our FY27 EBIT growth with the outer years unchanged. On our estimates, SAP currently trades on CY27E EV/Recurring revenue, P/E and EV/FCF of c.4x/c.15x/c.13x, for c.15%/22%/17% recurring revenue, EPS and FCF FY26-30 CAGRs. We reiterate our Buy with a 12-month price target of €215 (ADR \$245).

## Resilient S/4 HANA product cycle despite uncertain macro; CCB reiterated to slightly decelerate in FY26

At 2Q26, SAP reported CCB growth of 26% yoy ex-fx (c.25% organic), ahead of GSe and consensus expectations of 25%/24%, and sequentially steady despite elevated macro uncertainty, driven by continued strength in the Cloud ERP Suite. Management also highlighted momentum across its Autonomous Suite and Business AI Platform, resulting in improved pipeline coverage post SAPPHIRE.

Looking ahead, management reiterated its guidance on top-line metrics and continues to expect a slight deceleration in CCB through FY26, noting that the range of outcomes for H2 remains wide given the volatile backdrop. That said, management noted the post-SAPPHIRE pipeline is tracking better than expected with improved coverage vs the prior year, and management expressed confidence in achieving its FY guidance as predictability on cloud revenue improves seven months into the year. We remain prudent, modelling a c.200bps deceleration in our ex-fx CCB growth, expecting c.22% organic growth ex-fx. On cloud revenues, we modestly raise our FY26 forecast to 24.4% yoy ex-fx (vs. 24.0% previously), reflecting better licences and subscription revenue.

Non-IFRS EBIT came in modestly below expectations (2%/5% below GSe/consensus), growing 9% yoy ex-fx. The sequential deceleration was driven by lower cloud growth, higher-than-expected SBC (reversing the low SBC in 1Q), elevated R&D investment partly reflecting targeted AI hires at higher per-FTE costs, S&M spend around the Autonomous Enterprise launch, and the dilutive impact of Reltio. SAP also highlighted efficiency gains from internal AI adoption and plans to slow hiring over the next 12 months as it works towards its mid-term target of c.€2bn in efficiency gains. Management expects H2 operating profit growth to track similarly to the mid-teens growth delivered in H1 on a combined basis.

SAP updated its FY26 operating profit outlook to €11.8–12.2bn (previously €11.9–12.3bn), reflecting the dilutive impact of the Dremio and Prior Labs acquisitions (c.€100mn in H2). We modestly lower our Non-IFRS (incl. SBC) EBIT growth to 14.5% yoy ex-fx (vs. 14.8% previously) for FY26, landing just below the midpoint of guidance, and continue to expect double-digit ex-fx EBIT growth over the medium term (GSe forecasting c.80–180 bps margin expansion over FY26–30E).

## Price target risks and methodology

We are Buy rated. Our 12m PT of €215 and our ADR PT of US\$245 are based on c.22x 3Q27-2Q28E P/E (including SBC).

Key risks to our view and price target are as follows: (1) macro risks given broad-based exposure across geographies; (2) cloud and subscription risks, such as customer churn during the transition phase, would pose downside risks to our estimates; (3) difficulty in gaining traction in S/4 HANA/cloud and not being able to draw benefits from cross-selling synergies in its installed base could cause headwinds to overall cloud growth and margins; (4) increase in opex spending would result in stronger headwinds to margins, and (5) further management changes.
