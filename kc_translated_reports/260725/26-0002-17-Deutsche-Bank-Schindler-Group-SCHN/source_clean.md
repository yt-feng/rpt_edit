# Company Schindler Group
## Adjusting DB estimates, now in-line with consensus for FY26
# Adjusting forecasts, new TP of 273 CHF, Hold

Schindler's Q2 print included c3% order growth in lc similar to Q1 despite a softer comp with a similar story on revenue growth as we continue to see project selectivity filter into the numbers. Margin expansion in adj EBIT slowed down in Q2 at +35bps yr/yr vs Q1 levels with management citing stronger cost inflation which we expect for the remainder of the year. Management did reiterate that it expects negative mix effects in the remainder of the year with stronger cost headwinds larger in the mix now. Schindler, like other companies, will look to offset this with price increases and surcharges with a bit of timing mismatch on its longer-lead parts of the business model. Adjusting our forecasts slightly, we now expect Schindler to deliver +c53bps in EBIT margin expansion in FY26, a slight trim vs previous expectations. We see risk-reward as balanced at these levels with a new target price of 273. Hold

## Colour on the quarter and updated colour on price/cost dynamics

In our FY26 forecasts, we assume +DD growth in local fx for modernisation revenues, +LSD/MSD growth in service with -HSD declines in new installations. Versus previous DBe, we've made no meaningful change to FY26 group orders now CHF 11.5bn +3.5% yr/yr in lc. We've made slight adjustments to revenues by now

Q2 new installation (NI) orders grew +HSD in value and +LDD in units driven by AsiaPac ex China while Chinese NI continues to decline (Schindler declining faster than peers). Modernisation orders grew +11% yr/yr vs tough comps in Q2 (+24% a yr ago) with growth in every region including China. On a positive note, Schindler saw market share gains in both EMEA NI and Modernisation as its order backlog grew +6% in lc (where margin improved seq), driven by modernization growth of +LDD. Management continues to expect a more negative revenue mix for H2 as H1 margins were flattered with certain NI projects delayed. Previously, management guided there could be a time lag between price actions and subsequent P&L impact particularly in longer duration orders (read NI). On cost inflation, mgmt. highlighted a stronger headwind in H2 from raw materials with mgmt. confident on pass-through on energy cost increases (previously guided at Q1). That said, on cost inflation, the revised guide is c35m for the yr (1/3 in H1 and 2/3 in H2), as Schindler looks to mitigate cost increases in raw materials (copper/alum) through price increases. Guidance on tariff impact was unchanged at c15m with Schindler already starting to process IEEPA refunds but the quantum appears small in nature.

## Valuation: our target price adjusts to CHF 273 (was 290), Hold

We value the company using DCF analysis with explicit forecasts through 2030 with a terminal value calculation. Our assumptions include a CAPM framework to calculate a WACC of 7.8% and a CF growth rate of 3.5% in perpetuity. Downside risks include exposure to cyclical construction markets or an inability to drive operational improvements. Upside risks include better-than-expected order and revenue growth and/or a meaningful recovery in key construction markets

## Company Profile

Founded in 1874, Schindler is an international engineering and service company that provides elevators, escalators, and moving walks transport. With 69,000+ employees in 100 countries+, the company serve customers from 1,000+ branch offices with production sites in eight countries and six R&D facilities globally. Schindler is listed on the Swiss stock exchange.
