# Resilient performance amid sector slowdown
## 2Q26 sales momentum outperforms sector, indicating business resilience; reiterate Buy

ANTA announced a set of solid 2Q26 operating data (17 July): the ANTA core and FILA brands' sales increased by low-single-digit% (LSD%) and LSD% y-y, respectively, in 2Q26, while other brands' sales grew 25-30% y-y. Although ANTA, like its other peers, experienced a slowdown of sales momentum in 2Q26 (Fig. 1), its sales outperformed the sportswear sector, indicating the resilience of ANTA's multi-brand strategy and solid operational capability, in our view. For 1H26, the ANTA core/ FILA/ all other brands recorded y-y sales growth of mid-single-digit% (MSD%)/ MSD%/ 35-40%, on track to achieve its full-year sales targets. We maintain our FY26F full-year revenue/earnings estimates for ANTA, but cut our target price to HKD89.90, based on a reduced target F12M P/E of 17.5x in line with the derating of the sportswear sector. With that said, ANTA remains our preferred name in the sportswear sector, given its exposure to structural growth opportunities in the long term and attractive valuation levels.

## Operational adjustments evident on multiple fronts

ANTA has been adjusting operations across its brand portfolios, amid sales slowdown of the sportswear sector as well as increasing competition from niche segment leaders; such adjustments include the recent change of ANTA core brand's CEO, repositioning of ANTA SV/Super ANTA stores and introduction of the mega store format of ANTA Market in Shenyang/Shanghai, and the gradual rollout of new stores/products of the Jack Wolfskin brand. We believe these efforts should help the ANTA core brand to defend its market share in the mass market segment (e.g., online sales of the ANTA core brand recorded double-digit% y-y growth in 2Q26 after the product/channel adjustments since 3Q25). On the other hand, brands like DESCENTE and KOLON are still delivering solid growth thanks to their training/outdoor exposure.

## Maintain Buy; target price cut to HKD89.90 (implied upside of 21.2%)

We decrease our target price to HKD89.90 (vs. HKD125.00 previously) based on a lowered target F12M P/E of 17.5x (at a $35\%$ premium to our F12M P/E of Li Ning [2331 HK, Neutral], considering ANTA's leading position in the sportswear sector, in our view). ANTA now trades at an attractive 15.1x FY26F P/E (EPS: CNY4.47); maintain Buy.

## Company profile

The ANTA brand was established in 1991, while the ANTA Sports Products Limited was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2007. ANTA has been engaging in the design, development, manufacturing and marketing of branded sportswear products, including footwear, apparel and accessories. By embracing an all-round brand portfolio including ANTA, FILA, DESCENTE and KOLON SPORT, and by setting up an investor consortium to successfully acquire Amer Sports in 2019, ANTA has developed itself to unlock the potential of both the mass and high-end sportswear markets.

## Valuation Methodology

Our target price of HKD89.9 for ANTA is based on 17.5x F12M P/E, consistent to the company's leading position in China's sportswear sector and stable growth trajectory. The benchmark index of ANTA is the Hang Seng Index.

Risks that may impede the achievement of the target price

Downside risks to our Buy rating and target price for ANTA include: 1) intensifying competition from both domestic and global players; 2) slower-than-expected sales growth; and 3) weaker-than-expected macroeconomic dynamics.

## ESG

ANTA has been paying close attention to the risks brought by climate change. Besides complying with relevant environmental laws and regulations, ANTA has launched the "ANTA Grand Forum" to serve as a communication platform for employees to share and exchange their ideas on environmental protection. More importantly, it continues to strengthen its product innovation capability by actively exploring eco-friendly materials for its products series.
