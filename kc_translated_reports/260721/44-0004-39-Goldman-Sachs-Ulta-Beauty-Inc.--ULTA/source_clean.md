Ulta continues to be heavily debated, as investors are concerned on the top line and SG&A expectations for the remainder of the year. Many question whether Ulta's 1Q strength is sustainable, noting the company faces its most difficult compare in 2Q, and the likely set up of decelerating SSS. Jumbo Love and Love Your Skin event: Ulta's Jumbo Love and Love Your Skin events usually happen simultaneously and span a month. 21 Days of Beauty: The 21 Days of Beauty event occurs in March and September every year, and features daily $50 \%$ off promotions for a selection of skincare, haircare, makeup, and fragrance products. Management has stated that 21 Days of Beauty is a significant event that is used to determine customer preferences and marketing efficiency. In 3Q25, management attributed prestige makeup’s strength to 21 Days of Beauty, as brands such as Estee Lauder and MAC were standouts during the event. In 1Q26, management noted they had integrated marketplace brands into the event as well.

Spring Haul: Spring Haul is usually a \~10 day event in mid-April that features up to 30% off on select makeup, fragrance, hair care, and skincare. Management called out Spring Haul as a growth driver in 1Q26, and noted in 1Q25 that Spring Haul generated strong traffic and increases in member penetration.

Big Summer Beauty Sale: Big Summer Beauty Sale is a \~three week event in late June to mid July that offers up to 40% off and BOGO 50% off select brands. In 2Q25, management called out Big Summer Beauty Sale as a successful event, and noted that they had shifted promotional timing to better align with consumer behavior. In 2Q24, management noted that they had enhanced the event with a robust media strategy and engaging social content, which led to increased member reactivation and penetration of existing members.

When comparing the events this year to prior years, we found that foot traffic has accelerated for the Spring Haul and Big Summer Beauty Sale event. ## Takeaways from our call with IR

Ulta is focused on driving a higher percent of exclusivity in its mix. In FY25, exclusive products comprised 11% of the company's assortment, up from 9% the prior year. Especially given the evolution of distribution in beauty, the company is thinking about how to further differentiate its assortment. Management is positive on the company's newness pipeline and feels good about the consistency, while explaining it will likely continue to evolve and look different in terms of the brands. We will likely see more smaller, emerging brands given there are not as many large brands Ulta does not already carry.

Long term guidance remains intact; $12\%$ operating margin floor is no longer relevant. The company's long term algorithm includes top line sales growth in the $+4 - 6\%$ range with operating profit growth in-line or better than that and +LDD EPS growth. When considering how to manage SG&A, management is focused on efficiency; the company plans to continue investing to gain share, but not at the elevated level from the last couple of years. At a recent conference, management explained that the $12\%$ operating margin floor is no longer relevant as the company delivered $12.4\%$ during an investment year in FY25.

Ulta launched its TikTok Shop in March. While this is not expected to be material for the year, management is pleased with the brant interest. Ulta's initial approach is to focus on exclusive brands. From a strategic standpoint, the goal is to get in front of a younger beauty enthusiast (i.e., member acquisition), as well as to use this as a brand-building lever. The company is looking to drive a more economic model through bundles vs. individual SKUs, with Ulta selling multi-branded bundles. Management explained that Ulta is one of the only retailers offering multi-branded bundles, which is a key opportunity for them. The company launched with 17 exclusive brands and 30 exclusive bundles.

## Estimate Changes

We are increasing our 2Q comp growth estimate to +2.4% from +1.9% for what we think are better trends in the quarter based on our work around the bigger promotion events and observed sales. Additionally, we adjust our quarterly SG&A growth expectations. Our 2Q EBIT margin increases to 12.1% from 11.8%, and our 2Q EPS estimate increases to \$6.19 from \$6.02. Our FY26 comp growth estimate increases slightly to +3.4% from +3.3%, while our FY26 EBIT margin estimate remains consistent at 12.5%, and our FY26 EPS estimate increases to \$28.95 from \$28.80. Our FY27/FY28 EPS estimates increase by 0.5% to \$32.37/\$35.39, respectively.
