# FX Positioning Indicates Investors Increased Long NOK (vs. EUR) Positions

Our FX options positioning scores are now published daily on Bloomberg. A table of the tickers can be found in the Appendix.

## Key Takeaways

\- Options pricing data indicate that investors increased long NOK (vs. EUR) positions and increased short DXY positions.

In the futures market, investors increased long GBP positions, while they increased short AUD positions.

\- Overall, options data suggest that tactical investors are currently long NOK (vs. EUR) while being short EUR.

\- Our contrarian trading strategy would suggest long NZD/NOK offers value based on positioning score percentiles.
