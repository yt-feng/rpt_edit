## Read-across on Samsung Bio's acquisition offer for PolyPeptide

Reflects attractiveness of peptide CDMO business, but not likely to impact China-based CDMO players in short run

On 20 July morning (local time), Samsung Biologics (207940 KS, Buy) announced to acquire a $100\%$ stake in PolyPeptide Group AG (PPGN SW, Not rated), entering the peptide CDMO area. Our Korea Research team has published a report on this: Quick Note - Samsung Biologics (207940 KS) (Buy) Acquiring peptide CDMO company (PolyPeptide).

We think this deal reflects the attractiveness of peptide CDMO, as stated in our anchor report: Anchor Report: Global GLP-1 RAs - Asia players see opportunities, driven by the strong global demand for GLP-1 RAs – a major application area for peptide CDMO. The global market for peptide CDMO is also growing very fast.

Based on our understanding, the entry barrier to peptide CDMO is relatively high, as starting from scratch requires substantial effort and, more importantly, manufacturing capacity matters a lot. This explains why China-based CDMO players such as Wuxi Apptec (603259 CH/2359 HK, Buy) and Asymchem (002821 CH/6821 HK, Buy) have been investing heavily in capex over the past few years, and Samsung Biologics has announced to acquire PolyPeptide, even though the latter has made losses over the past three consecutive years.

According to PolyPeptide, its FY25 sales were EUR389mn, recording a CAGR of 10% during 2023-25, while Wuxi Apptec's TIDES business sales grew to CNY11bn in 2025 (c.EUR1.4bn by our calculation) at a CAGR of 49% over the same period.

As stated in our Anchor Report (link mentioned above), we think Asia CDMO's edge is efficiency, and we believe Samsung Bio's short-term goal would be to gain access to peptide CDMO knowhow and integrate PolyPeptide, rather than massive expansion.

Under these conditions, we don't foresee immediate capacity expansion for PolyPeptide that would challenge Wuxi Apptec's dominant position or deter Asymchem's rising capacity offering.

On running overseas CDMO facilities, we have witnessed unpleasant margin drag from such acquired facilities for several China-based CDMO players over the past few years, and hence, whether Samsung Bio can manage to turn around PolyPeptide efficiently needs to be closely monitored.
