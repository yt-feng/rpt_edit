## Lithium
## June China Export/Import Data

\- June lithium carbonate imports increased 46% y/y to 25.9kt vs 17.7kt in the prior-year period. 2026 YTD net imports are 177kt or 53.5% higher vs. the same period last year.

\- Lithium hydroxide exports from China in June decreased (4%) y/y to 6.0kt vs. 6.3kt in the prior-year period. 2026 YTD, China is a net importer of 8.9kt.

\- The lithium carbonate import price increased 4% m/m and 95% y/y in June: prices averaged \$19,695/t in June versus \$18,993/t in May and \$10,121/t in June 2025. See Table 6 for details.

\- The Chinese LiOH export price was 9% higher m/m, and 100% higher y/y in June: prices averaged \$21,663/t versus \$19,856/t in May. The average export price in June 2025 was \$10,858/t. See Table 5 for more details.

## Historical Information


Net lithium hydroxide exports in 2025 were 35.4kt or (69%) lower vs. 2024. Net exports for full-year 2024 of 113kt were (11%) lower versus 2023. FY2023 net exports were 126.2kt or 40% higher y/y. Net exports in 2022 were 90.3kt, 20.3kt or 29% higher vs. net exports of 70kt in 2021. Full-year 2024 exports of 121kt were (7%) lower vs 2023 exports of 130.0kt. 2022 exports of 93.4kt were about 27% higher y/y vs. 2021 exports of 73.6kt for the year.
