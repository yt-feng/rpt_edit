# Montage Technology Co Ltd | Asia Pacific
# 2Q beat driven by continued strong demand

Good 2Q preliminary results were driven by AI demand, despite a strong RMB. The company said its operations remain normal after the Korean prosecutor's recent antitrust investigation, which may take some time to complete.

Strong 2Q preliminary results: 2Q preliminary revenue was Rmb1.9bn, +28% Q/Q, +33% Y/Y, 6% above MSe, negatively affected by strong RMB vs USD. 2Q preliminary mid-point net profit was Rmb1.2bn, +36% Q/Q, +82% Y/Y, 50% above MSe. 2Q preliminary mid-point net profit excluding non-recurring items was Rmb0.7bn, +24% Q/Q, +27% Y/Y. The company noted the strong 1H results were due to AI demand across RCD and new interconnect chips products. 2Q net profit was helped by non-recurring items, such as investment gains, fair value changes. 3Q demand outlook remains strong and the company has strong order visibility.

Update on the recent investigation (see press reports): Montage announced that, on July 15, 2026, the Fair Trade Investigation Department of the Seoul Central District Prosecutors' Office conducted an on-site search and collected evidence at Montage's Korean office in connection with "potential violations of applicable antitrust laws and regulations". Montage said it is cooperating with all requests made by the Prosecutors' Office in connection with the investigation. As of the date of the announcement, the company said that neither it nor any of its directors or employees has been accused of misconduct by the Prosecutors' Office or any other government authority. The company's operations remain normal, and it will continue to focus fully on product research and development and on serving its customers. As the matter remains under investigation, the company said it is unable to predict the duration or outcome of the investigation.

Buyback announcement: On July 16, the Chairman and CEO, Dr. Howard Yang, proposed a Rmb300-600mn buyback plan of the company's A-share, pending board approval.

Maintain OW: We believe Montage is well positioned to benefit from expanding AI demand, driven by inference and agentic tasks through memory interface chips. We also see it as a unique proxy to benefit from China's AI server market through its PCIe business. Please refer to Global Technology: Innovating the Next-Generation Memory.
