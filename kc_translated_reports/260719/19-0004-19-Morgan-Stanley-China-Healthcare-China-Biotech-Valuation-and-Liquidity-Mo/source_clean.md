# China Biotech Valuation and Liquidity Monthly
## Key Takeaways

China biotech P/S compressed from c. 3.5x in May to troughs of c. 3.0x by late-June, then rebounded to 3.8x in mid-Jul from fund flow rotations and sector news.

Biotech liquidity rose to 7-7.5% from ASCO-associated flows during early-mid June, which retraced to c. 6% in late-June before accelerating to 9.7% by mid-Jul.

Valuation momentum: China biotech P/S compressed from c. 3.5x in late May to lows of c. 3.0x by end-June, with EV/S tracking a similar path from c. 2.8x to c. 2.4x. Both multiples then recovered likely following end-period fund rebalancing as well as fund flow rotation from the tech sector, reaching 3.8x and 3.2x by mid-Jul respectively. Beyond flow dynamics, sector updates including the Essential Drug List (EDL) policy tailwinds (link), overhang relief from BINSA (link), and recent deal momentum incl. Dizal (link), and Innovent (link) were also likely constructive drivers.

Liquidity momentum: Sector liquidity gradually increased to 7.5% by mid-June on ASCO-associated trading, which eased to c. 6% in the second half of June as flows faded on geopolitical concerns coupled with rotation out of the sector. This saw a sharp reversal at end-June with liquidity rising to 9.7% of HSI in mid-July on fund flow rotation and sector updates.
