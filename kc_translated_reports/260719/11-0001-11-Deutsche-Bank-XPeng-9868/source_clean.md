# Competitive Pricing and Appealing Design Drives "L03" SUV's Success

"Mona L03" SUV Targets Low-Price Segment for Volume Growth, Following "Mona M003" Success

On July 16, XPeng officially launched the "Mona L03" SUV at a final price of RMB 124-157k, approximately RMB 10,000 below its pre-sales price, making it the cheapest SUV model in XPeng's product portfolio. This launch represents XPeng's latest strategic move to penetrate the low-price SUV segment in pursuit of volume growth opportunities, following the successful 2024 entry into the low-price sedan market with the "Mona M03" sedan, which achieved over 10,000 units in average monthly sales volume due to its appealing design, competitive price, and advanced autonomous driving capabilities. We anticipate the "Mona L03" SUV will replicate the "Mona M03" sedan's success by leveraging a similar value proposition.

Distinctive Exterior and Interior Design Add to “Mona L03” 3 Competitive Edge

In our view, the “Mona L03” features a highly appealing exterior and interior design that should strengthen its competitiveness. The model’s front fascia builds on XPeng’s family design language while incorporating SUV-specific styling, with independent headlamp units, a fully covered front hood area, and slightly raised character lines on both sides, creating a strong sense of visual tension and sportiness. From the side, the “Mona L03” adopts flush side windows, frameless doors, and hidden window trim, giving the vehicle a clean and streamlined profile with limited body creases. Unlike traditional SUVs with pronounced wheel arches, the “Mona L03” uses a wheel-arch-free raised design to add muscularity to the side profile. At the rear, the horizontal waistline on the tailgate and smoked lower bumper create a more layered and robust visual effect, while the full-width “starring” taillight design incorporates dynamic lighting elements that enhance recognition when illuminated. The slightly upturned wind-blade-style rear bumper also reinforces the model’s coupe-SUV stance. Inside, the “Mona L03” adopts a wraparound cockpit design, with frequently touched areas finished in matte soft-touch materials and the headliner using velvet / suede-like materials to enhance perceived comfort and premium feel. The model also features 256-color wraparound ambient lighting, with segmented color contrast and music-synchronized lighting effects, adding to the cabin’s sense of technology and atmosphere. Notably, the “Mona L03” was designed by XPeng’s global design team led by JuanMa Lopez, a former Ferrari designer with an eight-year tenure at the company, where he led the development and design of several classic models before joining XPeng in June 2024.

"Mona L03" SUV: Strong Initial Demand Positions It Competitively in the Mid-Size SUV Segment

The "Mona L03," with dimensions of 4,650 mm in length, 1,920 mm in width, 1,600 mm in height, and a 2,850 mm wheelbase, is positioned to compete with models such as BYD's "Sealion 06" (RMB 130-200k), Leapmotor's "C10" (RMB 121-143k), Geely Galaxy's "M7" (RMB 120-148k), and Chang'an's "NEVO Q07" (RMB 130-180k). Thanks to its competitive pricing, the "Mona L03" SUV garnered 20,000 non-cancellable orders within the first seven minutes following its final price release, leading us to anticipate monthly deliveries of \~15,000 units in 2026, followed by an estimated full-year volume of 150,000 units in 2027.

## Flying car's mass production to start this year with >7,000 orders backlog

Meanwhile, XPeng also declared its flying car initiative aims for mass production in 2026, establishing two distinct product lines: the "Land Aircraft Carrier," focused on personal low-altitude flight experiences, and the new eVTOL "A868," designed for multi-person, long-range, and efficient travel. These offerings collectively address diverse low-altitude mobility needs across various distances and scenarios, with XPeng also announcing over 7,000 orders garnered to date, positioning the company as the global leader in flying car order volume. To facilitate the mass production of flying cars, Xpeng has established the world's first flying car factory with annual capacity of 10,000 units and a sprawling 120,000 square meters of floor space.
