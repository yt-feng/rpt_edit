## LGES wins 2.9GWh Google ESS project; SK On wins 13% of Korea AI ESS tender

On 15 July, Yonhap reported that LGES will supply ESS to Google's solar-plus-storage project aimed at meeting growing electricity demand from its datacenter and other projects. This will use LGES's Gen 2 LFP ESS battery system. Meanwhile, the Korea government has awarded its first AI-powered ESS tender of 640 Mwh to the K-battery trio. We note that the announcement of the $3^{\text{rd}}$ Korea ESS central tender (potentially $\sim$ 3.2 Gwh) is now expected in September. We view these developments as a minor positive for K-battery, with investor focus largely on profit turnaround OR repurposing/sale/monetization of unutilized battery assets as opposed to mere order wins. We prefer battery cell > battery materials, and recently moved SK Innovation to our top pick within K-energy after its $>140\%$ underperformance vs S-Oil, and $150\%/13\%$ underperformance vs SDI/LGES since 2Q25.

\- LGES to supply 2.9GWh ESS system to Google: On 15 Jul, Yonhap reported LGES's ESS supply partnership with Google and Cypress Creek Energy on building the Steel River Energy Center, a 2.46GW solar+storage project in Mississippi, Arkansas. Steel River is the US's largest solar+storage project to date with 2.94GWh of designed battery storage capacity. The project will proceed with a phase-based construction approach, with 2.0GWh of initial storage capacity, then expand to 2.9GWh when fully operational by 2029. Google, as both an anchor investor and offtaker, will support the development of the first two phases of the project. LGES is reportedly planning to supply its Gen II LFP ESS battery system, JF2 DC Link. Unveiled last September, JF2 DC Link features LGES's proprietary safety-reinforced pack and system design, which satisfies the stringent requirements on thermal runaway propagation mitigation from North American operators, including UL9540A and Large-Scale Fire Testing (LSFT).

\- SDI/LGES/SK Inno won 66%/22%/13% of 640MWh Korea AI ESS project. According to local media, Samsung SDI/LGES/ SK Inno have secured 420MWh/140MWh/80MWh of ESS battery orders from Korea's first government-led, AI-powered distribution grid ESS project. The “2026 AI-powered ESS deployment support project” featured 640MWh ESS, and was awarded to a consortium of nine operators including LGES, HD Electric, Hyundai E&C, SK Eternix and more. LGES won the largest order (140MWh) as a single operator, while Samsung SDI will supply NCA ESS battery cells to 6/9 operators. SK On won 80MWh, i.e. 13% of the total tender. The third batch of KPX central ESS orders is expected to be announced in September, which industry observers believe might be bigger than the first and second tenders (Figure 1).

\- Ultium Tennessee started LFP ESS supply, Warren EV plant set for August restart: On 10 Jul, GM Authority reported that the Ultium plant has begun production of LFP ESS battery cells at its Spring Hill, Tennessee facility. The plant will supply Gen II LFP batteries to LGES Vertech, LGES's US ESS system integration subsidiary (100% owned). The GM-LGES Ultium JV has been offline for 6 months for ESS line conversion, with current progress in-line with LGES' guided timeline of a 3Q re-start.
