# Initiation of Coverage
# Momenta
# Steering mainstream carmakers into the autonomous era; initiate at Buy

We initiate coverage on Momenta, the largest Chinese autonomous driving software provider (measured by OEM coverage), with a Buy rating. We believe its technology and scale make it a natural partner to leading domestic and global carmakers, helping accelerate their drive into the autonomous era. Despite competition, profitability and regulatory risks, its asset-light business model, along with strong revenue growth, provides the company with the operating leverage to generate high margins, thus justifying high P/S, in our view.

## A leading solutions provider to top global OEMs

Momenta collaborates with 24 domestic and global carmakers, and leads the market with a diversified customer base, and extensive installation and mass application experience. Carmakers we have spoken with have provided positive feedback on Momenta's technical capabilities and cost effectiveness. Aided by rapid technology upgrades and cost reduction, L2+ ADAS has accelerated its penetration across the model price range and is increasingly considered an essential feature in competitive models. We believe Momenta looks best positioned to benefit from the L2+ ADAS trend, and see high visibility for growth in its annual volume installations from 51k units in 2025 to 2.5m units in 2028E, representing an increase in market share from less than 2% to 10% of annual car sales in China over the same period. Supported by expanding OEM coverage, vehicle installations, and robotaxi and robovan operations, we forecast Momenta to achieve a 2025-30E revenue CAGR of c50% and break even in 2028.

## Competition, margin and regulatory risks

Momenta has a number of competitors in China's L2+ market, including Huawei, which lends its branding to partner OEMs, Horizon, which provides OEMs with hardware alongside its software solutions, as well as Zhuoyu DJI and DeepRoute. Competition aside, we also see margin risks as OEMs may exert pricing pressure to software suppliers amid fierce downstream competition. Memory price inflation may also curb near-term high-level NOA adoption. Regulations could also pose a risk to its overseas expansion amid geopolitical and data security concerns, and the heavily regulated robotaxi business faces similar risks, in our view.

## Valuation:

The stock is trading at 10x FY27E P/S vs 5x for Horizon and 12x for Tesla based on UBS estimates. While at a US\$8-9bn market cap similar to Horizon, Mobileye and Wayve, we see Momenta's fast revenue growth and high GPM leverage as highlights. Our price target of HK\$360.00 is based on an 8x FY28E P/S, implying 25% upside.

## Opportunities for Momenta

Momenta is the largest independent autonomous driving solutions provider in China, offering both L2-Urban NOA solution in mass produced vehicles and solutions for robotaxi services. Amid intense competition, advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) have increasingly become a must-have function amid rapid industry intelligence upgrades. UBS estimates the total addressable market (TAM) for high-level ADAS alone could reach approximately US\$142bn by 2030, driven by rising penetration rates, increasing software content per vehicle, and the industry's transition toward end-to-end intelligent driving architectures.

Looking at the China market, despite tepid overall domestic auto demand, L2++ ADAS-equipped models have continued to deliver solid year-on-year growth, supported by accelerating technology adoption and improving cost structures. Across different price ranges, urban ADAS features have recorded increasing penetration. At the same time, foreign OEMs have generally lagged leading domestic Chinese brands in L2 ADAS adoption and feature rollout. Momenta can be a natural partner to both domestic and JV OEMs for accelerating their intelligence upgrades leveraging leading technology and cost effectiveness.

## Risks to Momenta

Momenta operates in an increasingly crowded urban NOA market, facing intense competition from independent ADAS/autonomous driving solution providers. In China, rivals such as Huawei, DeepRoute.ai, QCraft, and Horizon Robotics are all pursuing scalable urban NOA deployments through partnerships with multiple automakers, while overseas competitors including Mobileye and Wayve are advancing software-centric autonomous driving platforms with global ambitions.

Beyond third-party technology vendors, Momenta also faces the risk of competition from its own OEM customers, many of which are investing heavily in in-house autonomous driving stacks, software platforms, and AI capabilities to gain greater control over differentiation, data, and costs. As OEMs increasingly internalise key ADAS and autonomous driving technologies, we believe Momenta will need to continue to demonstrate superior performance, faster iteration, and cost competitiveness to defend its position as a leading independent supplier.

Another risk may arise from memory chip cost inflation, as higher level ADAS requires more memory chip content than basic ones. Since last year, memory chip prices have more than tripled as of Q226, and UBS expects prices to continue to trend up towards end-2027. During our 2026 EV and AD tour, Horizon's management estimated that new car ADAS penetration increased from 70% in Q425 to 75% in Q126, but within models with ADAS capabilities, the adoption of high level NOA actually declined from 50% in Q425 to c40% in Q126, at least partially driven by memory chip cost pressure, per management.

In addition, Momenta is highly sensitive to evolving regulatory requirements in both China and international markets. In China, regulators are increasing their scrutiny of intelligent driving technologies, with stricter expectations around functional safety, accident accountability and data security, potentially increasing compliance costs and limiting commercialisation flexibility.

Overseas, Momenta faces additional challenges related to data sovereignty, local data storage and processing requirements, software certification, and cybersecurity regulations. Geopolitical tensions and tightening restrictions on Chinese technology in certain jurisdictions could further constrain market access, delay deployments, or require significant localization efforts, creating uncertainty for the company's global expansion strategy.

## WHAT'S PRICED IN?

## A high quality autonomous driving play

The company is trading at 10x FY27E P/S, compared to 5x for Horizon and 10x for Tesla on UBS estimates. While at a US\$8-9bn market cap similar to Horizon, Mobileye, Wayve, we see Momenta's fast revenue growth and high GPM leverage as highlights.

Compared with close peers such as Horizon, Mobileye, and Wayve (all trading at US\$8-9bn, almost the same as Momenta), we believe Momenta offers a high quality option to investors. Momenta and Horizon are in a similar ADAS industry with similar GPM, but as a software provider Momenta has smaller revenue, much lighter cost structure than hardware supplier Horizon. This should allow for higher potential operating leverage, in our view.

Compared with Mobileye, admittedly Mobileye is more mature and already profitable, but Momenta has much faster revenue growth. Over 2025-28E, UBS forecasts 10% revenue CAGR for Mobileye vs 64% for Momenta. Momenta, for now, is loss-making, but it shows strong revenue growth momentum with high contributing margins, which should support a higher valuation.

Compared with Wayve, which operates a highly similar business in the UK, we believe Momenta has an edge with its longer track record, a larger installation basis, and stronger customer relationships. Other robotaxi peers like Pony and WeRide have traded at historical valuation ranges of US\$2-9bn each, albeit Pony and WeRide have more of a track record and fleet in the robotaxi segment for now, while Momenta believes its L2+ data and expertise will help its robotaxi development.

## Valuing Momenta

Given its fast revenue growth and high contributing margin, we find it hard to value Momenta with conventional near-term P/E, P/B, P/S, EV/EBITDA multiples. Despite the risks relating to competition, margins and regulations, its light-asset business model generates high contributing margins and operating leverage, on top of high revenue growth, thus justifying a high P/S in the medium term, in our view. We value Momenta at 8x 2028E P/S, benchmarking it against the simple average 2027E P/S of its close peers Pony, WeRide, Tesla, Mobileye and Horizon, and set its price target accordingly at HK \$360.00, implying 25% upside from the current share price.

In the scalable robos segment, we assume Momenta operates 12k robotaxis by 2028E, and achieves Rmb2.4bn revenue from operating robotaxis and robovans in 2028E. Our 2026-28E total revenue assumptions are Rmb5.0-13bn, and we expect Momenta to break even in 2028E with net profit of Rmb1.5bn. Under our upside scenario, we value Momenta at HK\$450.00 based on 9x 2028E P/S.

BASE (HK\$360.00): Our base case assumes Momenta delivers 1.1-2.5m units of vehicles equipped with its ADAS solutions, deriving Rmb4.0-6.6bn of revenue from mass-produced vehicles in 2026-28E. In the scalable robos segment, we expect Momenta to operate 10k robotaxis by 2028E, and achieve Rmb4.1bn revenue from operating robotaxis and robovans in 2028E. Our forecast for 2026-28E total revenue is Rmb4.0-10.8bn, and we expect Momenta to break even in 2028E with net profit of Rmb1.0bn. Based on our base case, we set our price target for Momenta at HK\$360.00, based on 8x 2028E P/S, benchmarking it against its autonomous driving peers, implying 25% upside.

DOWNSIDE (HK\$200.00): Our downside case assumes Momenta struggles to commercialise its L2+ solutions and slows its L4 scalable robos applications.
