## Xtep core

Retail sales: Core Xtep retail sales declined by -MSD% yoy in 2Q26 with offline sales under pressure affected by macro headwind and unfavorable weather and online remained healthy growth. 3QTD trend largely similar with 2Q with no signs of meaningful improvement yet.

■ Discounts: 2Q retail discounts remained broadly stable yoy at 25-30%, among which online discounts improved yoy.

Products: mgmt highlighted running category maintained growth in 1H26, led by DD% growth in flagship running shoes and 70%+ yoy growth in the 2,000KM franchise, while Kids' Growth Sneaker series doubled in sales. Mgmt also highlighted positive consumer response to the 360X 3.0 and Qing Yun cushioning series.

■ Channel Inventory was relatively healthy at 4.5-5 months by 2Q26 (vs. 4.5x by 1Q26).

## ■ Update on channel strategies:

☐ Select Outlet Stores: The company has opened 60+ select outlet stores so far and has seen positive results, and will continue to roll-out in 2H26.

☐ DTC: DTC transformation is on track with its previous plan. Company indicated they have converted 100 stores in 1H26, and expect to convert an additional 400 stores in 2H2026.

Comments on Overseas: Online saw strong growth with 1H26 cross-border e-com sales + >100% yoy. Offline, the company has opened 13 new stores in Indonesia/Malaysia in 1H26, and will continue to open stores in ASEAN in 2026.

## Saucony:

Saucony's retail sales in 2Q26 was +LSD% yoy, moderated from >20% yoy in 1Q26, mainly to the brand's proactive tightening of online discounts, while offline momentum remained strong at 20%+ yoy in 2Q26.

Brand investments: mgmt highlighted that Saucony's key priority in 2026 is to further elevate its premium brand positioning, supported by increased engagement with high-end consumers through running events and event sponsorship.

■ Product: On top of professional running products as foundation of the core products across both racing and cushioning, the company sees lifestyle footwear and apparel as the key incremental growth opportunities for the brand going forward.

■ Channel: For offline channel, mgmt noted to focus on quality stores opening and store upgrades rather than aggressive stores opening, and expect to open 20-30 new stores in 2026. The new stores will be concentrated in high tier cities and core locations, typically in 200-300 sqm large format. Existing stores are also being upgraded through better floor/location adjustments.
