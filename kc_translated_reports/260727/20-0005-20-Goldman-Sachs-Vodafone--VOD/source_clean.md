# Vodafone (VOD.L): Modest 1Q27 beat and Africa-driven raise likely to reassure while limited Europe growth continues

Vodafone's 1Q27 results brought a modest beat, with Group underlying guidance upgraded to reflect an improved outlook in Africa. We would expect the shares to be up modestly today reflecting: 1) a Europe-driven beat in the quarter but which is mainly due to easier comps and lumpy revenues in Germany; 2) Group guidance driven by Africa which is only partly driven by structural factors. Company commentary on fundamental growth drivers across the Group is broadly unchanged. With Vodafone now guiding Group EBITDA and Adj FCF guidance to the upper end of its previous range, this implies around $7\%$ and $20\%$ yoy growth respectively for FY27, though we note that the Europe-based growth remains unchanged and limited.

Key investor focus likely to be on: 1) African growth better, drives Group guidance uplift – we note Vodacom (Vodafone’s listed main African business) raised guidance in its results today. This reflects a better than expected macro backdrop and favourable energy hedging as well as the more structural positive that recent price rises have landed well across the region; 2) Europe organic service revenue growth (OSRG) remains stable at +0.9% (vs +0.8% 4Q26); 3) Germany growth sustained but set to deteriorate through the year – OSRG was +1.2% yoy 1Q27 (vs +1.3% 4Q26), +1ppt vs consensus given the growing drag of lost 1&1 wholesale contract revenues was improved by better broadband ARPU growth and B2B digital services growth. Through FY27 VOD continues to guide for an increasing headwind from lost 1&1 revenues (particularly in Q2), and notes that Digital Services revenue is lumpy and was particularly strong in 2Q26 – we note consensus models OSRG -1.1% yoy 2Q27; 4) UK underlying trends broadly unchanged – OSRG was +0.6% yoy 1Q27 (vs -0.2% 4Q26 but this was due to easy comps from vs 1Q26 when there was a material fall in managed services revenues. Overall, VOD notes the underlying growth backdrop in the UK is unchanged and still sees VOD/3 synergies delivery is on track.

## Valuation and risks

We are Sell rated on Vodafone. Our 12-month price target is 85p (\$11.58 for the ADR) and is based on our EV/IC to ROIC/WACC methodology.

Key upside risks to our thesis and price target include: 1) 1&1 network rollout halt, independent of consolidation; 2) Vodafone participating in German consolidation; 3)

Greater market repair benefits than expected in the UK; 4) Stronger execution than expected; 5) Better competitive environment across key markets than expected; and 5) Emerging Markets upside risk
