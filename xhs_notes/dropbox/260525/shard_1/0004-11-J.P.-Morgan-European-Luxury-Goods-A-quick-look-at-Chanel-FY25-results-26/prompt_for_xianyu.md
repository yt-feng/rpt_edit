A quick look at Chanel FY25 results

Overnight Chanel released its full FY25 results, highlighting a return to topline growth at Group level and some signs of margin recovery. Overall FY25 sales were up 2% organic and +3% reported to \$19bn, while EBIT margin improved by 50bps to 24.5% and free cash generation accelerated 44% yoy to \$2.6bn (albeit still down almost 30% vs the 2022/2023 levels). Interestingly, both Leena Nair, Chanel Global CEO, and Philippe Blondiaux, Global CFO, noted in interviews, that the brand momentum started to improve in H2 last year (with sales running at HSD% since H2 25), already ahead of the new designer's shows.
