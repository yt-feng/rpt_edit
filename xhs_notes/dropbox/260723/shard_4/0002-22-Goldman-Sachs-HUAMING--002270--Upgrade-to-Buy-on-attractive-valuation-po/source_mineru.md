HUAMING (002270.SZ)

# Upgrade to Buy on attractive valuation post correction, while stable overseas growth driven by global grid upgrade remains intact

We upgrade Huaming from Neutral to Buy on valuation, as the 33% upside to our unchanged 12-month target price of Rmb23.9 underscores an attractive risk-reward. Amid market concerns about the visibility of AI capex and the valuation of high-beta AI infrastructure names, we believe Huaming offers a more defensive way to gain exposure to the multi-year global grid upgrade cycle with or without AI benefit, supported by aging infrastructure, renewable integration and overseas market-share gains. With a dominant domestic position, a rising export mix and structurally higher overseas margins, we expect Huaming to deliver resilient compound earnings growth, with net profit growing at a 17% CAGR over 2026E-30E.

Since the beginning of the 2026 stock has corrected $36\%$ , including a deeper drawdown of $52\%$ peak-to-trough compared to peers such as Sieyuan and Yingliu, as it traded alongside the U.S. power shortage theme despite fundamentally benefiting less, given the lack of supply shortage in on-load tap changers (OLTCs). After the stock price correction, we view valuation as relatively attractive, currently trading at 18.1x 12-month forward P/E, slightly below its historical average of 20.3x since 2015, suggesting room for valuation re-rating against unchanged EPS CAGR of $17\%$ in 2026E-30E.

Despite this reset in expectations and limited direct exposure to the U.S. power shortage, we believe Huaming's overseas growth ex-U.S. remains intact. Siemens Energy/Hitachi Energy forecast global grid investment to grow at a $7\%$ CAGR over 2024-35E, driven not only by data center build-out but also by aging infrastructure and renewable integration. Against this backdrop, we expect Huaming's global market share to increase from $13\%$ in 2026E to $16\%$ by 2030E, driven by on-par product quality, faster lead time, as well as a significant price advantage in markets that are still dominated by the competitor Maschinenfabrik Reinhausen GmbH (MR). As a result, we expect export contribution within the OLTC business to rise from $41\%$ in 2026E to $59\%$ by 2030E.

We forecast revenue CAGR of 14% over 2026E-30E, with margin expansion driven by a higher mix of overseas business, which enjoys 5-10pp higher gross margins than domestic operations. This should lift blended gross margin from 56% in 2026E to 57.5% by 2030E, translating into net profit CAGR of 17% over the same period.

## Key downside risks:

Slower-than-expected overseas market-share gains: Overseas expansion may fall short of our expectations if customer certification takes longer, product adoption is slower, or competition from established global suppliers remains stronger than anticipated.

## Investment thesis, valuation methodology and risks

Investment thesis: Huaming is China's dominant supplier of on-load tap changers, with an estimated $90\%$ domestic market share by volume, and is increasingly expanding overseas, where margins are structurally higher. We believe Huaming can leverage product quality broadly comparable with Maschinenfabrik Reinhausen, and we expect its materially lower pricing, faster delivery and new production capacity in Turkey and Indonesia to increase its global market share from around $13\%$ in 2026E to $16\%$ by 2030E, lifting overseas revenue to more than half of OLTC sales. This should support approximately $14\%$ revenue CAGR and $17\%$ net profit CAGR over 2026E-30E, alongside strong cash conversion, first-quartile ROE and CROCI among China Industrial Tech sector, and a $3\%$ dividend yield. Its competitive moat is reinforced by the mission-critical nature of tap changers, long customer qualification cycles, high switching costs and a structurally duopolistic global market. We therefore view Huaming as a high-quality beneficiary of the multi-year global grid upgrade cycle. We have a Buy rating on Huaming as we view valuation as attractive with compelling risk-reward against an EPS CAGR of $17\%$ in 2026E-30E.

Valuation methodology: Our 12-month target price of Rmb23.9 is based on 22x 2028E P/E, discounted back to 2027E at a 9.5% cost of equity.

Key downside risks: 1) Slower-than-expected overseas market-share gains; 2) Weaker-than-expected margin profile; 3) Weaker-than-expected domestic revenue growth

## GS Factor Profile

The GS Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows:

Growth is based on a stock's forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock's forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock's forward-looking P/E, P/B, price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile).

Financial Returns and Multiple use the GS analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics).

For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
