# Cutting Topsports/Pou Sheng estimates to reflect the termination of Nike online sales from CY2027

Topsports and Pou Sheng announced that Nike will terminate the companies' online platform sales of NIKE products in ML China from January 1, 2027. The affected business represented around $22\%$ of Topsports' FY2/2026 group revenue and $15\%$ of Pou Sheng's FY2025 group revenue. Topsports expects a material near-term negative impact, while Pou Sheng noted that the affected business made no significant contribution to group profitability; both companies will continue to cooperate with Nike on offline sales.

For Topsports and Pou Sheng, we revise down Topsports' FY2/27-29E sales and net income by 6-21% and 8-35% respectively; and cut Pou Sheng's FY27-28E sales and net income by 14% and 7-9% respectively to reflect the removal of Nike online sales from CY2027. We believe Nike's channel reform could continue to impact its sales in China which will impact Topsports and Pou Sheng's earnings in the near-term. We maintain Buy on Pou Sheng for manageable earnings impact and undemanding valuation, and Neutral on Topsports on already significant correction (share price down \~24% today, and down\~44% since the news out) and attractive dividend yield of 13% on our estimates. TP for Pou Sheng is maintained at HK\$0.49 (7x 2026E P/E unchanged) and that for Topsports is cut to HK\$1.2 on 8X FY2/28E PE (from 10X FY2/27 PE) reflecting the larger earnings impact.

For sportswear brands, we believe this could offer opportunities for other brands including Anta and Li Ning as Nike is still the largest sports brand in China with c.16% market share in 2025. Having said that, we will need to closely monitor Nike's strategy on the potential inventory management from these online channels which could lead to a more promotional environment, which could potentially serve as downside risks on margins to all the brands. Maintain Buy on Anta and Li Ning.

Background: The issue first surfaced in mid-June, when public news reports suggested that NIKE might terminate its online distributorship arrangements in mainland China from January 1, 2027. Topsports responded on June 25 that it had not received any official notice, while Pou Sheng did not publicly respond at the time. On Nike's FY4Q26 earnings call on June 30, management did not confirm any specific change, but reiterated plans to elevate China's online and offline marketplace, improve full-price realization and work closely with local partners.

## GS Factor Profile

The GS Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows:

Growth is based on a stock's forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock's forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock's forward-looking P/E, P/B, price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile).

Financial Returns and Multiple use the GS analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics).

For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.

## M&A Rank

Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.

## Ratings, coverage universe and related definitions

Each region manages Regional Conviction Lists, which are selected from Buy rated stocks on the respective region's Investment Lists and represent investment recommendations focused on the size of the total return potential and/or the likelihood of the realization of the return across their respective areas of coverage. The addition or removal of stocks from such Conviction Lists are managed by the Investment Review Committee or other designated committee in each respective region and do not represent a change in the analysts' investment rating for such stocks.

Total return potential represents the upside or downside differential between the current share price and the price target, including all paid or anticipated dividends, expected during the time horizon associated with the price target. Price targets are required for all covered stocks. The total return potential, price target and associated time horizon are stated in each report adding or reiterating an Investment List membership.
