While our survey work continues to indicate that CIOs see Salesforce constructively as a key partner they plan to leverage in deploying enterprise AI (Exhibit 5), there remains much to prove by means of effective monetization translating to durable growth acceleration, which we think is well evidenced by the longer journey for Salesforce (and SaaS broadly) relative to other areas of Software (Infra, some Security) that are actively seeing topline inflection from AI.
