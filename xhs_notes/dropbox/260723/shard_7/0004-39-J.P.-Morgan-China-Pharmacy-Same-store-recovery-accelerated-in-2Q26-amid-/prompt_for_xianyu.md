## Same-store recovery accelerated in 2Q26 amid consolidation; future growth driven by three reinforcing structural tailwinds

The pharmacy call series we held over the past few days points to a clear operating inflection for the listed China pharmacy chains. Same-store growth re-accelerated in 2Q26 across all three names (Yifeng \~6%, LaoBaiXing [LBX] 5–6%, Dashenlin also accelerating month-on-month), versus low-single-digit prints in 4Q25–1Q26, driven mainly by foot traffic. Management and our channel expert attribute the foot traffic growth to an accelerating industry clear-out in the last two years, and the store base is expected to shrink from \~700k toward 400–500k over 3–5 years, redistributing traffic to surviving stores, especially ones of leading chains. At the same time, tightening medical-insurance supervision (flight inspections, white-list regime, drug traceability requirement and prescription controls) is squeezing small and mid-size chains hardest, converting regulatory pressure into an M&A wave and franchise pipeline for the large chains at falling valuations (mid-small chain's P/S now is 0.3–0.5x). We see three reinforcing structural tailwinds for leading pharmacy chains from now on: consolidation-driven market share gains, a franchise-led asset-light growth engine, and margin resilience from product category mix improvement and cost cuts. Overall, we think the worst is over in the pharmacy space and the leading pharmacies are now well positioned to offer higher-quality growth with the risk narrative (online price parity, in-hospital price benchmarking, flight inspections) having largely shifted from fundamentals to sentiment.

\- 2Q26 same-store growth likely to re-accelerate due to traffic growth, driven by continued store closure and consolidation in the industry.
