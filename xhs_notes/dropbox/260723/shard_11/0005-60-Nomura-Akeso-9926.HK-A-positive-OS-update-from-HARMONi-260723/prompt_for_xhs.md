# A positive OS update from HARMONi Quick Note

Additional analysis suggests improvement in hazard ratio (HR), which is comparable to that of HARMONi-A conducted by Akeso

On 22 July night (HKT), the company's overseas partner – Summit Therapeutics (SMMT US, Not rated), announced that an additional analysis (conducted in Jun 2026) suggests that a hazard ratio (HR) of 0.76 was observed in both the full intention-to-treat (ITT) patients and the western patient subgroup, on Overall Survival (OS) of the HARMONi study.

Thus, full ITT patients' HR has improved from 0.79 when primary analysis was conducted in Apr 2025, and 0.78 from another analysis conducted in Sept 2025; while western patients' HR saw more improvement — from 0.98 in Apr 2025 and 0.84 in Sept 2025. Median follow-up of the western patient subgroup is 23.2 months, compared with 9.2 months and 13.7 months in the first and second analysis.

Note that the Overall Survival (OS) hazard ratio (HR) of the full ITT and western patient subgroups is largely comparable to 0.74 in the HARMONi-A study conducted by Akeso in China.

Increased likelihood of this indication receiving approval, though more detailed data to be disclosed later

Recall in the HARMONi analysis of Apr 2025, the primary endpoint Progression Free Survival (PFS) was met, while the other primary endpoint OS was not.

More detailed data will be presented at the upcoming academic meeting, according to Summit's management that mentioned the regulatory decision (PDUDA) action day is 14 Nov 2026 for this indication (2L+ EGFRm NSCLC after 3rd gen TKI).

We believe the improvement in OS HR data increases the likelihood of this indication being approved, though more detailed data need to be further released and examined.

We maintain our Buy rating and DCF-based TP of HKD142.50 (WACC of $10.8\%$ and terminal growth rate at $4\%$ ). The stock currently trades at 3.7x FY35F sales of CNY22.4bn.
