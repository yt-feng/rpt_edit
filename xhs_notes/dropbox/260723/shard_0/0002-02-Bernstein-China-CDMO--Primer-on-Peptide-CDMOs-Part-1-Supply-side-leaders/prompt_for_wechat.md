## PLAYER BENCHMARKS: SCALE, COST STRUCTURE, AND TECHNOLOGY

## Scale: despite limited disclosures in the industry, Wuxi Apptec is on a solid level of its own

Assessing peptide manufacturing scale remains challenging due to limited public disclosure. Among major peptide CDMOs, only WuXi AppTec and Asymchem have disclosed aggregate peptide capacity in kiloliters (kLs), reporting c.100kL and 45kL in 2025, respectively, with planned expansion to 130kL and 69kL in 2026. For most other players, capacity must be inferred from individual site disclosures, announced expansions, revenue scale and industry checks. More importantly, we believe metric tonnes of peptide output are a better measure than kL of reactor volume, as productivity is heavily influenced by peptide length, loading, yields and batch configuration. On this basis, WuXi AppTec stands in a category of its own, with estimated output of \~58 metric tons in 2025, while most peers remain in the range of single-digit to low-double-digit metric tons, highlighting the substantial scale advantage WuXi has established during the GLP-1 expansion cycle (Exhibit 6).

## Large batch capability remains a differentiator

Reported commercial batch sizes vary significantly across suppliers, reflecting differences in technology platforms, peptide complexity and customer mix. Most leading peptide CDMOs operate at average commercial batch sizes of roughly 15-30kg, with only a handful of players possessing demonstrated track records in consistently executing larger-scale campaigns (Exhibit 7).

## Chinese capacity expansion has yet to be globalized

Chinese players, led by WuXi AppTec and Asymchem, have expanded peptide capacity aggressively and now rank among the largest suppliers globally. However, unlike Western peers such as Bachem, PolyPeptide and Corden, whose manufacturing networks are distributed across North America and Europe, peptide production capacity at Chinese leaders remains concentrated almost entirely within China today. While WuXi plans to add peptide manufacturing capacity in Singapore by 2028, geographic diversification remains limited at present, leaving Chinese suppliers more exposed to customer concerns around supply chain concentration and geopolitical risk (Exhibit 8).

## Divergent cost structures

Despite operating within the same peptide value chain, profitability varies significantly across leading peptide CDMOs. WuXi AppTec stands out with a 47% GPM and 37% EBIT margin in 2025, supported by the lowest COGS ratio among peers at 53% of revenue. Of note, their group-level profit margin has improved over time with the increasing mix of TIDES revenue. By contrast, European specialty peptide players operate with materially lower profitability: Bachem generated c.22% EBIT margin, while PolyPeptide and EuroAPI remained near breakeven in 2025 (Exhibit 9).

WuXi AppTec has been the industry's most aggressive capacity builder. The biggest strategic differentiator among peptide CDMOs has been capital deployment. Since 2020, WuXi AppTec has consistently outspent all peers, from \$460 Mn in 2020 to c.\$1.5 Bn in 2022, before remaining elevated at \$831 Mn in 2025. In comparison, 2025 CapEx amounted to approximately \$408 Mn for Bachem, \$191 Mn for Asymchem, \$124 Mn for PolyPeptide and \$89 Mn for EuroAPI (Exhibit 12).
