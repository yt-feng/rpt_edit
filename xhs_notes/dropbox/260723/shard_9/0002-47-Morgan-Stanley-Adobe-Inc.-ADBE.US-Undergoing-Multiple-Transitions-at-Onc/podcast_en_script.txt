EN_B: That is another critical point in the report. Adobe already has the highest margins among its SaaS peers. But the report argues that margin expansion from here is very limited. EN_A: So the peak margin story might be behind us?
EN_B: That is the view. To get a step-function improvement in profitability, Adobe would likely need a major restructuring. But that would be hard to communicate while the company is trying to accelerate innovation and prove that AI can expand the creative base rather than cannibalize it.

EN_B: The report uses a framework called Moat and Journey. On the moat side, Adobe scores 33 out of 50. The brand, distribution, and deep product expertise are still strong. EN_A: So AI is both an opportunity and a threat for Adobe?
EN_B: Exactly. AI can help expand the user base, but it also risks eating into existing Creative Cloud revenue. The report says the magnitude of that risk is still very hard to quantify. And that uncertainty is a big reason for the cautious stance.
