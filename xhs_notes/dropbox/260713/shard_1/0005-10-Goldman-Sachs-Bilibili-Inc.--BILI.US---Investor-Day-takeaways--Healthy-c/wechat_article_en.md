The risk is no longer whether Bilibili can monetize—it already is. The question is whether it can sustain the compounding dynamics that drove its 2025 advertising growth of 23% and raise its non-GAAP EBIT margin from 8.0% to a projected 14.7% by 2028 without breaking the community trust that made the platform valuable in the first place.

## Bilibili's Community Is Not a Cost Center—It Is a Structural Barrier to Entry That Is Getting Stronger

The most important metric from the Investor Day was not a revenue number but an engagement statistic: comments exceeding 100 characters grew 32% year over year, outpacing overall comment growth of 19%. This is not a trivial detail. It signals that user participation is becoming deeper, not wider. When users write longer comments, they are investing cognitive effort into the platform. That investment creates switching costs, strengthens the bond between creators and audiences, and generates the kind of unstructured data that becomes valuable for advertising targeting.

The community's durability is visible in its content lifecycle. A video uploaded in 2009 still generated 2.03 million additional views in the past year. That is a structural advantage that short-form platforms cannot replicate. Bilibili's content library accumulates value over time rather than decaying. At the same time, the platform is incubating new long-form formats: video podcasts grew more than 500% year over year. This combination of archival depth and format innovation means Bilibili is not dependent on any single content trend.

Management's framing of AI as an amplifier rather than a disruption risk is strategically sound. The logic is straightforward: AI can improve content creation productivity, but it does not change the trust relationship between creator and audience. Bilibili's essence is community and trust, and generative AI is more likely to strengthen the platform model than replace it. This is a defensible thesis because the platform's value lies in the interaction layer—bullet comments, discussions, creator-audience rapport—not in the raw content itself. AI-generated content on its own lacks that interaction layer. Bilibili can therefore deploy AI to lower creation costs while preserving the social dynamics that drive retention.

## Advertising Growth Is No Longer Just About Traffic—It Is About Unlocking Three Distinct Monetization Layers

Bilibili's advertising revenue grew 30% year over year in the first quarter of 2026, but the headline number understates the strategic shift. Management framed growth around three pillars: traffic, user consumption power, and deep content scenario monetization. The first two are well understood. DAU grew 8% year over year, average daily time spent increased by 11 minutes, and the average user age reached 27, with 55% of users in tier-1 and tier-2 cities. This demographic profile should continue supporting consumption-oriented advertising budgets.

The third pillar is where the structural story lives. Bilibili is monetizing what it calls deep content scenarios—search, playback pages, and PC/OTT viewing. Search revenue grew more than 100% year over year in 1Q26. This is significant because search advertising captures high-intent user behavior. When a user searches for a specific topic on Bilibili, they are actively seeking information, not passively scrolling. That intent can be monetized at higher rates than feed-based advertising. Playback page revenue grew more than 80% year over year, leveraging the contextual information around video consumption. PC/OTT revenue grew more than 50% year over year, benefiting from larger screens and more immersive viewing that commands premium brand advertising positioning.

Together, these three channels represent a second advertising revenue base that is growing faster than the core feed business. They are also structurally less vulnerable to macro advertising cycles because they are tied to specific user behaviors that are sticky. Search and playback page monetization do not require incremental user growth—they require better matching of advertiser demand to existing user actions. That is a pure execution play, and the early results suggest Bilibili is executing well.

The flywheel effect is visible in creator economics. Average creator income increased 24% year over year in 1Q26, while content submissions from creators with more than 10,000 followers increased more than 50%. When creators earn more, they produce more content. More content attracts more users and deeper engagement. Deeper engagement creates more advertising inventory and better targeting data. The system is self-reinforcing, and the data supports the claim that monetization is reinforcing content supply rather than extracting from it.

Management stated unequivocally that AI will be the only major investment priority over the next several years. This is a deliberate capital allocation decision. Every AI project must directly improve business metrics through cost reduction, efficiency gains, user growth, or revenue expansion. This is not speculative R&D. It is applied machine learning with clear KPIs.

The most concrete results are in advertising monetization. With the help of AI, user behavior, comments, and creator content are being converted into structured advertiser insights. This semantic data asset has already delivered an ROI improvement of more than 11% within gaming advertisers and has expanded into education, consumer electronics, novels, and short-drama verticals. More than 50% of advertisement video covers and titles are already AI-generated on the Bilibili platform, accounting for over 40% of advertising spending. Management plans to roll out fully AI-generated video production across mini-games, short dramas, and gaming advertisers by year-end.

The recommendation and matching algorithm is the next frontier. Management expects ranking-layer upgrades in the second half of 2026 to deliver a 5% or higher eCPM uplift. Fully managed advertising products have already demonstrated strong results in e-commerce testing, with spending from mid-sized merchants increasing more than twofold. These are not theoretical gains. They are operational improvements that compound over time.

The strategic implication is that Bilibili is building an AI moat inside its community moat. The platform has a unique data asset: billions of user interactions that are contextual, long-form, and rich in sentiment and intent. Generic AI models cannot replicate this data. Bilibili can use its proprietary data to train models that understand its specific community dynamics better than any external competitor. If executed well, this creates a widening advantage in advertising targeting and content recommendation that is difficult for rivals to match.

## Bilibili's Game Pipeline Is No Longer a Bet—It Is a Portfolio Strategy with Clear Milestones and Category Discipline

The game business has been Bilibili's most volatile segment. Revenue swung from a 20% decline in 2022 to 40% growth in 2024, and the 2026 outlook calls for a 5% decline before returning to 10% growth in 2027. The volatility reflects the lumpy nature of game launches. What has changed is the strategy. Management reiterated its 2024 framework: build games that young people love, become first or best in selected vertical categories, and focus on long-term operations. The goal is to add at least two long-lived, category-leading games over the next two to three years.

The pipeline visibility is improving. For 2026, management highlighted Lumi Master and Sangokushi: Wangdao Tianxia as key fourth-quarter launches. Sangokushi: Wangdao Tianxia is positioned as a complementary offering to the existing Sanguo: NSLG, targeting more intense and immersion-oriented SLG players rather than cannibalizing the lighter game. Lumi Master is a self-developed pet-collection adventure game targeting the fast-growing monster-catching genre, with a global launch scheduled for the fourth quarter of 2026.

The 2027 pipeline includes several high-potential titles. Sanguo: Ncard is a strategy card game where management explicitly prioritizes long-term retention over short-term monetization. Sanguo: The Ravages of Time is a self-developed title based on a well-known comic IP, targeting the wargame/tactical RPG segment, which management views as a category with long lifecycle potential. The undisclosed MMORPG, described as the official sequel to a globally recognized top-tier gaming IP with a history spanning over two decades, is one of the most intriguing projects. Formal unveiling is expected around late July or early August. Escape from Duckov Mobile is slated for 2027, with development focused on reworking core gameplay systems rather than a simple mobile port.

The strategic discipline is evident in project approval. Management noted that game project approval within Bilibili is now far more disciplined than in previous years. The number of self-developed titles is expected to increase slowly but grow faster than licensed titles over time. This is a sensible approach. Self-developed titles have higher margins and give Bilibili full control over intellectual property, but they also carry higher development risk. By starting slowly and scaling gradually, Bilibili is building internal capability without overcommitting capital.

The Guild Wars Series represents Bilibili's first global publishing projects. Management views the title as highly compatible with Bilibili's content ecosystem given the strong concentration of strategy-card and MMO enthusiasts on the platform. Importantly, this will be a true global multi-platform launch across PC and mobile, underscoring Bilibili's growing international publishing capabilities. If successful, this could open a new revenue stream that is independent of China's regulatory cycle.

## A Decision Framework for Observers: Three Questions to Track Over the Next Four Quarters

The Investor Day provided enough specificity to construct a decision framework. Over the next four quarters, observers should focus on three leading indicators that will determine whether Bilibili's flywheel is accelerating or decelerating.

First, track the growth rate of comments exceeding 100 characters relative to overall comment growth. If deep engagement continues to outpace shallow engagement, the community moat is strengthening. If the gap narrows, it may indicate that user growth is diluting interaction quality. This is a qualitative metric with quantitative expression, and it is the earliest signal of community health.

Second, monitor the revenue contribution from deep content scenarios—search, playback page, and PC/OTT. These channels are growing at 50% to 100% year over year. If they maintain or accelerate that growth, Bilibili is successfully building a second advertising revenue base that is less dependent on macro conditions. If growth decelerates, the monetization ceiling may be lower than expected.

Third, watch the game pipeline execution. The fourth quarter of 2026 is a critical test. Lumi Master and Sangokushi: Wangdao Tianxia need to demonstrate strong initial retention metrics. If they do, the 2027 pipeline gains credibility. If they underperform, the game segment will remain a source of volatility rather than a growth driver.

The financial projections support the positive case. Non-GAAP EBIT margin is expected to expand from 8.0% in 2025 to 14.7% in 2028, driven by gross margin improvement from 37% to 41% and operating leverage in sales and marketing, which is projected to decline from 14.5% of sales to 12.4%. These are achievable targets if advertising growth remains above 20% and the game pipeline delivers. The risk is that advertising growth decelerates faster than operating expenses can be adjusted, compressing margins before the game pipeline matures.

Bilibili has reached an inflection point. It has proven it can monetize without destroying its community. It has a clear capital allocation strategy centered on AI. It has a game pipeline with more visibility than at any point in its history. The next four quarters will determine whether these are the foundations of sustained compounding or the peak of a cyclical recovery. The data from the Investor Day suggests the former, but the burden of proof now rests on execution.
