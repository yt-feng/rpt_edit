Our December 2026 PT of \$725 is based on \~21x our 2027E GAAP EPS of \$34.19, further supported by our DCF model. We apply a premium to the SPX as we have greater confidence in Meta's strong top-line growth and ongoing cost efficiencies. We use this valuation approach as we believe it appropriately balances Meta's valuation relative to its growth and industry peers. Our DCF results in an estimated value of \$725 per share and employs a \~14% WACC and a +10% terminal growth rate.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Upside risks include: 1) AI Ad initiatives drive engagement, revenue growth, & monetization; 2) Muse Spark & MSL model launches resonate and drive model efficacy, user growth, & engagement; 3) ROAS gains drive greater ad spend and market share gains, supporting revenue growth; & 4) Investments ease & cost discipline drives greater than expected GAAP EPS & FCF.

Downside risks include: 1) AI investments are heavy & monetization lags, which weighs on GAAP OI, GAAP EPS, & FCF; 2) slower than expected revenue growth limits AI monetization upside; 3) AI product & model launches do not resonate, weighing on engagement, revenue, & margins; 4) competition for online and mobile ad dollars from Google, TikTok, OpenAI, and other online advertising companies; & 5) regulatory litigations & charges.
