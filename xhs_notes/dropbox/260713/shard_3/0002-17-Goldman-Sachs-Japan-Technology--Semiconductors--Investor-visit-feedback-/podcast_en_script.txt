EN_B: Far fewer bulls there. And there is another interesting divide: investors prefer memory-related capex, especially CPU-linked, over GPU or ASIC. That is a bit counter to the typical AI narrative.

EN_B: Equipment price hikes were a big one. Tokyo Electron is pushing for price increases, and everyone wants to know if that will spread. Also, memory pricing. EN_B: It means earnings expectations for front-end companies could still go up. EN_B: Kioxia is interesting. The recent share price correction has some investors seeing a buying opportunity. But the report flags that the rate of change in NAND prices is slowing, making earnings surprises less likely. That is something to watch.
