PPI inflation likely peaked: China's CPI inflation softened slightly from $1.2\%$ yoy in May to $1.0\%$ yoy in June, mainly driven by lower energy and gold prices. PPI inflation ticked up from $3.9\%$ yoy to $4.1\%$ yoy, but declined sharply in sequential month-over-month terms from May to June. In light of our commodity team's lower oil price projections, we revised down our H2 PPI inflation forecast. For 2026 and 2027, we expect full-year PPI inflation to be $2\%$ and $0\%$ , respectively.

PBOC signals: The Q2 MPC meeting statement acknowledged challenges in the domestic economy, including strong supply and weak demand, but refrained from signalling broad-based easing measures. We expect no policy rate or RRR cuts in 2026. Separately, Governor Pan's latest speech in Hong Kong unveiled measures to improve CNH market infrastructure, liquidity, and products. With the central bank determined to advance RMB internationalization, we expect the RMB to continue strengthening against the USD and other currencies.

CNY has appreciated against both the USD and the CFETS basket

Government's plan to boost retail sales: On July 9, the Ministry of Commerce announced a document on “Accelerating the Innovative Development of the Retail Industry.” Most measures appear to focus on the supply side, such as ensuring easy access to supermarkets, convenience stores, and fresh food markets; improving the quality of goods and services; and developing new retail formats, models, and consumption scenarios. The document also emphasizes market regulation, including strengthening antitrust reviews of undertakings in the online platform sector and narrowing price gaps between online and offline sales.
