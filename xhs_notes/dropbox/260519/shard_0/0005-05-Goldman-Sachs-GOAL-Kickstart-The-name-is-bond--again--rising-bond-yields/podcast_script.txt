ZH_A: 最近债市波动很大，我看到一份研报说股票和债券的走势又“分道扬镳”了，这到底是怎么回事？

ZH_B: 是的，上周美国通胀数据超预期，经济数据又很坚挺，全球债券收益率大幅上升。一个关键指标是，美股和美债10年期收益率的两个月相关性，已经跌到1990年代末以来最负的水平。

ZH_A: 最负的水平？这意味着什么，是不是说股票和债券不再一起涨跌了？

ZH_B: 没错。当收益率上升是由通胀驱动时，股市会把这视为坏消息，因为高利率打压估值，而经济增长的乐观情绪不足以对冲。这和我们过去看到的“增长驱动型”收益率上升完全不同。

ZH_A: 所以，如果是增长驱动的，股市还能扛住，但现在是通胀驱动的？

ZH_B: 正是这样。报告里区分了两种情景：如果收益率上升是因为经济更好，股市通常能消化；但如果是通胀导致的，股债相关性就会转负。而且，最近市场把能源价格冲击和地缘政治风险也纳入了考量。

ZH_A: 那除了原因，收益率上升的速度和水平也很重要吧？

ZH_B: 对，速度是关键。上周美国10年期收益率一周内上涨超过两个标准差，这在历史上是股市很难消化的。过去，这种“无序”的上涨往往会引发市场动荡。

ZH_A: 那水平呢？比如10年期收益率超过5%会怎样？

ZH_B: 报告提到，历史上当10年期收益率高于5%，或实际收益率超过2%到2.5%时，股债相关性会更负。但一个更稳健的指标是，10年期实际收益率与长期共识实际GDP增长预期之间的差值——现在这个差值是2.1%，是正的，意味着货币政策偏紧，这对股市是更严峻的挑战。

ZH_A: 那在这种情况下，投资者该怎么办？报告有什么建议吗？

ZH_B: 研报建议，对于平衡型投资者，可以考虑“股市下跌、利率上升”这种双变量对冲结构，来防范收益率进一步上升导致的股市抛售。这种策略的赔付历史表现很吸引人。

ZH_A: 听起来像是一种“双输”对冲，但具体怎么操作？

ZH_B: 报告里没有完全展开所有细节。比如，这种对冲的隐含相关性虽然从2月高点回落了，但依然比实际相关性高，这意味着市场可能还没有完全定价这种风险。完整报告里会继续分析这些细节。

ZH_A: 好吧，那我们给听众留个悬念。如果你想知道更多关于如何构建这种对冲，或者想看到完整的资产配置建议，记得去读原报告。

ZH_B: 是的，报告里还有欧洲通胀数据、PMI等更多宏观事件的解读，这些都值得深入看。

EN_A: So, bonds are making headlines again. A recent report says the correlation between stocks and bonds is at its most negative since the late 1990s. What does that actually mean for investors?

EN_B: It means we are back in a regime where rising bond yields are hurting equities. Last week, US inflation surprised to the upside, and activity data stayed strong, so global bonds sold off. The key point is that the two-month correlation between US equities and the 10-year yield is now deeply negative.

EN_A: So stocks and bonds are moving in opposite directions again. But why does that matter? Isn't it just a technical indicator?

EN_B: It matters because it tells us what is driving the market. When yields rise because growth is improving, equities can usually handle it. But when yields rise because of inflation, it becomes a headwind. The report shows that since the Middle East conflict and the energy price shock, markets have seen inflation, not growth, as the main macro shock.

EN_A: So the source of the yield move is crucial. What about the speed and the level?

EN_B: Exactly. Speed is a big factor. Last week, the 10-year yield moved more than two standard deviations in one week. Historically, equities struggle with that kind of sharp, disorderly rise. And the level matters too, but less consistently. When real yields are above 2 to 2.5 percent, or when the 10-year nominal yield is above 5 percent, the correlation tends to become more negative.

EN_A: So we are in a dangerous zone. What is the report suggesting investors do about it?

EN_B: It recommends looking at "equity down, rates up" hybrid hedges. These are structures that pay out when both stocks fall and bond yields rise. They look attractive right now, especially for balanced portfolios.

EN_A: But are those hedges priced fairly? Or is there still risk that the market hasn't fully priced in?

EN_B: That is the interesting part. The implied correlation from options is still higher than the realized correlation, even though it has fallen since February. That means the market might not fully be pricing in the risk of another yield shock. The report does not fully answer why that gap persists, but it is something to watch.
