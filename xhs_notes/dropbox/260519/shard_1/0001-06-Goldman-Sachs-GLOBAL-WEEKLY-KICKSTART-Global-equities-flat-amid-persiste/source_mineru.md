# Global equities flat amid persistent geopolitical uncertainty

Global equities were broadly flat, with EM declining 2.5% and underperforming DM, while the US rose modestly (+0.2%) (Exhibit 1). Momentum continues to outperform (Exhibit 31), supported by Energy and Technology (Exhibit 3), large caps (Exhibit 29), and strong earnings revisions (Exhibit 21).

# The HALO (Heavy Assets, Low Obsolescence) effect in Japan:

■ Rotation to HALO: Investors are shifting toward asset-heavy, capital-intensive stocks, driven by geopolitics, supply chain realignment, and AI disruption risks in asset-light sectors.   
Performance & valuations: In Japan, the Growth vs. Value P/E premium peaked (\~140%) in 2022 and has since compressed (\~60%), with HALO outperforming LAHO since late 2025 as valuation gaps narrow. Our Japan Equity Strategists expect the geopolitical and policy backdrop to remain supportive of further HALO outperformance.   
■ Structural shift: Asset-heavy sectors (Utilities, Transport, Materials) benefit from durable capacity and visibility, while asset-light sectors face margin and terminal value pressure from AI and changing capital needs.
