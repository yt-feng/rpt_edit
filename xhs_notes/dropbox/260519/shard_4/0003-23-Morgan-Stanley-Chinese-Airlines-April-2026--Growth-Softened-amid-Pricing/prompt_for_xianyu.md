# April 2026: Growth Softened amid Pricing Inflation; LCC and Int'l the Bright Spots

# Key Takeaways

Domestic RPK growth for the Big 3 decelerated to 0–3% YoY in April 2026, down from 4–12% in 1Q26, which we attribute in part to pricing inflation.   
International RPK growth remained healthy at 11–14% YoY for the Big 3 in April, despite a modest softening from 17–18% in 1Q26.   
Remarkable outperformance from SPA: 18% YoY domestic RPK growth (22% in 1Q26) and international RPK growth of 18% YoY in Apr vs. 9% in 1Q26.   
SPA, CA and CEA saw 5-9ppt PLF hike on int'l routes – a positive surprise. We think China is attracting int'l transit traffic and gaining direct routes share.   
CA continued to outperform in Big 3 in both RPK growth and PLF improvement YoY, while CSA relatively underperformed.

Domestic: Growth softened MoM; CA continued to lead among the Big 3: SPA again delivered the strongest domestic RPK growth among peers at +18.4% YoY, while CA reported +2.7%, outperforming CEA (+0.5%) and CSA (-0.7%). CA has also achieved a 2.2ppt YoY PLF hike, to 86.0%, vs. +1.2ppt for CEA and a 0.9ppt decline for CSA. SPA's PLF continued to trend high with 4.2ppt YoY improvement to 93.0%.

International – resilient demand despite cost headwinds: International RPK grew 17.8% YoY for SPA and +11-14% for the Big 3. We see meaningful YoY PLF improvement for SPA, CA and CEA (+5-9ppt), while CSA's int'l PLF was slightly down by 0.3ppt.

Cargo demand gradually improved post-CNY: International RFTKs for CEA, CSA, and CA were +19%, +2.7%, and +4.2% YoY in Apr-26, respectively.
