EN_A: Okay, hit me with the first one.

EN_A: What kind of conditions?

EN_B: AT&T only promised to cover 75% of the US population in five years. The FCC added a year-ten requirement: AT&T has to cover 75% of every single PEA, which is a small geographic area, on a license-by-license basis. That means they cannot just focus on big cities. EN_A: So 2.4 billion is just a fraction of that.
