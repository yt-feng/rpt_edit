# Cosmetics Sector: Earnings Outlook (Apr-Jun)

## Need strategies to grow core brands, lift margins

Based on April–May data, we think domestic cosmetics shipments likely improved versus the January–March quarter. However, with imports rising, competition in the domestic cosmetics market remains intense. China's April–May data also point to a slowing recovery in the cosmetics sales environment. Against this backdrop, we think that even if April–June results at the cosmetics companies we cover track guidance, they will do little to raise expectations for future growth. We still need to confirm whether companies have strategies to grow their core brands and improve overall margins.

\- Rohto Pharmaceutical (4527, Overweight): We expect April–June sales growth of around 5% YoY on a constant-currency basis. We forecast operating profit growth of ¥800 million YoY to ¥12.5 billion. In Japan, we see a possible pullback from January–March new skincare launches and demand for Hada Labo ahead of price hikes, but expect Asia to drive sales and profit growth. We look to confirm whether acquired firm Eu Yan Sang is contributing to sales and profit growth. External data such as METI and point-of-sale data suggest the company's domestic skincare sales trends in April–May were broadly in line with to slightly above the market average.

\- Shiseido (4911, Neutral): We expect April–June sales to be roughly flat YoY on a constant-currency basis. We forecast core operating profit growth of ¥5.4 billion YoY to ¥20.5 billion. We see rising profits in China and travel retail (TR), plus the elimination of losses in the Americas, as the main drivers of profit growth. Key points to watch include progress on innovative product launches and sell-out trends in China and the US. The external data mentioned above suggest that Shiseido's domestic mid- to low-priced cosmetics sales in April–May were slightly below the market average for both skincare and makeup.

\- Kosé Holdings (4922, Underweight): We expect April–June sales growth of 6% YoY on a constant-currency basis. We expect the effects of upfront marketing investment in January–March to come through. We estimate operating profit of ¥5.6 billion, up ¥900 million YoY. Key points to watch are sales trends at the main brands and domestic cost-structure reforms. Based on external data, we estimate that the company's April–May mid- to low-priced cosmetics sales slightly outperformed the market average for skincare but were in line to slightly below for makeup.

\- Pola Orbis Holdings (4927, Underweight): We estimate April–June sales growth of 1% YoY. We expect sales to fall 3% YoY for POLA but rise 5% for ORBIS.
