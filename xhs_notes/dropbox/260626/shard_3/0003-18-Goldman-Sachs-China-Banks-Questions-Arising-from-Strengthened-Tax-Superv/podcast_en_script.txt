EN_B: The market is really asking two questions. First, will this push up effective tax rates for all Chinese banks? Second, could this tighten interbank liquidity? The report addresses both.

EN_B: Right. And the report expects large banks to keep buying more government bonds. Two reasons. One, they need to deploy credit. Two, government bonds give them tax exemption and capital relief.

EN_B: The model says the four large banks had an average effective tax rate of 13% over the past three years. For the next three years, it is expected to be 12%. This trend is not going to reverse because of one audit.
