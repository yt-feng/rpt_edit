# Reversal in merits of industrial structure: Hardware and physical assets now hold an advantage

Although guidance this results season was conservative, in such years guidance tends to rise in a stepwise fashion as 1Q and 2Q results are released. It is also important to note that the medium-term attractiveness of Japanese equities is increasing. Japanese companies provide semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SPE), components and materials that are essential to the AI supply chain. In addition, supply chains and production facilities are being rebuilt globally to mitigate geopolitical threats, and wealth is concentrated in these areas. Now is the time to reassess Japan's industrial structure, which has strengths in hardware and physical assets that are easy to pass on to prices. Previous catalysts, such as the shift to an inflationary economy and the acceleration of corporate reform, are also alive and well.

# Initial AI rally has passed, but uptrend likely to continue
