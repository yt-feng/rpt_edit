# Chinese Urea Export Window Opens

# Market sources report that China has opened its urea export window. At this

point, first round volume has not been confirmed, but trade sources suggest \~1.9mT, including \~400kT of government to government sales. Prices floors have reportedly been set at \$660/t FOB for prilled urea, \$670/t FOB for granular urea, and \$680/t FOB for prills being sent to India. The reported floor prices sit above most current major benchmark levels.

Current benchmark prices include: (i) Brazil, at a range of \$600–640/t CFR; (ii) US NOLA, at a range of \$545–575/st FOB; (iii) Southeast Asia, at a range of \$675–685/t CFR; (iv) the Baltic Sea, at a range of \$590–620/t FOB; and (v) the most recent Indian tender, which had lowest offers at \$935/t CFR, although letters of intent were issued on April 23 when global prices were higher than they are today.
