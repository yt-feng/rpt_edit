# SCLC: ZG006 reiterated the long-tail survival of DLL3 TCE in late-line setting

SCLC has seen new updates, led by Zelgen's ph1/2 update of alveltamig (ZG006, DLL3/DLL3/CD3 TsTCE) with highlight on the OS benefit in 3L+ setting: 12m OS rate of 65.9% / 59.2% for 10mg / 30mg, implying potential 15-20 months of median OS (vs. 13.6m for tarlatamab in 2L).
