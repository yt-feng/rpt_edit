## Tourism: Still-Resilient Leisure Travel Demand During Dragon Boat Festival

## CITI'S TAKE

Despite rainy weather, China domestic tourism demonstrated a resilient trend during the 3-day Dragon Boat Festival (19-21 Jun), with domestic tourist number/tourism revenue growing by 4.4%/4.0% yoy, respectively, according to the Ministry of Culture and Tourism. This came above our expectations given a slight decline in nationwide passenger throughput per Ministry of Transportation. Chinese outbound travel reverted to positive growth during the holiday while inbound travel continued to trend well. While macro and weather uncertainty may linger, we expect that resilient leisure travel demand, solid inbound travel momentum, and normalized industry supply should continue to underpin hotel RevPAR trends in China, especially on ADR strength. Atour remains our top Buy, with its underappreciated earnings resilience against RevPAR volatility and sustained retail strength.

Domestic tourism — According to the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, the domestic tourist number amounted to 124m person times during the 3-day Dragon Boat Festival, up 4.4% yoy. Domestic tourism revenue grew by 4.0% yoy to Rmb44.456bn, with per-capita spend marginally down 0.4% yoy (Figure 1). The discrepancy vs. the traffic momentum illustrated by the Ministry of Transportation, in Citi's view, could be due to less travel demand for non-tourism purposes given the rainy weather while leisure travel demand stayed resilient.
