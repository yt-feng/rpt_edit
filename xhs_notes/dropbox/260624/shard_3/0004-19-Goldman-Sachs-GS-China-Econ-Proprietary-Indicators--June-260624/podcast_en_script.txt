EN_A: So it's a lopsided recovery. Manufacturing is doing the heavy lifting, but the rest is kind of flat?
EN_B: Exactly. The report breaks down the activity indicator into three buckets: manufacturing, consumption, and others. EN_B: Financial conditions actually tightened slightly in May. The report has a Financial Conditions Index that looks at rates, the currency, the stock market, and credit. But the report also notes that government bond issuance is set to accelerate in the coming months.

EN_B: That is exactly the right question. The report has a metric called the fiscal spend-through ratio, which measures how much of the money raised is actually being deployed. The full report goes into more detail on this, and it is a key variable to watch.

EN_B: The report tracks a property policy relative tightness index across over 100 cities, and that index shows that easing policies are continuing.
