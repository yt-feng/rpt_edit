# Japan Summer School: Japan Equity Strategy: Structural Tailwinds and Investment Themes Behind Japan's Secular Bull Market

## Japan's equity market is entering a structural inflection point.

In this webinar, we will discuss the four key factors underpinning our constructive view on Japanese equities: the establishment of a more stable and sustained inflationary environment, the acceleration of growth-oriented policy initiatives, continued progress in corporate governance reform, and rising household participation in Japanese equities through NISA.

We will examine how these structural developments are supporting the long-term upward trajectory of Japanese equities, while also highlighting the near-term and cyclical themes that investors should watch in assessing the market outlook.

## Sustained, stable inflation

Japan is shifting to a phase where inflation moves toward the 2% target as the output gap gradually narrows. This shift opens a path to sustainable growth, wage gains, more flexible pricing, stronger capex, and higher productivity.

![](images/2842d4d2d41d633d803d05c0b9d1eacaf734bf7c859012817acdb18f3bdea40e.jpg)

## Expectation of long Takaichi tenure / faster growth strategy incl. national security

The LDP led by PM Takaichi won 316 Lower House seats, a landslide with a solo >2/3 supermajority. Via (1) higher policy predictability, (2) faster decision-making, and (3) higher execution certainty of growth strategy, this is likely positive for both mid-term earnings outlook and the risk premium demanded by capital markets.

## Progress in corporate governance reform

After reforms triggered by the TSE market restructuring in Apr 2022, Prime market PBR is up c.40%. In 2026, we expect a CG Code revision (around Jun) is to push better use of corporate cash. Faster reform should lift ROE and support Japan equities via PBR/PER expansion.

## But our bull/bear scenario gap is wide

Our base case remains constructive, but uncertainty is still high.

\- In a bear case where Middle East escalation drives a sharp oil spike and triggers a global recession, forward P/E could fall to 12.0x.

\- In this scenario, Japanese equities likely face both EPS pressure and valuation compression.

\- Conversely, in a bull case where US-led investment/consumption is stronger than expected and AI-driven productivity gains enable both lower inflation and easier financial conditions, fwd P/E of 18.0x is in sight.

## Japanese corporates steadily expanding margins

USD/JPY and Japanese equities show a positive correlation, but the regression beta has trended down over the past decade since Abenomics, suggesting export FX sensitivity has clearly declined.

\- Since experiencing higher import prices post-COVID, Japanese corporates have continued efforts to improve margins.

\- This should make earnings less vulnerable to JPY appreciation than in the past.

\- Therefore, we do not see USD/JPY as the sole driver of JPY-denominated earnings.

## Analyst Stock Ratings

Overweight (O). The stock's total return is expected to exceed the average total return of the analyst's industry (or industry team's) coverage universe, on a risk-adjusted basis, over the next 12-18 months.

Equal-weight (E). The stock's total return is expected to be in line with the average total return of the analyst's industry (or industry team's) coverage universe, on a risk-adjusted basis, over the next 12-18 months.

Not-Rated (NR). Currently the analyst does not have adequate conviction about the stock's total return relative to the average total return of the analyst's industry (or industry team's) coverage universe, on a risk-adjusted basis, over the next 12-18 months. Underweight (U). The stock's total return is expected to be below the average total return of the analyst's industry (or industry team's) coverage universe, on a risk-adjusted basis, over the next 12-18 months. Unless otherwise specified, the time frame for price targets included in MS is 12 to 18 months.
