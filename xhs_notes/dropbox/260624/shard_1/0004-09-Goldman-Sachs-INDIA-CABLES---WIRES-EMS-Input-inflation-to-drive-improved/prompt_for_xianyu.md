## Amber Enterprises

During the quarter, Amber announced a manufacturing collaboration agreement with OPPO Mobile, which marks its entry into smartphone manufacturing and covering 3 brands - Oppo, One Plus and Realme. Amber expects to commence trial production in Q4FY27 and commercial production by Q1FY28, expecting 8mn units in FY28E followed by a 13-15mn ramp up in the 2nd year of operations. It expects to sub-lease existing Oppo facilities in India.

It also expects to begin operations with assembly, following subsequently (in Year 2/3) with increasing value addition through HDI interconnect PCBs, which it aims to manufacture through its JV with Korea Circuit. While we expect initial margins would be lower than its existing businesses, working capital intensity and return ratios are lower and higher respectively. At this juncture, we do not incorporate these into our estimates, and await more clarity on details and timelines.

During the quarter, Amber has increased its stake in Ascent Circuits (from 60%) to 98.5% at a consideration of Rs3.4bn, from its existing shareholders. Mr. Manjunath (erstwhile Promoter of Ascent) has stepped down as MD, and PCB division will now be headed by Mr. Santhosh, (earlier the MD of AT&S India).

This has significantly reduced the minority interest burden for the company and our EPS estimates increase by 11-12% over FY27-28E. As a result our TP increases to Rs7,290 (from Rs6,800) and we remain Neutral.

## Quarter expectations

Railway sub-systems segment should improve as execution pace ramps up. Margins however, in our view, would be lower than Q4 due to delayed pass-through of cost increases in PCB business as well as currency depreciation.
