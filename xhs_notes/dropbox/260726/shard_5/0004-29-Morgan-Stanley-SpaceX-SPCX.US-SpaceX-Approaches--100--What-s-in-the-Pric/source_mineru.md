# SpaceX Approaches \$100: What's in the Price?

From our conversations, many investors expect SpaceX stock could realistically hit \$100/share with the first waves of lockup expiry. So, what would \$100/share mean? 18x EV/EBIT (FY28). Zero to negative implied AI value, with space & connectivity results materially below our ests. Reiterate OW.

Please see here for our SpaceX Initiation: SpaceX: AI's Final Frontier; Initiate at Overweight, PT \$300 (7 Jul 2026)

We believe the current disconnect between increasingly bearish investor sentiment and largely unchanged fundamentals creates an attractive entry point in SpaceX shares. While still early days, the frequency of investor conversations overall has been less intense than we would have expected. Despite no major fundamental changes to the story over the past few weeks, bears appear to have become gradually more convicted, while bulls appear more resigned to wait for more obvious positive catalysts. Most investors we speak with significantly discount Grok & Cursor and view the bulk of the AI monetization opportunity as neocloud. Many ascribe zero or even negative value for AI given the high capex requirements relative to Space & Connectivity, largely uncertain economics, and the high degree of management time devoted to the business.

\- Our view: We acknowledge that shares could face further near-term pressure from impending lockup releases (supply is undefeated?) and an earnings print that may leave many key investor questions unanswered. Nevertheless, we believe SpaceX remains uniquely positioned across launch, connectivity, and AI, with a dominant competitive moat in space launch and satellite connectivity (>3/4 of global mass to orbit and maneuverable satellites in orbit) and meaningful long-term optionality from Grok, Cursor, and neocloud. At less than \$120/share today, we believe investors are paying a reasonable multiple for the core Space + Connectivity business while receiving limited-to-no value for the company's AI opportunity, despite multiple potential monetization paths to our estimated \~\$100bn in 2028 AI revenues on 6 GW avg compute. We see the current valuation as an attractive entry point, with our \$300 price target implying substantial upside and supported by a 15-year DCF that values Space + Connectivity alone at approximately \$136/share.

\- At \$100, SPCX would trade at 16x FY28 PE, more than a 50% discount to WMT (33x FY28 PE) and a 40% discount to TJX (26.5x FY28 PE). WMT is covered by Simeon Gutman. TJX is not covered.

Many expect that \$100/share could be in play in the near term - what would that really mean?

\- SpaceX would trade at 18x EV/EBIT, roughly equivalent to the median of comps (see below for more details) but with 3 times the Revenue CAGR between 2025 and 2030 (76% vs. 26%).

\- 18x EV/EBIT would hypothetically rank #81 within the S&P 500 ex-financials & real estate, between companies such as Boeing (21x), Waste Management (19x), CSX (18x), and Garmin (18x).

\- Separating Space + Connectivity (Launch & Starlink - more predictable) and AI (more uncertain)...

• Assuming 40x EV/EBITDA on the Space + Connectivity business, which is slightly below the median of space comps (46x) for a market leader with a multi year moat, would imply zero value for the AI business.

• Assuming 50x EV/EBITDA on the Space + Connectivity business, which is slightly above the median of space comps, would imply a negative \$300bn value on the AI business.

\- The primary basis for our \$300/share price target is a 15-year DCF that implies 56x EV/EBITDA on Space + Connectivity, or \$136/share. Using that multiple, at \$100/share, the market would be implying zero value for AI and 2028 EBITDA for Space + Connectivity that's \~30% below our estimates.

\- Essentially, investors can receive a discounted Space and Connectivity business with a dominant market share (>3/4 of global maneuverable satellites in orbit and mass launched to orbit) growing nearly 50% this year with 40% EBITDA margins - and all AI optionality (orbital and terrestrial) is near free.

## What are the clearest positive catalysts to watch?

\- Starship Test Flight 14 and 15 (likely September and October), which could be the first catch of a V3 Starship Booster, first orbital Starship flight (and simultaneously launch of operational V3 sats), and the first Starship upper stage catch.

\- Material updates on Cursor ARR and Grok adoption on the platform likely before 3Q end when the Cursor acquisition is expected to close, 3Q earnings at the latest.

\- Additional chunky neocloud deals - very possible with SpaceX expected to double compute to 2 GW by year-end.

\- Grok 4.6 release (2 Tr parameter model) likely within the next month and Grok 5.0 release before year end (6 Tr parameter model).

\- With the market pricing in little to no value for AI, we view most AI-related developments as potential upside catalysts.
