## Fundamentals vs Fund-Flow Dashboard

## CITI'S TAKE

BYD's share price, which historically tracks its fundamentals closely, appeared to have decoupled from underlying fundamentals between April and late June 2026 — driven, in our view, by AI-driven fund flow disruptions, tariff-related sentiment noise, and soft China domestic NEV demand. To objectively measure fundamentals momentum independent of market noise, we have developed a 'BYD Weighted-Fundamental Index', assigning weights of 40%/25%/20%/10%/5% to BYD export MoM movements/orders/domestic market share/sector NEV YoY trend/inventory levels, where a positive reading (>0) signals improving fundamentals and is historically constructive for share price appreciation (Mar-2026 as shown in Figure 1). Despite the index remaining consistently positive throughout April–June 2026, BYD's share price declined — an anomaly we attribute to AI fund flows working against the stock (Figure 2); and the gap between price and fund flow positioning has been visibly narrowing and appears poised to reverse in July, in our view.

Looking ahead, we expect sustained export momentum into 3Q26 and a solid 2Q earnings delivery should meaningfully address 2H26 net profit visibility concerns, supporting a gradual re-rating of BYD's FY27 earnings through the market consolidation and export alpha thesis, while less volatile AI fund flow should lighten the overhang on BYD's valuation, and vice versa.
