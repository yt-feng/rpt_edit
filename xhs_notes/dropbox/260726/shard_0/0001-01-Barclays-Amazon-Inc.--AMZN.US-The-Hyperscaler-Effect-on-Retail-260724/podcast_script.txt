EN_B: In USD, they suggest selling the 2029s, 2032s, 2034s, and a few others. In EUR, they highlight the 2030s, 2032s, and longer-dated bonds. The logic is that as new supply comes at wider spreads, these older, tighter bonds will eventually widen too.
