## Framing further issuance needs and what to look for next week

We estimate that the company could have need for incremental debt of up to \$90bn in 2027 (if GlobalStar is fully debt funded) and \$125bn in 2028. This assumes capex of \$275bn in 2027 and \$360bn in 2028. While it has already issued \$92bn of USD-equivalent debt this year, in line with our prior estimate, there is still scope for it to consider other currencies, as discussed above, or pull forward some of its 2027 needs. In addition, the company has a \$17.5bn delayed draw TL that could be fully utilized by September; in our view, this would serve as a backstop in the event it chooses to delay issuance until 2027. As such, we estimate that there is room for \$25-35bn of further debt to be added to the balance sheet by YE26 (which assumes the potential for \~\$10bn more issuance outside the USD market).

\- Given year-to-date issuance, we think that there is scope for the company to raise its capex outlook for 2026 (from \$200bn currently), with Google having recently raised its outlook by \$15bn, or suggest a more tempered CFO performance. Our sense is that neither, however, is part of market assumptions.

## Valuation

We see fair value for the credit as 10-15bp behind Google and expect valuation to remain anchored to other hyperscalers, which will cause the gap to higher-quality retailers such as Walmart and Home Depot to remain elevated. At this point, we have yet to see spillover effects from Amazon to other retail names in terms of rel val; this will likely remain intact as investors continue to seek diversification away from hyperscalers amid growing capex and issuance needs.

## Trade ideas

\- In USD, we recommend selling some of the tighter trading bonds on the curve that are from older vintages and have held in as newer supply has come at wider levels. We should see the relationships narrow over time, pointing to room for these bonds to underperform.

\- Sell AMZN 4.65s of 2029, 4.7s of 2032, 4.8s of 2034, 4.95s of 2044, and 3.95s of 2052

\- Given scope for potential EUR issuance from Amazon and a fairly flat relationship to Google, we recommend selling select EUR bonds. As issuance from Amazon continues to build the credit's presence in the EUR market, we expect the relationship to Google to widen from one that is fairly flat currently to one that is closer to 10-15bp apart (in line with our fair value estimate).

\- Sell AMZN 3.1s of 2030, 3.35s of 2032, 4.05s of 2039, and 4.85s of 2064.

\- Buy AMZN 5y CDS. While AMZN 5y CDS has consistently widened relative to CDX.IG (both nominally and as a ratio), we expect the consistent room for issuance, coupled with the potential to move into private credit, to lead this pressure to persist.
