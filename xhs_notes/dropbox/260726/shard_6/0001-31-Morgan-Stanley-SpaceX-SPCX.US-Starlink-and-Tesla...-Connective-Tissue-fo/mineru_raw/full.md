# Starlink and Tesla... Connective Tissue for Robot Swarm

Tesla Cybercab + Starlink integration is another sign of 'mission' overlap between robotics and space.

**What Happened?** On the 2Q26 earnings call, Elon Musk confirmed that Starlink is being integrated into the Cybercab and will eventually roll out across all Tesla vehicles in markets where Starlink is active, framing it as essential to robotaxi operations given the persistent gaps in cellular coverage that could otherwise leave autonomous vehicles stranded in connectivity "dead zones."

## Significance to SpaceX:

- Robots impact SpaceX in 2 main ways: (1) accounts for the single largest cohort of Starlink ‘clients’ in long-term forecasts; and (2) represents potentially the largest and most valuable end market within the Enterprise AI business.
- By integrating Starlink, Cybercab gains a redundant, high-bandwidth connectivity solution that improves network availability and enables a consistent broadband experience for passengers.
- Robots as a Service (RaaS) may represent a significant long-term addressable market by 2050 (original equipment with downstream adjacent services).
- The robotaxi market is expected to be an obvious and early adopter for mobile satellite connectivity. Even if primarily served by terrestrial 5G, there is a need for redundancy and resiliency in communications for critical network infrastructure including transport networks.
- Estimates suggest a peak Starlink penetration on autonomous vehicles of 40% in 2040, or 83 million connected cars. Starlink revenues from autonomous vehicles are projected to reach meaningful levels by 2035 and 2040.
- Beyond robotaxis, Starlink forecasts include multiple other robotic client categories including large and small drones/eVTOLs, industrial robots, humanoids, etc.
