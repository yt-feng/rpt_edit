Beyond that, exporters' willingness to sell dollars also rebounded amid last month's USD/CNH rally. The gross FX settlement ratio rose to 65.3%, while the gross FX sales ratio remained near a multi-year low at 60.1%. As a result, the net FX settlement ratio, a key gauge of exporters' net dollar-selling interest, widened to 5.2%ppt (Figure 11),

marking the second-highest June reading on record. This should offer reassurance to CNY bulls. Persistent exporter dollar selling has remained an important anchor on USD/CNH, while the gradual lowering of the daily fixing continues to signal policymakers' comfort with a stronger currency. The consistent heaviness of USD/CNH during Asia hours corroborates robust exporter conversion flows (Figure 12), which have largely offset the more constructive USD tone seen during non-Asia trading hours.
