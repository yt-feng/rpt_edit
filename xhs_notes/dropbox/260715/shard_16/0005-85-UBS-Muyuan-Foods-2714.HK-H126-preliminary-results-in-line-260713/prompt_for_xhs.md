## Company Description

Muyuan Foods (Muyuan) is the largest hog producer in China, with value-chain coverage comprising feed processing, hog breeding, sow propagation, market hog finishing and slaughtering. Its main products include hogs, sows and piglets. Muyuan's hog output reached 71.6m heads in 2024.

## Valuation Method and Risk Statement

We derive our price target for Muyuan Foods based on a DCF model.

Risks for Muyuan Foods include: 1) limited production cost savings due to rising feed ingredient cost; 2) slower-than-expected hog destocking leads to continued sluggish hog prices, lowering Muyuan's profitability; 3) lower-than-expected hog sales volume growth; and 4) slower-than-expected development of its downstream slaughtering business.
