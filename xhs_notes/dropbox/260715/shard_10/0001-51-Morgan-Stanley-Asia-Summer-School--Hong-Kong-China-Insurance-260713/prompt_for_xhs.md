1. Industry Growth Potential

## 1.1 Solid household wealth growth; insurance could outperform

\- China's household financial assets reached Rmb320tn as of 1Q26 and have delivered double-digit growth over the past decade, despite Covid-related disruptions and a weaker macro backdrop.

\- Insurance products remain the only principal-guaranteed financial products in China that also offer reasonable yield and value-added services. As a result, insurance has continued to gain share within household financial assets in recent years.

\- We expect this trend to persist, with household financial assets growing at an 8% CAGR over 2024-30E to reach Rmb435tn by 2030E, versus Rmb310tn in 2025. We believe insurance products are well positioned to capture a growing share of incremental household savings.
