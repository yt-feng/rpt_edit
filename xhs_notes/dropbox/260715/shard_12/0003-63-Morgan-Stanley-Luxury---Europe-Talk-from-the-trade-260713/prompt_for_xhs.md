Bottega Veneta (=/+) Feedback suggests Bottega Veneta remains a healthy brand, although momentum has cooled from the highs achieved under Matthieu Blazy. Experts generally agreed that classic leather goods continue to perform well and that the brand's positioning as a high-quality leather house remains intact. One expert described growth in the low-single digits, while another cited high-single-digit to near-double-digit growth and improving desirability, crediting Louise Trotter's ready-to-wear as executing "quiet luxury really well". However, several noted that newer collections have yet to generate the same level of excitement as under the previous creative direction. Overall, Bottega Veneta appears to be transitioning from an exceptional momentum story to a steadier growth profile, with brand heat remaining broadly supportive.

Brunello Cucinelli (+) Brunello Cucinelli continues to stand out as one of the strongest brands in luxury. Experts consistently described double-digit growth, strong demand from affluent customers and sustained success in balancing quiet luxury credibility with increasing modernity. Several viewed Brunello, alongside Zegna and Loro Piana, as part of menswear's "gold triangle", noting it remains a favored brand among wealthy clientele. Burberry (+) Burberry was one of the few brands where feedback was consistently positive across all experts, pointing to a business that is gradually rebuilding both desirability and commercial momentum, with the brand moving in the right direction and in a meaningfully better place than a year ago. Several respondents attributed this improvement to management's decision to reconnect the brand with its heritage, with one expert describing the return to "roots and authenticity" as a "great bet" that is helping rebuild relevance with consumers. Another noted that Burberry's perception has improved materially and that the brand is becoming "more organic" in how it communicates with customers, contrasting this with the more forced repositioning attempts seen elsewhere in the sector. Another pointed to Burberry's disciplined approach to promotions, suggesting management is prioritising brand equity and full-price sales. Looking ahead, channel checks indicate growing optimism around upcoming product launches and the brand's Christmas programme, with several experts expecting momentum to continue building over the next year. While Burberry is not yet being discussed alongside the sector's strongest performers, the consensus view is that it has successfully stabilised the business, rebuilt credibility with consumers and is now entering the next stage of recovery, where improving desirability is increasingly feeding through into sales growth.
