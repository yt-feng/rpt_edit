## Company profile

Shengyi Electronics is a global PCB manufacturer and is the subsidiary of Shengyi Technology. The company produces high-end multi-layer PCBs for sectors such as telecommunication, server, auto electronics.

## Valuation Methodology

Our TP of CNY159.00 is based on 35x 2027F EPS of CNY4.55, in line with company's historical median P/E, and the FY26-28F earnings CAGR of $37\%$ . The benchmark index is CSI 300.

Risks that may impede the achievement of the target price

Downside risks include: 1) lower-than-expected PCB demand in downstream sectors such as 5G, servers, auto electronics; 2) increasingly fierce market competition on high-end PCBs leading to margin pressure and 3) higher-than-expected raw material cost pressure.

## ESG

Shengyi Electronics shares its social responsibility by offering electronic components for telecom infrastructures. While the production of PCB products may have impacts on the environment.

## Earnings forecast revisions

We raise our FY26-28F revenue forecasts by 7.0-15.0% to reflect the technology upgrade from ASIC customers and capacity expansion. We slightly lower FY26-28F GPM by 0.1-0.8pp to reflect the cost pressure from upstream materials' price hike. As a result, our FY26-28F earnings forecast increase by 6.5-11.1%.
