## Company profile

Shengyi Technology is the second-largest CCL (copper clad laminate) manufacturer globally. The company produces and ships CCL and prepreg products to PCB makers. Shengyi Technology also has PCB manufacturing business through its subsidiary Shengyi Electronics.

## Valuation Methodology

Our TP of CNY195.00 is based on 43x 2027F EPS of CNY4.51, in line with FY26-28F earnings CAGR of $43\%$ . The benchmark index is CSI 300.

Risks that may impede the achievement of the target price

Downside risks include: 1) lower-than-expected PCB demand in downstream sectors such as 5G, servers, auto electronics; and 2) more intensified market competition in the CCL market leading to margin pressures.

## ESG

Shengyi Technology shares its social responsibility by offering electronic components for telecom infrastructures. While the production of CCL and PCB products may have impacts on the environment.

## Earnings forecast revisions

We raise our FY26-28F revenue forecasts by 24.5-50.1%, thanks to stronger CCL segment performance driven by price hike and higher AI CCL contribution, as well as AI PCB customer's technology upgrade. Thanks to better product mix and price hike, our GPM forecasts increase by 0.6-1.4pp for FY26-27F. As a result, our earnings forecasts rise by 38.7-55.2% for FY26-28F.
