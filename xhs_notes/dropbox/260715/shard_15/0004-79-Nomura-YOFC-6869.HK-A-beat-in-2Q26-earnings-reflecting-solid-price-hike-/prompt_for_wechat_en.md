# A beat in 2Q26 earnings reflecting solid price hike Quick Note

## What happened?

YOFC reported a positive profit alert on 14 July after market close, with 1H26E earnings guided at CNY2.4bn-3bn (+711%-914% y-y). The 1H26E earnings guidance translates into 2Q26E earnings of CNY1.9bn-2.5bn (+1222%-1639% y-y), and the mid-point of CNY2.2bn strongly beat our estimates of CNY1bn for 2Q26F.

## NOM's view

We think the meaningful earnings uptick likely reflects the full-swing price hike for optical fiber products in both telecom and datacom markets in 2Q26, driven by the surging demand and constrained supply from major optical fiber / preform makers. We note a significant sell-off in the YOFC stock recently (down $46\%$ vs. HSlindex up $2\%$ in the past three weeks) due to concerns about the accelerated capacity expansion and more intensified competition from potentially new players. However, we think the strong profit alert would improve the market sentiment, and create short-term trading opportunities for the stock.

We maintain our Buy rating and TP of HKD266.00, which is based on 23.7x FY27F EPS of CNY9.75. The stock currently trades at 13.7x FY27F EPS.
