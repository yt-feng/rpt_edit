Franchisee Temperature Check: Expert Call Takeaways

We hosted an expert call on 27 July to assess the latest competitive dynamics. The speaker operates 49 Luckin Coffee stores, three Chagee stores, and two Mixue stores across Hebei, Henan, Shaanxi, Inner Mongolia, and Anhui. He noted that headwinds from the unwinding of third-party delivery subsidies and intensified competition are weighing on store-level execution. ## Luckin: leading position intact; ASP and gross margin recovering

\- SSS remains under pressure, while ASP is rebounding. The expert indicated that most of his 49 Luckin stores recorded YTD GMV declines of 10–15% YoY, driven primarily by softer demand and intensifying competition from both new Luckin stores and peers. Cup volume has declined, but ASP has improved due to (1) a reduction in RMB 9.9 promotions, (2) higher pricing on delivery platforms, and (3) increased premiumization via new product launches. Delivery mix has largely stabilized at \~50% (vs. as high as \~80% during the subsidy war last year). Further normalization may be limited given increasingly entrenched consumer delivery habits.

\- Store-level gross margin has improved; higher labor costs limit net margin expansion. Store-level gross margin has recovered to \~35% in 2Q26 (vs. a wide range of \~28–45% during last year's delivery war). However, higher staffing needs, rising social insurance costs, greater product complexity, and higher wastage are offsetting much of the benefit. The expert estimates incremental labor costs at \~RMB 4–5k per store per month (i.e., RMB 60–70k annually), implying a potential \~1–2ppt drag on net margin. He believes the payback period for a neighborhood store with \~300 cups/day is now \~3 years or longer.

\- Non-coffee beverages are becoming a meaningful growth lever, but raise operational complexity. Non-coffee products accounted for \~30% of total GMV YTD and could rise to 40–50% longer term as Luckin's network densifies and penetration increases in lower-tier cities. Franchisees are less enthusiastic given heavier fresh-fruit usage and higher labor intensity, which increases operational complexity.

\- Premiumization supports ASP, but SKU proliferation creates trade-offs. Luckin offers more customization options and premium coffee bean selections, enabling price-insensitive consumers to trade up and lift average ticket size. However, a broader SKU portfolio also increases inventory losses, wastage, and execution pressure at the store level.

\- Competitive lead remains intact vs. other coffee and milk tea brands. The expert attributes Luckin's advantage over Cotti primarily to a more professionalized franchise development and management system. While more milk tea brands are entering the freshly made coffee market, he views Luckin's continued product iteration and brand strengthening as key differentiators.
