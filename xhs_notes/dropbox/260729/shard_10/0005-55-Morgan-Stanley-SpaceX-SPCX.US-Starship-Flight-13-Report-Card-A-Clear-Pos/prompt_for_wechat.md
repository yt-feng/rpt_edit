First lockup release after August 4 earnings is still an overhang which likely keeps many would-be longs on the sidelines.

Overall stock implications: Positive. Next milestone... catching ship. If they can do that, bears may need to consider cryogenic hibernation.

## □ Catalysts to Watch

Flight 14 (targeted \~1 month out, late Aug / early Sept): shortly after Ship 40's splashdown, Musk posted that unless data review turns up problems, SpaceX will attempt to catch the Ship with the tower, the first-ever Ship catch on Mechazilla's arms at Starbase.

Source: MS

> implies Starship's first orbital flight: a tower Ship catch requires an orbital trajectory, so Flight 14 would likely be the first orbital attempt. After 13 flights and three years, SpaceX has yet to complete a full Earth orbit with Starship.

Likely no V3 booster catch yet: given Flight 13's hard booster splashdown, SpaceX will want a clean soft splashdown of the new V3 booster first, so Flight 14 should fly the booster to the water again.

> Potential first operational Starlink V3s: Flight 13's 20 V3s were a suborbital demo that demised \~20 min after deployment; an orbital Flight 14 could deploy V3s into a working orbit for the first time.

Flight 15 (roughly a month later): if Flight 14 lands a Ship catch and a soft booster splashdown, Flight 15 could be the first to attempt a Ship catch and a V3 booster catch on the same mission.

Flight 14/15 profiles are not yet officially confirmed by SpaceX; timing and catch sequencing are our estimates.
