## Company description

China jewelry retailer Laopu Gold has carved out a unique high-end product niche in “heritage gold,” combining modern designs and classic Chinese motifs, featuring at least two Chinese traditional handmade gold crafting techniques, often embedding diamonds and/or gemstones, with products sold in elegant boutiques styled like a traditional Chinese scholar’s study. Laopu’s key strengths include high ticket size (>Rmb50k on average), leading per-store sales (>Rmb500m), unique fixed-price model, superior profitability, and exquisite craftsmanship and product innovation in its “heritage gold” niche.

We rate Laopu Gold shares as Buy. As price-sensitive customers are leaving, we believe Laopu now has a higher proportion of loyal customers who care less about the price premium. Thus, we expect future sales to have lower correlation to gold prices and further downside risk from gold price decline to be limited, which should improve earnings predictability.
