## Key Takeaways

China power demand growth was $5.3\%$ YoY in 1H26, vs $5.7\%$ in 5M26 and $5.4\%$ in 4M26. Monthly demand growth slowed to $3.7\%$ YoY, vs $6.9\%$ in May.
