EN_B: It’s not a total surprise, but the details are stricter than many expected. And it’s retroactive — income from 2023 needs to be declared within 90 days.

EN_A: So is this specifically targeting Hong Kong real estate?

EN_B: Not really. It applies to all offshore assets — Singapore properties, stocks, you name it. So Hong Kong doesn’t become less attractive relative to other offshore assets. But it does narrow the gap between buying a home in mainland China versus Hong Kong. In China, if you hold a property for over five years and it’s your only home, the capital gains tax is waived. In Hong Kong, that exemption doesn’t exist.

Mainland-surname buyers account for about 30% of transactions by value, but many of them live in Hong Kong and may not be considered tax residents.
