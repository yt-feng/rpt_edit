EN_A: China just dropped a new tax rule on offshore trusts. Is this a game changer for Hong Kong property? Let’s break it down.
EN_B: It’s not a total surprise, but the details are stricter than many expected. Starting July 24, if you’re a Chinese tax resident and you put assets into an offshore trust, any gains or income from that trust are now taxed at 20%. And it’s retroactive — income from 2023 needs to be declared within 90 days.
EN_A: So is this specifically targeting Hong Kong real estate?
EN_B: Not really. It applies to all offshore assets — Singapore properties, stocks, you name it. So Hong Kong doesn’t become less attractive relative to other offshore assets. But it does narrow the gap between buying a home in mainland China versus Hong Kong. In China, if you hold a property for over five years and it’s your only home, the capital gains tax is waived. In Hong Kong, that exemption doesn’t exist.
EN_A: So what’s the actual impact on Hong Kong home sales?
EN_B: We estimate less than 2% of Hong Kong residential transactions are made through mainland-controlled offshore trusts. So the direct impact is manageable. But there are two bigger risks. First, if the tax authorities start targeting all individual mainland tax residents, not just trust structures, the impact could be much larger. Mainland-surname buyers account for about 30% of transactions by value, but many of them live in Hong Kong and may not be considered tax residents.
EN_A: And the second risk?
EN_B: This could slow down big-ticket commercial property deals, like entire office buildings. That would slow down property companies’ capital recycling. But leasing activity should be largely unaffected.
EN_A: Sounds like there’s still a lot of uncertainty around enforcement.
EN_B: Exactly. The biggest open question is how “mainland tax resident” is defined. If it’s based on your hukou, then many Hong Kong residents could be affected. But real estate isn’t covered by the common reporting standard yet, so enforcement is tricky. The full report dives deeper into these scenarios.
