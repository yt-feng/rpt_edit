RIO reported 1H26 underlying EBITDA/NPAT of US\$14.8bn/US\$6.9bn (+28%/+43% YoY) vs. GSe US\$14.9bn/US\$6.2bn and Visible Alpha Consensus Data at US\$15.1bn/US\$6.6bn.
