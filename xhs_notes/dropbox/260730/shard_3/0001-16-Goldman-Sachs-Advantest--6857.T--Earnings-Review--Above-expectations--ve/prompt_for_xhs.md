## Key points from the results briefing

The company noted that while CPU test times are on par with or slightly longer than for GPUs/ASICs in wafer testing, they are somewhat shorter in package testing.

(2) Market share: In SoC testers, Advantest's share is on an uptrend from $66\%$ in CY25. The gap versus other companies is widening, especially in the AI/HPC space where Advantest has a high share, and the company is looking to capture a market share of at least $70\%$ in SoC testers overall in the medium term. In memory testers, market share could decline slightly in CY26 (from $61\%$ in CY25) because of recovery in the NAND segment, where the company's presence is relatively weak, but management plans to leverage new product launches to expand its share going forward.
