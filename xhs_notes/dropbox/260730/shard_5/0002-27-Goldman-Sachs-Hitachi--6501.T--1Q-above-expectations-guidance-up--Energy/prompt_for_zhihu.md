## Key points from earnings

Energy: sales ¥911.9 bn, adjusted EBITA ¥129.0 bn (GSe ¥734.3 bn/¥102.8 bn). Sales grew in all regions, led by Europe and the US. On the profit front, contributions came from higher volumes as well as productivity improvements and a better sales mix. Orders were up 94% yoy. Hitachi looks for profit margins to continue improving, driven by: (1) better margins on the order backlog; (2) progress with AI utilization in addition to the full-scale contribution of the ERP system introduced in FY3/25; and (3) improved project management (e.g., minimizing the construction [C] portion of EPC contracts as much as possible).

FY3/27 guidance for Energy sales/adjusted EBITA was raised to ¥4,060 bn/¥576 bn from ¥3,700 bn/¥500 bn.

800V DC rack architecture: Hitachi is providing support for technical studies. Industry forecasts suggest adoption will begin around FY3/29.
