## Key points from earnings

Energy: sales ¥911.9 bn, adjusted EBITA ¥129.0 bn (GSe ¥734.3 bn/¥102.8 bn). Sales grew in all regions, led by Europe and the US. On the profit front, contributions came from higher volumes as well as productivity improvements and a better sales mix. Orders were up 94% yoy. Hitachi Energy's order backlog was ¥10.3 tn (+12% from end-FY3/26) / US\$63.6 bn (+10% from end-FY3/26). Orders for the energy segment as a whole, including nuclear, were up 87% yoy. While large projects contributed to orders, base orders (for equipment such as transformers and switchgear) were also strong.

Hitachi looks for profit margins to continue improving, driven by: (1) better margins on the order backlog; (2) progress with AI utilization in addition to the full-scale contribution of the ERP system introduced in FY3/25; and (3) improved project management (e.g., minimizing the construction [C] portion of EPC contracts as much as possible).
