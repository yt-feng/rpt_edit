# Air Liquide 1H26 - A Temporary Adjustment, but Long-Term View Remains Positive

We identified the potential for Air Liquide's 1H26 results to present a temporary adjustment, and the market has responded to a complex report with limited impact on longer-term fundamentals, in our view. After a modest expectations reset, we believe the trajectory improves from here. The backlog remains at record levels, we remain optimistic on further growth acceleration, and the 5 October CMD remains a catalyst. We maintain our positive view, and see 1H26 as a potential entry point.

We raise our comparable growth forecasts for 2H26 by 40 bps, upgrading expectations in Electronics and Industrial Merchant. Although we maintain our Large Industries conservatism, we follow management guidance of sustainable 8-9% growth in Electronics and forecast a sequential 50bps improvement in Industrial Merchant based on easing helium supply constraints and stable quarterly pricing. Overall, we forecast FY26 comparable growth of +3.2% in line with various consensus sources (FY26 estimates of +3.0% for Vara, and +3.4% for VA). This implies 2H growth is sequentially 90bps above 1H, but only +20bps vs. 2Q, and we believe this is consistent with the conservative Air Liquide definition of "slightly higher than the first half," particularly in the context of their previous 1Q=2Q guide. We continue to forecast higher sequential margin improvement in 2H26, at +120bps year-over-year, also based on the easing helium headwind.

There may be a helium overhang for a few more quarters, however. Our understanding is that pre-Iran Air Liquide was overweight Qatari helium, implying they sourced over 1/3 of their product from Qatar, and this is higher than peers. Although production has restarted, which can then be trucked to other ports by road, helium volumes are not likely to reach prior levels until Qatar can freely export LNG through the Strait of Hormuz. We see Air Liquide's helium headwind as temporary, but normalcy may be some quarters away and until then helium is likely to dampen the margin improvement story. And we see Air Liquide's helium impact as being greater than their US peers, hence our current preference for Linde and Air Products.

## Key Observations

We rate Air Liquide positively with a raised target of €202. We raise our 26 & 27 EPS estimates by approximately +3% and +5%, respectively. We roll our valuation framework from a FY26 P/E to a NTM P/E incorporating the average of our FY26 and FY27 EPS. On a multiple of 28x NTM P/E (unchanged), our price target is raised to €202 from €189.

## Valuation Methodology

### Air Liquide SA

We value the gas majors on prospective P/E and regard approximately 28x NTM as appropriate for Air Liquide. This is at a premium to the top end of AL's historical range reflecting the re-rating of the gas majors as the industry has consolidated and seen improved discipline, alongside the attractions of their resilient mix (with a high proportion of protected income) but also long-term growth potential. It also represents a modest discount to its closest peer, Linde plc given its stronger earnings potential and discipline. Hence, our target of €202.

## Risks

### Air Liquide SA

Downside: With a new CEO since June 2022 communicating a strong focus on performance any setbacks will likely have an exaggerated impact on the share price. Given its high exposure to North America IM sales (approximately 70% of regional sales) a deepening recession scenario would likely weigh heavily on the share price; If IM pricing falters; If the project backlog falters again, especially if uncertainties in the clean hydrogen story see a lack of new signatures here.
