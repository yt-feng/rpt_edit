On the risk of project delays (i.e. Project Jupiter in NM), the guidance raise should lower concerns around near-term financial impacts and the company offered multiple assurances (portfolios of other projects to deploy fuel cells, contractual obligations from customer/financing provider, diversification of customer backlog). On capacity expansions, no firm guidance as expected but continue to emphasize that manufacturing capacity will not be a constraint in delivering against the backlog and growth outlook. We think overall will be well received as incremental reassurance on these issues.

Strong 2Q beat, surprise 2026 guidance raise. ## OVERWEIGHT THESIS

We see BE as a key beneficiary of several key themes including: (i) time-to-power for large load customers, (ii) the growing "economic wedge" or value proposition of distributed energy (i.e., fuel cells for C&I customers), (iii) rising grid instability, and (iv) 30% fuel cell tax credit through the 2030s. In the near-term we see a strong growth outlook for its core fuel cell business, particularly for AI power demand, with longer-term tailwinds arising from the OBBB and its hydrogen electrolyzer business, supporting a robust multi-year growth and margin improvement outlook.
