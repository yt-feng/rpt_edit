ZH_A: 最近我看到一份关于中国汽车行业的报告，标题挺直接的，叫“还在寻底”。这到底是什么意思？市场到底有多冷？

ZH_B: 确实很冷。6月份国内零售销量同比下滑了超过21%，这个数字很能说明问题。报告认为，除非有新的政策刺激，否则在年底之前，我们可能看不到有意义的复苏。

ZH_B: 对，6月份电动车渗透率达到了59.2%。但内部结构变化很大。纯电动车的需求在增长，而插电混动车的需求却在急剧下滑，下降了将近29%。

ZH_A: 这很有意思。大家都在说混动是过渡方案，难道这个过渡期已经结束了？

ZH_B: 报告给出了几个理由。第一，新一代纯电动车的续航和快充能力提升很大，基本解决了里程焦虑。第二，纯电动车的价格已经和插电混动车差不多了。第三，政策风向变了，很多插电混动车不再享受购置税优惠，从2027年起还要交车船税。这些因素叠加，让插电混动的性价比大大降低。

ZH_A: 所以纯电动的时代真的来了。那在品牌层面，谁在领跑？

ZH_A: 那传统燃油车和豪华品牌呢？它们是不是最受伤的？

EN_B: It’s not that demand vanished. It’s that a huge chunk of it was pulled forward. The report estimates that the subsidy programs over the last two years shifted more than 5 million units of demand into the past. Now those subsidies are gone, and the market is paying the price.

EN_A: So the market is basically hungover from a stimulus party. But if domestic sales are this weak, where are all these cars going?

EN_A: Exports are the lifeline. What about the EV market itself? Penetration is still climbing, right?

EN_B: It hit 59.2 percent in June. But the interesting story is inside that number. Pure battery EVs are growing, but plug-in hybrids are crashing — down almost 29 percent.

EN_A: That’s a big shift. Everyone said hybrids were a bridge. Is the bridge already burning?

EN_B: The report gives a few reasons. First, new BEVs have much better range and faster charging. Range anxiety is fading. Second, BEVs are now priced similarly to PHEVs. Third, policy is turning against PHEVs. Many models no longer qualify for tax breaks, and starting in 2027 they’ll lose their exemption on the annual vehicle tax. That adds up over the life of the car.

EN_A: So pure EVs are winning. Who’s leading the pack?

EN_B: BYD is still the king. They sold 225,000 units in June with a stable 22 percent market share. Their new fast-charging models are doing really well. The report expects deliveries to accelerate in the third quarter as battery supply constraints ease. Geely and Leapmotor are in second and third.

EN_B: They’re getting crushed. ICE demand fell almost 34 percent. Among premium brands, BMW was down 30.6 percent, Audi down 30 percent, and Porsche down a shocking 45.8 percent. It’s a combination of the pull-forward effect and a tough comparison base.
