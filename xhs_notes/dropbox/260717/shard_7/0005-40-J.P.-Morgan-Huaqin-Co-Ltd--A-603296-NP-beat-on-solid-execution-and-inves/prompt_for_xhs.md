# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

Huaqin Co Ltd - A (Overweight; Price Target: Rmb112.00)

## Investment Thesis

Huaqin is an original design manufacturer (ODM) with diversified exposure to datacenter, smartphones, NBs, pads, wearables, AIOT and auto products. We estimate potential shrinking volumes in smartphones given downstream demand weakness, but we believe earnings will be well supported by strong momentum in the datacenter, wearables and PC segments this year. Meanwhile, we expect auto electronics and robots to bring extra revenue and support long-term growth. We favour Huaqin for its sustainable growth outlook from a diversified business platform, and project a 32% earnings CAGR from 2025-27E. Maintain OW.

## Valuation

Our Jun-27 PT of Rmb112 is based on a 21x 12-month rolling forward P/E, on par with peers' average, based on JPM and Bloomberg Finance L.P. estimates.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Downside risks to our rating and price target include: (1) competition among ODMs that hurts growth/margins; (2) unexpected suspension or constraints on GPU supply; and (3) a share price correction after the lock-up period expires.
