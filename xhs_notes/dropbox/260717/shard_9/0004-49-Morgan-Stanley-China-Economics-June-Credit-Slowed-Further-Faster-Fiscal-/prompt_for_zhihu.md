# June Credit Slowed Further; Faster Fiscal Deployment Awaited

## Key Takeaways

Broad credit growth slowed to 7.5% YoY (7.8% in May; MSe and Consensus: 7.6%), reflecting continued private sector deleveraging and slow public financing.

This echoes continued weakness in domestic investment, consumption, and softened housing sales in June.
