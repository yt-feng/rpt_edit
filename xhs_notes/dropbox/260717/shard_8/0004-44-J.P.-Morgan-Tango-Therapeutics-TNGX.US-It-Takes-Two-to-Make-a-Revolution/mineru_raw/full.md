# Tango Therapeutics: A Research Note on PRMT5 Inhibitor Development

## Company Overview

Tango Therapeutics (TNGX) is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing PRMT5 inhibitors for the treatment of MTAP-deleted tumors, particularly in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) and non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The company's lead asset, vopimetostat, has shown promising data both as a monotherapy and in combination with RAS inhibitors. Additionally, TNG456, a CNS-penetrating PRMT5 inhibitor, is being evaluated for glioblastoma.

## Key Research Observations

### RAS Inhibitor Combinations in PDAC

The combination of vopimetostat with RAS inhibitors represents a notable development in PDAC treatment. Data from the ongoing trial combining vopimetostat with daraxonrasib showed a 92% objective response rate (ORR) in 2L+ PDAC patients, with a 6-month progression-free survival (PFS) rate of 90%. This compares favorably to historical data for daraxonrasib monotherapy, which showed approximately 30-35% ORR in similar patient populations.

The company is planning a Phase 3 trial in 1L PDAC, pending regulatory feedback. Tango is also collaborating with multiple partners in the RAS inhibitor space, including Revolution Medicines and Erasca, to diversify its combination approach.

### Next-Generation PRMT5 Inhibitors

Vopimetostat represents a second-generation MTA-cooperative PRMT5 inhibitor, designed to selectively target MTAP-deleted cancer cells while sparing healthy tissue. This selectivity is achieved through a mechanism that exploits the accumulation of MTA in MTAP-deleted cells, creating a synthetic lethality approach.

The company has streamlined its pipeline to focus exclusively on PRMT5 inhibitors, deprioritizing earlier assets targeting other pathways. This strategic focus allows for concentrated development efforts on the most promising opportunities.

### CNS-Penetrant TNG456 for Glioblastoma

TNG456, a brain-penetrant PRMT5 inhibitor, is currently in Phase 1/2 trials for glioblastoma, where approximately 45% of cases have MTAP deletion. Preclinical data has shown 55-fold selectivity for MTAP-deleted cells and favorable brain penetration characteristics. Monotherapy data is expected in the second half of 2026, with a potential combination study with abemaciclib (a CDK4/6 inhibitor) to follow.

## Competitive Landscape

The PRMT5 inhibitor space includes several competitors, with Bristol-Myers Squibb's navlimetostat being the most advanced in Phase 2/3 trials. While monotherapy data appears comparable between vopimetostat and navlimetostat, Tango currently holds an advantage in RAS inhibitor combination data. However, competitive dynamics remain fluid as other companies also pursue combination strategies.

## Financial Position

As of the most recent reporting period, Tango had approximately $947 million in cash and cash equivalents, providing runway for ongoing development activities. The company's valuation is based on a discounted cash flow sum-of-the-parts model, with primary value attributed to vopimetostat in 1L PDAC.

## Key Considerations

- The combination data with RAS inhibitors is early-stage and requires confirmation in larger trials
- Competition from larger pharmaceutical companies with more resources remains a factor
- Clinical and regulatory risks are inherent to biotechnology development at this stage
- Additional capital may be needed to fund multiple development programs
