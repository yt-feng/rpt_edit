# Investment Thesis

## RAS Inhibitor Combinations Have the Potential to Continue the PDAC Treatment Revolution

While Tango's vopimetostat has shown promise as a monotherapy for PDAC, and Tango planned on initiating a Ph 3, 2L PDAC trial, things in the treatment landscape have changed with RVMD's daraxonrasib impressive showing with imminent approval expected. However, Tango has been able to take advantage of this with their ongoing daraxonrasib combo trial as well as RVMD's G12D KRASi zoldonrasib. Data presented last month was our first look at what is possible when daraxonrasib is combined with a non-overlapping and proven MoA such as PRMT5 inhibition in MTAP-deleted tumors.

The first look at 2L+ PDAC data for the vopimetostat + dara combination has shown a 92% ORR with all but one patient still on drug and a 6-month PFS rate of 90%, a number that is so far ahead of what we’ve seen in this difficult indication that Tango now plans on starting a pivotal trial in this setting ASAP. As a reference, daraxonrasib monotherapy in 2L PDAC has shown an ORR of 30%-35%, which is also considered extremely high compared to the meager historical response rates seen in PDAC with SoC chemotherapy.

Competition in the monotherapy setting for MTAP-deleted tumors is tough, however, Tango has an opportunity to differentiate itself as the first to show such strong combination data. We expect competitor BMY to come out with data in this combo setting as well as it also has a collaboration with RVMD. However, Tango is also hedging its bets in this setting with a recently announced collaboration with ERAS.

## Vopimetostat is Part of a Next Generation of PRMT5 Inhibitors Following Many Years of Development and Failure

Vopimetostat was developed as a second generation PRMT5i by Tango, following the discontinuation of TNG908 which was itself part of a new wave of MTA-cooperative

PRMT5is. Earlier PRMT5is worked through less selective mechanisms of action, such as substrate competition, which would inhibit PRMT5 but would not differentiate between healthy and tumor cells, creating a very limited therapeutic window. However, since MTAP-deleted tumors represent such a meaningful portion of all tumors (10%-15%), work continued to develop better, more selective, PRMT5 inhibitors and Tango's drugs are a result of this decades-long effort.

The ability of vopimetostat, TNG456 and other assets to bind and inhibit PRMT5 in the presence of high levels of MTA, which is a direct result of MTAP deletion, allows to differentiate between healthy and tumor cells, widely expanding the therapeutic window with a tolerable safety profile that opens up combination options, which has now become Tango's modus operandi.

## Combos and More Combos! Brain-Penetrant TNG456 Proof-of-Concept Coming This Year and a CDK4/6i Combo Soon After

Beyond the combination opportunity with RAS inhibitors in PDAC and NSCLC, Tango also has an ongoing Ph 1/2 trial in glioblastoma with brain-penetrant TNG456 and monotherapy data expected this year. Preclinical data has shown a lot of promise and the opportunity in this indication is meaningful with up to $45\%$ of cases originating from MTAP-deleted tumors. Tango has a collaboration agreement with Eli Lilly and if monotherapy data shows any promise (in an indication with very few treatment options), Tango has said they will initiate the second portion of the study which will combine TNG456 with abemaciclib, LLY's CDK4/6i. While it remains to be seen if this combination will play out in the clinic, the fact that MTAP deletion almost exclusively occurs in the setting of CDKN2A/B deletion, rendering cells sensitive to CDK4/6 inhibition, opens up another interesting combination approach for non-overlapping MOAs in another setting that is ripe for new options.

## Focusing on PRMT5 and Combinations: Tango is Hyper-focused with a Leaner Pipeline

Until earlier this year, Tango still had assets in its pipeline with targets beyond PRMT5, such as HBS1L (TNG961) and CoREST (TNG260), however, it recently deprioritized these assets and streamlined the pipeline to focus solely on the PRMT5i opportunity with vopimetostat and TNG456. This follows the promising monotherapy and combination data seen for vopimetostat and the large opportunities they present.
