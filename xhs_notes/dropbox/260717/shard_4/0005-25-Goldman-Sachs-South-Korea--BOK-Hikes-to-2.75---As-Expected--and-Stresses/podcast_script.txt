ZH_B: 核心逻辑有两条。第一条，韩国的通胀压力主要来自进口成本，尤其是汇率和能源价格，这不是短期内能消化的。第二条，经济增长集中在半导体出口和相关投资上，这种结构性不均衡让央行不敢停手。

ZH_A: 所以，即便就业数据最近不太好，央行还是得继续加息？

ZH_B: 这正是报告里一个很有意思的洞察。行长自己也承认，就业确实疲软，但他认为这更多是结构性转型和地缘政治不确定性导致的，不是周期性需求的问题。所以央行并没有因此改变紧缩方向。

ZH_B: 对，这也是央行的一个关注点。家庭贷款规模明显上升，金融稳定风险在积累。所以央行必须保持加息压力，不能因为其他数据短期波动就松懈。

EN_B: Where does this hiking cycle end. The report says the market is pricing in just one more move, but the analysts see two more quarter-point hikes, taking the rate to 3.25 percent.

EN_A: That’s a pretty clear call. What’s the logic behind it?

EN_B: Two main drivers. First, inflation is being pushed by import costs, especially from the weak currency and high energy prices. That’s not going away quickly. Second, growth is very concentrated in semiconductors and related investment. That kind of imbalance keeps the central bank on edge.

EN_B: That’s one of the most interesting parts of the report. The governor acknowledged the weakness, but he framed it as structural and geopolitical, not cyclical. So the central bank isn’t backing off just because jobs data softened.

EN_A: And what about housing? The report mentions rising household loans and Seoul property prices.

EN_B: Right. That’s the third pillar of their concern. Financial stability risks are building, so the central bank feels it has to keep the pressure on.
