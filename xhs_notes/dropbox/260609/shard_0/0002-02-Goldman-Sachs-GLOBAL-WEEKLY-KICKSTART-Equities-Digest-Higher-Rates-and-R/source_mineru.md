GLOBAL WEEKLY KICKSTART

## Equities Digest Higher Rates and Rising Supply

Global equities fell by more than 2% last week, with US markets underperforming (Exhibit 1), while momentum lagged the broader market (Exhibit 31) as stronger US labour data pushed expectations towards further rate tightening (Exhibit 4). Reflecting this shift, our economists have pushed the final two Fed rate cuts back to June and December 2027, and our rates strategists now expect US 10-year yields to reach 4.4% by year-end, up from 4.1%.

## Corporate equity demand should outweigh record IPO supply in 2026

Equity issuance is increasing. Our US team expects 2026 to be a record year for the dollar value of US equity issuance, driven by larger transactions.

However, (1) the number of IPOs this year is likely to only match the historical average of around 100; (2) when scaled, issuance equates to just 1.0% of US equity market cap, compared to a historical average of 1.5%; and (3) demand remains strong, supported by buybacks, M&A, and investor inflows.

As a result, demand is likely to outweigh supply this year, as the return of IPO activity ultimately reflects strong investor demand, with companies unlikely to come to market without confidence in the market's capacity to absorb new supply. The recent pickup in issuance therefore points to a more supportive demand backdrop.
