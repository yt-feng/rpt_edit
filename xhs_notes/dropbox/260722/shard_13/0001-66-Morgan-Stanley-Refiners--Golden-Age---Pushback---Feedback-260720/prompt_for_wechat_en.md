# "Golden Age": Pushback & Feedback

After weathering three major shocks in six years, fuel markets continue to demonstrate remarkable resilience. We see further upside to the refining cycle, with Asian refiners set to enjoy a higher than usual mid-cycle as the market overstates new capacity adds and understates ongoing disruptions.

1. Global Refinery utilisation is only 80%. Why do we see a Golden Age? Isn't the conflict key to the margins being above mid-cycle? Refining is a key bottleneck to the oil ecosystem, like power is for AI (see Energy: Supercycle Recharges). Hence we have seen fuel margins rise very consistently in Asia since 2021 and refinery level profitability has nearly risen 50% over five years. Most Asian and US refineries have reported 95% utilisation in last three years on average with refiners in India, Thailand and Malaysia reporting 100%+ utilisation rates. We believe refiners that are not able to operate at full run rates in the current margin environment, may struggle (Exhibit 2). Even post-conflict, we believe refining margins should settle 20% above pre-conflict levels for diesel and gasoline and our bullish view only got reaffirmed as conflict-led disruptions have led to significant tightness in an already tight market with declining fuel inventories (more: Refiners: The "Golden Age" and Why Crack Spreads Are So High)

India has capped fuel product margins to near US\$20-25/bbl for diesel and gasoline which benefits only from the highest capacity add globally. Japan and Australia have dynamics around fuel retail margins, but during an upcycle do benefit from higher fuel cracks. On an average US \$1/bbl rise in diesel margins imply 20-30% for our key picks – TOP TB, SPRC TB, S-Oil, HPCL NS, AMPOL and BCP TB (Exhibit 7).
