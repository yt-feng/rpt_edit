# "Golden Age": Pushback & Feedback

After weathering three major shocks in six years, fuel markets continue to demonstrate remarkable resilience. We see further upside to the refining cycle, with Asian refiners set to enjoy a higher than usual mid-cycle as the market overstates new capacity adds and understates ongoing disruptions.

1. Global Refinery utilisation is only 80%. Why do we see a Golden Age? Isn't the conflict key to the margins being above mid-cycle? Refining is a key bottleneck to the oil ecosystem, like power is for AI (see Energy: Supercycle Recharges). Hence we have seen fuel margins rise very consistently in Asia since 2021 and refinery level profitability has nearly risen 50% over five years. Most Asian and US refineries have reported 95% utilisation in last three years on average with refiners in India, Thailand and Malaysia reporting 100%+ utilisation rates. We believe refiners that are not able to operate at full run rates in the current margin environment, may struggle (Exhibit 2). Utilisation rate of 85% was also the peak in the last upcycle as refineries need to take downtime for maintenance every year and see large turnarounds every three to four years (Exhibit 4).

Even post-conflict, we believe refining margins should settle 20% above pre-conflict levels for diesel and gasoline and our bullish view only got reaffirmed as conflict-led disruptions have led to significant tightness in an already tight market with declining fuel inventories (more: Refiners: The "Golden Age" and Why Crack Spreads Are So High)

## 2.What are the differences within countries to play the Golden Age in Asia?

Thailand and Korea offer the purest plays to a refining upcycle with limited challenges around windfall taxes on fuel products. India has capped fuel product margins to near US\$20-25/bbl for diesel and gasoline which benefits only from the highest capacity add globally. Japan and Australia have dynamics around fuel retail margins, but during an upcycle do benefit from higher fuel cracks. On an average US \$1/bbl rise in diesel margins imply 20-30% for our key picks – TOP TB, SPRC TB, S-Oil, HPCL NS, AMPOL and BCP TB (Exhibit 7).

3.What's the new mid-cycle margins; are there triggers for stocks or do they fall as GRMs decline from the highs? Refining equities are pricing in mid-cycle margins, but we see the new mid-cycle to be 35% higher than last three cycles due to the market overestimating the supply and underestimating the supply disruptions (Exhibit 5). With well supplied oil markets in 2027, we do see crude discounts adding another layer of upside for fuel refiners in Asia. The recent Dubai crude discount over Brent of nearly US\$6/bbl highlights this dynamic as well. We see 40-60% upside to Street 2027 and 2028 EPS estimates for our key picks.
