# Rolling Through the Sunshine State

Tesla announced the launch of Robotaxi in Orlando and Tampa this morning. Tesla launched Robotaxi in two more Florida cities (reportedly including unsupervised Model Ys) on Tuesday, July 21st. Orlando and Tampa both were listed as "preparations underway" in the 1Q26 slide deck, along with Miami (launched), Phoenix (not yet launched), and Las Vegas (not yet launched).
