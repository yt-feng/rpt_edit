EN_A: So I keep hearing that SpaceX wants to put AI data centers in space. But the first question everyone asks is, how do you keep computers from overheating in a vacuum?

EN_B: It is true. Two Austrian physicists, Stefan and Boltzmann, figured out that the hotter an object is, the more energy it radiates. If you double the temperature, the radiated energy goes up by 16 times.
EN_A: 16 times? That is a huge difference.
EN_B: Exactly. And that is the key to cooling in space. You cannot use fans or water because there is no air. But you can radiate heat away as infrared light.

EN_B: They use a similar approach to the International Space Station. The ISS uses ammonia as a coolant, pumped to large radiator panels that glow in infrared. SpaceX will use a similar design with some modifications.
