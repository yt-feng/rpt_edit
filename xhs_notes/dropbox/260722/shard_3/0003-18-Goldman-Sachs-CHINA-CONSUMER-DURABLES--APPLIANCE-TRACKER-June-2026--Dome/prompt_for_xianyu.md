## Ex-factory shipments decline came better than expected in June

According to China Industry Online (IOL), AC total ex-factory shipments decline narrowed to -7% yoy in Jun (vs. -11% yoy in May), with narrower decline from domestic (-7% yoy in Jun vs. -15% yoy in May) but weaker growth from exports (-7% yoy in Jun vs. -4% yoy in May). Compared to previous production plan, both domestic and exports came above (vs. -23% and -21% yoy of previous production plan respectively). Similar to previous months, we note that overall exports by Chinese players should be greater than that indicated by IOL export data as leading companies such as Midea also export from their overseas factories.

Domestic shipment came in better than expected by IOL in Jun, likely supported by better demand as we noticed sequentially higher AC discounts during 618. Going forward, IOL production plan suggests SD% yoy decline in 3Q26, a narrower pace vs. 2Q26.

Exports remained resilient and came in better than expected in Jun, driven by Chinese players' continued share gains in our view. In particular, the surging demand for Chinese ACs amid the current heatwave in Europe has been a key investor focus, contributing to company sales in both 2Q-3Q. For example, Midea mentioned $70\%+$ yoy growth for its AC sales in France, Spain, Germany and the United Kingdom in 1H26 was driven by the blockbuster product PortaSplit. Property sales data (Exhibit 12) in DM and consumer sentiment (Exhibit 11) both showed sequential improvements in the past month. US appliances shipment decline also narrowed in 2Q26 (-3% yoy vs. -8% yoy in 1Q per AHAM). Going forward, the current IOL production plan forecasts a similar exports growth in 3Q26 as 2Q.

By brand, Haier and Hisense outpaced the industry in terms of total shipments growth, while Gree and Midea lagged. Haier's total AC ex-factory shipments were up by 8% yoy, underpinned by +4%/+23% domestic/exports growth. Hisense's total AC ex-factory shipments decreased by 3% yoy, underpinned by +12%/-17% domestic/exports growth. Midea's total AC ex-factory shipments decreased by 11% yoy, with larger decline of both domestic and exports (-11% and -10% yoy). Gree's total AC ex-factory shipments decreased by 13% yoy, with above-industry domestic and exports (-26% and -25% yoy).

Total ex-factory shipment volume growth for washing machines/refrigerators demonstrated mixed trends in May.

\- Washing machine total shipments growth moderated to +2% yoy in May (vs. +4% yoy in Apr). Domestic decline widened to -9% yoy in May (vs. -6% yoy in Apr), and exports growth decelerated to +12% yoy in May (vs. +14% yoy in Apr). By brand, both Midea and Haier came above industry. Midea's total shipments were up by +2% yoy, with domestic/export shipments growing by -5%/+11% yoy. Haier's total shipments grew by +3% yoy, with domestic/exports growth of -5%/+17% yoy.

■ Refrigerator total shipments growth accelerated to +9% yoy in May (vs. +6% yoy in Apr). Domestic decline widened to -9% yoy in May (vs. -4% yoy in Apr), but exports growth accelerated to +23% yoy in May (vs. +13% yoy in Apr). By brand, Midea and Hisense came above industry but Haier came below in May. Midea's total shipments grew by +27% yoy, with domestic/export shipments growing by +13%/+39% yoy.

Hisense's total shipments grew by $+18\%$ yoy, with domestic/overseas shipments growth of $-7\% / +33\%$ yoy. Haier's total shipments were $-1\%$ yoy, of which domestic/exports grew $-6\% / +57\%$ yoy.

Total shipment value for variable refrigerant flow systems (VRF) decreased 6% yoy, a wider decline vs. -5% yoy in Apr. Domestic shipment decline widened while export shipment growth stayed stable in May (domestic/exports -8%/+1% yoy in May vs. -6%/+1% yoy in Apr). By brand, in the domestic market, Daikin and Gree came above the industry at -1%/-7% yoy, while Hisense-Hitachi/Midea was below/in-line at -8%/-13% yoy. For exports, Daikin and Midea showed faster growth vs. the industry average.
