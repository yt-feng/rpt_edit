## CXMT: China's DRAM champion lands a record ~$85bn STAR Market IPO

CXMT's ~$8.5bn Shanghai listing – the largest STAR Market IPO ever.

## Profile

• Founded 2016 · HQ Hefei, Anhui
- China's only large-scale DRAM maker
- Node: D1z (1z-nm) DDR5 / LPDDR5X, no EUV
- ~99% of 2025 revenue is DDR / LPDDR

## IPO – Shanghai STAR Market

• Price ¥8.66/sh · trading from 27 Jul
- Raise ~¥57.9bn (~$8.5bn); up to ~$9.8bn
- Largest STAR Market IPO ever; Asia's biggest 2026
- Implied value ~¥579bn (~$85bn)
- FY26e P/E ~5.8x (trailing ~309x); P/B ~3x
• Strategic: Alibaba Cloud, Meituan, Xiaomi
