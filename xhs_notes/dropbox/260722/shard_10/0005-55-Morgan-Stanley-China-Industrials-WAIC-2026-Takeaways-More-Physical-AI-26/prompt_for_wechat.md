## Industrial AI

Solutions are emerging. Industrial AI has moved beyond conceptual toward deployable agentic systems for different process steps, although the broader transformation remains at an early stage. For now, these solutions primarily augment/support rather than replace human decision making. World model/digital twin at PipeChina helps engineers better monitor and predict the system, while industrial agentic operating systems, such as Cosmoplant's BaaS industrial brain, aggregate information and data across equipment and production processes, allowing operators to make faster and better-informed decisions. Per their staff, the system deployment is expanding across 3C electronics, home appliances and machinery. This operational monitoring approach is relatively easy to implement because it generally does not require additional licensing from equipment providers, as long as end-users are willing to open their operational data to solution providers. On the engineering side, Siemens' Eigen Engineering agent, trained on Siemens' extensive installed-base data, can understand natural language and autonomously support PLM and HMI programming, allowing engineers to focus on more complex tasks. Equipment upgrades are required for AI/data connectivity. ## Robotics and Humanoids

Deployment is the consensus, mainstream use cases concentrated, new applications exploration continues. Many humanoid integrators have similarly developed broad product portfolios targeting different use cases, including full-size and half-size bipedal humanoids, wheel-based humanoids and quadruped robots. Beyond this, we see ongoing effort to adopt humanoids in new use cases – for example, Tars humanoid fleet working in wire-harnessing lines, Psibot humanoids in CPO manufacturing lines across plug-ins, vacuum sealing, and boxing. Data remains the universally cited bottleneck. In our view, these startups provide relatively similar services with limited differentiation, making this a layer of rapid commoditization (i.e., data price quick deflation), although their ability to serve multiple customers may support industry-wide improvement.
