【金字塔原理写作原则】
1. 结论先行：文章开头先回答“这份报告最值得看的判断是什么”，而不是介绍背景。
2. 统领思想：全文只能服务一个主判断，避免变成摘要合集。
3. 纵向回答：每一层都要回答上一层提出的“为什么”或“所以呢”。
4. 横向 MECE：每个一级小节必须彼此独立、共同支撑主判断，避免重叠。
5. Synthesis over summary：不要复述报告段落，要提炼“这些事实合在一起意味着什么”。
6. So what：每个小节末尾必须落到对行业、公司、竞争格局、市场定价或读者观察框架的含义。
7. 信息型小标题：所有 `##` 小标题必须是完整、中性的事实句，不能是目录标签，也不能评价好坏或制造冲突。

## Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation

## Investment Thesis

Prada is a key luxury goods player in Fashion & Leather Goods. Its portfolio of brands includes Prada, Miu Miu, Versace, Church's, Car Shoe and Marchesi 1824. Following heavy investments across the group between 2016 and 2019, Prada and Miu Miu have been experiencing very solid momentum since 2021, benefitting from sustained product newness at the former and high desirability at the latter, despite a multi-year very tough comp. Such solid trends is driving the ongoing recovery of sales densities with profitability following suit. We think there is further potential for both brands to continue to extend their presence and lift their financial KPIs, increasingly catching up with the other mega-brands in the luxury arena. While the Versace acquisition adds execution risk in the short term and will require the Group to invest in restoring brand desirability and profitability, we think Prada Group still offers some of the most attractive fundamentals and valuation in the luxury sector, with earnings growth in the core Group continuing to run in double-digits. We rate the stock Overweight.
