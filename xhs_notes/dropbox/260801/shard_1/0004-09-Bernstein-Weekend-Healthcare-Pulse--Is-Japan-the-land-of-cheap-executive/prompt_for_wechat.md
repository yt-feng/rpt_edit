## JAPANESE EXECUTIVES HAVE BEEN COMPENSATED WITH FAR LESS EQUITY THAN THEIR US PEERS AS THERE IS LITTLE INSTITUTIONAL PRESSURE FOR THE JAPANESE CORPORATE TO ADOPT THE US PRACTICE
