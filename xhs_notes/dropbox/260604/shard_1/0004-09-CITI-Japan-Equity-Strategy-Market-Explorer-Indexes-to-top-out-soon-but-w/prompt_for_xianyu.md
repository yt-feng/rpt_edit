# Japan Equity Strategy Market Explorer

Indexes to top out soon but we see the Nikkei breaking above 70,000 by year-end

# CITI'S TAKE

Japanese equities have performed solidly over the past month, despite short-term corrections. Supporting factors have included: 1) hopes for a US-Iran ceasefire agreement and a peak in crude oil prices, 2) the continuation of the global AI and semiconductor boom, and 3) initial company guidance shown in earnings announcements remaining at normal levels of conservatism. Given that not only the Nikkei 225, driven by the tech boom, but also TOPIX recently hit YTD highs, and that earnings revision deterioration is expected in the immediate future, Japanese equities may peak for a while in the short term. However, we conclude that upside potential toward year-end remains significant and that there is no need to change our bullish fundamental view on Japanese equities.

FY26 company plans demonstrate earnings resilience — Japanese companies' FY26 earnings plans as revealed in full-year results suggest firms are anticipating +4.2% YoY revenue growth and +7.6% NP growth (TOPIX basis). We view this as strong guidance given uncertainties around Middle East conditions and widespread cost increases. While profit plan misses may lead to deterioration in earnings forecast revisions in the short term, creating a headwind for stock prices, any deterioration in forecast revisions is likely to be modest given that company plans show normal levels of conservatism. Meanwhile, if earnings beats in high-tech sectors/stocks and margin improvements from pricing pass-throughs support overall earnings, actual corporate earnings are likely to exceed company plans and consensus estimates, with earnings revisions turning upward again.

Japanese equity valuations still have room to rise — We believe margin improvements in an inflationary environment will push up Japanese equity valuations via higher RoE. While TOPIX's 12-month forward PER currently stands at 16.8x, near the upper end of its range over the past decade-plus, we see room for further PER expansion as the average RoE sets its sights on the 11%-12% range. Based on the above, we set our TOPIX target at 4,500 by applying a PER of 17.5x to FY27 forecast EPS (¥258.4).
