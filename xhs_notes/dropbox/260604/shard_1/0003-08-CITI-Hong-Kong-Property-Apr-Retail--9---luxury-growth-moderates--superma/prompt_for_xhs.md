# Hong Kong Property

Apr Retail +9%: luxury growth moderates; supermarket stabilizing

# CITI'S TAKE

HK April 2026 retail sales were +8.6%yoy, a solid performance but at milder pace relative to Mar (+13%) and 1Q26 (+12%). The luxury segment, proxied by “jewelry, watches & valuable gifts”, decelerated to +20%yoy (1Q: +28%), reflecting a fading favorable base effect and lower gold prices. May data supported by Golden Week tourism (+8%yoy); beware of upcoming higher comparable base — We estimate HK May retail sales could rise 5-8%yoy (to HK\$33-34bn), partly driven by the 8%yoy growth in visitor arrivals during the 5.1 holiday, which included +10%yoy increase in Chinese mainland visitors. Despite the tailwind from a weaker HKD FX against Rmb (5M26: -4%), we expect overall retail to post sequential deceleration in yoy growth, as the base for comparison strengthens given the recovery in market sentiment that kicked in from late-May to June 2025.
