Globally, smartphone wholesale prices rose 14% in Q1, with upward pressure expected to persist as pre-shock inventory is depleted. Several sub-\$150 price tiers are facing what appears to be a structural, potentially permanent exit from the market. In contrast, the premium segment is proving more resilient. In April, iPhone performance remained solid: global sell-through revenue rose 5.6% YoY, unit volumes increased 2.5% YoY, and ASPs climbed 3%.

Promotions from both Verizon and T-Mobile on Apple and Samsung devices have moderated. In China, iPhone sales rose 5.6% YoY in April, driven entirely by higher ASPs, with unit volumes largely flat.
