# Network Tokenization: The Next Pricing Opportunity?

What Is Network Tokenization?   
Tokenization replaces a payment card's account number with an alternative string of numbers called a token.   
![](images/364fdfa3c46265d69e1f87dcea64d473dde445db7d2eefe3ff49be3adcd02ef7.jpg)

# Benefits of Network Tokenization

Tokenization improves security, lifts revenue and lowers costs for the ecosystem

Improved Security: Fraud rates are \~30% lower for tokenized transactions, driven by lower third-party fraud.

Higher Authorization Rates: Network-tokenized transactions have 3-6% higher authorization rates, meaning more revenue to merchants, PSPs and issuers.

Can Reduce Merchant Costs: Visa interchange is 10bps lower for tokenized transactions in some categories; merchant PCI compliance cost lowered for COF use cases.

Better Consumer Experience: "Evergreen" credentials, fast checkout.
