EN_B: Because building new capacity takes time. The unit capex is 5000 to 8000 yuan per ton, and construction takes six to nine months. Even if you start today, new capacity will only come online in the fourth quarter of 2026. And then you need another three months for commissioning and ramp up.
EN_A: So the supply is basically locked in for the whole year.
EN_B: Exactly. That is the core tension. Supply is rigid, but demand is strong.

EN_B: The report says two types of producers benefit. First, companies with a vertically integrated model, meaning they produce FePO4 themselves and then process it into LFP cathode. Second, companies using technology routes that do not require FePO4 at all.
EN_A: What about the LFP cathode processing fee itself? Is that going up too?
EN_B: Yes, it is. But there is a really important structural split inside the LFP cathode market.
EN_A: What kind of split?
EN_B: Between fourth generation and third generation LFP cathode. The fourth generation has much higher technology barriers. The main client is CATL, and only three or four LFP cathode producers in the world can mass produce it.
EN_A: So fourth generation margins are much better.
EN_B: Right. Meanwhile, third generation LFP cathode margins will likely stay very low because competition is too intense.
