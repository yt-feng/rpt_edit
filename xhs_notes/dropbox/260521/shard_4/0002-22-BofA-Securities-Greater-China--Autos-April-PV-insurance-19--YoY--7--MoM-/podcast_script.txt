ZH_A: 最近我看到一份关于中国汽车市场的研报，四月的乘用车保险数据出来了，同比下降了19%，这个数字挺吓人的。你觉得这是市场崩盘了吗？

ZH_A: 去库存？所以四月的下滑不是需求崩了，而是经销商在有意减少库存？

ZH_A: 那如果需求没有崩，为什么零售数据这么差？是消费者不买车了吗？

ZH_A: 那燃油车这边，合资品牌是不是压力特别大？

ZH_A: 说到新能源，比亚迪四月保险注册量18.4万辆，同比跌了30%，这是怎么回事？比亚迪不是一直很猛吗？

ZH_A: 零跑四月卖了5万辆，同比增长53%，蔚来也涨了34%，小鹏跌了17%。这个分化很有意思，零跑凭什么涨这么多？

ZH_B: 零跑靠的是性价比和精准的产品定位，它的C系列在10到15万这个价格带非常有竞争力。而蔚来靠的是换电网络和高端服务，它的用户粘性很强。小鹏的下跌可能跟产品周期有关，它在等新车型。这些细节在研报里都有展开，但有一个问题研报没有完全回答。

EN_A: So the April passenger vehicle insurance registrations dropped 19 percent year-on-year. That sounds pretty brutal. Is the Chinese auto market falling apart?

EN_B: Not exactly. If you look closer, the wholesale numbers were only down 4 percent. The big gap between wholesale and retail tells us something important. Dealers are destocking. EN_A: So the 19 percent drop is more about inventory management than consumers walking away?

EN_B: Exactly. The report estimates that channel inventory decreased by about 23,000 units in April. That means manufacturers are controlling their shipments, and dealers are clearing out old stock. It is actually a healthier sign for the market than a panic sell-off.

EN_A: But if demand is still there, why are retail sales so weak? Are people just not buying cars?

EN_B: People are buying, but they are being more selective. Look at the EV segment. April EV insurance registrations were 827,000 units, down only 6 percent year-on-year and up 3 percent month-on-month. The structural demand for EVs is still there. The pain is concentrated in the internal combustion engine segment.

EN_A: What about the Chinese domestic brands? Are they all winning?

EN_B: It is highly polarized. SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle grew 33 percent year-on-year, thanks to new model deliveries. EN_A: Speaking of new energy, BYD saw a 30 percent drop in April insurance registrations. That is a big surprise. What happened?

EN_B: This is a key point. BYD's market share fell from nearly 30 percent late last year to 22.2 percent in April. It is still number one, but the gap is narrowing. Two reasons. One, the base effect, BYD had extremely high numbers last year. Two, competitors are catching up fast. Startups like Leapmotor, NIO, and XPeng are growing very quickly.

EN_A: Leapmotor sold 50,000 units, up 53 percent. NIO up 34 percent. XPeng down 17 percent. That is a huge divergence. What is driving Leapmotor's surge?
