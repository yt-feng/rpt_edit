EN_B: Yes. The report projects that by 2035, humanoid robots could add up to 3.8% to China’s effective labor force. That is roughly 24 million units, which could offset up to 60% of the projected workforce decline.

EN_B: In 2025, about 15,000 humanoid units were deployed globally. For 2026, the guidance points to 60,000. And by 2035, the report’s upper bound is 13 million units per year, with about 11 million of those in China.

EN_B: The report says adoption in the West will likely come later, driven more by consumer demand. But there is one key variable: security concerns. Worries about data and IP risks from Chinese humanoids could create guardrails.
