# South Korea: Rebound in Early May Exports, Driven by Semiconductors

# Bottom line:

Korea's per-workday exports in the first 20 days of May rose 2.8% mom sa, rebounding from -2.5% the prior month. Total exports during the 20 days gained 8.7% mom sa on positive workday effects, posting a record high pace of 64.8% yoy. Semiconductor exports extended an 8-month streak of increase at an accelerated pace of 11.9% and accounted for roughly half of the headline sequential increase. Exports to Taiwan and mainland China gained the most, by 20.6% and 10.4% respectively and accounted for roughly 40% of the headline gains. Total imports during the 20 days rebounded to 6.7% mom sa, with around half of the gains accounted for by energy-related products. The trade surplus (not seasonally adjusted) widened from US\$10.5bn to US\$11bn, the second largest on record.
