# US Tariff Impact Tracker: Imports into LA Mixed Over Next Two Weeks

US Tariff Impact Tracker: This past week, laden vessels from China to USA were down sequentially (-19% WoW) and down on a YoY basis (-25.5% YoY). Data suggests TEUs coming into Port of LA will turn positive next week (+3% WoW) following this past week's -6% sequential move, before seeing a decrease of -7% WoW two weeks out (YoY is expected to be -7% and then unchanged, one week and two weeks out). Trade uncertainty remains prevalent in global trade, especially following the recent Supreme Court ruling and the subsequent announcement from the administration for new tariffs under Sec. 122 (see our macro team's assessment).
