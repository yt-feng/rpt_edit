# Quick Take: Renesas Memory Interface IC Investigation Likely Manageable

South Korea and U.S. investigation into potential memory interface price collusion by Renesas, Rambus (not covered) and Montage (covered by Qingyuan Lin) on $15^{\text{th}}$ July as reported. The investigation focuses on whether the three vendors coordinated pricing of memory interface products, including DDR5 registered clock drivers (RCDs) and data buffers. The Korea probe follows a separate U.S. investigation. Earlier this year, the U.S. DOJ was investigating Renesas over suspected semiconductor price-fixing, while Rambus subsequently disclosed receipt of a grand jury subpoena related to the investigation.

Memory interface GM is not unusually high. Relative to leading fabless peers that often have 60-70% GM, Renesas, Montage and Rambus all generate structurally high margins of 60-70% from memory interface, mostly due to structural growth, IP, controller/platform qualification, validation cycles, scarce supply, and limited alternatives.

Memory interface exposure is limited within Renesas' revenue mix, accounting for \~6% of Renesas' revenue in 2025, and we estimate this to increase to \~7-8% in 2026E. Assuming a \~65% GM and similar opex ratio to the group, we estimate the business contributes \~8% / \~9.5% of Renesas' OP in 2025 / 26E, respectively.

Limited impact even under a negative scenario. We can't say if the investigation would or would not lead to any action, but any legal action would find it hard to change the market structure and hence structural profitability. But if indeed pricing get reset lower, leading to some margin normalization, the impact would still be limited. Assuming the price of Renesas' memory interface business drops by 10ppts to $55\%$ , it would only impact $0.8\%$ of corporate revenue and $1.8\%$ of OP (Exhibit 1).  
Reiterate Outperform. We believe the probing has limited impact and remain constructive on power semis given the rise of AI power demand (Infineon/Renesas: CPU renaissance emerges as a second engine for power semi demand, set to outstrip supply). We hence reiterate our Outperform rating on Renesas.
