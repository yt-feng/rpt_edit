EN_A: What is the fundamental difference between WAM and VLA?
EN_B: VLA is essentially language to action. WAM, on the other hand, predicts future states based on the environment, context, and experience, and then generates actions from those predictions. The report compares it to how humans imagine outcomes before acting.
EN_A: Imagine before acting. That is much smarter. But does that mean it needs more data?
EN_B: Absolutely, and that is a key change. WAM demands much more diverse data, not just robot action data but also environmental data. The report highlights that non-vision data, like tactile and material properties, is still very scarce.

EN_B: The report has a very interesting take. It argues that WAM could reshape the competitive moat. Instead of who has the most deployment data, the advantage may shift to who has the best model architecture and data consolidation capabilities.
