# Vodafone (VOD.L): 1Q27 Results and Updated Guidance Overview

Vodafone's 1Q27 results showed a modest outperformance relative to expectations, with the Group's underlying guidance adjusted upward to reflect an improved outlook in Africa. The company's commentary on fundamental growth drivers across the Group remains broadly unchanged. Key observations include: 1) a Europe-driven quarter that benefited from easier comparisons and certain one-time revenue items in Germany; 2) Group guidance supported by Africa, where both structural and cyclical factors are at play. Vodafone now guides Group EBITDA and Adjusted Free Cash Flow toward the upper end of its previous range, implying approximately 7% and 20% year-on-year growth respectively for FY27, though Europe-based growth remains stable and limited.

Key areas of focus for observers include: 1) African growth improving, driving the Group guidance uplift – Vodacom (Vodafone's listed main African business) raised its guidance in its results today, reflecting a better-than-expected macro backdrop and favorable energy hedging, alongside structural positives from recent price adjustments across the region; 2) Europe organic service revenue growth (OSRG) remains stable at +0.9% (vs +0.8% in 4Q26); 3) Germany growth sustained but expected to moderate through the year – OSRG was +1.2% year-on-year in 1Q27 (vs +1.3% in 4Q26), +1ppt vs consensus, as the growing drag from lost 1&1 wholesale contract revenues was offset by improved broadband ARPU growth and B2B digital services growth. Through FY27, Vodafone continues to guide for an increasing headwind from lost 1&1 revenues (particularly in Q2), and notes that Digital Services revenue is lumpy and was particularly strong in 2Q26 – consensus models OSRG at -1.1% year-on-year for 2Q27; 4) UK underlying trends broadly unchanged – OSRG was +0.6% year-on-year in 1Q27 (vs -0.2% in 4Q26, but this was due to easy comparisons against 1Q26 when there was a material decline in managed services revenues). Overall, Vodafone notes the underlying growth backdrop in the UK is unchanged and the VOD/3 synergies delivery remains on track.

## In the Detail

### Germany

- The company reported +1.2% OSRG growth. This compares to +1.3%/+0.7%/+0.5%/-0.3% growth in 4Q26/3Q26/2Q26/1Q26 excluding the impact of TV billing headwinds, and +1.3%/+0.7%/+0.5%/-3.2% in 4Q26/3Q26/2Q26/1Q26 including.
- Broadband net adds for this quarter declined -98k (vs. -90k/-63k/-26k/-23k in 4Q26/3Q26/2Q26/1Q26).
- Vodafone Germany reported -85k postpaid net adds in 1Q27 (vs. -77k/+11k/-1k/-36k in 4Q26/3Q26/2Q26/1Q26).

### UK

- Vodafone lost -48k postpaid subscribers in the quarter (vs. -22k/-73k/+14k/-46k in 4Q26/3Q26/2Q26/1Q26).
- 1Q27 broadband net adds were +34k (vs. +64k/+64k/+50k/+44k in 4Q26/3Q26/2Q26/1Q26).

## Valuation and Risks

Our 12-month price target is 85p ($11.58 for the ADR) and is based on our EV/IC to ROIC/WACC methodology.

Key upside risks to our thesis and price target include: 1) 1&1 network rollout halt, independent of consolidation; 2) Vodafone participating in German consolidation; 3) Greater market repair benefits than expected in the UK; 4) Stronger execution than expected; 5) Better competitive environment across key markets than expected; and 6) Emerging Markets upside risk.
