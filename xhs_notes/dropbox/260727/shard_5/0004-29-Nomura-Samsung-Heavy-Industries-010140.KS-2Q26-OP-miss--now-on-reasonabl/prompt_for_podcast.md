## Company profile

Samsung Heavy Industries is Korea shipbuilding and offshore EPC company. The company was founded in 1974 as a part of governments supporting plan for heavy industries by Samsung group.

## Valuation Methodology

Our TP of 25,000 is based on a 12-month-forward (12MF) BVPS of KRW7,456 (previously: 12MF BVPS of KRW6,738), multiplied by a target P/B of 3.35x (previously: 4.01x). Our target P/B implies a $4.3\%$ premium to SHI's implied P/B of 3.21x. We factor in the premium coming from the US naval opportunity, as SHI is now participating in MASGA (Make America Shipbuilding Great Again) project.

## Risks that may impede the achievement of the target price

Upside risks are (1) blockage of Bab Al-Mandab Strait which could lead to massive new orders of tankers and (2) unexpected FDC new orders. Downside risk will be continued expansion of Chinese shipyard.
