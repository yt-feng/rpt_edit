EN_B: Precisely. The second finding is the divergence between exports and domestic consumption. Exports surged 19.4% year-on-year in May, driven by renewable energy and AI-related goods. But core CPI fell 0.1% month-on-month. Export is hot, domestic demand is cold.

EN_B: The third is about PPI and inflation pass-through. PPI rose 3.9% year-on-year, but the gap between purchase prices and factory-gate prices remains wide. That means upstream cost pressures are not being passed down to downstream sectors. Those downstream companies are still in a disinflationary environment. We are far from a true reflation.
