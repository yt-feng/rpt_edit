ZH_A: 等等，IFRS 18到底是什么？它和现在的会计准则有什么不同？

ZH_B: 简单来说，现行的IAS 1准则比较模糊，只要求披露收入、净利润等几个大项。但IFRS 18会强制要求公司把利润表分成五个类别，比如运营、投资、融资，并且要列出五个小计，比如运营利润和融资税前利润。这意味着投资者能看到更清晰的盈利结构。

ZH_A: 所以这意味着什么？对普通投资者来说，这有什么实际好处？

ZH_B: 最大的好处在于现金流表。IFRS 18强制要求公司使用“间接法”来编制现金流表，也就是从运营利润开始，而不是净利润。这对投资者来说更透明，因为你能直接看到运营活动产生了多少现金。但很多公司以前不喜欢这么做，因为信息太暴露了。

ZH_A: 那报告里提到的“失望”呢？它说有两个失望点。

ZH_B: 第一个失望是，IFRS 18仍然没有定义“非经常性项目”。这意味着分析师还得自己去判断哪些收入是可持续的。第二个失望是，它取消了公司的“财务结果”这个单独科目。比如，现金投资的利息收入现在被归到“投资”类别，而债务利息归到“融资”类别。这听起来有点哲学，但可能会让公司的盈利结构看起来更复杂。

ZH_A: 这听起来确实有点抽象。那报告第二部分提到的Technip Energies案例呢？它怎么就和IFRS 15“相爱”了？

ZH_B: 这是个很精彩的案例。Technip Energies在2018年采用IFRS 15后，巧妙地利用了准则中的“合同资产”和“合同负债”分类。通过只披露有限的细节，它成功地让自己的净现金状况保持低调。直到2024年的一次投资者日，管理层才透露了更多信息，这时大家才发现，它的净现金其实比想象中高很多。

EN_B: Simply put, the current IAS 1 is pretty vague. It only requires companies to disclose a few big items like revenue and net profit. But IFRS 18 will force companies to break down their income statement into five categories, like operating, investing, and financing, and show five subtotals, like operating profit and profit before financing and taxes. This means investors get a much clearer picture of the earnings structure.

EN_A: So what does that mean? What’s the real benefit for a regular investor?

EN_B: The biggest win is the cash flow statement. IFRS 18 requires companies to use the indirect method, starting with operating profit instead of net income. This is way more transparent because you can directly see how much cash the operations are generating. But many companies disliked this because it exposes too much information.

EN_A: The report mentions two disappointments. What are they?

EN_B: The first disappointment is that IFRS 18 still doesn’t define “non-recurring items.” That means analysts will still have to figure out what’s sustainable earnings. The second is that it eliminates the separate “financial result” line. For example, interest income from cash investments is now under “investing,” while interest on debt is under “financing.” It sounds a bit philosophical, but it could make earnings look more complicated.

EN_A: That does sound abstract. What about the Technip Energies case in the report? How did it “fall in love” with IFRS 15?
