ZH_A: 最近有一份关于半导体设备行业的深度报告，来自某外资投行的日本和台湾考察总结，听起来信息量很大。你觉得这份报告最核心的判断是什么？

ZH_A: 等等，我记得三个月前市场还在担心存储芯片的现货价格下跌，周期可能见顶。现在怎么又乐观起来了？

ZH_B: 对，那次担忧确实存在。但报告提到，最近现货价格又开始回升了，这让投资者确信存储芯片的短缺可能会持续2到3年。再加上先进逻辑芯片的投资依然强劲，所以前道晶圆设备厂商会持续受益。

ZH_B: 没错，它真正要回答的问题是：在AI驱动下，存储和逻辑芯片的需求到底能撑多久，以及后道封装领域下一个技术瓶颈在哪里。这两个问题决定了不同设备公司的增长空间。

ZH_A: 那先聊第一个关键洞察，AI对存储芯片的拉动到底有多强？报告里有没有具体的测算？

ZH_B: 这意味着AI对存储的需求不是概念，而是实打实的物理消耗。而且这还只是AI推理芯片，还没算上训练芯片和HBM内存。所以报告认为，DRAM的紧缺可能会持续好几年。

ZH_A: 那第二个关键洞察呢？报告提到了一个叫“铜墙”的概念，这又是什么？

ZH_B: 这是从Computex大会上传来的一个技术瓶颈。简单说，数据中心内部现在用铜线传输信号，但随着速度提升到每通道400G，铜线的传输距离会缩短到1.25米，而机柜高度是2米。这就意味着，到2028年左右，完全用铜线连接机柜内部会变得不可能。

ZH_A: 所以必须改用光信号？这就是CPO，共封装光学技术的用武之地？

ZH_B: 正是。报告认为，CPO会成为2027到2028年之后的一个重大主题。这会拉动测试设备的需求，比如Advantest，因为CPO芯片需要更高精度的测试来保证良率。

ZH_A: 听起来测试设备公司会迎来一波新需求。那这份报告有没有提到一些尚未完全展开的悬念？

ZH_B: 有两个很关键。第一个是关于Kokusai Electric的。这家公司给出了比较保守的业绩指引，但报告认为，下半年需求有上行风险，尤其是来自中国DRAM厂商的额外订单。这个悬念要等到一季度财报公布后才能验证。

ZH_A: 所以现在买入Kokusai有点像在赌一个业绩上调的催化剂？

ZH_A: 这确实值得去读完整报告。最后，你个人觉得这份报告里最值得关注的长期趋势是什么？

ZH_B: 我会关注CPO。它不仅是技术瓶颈的解决方案，还会重塑整个后道封装和测试设备的价值链。报告提到，CPO测试设备的总市场空间可能在2028年达到3到7亿美元，之后会超过10亿美元。这是一个从零到一的爆发机会。

EN_A: So you just went through a major research report on semiconductor production equipment, based on a trip to Taiwan and Japan. What is the one big takeaway?

EN_B: The big takeaway is that while investor interest in the sector has cooled a bit from the peak in late 2025 and early 2026, confidence in the memory cycle is actually stronger now. People are much less pessimistic about memory through 2027 than they were three months ago.

EN_A: Wait, three months ago everyone was worried about spot prices falling and the cycle ending. What changed?

EN_B: Spot prices turned upward again. That gave investors more conviction that memory shortages could last for another two to three years. Plus, advanced logic investment remains strong. So front-end wafer equipment companies are still in a good position.

EN_A: So the report is not just a simple bullish or bearish call. It is about a structural shift.

EN_B: Exactly. The real question it tries to answer is: how sustainable is AI-driven memory and logic demand, and what is the next technical bottleneck in the back-end process? The answers determine which equipment companies will grow.

EN_A: Let us start with the first key insight. How strong is the AI pull on memory demand? Is there a specific number in the report?

EN_B: There is a very concrete example. The report talks about the next-generation AI chip, the Vera CPU. It needs three times as much LPDDR5X memory as the previous generation. EN_A: Ten percent from one chip? That is huge. What does that mean?

EN_B: It means AI demand is not a story anymore. It is a physical reality that consumes real wafer capacity. And this is just for inference chips, not even training or HBM. So the report believes DRAM tightness could last for years.

EN_A: What about the second key insight? The report mentioned something called the copper wall. What is that?

EN_B: That came from Computex. It is a technical bottleneck. Inside data centers, signals are transmitted over copper cables. But as speeds increase to 400 gigabits per lane, the distance those signals can travel over copper shrinks to 1.25 meters. A standard server rack is about 2 meters tall. So by around 2028, you cannot fully connect racks with copper anymore.

EN_A: So you have to switch to optical signals? That is where co-packaged optics, or CPO, comes in?

EN_B: Exactly. The report sees CPO becoming a major theme from 2027 to 2028 onward. That will drive demand for testing equipment, especially for companies like Advantest, because CPO chips need very precise testing to ensure good die yield.

EN_A: So test equipment companies have a new growth driver. Are there any open questions the report does not fully answer?

EN_B: You could say that. The second open question is about Lasertec. Many investors are very optimistic about its new product, the A200HiT.
