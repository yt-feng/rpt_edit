EN_A: What makes CBUs different from traditional BBUs?
EN_B: CBUs use supercapacitors, which are better for handling frequent, short power fluctuations, while BBUs handle longer outages. In AI data centers, where power demand is very unstable, CBUs could become the standard.

EN_B: They’re targeting 430 billion yen in AI-related sales for the industry segment by FY2028, and 500 billion yen by FY2030, with a margin around 20%. The key products are advanced substrate materials and conductive polymer capacitors, and they plan to double production capacity for both by 2030.
