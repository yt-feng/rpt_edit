## Superior TSR to support relative outperformance, upgrade to OW

We are upgrading BOCHK to OW from Neutral with a new Dec-27 PT of HK\$53.3. We revise up our earnings forecasts by 8/9% for FY26/27E and expect continued share price outperformance in the coming 6-12 months on the back of valuations remaining reasonable and superior total shareholder returns (TSR).

\- Resilient NIM migration to support NII. With a more hawkish tone from the FED interest rate meeting and rising forward yield curve at both SOFR and HIBOR in the coming 12-18 months, we now expect BOCHK to sustain its NIM at around $1.6\%$ (adjusted basis by including swaps revenues) throughout FY26 and FY27 compared to our previous forecast of a 3-4bps sequential YoY decline in the next two years. This would allow NII to grow at high single-digits in FY26.

\- Receding overhang from HK CRE exposure. Both the industry data and our discussions with management suggest no material new delinquent formation in the HK CRE portfolio in 1H26, thus we expect BOCHK to enjoy lower credit costs YoY in 1H26 and FY26. A sequential decline in credit costs would be an earnings tailwinds in FY26-28.

\- Shareholder return program likely a share price catalyst. The market has priced in expectations of TSR enhancement measures, via either a share buyback or special DPS, equivalent to a 1.0-1.5% yield on top of the ordinary DPS. Our analysis suggests BOCHK has the capacity to reward shareholders with up to a 3% yield in each year FY26-28 while still maintaining CET1 ratios superior to the industry average. Our base case is a special DPS of HK\$0.95 each year, adding another 2ppt to the TSR.

\- Upgrade to OW. Our new PT is based on 1.4x P/B and by moving forward our valuation base to Dec-27; our valuation multiple is justified by our $13\%$ ROE estimates for FY26 and FY27. The share price has outperformed HSI by $29\%$ YTD but current levels still imply potential upside to our fair value and close to $8\%$ TSR, so we expect continued share price outperformance in the coming 6-12 months. Risks to our call are a potential downgrade on Vanke-related lending that results in more meaningful cost increases or more adverse ODI rule changes that dampen market sentiment.
