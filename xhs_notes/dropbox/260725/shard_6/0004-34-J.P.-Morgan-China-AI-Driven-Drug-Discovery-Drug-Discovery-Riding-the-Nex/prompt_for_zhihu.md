## Drug Discovery Riding the Next Wave of AI; initiate on Insilico & XtalPi at OW

We initiate coverage of two China AI-driven drug discovery (AIDD) companies, Insilico (3696.HK) and XtalPi (2228.HK), with Overweight (OW) ratings. China's AIDD sector is shifting from business exploration to early commercialization, supported by rising business development (BD) activities and clinical catalysts. Our positive view on China AIDD rests on three pillars: 1) AIDD has materially improved drug R&D productivity and could further increase success probability through “dry-wet lab” closed loop platforms; 2) AIDD coupled with China's structural cost and R&D pace and regulatory advantages could make China a more attractive hotbed for applying AIDD compared to overseas; and 3) The above factors have driven Big Pharma deal flow, clinical milestones, and significant topline growth, providing early validation for AIDD companies. We see a multi-year rerating opportunity, with Insilico and XtalPi offering complementary investment theses. We like Insilico for its high-risk, high reward pipeline built through AIDD and like XtalPi for its diverse businesses led by AIDD services and robotics wet lab. We rate both Insilico and XtalPi OW with PTs of HK\$71 and HK\$10, respectively.

\- AIDD enables a measurable drug R&D unit-economics shift and provides real clinical promise to improve drug success probability. Closed-loop “dry lab + automated wet lab” platforms move discovery from trial-and-error to iterative, data-recycling execution that compounds a proprietary data flywheel, reducing early drug discovery cost and boosting efficiency. Beyond small molecule and antibody drugs, AIDD is capable of tackling complex and novel drug modalities. In addition, AI platforms are increasingly being integrated into clinical and regulatory workflows to de-risk late-stage drug development.
