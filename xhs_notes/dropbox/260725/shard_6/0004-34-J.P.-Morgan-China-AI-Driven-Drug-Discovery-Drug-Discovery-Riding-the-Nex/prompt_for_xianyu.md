## Drug Discovery Riding the Next Wave of AI; initiate on Insilico & XtalPi at OW

We initiate coverage of two China AI-driven drug discovery (AIDD) companies, Insilico (3696.HK) and XtalPi (2228.HK), with Overweight (OW) ratings.
