# DISCO 1Q27/3: Silicon Photonics emerges as new growth driver; lifting PT to ¥99,000

DISCO Q2 shipment guidance was strong with continued strength in HBM, as well as a new growth driver in Silicon Photonics. We reiterate our Outperform rating.

Strong Q2 shipment guidance. DISCO's 1Q FY26/3 shipment of ¥135.9bn (+12% QoQ) was slightly lower than expected from prelim due to some prototype equipments for R&D being recognized at parent level but not on consolidated basis. Q2 guidance shipment of ¥141bn (+4% QoQ) was above consensus of ¥131.1bn. By application, memory was in-line with guidance at 35% of shipment in Q1, and company expects memory strength to continue into Q2, with HBM driving the strength. OSAT in Q1 was stronger with 35% of total, vs. guidance of 30%, although Q2 was guided to decline, as the strong investment from China OSAT comes down.

Silicon Photonics (SiPh) emerges as one new growth drivers. While still smaller than HBM or CoWoS, SiPh is growing into Q2, and revenue could come from multiple streams: D2W hybrid bonding, laser semi, optical module/transceivers. On manufacturing support, while company continued to refuse to quantify how much of shipment it translates into, they expect the scheme to continue for the foreseeable future, likely with a similar scale as the currently dispatched \~100 personnel. Margins improving for consumables. Q1 GPM was 71.3% (vs. guidance \~69%), and OPM was 42.9% (vs. guidance 39.6%). Q2 GPM was guided to be -1%pt QoQ, likely due to the usual conservatism. Conversely, IR seemed to expect the improvement in consumables margin to continue into Q2. SG&A was guided to be slightly higher than ¥34bn, with OPM of 43.5% (vs. consensus 45.7%).

Outperform. We reflect Q1 earnings as well as Q2 guidance, and raise FY27 / FY28 revenue by 2.7% / 8.6% and bottom line by 5.6%/9.9%, to reflect improved overall semi market growth outlook as well as new drivers such as D2W hybrid bonding, V10 NAND and SiPh kicking in. Keeping the same 40x P/E multiple, we raise our TP to ¥99,000, with 43% upside. Maintain Outperform.
