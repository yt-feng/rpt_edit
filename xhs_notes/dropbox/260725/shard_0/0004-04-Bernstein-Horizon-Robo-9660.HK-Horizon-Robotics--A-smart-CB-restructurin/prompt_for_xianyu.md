# Horizon Robotics: A smart CB restructuring equals to 1% share buy back

Horizon announced a convertible bonds (CB) restructuring and expanded its Volkswagen (VW) partnership on L3/L4 automated driving on July 23, 2026. We see both events as positive, the CB restructuring avoided a potential 4.9% dilution and effectively served as 1% share buy back. The market traded -3.8% after the announcement which should be wrong (in our view); we consider this as a good opportunity to buy the dip. Maintain OP.

The restructuring of VW's CB avoids potential $4.9\%$ dilution and underscores Horizon's commitment to protecting shareholder value. Under the original structure, the c.716mn shares exceeding VW's $9.9\%$ ownership threshold would likely have been placed with financial investors, creating both dilution and substantial near-term selling pressure. Horizon has eliminated this overhang by issuing a new CB to replace the excess shares. VW also voluntarily extended the lock-up on its entire stake by 12 months, reinforcing the long-term partnership.

Replacing an interest-bearing CB with a zero-coupon CB saves USD 20Mn interest expenses, higher convertible price serves as an effective 1% share buy back. Early termination saves around USD 20Mn of interest expense. The new USD 450Mn CB carries zero coupon and zero yield, with a HKD5.55 conversion price at a 16.8% premium to the latest close price. Its potential 3.9% dilution remains below 4.9% issuance under the original structure, equivalent to 1% buyback. Horizon has therefore reduced financing costs, raised the conversion threshold and lowered potential dilution in a single transaction.
