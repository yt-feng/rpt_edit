1) Authorities have tightened oversight of outbound investment channels. Since late May, regulators have stepped up scrutiny of cross-border stock trading and other offshore investment channels, consistent with an effort to retain capital domestically and support local financial markets $^{1}$ . This follows the expansion of outbound investment regulations to explicitly cover individual investors for the first time, broadening the authorities' ability to monitor and manage capital outflows $^{2}$ .

2) New outbound investment regulations increase official control over overseas flows. The new framework gives policymakers greater discretion to slow, delay or reject large outbound transactions should they judge them to pose risks to capital flow stability or the balance of payments.

Taken together, these measures reinforce our view that policymakers are seeking to moderate the pace of CNY appreciation rather than engineer a weaker currency.
