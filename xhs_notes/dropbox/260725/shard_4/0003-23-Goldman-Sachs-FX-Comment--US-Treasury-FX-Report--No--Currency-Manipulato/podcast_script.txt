ZH_A: 最近美国财政部发布了半年度外汇报告，这份报告和我们普通投资者有关系吗？
ZH_B: 其实关系很大。这份报告直接影响市场对汇率干预风险的判断，也间接影响人民币、日元这些主要货币的走势。
ZH_A: 所以这期节目，我们想聊清楚一个问题：这份报告到底说了什么，又没说什么？
ZH_B: 对。报告的核心结论是：没有国家被列为“汇率操纵国”，监控名单也没新增。但真正的看点，藏在细节里。
ZH_A: 那我们先从最直观的结论说起。没有新增操纵国，市场是不是可以松一口气了？
ZH_B: 短期看是这样。但你要注意，报告对中国的措辞有明显软化。比如之前强调人民币要“及时有序升值”，这次改成了“逐步且可控的升值”。
ZH_A: 这意味着什么？
ZH_B: 意思是美国认可了中国近期让人民币升值的动作，但同时也暗示——你做得还不够，还需要继续。
ZH_A: 那对其他经济体呢？比如日本、韩国，报告里有没有什么新线索？
ZH_B: 报告特别提到日本和韩国面临“超常的贬值压力”，所以没有批评他们干预汇市。但报告也首次详细分析了日本GPIF和韩国NPS的操作。
ZH_A: 所以美国在悄悄扩大审查范围？
ZH_B: 没错。报告虽然没有据此作出负面结论，但框架已经铺开了。未来如果条件变化，这些都可能成为施压的工具。
ZH_A: 那这份报告里，有没有哪些问题是没有完全说清楚的？
ZH_B: 有两个关键悬念。第一，报告删除了关于中国主权财富基金操作的章节，但理由只是“不积极交易外汇”，这并不代表未来不会重新审查。
ZH_A: 那第二个悬念呢？

ZH_A: 所以整体上，你对这份报告的判断是什么？
ZH_B: 我觉得是“温和但谨慎的积极”。美元贬值让报告语气更温和，但美国仍然关注“大规模且持续”的失衡。更多细节，完整报告里会继续展开。

EN_A: So the US Treasury just released its semi-annual FX report. What’s the one thing we should really take away from it?
EN_B: The headline is that no country was labeled a currency manipulator. But the real story is in the tone shift, especially around China.
EN_A: Let’s start with China. What changed?
EN_B: The language softened. Earlier reports stressed that China should let the yuan appreciate in a timely and orderly way. This time, the Treasury acknowledged that China has allowed a gradual and managed appreciation.
EN_A: So what does that mean in practice?
EN_B: It means the US is giving China some credit for recent moves, but it’s also saying: you need to go further. The report still notes a lack of transparency and large-scale non-market support for manufacturing.
EN_A: What about other countries like Japan or Korea?
EN_B: The report recognized that both faced extraordinary depreciation pressure, so it didn’t criticize their FX intervention. But for the first time, it detailed the activities of Japan’s GPIF and Korea’s NPS.
EN_A: So the scope of scrutiny is widening?
EN_B: Exactly. The Treasury hasn’t drawn negative conclusions yet, but it’s laying the groundwork. The framework is now broader than just the three traditional criteria.
EN_A: Are there any questions the report leaves open?
EN_B: Two big ones. First, it dropped the section on China’s sovereign wealth fund, but only because it doesn’t actively trade FX. That doesn’t mean it’s off the radar.
EN_A: And the second?
EN_B: The report didn’t include the annex on FX policy transparency that was in the January version. In theory, more transparency could discourage intervention, but historically, it hasn’t made a big difference. The report doesn’t fully address that.
EN_A: So what’s your overall read?
EN_B: Cautiously constructive. The dollar’s decline made the tone more positive, but the Treasury remains focused on large and persistent imbalances. For the full picture, you really need to read the complete report.
