【目标】

- 风格：稳重、专业、克制、有洞察，像咨询公司合伙人写给专业读者/产业决策者的报告导读。
- 长度：约 1200 字，整体以短导读为主，通常控制在 900-1200 个中文字符。
- 不要使用 emoji。
- 可以基于报告内容做适度发散，但必须是从原文逻辑推出的判断，不要编造数据、公司动作或引用。

【金字塔原理写作原则】
1. 结论先行：文章开头先回答“这份报告最值得看的判断是什么”，而不是介绍背景。
2. 统领思想：全文只能服务一个主判断，避免变成摘要合集。
3. 纵向回答：每一层都要回答上一层提出的“为什么”或“所以呢”。
4. 横向 MECE：每个一级小节必须彼此独立、共同支撑主判断，避免重叠。
5. Synthesis over summary：不要复述报告段落，要提炼“这些事实合在一起意味着什么”。
6. So what：每个小节末尾必须落到对行业、公司、竞争格局、市场定价或读者观察框架的含义。
7. 信息型小标题：所有 `##` 小标题必须是完整、中性的事实句，不能是目录标签，也不能评价好坏或制造冲突。

## Resilient S/4 HANA product cycle despite uncertain macro; CCB reiterated to slightly decelerate in FY26

At 2Q26, SAP reported CCB growth of 26% yoy ex-fx (c.25% organic), ahead of GSe and consensus expectations of 25%/24%, and sequentially steady despite elevated macro uncertainty, driven by continued strength in the Cloud ERP Suite. Management also highlighted momentum across its Autonomous Suite and Business AI Platform, resulting in improved pipeline coverage post SAPPHIRE.

Looking ahead, management reiterated its guidance on top-line metrics and continues to expect a slight deceleration in CCB through FY26, noting that the range of outcomes for H2 remains wide given the volatile backdrop. That said, management noted the post-SAPPHIRE pipeline is tracking better than expected with improved coverage vs the prior year, and management expressed confidence in achieving its FY guidance as predictability on cloud revenue improves seven months into the year. We remain prudent, modelling a c.200bps deceleration in our ex-fx CCB growth, expecting c.22% organic growth ex-fx. On cloud revenues, we modestly raise our FY26 forecast to 24.4% yoy ex-fx (vs. 24.0% previously), reflecting better licences and subscription revenue.

Non-IFRS EBIT came in modestly below expectations (2%/5% below GSe/consensus), growing 9% yoy ex-fx. The sequential deceleration was driven by lower cloud growth, higher-than-expected SBC (reversing the low SBC in 1Q), elevated R&D investment partly reflecting targeted AI hires at higher per-FTE costs, S&M spend around the Autonomous Enterprise launch, and the dilutive impact of Reltio. SAP also highlighted efficiency gains from internal AI adoption and plans to slow hiring over the next 12 months as it works towards its mid-term target of c.€2bn in efficiency gains. Management expects H2 operating profit growth to track similarly to the mid-teens growth delivered in H1 on a combined basis.

SAP updated its FY26 operating profit outlook to €11.8–12.2bn (previously €11.9–12.3bn), reflecting the dilutive impact of the Dremio and Prior Labs acquisitions (c.€100mn in H2). We modestly lower our Non-IFRS (incl. SBC) EBIT growth to 14.5% yoy ex-fx (vs. 14.8% previously) for FY26, landing just below the midpoint of guidance, and continue to expect double-digit ex-fx EBIT growth over the medium term (GSe forecasting c.80–180 bps margin expansion over FY26–30E).
