# 2Q26 Results Beat, Lifting TP to Rmb45.0 on Strong Top Line and Further GPM Expansion

## CITI'S TAKE

Global leading relay maker Hongfa reported better-than-expected 2Q26 results. While 2Q26 net profit of Rmb672mn (+21% YoY) was only 2% ahead of Bloomberg consensus, revenue of Rmb5.9bn (+36% YoY) and GPM of 33.2% (-1.5pp) beat consensus by 4% and 2.3pp, respectively, driven by strong demand and ASP hike. Mgmt. commented on the results briefing that 1) strong demand is across the board due to not only industry demand growth but also Hongfa's market share gain and 2) more importantly, GPM could recover to 3Q25 level (\~35%) given ongoing ASP hikes and silver price decline. We therefore raise our 2026/27E earnings forecasts by 5%/11% and lift our TP by \~5% to Rmb45.0 as we increase revenue and GPM estimates. Amid the volatile market, Hongfa remains one of our top picks given its strong earnings visibility and potential upsides from 800V AIDC relays.

1H26 revenue by product – NEV HVDC relay grew by 45% YoY to Rmb3.6bn in 1H26 due to market share gain; home appliance relay also increased by 40% YoY to Rmb2.3bn, helped by ASP hikes which contributed 20pp revenue increase; auto relay grew by 20% YoY to Rmb1.4bn due to ASP hike starting from 2Q26 while power relay was also up 27% YoY due to recovering demand from Europe and China national grid; new energy relay rose by 60% YoY to Rmb1.0bn; and industrial and communication relay increased by 50% and 20% YoY driven by demand recovery. Among new businesses, PDU/BDU was the best performing one with >100% YoY revenue growth in 1H26 (Figure 2).

2H26 outlook – Mgmt. guided a strong demand outlook given 3-6 months order visibility and record high order book. GPM outlook was also quite positive as Hongfa will continue to raise ASP for certain products due to tight supply while silver price has declined by \~20% since 3Q26. (Figure 3) Regarding AIDC, mgmt. indicated that the business opportunity would be bigger than their previous expectation and 800V relay products are likely to be shipped to main customers like Delta Electronics (2308.TW) or Lite-On (2301.TW) in 4Q26 at earliest.

Valuation – Our new TP of Rmb45.0 is based on \~32x 2026E P/E, set at +1.0x SD to reflect Hongfa's faster earnings growth driven by strong demand and ASP hikes.
