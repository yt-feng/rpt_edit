## GSK and AZ 2Q26 results: Read-through to Hansoh, Hengrui and Kelun Biotech

GSK and AstraZeneca's 2Q26 results reinforced their commitment to several therapeutic modalities where Chinese pharma/biotech companies have out-licensed. Overall, we view these updates as another strong validation of China's innovation capabilities. More importantly, they demonstrate that multinational companies are not only in-licensing Chinese assets, but are also committing substantial late-stage development resources to maximize their global commercial potential.

\- For Hansoh, GSK's intention to launch multiple Ph3 studies for its B7-H3 and B7-H4 ADC portfolio signals increasing strategic confidence in both programs.
