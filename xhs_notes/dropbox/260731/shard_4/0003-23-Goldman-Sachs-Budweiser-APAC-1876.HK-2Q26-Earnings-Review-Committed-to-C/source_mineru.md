Bud APAC reported 2Q26 results with better-than-feared sales/EBITDA and reported NP beating VA consensus on ASP/ETR/D&A saving on 30 July, and notably, with ASP positive at $2\%$ vs. volume decline at $-4.1\%$ yoy at group level (strong India and South Korea outperforming industry, partially offsetting China decline). We attended the earnings call today and will host an NDR next week. The management team mentioned key priorities for 2026E/2H26E, with stabilizing China volume growth as well as recovering leading market share position as the top priority while acknowledging a still challenging macro backdrop. The company will continue to invest in brand equity/premiumization/healthy products/in-home and O2O channels (strong DD% growth in 2Q26) expansion, and new consumption occasions with more consumer interactions, amid volatility in macro/weather/consumer sentiments. Mgmt team expects 2026 China recovery to still take time: 1) the company was cautious on near-term demand recovery, noting that industry volume still remained soft in Jul with limited improvement for on-trade beer consumption in 2Q26, and mgmt team remains cautious for 3Q26 which will likely still be focusing on channel inventory optimization; 2) the company will keep investing in brand equity in China for in-home/O2O channels and ongoing premiumization with stronger focus on execution. O2O growing at strong DD% in 2Q26 driven by premium SKUs and packaging innovation, and 3) the company will also aim to maintain disciplined channel inventory, with likely a bit more adjustment into 2H26 (tier 1/ tier 2 inventory level still see room to optimization per mgmt). Therefore, we also expect profitability to see some pressure in 2H in China given 1) raw material costs headwinds to kick in going into 2H26/2027 given company's 1-yr hedging cycle, especially Aluminum (Aluminum price up by $19\%$ yoy in 1H26 per our cost tracker, July TD $+23\%$ vs. 2025 Ave, or up $11\%$ yoy) despite some cost savings in Barely. 2) mix shift to O2O

For Korea and India, the company is confident on its competitive advantages in both market with Korea driven by innovation/SKU expansion. India will be still driven by premiumization and penetration.

For China, we now look for -3.5% organic sales decline in FY26 driven by -5%/1.5% volume/ASP due to continued weak momentum into Jul while premiumization still intact. We look for -12.1% organic EBITDA decline in China in 2026 with slightly yoy lower margin at 31.4% mainly on higher commercial investment in O2O/in-home channels and raw material cost headwinds to kick in since 3Q26. Into 3Q26, we expect volume to still under pressure at -8% yoy decline, with 2.8% ASP growth but a -13% EBITDA decline on continued investment, followed by a -1%/5%/8% volume/ASP/EBITDA growth in 4Q26 lapping easy comp. For India which is about 10% of AP West volume on GSE, we expect continued strong momentum with sufficient room for revenue growth and EBITDA margin expansion. For AP East/South Korea, we expect EBITDA growth of c.3.5% yoy with 0.1% ASP growth (both organic) for AP East in 2026. We expect -2% volume decline in 3Q26, with -1% ASP on a high comp, followed by a 1.3%/-0.6%/+4% volume/ASP/EBITDA 4Q26. At the Group level, we look for -1.9% sales decline driven by -4.1% volume decline and 2.3% ASP increase in 3Q26 with organic EBITDA decline of 8.9%, due to mix upgrade in China/India while cautious on volume recovery pace.

Dividend: The company is noting currently still low visibility on payout ratio % target for the full-year, with key priorities on cash flow deployment toward investments in restoring volume growth. That said, the company still remains committed to shareholder returns in the long term.

Estimates and TP: We revise down our 2026E-28E sales estimates by within 1% mainly on better ASP trend in APW likely on better-than-feared O2O/off-trade promotions and more favorable brand mix, offset by softer volume on continued de-stocking. Our NP forecast is up 0.1-6% mainly reflecting 2Q26 NP beat/lower ETR/D&A expense, as well as better ASP assumption for China. We currently expect US\$5.83 bn/US\$1.51bn/US\$663mn sales/normalized EBITDA/recurring NPAT in 2026E. The stock is trading at c.19x/17x 2026/2027 P/E; and trading at 2026/27E 6.2x/5.6x EV/EBITDA vs. global peers average at 9x/8x, with FCF yield at 5.2%/6.4% in 2026E/2027E. Our 12-m TP is up to HK\$7.7 vs. HK\$7.6 prior, with other assumptions unchanged.

## GS Factor Profile

The GS Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows:

Growth is based on a stock's forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock's forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock's forward-looking P/E, P/B, price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile).

Financial Returns and Multiple use the GS analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics).

For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.

## M&A Rank

Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
