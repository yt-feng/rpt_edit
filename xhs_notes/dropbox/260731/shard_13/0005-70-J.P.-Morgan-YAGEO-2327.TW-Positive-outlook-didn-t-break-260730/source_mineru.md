## YAGEO

Positive outlook didn't break

\- Our view on the 3Q guidance. While YAGEO delivered strong 2Q rev growth and decent margins (see below) in 2Q, the 3Q rev outlook of “moderate growth” (which we believe means \~5% up sequentially) was meaningfully below the street expectations of 11-12% rev growth q-o-q. Interestingly, YAGEO suggests that the 3Q UTR will rise to the 90-95% level for both commodity and premium segments, a bit higher vs. 80-85% in 2Q. Some of the production could be geared toward inventory rebuilding (2Q inventory days dropped to \~110 days from its typical \~120). While we largely keep our original 3Q rev forecast of \~NT\$46bn (which implies 5% growth q-o-q), we see decent upside risks in guidance/JPMe. We still see a decent chance of price increases taking place across many customers/products throughout 3Q. Meanwhile, we also don’t rule out that YAGEO is monitoring shipments to certain channels. In recent months, the company has quickly gained a more prominent role in the Greater China general IT market (as North Asian peers are shifting focus to AI), which should ensure the longevity of the ongoing supply/demand balance.

\- Slightly lift PT to NT\$1,030; positive outlook didn't break. The shares of many Asian passive names saw +50% corrections in the region after the June peak. While some investors resetting from extremely bullish pricing expectations has been a key reason, some negative pricing data points from Greater China also contributed to the share weakness (but note this applies to secondary/resell markets, not directly linked to YAGEO and other passive component makers). We believe our current pricing assumptions of 5-10% hikes in 2026/10-20% hikes in 2027 (which were previously deemed conservative by many investors back in June) are still holding up well, as we believe the long-term thesis of AI creating long-term supply tightness for the passive industry is still holding up well. We expect more newflows on price hikes to take place in the industry in the coming weeks. In our view, the overflow orders/supply chain shift will lift the pricing for YAGEO in the foreseeable future. So investors should accumulate YAGEO, which is trading at 16x 2027e EPS, after the likely weakness post results.

## Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation

## Investment Thesis

We are OW on YAGEO, given its leading position in tantalum capacitors after acquiring Kemet and its strong presence in MLCCs/R-Chips, a market that has consolidated over time with disciplined supply expansion. We believe less exposure to commodity-type products can help smooth the revenue fluctuations, while increasing demand for tantalum capacitors can support YAGEO's future top- and bottom-line growth.

## Valuation

Our Dec-26 PT of NT\$1,030 is based on 26x 2027/28E EPS. 26x is beyond the 1.5-STD above-average forward P/E at which the company has traded since 2021. We believe the multiple is justified as passive components enter a demand-driven upcycle, which is more structural and healthy versus the previous cycle in 2018-19. We believe YAGEO is one of the key beneficiaries of this cycle, with 1) dominance in high-end passives (global leader in Tantalum), and 2) MLCC price hikes starting from the lower-end segment.

# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

YAGEO (Overweight; Price Target: NT\$1,030.00)

## Investment Thesis

We are OW on YAGEO, given its leading position in tantalum capacitors after acquiring Kemet and its strong presence in MLCCs/R-Chips, a market that has consolidated over time with disciplined supply expansion. We believe less exposure to commodity-type products can help smooth the revenue fluctuations, while increasing demand for tantalum capacitors can support YAGEO's future top- and bottom-line growth.

## Valuation

Our Dec-26 PT of NT\$1,030 is based on 26x 2027/28E EPS. 26x is beyond the 1.5-STD above-average forward P/E at which the company has traded since 2021. We believe the multiple is justified as passive components enter a demand-driven upcycle, which is more structural and healthy versus the previous cycle in 2018-19. We believe YAGEO is one of the key beneficiaries of this cycle, with 1) dominance in high-end passives (global leader in Tantalum), and 2) MLCC price hikes starting from the lower-end segment.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Downside risks include MLCC/R-Chip price reversals (which could be driven by a slower pace of auto electrification, inventory corrections, etc.) and execution issues during mergers.
