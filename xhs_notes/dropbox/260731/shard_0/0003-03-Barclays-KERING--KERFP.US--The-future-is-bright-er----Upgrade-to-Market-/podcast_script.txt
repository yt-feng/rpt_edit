ZH_A: 最近开云集团成了信用债市场的一个热点，一份外资投行报告刚刚把它的评级从低配上调到了标配。今天我们就来聊聊，这家拥有古驰的奢侈品巨头，到底发生了什么变化。

ZH_B: 核心一句话，就是这家公司最坏的时候可能已经过去了。报告上调评级，是因为它看到了几个非常明确的信号，说明开云的业务正在企稳，而且债务压力也大幅减轻了。

ZH_A: 债务压力这个词，在过去两年一直是悬在开云头上的剑。这份报告说压力减轻了，依据是什么？

ZH_B: 最直接的证据是净债务从去年底的80亿欧元，大幅降到了今年上半年的33亿欧元。这个降幅非常惊人，主要靠两笔钱，一是卖掉美妆部门拿到了40亿欧元现金，二是处置房地产回笼了5亿欧元。

ZH_A: 卖美妆部门，就是卖掉Creed香水那个业务吧？这个交易对市场来说，信号意义是不是大于现金本身？

ZH_B: 对，现金是一方面，但更重要的是它消除了一个很大的不确定性。原来少数股东有一个卖出期权，可能迫使他们提前买断华伦天奴的股权，现在这个期限往后推了，财务压力就小了很多，管理层可以专心做古驰的复兴。

ZH_A: 那古驰的复兴，这份报告给了什么新证据？

ZH_B: 报告里提到一个非常关键的细节，就是古驰在第二季度的有机增速是负2%，听着还是负的，但市场预期是负4.7%。这算是一个明显的超预期。更重要的是管理层说皮具品类恢复了增长，这是品牌重新吸引消费者的一个早期信号。

ZH_A: 所以不光是财务数字在变好，经营层面也有拐点的迹象了？

ZH_B: 是的，报告用了“好消息接二连三”这个说法。除了古驰的改善，集团整体营业利润率也在提升，从上半年的数据看，比去年同期高了40个基点，而且环比改善更明显，比去年下半年提升了300个基点。

ZH_A: 利润率提升300个基点，这幅度不小，主要靠什么？

ZH_B: 报告说主要是成本控制，营业费用下降了5%。另外珠宝业务也表现强劲，有机增速达到20%。这些加在一起，让管理层在资本市场日上提出的那个利润率翻倍目标，看起来不再那么遥不可及了。

ZH_A: 说到目标，市场之前对那个目标是很怀疑的。现在报告的态度变了，它怎么看未来几年的盈利路径？

ZH_B: 它的股票同事现在预期，到2029财年，营业利润率能从现在的11%左右翻倍到22%以上，比管理层自己定的目标还提前了一年。而且他们对未来两年的盈利预测，比市场共识要高出3%到14%。

ZH_A: 从债务角度看，评级机构的态度也变了吗？

ZH_B: 标普在去年11月已经把展望从负面调回稳定了，这个时间点就在卖掉美妆部门之后。标普估算，调整后的债务对EBITDA比率，会从2025年年底的4到4.5倍，降到2026年的3倍左右。

ZH_A: 那评级上调的可能性呢？报告里有提到吗？

ZH_B: 提到了，标普说如果开云能把调整后的债务对EBITDA持续压在2.5倍以下，而且古驰的复兴有清晰进展，就可能上调评级。这份报告认为，考虑到现在的经营势头，这个风险收益比在往上倾斜。

ZH_A: 既然基本面在好转，那在债券投资上，这份报告有什么具体的建议？

ZH_B: 它给了六个交易建议，包括三笔直接买入，两笔期限延伸交易，还有一个是换券。整体思路是偏好高票息的债券，因为它觉得公司手里现金多，有可能会发起现金要约收购来降低融资成本。

ZH_A: 高票息债券，就是那些发行时利率比较高的债券吧？为什么偏好它们？

ZH_B: 对，因为如果公司真的做要约收购，通常会优先处理这些成本高的债务。所以这些债券的价格有额外的支撑。报告也提到，如果做期限延伸，它倾向于在收益率曲线长端找机会，因为那里相对利差更有吸引力。

ZH_A: 具体到个券，它最看好哪几个？

ZH_B: 直接买入的包括英镑计价的5%票息2032年到期，以及欧元计价的3.875% 2035年和3.625% 2036年。另外它建议把2031年的债换成2033年的，把2032年的换成2034年的，都是往长端延伸，获得更高的利差。

ZH_A: 换券能多拿多少利差？

ZH_B: 比如把2031换成2033，现金价格上能多拿大约1.7个点，利差上多拿18个基点。这个价差水平比过去一年的平均值要宽，所以报告觉得这个交易有性价比。

ZH_A: 还有一个换券是把另一家奢侈品公司的债换成开云，这个逻辑是什么？

ZH_B: 这个比较有意思，是把那家公司的2036年债换成开云的2033年债，期限缩短了三年，利差还能多拿24个基点。报告认为，虽然两家公司评级有差异，但开云的风险收益比更好，尤其是如果评级真的上调，收益会更大。

ZH_A: 除了开云本身，这份报告还下调了整个欧洲高等级零售板块的评级，这是为什么？

ZH_B: 这个变化很有意思。之前上调板块是因为看好开云，加上亚马逊进入了欧元投资级市场。但现在分析师把亚马逊降到了低配，觉得它有发行压力。亚马逊在这个板块里权重很大，占到25%，所以它一降，整个板块的吸引力就下降了。

ZH_A: 亚马逊对板块的影响有这么大？

ZH_B: 报告算了笔账，如果把亚马逊剔除，欧洲零售板块的利差今年基本没怎么动，但算上它，整个板块的利差被拉宽了将近7个基点。而且亚马逊还拉长了板块的平均久期，所以它一弱，板块的配置价值就明显下降。

ZH_A: 所以这份报告的结论是，开云在变好，但板块整体在变差？

ZH_B: 对，这就是一个很典型的个股和板块分化。开云从低配升到标配，但板块从标配降到低配。报告的逻辑是，开云自己的风险收益比在改善，但亚马逊带来的板块性拖累，让其他名字的吸引力没那么强了。

ZH_A: 报告里提到信用违约互换，就是CDS，它怎么看开云现在的CDS价格？

ZH_B: 它觉得五年期CDS现在大概在57.5个基点，处于过去一年的低位，这个定价算是合理的。因为好消息已经反映进去了，除非古驰的复兴出现更明确的加速，否则CDS进一步大幅收窄的空间可能有限。

ZH_A: 有一个点我很好奇，报告提到管理层说了一个杠杆率目标区间，但没有给时间表，这个怎么理解？

ZH_B: 对，管理层说的是希望把杠杆率降到1到1.5倍，但没说什么时候实现。报告推算了一下，如果按标普的算法，这个目标对应的就是2.5到3倍，正好落在BBB+评级区间里。所以这更像是一个方向性的承诺，而不是一个近期要达成的硬指标。

ZH_A: 这份报告里有没有提到什么风险，或者它承认自己之前判断错的地方？

ZH_B: 有的，它很坦诚地说，之前对古驰的复兴持谨慎态度，觉得光靠运营重整可能不够。但上半年财报出来后，它承认自己被数据说服了，尤其是利润率改善的幅度，还有新系列如Borsetto和Paparazzo带来的品牌热度，确实超出了预期。

ZH_A: 那还有什么是这份报告没有完全展开，需要读者自己去完整报告里找答案的？

ZH_A: 这两个点确实值得关注。如果Demna的首秀能带来超预期的反响，那对古驰的复兴故事会是很大的加分项。

EN_A: So Kering has been one of the most watched names in the credit market, and a fresh report from a global investment bank just upgraded it from underweight to market weight. Today we are going to talk about what actually changed for the owner of Gucci.

EN_B: The one-line summary is that the worst is probably over. The upgrade is based on a few very clear signals that the business is stabilising and the debt pressure has eased significantly.

EN_A: Debt pressure has been the sword hanging over this company for the past two years. What exactly made the report think that pressure is gone?

EN_B: The most direct evidence is that net debt dropped from 8 billion euros at the end of last year to 3.3 billion in the first half of this year. That is a massive reduction. It came from two main sources, the sale of the beauty division which brought in 4 billion in cash, and real estate disposals which added another 500 million.

EN_A: The beauty division sale, that is the one with Creed perfume, right? Is the signal bigger than the cash itself?

EN_B: Exactly. The cash is one thing, but more importantly it removed a big overhang. There was a put option that could have forced them to buy out Valentino early. Now that timeline has been pushed out, so the financial pressure is much lower and management can focus on the Gucci turnaround.

EN_A: And on the Gucci turnaround, what new evidence does the report point to?

EN_B: There is one key detail. Gucci's organic growth in the second quarter was minus 2 percent year on year. That is still negative, but the market was expecting minus 4.7 percent. EN_A: A 300 basis point improvement sequentially, that is a big move. What drove it?

EN_B: The report says it was mostly cost control, with operating expenses down 5 percent. And the jewelry business was also very strong, with organic growth of 20 percent. All of that together makes the margin doubling target from the capital markets day look a lot more achievable than it did before.

EN_A: The market was very sceptical of that target before. Now the report seems to have changed its mind. How does it see the earnings path going forward?

EN_B: Its equity colleagues now expect the operating margin to double from around 11 percent to above 22 percent by fiscal 2029, which is one year ahead of the company's own target. And their earnings forecasts for the next two years are 3 to 14 percent above consensus.

EN_A: On the debt side, have the rating agencies also changed their view?

EN_B: S&P moved the outlook back to stable from negative back in November. That was right after the beauty sale. They estimate that adjusted debt to EBITDA will improve from a peak of around 4 to 4.5 times at the end of 2025 to about 3 times in 2026.

EN_A: What about the possibility of an actual upgrade?

EN_B: The report mentions that S&P has laid out the criteria. If Kering can keep adjusted debt to EBITDA sustainably below 2.5 times and show clear progress on the Gucci turnaround, an upgrade is possible. The report thinks the balance of risk is tilted to the upside here.

EN_A: So if the fundamentals are improving, what does the report recommend on the bond side?

EN_B: It gives six trade ideas. Three outright buys, two extension trades, and one switch. The overall theme is a preference for higher coupon notes, because with that much cash on the balance sheet, the company could look to do tender offers to reduce its cost of debt.

EN_A: Higher coupon notes, those are the bonds issued when rates were higher, right? Why prefer them?

EN_B: Right. Because if the company does a tender, it usually targets the most expensive debt first. So those bonds have extra support. The report also says that if you are extending duration, the long end of the curve looks more attractive on a relative value basis.

EN_A: Which specific bonds does it like?

EN_B: For outright buys, it likes the sterling 5 percent notes due 2032, and the euro 3.875 percent notes due 2035, and the 3.625 percent notes due 2036. It also suggests switching out of the 2031s into the 2033s, and the 2032s into the 2034s, which are both extensions to pick up spread.

EN_A: How much spread do you pick up on those switches?

EN_A: There is also a switch involving another luxury company's bonds. What is the logic there?

EN_B: This one is interesting. It is switching out of that company's 2036 bonds into Kering's 2033s. You reduce duration by three years and still pick up 24 basis points in spread. The report thinks that despite the rating difference, the risk reward is better on Kering, especially if there is positive rating action.

EN_A: Does Amazon really have that much influence on the sector?

Amazon also increases the average duration of the sector. So when the view on Amazon deteriorates, the sector level call has to change.

EN_A: So the conclusion is that Kering is getting better, but the sector is getting worse?

EN_B: Exactly. It is a classic case of stock versus sector divergence. Kering goes from underweight to market weight, but the sector goes from market weight to underweight. The report's logic is that Kering's own risk reward is improving, but Amazon is a drag on the sector that makes other names less compelling.
