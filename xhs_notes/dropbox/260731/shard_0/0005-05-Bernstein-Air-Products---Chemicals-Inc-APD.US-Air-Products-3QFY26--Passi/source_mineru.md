## Air Products 3QFY26: Passing the duck test

Air Products again delivered a solid update (Quick Take: Air Products 3QFY26 - Another beat and raise quarter). More of these quarters would go some way to fulfilling our thesis set out in our recent deep dive: Air Products: Sustainable above-consensus EPS growth, even including NEOM. The quarter provides more evidence that Air Products is focusing on being an industrial gas company, and we continue to struggle to explain the historically large valuation gap versus industrial gas peers. We believe Air Products passes the “duck test”, but for industrial gas companies - if it mostly sells industrial gas and performs like an industrial gas company, then it probably is an industrial gas company. And should be valued like one. Outperform.

No NEOM headwind in expected in FY2027. As part of our deep dive, we made a proprietary NEOM model, which remains available to clients on request. We make minor updates to bring the model in line with management guidance for FY27, of no major financial impact, which we have done by slowing the full commissioning of the facility, and therefore the project finance ramp. Therefore, our FY27 EPS forecast increases, and this addresses part of the investor bear case.

The pace of EPS growth may decline slightly yoy in FY27, but we still see an opportunity for compounding. APD has had some assistance from China gasification projects in delivering FY26's likely 11-12% Adj. EPS growth. The volume environment will likely remain highly uncertain. The helium headwind will likely persist (but declining) until a large US contract rolls over next February. But none of these things take away from the productivity opportunity, or the ongoing ramp up of more electronics lines in Asia, or the ability to use pricing, particularly in the space business when a key customer repairs their launch pad. However, part of being an industrial gas company is guiding conservatively, so in order to pass the duck test investors may need to prepare for an opening \~6-9% yoy Adj. EPS FY27 guidance range. Which hopefully improves as the business executes.

## Investment Implications

We rate Air Products Outperform. We raise both our FY26E and FY27E EPS by 1%. We roll our valuation framework from a FY26 P/E to a NTM P/E incorporating 3 months of FY26 and 9 months of FY27 EPS. On an unchanged multiple of 26x NTM P/E, our price target increases to \$373 from \$345.

## Air Products & Chemicals Inc

Downside – Setbacks in APD's mega-projects strategy continue notably in terms of uncertainties associated with the market development, a supportive regulatory environment, and a continued lack of off-take customers for the key green NEOM and Louisiana blue hydrogen projects. Any fresh setback are likely to have a major impact on the share price (as seen in 2020/21 and 2023/24) and could see the shares down.
