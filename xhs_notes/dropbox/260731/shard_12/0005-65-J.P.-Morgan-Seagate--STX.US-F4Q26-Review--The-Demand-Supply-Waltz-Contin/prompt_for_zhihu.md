## F4Q26 Review: The Demand-Supply Waltz Continues; Raise Estimates & PT Higher

Seagate once again delivered a clear beat-and-raise, one that looks more like a continuation of the durable setup we saw in F3Q26, with results coming in ahead of expectations driven in large part by upside to pricing. On a per-exabyte basis, pricing grew +11% y/y (vs. +4% y/y in F3Q26), with an implied acceleration to at least +20% y/y in F1Q27. Beneath the aggregate trends, management pointed to data center pricing tailwinds from a widening demand-supply gap, while the robust tailwinds in the Edge IoT business reflect opportunistic pricing against a backdrop of very strong price increases for competitors' NAND-based storage products. These increases supported incremental gross margins of 83%+ y/y in F4Q26, along with implied guidance for 88%+ incremental gross margins in F1Q27. Stepping away from pricing, management characterized demand as structurally strengthening, led by sustained cloud buildouts and AI-driven incremental data creation and retention on top of already data-intensive workloads (including video) with no evidence of a near-term slowdown and customer planning horizons extending multiple years, supporting guidance for accelerating revenue growth into FY27. Supply, meanwhile, continues to lag this robust demand, as evident in the vast majority of Nearline exabyte supply already being allocated into CY28 under long-term agreements, with customers actively extending their planning horizons into CY29 and beyond.
