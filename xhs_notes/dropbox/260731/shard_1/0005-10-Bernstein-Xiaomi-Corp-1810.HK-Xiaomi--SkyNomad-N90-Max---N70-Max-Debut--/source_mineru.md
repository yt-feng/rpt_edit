# Xiaomi: SkyNomad N90 Max & N70 Max Debut - Our thoughts

Xiaomi unveiled its SkyNomad EREV SUV series earlier today, with the N90 and N70 as its first models. Both vehicles were priced below expectations, which could support stronger demand. We estimate N90 monthly deliveries of over 10k units, and N70 at c.15k units.

Highly competitive pre-sale price: Full-size 7-seater SUV at RMB 299.9k only. The N90 is a full-size 7-seater SUV, offering up to 464km electric range and 1,705km combined range. Beyond range, Xiaomi emphasized spaciousness and flexible cabin configurations for the N90. For e.g., the third row seats can be folded down, and the front seats can rotate 180 degrees to face the second row to create a “small living room.” While the practical use cases for some of these features may be niche, we are impressed by the vehicle's pricing. The N90 Max carries a pre-sale price of RMB299.9k. Given recent industry practice, final launch pricing could come in RMB10k or more below the pre-sale level, while lower-spec trims should be available at even more attractive price points. The N70 is a mid-large 5-seater EREV SUV, offering an electric range of up to 505km and has a pre-sale price of RMB259.9k.

We believe SkyNomad pose the greatest competitive threat to Li Auto and AITO, whose EREV SUVs currently dominate the premium family SUV segment. Xiaomi's pricing is particularly disruptive. For comparison, compared to N90 that is priced at RMB 299.9k, the Li L9 and AITO M9 EREV start at RMB459.8k and RMB479.8k, respectively.
