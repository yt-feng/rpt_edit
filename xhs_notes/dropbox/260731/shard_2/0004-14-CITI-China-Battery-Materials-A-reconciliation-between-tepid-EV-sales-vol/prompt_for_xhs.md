# China Battery Materials

A reconciliation between tepid EV sales vol and strong battery installation

## CITI'S TAKE

Monthly battery installation data pack from Real Lithium suggests that 1H26 vehicle battery installation in China was +33% YoY to \~451.6 GWh. Within that, battery installation of EV-PV / PHEV-PV / commercial vehicle was up by 20%, 27%, and 94% YoY respectively. We reconcile the tepid EV sales vol (+10% YoY) and strong battery installation (+33% YoY) in 1H26, and believe it's attributable to (1) the continuing battery upsizing trend across the board (including both passenger vehicle and commercial vehicle), (2) rising commercial vehicle, and (3) higher exposure to the high-end class passenger vehicle. CATL continues to be the market leader and kept taking market share during the period, whereas its market share was up by 3ppt YoY to 45% during 6M26. We maintain our near-term bullish call on lithium carbonate price into Aug/Sep peak season. Top picks Ganfeng, Yuneng, CATL.
