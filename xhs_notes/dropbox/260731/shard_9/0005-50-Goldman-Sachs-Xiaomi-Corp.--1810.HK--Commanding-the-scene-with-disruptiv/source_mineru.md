Xiaomi held its second EV Technology Conference on Jul 30 and introduced the Kunlun technology architecture including its manufacturing platform, range extender system and omni-domain safety system, which are key technology infrastructure for the upcoming SkyNomad EREV SUV models. Meanwhile, Xiaomi revealed the pre-sale prices for SkyNomad N70 Max (standard wheelbase 5-seater)/N90 Max (long wheelbase 7-seater) models at Rmb259.9k/Rmb299.9k, respectively (equivalent to US\$38k/44k).

## GS takes

\- Technology: The Kunlun Manufacturing Platform enables a large, intelligent and fully configurable interior featured by a flat floor and a long-track system, a refreshing feature in the auto sector; we see this native platform as a key technology differentiator for SkyNomad vs. competitors that may need time to replicate it and have to commercially balance a new platform against their existing ones. On the other hand, the long range support (e.g., 1705km CLTC range for N90 Max, or up to 505km pure electric CLTC range) enabled by the Kunlun Range Extender System is key value proposition to attract ICE consumers, in our view. Lastly, while SkyNomad adheres to Xiaomi's highest safety standards (e.g., an embedded roll cage constructed from as high as 2,200 MPa ultra-high-strength steel), Xiaomi has also achieved deep end-to-end involvement in battery manufacturing (i.e., leading product definition as well as battery pack design and development, participating in cell design and development, and governing full-process quality control) which we believe implies its control of core technologies instead of being a mere battery purchaser.

## What's next?

1) Xiaomi is scheduled to report 2Q26 results on Aug 18; as discussed in our prior note Setting the Scene, 2Q26 results may be the weakest of the year and we expect Xiaomi's revenue growth ex. EV, AI and other new initiatives to resume positive yoy growth from 3Q26E and net profit to improve qoq in 3Q/4Q26E.

2) Regarding SkyNomad, while Xiaomi has started receiving intentional orders, we see low likelihood that the number of intentional orders will be disclosed; instead, we believe investors should focus on confirmed order volume upon official release (i.e., we expect the initial batch of sales will likely extend till Oct 7, end of the National Day Golden Week). By comparison, the SU7 new facelift released in Mar 2026 received 15k+ confirmed orders within the first 34min or 80k+ confirmed orders during the initial batch of sales (48 days till the Labor Day Golden Week). That said, we do not see YU7 from last year (i.e., 240k+ confirmed orders within 18 hours) as a good comp for SkyNomad, as Xiaomi's execution in the SU7 new facelift demonstrates its enhanced abilities to smooth peak orders at initial launch and sustain healthy order volume post initial launch.

3) On AI, we see more cost-efficient MiMo-V3 series under way. As per company at the International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2026, the upcoming V3 model will feature HySparse (Hybrid Sparse Attention), a new architecture that was introduced by Xiaomi LLM-Core team in Feb 2026 and enables sparse attention layers to reuse the full attention KV cache, thereby reducing both computation and memory. Compared to the 7:1 sparsity ratio for V2/V2.5, MiMo-V.3 may adopt a more aggressive 11:1 sparsity ratio, implying potential to further push efficiency frontier and reduce inference costs. Also see our prior note on Xiaomi Robotics.

## GS Factor Profile

The GS Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows:

Growth is based on a stock's forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock's forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock's forward-looking P/E, P/B, price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile).

Financial Returns and Multiple use the GS analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics).

For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.

## M&A Rank

Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
