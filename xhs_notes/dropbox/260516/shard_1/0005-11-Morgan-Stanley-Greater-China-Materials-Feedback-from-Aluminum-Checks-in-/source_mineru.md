# Feedback from Aluminum Checks in Henan

We visited multiple aluminum processors and traders in Henan province to check on aluminum demand. Please see our trip feedback below.

Export demand is picking up with profit improving: Our talks with leading processing plants and traders involved in the export business indicate that export orders increased by \~30% MoM in April. One leading player indicates that export orders in May are up by 50% compared to that in March 2026. Processing plants that have sales volume 100% locked in long-term contracts noted that export profit increased by \~Rmb1,800/t in April compared to 1Q26, with volume up by 10% due to some pre-load orders resulting from tight supply overseas.

Aluminium twisted wire exports remain strong: We visited an aluminum twisted wire producer in Henan. The company's export volume increased \~200% MoM in April, and it currently has \~20kt export orders on hand. Processing fee is Rmb1,200/t excluding transportation costs. The wire producer we spoke to commented that if it implements the 13% VAT rebate, it will offer 10% discount on the aluminum price to the buyer. Twisted wire producers, especially those located in Hebei province, intend to expand capacity, but it might be slow as the machines take time to be delivered.

Domestic demand delayed but not disappeared; market share moving to leading players: Aluminum plate and strip capacity for a few leading players has increased 20-30% with orders staying relatively full. While smaller processing plants, especially those with notable exposure to the property market, noted weaker demand in May. This implies a more consolidated market and higher market share for leading players. One processing plant we met with mentioned 80-90% exposure to building materials and that orders declined by 20-30% in April and are staying roughly flattish MoM in May. Meanwhile, basically all the processing producers we met with commented that when aluminum price drops to \~Rmb23,500-24,300/t, pick up or shipment to downstream will increase.

Minimum safety inventory to ensure normal turnover to avoid the swing in aluminum price. In addition, there are a lot of warehouses in the Gongyi region. Aluminum ingot procurement is very convenient.
