# AI Capex is likely to continue for the time being; we expect yoy momentum to peak in FY3/27E to FY3/28E

Multiple companies have mentioned at earnings presentations and elsewhere that April order trends or recent inquiries are at record-high levels, suggesting that activity is at an intense level comparable to the past peak cycles of CY2021–22 and CY2017–18. The most prominent example is arguably Japan Machine Tool Builders' Association's March–April order data, which unexpectedly set new all-time monthly highs, centered in Asia/America. We believe this is driven by the strength of AI Capex demand expanding from semiconductors at the forefront to batteries, servers, and their peripheral components, with benefits extending beyond tech/electronics equipment to general industrial machinery as well. This trend has become more pronounced since the Lunar New Year and appears to have accelerated from April onward. We believe as long as the acceleration in core AI Capex areas, led by semiconductors, remains strong, this trend is likely to continue in the near future.
