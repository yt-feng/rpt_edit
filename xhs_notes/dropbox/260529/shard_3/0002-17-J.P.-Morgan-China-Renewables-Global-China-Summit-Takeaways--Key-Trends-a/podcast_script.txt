ZH_A: 最近投资者对中国可再生能源板块的兴趣明显回升，这份研报就来自某外资投行最近的一次全球中国峰会。研究员，你觉得这次兴趣回升背后，最核心的驱动力是什么？

ZH_B: 两个关键因素。一是中东冲突让大家重新担心能源安全，二是发达市场的数据中心电力基础设施出现瓶颈。这两个问题叠加，让资金开始重新审视中国可再生能源的一些细分领域。

ZH_A: 所以不是整个板块普涨，而是有选择性的。那这份报告真正想回答的问题是什么？

ZH_B: 报告的核心问题是：在政策变化和全球需求结构改变的背景下，哪些中国可再生能源公司能抓住新的增长点。它给出了四个重点标的，但每个标的背后的逻辑完全不同。

ZH_A: 那第一个洞察是什么？哪个公司最受关注？

ZH_B: 阳光电源是投资者兴趣最集中的标的。它透露了一个新动向：已经拿到了与人工智能数据中心相关的储能订单。这意味着储能系统在数据中心领域找到了一个全新的应用场景，比如帮助老化的电网支撑数据中心的快速启动。

ZH_A: 这听起来像是一个爆发点。但报告有没有提到风险？

ZH_B: 有。管理层也提示了，公用事业级储能项目从签约到交付周期很长，所以第二季度依然面临成本压力。这意味着新故事虽然好，但短期业绩可能不会立刻反映。

ZH_A: 那第二个洞察呢？海上风电好像也有新政策。

ZH_B: 对，这是另一个关键点。国务院首次明确了2030年海上风电装机目标，是100吉瓦。而且从历史数据看，中国的可再生能源装机目标通常会被大幅超额完成。这对海底电缆这类高壁垒环节是重大利好，报告特别看好东方电缆。

ZH_A: 为什么海底电缆壁垒高？

ZH_B: 因为市场集中度高，而且新进入者很难突破技术和认证门槛。所以一旦政策目标落地，龙头企业增长确定性很强。

ZH_A: 第三个洞察是关于新兴市场的分布式储能，对吗？

ZH_B: 没错。报告提到中东冲突后，新兴市场特别是东南亚的分布式储能需求激增。这类订单交付周期短，成本传导能力强，对德业股份是直接利好。同时，金风科技也可能受益于新兴市场风电装机的增加。

ZH_A: 那太阳能板块呢？反内卷政策推进得怎么样？

ZH_B: 这块进展缓慢。中央虽然定了产业整合的方向，但还没有可执行的策略。所以报告在这个环节很谨慎，只推荐成本领先或估值有安全垫的公司，比如协鑫科技和大全能源。

ZH_A: 最后，关于可再生能源运营商的电力价格问题，报告怎么看？

ZH_B: 这是报告里一个值得深思的对比。电力市场化改革正在压低风电和光伏的电价，但核电和水电的电价反而相对稳定，因为地方政府在主动稳定核电价格，水电则有成本优势。所以报告更看好核电和水电运营商。

ZH_A: 听起来，这份报告其实埋了一个重要的悬念——哪些问题它没有完全展开？

ZH_B: 对，有两个。第一，人工智能数据中心到底能带来多少储能增量需求，目前还只是初步订单，没有量化测算。第二，反内卷政策什么时候能真正落地，会以什么样的形式执行，报告也没有给出明确时间表。想了解这些细节，就需要去读完整报告了。

EN_A: So investor interest in China renewables is picking up again. What’s really driving that shift?

EN_B: Two big things. First, the Middle East conflict has renewed concerns about energy security. Second, developed markets are hitting real constraints on power infrastructure for data centers. Together, these are pushing capital back into selective parts of China’s renewable supply chain.

EN_A: Not the whole sector, then. So what is this report really trying to answer?

EN_B: It’s trying to identify which Chinese renewable companies can capture new growth pockets, given shifting policies and changing global demand. It highlights four specific picks, but each one has a very different story behind it.

EN_A: Let’s start with the first insight. Which company is getting the most attention?

EN_B: Sungrow. They shared that they’ve already secured orders for energy storage systems tied to AI data centers. That’s a brand-new use case. It means storage can help aging grids support the fast startup and reliability needs of data centers.

EN_A: That sounds like a potential breakout. But does the report flag any risks?

EN_B: Yes. Management also warned that utility-scale storage projects have a long lead time from contract to delivery. So cost pressure in the second quarter is still real. The new story is exciting, but near-term earnings may not reflect it yet.

EN_A: What about offshore wind? I saw there’s a new policy target.

EN_B: That’s the second key insight. For the first time, the State Council set a 2030 offshore wind target of 100 gigawatts. And history shows China usually beats its renewable targets by a wide margin. That’s a big positive for submarine cable companies, which have high entry barriers. The report is bullish on Orient Cables.

EN_A: Why are submarine cables so hard to break into?

EN_B: The market is very concentrated, and new players face tough technology and certification hurdles. So when policy targets land, the leaders have very strong growth visibility.

EN_A: The third insight seems to be about distributed storage in emerging markets.

EN_B: Exactly. The report notes a surge in distributed storage demand in emerging markets, especially Southeast Asia, after the Middle East conflict. These orders have short delivery cycles and better cost pass-through. That’s a direct positive for Deye. And Goldwind could also benefit if utility-scale renewable deployment picks up in those markets.

EN_A: And what about solar? The report mentions an anti-involution initiative.

EN_B: Progress there is slow. The central government has set the direction for industry consolidation, but there’s no clear execution plan yet. So the report is very selective in solar, only recommending cost leaders like GCL Tech or names with valuation support like Daqo.

EN_A: Lastly, what about power prices for renewable operators?

EN_B: This is a really interesting contrast. Power market liberalization is putting pressure on wind and solar tariffs. But nuclear and hydro tariffs are actually more stable, because local governments are actively stabilizing nuclear prices, and hydro has a natural cost advantage. So the report prefers nuclear and hydro operators over wind farms.

EN_B: Two big ones. First, how much storage demand will AI data centers actually create? We only have early orders, not a quantified estimate.
