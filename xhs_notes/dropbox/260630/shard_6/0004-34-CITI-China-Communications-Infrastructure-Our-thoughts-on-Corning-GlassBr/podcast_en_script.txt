EN_B: The CPO solution for the second half of 2027 is expected to be locked in later this year, and it is unlikely to be replaced by GlassBridge. The report sees GlassBridge potentially competing for new deployments when CPO and NPO really scale up around 2028 to 2030.
EN_A: So it is more about the incremental market, not the installed base?
EN_B: Correct. GlassBridge could compete for new iFAU sockets, but it will not replace existing ones. Plus, it still needs to pass qualification, reliability tests, and yield ramps. There is a lot of uncertainty here.
