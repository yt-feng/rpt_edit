EN_B: That’s the critical divergence. The buying might be coming from hedge funds or more nimble capital, while active long-only funds are reducing weight. That means the rally is driven by a different type of investor than usual.
EN_A: So what does that divergence imply for the next few months?
EN_B: If earnings continue to improve, those underweight active funds may be forced to cover, creating a second leg up. But if earnings disappoint, this foreign inflow could reverse quickly.
