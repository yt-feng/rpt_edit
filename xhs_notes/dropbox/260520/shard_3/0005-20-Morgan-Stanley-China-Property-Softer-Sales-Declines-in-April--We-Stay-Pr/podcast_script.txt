ZH_A: 最近房地产市场的消息好像有点矛盾，一边是四月销售数据跌幅收窄，一边是开发商还在收缩。这份外资投行的报告到底想告诉我们什么？

ZH_B: 核心判断其实很明确：四月的改善主要靠低基数，反弹的持续性非常可疑。报告标题直接说了，We Stay Prudent on Durability。

ZH_A: 所以“低基数”到底是什么意思？

ZH_B: 简单说，去年四月基数太低，所以今年四月同比看起来好一些。报告里数据很清楚：四月全国销售金额同比下降7.6%，而一季度是下降17%。看起来是改善，但环比三月其实是下降的。

ZH_A: 那这不就是“数据好看，但实际没那么好”？

ZH_B: 对。更关键的是，报告认为这种销售改善很难持续。它提到几个理由：库存仍然很高，买家信心脆弱，居民收入和杠杆没有明显改善。

ZH_A: 那最近一些城市二手房卖得不错，是不是说明市场在好转？

ZH_B: 报告特别关注了这个点。它说三月到四月部分高能级城市的二手房确实超预期，但五月最新几周已经明显减速了。而且城市之间分化很严重，一些二线和低线城市还在恶化。

ZH_A: 所以二手房的热度可能只是昙花一现？

ZH_B: 报告没有直接这么说，但它的语气明显偏谨慎。它认为政策效果在递减，居民情绪依然脆弱，所以销售反弹的“耐久性”存疑。

ZH_A: 那开发商这边的情况呢？

ZH_B: 更糟。三月竣工面积同比下降19%，新开工面积下降27%。房地产投资跌幅从一季度的11.2%扩大到前四个月的13.7%。开发商对前景不确定，所以拿地很谨慎。

ZH_A: 那这份报告有没有给出什么投资方向？

ZH_B: 它认为行业风险回报已经偏向负面，尤其是股价最近反弹之后。但它还是选了一些有“自救故事”的标的。一类是拥有优质购物中心、有C-REIT潜力的公司，比如华润置地和新城控股。另一类是能看清利润率恢复路径的住宅开发商，比如建发国际。

EN_A: So April home sales data showed a milder drop. But is that actually good news for China’s property market?

EN_B: Not really. The report from a global investment bank says the improvement is mostly due to a low base effect. And they are very cautious about whether this rebound can last.

EN_A: What does “low base” mean in this context?

EN_B: Last April was a really weak month. So compared to that, this April looks better. Nationwide sales value fell 7.6 percent year on year, which is way better than the 17 percent drop in the first quarter. But month over month, it actually declined.

EN_A: So the headline number is misleading?

EN_B: Exactly. And the report points to several reasons why this is not sustainable. High inventory, fragile buyer confidence, and no real improvement in household income or leverage.

EN_A: But what about the strong secondary home sales we saw in some cities? Doesn’t that signal a recovery?

EN_B: The report addresses that directly. It says the strong secondary sales in March and April were a surprise, but the latest weeks in May already show a sharp deceleration. And there is a growing divergence between cities. Some tier-2 and lower-tier cities are actually getting worse.

EN_A: So the heat in the secondary market might be temporary?

EN_B: That’s the implication. The report thinks policy effects are fading, and buyer sentiment is still fragile. So the durability of any sales rebound is very much in question.

EN_A: What about the supply side? How are developers doing?

EN_B: Not well. Construction activity is sluggish. Completions fell 19 percent in March, new starts dropped 27 percent. Real estate investment declined 13.7 percent in the first four months, worse than the first quarter. Developers are cautious about buying land because the sales outlook is uncertain.
