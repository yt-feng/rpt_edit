# Top 15 countries' new energy vehicle sales volume tracker - March 2026

This monthly chartbook tracks the sales trends of new energy vehicles (NEV) in the top 15 global markets (G15).

This report tracks new NEV car sales in the top 15 NEV markets globally, including pure electric vehicles (EV), plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEV) and fuel-cell vehicles (FCV). The 15 countries tracked account for about 90% of global NEV sales, including 12 major countries (China, US, Japan, India, Germany, France, Brazil, UK, South Korea, Canada, Italy and Thailand) and 3 Nordic countries (Norway, Sweden, Finland).
