# Company profile

Based in the Netherlands, Aalberts is a leading provider of mission-critical technologies used in residential, commercial and industrial buildings, semicon equipment and industrial applications (automotive, aerospace, power generation and machine building). It designs, develops and manufactures hydronic flow control and integrated piping systems for building heating/cooling, as well as semiconductor equipment (lithography machine base frames, vibration isolation and linear motion systems, advanced mechatronics), and also provides a broad range of services in surface treatment and material extrusion.

# Key themes

i) Short-term organic growth outlook in the different segments; ii) margin improvement potential and improvement levers in the various segments; iii) 2030 financial targets; iv) potential impacts from the war in the Middle East; and v) capital allocation and M&A (and divestment) strategy.
