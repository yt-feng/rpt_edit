# Key Takeaways

China Telecom introduced its own token plans facing both enterprise and consumers. The plans are standalone plans on a monthly subscription basis.   
It costs on average Rmb2.0-2.7 per mn tokens for enterprises and Rmb0.6-1.0 per mn tokens for consumers assuming full usage.   
China Mobile and China Unicom also had similar product offerings earlier but only at the provincial level. CT's is the first product launch on national scale.

How it differs vs hyperscalers: We note hyperscalers such as Alicloud also offer token plans but the package includes credits per seat per month, where different underlying models would result in different credit usages. Whereas China Telecom's plan seems to price all models at the same token price.

Our take: We think as mobile data penetration peaks and data prices continues to deflate, the Chinese telcos are looking for new revenue streams that can leverage their extensive data/computing infrastructures. We see it as a positive move to test a new business model in the AI era. However, in the near term, we think consumer adoption would still rely on the emergence of AI applications; meanwhile, the enterprise race would depend on the service capability, MaaS unit metrics and proprietary models.
