# China Conference Takeaways: accelerating AI monetization; focus on growth visibility

Industry Overview

# ERP/photo & design tools better; solid AI monetization

We hosted investor meetings with the management of 10+ software companies (e.g., Kingdee, Meitu, Chinasoft, Ming Yuan Cloud, Xunce and etc.) at the 2026 BofA China Conference in Shenzhen during the week of May 11. We also hosted an expert meeting on agentic AI and AI models.

Key takeaways include: (1) software AI monetization is generally accelerating, with ARPU uplift potential driven by token-based revenue models; (2) AI is driving a shift in software service models, transitioning from SaaS to RaaS; (3) demand trends are diverging: ERP / photo editing & design tools demonstrate better growth visibility, supported by rising AI revenue contributions and a higher share of recurring revenue; while IT outsourcing, cybersecurity, and property software demand remain soft; (4) by client type and vertical: SOEs—particularly those in critical infrastructure sectors (e.g., energy)—as well as large private enterprises, are showing solid demand, while demand from gov't-related clients has yet to recover; and (5) overall cash flow and profitability are expected to further improve in FY26E, supported by disciplined headcount expansion and rising operating efficiency driven by AI. Considering growth visibility and risk/reward on valuation, our preferred Buy-rated names are Kingdee and Meitu.

# Key takeaways from agentic AI expert meeting

We hosted an expert meeting with Mr. Wang Zhe (Founder of Hangzhou Baizefeng Intelligent Technology) to discuss recent developments in AI applications and LLMs. Key takeaways: 1) AI coding capability has experienced rapid improvement, significantly enhancing developer productivity, driving a structural decline in software development costs; 2) software monetization models are shifting from traditional seat-based pricing toward usage- and token-based pricing; 3) industry structure is evolving, with large software vendors and small, agile teams emerging as potential beneficiaries, while mid-sized software companies may face increasing pressure for business transformation; and 4) rising adoption of multi-model strategies is lowering switching costs and intensifying competition across LLM vendors.

# Key takeaways from UCloud mgmt. meeting

We hosted an investor meeting with UCloud (Not covered) mgmt. during our AI/Tech tour in Shanghai. Mgmt is positive on the AI cloud computing demand especially for AI inferencing. Due to recent stronger demand and higher costs for AI servers, they have raised the price of GPU-based AI cloud services by 20-25% on average. Its profitability has been improving in the past few quarters and has achieved breakeven since 4Q25. Looking ahead, mgmt. targets to expand both their AI cloud services and data center business (in Shanghai and Inner Mongolia).

# Kingdee (268 HK) - Buy

# Accelerated revenue growth in 1Q26; NDR for large enterprises to recover

Mgmt. sees faster revenue growth in 1Q26, compared with $12\%$ YoY in FY25, indicating solid downstream customer demand, along with strong OCF and profitability improvement. Despite a decline in NDR (net dollar retention) for large enterprises to $103\%$ in 1Q26 (vs. $110\%$ in 4Q25), mgmt. expects the number to exceed $110\%$ in 4Q26.

# Double-digit % YoY revenue growth and >7% adjusted NPM in FY26E

Mgmt. reiterated FY26E target, namely: 1) double-digit% YoY revenue growth, 2) adjusted NPM to exceed 7%, and 3) >20% YoY OCF (operating cash flow) growth. Total number of employees further reduced by 6% YTD to around 10.6k, mainly on R&D and implementation staff, and mgmt. plans to reduce the total number of employees to less than 10k by FY28E, which we think is a conservative target.

# FY26 AI-native product revenue to exceed RMB1bn

Contract value of AI-native products was RMB230mn in 1Q26 and mgmt. expects revenue from AI-native products to exceed RMB1bn in FY26. Kingdee will host a product launch event on 20 May to release more AI agent products, featuring multi-function and multi-agent applications.

# Meitu (1357 HK) - Buy

# Promising ARPU uplift potential, especially productivity tools

Mgmt. sees promising ARPU uplift potential across both products for leisure and productivity tools. For leisure products, a rising portion of overseas subscribers could drive ARPU uplift, given higher user subscription fees than in the domestic market. For productivity tools, ARPU uplift mainly comes from rising usage of AI-driven features driven by AI agent capabilities.

# Long-term GPM to be at c.70%

Mgmt. is confident in maintaining GPM at c.70% in the long term, despite rising AI credits consumption from productivity tools. We believe the third-party API cost is manageable, as Meitu constantly shifts high-usage functions from third-party to its self-developed / fine-tuned vertical models, which could fulfill most user requirements.

# More AI functions featuring agent teams to launch

Meitu will host its Image Festival in mid-Jun 2026, and is expected to launch more AI functions for productivity tools (e.g., DesignKit and Kaipai) featuring agent teams.

# Recent investor feedback: getting back to fundamentals

We believe Meitu's 1Q26 results may have partially alleviated market concerns on potential AI-driven disruption, at least in the near term, as robust 1Q26 product revenue growth with continued subscriber expansion underscores the resilience of its business model. Recent feedback from a few investors we spoke to suggests to us market focus may be gradually shifting away from AI disruption concerns toward more fundamental drivers, including sustainability of subscriber growth, ARPU uplift and AI-related cost optimization, which could be key variables for valuation re-rating going forward.
