Post MIR's estimated sales data (vs. production data reported by the NBS) as well as our coverage companies' business segment disclosures, we compile our latest landscape analyzer within.

Industrial automation (IA) market growth came in at +6% yoy in 2Q26, with the project/OEM (mainly discrete automation) market at -1%/+17% yoy, extending the visible OEM-led recovery observed in 1Q26.
