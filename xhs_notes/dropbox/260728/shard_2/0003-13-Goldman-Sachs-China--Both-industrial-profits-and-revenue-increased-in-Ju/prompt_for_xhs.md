# China: Both industrial profits and revenue increased in June

## Bottom line:

China's industrial profits increased by $18.3\%$ yoy, and revenue rose by $11.4\%$ yoy in June (vs. $+21.0\%$ yoy and $+6.7\%$ yoy, respectively, in May). In seasonally adjusted sequential terms, industrial profits increased by $1.5\%$ (vs. $-8.4\%$ in May), and revenue grew by $3.1\%$ sa non-annualized (vs. $+0.6\%$ in May).

## Key numbers:

Industrial profits: +18.3% yoy in June (sequential growth: +1.5% non-annualized, seasonally adjusted by GS); May: +21.0% yoy (sequential growth: -8.4% sa non-annualized).

Industrial revenue: +11.4% yoy in June (sequential growth: +3.1% non-annualized, seasonally adjusted by GS); May: +6.7% yoy (sequential growth: +0.6% sa non-annualized).

## Main points:

1. In year-over-year terms, China's industrial profits increased by $18.3\%$ yoy in June, compared to $21.0\%$ yoy in May. In sequential terms, profits increased by $1.5\%$ non-annualized in June after seasonal adjustment, following a decline of $8.4\%$ in May (Exhibit 1).

2. On a year-over-year basis, downstream profits rose 16.2% in June, accelerating from 10.2% in May. By contrast, upstream profit growth slowed sharply to 24.5% yoy, from 58.3% in May, with the drag mainly coming from chemical materials, petroleum, and natural gas extraction. The NBS commented that raw materials manufacturing sector contributed 8.8pp to the 18.7% industrial profit growth in H1, followed by electronics sector at 8.5pp.

3. In year-over-year terms, industrial revenue increased by $11.4\%$ yoy in June, vs. $+6.7\%$ yoy in May. Sequentially, revenue increased by $3.1\%$ non-annualized in June (vs. $+0.6\%$ in May). Overall profit margins (total profits divided by revenues) edged up in June on a 12-month average basis (Exhibit 2), driven by improving profit margins in both upstream and downstream sectors.
