# Lifting Copper Prices on Ex-US Tightness Ahead of US Tariff Catalyst. Buy LUN, ANTO.

The GS commodities team sees a materially tighter ex-US copper market through 2026-27, driven by both weaker mine supply and continued tariff related shifts in trade flow. Global mine supply has been is reduced by \~350kt (\~1.5% global mine supply) in 2026 and 2027, primarily reflecting slower recoveries at Grasberg and Kamoa-Kakula (full recovery expected in 2028), while scrap is not offsetting refined tightness, given weaker China domestic availability. The team believe this will drive a material step change in the ex-US balance to deficits of \~640kt/170kt in 2026/27, tightening the global market where the LME price is set, underpinning higher pricing.

The key near term catalyst in our view is the tariff outcome (expected this month) for the US.
