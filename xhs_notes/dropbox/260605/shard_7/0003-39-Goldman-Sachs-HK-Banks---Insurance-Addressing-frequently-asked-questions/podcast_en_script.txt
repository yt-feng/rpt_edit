EN_B: Exactly. There were news reports that some banks had paused opening Hong Kong investment accounts for mainland residents at their mainland branches. EN_B: Now when you open an investment account, you need to provide a declaration that the funds are legally sourced and that they originate from outside mainland China. This is really about tightening KYC and mitigating cross-border capital risks.

EN_B: In the short term, it will slow things down. But it does not necessarily break the long-term logic.
