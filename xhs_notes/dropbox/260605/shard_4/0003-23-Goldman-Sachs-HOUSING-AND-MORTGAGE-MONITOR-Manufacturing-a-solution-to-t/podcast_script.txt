ZH_A: 3到4百万套？这个缺口太大了。那制造房屋现在到底有多“小众”？

ZH_A: 从26万跌到8万？为什么会出现这么大的滑坡？

ZH_A: 所以这个钢制底盘反而成了障碍？那现在有什么新变化吗？

EN_A: So it's cheap, but nobody wants it. How cheap are we talking?

EN_A: That's a huge price advantage. So why aren't more people buying them? What's the catch?

EN_B: The biggest hurdle is financing. About 40% of manufactured homes sit on rented land in communities. So the homeowner doesn't own the land. EN_A: So it's more like buying a car than a house. What if you own the land?

EN_B: Even then, the fixed costs of getting a traditional mortgage can be too high relative to the small loan amount.
