## EMAX resilience has a trifecta of underpinnings

In contrast to downside risks in China, there are upside risks to growth in the rest of Asia. Three factors have combined to underpin this. First, EMAX has proved relatively resilient and resourceful in the face of the energy shock. Third, fiscal policy is active in the region to cushion the energy shock. In India, the government has borne a significant fraction of the negative terms of trade shock, with retail prices increasingly more modestly.
