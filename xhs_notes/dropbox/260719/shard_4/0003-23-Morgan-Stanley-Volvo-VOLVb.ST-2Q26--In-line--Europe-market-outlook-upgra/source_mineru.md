## Volvo (VOLVb.ST, VOLVB SS)

We expect a flattish positive market reaction to the results.

Group earnings in line, strength in Trucks coming through. Volvo delivered Q2 adjusted EBIT of SEK 14.8bn (+2% vs consensus, +10% YoY) at an 11.7% margin (+70bps YoY), on revenue of SEK 126.3bn (+1% vs cons.). The beat was led by Trucks (Adj. EBIT +7% vs cons., margin 11.2%). Volvo describes European truck demand as still being replacement driven, with good freight activity, vehicle replacements, and supportive manufacturing PMIs. North America freight rates and volumes have increased but are not yet driving a retail sales increase. Volvo expects the increased orders to drive production, deliveries and retail sales in H2. Construction Equipment earnings were in line with expectations, whilst Penta was well below (-23%). Volvo has filed the IEEPA tariff refund, to be recognised in Q3 and fully offset the SEK 1.1bn Q3 tariff cost. FCF of SEK5.8bn was slightly below (-8%) consensus at SEK6.3bn.

European Truck market outlook upgrade. In Trucks, orders strength was notable, growing +33% at group level, with +122% in North America, running +18% ahead of cons. Management raised the China market forecast by 120k to 880k and Europe by 5k to 315k, while again holding North America at 265k. We wrote about the debate on North America in PMIs to Power Multiple Inflection. Supportive PMIs are starting to gradually improve freight volumes, which could continue to support recent order strength. With valuations already reflecting the upcycle, the question from here is whether truck markets can become meaningfully stronger with freight markets still mostly supply, rather than demand driven.

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Volvo (VOLVb.ST)

We value Volvo using a P/E methodology. Volvo is the highest quality OEM in our coverage and should trade at a premium to EU Truck peers. We apply a 13.5x multiple to FY27e EPS, just above the upper end of its long-term interquartile range of c.10-13x, as we expect Volvo to benefit from improving demand sentiment. A 13.5x multiple implies an appropriate premium to Daimler Truck, typically trading at \~8-9x.

## Risks to Upside

■ Truck margins are supported by upcoming emissions regulation.

■ Volvo's strong cash flows could support further cash returns to shareholders.

■ Construction Equipment margins stay at high levels as rates fall.

## Risks to Downside

■ Economic growth deteriorates as rates rise and PMIs fall.

■ Construction markets remain weak in FY25-26.

■ EPA '27 pre-buy effect does not materialise.
