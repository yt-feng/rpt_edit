EN_A: What about its traditional EV connector business? Is that under pressure?
EN_B: Yes, it is. EV connectors face margin pressure from OEM price cuts and more competition. But the report sees power whips for data centers as a new growth driver. In the base case, they assume no revenue from power whips in 2026, but in a bull case, mass production could start in the second half and bring in about 500 million RMB.
