Two-day Healthcare Tour takeaways: Rising innovation bar, smarter BD, and verifiable AIDD revenue

On 18-19 May, we visited a number of Chinese healthcare companies spanning the AI drug discovery (AIDD), Biotech, and Medtech spaces.
