# Shanxi mine disaster to lead to further regulatory tightening

The gas explosion at the Liushenyu coal mine in Qinyuan county in Shanxi on 22 May has killed at least 82 people. China's mining safety regulator also issued an announcement on May 24 to crack down on the country's coal mine gas hazards. We see near-term supply shocks to the coking coal market, but the price impact on coking coal will likely be capped due to narrow steel margins. While initial safety suspensions in Qinyuan were mandatory, broader province-wide inspections have been voluntary so far.
