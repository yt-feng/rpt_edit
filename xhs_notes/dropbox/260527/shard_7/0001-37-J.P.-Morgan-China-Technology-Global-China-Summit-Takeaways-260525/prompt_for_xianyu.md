# China Technology

Global China Summit Takeaways

We hosted a 2-day China AI enabler tour and talked to various tech companies during the Global China Summit last week. We expect indigenous AI chip suppliers to ramp up shipments significantly from 2H26; while enablers, such as wafer fab equipment (WFE) and OSAT suppliers are top beneficiaries. Despite low visibility of approval granted for advanced NVIDIA chips, domestic AI chip suppliers are constructive about the supply/demand dynamics, and expect significant sales growth into 2027E. Thanks to the upcoming launch of new products with competitive performance, key players are targeting at tapping into the training purpose instead of inference demand only. But, owing to concentrated customer portfolio and rising memory costs, the margin upside should be limited, in our view.   
- Semi equipment as the sub-sector with high conviction. As the key beneficiaries of building an indigenous AI supply chain, we expect WFE players to retain the solid outlook throughout 2027, especially the clearer IPO timeline of CXMT and YMTC could be the positive catalyst for both, fundamentals and sentiment. Net-net, we suggest a selective view on fabless owing to mixed demand outlook but increasing cost pressure.
