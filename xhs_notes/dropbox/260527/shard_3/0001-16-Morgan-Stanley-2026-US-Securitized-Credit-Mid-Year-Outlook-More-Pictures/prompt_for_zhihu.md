# Affordability

With 10yr Treasuries stuck above 4.15, our affordability assumptions estimates assume a 30yr mortgage rate that finishes 2026 at $\sim 6\frac{1}{4}$ before ending 2027 at $\sim 6\frac{1}{8}$ . Affordability improves over the forecast horizon as income growth exceeds HPA, but it remains near the most challenged it has been in decades.

# Housing Activity

The combination of challenged affordability and geopolitical uncertainty/volatility should keep home sales range-bound. Meanwhile, accelerating weakness in new home pricing and the stubbornly elevated inventories there should keep building volumes muted.

# Home Prices

As inventory growth has outpaced demand, the pace of YoY HPA has decelerated. As we approach 0% nationally, we expect home prices to find a floor. There simply is not enough for-sale inventory to drive meaningfully negative home prices. As mortgage rates languish above 6%, we expect the pace of existing inventory growth to slow, which will allow for a slight acceleration of HPA back to +2% by the end of 2026, where we expect it to stay for 2027.
