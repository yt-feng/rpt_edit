# View on mid/small caps in tech subindustries

# SPE-related

- For component-related names benefiting from WFE growth, growth rates are widening as inventory adjustments end and demand for advance orders emerges.   
- In test-related areas such as testers, probers, and probe cards, demand is being created by both higher volumes of AI-related devices and the increase in test items associated with performance improvements.   
- Demand for probe cards for memory is increasing not only for HBM and general purpose DRAM, but also for NAND.   
- For probers, improving high precision temperature control functions and increasing supply capacity create opportunities to win orders.   
- Semiconductor capex in China remains strong, and the degree of penetration into the China supply chain is becoming a differentiator in terms of earnings contributions.

# Renewed focus on strength in data center-related demand

# Semi materials-related

- Procurement risk related to the Middle East has eased, but high prices and delays in price pass-throughs remain risks.   
- Low CTE and low dielectric materials driven by AI-related demand. Compared with quartz glass, glass cloth has inferior performance, but when combined with resin it can differentiate through CCL performance and lower processing difficulty. Low CTE glass has a technological barrier in terms of the difficulty of yarn production.   
- Capacity expansion by materials companies is aimed in part at meeting demand around 2030. From land acquisition to plant construction and equipment installation, it takes at least two years to begin mass production.   
- Demand for front-end materials such as photoresists, CMP slurry, and wiring related materials remains firm.   
- In advanced packaging, increasing size, multilayering, and higher density are growth drivers, beyond mere volume expansion

# Device-related

- Power equipment related: Demand for battery storage systems continues, and interest in transformers for private sector demand is increasing further.   
- Computers: Demand related to data centers remains strong, mainly AI related. We will monitor tech developments such as vertical power delivery.   
- Smartphones & PCs: We're cautious over potential demand weakness and lower production volumes triggered by rising memory prices.   
- Home appliances & consumer electronics: Demand stagnation is built into company plans, reflecting factors such as inflation.   
- Industrial equipment & automotive: Semiconductor- and defense-related areas are promising. In automotive, watch demand changes stemming from differences in customer mix.
