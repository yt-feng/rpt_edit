# Corporate equity demand should outweigh record IPO supply in 2026

- IPO activity is continuing to recover after four years of muted issuance. 40 deals worth \$28 billion have launched YTD, the largest totals through May since 2021. While elevated IPO activity has characterized some previous equity market peaks, the number of IPOs YTD is only on pace to reach the historical annual average of 100. 2026 should be a record year for IPOs in dollar terms.
