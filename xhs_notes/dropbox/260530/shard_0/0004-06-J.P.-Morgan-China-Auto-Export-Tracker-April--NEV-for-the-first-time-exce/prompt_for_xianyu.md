# China Auto Export Tracker

April: NEV for the first time exceeding 50% of total

China Auto export tracker: China auto's export story is not new to most investors, and it will play an increasingly critical role in the global auto marketplace – not only for equity investors but also corporate management across global OEMs. In our recent analysis, we have seen evidence that Chinese OEMs are gaining share rapidly in Europe, and Southeast Asia. In this note, we aim to provide a timely update on: 1) the latest export dynamics – including growth and geographic momentum, and 2) powertrain mix – we believe PHEV could surprise many overseas investors – a similar pattern seen in China in recent years.

- YTD exports up nearly 70% yoy: Exports provide a meaningful growth and profitability opportunity for Chinese brands. In 4M26, China exported over 2.7mn units of PVs (+69% y/y), per CAAM. Specifically, Europe (including west and east) accounted for 37% of the total – the largest destination worldwide, followed by Asia at 30% and South America at 17%, per ThinkerCar customs data.   
- China's export mix has shifted quickly towards more NEVs: $55\%$ of PV exports were NEVs in Apr-26, up from $50\%$ in Mar-26 and $33\%$ in early 2024, based on ThinkerCar customs data. Within NEV, PHEV play an increasingly important role, accounting for $39\%$ of total NEV exports by Apr-26 vs only $11\%$ in Jan-24 (Figures 1-2).   
- We continue to view Chinese OEM overseas share gains as structural rather than cyclical, with exports increasingly becoming a core growth driver (Figures 3-4). We recently highlighted the export structural shifts – towards more NEVs and Europe, and China OEMs' market share gains in Europe (here) and Asia (here). We believe Chinese OEMs will replicate the same strategy in overseas markets, offering advanced connectivity content and interior features as standard options, which works well, especially for young buyers. We raised our projections of China's total exports (PV+CV), expecting it to hit another record high of \~9.8mn units in 2026 with \~40%y/y growth (JPM note).   
- Major Chinese OEMs' export structures are becoming more regionally diversified, although with distinct paths by OEM: Among selected players: SAIC is the clearest Europe-led case, with Europe's share rising from 34% of exports in 2024 to 46% in 4M26, while Asia declined from 32% to 23%. Chery Auto and BYD also show a rotation towards Europe and South America, while Asia's share has moderated. For Geely Auto, Asia remained the largest region, but fell from 50% in FY25 to 39% in 4M26, Europe was the second-largest region at 27%, while South America rose sharply from 4% in 2024 to 18% in 4M26. In contrast, Great Wall Motor is reducing its reliance on Europe, with Europe's share dropping from 65% in FY24 to 38% in 4M26, while Asia and South America increased to 25% and 17%, respectively. Overall, Europe remains a critical export destination, but LatAm and ROW are becoming increasingly important growth drivers. Chinese OEM's overseas expansion is becoming more balanced rather than dependent on one region, which we believe is a new positive trend with further market share gain potential in overseas markets.
