# 'Price is not an issue' - Key takeaways from our Expert Call

## Deep Dive with Former Powertrain Purchasing Head

We hosted an expert call with Antonio Trotta who previously served as Head of Purchasing Department (Powertrain and E-Powertrain) at Ferrari between 2021 – 2025. On this call we discussed the overall market trends for Ferrari as well as the wider luxury segment as well as the path towards electrifying luxury sport cars. On this point we also touched on the recently-introduced Luce.

\- Entering the sports and luxury car segment requires enormous financial resources and building a Ferrari-like brand takes decades. From today's perspective, no major competition risk is visible.

## Brand and Positioning

For many UHNWI the vehicle is also an expression of their lifestyle.

According to Mr. Trotta, Ferrari has a unique position in the industry, built on its road car heritage and F1 success. This combination is so far unmatched and also shields against competitors.

## Pricing and exclusivity

We understand that Ferrari's pricing power remains exceptional and there is no obvious reason for the company to slow price increases. In fact, the price is often the last consideration for this customer group.
