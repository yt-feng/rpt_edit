## Company profile

Tencent provides a wide range of services, including gaming (including PC games and mobile games), other value added services (e.g. video entertainment), online advertising, fintech and business services (including payment and cloud services). Tencent also operates WeChat, China's largest messaging app.

## Valuation Methodology

We value Tencent's online gaming business at USD282bn (FY26F P/E of 18x), online advertising business at USD236bn (FY26F P/E of 20x), Fintech business at USD169bn (FY26F P/E of 15x), and Cloud business at USD40bn (FY26F P/S of 5x). We value investees at USD106bn. The resulting TP is HKD727. The benchmark for the stock is the Hang Seng Index.

Risks that may impede the achievement of the target price

Risks: High market expectations, aggressive spending on WeChat Pay and overseas marketing, and any potentially disruptive products and business models from competitors.
