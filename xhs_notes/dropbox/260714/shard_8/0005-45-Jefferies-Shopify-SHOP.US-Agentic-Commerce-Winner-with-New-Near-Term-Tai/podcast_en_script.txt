EN_A: That price increase call is interesting. Walk me through the logic.
EN_B: The last price increase for non-Plus plans was in 2023, about 33 to 34 percent. Plus plans went up 25 percent in 2024. Since then, they have launched a ton of features, most notably Sidekick, their AI assistant. Shopify has been absorbing those costs. Now that more merchants are using it and seeing value, they have room to raise prices.
EN_A: So it is not a guess. There is a clear narrative. How much could it move the needle?
EN_B: The report did the math. If they raise non-Plus prices again, similar to 2023, it would be a small amount per merchant. But for Shopify overall, it could add 3 to 4 percent to 2027 revenue. And most of that would drop straight to the bottom line.
EN_A: 3 to 4 percent does not sound huge, but if it is pure profit, that matters. What about the partner commission change?
EN_B: This is clever. Starting August 2026, partner commissions are capped at four years. The first four years pay much more than before. For an Advanced plan merchant, the first four years payouts are 104 percent higher. But after four years, it is zero. Under the old model, the ten year total was 7180 dollars. Under the new model, it is 5872 dollars. That is 18 percent less.
EN_A: So short term, partners get more incentive to bring in big clients. Long term, Shopify saves money.
EN_B: Exactly. And the structure forces partners to focus on new logos and cross selling, not just sitting on a recurring income stream. Products like B2B, POS, and Shopify Components also get more attention.
EN_A: And the confidence on the second quarter. Where does that come from?
EN_B: They looked at web traffic to shop.app subdomains. They found a 94 percent correlation with GMV. Based on that data, they see room for material upside to the consensus estimate of 27 percent GMV growth.
EN_A: 94 percent correlation? Is that data source reliable?
EN_B: It is from a third party data platform. The correlation itself needs more verification. But it is an interesting leading indicator.
EN_A: There is also a big theme around agentic commerce. This report is very bullish on Shopify in that era.
EN_B: They see agentic commerce as the next interface shift in ecommerce. The core idea is that when AI agents start making purchase decisions for consumers, whoever controls the transaction rails and the merchant relationship captures the value. Shopify is uniquely positioned. They are not just infrastructure for merchants. They could become the toolkit for agents.
EN_A: That sounds abstract. How does it translate into numbers?

If agentic commerce shifts 1 to 4 percent of global ecommerce GMV from closed platforms and big box retailers to independent merchants, and Shopify merchants capture 10 to 35 percent of that, then by 2028, the incremental GMV for Shopify could be between 0.9 and 25 billion dollars. And that is before any lift from higher conversion or purchase frequency.
