Restructured Partner Incentives Reduce Payouts Over the LT. In the new partner incentive model announcement, SHOP provided illustrative examples of the impact to partner payouts in the old and new models. The examples demonstrate that payouts to partners are initially higher vs. the previous model, but eventually taper off as partners lapse the four-year cap.

For an Advanced plan merchant generating \$750K in annual eligible online GMV, total payouts in the first four years are 104% higher under the new model. However, the old and new models reach a breakeven point after \~8 years, after which cumulative payouts are lower under the new model. After ten years, the total payout is 18% lower under the new model.

Notably, larger merchants with higher annual GMV will take longer to reach a breakeven point vs. the old model.

For a Plus plan merchant generating \$10M in annual eligible online GMV and \$2M in annual retail GMV, and that was sales-assisted, total payouts in the first four years are 137% higher under the new model. Because the merchant's higher GMV generates a proportionally higher payout in the first four years, it takes \~11 years to reach breakeven vs. the old model.
