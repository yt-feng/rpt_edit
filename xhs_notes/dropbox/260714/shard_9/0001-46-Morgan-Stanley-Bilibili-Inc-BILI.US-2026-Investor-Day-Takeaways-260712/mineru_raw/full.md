# Bilibili Inc | Asia Pacific

# 2026 Investor Day Takeaways

The visualization of Bili's community strength increases our confidence in the sustainability of its commercialization growth. The depth of the game pipeline also came as a positive surprise.

**Content and community.** Users' sense of belonging and trust form the foundation of Bili's online community. In the digital world, a sense of belonging stems from high-quality content, social interaction, and time accumulation. Time accumulation, in particular, differentiates a content community from a content distribution platform. Much of the content on Bili has a long lifecycle, helping users build and reinforce shared interests and consensus. Trust, reflected in users consistently following creators' updates, enables creators to build their personal brands and accelerate their content creation flywheel. Over the past year, video podcasts emerged as the fastest-growing category on the platform, combining creators' personal brands with deep-dive content.

**Commercialization.** The key drivers of commercialization are user engagement growth (expanding advertisers' TAM) and community mindshare (increasing the value of each impression). Beyond strong DAU and time-spent growth, Bili's users also demonstrate high levels of immersion (65% of time spent on videos longer than five minutes), interaction (17bn monthly interactions), trust (>8mn users who have followed creators for more than 10 years and actively interact with them), and rising purchasing power (average age of 27, with 55% located in tier 1–2 cities). Together, these factors continue to drive strong advertising revenue growth. Bili has monetized a broad range of user use cases effectively and is exploring additional opportunities to monetize increasing user engagement. Regarding AI, internal tests suggest that AI-generated ad cover pages can improve click-through rates by 8ppt, while broader adoption could increase eCPM by 5%. The next step is to promote Bili's AI agent among advertisers, which could significantly expand spending from mid- and long-tail advertisers.

**Game.** Bili aims to add two more evergreen titles (vertical leaders) over the next two to three years. Management disclosed a rich game pipeline, including well-known titles such as San Wang (which recently entered a new testing phase), N Card, and Lumi Master, as well as new releases including Three Kingdoms: The Ravages of Time (Huofengliaoyuan), a tactical RPG based on The Ravages of Time IP that will begin testing in late July, and Mistbound: Guild Wars Card Game, a strategy card game based on the Guild Wars IP. Management also indicated that it plans to unveil an MMORPG title in late July. In addition to Bili's existing game strategy of targeting younger users, building vertical leaders, and emphasizing long-term operations, the company now places greater importance on two key trends: globalization and cross-platform development. Bili also plans to expand its self-developed game pipeline modestly.
