# Implications of Japanese OEM New Auto Sales in China (June 2026) for Auto Parts Industry

## Key Takeaways

China new vehicle sales by Japanese OEMs in June 2026 were -27% for Toyota, -44% for Honda, and -30% for Nissan. Implications for auto parts suppliers are somewhat negative.
