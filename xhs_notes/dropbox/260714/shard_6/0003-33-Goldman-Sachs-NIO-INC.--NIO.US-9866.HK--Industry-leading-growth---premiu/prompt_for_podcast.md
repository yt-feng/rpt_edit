## (2) Strong brand power creates higher competitive moat

Additionally, according to the Net Promoter Score (NPS) surveyed by JLL, Nio brand is ranking No.1 and Firefly brand is ranking No.2 among all brands in the China market as of

1H26 (Exhibit 5), mainly thanks to 1) product experience, sales and delivery services and after-sales services for Nio brand, and 2) more balanced strengths in product quality, brand and services for Firefly brand. Nio's ES8 also ranked No.2 among D-segment SUVs in terms of NPS as of 1H26 (Exhibit 6), reflecting high satisfaction ratings from consumers for this model. We believe this is a reflection of Nio's strong foothold in the premium segment, supported not only by its products but also services. We expect this strong brand equity will create a higher competitive moat and could be more difficult to displace by competitors.

Looking ahead, we believe the company can apply similar strategy to its 5 series and 6 series models (priced between Rmb200k-400k) and rejuvenate the sales volume of these models in 2027E and beyond, further solidifying its positioning in the premium market and driving continued market share gain.
