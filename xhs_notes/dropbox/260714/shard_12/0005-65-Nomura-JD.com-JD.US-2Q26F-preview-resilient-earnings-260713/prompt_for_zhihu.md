## Company profile

JD is one of the leading ecommerce platforms in China with a focus on 1P model. Its platform also provides marketplace and advertising services to third-party merchants. JD's subsidiary JD Logistics provides logistics services including transportation, warehousing and delivery.

## Valuation Methodology

Our SOTP-based TP is USD41. We value JD Retail at USD21bn based on 6x FY26F P/E. The benchmark index for the company is Nasdaq Composite.

Risks that may impede the achievement of the target price

Downside risks include 1) further deterioration of macro economy, and 2) weaker margin ramp up for JD Retail and/or JD Logistics.

## ESG

JD's main ecommerce business is generally environment-friendly as it does not cause pollution directly. Its ecommerce business may indirectly lead to an increase in paper waste from packaging materials used for parcels.
