# Chinese AI Models Catching Up, Vulnerabilities Accelerating

Adding TENB to Analyst Focus List, Upgrading QLYS to Neutral, CRWD and PANW Remain Well Positioned for Incremental Demand

![](images/0d38106aeb414f774d345f37332c5b3078d7be000adf0fc18d521094084aea2a.jpg)

Chinese models are catching up; demand poised to accelerate. In our April note on Project Glasswing, we walked through Anthropic's frontier-AI vulnerability discovery launch and concluded the resilience factors across our preferred names remained intact, with CRWD and PANW best positioned as essential defensive layers rather than disruption targets. The picture has moved since. Open-weight Chinese AI models are closing the gap with frontier models in vulnerability discovery capabilities. Meanwhile, we already see evidence of vulnerability acceleration, illustrated by CVEs tracked for the first quarter of the year (Figure 1). This comes at a time when enterprises are preparing to assess and manage a 'tsunami of vulnerabilities' expected across their IT estates, and the progress of the Chinese models likely accelerates levels of urgency. Accordingly, we are adding TENB to our Analyst Focus List as we view TENB as the best positioned exposure management vendor to help organizations face this threat. We are also upgrading QLYS from Underweight to Neutral as we think the company will also benefit across its core installed base, and we could see upside to growth expectations in spite of the execution issues that the company has been managing through. Outside of the exposure management vendors, we anticipate that the collapse of mean time to exploit will also accelerate demand across the rest of our coverage universe as the number of vulnerabilities (and likely exploits) accelerate. We continue to view consolidators that have effectively invested in their platforms as best positioned to benefit from incremental demand with PANW and CRWD being best positioned.

Tenable: Best positioned to capture acceleration of exposure management demand. We think an acceleration in vulnerability volumes will likely lift exposure management vendors with VM exposure, and we view TENB as best positioned to benefit. In addition to greater asset coverage under the TENB platform, acceleration of the vulnerability landscape could drive better new product adoption across the company's exposure management platform, in our view. Hexa AI went GA on May 20, 2026 inside Tenable One Foundation and Advanced and is not available to standalone VM customers, making the product a concrete reason in the cycle to upgrade off legacy scanning.
