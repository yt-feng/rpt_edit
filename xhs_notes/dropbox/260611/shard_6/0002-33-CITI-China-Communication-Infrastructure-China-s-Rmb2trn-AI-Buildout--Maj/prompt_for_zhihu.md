## Data Center Industry Tailwinds

At an assumed all-in unit cost of approximately RMB200m/MW (inclusive of data center build-out and AI servers), the 2 trillion yuan plan implies the addition of approximately \~10GW of new AIDC capacity over five years, equivalent to \~2GW per annum.
