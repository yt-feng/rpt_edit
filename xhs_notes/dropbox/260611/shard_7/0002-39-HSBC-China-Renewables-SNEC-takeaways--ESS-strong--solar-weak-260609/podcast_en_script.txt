EN_B: Exactly. Look at markets outside China, the EU, and the US. Their combined solar and wind penetration is approaching double digits. Grids are struggling to handle that. They need storage to fix the bottleneck. The report argues that policy priority will shift to storage deployment. That's a big tailwind for companies like Deye, which had about 24% of the global residential storage inverter market in 2024.
EN_A: What about utility-scale storage? Not as rosy?
EN_B: Right. The EU passed a ban in April 2026, stopping EU funds from going to renewable projects that use Chinese inverters. That's a risk for companies like Sungrow.
