ZH_A: 最近光伏和储能圈子最热闹的事，就是上海的SNEC展会了。我们拿到了一份刚从展会回来的研报，里面信息量很大。你觉得这次展会最大的感觉是什么？

ZH_B: 一句话总结就是：储能很旺，光伏很弱。尤其是户用储能，参展商的信心非常强。但地面光伏这边，需求前景比较暗淡。

ZH_A: 地面光伏需求暗淡，这听起来有点反直觉。毕竟光伏一直在涨，怎么突然就弱了？

ZH_B: 关键原因是政策切换。中国从2025年6月开始，把光伏的补贴电价改成了市场化定价。这直接影响了2026年的需求预期。而且竞争更激烈了，像京东方这样的非传统玩家都来展出了自己的组件产品。

ZH_A: 所以光伏这边是供过于求。那储能这边呢？你说户用储能很旺，数据上有什么支撑？

ZH_B: 有很具体的数据。今年前四个月，中国锂电池出口到前十大市场的总额增长了29%。尤其是澳大利亚，增长了1.9倍。这背后是澳大利亚的电池补贴计划在推动。虽然那个计划在5月开始退出，但英国的暖家计划要接棒了，规模是150亿英镑。

ZH_A: 也就是说，全球政策重点正在从推光伏转向推储能？

ZH_B: 没错。你看全球除中国、欧盟和美国之外的市场，太阳能和风能的发电占比正在逼近两位数。电网承受不住了，必须配储能来缓解瓶颈。所以报告判断，政策的优先方向会转向储能部署。这对像德业股份这样的户用储能逆变器龙头是大利好，它在全球的市场份额大约有24%。

ZH_A: 那大型地面电站的储能呢？听起来没那么乐观？

ZH_B: 对。欧盟在2026年4月通过了一个禁令，禁止用欧盟资金支持使用中国逆变器的可再生能源项目。报告估算，这会影响欧洲大约20%的大型地面光伏和储能市场，而工商业项目只影响5%左右。这对阳光电源这样的企业是个风险。

ZH_A: 除了这些，展会上还有什么新技术值得关注？

ZH_B: 有两个技术方向值得聊。一个是空间太阳能用的钙钛矿电池，很多公司都展出了样品。但报告认为，太空应用会非常缓慢，因为可靠性的验证需要时间。反倒是地面应用，比如光伏建筑一体化和智能门锁，可能会更快落地。

ZH_A: 那另一个技术方向呢？

ZH_B: 固态变压器。很多公司展出了原型，但报告认为大规模应用会很渐进，因为电网稳定性和安全问题还没解决。相比之下，800伏高压直流技术更成熟，对现有数据中心基础设施改动小，落地会更快。

ZH_A: 所以这次展会透露出的核心投资逻辑是什么？

ZH_B: 核心就是：户用储能正在迎来政策红利期，而光伏和大型储能则面临政策逆风。具体的个股机会，比如德业股份和宏发股份，报告里有详细的估值和风险分析。不过报告里还有一些问题没有完全展开。

ZH_A: 什么问题？

ZH_B: 比如，欧盟的禁令会不会进一步扩大范围？澳大利亚补贴退出后，需求会不会断崖式下跌？这些都需要后续数据来验证。完整的报告里对这些风险有更深入的讨论。

EN_A: So we just got back from the SNEC conference in Shanghai, which is basically the biggest solar and energy storage event in China. What was the vibe like?

EN_B: It was a story of two sides. Energy storage suppliers, especially residential ones, were really confident. But the solar side was pretty gloomy.

EN_A: Gloomy for solar? That sounds surprising. I thought solar was still booming.

EN_B: The key reason is a policy shift in China. They moved from a feed-in tariff to a market-based pricing system in June 2025. That's hitting demand expectations for 2026. And competition is getting tougher, with non-traditional players like BOE showing their own solar modules.

EN_A: So oversupply on the solar side. What about energy storage? You said residential was strong. Any numbers to back that up?

EN_B: Absolutely. China's lithium battery exports to its top 10 markets surged 29% year-on-year in the first four months of 2026. Australia was a standout, up 1.9 times, driven by their Cheaper Battery Program. That program started phasing out in May, but the UK's Warm Home Plan is coming in late 2026 with a 15 billion pound budget.

EN_A: So the global policy focus is shifting from promoting solar to pushing energy storage?

EN_B: Exactly. Look at markets outside China, the EU, and the US. Their combined solar and wind penetration is approaching double digits. Grids are struggling to handle that. They need storage to fix the bottleneck. The report argues that policy priority will shift to storage deployment. That's a big tailwind for companies like Deye, which had about 24% of the global residential storage inverter market in 2024.

EN_A: What about utility-scale storage? Not as rosy?

EN_B: Right. The EU passed a ban in April 2026, stopping EU funds from going to renewable projects that use Chinese inverters. The report estimates this could impact about 20% of the utility-scale solar and storage market in Europe, compared to just 5% for commercial and industrial projects. That's a risk for companies like Sungrow.

EN_A: Any new technologies that caught your eye at the conference?

EN_B: Two things. First, space-grade perovskite solar cells. Lots of prototypes on display. But the report thinks space adoption will be slow because reliability data takes years to gather. On the ground, though, things like building-integrated solar and smart locks could see faster adoption.

EN_A: And the second technology?

EN_B: Solid-state transformers. Several companies showed prototypes, but the report says widespread use will be gradual due to grid stability and safety concerns. In contrast, 800-volt high-voltage direct current technology is more mature and requires fewer changes to existing data center infrastructure, so it could roll out faster.

EN_A: So what's the main investment takeaway from this report?

EN_B: The core idea is that residential storage is entering a sweet spot of policy support, while solar and utility-scale storage face headwinds. The report has detailed valuations and risk analysis for specific names like Deye and Hongfa. But there are some open questions.
