But the big surprise was Tokyo’s April core CPI, which printed well below expectations, partly because of a new free childcare program. The Bank of Japan kept rates unchanged at 0.75 percent in April, but three out of nine board members wanted a hike to 1.0 percent. The report still sees a July rate hike as the base case, but it’s conditional on data showing the economy won’t take a major hit.
