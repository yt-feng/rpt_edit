# Natural Gas Comment: Preliminary Signs of Asia LNG Demand Recovery Keep Risks to 2H26 TTF Skewed to the Upside

We have argued that weak Asia demand for LNG has bought Europe time. The lower the LNG imports in places like China, South Korea and Japan, the higher the LNG volume available to go to Europe, with this dynamic ultimately limiting the risk premium embedded in TTF prices.   
However, because we expect LNG imports to rebound in China and South Korea in particular relative to this spring's lows, driven by summer demand and storage rebuilding, we have argued that risks to TTF (and Asia LNG) prices remain skewed to the upside, should the Hormuz disruption linger.
