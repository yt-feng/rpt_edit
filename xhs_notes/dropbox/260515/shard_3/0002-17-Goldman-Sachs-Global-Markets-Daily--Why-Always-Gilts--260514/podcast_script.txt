ZH_A: 最近英国30年期国债收益率突破了5.8%，这是1998年以来的最高水平，你怎么看这个现象？
ZH_B: 这确实是个大数字，但更值得关注的是背后的原因。这份报告的核心判断是，英国国债的弱势表现主要来自期限溢价的上升，而这是有宏观逻辑支撑的。
ZH_A: 期限溢价是什么？为什么它会上升？
ZH_B: 简单说，期限溢价是投资者持有长期债券要求的额外回报。英国经济在过去几年呈现低增长、高通胀的组合，这削弱了债券作为避险资产的价值，投资者自然要求更高的补偿。
ZH_A: 那英国的情况和其他国家有什么不同？
ZH_B: 区别很大。报告显示，无论是增长不确定性还是通胀不确定性，英国在主要经济体中都是最高的。而且英国能源结构高度依赖天然气，加上工资和价格有较高的通胀指数化特征，这让不确定性更深层。
ZH_A: 所以这是英国特有的问题？
ZH_B: 不完全。报告特别强调，全球长期收益率都在重新定价，日本、德国、美国的30年收益率也都处于高位。英国的问题有全球背景。
ZH_A: 那英国国债最近几个月的弱势，是不是因为风险溢价在上升？
ZH_B: 恰恰相反。报告发现，最近几个月的弱势主要来自市场对英国央行政策路径的重新定价，而不是风险溢价本身。也就是说，市场在调整对利率走向的预期。
ZH_A: 这个区别很重要。那供给和需求方面呢？
ZH_B: 这是另一个关键因素。英国财政赤字巨大，加上量化紧缩，导致国债供给大增。同时，国内养老金系统的需求在2022年危机后结构性下降，供需失衡推动了收益率上行。
ZH_A: 这意味着什么？投资者该怎么看？
ZH_B: 报告的核心启示是，虽然短期内有财政和政治风险，但宏观基本面才是主导因素。如果英国央行在2026年维持利率、2027年降息，那将是英国国债的长期利好。
ZH_A: 听起来还有不少悬念，报告里有没有没完全解答的问题？
ZH_B: 有的。比如财政政策变化对供给预期的影响，报告估算中期赤字每增加1个百分点，收益率可能上升30到40个基点。但具体如何演变，还需要看政策走向。
ZH_A: 那量化紧缩的退出时机呢？
ZH_B: 这也是个开放问题。报告提到量化紧缩会随着央行缩表而放缓，但具体节奏和影响，完整报告里有更详细的模型分析。

EN_A: So the UK 30-year gilt yield just hit 5.8 percent, a level we haven't seen since 1998. What's going on?
EN_B: It's a striking number, but the real story is why. This report argues that gilt underperformance is mostly about a big rise in term premium, and there's a solid macro reason behind it.
EN_A: Term premium? Can you break that down?
EN_B: Sure. It's the extra compensation investors demand for holding long-term bonds. The UK has had weak growth and high inflation, which makes bonds less useful as a hedge. So investors want more return for taking that risk.
EN_A: Is this just a UK problem?
EN_B: Not entirely. The report points out that global long-end yields are repricing everywhere. Japan, Germany, and the US all have high 30-year yields. But the UK stands out because it has the highest macro and policy uncertainty among major economies.
EN_A: So what's driving the recent weakness in gilts?
EN_B: Surprisingly, it's not about rising risk premium. The report shows that recent moves are more about repricing of the Bank of England's policy path. The market is adjusting its view on where rates are headed, not demanding more compensation for uncertainty.
EN_A: That's a key distinction. What about supply and demand?
EN_B: That's another big factor. The UK has a huge fiscal deficit and the Bank of England is doing quantitative tightening, so bond supply is high. Meanwhile, pension fund demand has dropped structurally after the 2022 crisis. That imbalance pushes yields up.
EN_A: So what does this mean for investors?
EN_B: The key takeaway is that macro fundamentals will dominate in the long run. If the BoE holds rates in 2026 and cuts in 2027, that could be a major relief for gilts. But near-term fiscal risks remain.
EN_A: Any big questions the report doesn't fully answer?
EN_B: Yes. One is how fiscal policy changes might affect supply expectations. The report estimates a 1 percent increase in the deficit could add 30 to 40 basis points to yields. But the exact path is uncertain.
EN_A: And the timing of quantitative tightening ending?
EN_B: That's another open question. The report notes QT will slow as the balance sheet shrinks, but the details are in the full report. There's a lot more model analysis there.
