# AI demand structure is unchanged

The expansion of coding AI has led to a sharp increase in demand for tokens and AI inference, and the fundamentals of AI data centre-related businesses are likely to remain robust. We believe growth in AI-related hardware demand is likely to continue, including for semiconductors, semiconductor manufacturing equipment, electric power, and high-performance packaging. However, the performance of coding AI is advancing not only through Anthropic Mythos but also via multiple product launches and improvements from OpenAI's Squad and China's Kimi, among others. Concerns about software-related businesses, including the "death of SaaS" narrative, are likely to persist. In the domestic IT services market, the greatest risk factor appears to be the shift by customers toward in-house development.

# Among tech conglomerates, focus on SoftBank and SONY

We think growth in SoftBank's share price is likely to be driven by ARM due to capturing AI inference demand and the growing business value of OpenAI, due to accelerated monetisation from stronger coding AI. SONY has managed to offset the negative impact of DRAM price hikes, which was the most concerning issue before the full-year results announcement, through price increases. Furthermore, the share price is currently at a low level that, in our view, excessively reflects concerns about content value erosion due to AI. Panasonic's share price mostly reflects positive factors such as data centre BBUs and structural reform effects, pushing it down in terms of preference. At Fujitsu, a profit decline for hardware is likely hard to avoid in Q1, and we believe the outlook will remain challenging for a while.

# Among semiconductor-related companies, focus on Tokyo Electron and Renesas

Tokyo Electron's price hike was one development not anticipated in this results season. Following this, market share and profit margins seem likely to expand going forward. At Renesas, sales appear likely to continue growing qoq in April-June as well, which would mark a sixth straight quarter of sales growth and reconfirm an upcycle. Overseas competitors have implemented further price hikes, following those in March-April. We expect Renesas to also see profit margin improvement. While Advantest and Disco are seeing solid trends amid tailwinds from AI demand, we believe their multiples are excessively high, leaving little appeal in their share prices at present.
