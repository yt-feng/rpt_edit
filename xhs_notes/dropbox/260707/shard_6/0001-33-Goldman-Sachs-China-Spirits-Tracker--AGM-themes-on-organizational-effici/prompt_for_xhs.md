# China Spirits Tracker: AGM themes on organizational efficiency and product differentiation; Original case Feitian Wholesale price still

In June-July MTD, leading spirits companies held their AGMs, and the key themes focused on protecting price discipline and channel health rather than pursuing shipment growth, while continuing to invest for differentiated products, organizational efficiency and selective market expansion to strengthen long-term competitiveness. The industry's strategic focus has clearly shifted from sell-in driven growth to consumer-led, high-quality growth, while destocking will still take time and remain the key focus of spirits brands and channels.

National brands: Leading players continue to concentrate resources on their core SKUs while simplifying product portfolios.
