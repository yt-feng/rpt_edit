## ORAL GLP-1 LAUNCH DYNAMICS

Early, daily-to-weekly script conversion for Foundayo seems to correlate (Exhibit 12), but large gaps in conversion of absolute daily TRx into weekly numbers continue (Exhibit 14). Novo has been suggesting IQVIA has a capture rate of only 50% for the Wegovy pill to date - similar to the low capture rates we saw for the Zepbound vial when launched in Lilly Direct, and triangulating with their new 3x tirzepatide figure.
