EN_B: This is where it gets counterintuitive. Most AI capital spending is on imported goods, like semiconductors from Asia, and those don't even show up in US GDP calculations. But core PCE inflation is more stubborn, partly because the stock market rally pushes up imputed financial services prices, and software and accessories have a much bigger weight in PCE than in CPI. That said, trimmed-mean measures look more benign.
