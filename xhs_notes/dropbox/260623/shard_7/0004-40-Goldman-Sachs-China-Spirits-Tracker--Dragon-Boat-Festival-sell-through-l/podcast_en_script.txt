EN_A: So the Dragon Boat Festival just wrapped up. What does the data say about China's baijiu market?
EN_B: The headline is a slight year-on-year recovery, but it's coming off a very low base. Demand remains highly polarized. Super-premium brands did well, but the upper-mid tier is still under serious pressure.
EN_A: So not all brands are benefiting. What is this report really trying to figure out?
EN_B: The core question is whether demand has bottomed out after the anti-graft policy and consumer slowdown. Dragon Boat Festival is a good test because it sits between 618 and the mid-year, so it reflects real sell-through rather than just channel filling.
EN_A: What's the first big insight? Is super-premium really that strong?
EN_B: Yes. Moutai and Wuliangye saw 10 to 20 percent plus year-on-year growth in most regions, with prepayments at 60 to 70 percent. But remember, last year's festival was a disaster. So this is a low-base rebound, not a full recovery.
EN_A: What about mid-range and mass-market products? Are they doing any better?
EN_B: Not really. Except for a few key SKUs like King's Luck Danya, most mid-tier brands were flat or down. Yanghe was slightly down in the second quarter. Luzhou Laojiao saw a significant decline in Henan. What this means is that consumers are either buying the best or not buying at all. There's no trading down happening.
EN_A: Did the online 618 data tell the same story?
EN_B: Exactly the same. JD's 618 report showed overall baijiu GMV grew 25 percent year-on-year, but it was almost entirely driven by Moutai and Wuliangye. Moutai Feitian original case GMV hit 300 million RMB. Wuliangye's World Cup edition grew over 13 times. Mid-tier brands were barely mentioned.
EN_A: What about regional differences? Any bright spots?
EN_B: Divergence persists. Anhui remains weak due to constrained business and government activities. Jiangsu and Sichuan saw a slight recovery. Jiangsu is benefiting from tech sector development. Sichuan's business demand grew by a teens percentage year-on-year, but it's still 30 to 40 percent below 2024 levels.
EN_B: So the picture is mixed. Some regions are stabilizing, but the overall demand is still far from normal.
EN_A: And what about wholesale prices? Has Moutai's price held up?
EN_B: In the past week, original case Feitian Moutai dropped from 1,670 to 1,645 RMB per bottle. Unpacked fell from 1,635 to 1,630. Wuliangye and Guojiao 1573 were roughly flat. Non-standard Moutai SKUs like the Zodiac and 1-liter bottles saw some declines. So prices are stable but not strong.
EN_A: Are there any big questions the report leaves open? What should we watch next?
EN_B: Yes, two key unknowns. First, can this rebound sustain through the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day? Second, if business demand stays weak, can gifting and self-consumption alone keep super-premium growing? Also, King's Luck Danya's strong performance — is it a sustainable trend or just a short-term channel strategy? The report doesn't fully answer that yet.
