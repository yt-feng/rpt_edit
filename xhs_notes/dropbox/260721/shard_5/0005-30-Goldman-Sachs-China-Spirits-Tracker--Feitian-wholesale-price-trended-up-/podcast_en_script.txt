EN_B: Not really. The report says most distributors expected this price hike, though it came a bit earlier and was a bit bigger than anticipated. Moutai's main goal is to keep channel profitability stable, not to push inventory. Distributor inventory is only about two weeks, which is very lean.

EN_A: So they are sacrificing short-term sales for long-term pricing health. Does that make sense?
