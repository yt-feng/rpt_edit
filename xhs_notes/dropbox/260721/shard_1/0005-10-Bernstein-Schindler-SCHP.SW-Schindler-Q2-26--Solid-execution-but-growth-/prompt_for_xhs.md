# Schindler Q2'26: Solid execution but growth disappoints again

Schindler released its Q2'26 results today, ahead of its conference call at 10.00 AM CEST.

Headline view: We expect investors to react slightly negatively to the headline results. Whilst margin progression continued to be solid (Q2 +30bps vs cons), Q2 organic sales growth of 1.1% was -160bps below consensus. Schindler has maintained its FY top line guidance for LSD to MSD (local currency growth) although this requires a strong acceleration in H2 to meet even the lower end. We expect consensus of 3.1% for the FY (5% implied H2 growth) to be trimmed subject to more detail from the call.

Positives: (1) Order growth ahead of expectations. Schindler saw sequentially accelerating LFL order growth at the group level of 2.9% (+40bps vs cons, +10bps vs Q1'26) driven by Modernization order growth of c.11%, while Schindler call out New Installations (units) order growth in EMEA was strong at 12.8% for H1 (Q1 >10%), citing market share gains. Americas Modernization and New Installations order intake sequentially softened from Q1, while selectivity in Service has continued delivering small negative LFL order growth. (2) Margins outperformed. Margins of 13.5% are up +30bps vs cons and +90bps yoy, with Schindler citing efficiencies, mix, and pricing driving the progression. We note Schindler omit 'Mix impact' from their Q2'26 outlook (as compared to Q1), which could imply Modernization margin improvement is neutralising the otherwise negative mix effect, coupled with NI rebalancing China exposure through the degrowth. Absolute reported EBIT beat cons by +1%.

Negatives: (1) Sales missed expectations. Sales growth (LFL) of 1.1% for Q2 came in -160bps below cons (and -80bps vs BernE), with the Schindler citing China weakness. Sales came in -2% below cons. (2) Guidance unchanged implies back-end loaded H2'26. We had expected Schindler to need to downgrade guidance for FY26, and they posted growth below our expectations (-80bps) and held the guide. We think even the top end of LSD (3% LFL, -10bps vs cons) may be slipping out of reach, requiring 4.6% LFL growth for H2'26 (+60bps vs cons, and 3x H1'26 run-rate), while MSD growth requires H2'26 to beat cons by at least +260bps, and seems unlikely.

Focus on the call will likely be more detail on business area order and revenue growth, current trends in China, duration of selectivity approach, and commentary on margin levers.
