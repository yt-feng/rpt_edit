Specialty pharmacies, on the other hand, actually dispense the drugs and provide patient care. The interesting thing is, because the pharmacy role is more clearly a core healthcare function, it faces less policy risk than the PBM model.
EN_A: So investors might prefer specialty pharmacies because they are more stable?
EN_B: That is one of the big questions the report tries to answer. Specialty pharmacies do have lower policy risk, but they also face the threat of regulatory change. If PBMs are forced to divest their pharmacy arms, the whole competitive landscape could shift. The report explores this, but it does not give a final answer.
