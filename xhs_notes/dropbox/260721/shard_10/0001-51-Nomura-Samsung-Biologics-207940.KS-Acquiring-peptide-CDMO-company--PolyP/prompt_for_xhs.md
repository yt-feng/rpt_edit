# Acquiring peptide CDMO company (PolyPeptide) Quick Note

Samsung Biologics (SB) on 20 July announced to acquire PolyPeptide Group AG (PPGN) (PPGN SW, Not rated; market cap USD1.7bn), a peptide CDMO company. SB plans to secure a $100\%$ stake through a tender offer, with total deal value amounting KRW2.7tn. This acquisition represents SB's strategic expansion beyond its antibody contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) capabilities into the peptide CDMO market. We regard SB's key strategic rationale as it could capture the rapidly growing outsourcing demand for GLP-1 peptide manufacturing.

PPGN recorded revenue of EUR236.6bn (+41.6% y-y) in 1H26 with EBITDA margin of 20.7%. The company guided for 2026E revenue growth of 25-30% y-y and high-teens EBITDA margin. Based on PPGN's record (PPGN is participating in one-third of global peptide Phase III trials) and solvent reduction technology, the company has secured contracts with many of the global top 20 big pharmas, to our knowledge.

Total deal value of KRW2.7tn, assuming 100% stake acquisition

\- On 20 July, SB announced its plans to acquire $100\%$ of PPGN at CHF44.31/share, implying an equity value of CHF1.46bn (KRW2.7tn). The acquisition will be financed through a combination of existing cash (KRW1.6tn of cash, cash & equivalent, as of 1Q26), future operating cash flow, and debt while management indicated that equity financing could be a potential option.

\- Prior to the launch of the tender offer, Draupnir Holding B.V. (unlisted), PolyPeptide's controlling shareholder with 55.6% interest, irrevocably committed to tendering its entire stake. As such, SB has secured control over the majority of the shares. Completion of the transaction is conditional upon SB acquiring at least two-thirds (66.7%) of the voting rights through the tender offer. The tender offer will be launched by end-August 2026 and continue for \~2 months.

## Our thoughts – capturing a share of growing GLP-1 market at 2026E EV/EBITDA of 19.1x

We view the acquisition as strategically attractive, EPS accretive, and financially reasonable.
