# Semiconductor/Tech Materials: Earnings Outlook (Apr-Jun)

## Watch WFE and pricing for semiconductors, semi-related for glass, and non-domestic for cement

Our order of interest for Apr-Jun results is semiconductors > glass > cement. Semiconductor-related stocks have recently corrected due to concerns about the AI market overheating, but we maintain a bullish stance as consensus earnings outlooks are continuing to get revised. We raised our WFE market outlook on June 17, and now forecast growth at 28% YoY in 2026, 29% in 2027, and 16% in 2028 (see here for details), but we believe there is still upside potential. Amid discussions about price revisions, we are also watching for initiatives to raise profit margins without relying solely on demand. Key focus points in the glass subsector are production capacity trends at Nittobo (3110; Overweight) and longer-term TAM growth in terms of M9 and MLCs (multi-layer cores). In the cement subsector, with domestic demand remaining weak, we think attention could focus on trends for non-domestic earnings drivers (electrostatic chucks at Sumitomo Osaka Cement, US business at Taiheiyo Cement).

\- Semiconductor: We highlight Kioxia Holdings (285A, Overweight; co-covered with Jay Kwon), Tokyo Electron (8035, Overweight), and Advantest (6857, Overweight). Key points for Kioxia include recent price trends, the direction of LTAs, and progress for Super High IOPS. For Tokyo Electron, we expect attention to focus on a progress update for price revisions and the details of additional initiatives. When reporting Jan-Mar results, Tokyo Electron said it aims to raise the gross margin to 50% in two years, but we expect it to achieve this sooner. As for Advantest, we are interested in trends for GPUs and ASICs and whether it comments on CPUs and CPOs. We expect further earnings upside from TAM growth for CPUs and CPOs.

\- Glass: We highlight Nittobo (3110, Overweight) and Nippon Electric Glass (5214, Overweight). Nittobo's share price has corrected sharply on concerns about intensifying competition and views of its pricing strategy, but we still take a positive view of its longer-term presence. We believe Nittobo might not announce specific plans to raise production capacity when it reports Apr-Jun results, but we anticipate TAM growth driven by the adoption of NER-glass for M9 and T-glass in the future for MLCs. We believe there could be strong interest in the direction of production capacity expansion and related pricing strategies. At NEG, we will watch for the details of glass fiber business restructuring announced on July 6 and for any comments that could bolster the outlook for a recovery in semiconductor support glass.

\- Cement: As domestic cement demand remains weak, we think each company's specific initiatives to increase capacity utilization (e.g., expansion of exports, reduction of production capacity, cement business initiatives with other companies) are a key focus point in FY2026. Cement companies do not plan to hold briefings for Apr-Jun results and we think major announcements are unlikely, but we will focus on medium-term initiatives. In the near term, a key point for Sumitomo Osaka Cement is the degree of recovery for electrostatic chucks, where the company's forte is NAND applications (we expect its NAND investment to grow 30% YoY in CY2026). At Taiheiyo Cement, we think attention could focus on synergies from the acquisition of Vulcan Materials' ready-mixed concrete business assets in California.
