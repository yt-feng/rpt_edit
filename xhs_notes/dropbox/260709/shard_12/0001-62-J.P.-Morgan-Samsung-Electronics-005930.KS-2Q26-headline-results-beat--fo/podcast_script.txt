ZH_A: 最近三星电子公布了二季度初步业绩，听说大幅超出了市场预期，这个数字到底有多炸裂？

ZH_B: 确实很震撼。二季度营业利润达到了89.4万亿韩元，比市场预期的75到84万亿韩元高出一大截，同比增幅超过18倍。但这里有个关键细节——里面包含了超过15万亿韩元的一次性劳资成本拨备。

ZH_A: 等一下，包含了这么大一笔拨备还能超预期？那实际经营表现岂不是更猛？

ZH_B: 没错，这正是报告的核心洞察。剔除拨备后，实际经营利润可能超过100万亿韩元。核心驱动力是存储芯片价格超预期上涨，特别是NAND闪存，季度涨幅超过70%，比分析师预期的53%高了很多。

ZH_A: 那这波涨价能持续吗？市场好像一直担心周期见顶。

ZH_B: 报告认为远未见顶。它提出了一个关键判断：目前存储芯片的供应满足率只有50%到60%，也就是说近一半的需求无法被满足。这种供需缺口还会持续扩大，因为超大规模云服务商对企业级SSD的需求正在爆发。

ZH_A: 所以这意味着什么？对投资者来说，这个判断有多重要？

ZH_B: 这意味着盈利预期还有大幅上调空间。目前三星的远期市盈率只有5.2倍，是全球最便宜的存储芯片股。市场只定价了很小一部分结构性变化，比如多年短缺、长期协议占比提升等。报告认为，市场严重低估了这轮上行周期的持续时间。

ZH_A: 那除了存储芯片，三星的其他业务表现如何？比如晶圆代工。

ZH_B: 这里有个很有意思的进展。随着台积电先进制程产能紧张，三星代工正在接触多个新客户，包括谷歌、Anthropic、Meta、AMD、比亚迪等，用于AI芯片项目。这可能会成为下一个催化剂。但报告也指出，劳资成本结算后，代工部门的固定成本压力更大了，新订单的获得对业务扭亏至关重要。

ZH_A: 听起来机会很大，但风险也不小。报告有没有提到哪些不确定性？

ZH_B: 当然有。这里我留两个悬念：第一，关于HBM内存，三星在技术领先性上还没有完全证明自己，这关系到未来能否赢得更多AI客户；第二，报告提到下半年可能会看到客户因为成本压力而出现抵制，但服务器需求的增长又可能抵消这部分影响。这两个问题在完整报告中有更深入的分析。

ZH_A: 那最后的结论是什么？投资者现在应该怎么做？

ZH_B: 报告给出了48万韩元的目标价，相比当前股价有超过60%的上涨空间。核心逻辑是“更长更高的上行周期”。但关键在于，7月30日三星将公布完整财报，届时会有更多细节，包括长期协议进展和股东回报计划。如果你想了解这些关键催化剂的具体分析，建议去读一下完整的研报，里面还有更多数据和推演。

EN_A: Samsung just reported a blowout second quarter. The headline numbers look incredible. But I am guessing there is more to the story.

EN_B: You are right. Operating profit came in at 89.4 trillion won, way above the market consensus of 75 to 84 trillion. But here is the catch: that number includes over 15 trillion won in one-off labor cost provisions.

EN_A: Wait. So even after booking that huge charge, they still beat expectations? That means the underlying business is even stronger.

EN_B: Exactly. If you strip out the provisions, the real operating profit was probably over 100 trillion. The main driver was memory pricing, especially NAND flash, which jumped over 70 percent quarter on quarter. That was way above the 53 percent analysts had expected.

EN_A: So the big question is, can this pricing momentum last? The market has been worried about a peak cycle.

EN_B: The report says no, not yet. It makes a bold call: memory supply is only meeting 50 to 60 percent of procurement demand. That gap is likely to widen because hyperscalers are ramping up enterprise SSD orders for AI workloads.

EN_A: So what does that mean for investors? Why should they care?

EN_B: It means earnings estimates still have room to go up. Samsung is trading at just 5.2 times forward earnings, the cheapest among global memory stocks. The market is pricing in very little of the structural shift, like multi-year shortages and growing long-term agreements. The report argues investors are underestimating how long this upcycle can last.

EN_A: What about the other parts of Samsung, like foundry? Any good news there?

EN_B: Actually, yes. With TSMC capacity tightening, Samsung Foundry is in talks with several new customers for AI chip projects, including Google, Anthropic, Meta, AMD, and even BYD. That could be a major catalyst. But the report also notes that after the labor cost settlement, the foundry division faces higher fixed cost pressure, so winning those orders is critical for a turnaround.

EN_A: Sounds like a lot of upside, but there must be risks. What are the unknowns?

EN_B: Definitely. I will leave you with two open questions. First, on HBM memory, Samsung has not fully proven its technology leadership yet. That is key to winning more AI customers. Second, the report mentions that customer pushback due to rising costs could emerge in the second half, but server demand may offset that. The full report dives much deeper into these two issues.

EN_A: So what is the bottom line? What should investors do now?

EN_B: The report has a price target of 480,000 won, implying over 60 percent upside from current levels. The core thesis is a longer and higher upcycle. But the full earnings release on July 30 will provide more details on long-term agreements and shareholder returns. If you want to understand the key catalysts and the full data behind this call, I recommend reading the complete report.
