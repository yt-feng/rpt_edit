EN_A: There is a new report on BOE Technology that downgraded the stock from Buy to Neutral, but raised the target price from 5 to 8.7 yuan. That sounds a bit contradictory. How do you read it?
EN_B: It is not as contradictory as it sounds. The target price went up because the report started to factor in the value of new businesses like glass-based packaging. But the downgrade came because they believe those expectations are already priced into the current stock price.
EN_A: So the real question is, how big is BOE's new story? It is not just about displays anymore, right?
EN_B: Exactly. BOE presented three growth curves at its investor day. The first is displays, where they are already the global leader in shipment volume. The second is innovation business, targeting 60 billion yuan in revenue by 2026, about 30 percent of total. The third curve is where the real imagination lies: glass-based packaging, optical interconnect, perovskite photovoltaics, foldable glass, and even robotics and regenerative medicine.
EN_A: That is a very broad portfolio. But what is the single most important area the report focuses on?
EN_B: Glass-based packaging substrates. This is a next-generation material for AI and HPC chips. Compared to traditional PCBs, it offers fundamental advantages in flatness, heat dissipation, and signal integrity. BOE has been working on this for over six years. In 2024, they built China's first pilot line for glass substrates, and in 2025, they delivered second-generation samples.
EN_A: What are the key technical challenges? This sounds extremely difficult to manufacture.
EN_B: There are three major bottlenecks. First, TGV drilling: creating holes with a 20-to-1 aspect ratio in glass without cracks. Second, metal filling: achieving void-free, highly uniform electroplating. Third, build-up and cutting: glass is brittle, so cutting after multi-layer build-up requires chip-free technology. BOE claims breakthroughs in all three areas. Their single-layer substrate passed industry reliability tests, including 1,000 temperature cycles. A 75-millimeter square sample showed only 40 microns of warpage after reflow soldering at 260 degrees Celsius.
EN_A: 40 microns. What does that mean for chip packaging?
EN_B: It means it can meet the tight warpage requirements of AI chips. Traditional PCBs deform under high heat, which can cause solder joint failures. Glass has a coefficient of thermal expansion close to silicon, so it stays much more stable. This allows for higher density and larger area packaging.
EN_A: When does BOE expect to start mass production? Does the report give a timeline?
EN_B: Yes. BOE targets a final mass-production investment decision by mid-2027. So they are still in the validation and optimization phase. The next two years will focus on yield improvement and stable production capability.
EN_A: Their partnership with Corning covers four areas. Which one is closest to commercialization?
EN_B: Perovskite photovoltaics might actually come sooner. BOE expects to make a production investment decision by late 2026 or early 2027, with the business becoming a meaningful pillar by 2028 to 2029. But the report stresses that this depends on meeting requirements for efficiency, stability, and lifetime.
EN_A: What about optical interconnect? That is directly tied to AI data transmission.
EN_B: BOE has developed a Micro LED light source with 1.8 GHz response speed and 3 Gbps transmission rate. They plan to launch a 2D multi-channel demo in 2027, and mature product demos aligned with 1.6T, 3.2T, and 6.4T standards in 2028. But the report clearly states that optical interconnect devices are still at the validation stage and not yet in mass production.
EN_A: So these new businesses are still in the story-telling phase, but the valuation already reflects some of that potential? That is why the downgrade?
EN_B: Exactly. The report believes current valuation already factors in an improving LCD business and potential glass substrate contribution. But there is a big gap between validation and profit contribution. For example, the mass production decision for glass substrates is not until mid-2027, and mature optical interconnect products are not expected until 2028.
EN_A: If we look just at the display business, how is BOE's fundamental performance?
EN_B: There are some positive signals. Consolidation in the LCD TV panel market is nearing completion. The concentration ratio of the top five players has stabilized. They expect the average TV size to reach 55 inches in 2027, which they view as a key point for supply-demand balance.
EN_A: Why is 55 inches that important?
EN_B: Because BOE believes once the average TV size hits 55 inches, the TV panel industry can achieve supply-demand balance. That would be favorable for BOE as a leader in large-size panels. However, on the OLED side, competition may persist for another two years, and the business is still loss-making at the operating level.
