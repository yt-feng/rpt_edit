## Company profile

Alibaba operates China's largest ecommerce platform (Taobao and Tmall). Alibaba is also the largest cloud service provider in China. Aside from ecommerce and cloud businesses, the company also provides digital media services (e.g. Youku), digital map service (Amap), among others.

## Valuation Methodology

We value Alibaba's China Ecommerce Group at USD89bn, based on 5x FY27F P/E; its AliCloud business at USD270bn, based on 7x FY28F P/S; and its net valuation of non-core assets (including international ecommerce business) at USD40bn. The resulting TP is USD178. The benchmark index for this stock is Nasdaq Composite.
