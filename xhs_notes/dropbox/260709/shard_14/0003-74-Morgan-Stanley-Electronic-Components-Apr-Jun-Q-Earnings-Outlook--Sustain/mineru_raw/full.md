# Apr-Jun Q Earnings Outlook: Sustainability of Strong Earnings the Key Consideration

We forecast total Apr-Jun OP for 19 firms will increase 33.6% YoY, with strong demand from AI/data center applications continuing, but rising raw material costs warrant close attention.

## Key Takeaways

We expect continued growth for ABF package substrates for AI/DC semis and MLCCs; rising raw materials cost a key risk for aluminum electrolytic capacitors.

Murata Mfg. still our Top Pick, continue to rate Taiyo Yuden UW. We expect the strength of Murata's high-value-added MLCCs to become increasingly evident.

We also expect Ibiden's earnings expansion to continue over the longer term, but remain UW as market expectations and valuation look too high.

Expect strong Apr-Jun results for beneficiaries of AI/DC-related sales growth, inc. Ibiden & Murata: For Ibiden, we expect strong growth in sales of high-value-added ABF package substrates, with sales for NVIDIA's Rubin platform exceeding those for Blackwell. With use of the declining-balance method, we estimate 1Q depreciation expense will decline by ¥4.5bn QoQ, enabling high earnings from 1Q. For Murata, we expect high earnings from 1Q driven by rising sales of high-value-added MLCCs for AI/DC applications and higher utilization rates in core MLCCs. OW on Murata, TDK, Hirose Electric, Niterra, Alps Alpine: Murata's longer-term earnings growth is driven by an improving mix on rising sales of high-value-added MLCCs for AI/DC use. For TDK, profit growth from rechargeable batteries is limited in F3/27, but expect HDD heads and passive components to drive growth, with rechargeable batteries for DC BBUs to contribute from F3/29. For Hirose, we expect sales growth in connectors for both industrial equipment and AI servers, and continued ROE improvement longer term via enhanced shareholder returns and strategic investments. For Niterra, we expect stable, high level profit from spark plugs to increase gradually, market share gains in exhaust gas oxygen sensors, and continued sales growth of electrostatic chucks for SPE customers in Japan/US. UW on Taiyo Yuden, Ibiden, Nippon Chemi-Con, Daishinku: Taiyo Yuden's core MLCCs should continue expanding, but we see the earnings gap vs. Murata (higher weighting of high-value-added MLCCs for AI/DC) widening, and valuation is rich. Ibiden's near-term earnings are strong, but bar to achieving its F3/31 OP target of ¥300bn is high (if achieved, F3/31 P/E still a lofty ~24x). Chemi-Con has risk to 2H F3/27 OP guidance (¥5bn) and high valuation (~50x F3/27e EPS for common shares, post dilution & preferred divs). For Daishinku, we're cautious on risk of delayed contributions from crystal oscillators for optical transceivers or failure to gain meaningful share, slowing demand for crystal devices in Chinese smartphones/PCs.
