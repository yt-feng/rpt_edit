## Key Takeaways from Inaugural MS Biopharma Symposium

\- Global recognition of China innovation is moving beyond engineering to the 2.0 Era - more novel modalities, disease areas and next-gen technologies.

• Correspondingly, BD deal models are broadening from pure out-licensing to NewCo, Co-co (co-commercialization) and strategic partnerships.

\- Clean IP, corporate structure and globally translatable clinical development strategies are additional factors to consider in preparing for globalization.

\- AI-driven drug discovery is shifting from platform narrative to asset generation.

\- Hengrui is an example of China biopharma globalization, underpinned by end-to-end development capabilities and monetized via versatile BD models.

## CRO/CDMO – Demand for China CRO Services Is on the Rise

\- MNCs/biopharmas are more inclined to partner with Chinese CROs due to their advantages on cost, efficiency, tech capability and favorable regulatory environment.

\- The IND review process has been cut from 60 to 30 days in China.

\- The US FDA has now unveiled plans to shorten the IND phase by 6-12 months, following its call to ban the use of China data in IND applications, to compete with China.
