## Investment Thesis

Wanhua is one of the world's largest producers of polyurethanes, with a $\sim 30\%$ global market share for MDI, $19\%$ for TDI and $21\%$ for aliphatic diisocyanates (ADI). Wanhua holds a distinct global cost leadership in MDI and presents long-term growth potential from fine chemicals. In Jan 2026, the company successfully completed feedstock switching of its 1mtpa ethylene cracker in Yantai from propane to ethane while its phase II 1.2mtpa cracker allows flexible switching between naphtha and ethane feedstock. In an \$70-80/bbl oil price environment, we expect ECC margins to continue to outperform NCC. We rate the stock OW.
