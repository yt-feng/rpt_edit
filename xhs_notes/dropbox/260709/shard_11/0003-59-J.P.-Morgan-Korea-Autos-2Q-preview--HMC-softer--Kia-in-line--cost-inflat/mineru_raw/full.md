## Investment Thesis

We reiterate a positive view on Hyundai Motor (HMC). Near-term earnings face headwinds from a slower utilization rate (~85%), input cost increases led by aluminum, and volume softness in Korea amid an aging model cycle. However, we expect a meaningful recovery in 2H as new models launch across Korea, the US, and Europe, driving volume normalization and utilization rate improvement. Beyond the core auto business, tangible progress in HMG's robotics and autonomous driving efforts provides additional upside optionality.

## Valuation

Our Dec-27 price target of W640,000 is based on SOTP: 1) valuing the auto business at a referenced valuation multiple; and 2) including the value of a 28% stake in Hyundai Motor Group's robotics business at approximately W14tn, which reflects the mid-point of our analysis after applying a holding company discount.
