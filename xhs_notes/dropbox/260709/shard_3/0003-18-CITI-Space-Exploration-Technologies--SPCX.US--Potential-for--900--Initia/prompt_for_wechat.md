【金字塔原理写作原则】
1. 结论先行：文章开头先回答“这份报告最值得看的判断是什么”，而不是介绍背景。
2. 统领思想：全文只能服务一个主判断，避免变成摘要合集。
3. 纵向回答：每一层都要回答上一层提出的“为什么”或“所以呢”。
4. 横向 MECE：每个一级小节必须彼此独立、共同支撑主判断，避免重叠。
5. Synthesis over summary：不要复述报告段落，要提炼“这些事实合在一起意味着什么”。
6. So what：每个小节末尾必须落到对行业、公司、竞争格局、市场定价或读者观察框架的含义。
7. Action title：所有 `##` 小标题必须是“直接讲述洞察的完整句子”，不能是目录标签。

## Potential for \$900+; Initiating at Buy Summary

SPCX is a highly vertically integrated disruptor leveraging unrivaled capabilities in launch to establish dominant positions in Global Connectivity and Artificial Intelligence. Because the company's ultimate advantage is rooted in its ability to deliver mass to orbit at greater scale and lower unit cost than any other entity on the planet, if SPCX succeeds in demonstrating key technological and operational milestones in the upcoming, catalyst-rich \~2-3 years without faltering, then we see a path to generating \~\$1 trillion in revenue and \~\$830B in Adj EBITDA in 2031. In that scenario, and if milestones have been hit smoothly, we can imagine the stock moving toward a long-term valuation level of over \$900/share, justified by SPCX's superior ability to scale and growth outlook even beyond 2031. More likely, as SPCX themselves has quipped, they are very good at delivering results – but not always on time. Accordingly, we initiate with a \$200 base-case price target implying 25% upside, which is a heavily discounted version of the \$900/share+ we believe can ultimately be achieved, and anticipate raising our price target at regular intervals as risks are retired, milestones are hit, and launch cadence grows. Below, we highlight a number of upcoming milestones of particular importance and elaborate on valuation at the end of this section.

## Key milestones

Looking forward, we focus on the following key milestones:

■ Starship commences payload delivery to orbit in the second half of 2026 following additional flight tests.

■ SPCX demonstrates the ability to catch the Starship second stage with the “chopsticks” by the end of 2026.

■ Next generation of Grok (including Grok 5) trained on Blackwell clusters closes gap vs. other frontier model providers by the end of 2026.

\- Cursor’s Composer 3 model release drives additional share gains in AI coding market by the end of 2026.

■ Approval for Starlink to offer broadband and/or D2D in additional countries.

■ Launch Starship ‘tens of times’ in 2027, ‘hundreds of times’ by 2028, and ‘thousands of times’ by 2031.

■ Deployment of the Starlink V3 satellite.

■ Successfully launching the first iteration of the orbital AI satellite (AI1).

■ Demonstrating full and rapid reusability of the Starship by turning the spacecraft and boosters to refly within 24 hours.

\- Successfully introducing Starship V4 (\~200 ton variant) and demonstrating its ability to operate at scale.
