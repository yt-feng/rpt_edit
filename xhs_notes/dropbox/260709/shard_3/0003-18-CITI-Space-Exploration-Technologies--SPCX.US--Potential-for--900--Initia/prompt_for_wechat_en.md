## Potential for \$900+; Initiating at Buy Summary

SPCX is a highly vertically integrated disruptor leveraging unrivaled capabilities in launch to establish dominant positions in Global Connectivity and Artificial Intelligence. Because the company's ultimate advantage is rooted in its ability to deliver mass to orbit at greater scale and lower unit cost than any other entity on the planet, if SPCX succeeds in demonstrating key technological and operational milestones in the upcoming, catalyst-rich \~2-3 years without faltering, then we see a path to generating \~\$1 trillion in revenue and \~\$830B in Adj EBITDA in 2031. In that scenario, and if milestones have been hit smoothly, we can imagine the stock moving toward a long-term valuation level of over \$900/share, justified by SPCX's superior ability to scale and growth outlook even beyond 2031. More likely, as SPCX themselves has quipped, they are very good at delivering results – but not always on time. Accordingly, we initiate with a \$200 base-case price target implying 25% upside, which is a heavily discounted version of the \$900/share+ we believe can ultimately be achieved, and anticipate raising our price target at regular intervals as risks are retired, milestones are hit, and launch cadence grows.
