# CMS Company Ltd - H

2Q26 profits up 160-198% y/y, a beat; but does this matter?

2Q26 result a strong beat. After market close on 7 July, CMS announced its preliminary 1H26 results (link). 2Q26 net profits rose 160-198% y/y to Rmb6.7-7.7bn, a record high and likely ahead of market expectation. \- 2Q26 result a strong beat: After market close on 7 July, CMS issued an exchange announcement on its preliminary 1H26 results (link).
