# Costco Wholesale (COST): June Sales Update

Costco reported June same-store sales (excluding gas and foreign exchange) of +7.0%, compared to the consensus estimate of +7.3%, and moderating from +8.0% in May. US comparable sales (excluding gas) came in at +7.6%, versus the consensus of +7.8% and compared to +8.7% in May. The company noted that the impact of cannibalization was approximately 50 basis points in June.

In discussions with management, they indicated no significant changes in consumer trends or the competitive landscape during June. The company also discussed its first standalone gas station, which opened in Mission Viejo, California due to space limitations at the nearby warehouse. Going forward, standalone gas stations will be opened as needed, based on real estate considerations of existing locations, and are intended to serve current members.

## Traffic and Ticket Trends

Global traffic moderated to +3.2% in June (compared to +3.9% in May, +4.2% in April, and +1.5% in March), while global average ticket (excluding gas and foreign exchange) moderated to +3.7% (versus +4.0% in May, +3.4% in April, and +4.6% in March). In the US, traffic moderated sequentially to +3.2% (compared to +3.7% in May, +3.8% in April, and +0.7% in March), while ticket (excluding gas) moderated to +4.4% (versus +5.0% in May, +4.2% in April, and +5.5% in March).

## Category Performance

Fresh Foods showed mid-single-digit growth (compared to high-single-digit in May, high-single-digit in April, and mid-to-high-single-digit in March), with strength in bakery and meat. Food & Sundries grew low-to-mid-single-digit (versus mid-single-digit in May, mid-single-digit in April, and low-to-mid-single-digit in March), driven by food, candy, and frozen foods. Non-foods increased mid-to-high-single-digit (compared to high-single-digit in May, mid-to-high-single-digit in April, and mid-to-high-single-digit in March), with strength in jewelry (primarily gold), home furnishings, and major appliances. The ancillary business increased in the high twenties (versus high thirties in May, mid thirties in April, and mid twenties in March), with top performers being pharmacy, gas, and hearing aids. Management attributed pharmacy strength to GLP-1 usage, the expansion of Costco's specialty pharmacy business, and the addition of new payment options.
