# 1H26 Preview: Earnings Performance amid Margin Changes

Developers could report another set of results for 1H26, with a 40-50% y-y core profit decline on average amid reduced revenue booking and changes in DP margins. Outlooks for 2H pre-sales, margin, and recurring income will be key.

## Key Takeaways

We expect declines of $>20\%$ in revenue and $>40\%$ in core profit y-y for our coverage amid reduced sales and $\sim 2$ ppt GPM compression in DP business.

DP margin could be affected by continued home price changes and asset impairment, leading consensus for 2026-28 to adjust.

Slow recovery in POE balance sheets given property sales conditions, but SOEs could show lower leverage because of less landbanking in 1H.

## We see 1H26 earnings changes (Exhibit 1):

\- 0-10% declines: CR Land (large disposal gain offsets DP GPM compression) and C&D (higher GPM offsets revenue decline).

\- 10-25% declines: COLI (stable revenue but lower GPM), Jinmao (less disposal gain), and Seazen A/H (reduced DP booking).

\- 50-100% declines: Poly, Longfor, CMSK, Yuexiu, and Greentown (all owing to notably lower DP GPM)

\- Deep losses: Vanke, Gemdale (owing to large DP losses and asset impairment).

\- Profit warnings: Poly, Longfor, Greentown, and Yuexiu, on top of announced players (Vanke, CMSK, and Gemdale).

What to watch next: 1) Pre-sales outlook – we expect most players to adjust full-year sales growth targets despite low bases in 2H. 2) Margin pressure and asset impairment risk owing to continued home price changes. 3) Recurring income outlook – strong mall retail sales in 2H25 present a high base. 4) Refinancing/interest-saving plans of POEs/semi-SOEs.

Stay selective: Considering potentially lower property sales in 3Q and limited policy changes, we continue to suggest focusing on quality players with credible self-help alpha. We think CR Land (1109.HK), C&D (1908.HK), and Seazen (601155.SS/1030.HK) offer more attractive risk-reward at current valuations. We upgrade Jinmao (0817.HK) to OW for its improving pre-sales outlook (y-y may accelerate in 3Q); current valuation has largely priced in short-term earnings headwinds.
