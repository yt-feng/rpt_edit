## The Silicon Cycle

The equipment market stagnated after the dot-com bubble, but returned to growth following the late-2010s expansion in data-center investment.

Excluding the post-2000 stagnation, the market has continued to roughly double every six years on a cumulative basis.

Capital intensity (semiconductor capex / sales) has risen from around 20% historically to roughly 30% today.
