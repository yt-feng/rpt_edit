Within that region, South Korea grew 11.0 percent, a bright spot, while Japan declined 2.0 percent, a reminder that not all Asian markets are participating equally in the recovery.

The regional dispersion matters because it tells observers where the brand’s equity is strengthening and where it remains fragile. The Americas and Greater China are the two largest luxury markets globally, and Burberry is performing well in both. That is the foundation of a credible turnaround. The recovery is real, but it is not uniform.

## The Upgrade to Wholesale Guidance Signals That Partners Are Voting with Their Orders, a Leading Indicator of Brand Momentum

One of the most significant data points in the quarter was not a retail sales number but a guidance revision. Burberry upgraded its first-half fiscal 2027 wholesale revenue expectation from mid-single-digit growth to high-single-digit growth. This is a signal that should not be underestimated. Wholesale partners, particularly department stores and multi-brand retailers, place orders based on their assessment of consumer demand and brand heat. When they increase their commitments, they are effectively placing a bet that Burberry’s recovery has staying power.

The wholesale channel has been a source of pain for Burberry in recent years. During the brand’s prolonged struggles, wholesale partners reduced their exposure, wary of being left with inventory that would not sell at full price. The upgrade to guidance suggests that this dynamic is reversing. Partners are seeing stronger sell-through rates, better consumer response to new collections, and a brand that is once again generating interest. This is a self-reinforcing cycle: stronger wholesale orders lead to better product availability, which drives consumer awareness, which in turn drives further wholesale demand.

The wholesale upgrade also has direct financial implications. Wholesale carries lower margins than direct retail, but it also comes with lower fixed costs and less capital intensity. A high-single-digit growth rate in wholesale, combined with the retail momentum already demonstrated, gives Burberry a more diversified revenue base and reduces the pressure on any single channel to carry the entire recovery. The guidance revision is not just a positive data point; it is a structural improvement in the quality of the business.
