## China Gas Utilities

## 1H26 preview - results and consensus earnings adjustments are likely; turning point yet to come

We expect 1H26 results for China Gas utilities to show a moderate performance, with the majority of the companies reporting lower earnings year-on-year (Table 3) due to gas volumes, a decline in new connections and gas margins. We expect a round of earnings adjustments post results; our FY26-27E earnings forecasts are \~7% below consensus. Despite YTD price movement and current valuations (\~6% yield on average), we believe a turning point for the sector is yet to come as winter gas prices may climb year-on-year, bringing uncertainty to winter gas margins. We remain selective on stocks. Our top pick is ENN Energy, as the company offers >7% yield in FY26E with guaranteed DPS (HK\$3/share). We see room for a reduction in growth capex and improving FCF, and we think management may consider a buyback or increasing DPS amid the significant price movement YTD, which is not factored in to the price.

\- 1H results likely to show a moderate performance: We forecast most gas utilities to report 1H26 results below expectations (see Table 3) with \~4% decline in profits on average. HKCG is the only company for which we expect positive earnings growth on strong SAF profits amid elevated oil prices. We adjust our earnings for 26-27E by \~5-6% on average (Table 4) and our forecasts are \~7% below consensus. We expect consensus earnings adjustments post earnings.

\- Turning point yet to come: Despite a $>20\%$ share decline YTD (HSCEI $\sim 7\%$ ), we remain selective on stocks. This comes as the operating outlook remains challenging with moderate gas volume growth ( $<1\%$ in 26E for our covered names), elevated gas prices and a continued decline in new connections. These factors aren't likely to change much during 2H26. Also, we see uncertainties around winter gas margins on the back of continued uncertainty in global gas supply and LNG procurement costs. Yet, we see value for names with attractive yield with upstream resources (which could better weather gas price volatility). These include ENN Energy ( $>7\%$ yield in 26E with LNG trading profits in 2H with its overseas contracts) and ENN Natural Gas ( $>6\%$ yield in 27E with $>3$ bcm of overseas LNG sales on JPMe).

\- Winter gas margin remains uncertain: Our global energy analyst forecasts winter gas prices to almost double year-on-year in 4Q26 with tight global LNG supply-demand balances and low European storage levels. Note that NWE+UK storage facilities were $41\%$ full as of July 12 (vs $56\%$ at the same time last year and the 2022-25 average of $70\%$ ) (note). This poses downside risks to winter gas margins in our view, as residential heating volume was low last year due to the warm winter. Some gas utilities may need to procure spot LNG to satisfy winter heating demand. We believe the sector is unlikely to see a positive re-rating until investors have more clarity on the gas price outlook.
