NFLX reported its 2Q26 results after the market close.

\- Revenues grew by +13% Y/Y (on reported and +12% on FxN basis) to \$12.6B in 2Q26, slightly below the company's previous guidance and consensus estimates.

\- APAC led revenue growth on a FX Neutral basis and was up by 18% YoY (16% w/ FX). It was followed by LATAM with 16% growth YoY on a FX Neutral basis (21% w/ FX). EMEA grew by 11% YoY (14% w/ FX) and UCAN grew 10% YoY on a FX Neutral basis.

\- The company guided to \$12.86B of revenue for 3Q26, which is equivalent to +11.7% YoY and slightly lower than consensus.

\- Netflix' management mentioned that they have three areas of focus to achieve their goals: 1) delivering more entertainment value to members, 2) leveraging technology to improve service, and 3) improving monetization.

\- Netflix shared that they will reduce the frequency of their “What We Watched” report, which discloses the quantity of view hours watched on the platform, from a semi-annual to an annual publication.

\- Management explained that there isn't a linear relationship between view hours and revenue, and that they measure engagement by more than just quantity of view hours but also the quality and variety of their offering. For example, live events and animation both represent about $5\%$ of the content budget. However, they represent $1\%$ and $8\%$ of view hours respectively. The company estimates that both contribute similar value to the business despite one producing 8x the viewing than the other because 6 out of the top 10 new member sign-up days over the past 5 years have come from live events.

\- Netflix shared that they are encouraged with early video podcast performance. Approximately half of Netflix' viewing occurs in the evening, but the recently launched video podcasts over-index on viewing during the day and on mobile devices, which is an indicator of engagement being incremental.

\- Netflix shared that its recent price changes have gone well. Additionally, they mentioned that they are always testing the best ways to bring new members to the platform, and that includes testing free trials for non-rejoining new members in a number of countries, and potentially even having a free AVOD tier in some cases down the line if advertising monetization improves and there is no cannibalization of paid tiers.

\- Margins beat expectations at 33.4%. However, 3Q26 operating margin guidance was only 33.2% vs. consensus estimates of 33.5%. Management reiterated its amortization YoY growth guidance of 10% for 2026 (higher than the 8% averaged over the last five years). Amortization growth was 12.5% in 2Q26, but management still expects content amortization to grow slower in the second half of the year.

\- FCF reached \$1.5B for 2Q26 (vs. \$2.2B in 2Q25). The decrease was driven in part by the higher cash tax payments related to the WBD termination fee. The company reiterated 2026 full year FCF guidance of \$12.5B (which had been increased from \$11B in the previous quarter due to the WBD termination fee), and continues to assume that the ratio of cash content spend to content amortization remains at \~1.1x.

\- Share repurchases during 2Q26 totaled \$4.7B, the largest quarter of share buybacks. The company now has \$27.1B left of buybacks authorizations since the boar approved an additional \$25B in April.
