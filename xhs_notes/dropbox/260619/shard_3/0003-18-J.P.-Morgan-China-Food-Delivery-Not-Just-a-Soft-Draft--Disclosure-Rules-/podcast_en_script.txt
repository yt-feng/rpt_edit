EN_A: So there is this new draft regulation from China's market regulator on food delivery subsidies. How big of a deal is it really?

EN_B: It works through disclosure. Seven-day pre-notification, funding source reporting, and a competitive impact self-assessment. EN_A: So for Meituan, this is a positive catalyst?
EN_B: It reduces the downside tail. If the rules stick, competition shifts away from capital-funded cash burn toward operational execution. That plays right into Meituan's strengths: order density, routing efficiency, merchant coverage, rider management, and consumer habits.

EN_B: Exactly. The biggest unknown is how they interpret "own operating profit." If it means segment-level profit, challengers cannot easily fund delivery losses with group capital. If it allows group-level profit, then the asymmetry weakens significantly. That is the key clause to watch.
