## Investment Thesis - COSCO Shipping Energy

COSCO Shipping Energy is a Chinese energy materials (e.g., oil, natural gas) shipping company with the largest oil tanker fleet (among listed companies) in the world. COSCO Energy has 62 crude tankers (incl. 45 VLCCs) and 31 product tankers in its owned international trade fleet, and another 54 tankers in its domestic trade fleet (as of end-24). COSCO Energy's crude oil has usually accounted for over $50\%$ of its profit historically. The company has a $5\%$ share of the global seaborne crude oil tanker market (in terms of turnover) and a $1\%$ share of the product oil tanker market (as of Aug-23). We are positive on COSCO Energy given 1) Supply: Limited capacity additions due to still low orderbook, ageing fleet and slower vessel speed required to be phased out by tightened environmental protection measures; 2) Demand: Structurally lifted demand given longer shipping distance, due to rerouting post sanctions on Russian crude/product oil, global refinery dislocation, and wider Dubai/WTI spread could also stimulate Asian imports from the US gulf; and 3) COSCO Energy's exposure: Largest listed oil tanker fleet, with diversified exposure to crude and product oil shipping, and more exposed to VLCCs, which carry long-haul crude oil. We believe the upcycle could continue for longer with potential ROE upside, warranting a higher valuation. We are Buy rated on COSCO Shipping Energy (A/H).
