【金字塔原理写作原则】
1. 结论先行：文章开头先回答“这份报告最值得看的判断是什么”，而不是介绍背景。
2. 统领思想：全文只能服务一个主判断，避免变成摘要合集。
3. 纵向回答：每一层都要回答上一层提出的“为什么”或“所以呢”。
4. 横向 MECE：每个一级小节必须彼此独立、共同支撑主判断，避免重叠。
5. Synthesis over summary：不要复述报告段落，要提炼“这些事实合在一起意味着什么”。
6. So what：每个小节末尾必须落到对行业、公司、竞争格局、市场定价或读者观察框架的含义。
7. Action title：所有 `##` 小标题必须是“直接讲述洞察的完整句子”，不能是目录标签。

## Satisfactory Business Performance

We hosted an investor call with Dongfang Electric ('DF') on 9 Jul. The company has had steady operational performance year to date. Its new orders were up 10% yoy in 5M26, in line with its full-year target to rise the same extent. Its new orders were Rmb117.2bn in 2025, including Rmb5.4bn or 4.6% from overseas. Order backlog was +7% to Rmb150bn by end 5M26, from Rmb140bn by end 2025. Revenue and profit growth were on-track year to date.

## More Gas Turbine Sales to Overseas Data Centers

DF has gas turbine production capacity of 50MW each of 10-12 units pa. The production capacity would rise to 30 units by end 2027E after Ph.1 expansion at capex of Rmb760m. Ph.2 expansion is in planning. The company has orders on hand of 20 sets for gas turbine generators of 50MW each so that its gas turbine production capacity will be running at full utilization in 2025-26E with 12-month lead time.

New orders were signed in 1H26 for sales of 10 units at Rmb150-200m each making GP margin >30% to data centers in Canada placed by their representatives in mainland China; orders for another 10 units could be signed from the same customer in 2H26E. Inquiries from the US data centers could render more new orders ahead, pending UL certification from the US (while Canadian certification has already been secured). DF also provides after-sale services as well as repairs & maintenance for these exports. It sold its 50MW gas turbine to Kazakhstan previously.

The energy conversion efficiency of DF's 50MW gas turbine is $64\%$ , 2ppts below those made by Siemens. DF views its gas turbine sales as competitive in terms of pricing and delivery time. $80 - 90\%$ of gas turbine sales proceeds are received on product delivery.

DF's capacity expansion to make 200MW gas turbines under cooperation with Mitsubishi Heavy (7011 JT, Buy) is in planning. Such potential product probably could be made in 2028E and used in the domestic market.

## Revenue Rise of Hydropower Equipment from Pump Storage

DF forecasts its revenue from hydropower equipment sales to rise to Rmb10bn in 2029/30E (comprising 80% from pump storage facilities), from Rmb3.9bn in 2025. Its orders for hydropower equipment kept rising mainly driven by more demand for pumped-storage facilities. To recap, PRC government targets to add 100GW newly commissioned pumped storage capacity in the 15 $^{th}$ five-year period (2026-30E) so that PRC pumped storage facilities would rise to around 160GW by end 2030E, which would be 2.6x of 62GW by end 2025. This massive buildout is designed to coordinate with the 300GW ESS so that China would have total 460GW energy storage resources to stabilize power grids and facilitate the increase of PRC non-fossil power generation mix to 50% by 2030E.

DF guides the GP margin of its hydropower equipment sales could rise to 15-18%, from 12% in 2025, boosted by more pump storage equipment sales. New orders from the world's largest hydropower project to be built on the lower reaches of Yanlung Zangbo River in Tibet would start to receive from 2028E.

## Other Business Updates

1. Less market tendering of coal-fired power equipment in China – PRC market bidding of coal-fired power equipment demand could drop from 80GW pa in previous years to 50GW pa in 2026-30E estimated by DF, though the company's revenue from coal-fired power equipment sales would rise yoy in 2026E based on orders on hand. The sales prices of 2x1,000MW thermal power equipment is Rmb1.1bn; and DF assumes GP margin of this business to be steady around 20-22% (vs. 20.7% in 2025).

2. Revenue rise from nuclear equipment sales – DF guides PRC newly approved nuclear construction to remain at 10 units pa in the 15 $^{th}$ FYP. Its revenue from nuclear equipment sales could rise from Rmb5.7bn in 2025 to Rmb8bn pa in coming years depending on delivery schedule.

3. Low margin of wind turbine sales - DF estimates its revenue from wind equipment sales to rise 10% yoy to Rmb20bn in 2026E; GP margin of this business (3% in 2025) would remain low.
