SpaceX is a vertically integrated technology leader building the foundational hardware and software infrastructure for the future of space, global connectivity, and artificial intelligence. We rate shares of SPCX a Buy due to the following points: 1) Unrivaled launch capabilities right now and for the foreseeable future; 2) Unique capability of scaling space infrastructure and unlocking trillion-dollar market opportunities (Orbital AI and Starlink); 3) Extreme vertical integration, which reinforces the company's ability to drive costs down, throughput up, and deliver at a scale at which no competitor can replicate; and 4) Differentiated margin and growth profiles.
