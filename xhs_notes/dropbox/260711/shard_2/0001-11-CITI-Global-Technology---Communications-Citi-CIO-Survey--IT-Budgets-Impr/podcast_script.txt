That number has jumped significantly from 30% in December to almost 50% now. So it’s not a rising tide that lifts all boats. It’s a fierce competition for a finite pool of dollars.

EN_A: Cannibalization. So which areas are getting squeezed?

EN_B: Cybersecurity is the most visible example. It’s still the second-highest priority, but its budget growth has decelerated by about one percentage point. Digital transformation and back-office software are also losing some ground. Meanwhile, customer-facing applications jumped from sixth to fourth place. So companies are shifting money from defensive or support IT projects toward AI applications that can directly impact revenue or customer experience.

EN_A: So for investors, who are the clear winners and who should be worried?

EN_B: The winners are pretty clear. Microsoft remains the top vendor CIOs want to spend more on, followed by Amazon and Google. Companies like Cisco, Dell, IBM, and ServiceNow were named as vendors CIOs would cut spending on to fund AI.
