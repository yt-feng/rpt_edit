## Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation

## Investment Thesis

We believe NFLX is a key beneficiary and driver of the ongoing disruption of linear TV, with Netflix's content performing well globally and driving a virtuous circle of strong subscriber growth, more revenue, and growing profit. With 325M+ global subs, Netflix has a strong leadership position in a rationalizing streaming industry, & we expect NFLX to benefit from the global proliferation of Internet-connected devices and increasing consumer preference for on-demand video consumption over the Internet. NFLX's ad-supported tier should further expand NFLX's subscriber base while driving high-margin incremental revenue. We're projecting 2026-2028 CAGRs of +12% for FXN Revenue, +21% for Operating Income, +24% for GAAP EPS, & +23% for FCF, all of which support NFLX's premium valuation & our Overweight rating.

## Valuation

Our December 2026 PT of \$118 is based on \~30x 2027E GAAP EPS of \$3.92. This is a premium to mega-cap tech peers trading at \~22x 2027E GAAP EPS on average, which we think is justified by NFLX's similar top-line growth & faster bottom-line growth.
