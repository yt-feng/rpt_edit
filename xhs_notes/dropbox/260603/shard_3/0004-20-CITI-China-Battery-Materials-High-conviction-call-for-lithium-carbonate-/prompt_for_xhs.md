# On-and-off supply disruption on the course of the year

Even though demand has been gaining conviction YTD, lithium becomes more volatile in trading as there are on and off supply disruptions during the course of the year, putting upside/downside risks on the dynamics. Here we list out the topical supply disruption factors and our read on the latest update.

- JXW (-ve to lithium price) - JXW has been suspended since Aug-25 due to the mining permit expiration, the inappropriate mining license as ceramic clay and environmental issues (i.e., river encroachment, tailings treatment, etc.). According to our channel checks with industry contacts & public government announcement, CATL has obtained proper mining permit in early-26 and is still pending approval for environmental license. It's difficult to arrive at a market consensus view on the clear resumption timeline as it depends on the government process instead of the corporate initiatives. Overall, it's a hovering negative alert to the lithium price before the finalized approval.   
Lepidolite mines update (+ve to lithium price) – 4 of the remaining lepidolite mine might need to go through the same procedure (i.e., mining permit, environmental license, etc.) sooner or later during the year, indicating further downside risk to the lithium supply. According to our channel check with industry contacts, companies are expediting the lepidolite production YTD to pile up the lepidolite inventory for conversion later. That said, if the remaining lepidolite mines do need to suspend for a license update, the actual impact this year might be less than the on-paper figures (-8k tons LCE per month, taking up 7-8% lithium supply in China), but the impact in FY27 or years after depends on the approval timeline.

Australian mines update (-ve to lithium price) – Australian high-cost spodumene mines are gradually incentivized to restart and/or expand since 2H26 due to the attractive spodumene prices YTD. Below please find the summary on incremental spodumene incentivized from high spodumene price.
