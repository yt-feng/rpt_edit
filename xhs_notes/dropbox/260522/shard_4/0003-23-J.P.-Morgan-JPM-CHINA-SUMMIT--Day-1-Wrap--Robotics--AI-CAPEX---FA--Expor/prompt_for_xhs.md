# Industrial Innovation - Robotics

Our expert session reaffirms that humanoid and embodied AI robots are firmly established as a long-term structural growth theme, though the industry remains in an early, validation-heavy phase. Near-term progress will be driven less by headline model breakthroughs and more by incremental advances in hardware reliability, system architecture (VLA + world models), and high-quality data generation. Commercial value is expected to first emerge in structured industrial settings—such as logistics, manufacturing, and pharmacies—while broader generalization and household adoption are likely to remain longer dated. Over the next 1–3 years, China is positioned to lead real-world deployment as pilots scale and ROI becomes demonstrable, laying the foundation for more meaningful volume growth in the longer term.

Investor questions focused on understanding key leadership aspects of humanoid robots, including brain (algorithm), hardware and cost leadership, as well as bottlenecks for accelerating adoption. The session with Harmonious Drive Systems (688017 CH) attracted high investor interest, with the company maintaining stable product quality and only a small gap versus Japanese peers, while offering substantial price advantages ( $\sim 50\%$ ) to capture meaningful volumes at top global robotic players such as KUKA, ABB, and Yaskawa.
