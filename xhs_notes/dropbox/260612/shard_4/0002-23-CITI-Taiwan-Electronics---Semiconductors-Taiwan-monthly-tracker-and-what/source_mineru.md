# Taiwan Electronics & Semiconductors

Taiwan monthly tracker and what's new in AI — Supply chain tightness continues

## CITI'S TAKE

Despite recent market chatter about slower new technology adoption such as 800V HDVC, CPO and lower memory content adoption by VR NVL72, we see Taiwanese tech supply chain's near-term revenue trend on track. Like we highlighted in our previous reports on Delta and Nvidia result implications - "Blackwell expanding strongly, entering the Rubin Cycle in Feb, we highlighted that 800V HDVC adoption will be gradual and CPO will start from scale-out first in 2H26 and 2027. This is also confirmed by our recent Computex tour and Taiwan tech conference. Among Taiwan tech supply chain, we see tight supply across advanced node foundry, packaging, testing interface and substrate/CCL. There is also potential further price hikes into 2H26 reflecting rising cost across the supply chain.

Semiconductor foundry and OSAT: TSMC and UMC on track, potentially more upside into 2H; ASEH and KYEC inline – Thanks to solid AI demand and high UTR, we see leading foundry and OSAT players' May sales on track. TSMC's May sales reached NT\$417bn (up 2% MoM and 30% YoY), TSMC's YTD revenue up 30% YoY. With more N3 AI chip and N2 smartphone SoC shipment in 2H26, we think there is a great chance of TSMC further lifting its full year revenue growth target, in particular that the CPU for agentic AI would provide incremental upside into next year. For UMC and VIS, we are looking for price upward trend into 2H26. We also see better order flow for both ASEH and KYEC in 2H26 on the back of its rising LEAP and new AI chip testing business.

Testing interface and equipment – MPI, Hon Precision and Chroma beat outstanding - In testing interface, we see great chance of MPI, Hon Precision and Chroma beating market expectation on their 2Q26 revenue growth thanks to solid order flow from GPU/AI ASIC clients and specs upgrade. CPO testing equipment are also ready for certification. CHPT delivered another record-high monthly sales in May-26 benefiting from strong demand for AI/HPC testing. Backed by its scaled capacity, mgmt. remains confident of monthly revenues to see MoM growth throughout the year. WinWay's monthly results were capped by capacity, but Renwu leased plant capacity is expected to start contributing in Jun. We still expect both companies to see solid revenue momentum by 2Q26E thanks to the further ramp-up of next-gen AI GPU mass production.

Fabless - Aspeed continues outperforming, GUC better; Mediatek, Realtek and Novatek better than feared on non-AI market; Alchip remains slow - AI related names including Aspeed and GUC are better than expected; we believe the capacity constraint would be the near-term risk, yet the constructive growth trend remains intact. While consumer demand continues to be muted, we see Mediatek, Realtek and Novatek holding well thanks to better mix. We also expect potential price hike for Mediatek into 2H26 reflecting rising cost. For Alchip, the Trn3 ramping up would be more in 2H26, and we expect an uptick June sales.

Substrate, PCB and CCL - continued CCL price hike — Though the overall substrate sector posted steady MoM growth, we think sales momentum came lower than the bulls' expectation, as price hike magnitude seemingly benefited the sector slower than expected. For CCL sector, sales posted stronger than expected, driven by continuously effective price hikes customer by customer, resulting in strong uptick in both April and May sales. PCB industry saw raw material price hike passed through to the downstream, leading to higher-thanx-expected sales growth as well.

ODMs and PC Supply chain - Hon Hai tracking ahead, Quanta and Wistron slightly better, Wiwynn below — Overall, May sales of ODM/OEM sector posted slight MoM growth, backed by gradual ramp in AI server but likely partially offset by some early pull-in impact in April. Hon Hai's May sales of NT\$859.4bn (up 3% MoM and 40% YoY), ahead of our 15% QoQ revenue estimate for 2Q26. For AI ASIC camp, Wiwynn's muted May sales likely suggest slower ramp in AWS AI ASIC server, which we think supposedly should kick off in June at the latest. We see B300 and GB300 demand remains strong and VR NVL72 will start ramp-up gradually in 3Q26. We continue to project NVL72 shipment in 2Q at 13-15k.

Overall NB shipments in May grew 2% MoM as NB customer continues to pull-in order in fear of continued rising components cost, including memory, chips, substrate, etc. Looking ahead, we estimate 2Q26E NB shipments at +2% QoQ/-10% YoY given ongoing pull-in momentum. While there is increasing expectation of accelerating AI PC penetration rate given the emerging of agentive AI demand, we believe it still will take time for the whole ecosystem to get ready, and overall NB order visibility into 2H26 remains limited due to the dampening consumer purchasing power on rising retails prices.

Power and thermal component – slightly below – Delta’s May sales of NT\$59bn (flat MoM and up 44% YoY), tracking slightly below market expectation of 15-20% QoQ revenue growth. We believe it is mainly due to some component tight supply and data center deployment schedule. Yet we see its HDVC power solution ramp up on track. For thermal components, May-26 sales as reported by AVC & Auras were slightly up from Apr at +3% MoM/+65% YoY. AVC continues to see moderate demand due to the GPU platform transition, while Auras has recovered from previous month due to fewer working days at its Thailand plant. We expect both companies to deliver further improved monthly results thanks to the ramp-up of TRN3 platform with sequential sales growth in 2Q26E even during the transition period.

Passive components-Yageo reported record-high monthly sales in May-26, implying potential upside to current quarter estimates. This was driven by demand for AI-related applications remaining strong, and both standard and specialty products continuing to show steady growth.

## Guide to Citi Fundamental Research Investment Ratings:

Citi stock recommendations include an investment rating and an optional risk rating to highlight high risk stocks.

Risk rating takes into account both price volatility and fundamental criteria. Stocks will either have no risk rating or a High risk rating assigned.

Investment Ratings: Citi investment ratings are Buy, Neutral and Sell. Our ratings are a function of analyst expectations of expected total return ("ETR") and risk. ETR is the sum of the forecast price appreciation (or depreciation) plus the dividend yield for a stock within the next 12 months. The target price is based on a 12 month time horizon. The Investment rating definitions are: Buy (1) ETR of 15% or more or 25% or more for High risk stocks; and Sell (3) for negative ETR. Any covered stock not assigned a Buy or a Sell is a Neutral (2). For stocks rated Neutral (2), if an analyst believes that there are insufficient valuation drivers and/or investment catalysts to derive a positive or negative investment view, they may elect with the approval of Citi management not to assign a target price and, thus, not derive an ETR. Citi may suspend its rating and target price and assign "Rating Suspended" status for regulatory and/or internal policy reasons. Citi may also suspend its rating and target price and assign "Under Review" status for other exceptional circumstances (e.g. lack of information critical to the analyst's thesis, trading suspension) affecting the company and/or trading in the company's securities. In both such situations, the rating and target price will show as “—” and “-” respectively in the rating history price chart. Prior to 11 April 2022 Citi assigned "Under Review" status to both situations and prior to 11 Nov 2020 only in exceptional circumstances. As soon as practically possible, the analyst will publish a note re-establishing a rating and investment thesis. Investment ratings are determined by the ranges described above at the time of initiation of coverage, a change in investment and/or risk rating, or a change in target price (subject to limited management discretion). At times, the expected total returns may fall outside of these ranges because of market price movements and/or other short-term volatility or trading patterns. Such interim deviations will be permitted but will become subject to review by Research Management. Your decision to buy or sell a security should be based upon your personal investment objectives and should be made only after evaluating the stock's expected performance and risk.

## Catalyst Watch/Short Term Views ("STV") Ratings Disclosure:

Catalyst Watch and STV Upside/Downside calls: Citi may also include a Catalyst Watch or STV Upside or Downside call to indicate the analyst expects the share price to rise (fall) in absolute terms over a specified period of 30 or 90 days in reaction to one or more specific near-term catalysts or events impacting the company or the market. A Catalyst Watch or STV Upside/Downside call will automatically expire at the end of the specified 30/90 day period.
