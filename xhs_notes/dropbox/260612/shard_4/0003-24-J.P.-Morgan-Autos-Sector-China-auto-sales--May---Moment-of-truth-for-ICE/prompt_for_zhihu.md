China auto sales (May): Moment of truth for ICE autos this summer; NEV shift continues, despite reversal of price gap with ICE

China's passenger vehicle retail sales continued to fall in May, but exports hit a new record high for the third straight month, breaking above 800k units for the month. The shift to new energy vehicles (NEV) was evident in domestic demand, with the NEV penetration rate rising 160bp MoM to $63\%$ . In response to soaring export demand, major Chinese manufacturers are extending delivery dates and raising prices on some NEV models. On the other hand, popular internal combustion engine (ICE) models dropped out of the top sales rankings, despite deeper discounts. We assume that ICE auto sales will fall this summer because of seasonal factors, but with the number of used cars (mainly ICE autos) with zero recorded mileage rising again, declines in new vehicle prices and sales volumes could become more of a concern.

- Domestic NEV penetration accelerates, price gap with ICE autos reverses: China's May retail sales of passenger vehicles amounted to 1,528k units (–22% YoY), down YoY for the eighth consecutive month. Major Chinese OEMs have hiked NEV prices, mainly reflecting intelligent vehicle systems, whereas ICE automakers have lowered prices substantially.
