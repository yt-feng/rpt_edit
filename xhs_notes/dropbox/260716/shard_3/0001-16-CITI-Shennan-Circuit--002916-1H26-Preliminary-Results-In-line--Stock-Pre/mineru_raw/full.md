# Shennan Circuit (002916.SZ)

1H26 Preliminary Results In-line; Industry Observation on Material Cost Trends

## RESEARCH HIGHLIGHTS

Shennan guided 1H26 net profit of Rmb2.10-2.30bn, up 54-69% yoy. This growth is attributed to AI-driven storage and computing-power demand associated with the Guangzhou plant on ABF substrate ramp releasing capacity. Shennan delivered 2Q26 net profit growth of 55% along with quarterly net profit run rate to rise 59% qoq in 2Q to Rmb1.35bn. However, this appears as a different performance pattern compared to upstream AI-CCL player Shengyi with mid-pt guiding net profit increase of 135% yoy and quarterly net profit run rate of 76% qoq to Rmb2.04bn. On the other hand, vertical integrator KB Laminate shows a distinct profile this year. Estimates suggest KB Laminate 1H26 net profit would increase by 3.3X and quarterly net profit run rate would rise by 2X yoy in 2Q. The observation thus points to upstream over downstream on AI-CCL supply chain as KB Laminate > Shengyi Tech > Shennan.

Moderating 2Q26 net profit growth on material cost trends and base effects – Shennan net of 1Q26 NP of Rmb850m (+73% yoy), the guide implies 2Q26 NP of Rmb1.25-1.45bn, up 44-67% yoy (mid +55%) and +59% qoq. The yoy moderation from 1Q26 partly reflects material cost changes and a firmer 2Q25 base (+42.9% vs 1Q25's +29.5%). Among China AI-PCB players, Shennan maintains a distinct business model with BT substrate (via Wuxi plant) for smartphone and ABF substrate (via Guangzhou plant mainly for memory and margin recovery since 3Q25).

Read-across to KBL and Shengyi Tech - Both Shennan and Shengyi benefit from mix upgrade into AI-PCB and AI-CCL respectively. However, Shengyi's implied 2Q at ~+135% at mid-point vs Shennan's ~+55%. Shengyi delivered a higher quarterly profit run rate of 76% qoq in 2Q vs Shennan's 59% in 2Q. More notably, projections for KBL 1H26 net profit indicate a 3.3X increase to HK$4.0bn with quarterly net profit run rate rising by 2X from ~HK$1bn in 1Q to HK$3bn in 2Q. This illustrates the rationale for observing upstream over downstream i.e., fiberglass fabric & copper foil > CCL > PCB on AI-CCL supply chain as KB Laminate > Shengyi Tech > Shennan Circuit.

## Shennan Circuit

## Valuation Framework

The target price for Shennan shares of RMB360 is based on forward 2027E PE target of 38x at +2sd over mean as the framework rolls over from prior 43x PE to 38x PE (at +2SD) following the release of 1Q26 results. The +2SD over mean approach is applied for Shennan's earnings cycle under AI-PCB demand. This is supported by 3-yr EPS CAGR of 32% through 2028E. The company's position is one of the key proxies on AI server and BT substrate – both contributed ~75% of total sales. The PE target reflects the sustained PCB industry cycle with datacenter server, AI server, EV, etc. during 2026-28E, though the 5G base station installations maintain modest growth in China this year. The start-up loss for new plant for Guangzhou FC-BGA Ph1 took place in 2025 but this would start operate breakeven from 2026 which is about 1yr earlier than prior guidance of 2027. The target PE of 38x forward equates to a ~20-30% premium to the sector average, which appears warranted given: 1) Shennan's leading position in the communication PCB sector, enhanced by its background; and 2) the company's technology leadership position on AI-server, communication, datacenters, autos (on ADAS) among peers with the potential to deliver a higher margin on a more advanced technology curve.

## Risk Considerations

Factors that could affect the shares relative to the target price include: 1) Foreign AI server developments and other leading players progressing at a different pace than expected; 2) demand on auto and ADAS system differing from projections; 3) profit of BT substrate new plant in Wuxi varying from estimates; and 4) laminate cost changes differing from expectations.

# Kingboard Laminates Holdings

(1888.HK; HK$58.3; 14 Jul 26; 16:10)

## Valuation Framework

The target price for KBL shares of HK$130 is based on a ~28-29x P/E for 2027E, at 3SD over mean, which is above its historical cycle peak between +1SD and +2SD, to reflect the growing share of its AI-fabric new business from 2H26E which could trigger re-rating along with further earnings revision possibility on e-glass fabric given the run rate of price changes in June. The +3SD approach reflects recent kickoff of three kilns on AI-fabric from 2Q26. This premium is considered justified given expectations for a continued GM expansion cycle during 2026E-28E along with potential earnings revisions as KBL would expand its customer base for AI-fabric under the NVDA and ASIC ecosystem this year. A PE methodology appropriately captures the company's medium-term operating performance. The target price translates to 0.3x PEG (regional comp around 0.6x) supported by strong three-year earnings CAGR of 99% (was 92%) through 2028E. This appears reasonable compared with regional CCL peers.

## Risk Considerations

Factors that could cause the shares to differ from the target price include: 1) demand on upstream materials such as glass fabric and copper foil on AI-related products developing at a different pace than expected; 2) China macro growth performing differently than projected; 3) policy developments in China varying from expectations; or 4) changes in end-demand of electronic goods.

## Shengyi Technology

(600183.SS; Rmb147.9; 14 Jul 26; 15:00)

## Valuation Framework

The target price for SYTECH of Rmb195 is based on a 56x 2027E PE, +4SD. The PE target multiple at +4SD reflects Shengyi demonstrating persistent increase of wallet share at NVDA from GB200 to GB300 and further in Vera Rubin. Continued earnings revision opportunity for 2026E-28E is supported by rising revenue share from AI-CCL in coming years. The premium PE target over mean is considered justified based on a 3-year EPS CAGR of 45% over 2025-28E, following a ~24% change in 2023. The target PE implies 1.2x PEG which appears comparable with major regional peers. The high PE target over mean is considered appropriate given: 1) SYTECH's track record of market share growth in the global CCL market (from 8% in 2007 to 13% in 2024); 2) SYTECH's technology position as an early entrant into Nvidia AI supply chain among China CCL players; and 3) increasing premium for domestic component sourcing, underpinned by policies affecting sourcing from US companies.

## Risk Considerations

Factors that could affect achieving the target price include: 1) demand for AI-CCL orders differing from expectations; 2) changes in China consumption patterns; and 3) capex on 5G/IoT products varying from projections. Factors that could positively affect achieving the target price include: 1) securing ASIC-based customers for CCL orders; 2) macro-economic conditions in China developing favorably; 3) operating margin improving on higher revenue growth due to operating leverage; and 4) AI demand and consumption trends developing strongly.
