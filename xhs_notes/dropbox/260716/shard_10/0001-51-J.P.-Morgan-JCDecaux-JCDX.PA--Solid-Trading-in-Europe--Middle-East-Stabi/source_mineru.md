## JCDecaux

Solid Trading in Europe, Middle East Stabilization, and Incremental World Cup Revenues - A Setup for Further Upgrades; Placing on Positive Catalyst Watch into Q2 results.

We are bullish into Q2 results for JCDecaux given solid trading in Europe, better than feared trends in the Middle East, incremental World Cup revenues and ongoing margin improvement that confirms the turn-around story. As we outlined in our March 2026 deep-dive on the European Outdoor advertising market, with a corresponding upgrade of the subsector (“It’s about time”, here), we believe the sector is at an inflection point, with multiple structural tailwinds continuing to build (see below) as Outdoor takes share within the advertising mix, supported by the ongoing digitisation of billboards. While the shares have outperformed YTD (+38% versus CAC40 +3%), investor sentiment and near-term advertising momentum have softened somewhat in recent months, largely reflecting geopolitical developments that weighed on passenger volumes, in particular in the Middle East (5% of revenues for JCD). However, the key advertising data points appear to be stabilizing and trending incrementally better, suggesting the demand backdrop is improving. We preview our JCDecaux estimates ahead of Q226 results (due July 30 $^{th}$ ) and overall, we anticipate a solid performance in the quarter with the company likely to over-achieve guidance for c.3% organic growth (JPMe +3.4%, VA Cons +3.2%), driven in particular by Street Furniture and Transport helped by incremental World Cup revenues (spread across Q2 & Q3). Our H1 operating margin estimate of €329m stands about 3% above consensus estimates, likely triggering further upgrades and as such we place the name on Positive Catalyst Watch. Next to better than expected Q2 results, we expect continued trading momentum also into Q3, and anticipate Q3 guidance on the day for well above 5% organic growth in Q3 (JPMe 5.7% vs VA Cons 4.9%). We continue to see the shares as undervalued with Decaux likely to approach €400m in FCF in the next few years versus a current market cap of €4.5bn and reiterate our OW rating on the name.

\- Q226 Expectations - we are 3% ahead on operating margin; placing the name on Positive Catalyst Watch. We expect a strong performance across most geographies with less negative impact from the Middle East than previously expected, though we expect China to be slightly negative – after returning to growth in Q1 – on weak consumption levels. We expect group organic revenue growth of +3.4% (vs VA Consensus +3.2%) – slightly ahead of company guidance for organic growth of c.3% – with strong performance across Street Furniture (+5.4%) and Transport (+2.0%) also driven by incremental World Cup revenues. We expect better than expected trading in the Middle East (which represents c.5% of revenues) as well as DDG growth in the US and UK which should both drive positive sentiment, though this will be slightly offset by China. We also anticipate strong execution on costs with an H126 operating profit margin of €329m (17.0% margin), 3% above VA consensus estimates of €320m (16.6% margin).

## Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation

## Investment Thesis

After 4 years of being Neutral/Underweight on JCDecaux, we recently left behind (here) our cautious view as we see a clear inflection: 1) Digital now reached close to 50% of revenues in 2025, becoming a key driver behind improving margins, turning for the first time in years; 2) FCF dynamics are improving quickly as management finally executes well on working capital and capex (for screens) now beyond peak; 3) the advertising backdrop is improving across most regions, particularly in China; 4) recent contract wins in Stockholm, Denver and Barcelona as well as the retail contract with Carrefour/Carmila in France add revenue visibility for 2026 and 5) JCDecaux's programmatic platform continues to scale.

## Valuation

We set a DCF-based Dec-27 PT of €30 (previously €29). We assume a WACC of 9.5% and Terminal Growth rate of 2.0%.

## Company Description

JCDecaux is the number one Outdoor advertising company worldwide, with a more than 1.1m advertising panels in over 79 countries and overall revenues of €3,967m in 2025. The company is able to replicate its business model globally and holds a diversified geographic portfolio with Europe accounting for about 58% of revenues and Emerging Markets contributing about 35%, while North America takes a relatively low share of 8%. Revenues are driven by both organic growth and ongoing bolt-on acquisitions. We see a strong growth opportunity arising from digital billboards, particularly in the company's Street Furniture and Transport divisions.

Types of revenues: JCDecaux operates in three different business segments: 1) Street Furniture (Street Furniture and self-service bicycles); 2) Transport (advertising in airports, train stations, etc.); and 3) Billboard (print and digital billboard advertising). Other outdoor advertising activities, such as advertising on shopping trolleys or in petrol stations, are relatively small in the group context.

A strong market leader and key consolidator: JCDecaux is globally the largest outdoor advertiser and a strong market leader in most geographies with the exception of the US market.

## JCDecaux - momentum is turning; we see several positives at (still) highly attractive valuation

## 1) Digital share is improving rapidly in the portfolio and margins are moving back above 2019 pre-Covid levels as a result

The mix in JCDecaux is quickly improving, with digital revenues approaching the 50% threshold (Q425 was 44.8%, though Q126 was 41.7%). This rising digital share supports materially stronger yield (often 2–4x analog on our estimates), while tight cost discipline drives incremental margin expansion. In 2025, digital grew 10.0% versus group growth of 1.8%, underscoring that the company's growth engine is increasingly digital and, importantly, programmatic. Management reiterated that digital has compounded organically at 16% historically and continues to expand at a double-digit pace, while programmatic is scaling at roughly 2x digital growth and now represents a little over 10% of digital sales. The acceleration of programmatic broadens demand and enhances monetization across the digital estate, adding a new growth cylinder that structurally improves pricing power, mix, and profitability.

## 3) Advertising momentum remains solid with China turning back to growth after years of declines and limited exposure to the Middle East

JCDecaux's country mix is widely split, de-risking the portfolio when it comes to downturns. We note that the business sees several tail-winds from new contract wins while ad trends remain still pretty solid.

\- Importantly, China, JCDecaux's problem market in the past few years, has been turning back to growth. JCDecaux attributes China's Q1 2026 return to growth to three factors. First, the ongoing digitization across Chinese airports and metro networks is lifting the revenue profile by expanding higher-yield digital inventory. Second, advertiser sentiment has improved in select categories, with a notable surge from large tech platforms actively using JCDecaux's formats to promote brands and solutions across environments. Third, the Chinese New Year generated record travel volumes in Q126, boosting audience reach and campaign activity. Management emphasized that these elements collectively supported Q1 performance. However, the company has recently signalled a slightly negative performance in Q226 with weaker consumption levels in the country.

\- Middle-Eastern exposure appears manageable and has shown signs of stabilization. On the recent conference call (FY25 results), management highlighted that the Middle East accounts for “a bit under 5% of group revenue and is reported within Rest of the World”. Decaux’s exposure is primarily airport advertising, with the largest footprint in Dubai/Abu Dhabi and a smaller footprint in Saudi Arabia as well as, street furniture in Qatar and Oman. Management emphasized that risk intensity varies by country, citing “Saudi Arabia and Oman as less exposed, while Abu Dhabi is more exposed than Dubai, and Bahrain is also exposed”. That said, JCDecaux management cautioned that continued or intensifying conflict could affect the business. Mitigation would involve three-party discussions with clients and airport partners to adjust and manage exposure. Operationally, the business is at this point functioning normally, though local teams are working remotely for security reasons. The key swing factor is the duration and potential escalation of the conflict. We note that the profit exposure is likely to be higher as Transport is generating the highest margins in the group. More recently, the company has highlighted while there has been a negative impact on Middle East revenues, the impact is better than had been previously expected. We also highlight air travel industry data (see below) that indicates a gradual recovery in MENA outbound air travel.

\- Key Sporting Event - World Cup: As previously stated, the company has signalled a €30m incremental advertising spend as a result of the 2026 World Cup (Q226-Q326) to be hosted in the US, Canada and Mexico. We anticipate that the remaining c.60% of revenues will fall within Transport (as we estimate that the other 40% is recorded in Street Furniture). Similarly, we expect better margins given this is incremental revenue.

\- Key Contract Losses - Hong Kong Citybus and Danish Rail: During JCDecaux's FY25 results, management confirmed that the company had not renewed its Hong Kong Citybus and Danish Rail contracts, which we estimate accounted for a combined €20m in revenue at average operating margins. The Danish Rail contract was lost to Bauer Media, who had acquired Clear Channel's North-Europe assets and management notes that the winning bid was made on sensible terms.

\- Key Contact Win - Stockholm Bus & Metro: In September 2024, JCDecaux announced that it had been awarded the 7-year Stockholm bus shelter and subway station advertising contracts. The contract was initially expected to begin in January 2026; however, following delays, we estimate that the bus component of the contract began in February and the Metro component in May. Overall, we anticipate a €15m revenue uplift in FY26 at a below average operating margin.

\- Key Contact Win - Denver Airport - now pushed to 2027: In March 2026, JCDecaux announced that it had won a 10-year advertising contract for Denver International Airport. While originally the contract was expected to go into effect in May 2026, it has now been delayed to 2027. We expect a c.€20m revenue uplift in

FY27 at below average margins given it will the first year of the contract.

# Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks

JCDecaux (Overweight; Price Target: €30.00)

## Investment Thesis

After 4 years of being Neutral/Underweight on JCDecaux, we recently left behind (here) our cautious view as we see a clear inflection: 1) Digital now reached close to $50\%$ of revenues in 2025, becoming a key driver behind improving margins, turning for the first time in years; 2) FCF dynamics are improving quickly as management finally executes well on working capital and capex (for screens) now beyond peak; 3) the advertising backdrop is improving across most regions, particularly in China; 4) recent contract wins in Stockholm, Denver and Barcelona as well as the retail contract with Carrefour/Carmila in France add revenue visibility for 2026 and 5) JCDecaux's programmatic platform continues to scale.

## Valuation

We set a DCF-based Dec-27 PT of €30 (previously €29). We assume a WACC of 9.5% and Terminal Growth rate of 2.0%.
