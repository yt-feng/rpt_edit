## Investment Thesis

After 4 years of being Neutral/Underweight on JCDecaux, we recently left behind (here) our cautious view as we see a clear inflection: 1) Digital now reached close to 50% of revenues in 2025, becoming a key driver behind improving margins, turning for the first time in years; 2) FCF dynamics are improving quickly as management finally executes well on working capital and capex (for screens) now beyond peak; 3) the advertising backdrop is improving across most regions, particularly in China; 4) recent contract wins in Stockholm, Denver and Barcelona as well as the retail contract with Carrefour/Carmila in France add revenue visibility for 2026 and 5) JCDecaux's programmatic platform continues to scale.
