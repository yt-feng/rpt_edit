# Monthly Databook: 3Q iPhone/iPad Build Estimates Maintained

## Key Takeaways

3Q26e iPhone build estimate remains at 54M units (+4% q/q, -2% y/y).

3Q26e iPad build estimate remains at 13M units (flat q/q, -7% y/y).

2Q26 iPhone build was in-line at 52M units (-7% q/q, +12% y/y), compared to the typical 15-25% q/q decline observed in the second quarter. Our supply chain observations indicate that major assembly partners, including Hon Hai, experienced better-than-seasonal production levels this quarter, with iPhone shipments in June showing month-over-month growth. This suggests to us that iPhone sell-through continues to trend well amid memory cost adjustments.

We maintain our 3Q26 iPhone build estimate at 54M units (+4% q/q, -2% y/y). The slower year-over-year run rate reflects new model SKUs shifting toward the premium segment (18 Pro/Pro Max and iPhone Fold), while the iPhone 18 model is scheduled for introduction in 1H27. We continue to expect 7-8M iPhone Fold builds in 2H26 (unchanged) and will monitor the ramp-up progress for any potential adjustments.

2Q26 iPad build is in-line with estimates at 13M units (+8% q/q, -10% y/y). The year-over-year decline reflects a higher comparison base in 2Q25 due to product launches. We maintain our 3Q26 iPad build forecast at 13M units (flat q/q, -7% y/y), as we expect stocking to remain at normal levels, although memory and material cost adjustments could have an impact.
