## Our Takeaways

## Bullish tone and raise in the guidance is a function of a strong setup

ASML has indicated that aggressive capacity adds in leading edge nodes (including 5nm and below as well as 1b and 1c in memory) has taken capex higher in both logic and DRAM. Long term agreements (LTAs) at customers provide more visibility, which increases customer confidence to spend capex and add capacity. Besides the capacity the needed, ASML also commented on higher litho intensity with increasing number of immersion and EUV layers in memory. Customers are believed to continue to add more capacity in FY27 and FY28.

## Indicated capacity is not a hard ceiling and the focus should be on revenue capacity rather than number of boxes

ASML has indicated capacity for 65 EUV tools this year, 85 in 2027 and 110 in 2028. Interestingly, on the call they were not willing to say that 85 was peak 2027 capacity. ASML has indicated that 85 tools for 2027 is a good representation of customer needs and capabilities at this time of this report, but this could evolve. ASML has indicated that the impact from higher productivity tools in the mix would be equivalent to another 10% increase in capacity and thus if revenue capture is to be proportional to capacity provided to the customer, it is thus implied is that EUV revenue growth should be theoretically equivalent to 45% YoY.

## More favorable pricing environment

The co. gave a new msg - they see potential to use the current short-supplied market to make pricing changes that they would not be able to do in a normal market. That change will take time, but the co. plans to continue to implement value-based pricing, which will enable it to raise prices on next-generation tools as customers are capturing significantly more value in the current environment. On the F tool in particular, we see much more potential for the company to change the pricing dynamic.

On the much stronger than expected gross margin guidance for the year the company said this was driven by 1) more EUV and immersion 2) better priced EUV tools (D tools fading out and F tools coming in later) 3) IBM revenue higher and with a better mix – both upgrades and higher EUV services revenue 4) higher volume (fixed cost coverage).
