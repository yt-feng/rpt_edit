# China Industrials Shipping supply chain weekly: trade flows, freight and Hormuz updates (week 28)

## Key highlights from high-frequency data

We compile weekly data from UBS Evidence Lab, the Ministry of Transport and other third-party data providers to track the latest trade flows across shipping, shipbuilding and ports.

## Uncertainties remains at Hormuz Strait

Transits in Hormuz Strait have eased back, with an average of 30 vessels transited per day last week, down from 45 per day across the first week of July. Our Maritime Trade Disruption Monitor (> Access Dataset) indicated a notable decrease in the oil tankers departing eastbound from Hormuz from July 8th. VLCC average earnings from Middle East/West Africa to China strengthened last week with resurgence of VLCC activity in the USG, with Middle East/US Gulf/West Africa-China VLCC TCE +65%/+0%/-14% vs. pre-conflict level.

## Container shipping volume and rates eased

Container throughput at key ports in China decreased 15% WoW and 11% YoY last week under the impact of Typhoon. After hitting the intra-year high, main lane freight rates softened last week: overall SCFI freight rates decreased by 4% WoW last week (+84% YoY), with Shanghai-Europe -3% WoW and Transpacific -2-6% WoW; Port of LA imports were decreased 3% YoY in week 28 and estimated to increased 14% YoY in week 29. Intra-Asia containership chartering index increased by 1.7% WoW last week.

## BDI & earnings of bulker and new build price remained elevated

The bulker market surged again this week, with average earnings of capesize increasing 15%, supported by robust demand in the Pacific. The shipbuilding newbuild price index maintained upward momentum and CNPI hit record high in June, with sustained demand for tanker and bulker.
