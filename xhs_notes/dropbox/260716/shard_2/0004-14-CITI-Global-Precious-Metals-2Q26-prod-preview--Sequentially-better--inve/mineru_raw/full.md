# Global Precious Metals

## 2Q26 Production Preview: Sequentially Better; Investor Focus Likely on Cost and Inventory Release; Changes to Target Prices

## CITI'S TAKE

Reporting season for precious miners will kick off on 17 July and we expect production performance to be sequentially better on a lower base (1Q is generally weaker seasonally). However, production performance could look somewhat mixed on a year-over-year basis for these companies. Overall, we expect the quarterly production trends to closely track the full-year guidance across the coverage. The market will likely focus on outlook commentary to gauge, among other factors: 1) cost performance in the June quarter (impact of higher crude oil prices); 2) production performance at some of the mines, e.g., Mogalkewna (1Q was weaker on maintenance being brought forward), Styldrfit, and Eland (both ramping up), IMP's Canadian operations (whether production will exceed guidance), and the Keliber lithium project; and 4) inventory release (IMP/NPH). We have also updated our model to incorporate the latest commodity prices and revisited our assumptions, resulting in changes to target prices but unchanged ratings.

VAL: 2Q26 production preview – we expect stronger production performance quarter-over-quarter on a lower base — VAL reports on 17 July. Total production is likely to increase by 10% quarter-over-quarter while mined production is likely to increase by 16%. The increase is primarily due to a lower base as 1Q was impacted by seasonality. Year-over-year we expect mined production to increase by 22% primarily due to higher production at Amandelbult (2Q25 was impacted by rainfall-led operational issues). Refined production is expected to increase by 17% quarter-over-quarter as (1) 1Q refined production run rate was lower than what the annual guidance suggests (lower base), and (2) VAL plans annual stock counts and planned maintenance in 3Q that could result in lower refined production in the September quarter and higher-than-normal production levels during 2Q/4Q.

IMP: FY26 production preview – saleable production likely to be flat year-over-year and towards the upper end of guidance — We expect group saleable production to remain flat (but towards the upper end of the guidance) at approximately 3.49 million ounces as higher production from the Rustenburg and Zimplats operations are likely to be offset by lower production from Canadian operations, Mimosa, and Marula mines. Refined production is likely to increase by approximately 5% year-over-year within the guidance. Quarter-over-quarter we expect 4Q FY26 production to increase by 11% on a lower base (lower matte production at Zimplats and in general a weak March quarter).

SSW: 2Q FY26 production preview – expecting stronger production performance quarter-over-quarter — South African PGMs and gold production are likely to increase quarter-over-quarter on a lower base as the March quarter is generally weaker. US PGM production is also likely to be stronger but in line with the annual guidance.

GFI: 2Q FY26 production preview – expecting stronger production performance year-over-year — Gold production is likely to increase by 7% year-over-year on (1) Salares Norte ramp up and (2) accounting for 100% of Gruyere mine production versus 50% in the previous comparable period. This is likely to be offset by lower production from the Damang mine (lease expiry). Quarter-over-quarter production is likely to marginally decline. For 1H FY26, production is likely to be 11% higher year-over-year.
