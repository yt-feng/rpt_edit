1. 结论先行：文章开头先回答“这份报告最值得看的判断是什么”，而不是介绍背景。

## Solid quarter with 1% revenue beat driven by Innovative Medicine

Sales of \$25.3bn grew 5.7% organic despite a 760-bps headwind from declining STELARA sales. Growth in the U.S. was 7.4% organic and 3.5% organic outside the U.S. Acquisitions and divestitures had a net negative impact on WW growth of 10bps. Adj EPS rose 4.7% to \$2.90, slightly above consensus (\$2.87).
