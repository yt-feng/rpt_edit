ZH_A: 今天聊IBM，这家公司刚刚发布了远低于预期的季度业绩预告，你怎么看？
ZH_B: 核心问题很明确，IBM的大型机业务正在经历一次比市场预想更剧烈的需求下滑，这直接拖累了整个财报。
ZH_A: 那这份报告真正要回答的问题是什么？是大型机不行了，还是IBM整体出了问题？
ZH_B: 报告想回答一个更关键的问题，CIO们对大型机的真实态度到底发生了什么变化，以及这种变化会不会持续。
ZH_A: 所以先说说业绩本身有多差？
ZH_B: 第二季度初步收入是172亿美元，比市场预期的178.6亿低了接近百分之四，每股盈利也比预期少了大约十美分。
ZH_A: 差在哪？
ZH_B: 主要就是基础设施收入，也就是大型机，同比下滑了百分之七，连带拖累了软件里的交易处理业务，导致软件增速只有百分之五。
ZH_A: 所以软件增长慢不是因为红帽，而是因为大型机相关的软件？
ZH_B: 对，报告明确指出，红帽仍然有大约百分之十一的增长，所以软件端的疲软是结构性的，不是全面性的。
ZH_A: 那CIO调查里怎么说？
ZH_B: 调查显示，有百分之三十三的CIO计划在未来五年减少大型机容量，这个比例从去年十一月的百分之二十三大幅上升。
ZH_A: 这意味着什么？
ZH_B: 这意味着大型机的长期需求预期在恶化，而且IBM自己解释说客户在抢购服务器和存储，因为担心涨价，但这反而说明客户在提前囤货，未来需求可能更弱。
ZH_A: 那咨询业务呢？
ZH_B: 咨询收入基本持平，但调查里净百分之十五的CIO表示会减少使用第三方咨询，这个趋势对IBM的咨询业务是进一步的压力。
ZH_A: 所以整体来看，IBM面临的是传统业务被AI和网络安全支出挤压的局面？
ZH_B: 没错，报告里一个关键洞察是，CIO的支出意愿正在从大型机和咨询转向生成式AI和网络安全，IBM在这两个领域的优势还不明显。
ZH_A: 那报告里有没有说哪些问题还没完全展开？
ZH_B: 有两个。第一，IBM的AI战略，特别是小模型路线，到底能不能在竞争中突围，报告没有给出明确答案。第二，咨询业务的疲软是暂时的还是结构性的，还需要更多数据验证。
ZH_A: 所以如果我想知道IBM的AI和咨询业务到底怎么走，还得看完整报告？
ZH_B: 对，完整报告里有更详细的CIO调查数据拆解和分业务的风险分析，这些能帮你判断IBM的底部到底在哪。

EN_A: So IBM just pre-announced a pretty disappointing quarter. What is the core issue here?
EN_B: The big picture is that mainframe demand is weakening faster than the market expected, and that is the main reason behind the miss.
EN_A: What question does this report really want to answer?
EN_B: It wants to understand what CIOs are actually thinking about mainframes right now and whether that shift is temporary or permanent.
EN_A: Let’s start with the numbers. How bad was it?
EN_B: Preliminary Q2 revenue came in at 17.2 billion, which is about four percent below consensus. EPS was also light, 2.93 versus the street at 3.03.
EN_A: Where did it go wrong?
EN_B: Infrastructure revenue, which is mostly mainframes, was down seven percent year over year. That also dragged down software growth to only five percent because transaction processing software is tied to mainframe sales.
EN_A: So the software weakness is not across the board?
EN_B: Exactly. The report points out that Red Hat is still growing at around eleven percent. So the weakness is concentrated in the more cyclical part of the software stack.
EN_A: What does the CIO survey tell us?
EN_B: It shows that 33 percent of CIOs plan to decrease mainframe capacity over the next five years, up from 23 percent just six months ago. That is a meaningful jump.
EN_A: What does that mean for IBM?
EN_B: It means the long-term outlook for mainframes is deteriorating. And IBM’s explanation that clients are buying ahead of price increases actually suggests future demand could be even weaker.
EN_A: What about consulting?
EN_B: Consulting revenue was flat. But the survey shows a net 15 percent of CIOs expect to use fewer third-party consultants, which is a headwind for that business.
EN_A: So the big picture is that traditional IT spending is losing to AI and cybersecurity?
EN_B: Right. The report’s key insight is that CIO spending intentions are shifting away from mainframes and consulting toward generative AI and security. IBM is not yet a clear winner in those areas.
EN_A: Are there any open questions the report doesn’t fully answer?
EN_B: Two big ones. First, IBM’s small model AI strategy remains an open question. Second, whether the consulting slowdown is cyclical or structural needs more data to confirm.
EN_A: So if I want to understand those risks better, I need to read the full report?
EN_B: Yes. The full report has a deeper breakdown of the CIO survey and a more detailed risk analysis that can help you figure out where the bottom really is for IBM.
