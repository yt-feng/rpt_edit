EN_B: It means the market is more focused on companies with complicated stories than on a deteriorating macro environment. The report makes a key point: the weakness is more about single stock stories getting messier, not the macro getting worse.

EN_B: Correct. The report says there are no flashing signs of a slowdown from J&J's data. But the problem is, these numbers are not going to provide any relief for the sector. Investors are hyper-sensitive to any negative news right now.

EN_B: It forecasts a gradual recovery as company-specific risks get resolved one by one.
