The Japanese Yen recently traded to its weakest level versus the US Dollar in 40 years, keeping the MoF focused on the exchange rate and seemingly ready to conduct JPY-buying operations again. In fact, the latest reports suggest that the MoF may end the warnings ahead of official intervention to further discourage short positioning.
