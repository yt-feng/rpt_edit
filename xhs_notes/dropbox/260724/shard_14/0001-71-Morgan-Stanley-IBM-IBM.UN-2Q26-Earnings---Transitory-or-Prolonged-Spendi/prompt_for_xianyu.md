What we learned from IBM's 2Q26 earnings conference call and IR callback:

(?) Why management believes the 2Q shortfall is transitory vs. the risk to this view. Management's core argument on deal slippage being a transitory issue resides in the details, which was that a low-double-digit number of large capex ELA deals at large clients were redirected in the final weeks of June toward servers, storage, and memory purchases ahead of expected supply-constrained price increases, rather than being a loss of demand or a structural shift away from IBM's platform. The key proof point is that roughly 1/3rd of those slipped deals had already closed within the first three weeks of 3Q, well ahead of the typical two-thirds to three-fourths closure rate management would expect over six months. And IBM's "base case" Software guidance of $6\%$ Y/Y in CY26 assumes the capex-deferral environment continues into 2H, with pipeline yields assumed to remain below historical norms.

That said, the risk to this argument, and guidance, is clear. We already knew from last week's pre-announcement that Infra revenue would fall short of expectations due to mainframe deferrals more than offsetting strength in Servers and Storage.
