Most importantly, it confirms that Soitec's near-term revenue trajectory is very strong and aligned with those customers. This should make the stock more accessible, as modelling becomes more straightforward. It also shows that Soitec's photonics revenue is not solely tied to the potential introduction of CPO, a common bear argument. We expect a material increase in consensus expectations for Soitec's FY27, which ends in March and is therefore best compared with CY26. What the market may still miss is that growth is more likely to accelerate from here than decelerate. Customers such as Tower, GFS and STM have all pointed to multi-year growth trajectories.
