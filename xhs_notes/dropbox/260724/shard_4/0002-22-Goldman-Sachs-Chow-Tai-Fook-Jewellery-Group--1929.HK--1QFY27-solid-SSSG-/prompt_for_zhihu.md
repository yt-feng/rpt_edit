## 1QFY27 trading updates

## Mainland China:

1QFY27 SSSG of self-operated stores came in at +19.6% yoy, mainly driven by ASP increase offset by 5.2% volume decline. It implies Jun-quarter SSS at 5% below the same period in 2019 (or pre-COVID level), accelerated from 15% below in the Mar quarter. SSSG of franchised stores was at 15.7%.

By product, fixed-price jewelry SSS was down by 1% yoy, with fixed-price gold/gem-set ASP growing by +40%/+4% yoy to HK\$9,100/8,700. Weight-based jewelry SSSG was up 38% yoy, with ASP increased by +26% to HK\$10,600. The company is still going through retail network optimization and it recorded net closed 258 stores in mainland China in the Jun quarter. RSV of e-commerce remained strong and grew by 28.9%, contributing to 8.9% of retail sales.

## HK and Macau:

1QFY27 SSSG was up 41.7% yoy, with Hong Kong up by 35% and Macau up by 64.6%, mainly driven by ASP increase while volume also saw 9.4% yoy increase. It implies Jun-quarter SSS at 71% of the level in 2018 (or normal level, i.e. pre-COVID and before the HK macro situation), vs. 4QFY26 of 80%.

By product, fixed-price jewelry SSS was up by 13.1% yoy, with fixed-price gold/gem-set ASP changed by +71%/+2% yoy to HK\$8,700/16,600. Weight-based jewelry SSSG was up 63.7% yoy, with ASP increased by +10% to HK\$17,100.

FY27 guidance reiterated: Mgmt reiterated MSD-HSD revenue growth, HSD SSSG in ML China and low-teens SSSG in HK and Macau. Under the assumption of gold price US\$4,300/oz, GPM/OPM guidance remained at 26.5%-27.5%/c.14%, respectively; and HK\$500-800mn of hedging gains. At the current c.US\$4,100 per ounce, mgmt expects limited revenue impact with weight-based gold demand being supported. A higher weight-based gold mix may affect GPM, but would be offset by operating leverage from higher weight based products sales/hedging gains, leaving full-year net profit broadly stable.

Recent trends: For Jul 1-19, ML China SSSG decelerated to SD% growth, while HK and Macau posted double-digit growth. Management noted that the SSSG performance in the first two weeks of Jul was negatively impacted by weather headwinds and traffic dilution from World Cup event, while noting that the performance improved in the third week of Jul. On product mix, while fixed priced products underperformed in Jun quarter amid gold-price volatility, management noted that Jul MTD fixed-price products SSSG at ML China self-operated stores was at DD%, outperforming vs. the overall performance, driven by new products.

Dawn collection a positive surprise: Dawn collection's 1QFY27 RSV exceeded HK\$870mn, ahead of mgmt expectations, with new members accounting for over 20% of customers. Mgmt raised FY27 sales guidance to over HK\$3bn (prior: HK\$2bn). Management also noted that overall gem-set products sales grew over 80% yoy, mainly driven by the Dawn collection. The Chinese Couture High Jewelry collection sold over 250 pieces during its June launch events.

Channel strategy: In ML China, the company opened 14 stores while net closed 258 stores in 1QFY27, versus 311 closures 1QFY26. Mgmt expects 600-700 net closures in FY27, mainly in 1H. That said, new stores generate around 3 times the sales of closed stores, limiting the revenue impact from network optimization. Overseas, the company recently opened luxury stores in Bangkok and Canada and upgraded its Malaysia Pavilion store. A new Singapore store is planned by year-end. Future expansion will focus on prime locations in Southeast Asia and North America.

Regional markets comments: 1) ML China: Tier 1/2 cities outperformed lower-tier cities, self-operated stores outperformed franchised stores, and shopping-mall stores outperformed lower-traffic locations. 2) HK&Macau: Tourists contributed c.60% of HK sales and over 80% of Macau sales, broadly unchanged from a year ago and pre-Covid levels.
