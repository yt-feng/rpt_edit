## Key takeaways from conference call

FY27 guidance reiterated: Mgmt reiterated MSD-HSD revenue growth, HSD SSSG in ML China and low-teens SSSG in HK and Macau. Under the assumption of gold price US\$4,300/oz, GPM/OPM guidance remained at 26.5%-27.5%/c.14%, respectively; and HK\$500-800mn of hedging gains. At the current c.US\$4,100 per ounce, mgmt expects limited revenue impact with weight-based gold demand being supported. A higher weight-based gold mix may affect GPM, but would be offset by operating leverage from higher weight based products sales/hedging gains, leaving full-year net profit broadly stable.

Recent trends: For Jul 1-19, ML China SSSG decelerated to SD% growth, while HK and Macau posted double-digit growth. Management noted that the SSSG performance in the first two weeks of Jul was negatively impacted by weather headwinds and traffic dilution from World Cup event, while noting that the performance improved in the third week of Jul. On product mix, while fixed priced products underperformed in Jun quarter amid gold-price volatility, management noted that Jul MTD fixed-price products SSSG at ML China self-operated stores was at DD%, outperforming vs. the overall performance, driven by new products.

Dawn collection a positive surprise: Dawn collection's 1QFY27 RSV exceeded HK\$870mn, ahead of mgmt expectations, with new members accounting for over 20% of customers. Mgmt raised FY27 sales guidance to over HK\$3bn (prior: HK\$2bn). Management also noted that overall gem-set products sales grew over 80% yoy, mainly driven by the Dawn collection. Channel strategy: In ML China, the company opened 14 stores while net closed 258 stores in 1QFY27, versus 311 closures 1QFY26. Mgmt expects 600-700 net closures in FY27, mainly in 1H. That said, new stores generate around 3 times the sales of closed stores, limiting the revenue impact from network optimization. Future expansion will focus on prime locations in Southeast Asia and North America.

Regional markets comments: 1) ML China: Tier 1/2 cities outperformed lower-tier cities, self-operated stores outperformed franchised stores, and shopping-mall stores outperformed lower-traffic locations. 2) HK&Macau: Tourists contributed c.60% of HK sales and over 80% of Macau sales, broadly unchanged from a year ago and pre-Covid levels.
