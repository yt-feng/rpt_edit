• UW: WIN Semi, Silergy, ASMedia

Long-term demand drivers:

\- Chip inflation: We expect “price elasticity” to affect demand for tech products. Rising wafer, OSAT, and memory costs create more margin headwinds for chip designers in 2026.

\- AI cannibalization: Besides demand weakness (AI to replace some human jobs), we see the semi supply chain also prioritizing AI semis over non-AI semis, e.g., T-Glass and memory shortage.

\- China – AI: DeepSeek is triggering inferencing AI demand: DeepSeek has demonstrated cheaper inferencing. Local foundry supply chain is also becoming more capable for AI GPU production.
