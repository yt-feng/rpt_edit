# Sequentially weakening momentum in 2Q26

Operational improvement a key to defend market share amid sluggish sales and fierce competition

2Q26 sector's sales losing momentum

Major domestic sportswear companies have lost sales momentum in 2Q26 after experiencing solid sales growth in 1Q26 (Fig. 1); brands such as Li Ning (2331 HK, Neutral) and Xtep (1368 HK, Neutral) started to see y-y sales declines in 2Q26, while brands such as ANTA core and FILA (both owned by ANTA, 2020 HK, Buy) remained on positive sales growth tracks (sales growing by a low-single-digit percentage, or LSD%, for both brands, but also slowed down meaningfully from the brands' 1Q26 sales numbers). We believe major headwinds on sportswear companies' 2Q26 sales momentum included increasingly sophisticated Chinese consumers (thus higher demand for both value and functions of the products), lack of significant product innovation/differentiation, fierce competition, as well as unfavorable weather conditions in 2Q26 (mainly impacting offline sales). Also in 2Q26, major domestic players have seen relatively deeper discount levels with their efforts to accelerate sales, while their inventory levels have been maintained at more or less healthy levels (Fig. 2), except for Xtep (inventory as of end-2Q26 at 4.5-5.0 months, worsening from 4.5 months as of end-1Q26).

## Operational improvement a key to defend market share

Looking into 2H26F, we see a continued drag on the sector from the aforementioned headwinds; on the positive side, most of the sportswear brands, such as the ANTA core brand and Li Ning, will be helped by low comparison bases from 2H25 (see Fig. 1) and mismatching of the Mid-autumn Festival/National Day Holiday in 2025/2026. Net net, we expect the three domestic names under our coverage to deliver mediocre revenue growth of c.3% y-y on average. For sportswear players, we expect measures to improve their operations will be the key to defend their market share, as there might be no meaningful product differentiation among the sector in the next few quarters. For example, ANTA has recently reshuffled the top management of the core ANTA brand and repositioned some of its signature store formats (scaling back the ANTA Sneakerverse and Super ANTA stores; opening the new ANTA Market stores); Li Ning has been closing down its underperforming directly operated stores; even international players such as Nike (NKE US, Not rated) have been taking proactive moves to rejuvenate their China businesses (closing down of third-party online distribution in China, investing more in the local market-driven product design).

## We prefer ANTA in the China sportswear sector

We have summarized the operational highlights of major domestic sportswear players in 2Q26 on the second page of this report. Among them, ANTA remains our preferred name in the sportswear space, given its exposure to structural growth opportunities (especially running and outdoor activities) in the long term and attractive valuation levels.

## Key operations in 2Q26 for major domestic sportswear names

ANTA (2020 HK, Buy) – resilient performance amid sector slowdown
ANTA announced a set of solid 2Q26 operating data (17 July).

\- The ANTA core and FILA brands' sales both increased by LSD% and LSD% y-y in 2Q26, while other brands' sales grew by 25-30% y-y. Although ANTA, like its other peers, experienced a slowdown in sales momentum in 2Q26, its sales outperformed the overall sportswear sector, indicating the resilience of ANTA's multi-brand strategy and solid operational capability, in our view.

\- ANTA has been adjusting operations across its brand portfolios, amid a sales slowdown for the sportswear sector as well as increasing competition from niche segment leaders, which in our view should help the ANTA core brand defend its market share in the mass market segment (e.g., online sales of the ANTA core brand recorded double-digit-percentage y-y growth in 2Q26 after the product/channel adjustments since 3Q25).

\- On the other hand, brands such as DESCENTE and KOLON are still delivering solid growth thanks to their training/outdoor exposure.

\- ANTA remains our preferred name in the sportswear sector, given its exposure to structural growth opportunities in the long term and attractive valuation levels. Our target price for ANTA is HKD89.90, with a Buy rating. For more details, please refer to our latest report.

Li Ning (2331 HK, Neutral) – A disappointing second quarter

Li Ning reported its 2Q26 operating data (15 July).

\- Quarterly retail sales (excluding LI-NING YOUNG) declined by LSD% y-y, with the offline and online channels registering a LSD% sales decline and mid-single-digit percentage (MSD%) y-y sales growth, respectively; including sales of LI-NING YOUNG, the company reported largely flat y-y sales growth for 2Q26.

\- While the company has maintained its full-year sales and margin targets, we believe the likelihood of Li Ning missing its FY26 sales target has increased following its lackluster 2Q26 sales performance. Li Ning's two major product categories – Running and Sports Leisure – recorded y-y sales declines in 2Q26, reversing their y-y growth trajectories in 1Q26, despite the company's efforts to introduce refreshed products, innovative running technologies and new brand ambassadors to the sports leisure segment.

\- We believe Li Ning's weak sales performance in these two segments was mainly a result of tepid consumer demand and fierce competition, which are unlikely to get better in the short term, in our view. Our target price for Li Ning is HKD16.00, with a Neutral rating. For more details please refer to our latest report.

## Xtep (1368 HK, Neutral) – Core brand sales dropped in 2Q26

Xtep reported a set of mediocre 2Q26 operating data (17 July).

\- The Xtep core brand recorded a y-y sales decline of MSD% in 2Q26, leading to a LSD% y-y sales decline in 1H26. The softening sales momentum was due to tepid demand, Xtep's less exposure to shopping malls with higher consumer foot traffic, intensified competition in the mass market, as well as unfavorable weather conditions.

\- On the other hand, for the historically fast-growing Saucony brand as well, sales moderated with only LSD% y-y growth of 2Q26 sales, slowing down from the $20\% +$ y-y growth in 1Q26. Saucony's declining sales momentum was mainly due to the brand's conservative actions for the online channel with no aggressive promotions, dragging its online sales; the offline sales of Saucony still grew $20\% +$ y-y in 2Q26.

\- In all, we see growing uncertainties for the company in defending its market share, mainly considering the sluggish sales of the Xtep core brand. Our target price for Xtep is HKD4.20, with a Neutral rating. For more details please refer to our latest report.
