# LG Display (034220.KS)

# Earnings Review: 2Q26 miss; expect higher OLED shipment but stronger pricing pressure

034220.KS 12m Price Target: W11,600 Price: W10,110 Upside: 14.7%

2Q26 miss likely due to one-off voluntary retirement cost LG Display (LGD) reported 2Q26 revenue of W5.6tn and operating loss of W108bn, where operating loss was in-line with Bloomberg consensus estimate of W110bn but slightly worse compared to GSe of W80bn. Given the sizable one-off voluntary retirement cost of 240bn, excluding the one-off cost, the company would have generated around a W130bn normalized OP. Despite improving OP trend on a yoy basis, the company provided weak ASP guidance which we believe could be due to the pricing pressure stemming from higher memory price, and we hence lowered our expectation for 2H26. Remain Neutral-rated on the stock with updated TP of W11,600 (from W13,500).

## Key Takeaways

\- Mobile OLED: LGD guided company overall ASP to increase by high-teens% in 3Q26, which is well below our prior expectation (up $30\%$ qoq) and implies a yoy decline of $5\%-8\%$ . We believe the weak ASP guidance implies pricing pressure on OLED for smartphone and tablet, where key customer brand is facing higher cost burden due to rising memory price (link). On the other hand, we expect stronger smartphone OLED shipment this year thanks to solid sales of iPhone 17, the company's improved capability of mass production of upgraded specs, and favorable competitive dynamics, and hence raise our smartphone OLED shipment estimates from 82mn units to 86mn units ( $+13\%$ yoy).

Large-size OLED (OLED TV and OLED monitor): We believe 2Q26 shipment was meaningfully better than expected due to World cup event effect and solid demand for OLED gaming monitor. Reflecting solid demand for OLED gaming monitor, we raise our estimate for this year large-size OLED shipment to 7.5mn units (+17% yoy) where yoy growth is mostly led by growth in gaming monitor. On margin, due to the end of the depreciation schedule of Guangzhou OLED TV fab, we expect large-size OLED OPM meaningfully improved yoy and reached double-digit%.

IT OLED (tablet): We believe weak tablet OLED demand continued in 2Q26 and with the absence of new iPad Pro model launch this year, we lower our annual shipment expectations from 4mn to 3.2mn units and expect $26\%$ yoy decline in shipment. Despite lower fixed costs due to improved capacity efficiency, we expect weak IT OLED shipment and pricing pressure to lead to operating loss for the business this year.

\- Our updated view on 3Q26 and 2026 full year: Although we raise our shipment estimates for TV and smartphone OLED, reflecting the weak ASP guidance and our view of stronger pricing pressure, we lower our 3Q26 and 2026 full year OP estimates to W400bn (from W564bn) and W958bn (from W1.2tn), respectively.

## Lower earnings estimates and TP to W11,600; remain Neutral-rated

Mainly reflecting 2Q26 results, 3Q26 guidance, higher large-size OLED/smartphone OLED shipment, but lower large-size OLED/smartphone OLED ASP and lower tablet OLED shipment, we lower our 2026E/2027E/2028E EPS estimates from KRW 251/2,093/2,221 to KRW -662/1,505/1,843. We maintain our target P/B multiple of 0.9X, while we lower our 12m fwd target P/B based TP to W11,600 (from W13,500) and remain Neutral-rated. Key risks include higher/lower IT LCD panel pricing and higher/lower TV OLED shipment.

## GS Factor Profile

The GS Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows:

Growth is based on a stock's forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock's forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock's forward-looking P/E, P/B, price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile).

Financial Returns and Multiple use the GS analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics).

For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.

## M&A Rank

Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
