# SK Telecom (017670.KS): Established SK Hyper as AIDC development vehicle

## What happened

On Jul. 23, SKT disclosed that its board approved the establishment of SK Hyper, a wholly owned AI data-center development subsidiary, with an equity commitment of up to W750bn through 2030 (link). SKT plans to inject an initial W330bn by end-July, with the remaining W420bn to be deployed in stages depending on project progress. SK Hyper will sit between SKT and individual project companies. Separate project-level SPVs are expected to be established for each AIDC, with SKT seeking financial investor (FI) capital at the SPV level rather than funding the entire build-out directly. The announcement follows SKT's Jun. 29 disclosure outlining a first-phase 5GW rollout from 2029 and a longer-term ambition to expand capacity to 15GW by 2035.

According to local media (link), SK Hyper will oversee early-stage development activities including site acquisition, power-grid access, permitting, substation development, customer acquisition and commercialization, supporting SKT's previously announced plan to open 5GW of AIDC capacity in phases from 2029. The initial funding will mainly support development of the first c.2GW pipeline, centered on c.1GW projects in Ulsan and the western Chungcheong region. SKT also plans GW-scale infrastructure in the southwestern region.

## GS view

Financing structure provides greater clarity, but does not eliminate funding risk: Based on the announcement, the W750bn commitment appears to fund the AIDC development platform rather than represent the full construction cost of the previously announced 5GW strategy. While SKT intends to ring-fence individual projects into SPVs and attract project-level FI capital, no specific investors, financing terms or equity-sharing arrangements have yet been disclosed.

■ Capital is being committed ahead of tenant and financing visibility: The initial W330bn injection will primarily fund land, power access, permitting and substation infrastructure for the initial c.2GW pipeline. Although these investments may improve project readiness, they will be deployed before final tenant commitments and project-level financing are secured, creating a risk of extended development periods, lower asset utilization or additional funding

## GS Factor Profile

The GS Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows:

Growth is based on a stock's forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock's forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock's forward-looking P/E, P/B, price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile).

Financial Returns and Multiple use the GS analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics).

For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
