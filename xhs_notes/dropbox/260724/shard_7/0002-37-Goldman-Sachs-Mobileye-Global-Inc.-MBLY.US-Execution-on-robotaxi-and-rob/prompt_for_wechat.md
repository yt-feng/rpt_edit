3. 纵向回答：每一层都要回答上一层提出的“为什么”或“所以呢”。
4. 横向 MECE：每个一级小节必须彼此独立、共同支撑主判断，避免重叠。

## Results

Mobileye reported 2Q26 revenue of \$508 mn (down 9% qoq and about flat yoy), which was above both GS and the Street (FactSet) at \$481 mn. The company commented that revenue reflected an increase in volume of systems shipped, offset by a lower EyeQ ASP due to higher than expected China OEM export volumes.

Adjusted gross margin in the quarter decreased by \~300 bps yoy to \~65.6%, compared to GS at 66.1%. The company commented that the yoy decrease was primarily due a reduction in the EyeQ ASP due to higher China OEM volumes and a higher portion of SuperVision related revenue.

Adjusted operating margin in the quarter was up yoy from 20.9% to \~30.5%, compared to GS and the Street at 8.9%. The company commented that the yoy increase was primarily due to a new R&D incentive passed into law recently in Israel, and the company recognized the entire 1H26 amount in 2Q which totaled \~\$93 mn (with the amount attributable to Q1 and Q2 roughly equivalent). We estimate that the operating margin excluding the R&D offset would have been 12.2%. Per the company, the R&D law benefit has no scheduled expiration date.

Non-GAAP EPS (ex. SBC) was \$0.19, compared to GS at \$0.05 and the Street at \$0.06. Excluding the R&D expense incentive, we estimate non-GAAP EPS would have been \~\$0.08-\$0.09.

Including SBC, EPS was \$0.08 vs. GS at -\$0.07.

The company ended 2Q26 with \$1.4 bn in cash and cash equivalents and repurchased \$24 mn in shares in the quarter.

## Business updates

In a separate press release, Mobileye announced that its CEO and Founder Professor Amnon Shashua informed the board of his intention to step down as CEO upon the appointment of a successor. Prof. Shashua will remain a director of the company, and the Board has offered Prof. Shashua the role of Chairman once a new CEO has been appointed.

For robotaxis, Mobileye highlighted its recent plan to launch its vertically integrated robotaxi fleet in 2027 starting with 100 vehicles in a major US metro and then expand to \~17,000 vehicles over the following five years. Recall in June the company announced its intention to utilize its AV technology (Mobileye Drive) to enter the robotaxi business more directly. Also, recall Mobileye would leverage its Moovit subsidiary for aspects such as its consumer facing application, fleet management, and teleoperation support.

The company also commented that its preparations for commercial robotaxi services with VW and MOIA continued on-track throughout the quarter with MOIA recently beginning public user testing in Hamburg, Germany with safety drivers in vehicles equipped with Mobileye's self-driving-system. Per management, VW remains on track for its 2H26 launch in Orlando, though Los Angeles is now expected to start in 2027 (from by the end of 2026 prior).

Mobileye also discussed its recent win with Stellantis to integrate Mobileye's REM production, which is its crowdsourced mapping technology that creates HD digital maps, with the first planned application expected in select US models next and additional

integration to follow.

On Mentee, the company believes it is on track for a 2028 commercial launch of its v4 robot (expecting \~500 units), with v3 expected later this year and v4 production intent units in 1Q27.

## Guidance

Mobileye increased its FY26 revenue guidance to \$1,970-\$2020 mn (up 4% to up 7% yoy) from \$1,935-\$2,015 mn (up 2% to up 6% yoy) prior, which is slightly above both GS prior at \$1,985 mn and the Street at \$1,984 mn at the midpoint. 3Q revenue is expected be down 5-6% yoy.

SuperVision shipments in 2Q were 20k, above company expectations, but Mobileye maintained its just under 60k outlook for the full year, citing potential inventory build in the quarter. The company now expects 2026 EyeQ shipments to be around 39 mn (up from 38 mn prior) reflecting the 2Q upside (with the company guiding 3Q EyeQ shipments to 9.3-9.5 mn, which implies 8.6-8.8 mn in 4Q).

Mobileye also increased its FY26 adjusted operating income guidance to \$365-\$425 mn from \$185-\$235 mn prior, which is above GS prior at \$212 mn and above the Street at \$215 mn. The new guide includes \$180-200 mn of a full year R&D incentive, and the new EBIT guidance at the midpoint excluding this would have been at \$205 mn at the midpoint vs. the Street at \$215 mn (and compared to the prior midpoint of \$210 mn).

On the R&D incentive, management noted that the cash inflow from the refund would be on a lag, with the company expecting cash for its 2026 incentives to start in 2028.
