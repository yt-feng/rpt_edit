## Investor Feedback Ahead of 2Q26 Earnings

Korea shipbuilding sentiment remains cautious following a sharp share price correction after the Canada submarine contract loss, leaving investors focused less on near-term upside and more on downside protection and “what can re-rate the group”. Into results, we expect the debate to center on (1) whether core commercial margins can hold up as input costs and wage negotiations evolve, (2) whether engine capacity/backlog constraints are easing (especially at HHI) and what that implies for delivery cadence and order timing, and (3) whether any “option values” (navy, AI data-center engine exposure, FDC) can become tangible enough to be reflected in valuation rather than remaining only as a narrative.

Retail investors net bought Hanwha Ocean and Samsung Heavy, while local institutions net bought KSOE.

\- Earnings estimate changes: Hanwha Ocean witnessed the biggest estimate change for 2Q26 OP, up $9\%$ vs. 1Q26 earnings release, HHI/KSOE 2Q OP estimates were mildly lifted by $2\% / 1\%$ , while Samsung Heavy saw 2Q earnings being cut by $8\%$ , largely in-line with our view for the quarter.
