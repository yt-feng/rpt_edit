## ServiceNow

F2Q Review: Results to Calm Fears Around Both Near-Term and Long-Term AI Disruptions as AI Control Tower Led Momentum Builds; Reiterate OW

ServiceNow results and outlook managed to allay investor concerns on Software companies from a combination of specific negative prints from IBM and PEGA, and on a more long-term basis concerns around slowdown in growth from increasing displacement of use cases from higher AI leverage by Enterprise customers. ServiceNow's results were robust and managed to demonstrate the same predictability in outcomes that investors would expect of a Software company with high visibility into revenues through its RPO, as well as the steady and accumulating acceleration of revenues tied to AI implementations tracking to strong growth and adoption. In relation to the AI outcomes that investors are keenly focused on to reassure themselves of the AI strategy, in particular AI Control Tower resonating with customers through visibility, security and governance controls, the company provided commentary to highlight the building momentum which includes: 1) AI ACV already crossing \$1 bn; 2) tracking to beat the guidance for \$1.5 bn of ACV in FY26; 3) on track already to beat long-term target of 30% of FY30 ACV mix to be driven by AI; 4) first time ServiceNow Agentic AI buyers growing over 45% year-over-year; and 5) Pro Plus SKUs offering pricing uplift of 30%+ helping in growth as customers incrementally adopt higher-tiered SKUs. The AI momentum helped in relation to the strong performance in the quarter as well as being incorporated in the outlook, where 1) cRPO growth of 21.5% in constant currency (19.3% organic, per our math which excludes Moveworks as well), about 200 bps ahead of consensus and pointing to continued underlying demand momentum; 2) cRPO outlook of 20% growth on a CC basis in F3Q, or 17.4% on an organic basis, per our math, which implies a \~200 bps deceleration in growth vs. 19.3% in F2Q, though the company has an established track record of beating; and 3) raise to the full-year Subscription revenue constant currency growth guidance which was raised to a growth of 21.0% y/y vs. 20.8% prior. That said, part of the upside in F2Q was driven by timing shift of on-premise government spend from F3Q to F2Q, limiting the magnitude of the raise to the full-year outlook to a lower level than the robust F2Q beat. Outside of the revenue drivers, the company delivered strong operating margin outperformance as well, despite lower than expected gross margins as the company continues to exercise operating discipline with focus on exiting the year with stable headcount despite multiple acquisitions. That said, the company did lower full-year gross margin expectations, which is not atypical relative to peer companies which are also trying to manage inference compute costs relative to customer engagement on AI products, even though for ServiceNow the primary driver of the softness is higher customer adoption of their solutions on the Public Cloud. Following the results and outlook, we believe investors should feel more reassured around the strong position that ServiceNow is establishing with its Enterprise customers through AI control tower and associated orchestration, augmented further through Veza in relation to visibility and Armis in relation to security, to put itself in a position to make robust progress (and potentially exceed) its FY30 targets for AI ACV as well as Rule of 60. We are reiterating our Overweight rating and establishing a Dec-27 price target of \$150 (vs. our prior Dec-26 PT of \$145).
