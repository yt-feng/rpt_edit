## Europe

Inflation persistence driving the EUR front-end. The ECB became the first major central bank to hike in the aftermath of the war in Iran, motivated by a persistent inflation overshoot in its latest projections. President Lagarde refrained from providing explicit guidance on the upcoming July decision, but relief headlines on a potential US-Iran deal have taken July pricing towards 6bp as of time of writing. This matters because the hawkish aspects of the ECB’s inflation projections come from the persistence of inflation, rather than the magnitude of the near-term oil spike. This suggests to us there is more room to price sharper relief in the UK front-end (given the gap to our policy rate views), but also that the ECB’s front-footed approach to policy tightening vs G4 peers should see the EUR curve — beyond 1y rates — outperform on selloffs. UK front-end remains an inflation relief trade. With no change in rates expected at next week's BoE meeting, focus will be on the Governor's message.
