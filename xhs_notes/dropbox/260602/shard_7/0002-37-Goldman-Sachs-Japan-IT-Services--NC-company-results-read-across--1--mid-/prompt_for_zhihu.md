# Japan IT Services: NC company results read-across (1) mid-tier SI/NI: IIJ (NW infra/services), Dentsu Soken (SI/financial/manufacturing)

In this report, we consider the potential implications for our IT services sector coverage from visits we made to two Not Covered companies in the mid-tier Sler (system integrator) and Nler (network integrator) space, Internet Initiative Japan (IIJ; network infrastructure/network services/MVNO) and Dentsu Soken (SI/financial/manufacturing). While IIJ's FY3/26 results missed guidance, there were signs in 4Q3/26 (Jan-Mar) that profitability in the network services business is bottoming out, and FY3/27 guidance calls for double-digit (%) profit growth driven by network infrastructure business, for which orders are strong. Dentsu Soken is generally performing well outside of the auto sector, with a strong start in 1Q12/26 (Jan-Mar) including double-digit (%) yoy growth in both operating profits and orders, driven by custom system development and proprietary software. In terms of read-across, we view strong demand for network infrastructure construction as a positive for BIPROGY (Buy), which has a strong presence and a high sales weighting in this field, and as a tailwind for NEC (Buy) and Fujitsu (Buy) as well. Below we outline our key takeaways from NC company commentary. (see Exhibit 1/Exhibit 2/Exhibit 3 for company earnings)

# IIJ (3774.T; NW infrastructure/NW services/MVNO)

(1) Guidance: FY3/27 guidance calls for operating profits of ¥38.5 bn (+11% yoy), with growth expected to accelerate on an underlying basis excluding the dropout of gains from the reversal of retirement benefit provisions (¥1.17 bn). In addition to bottoming profitability in network services, system integration (network infrastructure) is expected to drive growth, backed by strong orders. The company aims to improve profitability in network services by implementing price hikes, mainly for corporate customers (profit impact of just under ¥0.8 bn), and controlling other costs.   
(2) System integration (SI) business: 4Q3/26 orders remained strong, rising +51% yoy, with brisk inquiries from government agencies and the financial sector. While existing customers account for the majority, projects in new fields are increasing, and the company's share of wallet (within customer share) is on an uptrend. Demand is strong in network infrastructure construction for structural reasons, and the company expects the trend to continue in FY3/27. It also expects gross margin improvement as the weighting of maintenance business increases.

# Dentsu Soken (4812.T; SI/financial/manufacturing)

(1) 1Q12/26 (Jan-Mar) earnings: Operating profits were up +14% yoy in a strong start to the year. The order backlog at the end of 1Q was up a robust +33% yoy, and the company said it had greater confidence in achieving guidance.
