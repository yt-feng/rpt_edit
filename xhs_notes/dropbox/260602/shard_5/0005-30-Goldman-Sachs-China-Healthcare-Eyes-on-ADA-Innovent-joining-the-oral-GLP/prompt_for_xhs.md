# Investment Thesis - Hengrui

Hengrui is a leading pharma company in China that is in the process of transforming its business focus to innovative drugs from a “generics + innovation” model previously. Supported by an established strong sales team, Hengrui has launched 9 drugs with sales of Rmb1bn+ in the past few years, including 4 innovative drugs. With the majority of the impact from VBP price cuts largely reflected and reset to a lower base in 2022, we expect the future drivers for Hengrui to come from innovative drugs including PARP, CDK4/6, AR antagonist, PI3Ki and diabetes drugs. We are Buy rated as we believe the current valuation largely only reflects the company’s China prospects and overseas progress should present further upside. We expect growth in the current product cycle to slow down post the ramp-up of blockbuster PD-1, and that overseas progress will be the next key milestone for the company. Key risks: 1) a slower ramp-up of innovative drugs post the NRDL listing; 2) risk of failure in key late-stage R&D programs; 3) higher-than-expected R&D and admin expenses for global expansion; and 4) greater-than-expected price cuts for generics and innovative drugs.
