EN_A: So what does that mean for the industry?
EN_B: It means utilization rates will tighten significantly. The report forecasts marketed rig utilization reaching the mid-to-high 80 percent range by 2028. Historically, that level gives drillers strong pricing power.
EN_A: Pricing power on dayrates. How much are we talking about?
EN_B: They expect leading-edge dayrates for high-spec assets to recover toward 2023 peaks within one to two years. By 2028, 6G plus drillships could rise about 20 percent to around 525 thousand dollars per day. High-spec jackups could increase 10 to 15 percent to about 175 thousand by 2030.
EN_A: Which regions are driving this growth? Is it just Brazil?
EN_B: Brazil is important, but the report highlights Africa, Latin America excluding Brazil, and Asia Pacific as the fastest-growing markets. In those regions, offshore capex could grow more than 15 percent year over year.
EN_A: How should investors play this theme?

EN_A: Is RIG cheap?

EN_B: NOV actually has the highest offshore capex exposure among the stocks covered, over 40 percent of revenues. But the report notes that without any cold-stacked rig reactivations or newbuild deliveries, NOV stock performance might just track the peer group.
