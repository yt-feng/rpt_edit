ZH_A: 这次央行一季度货币政策报告，你觉得最值得关注的是什么？
ZH_B: 整体基调是“温和宽松”，但和去年四季度相比，央行的措辞明显更乐观了。
ZH_A: 乐观在哪里？经济增长还是通胀？
ZH_B: 两者都有。报告提到2026年开局强劲，PPI在三月份转正，所以央行对增长和通胀的判断更积极了。
ZH_A: 那是不是意味着宽松政策要收紧了？
ZH_B: 没那么简单。央行确实删掉了“逆周期和跨周期调节”这个表述，更强调“精准有效”的政策支持。
ZH_A: 所以重点从总量宽松转向了精准投放？
ZH_B: 对。报告还新增了一个专栏讲宏观审慎管理，说明央行现在更关注风险管理和政策传导，而不是全面加码宽松。
ZH_A: 这对利率和流动性意味着什么？
ZH_B: 报告明确说“引导隔夜市场利率围绕政策利率运行”，而不是之前的“短期市场利率”。这暗示央行更看重隔夜回购利率。
ZH_A: 那银行间流动性会怎么走？
ZH_B: 我们判断今年不会降政策利率或降准。但央行会通过充足的银行间流动性来支持实体经济，这种方式更灵活，也更容易逆转。
ZH_A: 具体来说，央行是怎么操作的？
ZH_B: 报告提到，春节后季节性现金需求下降，加上财政支出增加，金融机构的流动性需求减少了。所以央行顺势收了一些流动性。
ZH_A: 这不是收紧吗？
ZH_B: 是温和的回收，主要通过到期工具自然缩减，而不是主动紧缩。而且二季度中东局势不确定，央行会保持流动性充裕来管理风险。
ZH_A: 那什么情况下央行会真正收紧？
ZH_B: 关键变量是输入性通胀。如果进口价格上涨导致国内通胀预期升温，央行就可能逐步正常化流动性，引导隔夜回购利率回到政策利率附近。
ZH_A: 所以现在是一个“观察期”？
ZH_B: 没错。报告里还留了一些悬念，比如结构性工具的具体规模，以及央行在债券市场的新角色，这些都需要看完整报告才能完全理解。

ZH_B: 对，这些内容在报告里有更详细的展开。感谢收听，我们下期再见。

EN_A: So the PBOC just released its Q1 monetary policy report. What stood out to you?
EN_B: The tone is still moderately loose, but compared to the Q4 report, they sound much more confident about growth and inflation.
EN_A: More confident in what way?
EN_B: They cited a strong start to 2026 and noted that PPI turned positive in March. So they see the economy on a firmer footing.
EN_A: Does that mean they are ready to tighten?
EN_B: Not exactly. They removed the phrase about counter-cyclical and cross-cyclical adjustments, and instead emphasized precise and effective policy support.
EN_A: So it is less about broad easing and more about targeted measures?
EN_B: Exactly. They also added a special column on macro prudential management. That signals a shift toward risk management and better policy transmission.
EN_A: What does that mean for interest rates and liquidity?
EN_B: They now say they want overnight market rates to move around the policy rate, not just short-term rates. That is a subtle but important change.
EN_A: So the focus is on the overnight repo rate?
EN_B: Right. And our baseline is no policy rate cut or RRR cut this year. Instead, they will rely on ample interbank liquidity to support the economy.
EN_A: How are they managing liquidity then?
EN_B: The report says that after the Lunar New Year, seasonal cash demand dropped and fiscal spending picked up, so financial institutions needed less liquidity.
EN_A: So the PBOC pulled back?
EN_B: They drained some liquidity through maturing tools, but it was very measured. They are not actively tightening.
EN_A: What would make them actually tighten?
EN_B: Imported inflation is the key risk. If it pushes up domestic inflation expectations, they could normalize liquidity and guide overnight rates back toward the policy rate.
EN_A: So we are in a wait-and-see mode?
EN_B: Exactly. The report also leaves some questions open, like the scale of structural tools and the PBOC's evolving role in the bond market.
EN_A: Those are definitely worth digging into.
EN_B: For sure. The full report has a lot more detail on those topics. Thanks for listening, and we will catch you next time.
