## Investment Thesis

J&T Global Express is a leading platform-neutral, technology-driven 3PL operator – the market leader in Southeast Asia, a growing player in China, and an emerging challenger in high-growth New Markets (Brazil, Mexico, Middle East). The company's regional sponsor model combines entrepreneurial local execution with centralized technology, enabling rapid, capital-light expansion, while China-built automation and digital management systems are exported across regions, underpinning a structurally advantaged cost curve. Since its October 2023 IPO, J&T's share price has underperformed, reflecting sector price wars in China, initial loss-making status, and external shocks such as recent fuel price volatility. The market has also questioned the scalability of J&T's model outside China and the sustainability of margins in a maturing Southeast Asia, while New Markets were seen as too early-stage to move the needle. However, the business has now pivoted to profitability, is delivering industry-leading volume and margin growth, and is unlocking new synergies through its partnership with SF Holdings. Overweight on J&T.

## Risks to Rating and Price Target

Downside risks to our rating and price target include: 1) continued structural margin compression in China, if anti-involution weakens; 2) slowdown of SEA ecommerce GMV growth and increasing competition from competitors like SPX etc; 3) execution and profitability slippage in New Markets as heavy upfront investment and slower-than-expected localization of franchise models prolong the path to scale; and 4) geopolitical, regulatory, and trade-policy shocks could increase compliance costs and directly pressure cross-border parcel flows and demand.
