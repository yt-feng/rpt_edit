【金字塔原理写作原则】
1. 结论先行：文章开头先回答“这份报告最值得看的判断是什么”，而不是介绍背景。
2. 统领思想：全文只能服务一个主判断，避免变成摘要合集。
3. 纵向回答：每一层都要回答上一层提出的“为什么”或“所以呢”。
4. 横向 MECE：每个一级小节必须彼此独立、共同支撑主判断，避免重叠。
5. Synthesis over summary：不要复述报告段落，要提炼“这些事实合在一起意味着什么”。
6. So what：每个小节末尾必须落到对行业、公司、竞争格局、市场定价或读者观察框架的含义。

# Scaling on track: record slurry month; photoresist ramping up Quick Note

1H26 revenue +11% y-y, profit up 64-74% on semi materials acceleration
On 9 July 2026, Dinglong released its 1H26 preliminary earnings forecasts. The company expects 1H26 revenue of CNY1,925mn, up c.11% y-y, and net profit of CNY510-540mn, up 64.0-73.6% y-y, vs. CNY311mn in 1H25. Recurring net profit is guided at CNY483-513mn, up 64.4-74.6% y-y. Excluding the profit contribution of the deconsolidated printing consumables business, management estimates underlying net profit to grow more than 80% y-y. Non-recurring gains of c.CNY27mn (1H25: CNY17mn) mainly reflect government subsidies. In our view, the strong growth mainly benefited from capacity expansion at the two leading domestic memory makers and other downstream foundry clients, together with the continued rise in domestic localization rates for CMP (chemical mechanical polishing) materials and photoresist. With management guiding for the semiconductor materials segment to sustain significant growth into 2H26E, we see the pre-announcement as further evidence that Dinglong's semiconductor materials business is entering a phase of profitable scale.

Slurry +63%, pads +57%; photoresist orders scale; Haofei Materials consolidates

The earnings inflection was broad-based across the semiconductor materials platform. i) CMP slurry and cleaning solution revenue rose 63% y-y, generating profit at scale of c.CNY47mn, with June monthly sales exceeding CNY40mn — a record high — and management guiding further margin gains in 2H26E. ii) CMP pad revenue grew 57% y-y, with June shipments surpassing 50k pieces for the first time, and the company has started building a third soft-pad line at Qianjiang, targeting glass-substrate and large-size (>2m diameter) pads. iii) In high-end wafer photoresist, Dinglong secured c.1,000 gallons of purchase orders in 1H26, has cumulatively developed 40+ SKUs with nearly 30 under customer validation — in-testing SKU count doubling y-y — and is adding BARC (bottom anti-reflective coating) and SOC (spin-on carbon) ancillaries. iv) Display materials revenue declined slightly on soft panel utilization, though PSPI (photosensitive polyimide) and TFE-INK (thin-film encapsulation ink) achieved first batch supply on China's first G8.6 AMOLED (active-matrix organic light-emitting diode) line. v) In addition, the revenue of newly-consolidated Haofei Materials (Unlisted) grew c.60% y-y over March-June and, with the parent, is intensively developing innovative lithium-battery functional materials addressing safety, thermal management, electrical performance and energy density with top domestic customers.

Semi renaissance thesis intact: slurry runway broadens and photoresist inflects
We believe the pre-announcement reinforces the structural growth thesis in our 21 May 2026 report: A guide to Semi renaissance in 2026-30F. First, we expect slurry wins to broaden the runway: qualifications in large-silicon-wafer fine slurry (12-inch single-crystal), ceria slurry at a leading domestic memory maker and TSV (through-silicon via) slurry at a leading advanced-packaging house unlock a domestic TAM (total addressable market) of over CNY1bn currently dominated by imports. With domestic localization rates at only \~15% for KrF and 2-3% for ArF, penetration headroom remains, in our view.

We maintain our Buy rating and TP of CNY104, which is based on 96x 2026F EPS, at +2.5x SD of its 5-year historical average P/E of 51x. The stock currently trades at 89x 2026F P/E.
