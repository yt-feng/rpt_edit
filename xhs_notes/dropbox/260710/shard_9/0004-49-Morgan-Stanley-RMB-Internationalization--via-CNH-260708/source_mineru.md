We see this as continued, calibrated RMB internationalization under controlled channels.

PBoC Governor Pan announced new measures to reinforce Hong Kong as the offshore RMB hub:

\- Expand offshore bond investment channels: Raise the Southbound Bond Connect quota from Rmb500bn to Rmb800bn and broaden eligible investments.

\- Boost CNH liquidity: Increase the RMB Business Facility from Rmb200bn to Rmb500bn and extend tenor to up to three years.

\- Add hedging tools: Launch 5-year offshore RMB CGB futures.

\- Promote RMB pricing role: Build cross-border gold clearing/settlement and promote RMB-denominated commodity futures and spot products.

We view these measures as part of continued RMB internationalization beyond current-account usage, aimed at building a deeper offshore RMB ecosystem through controlled channels. The measures may help alleviate key bottlenecks in the CNH market, including relatively thin liquidity, a less developed benchmark yield curve, and insufficient hedging, repo and collateral channels.

Overall, this echoes our view that China will continue to adopt an asymmetric approach to capital account opening and RMB internationalization: tightening informal outflows while expanding regulated, Hong Kong-based RMB investment and funding channels.
