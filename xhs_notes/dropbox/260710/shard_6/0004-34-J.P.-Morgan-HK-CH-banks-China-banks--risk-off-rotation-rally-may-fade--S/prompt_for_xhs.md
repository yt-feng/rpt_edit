## China banks' risk-off rotation rally may fade; STAN pullback a chance to add

Renewed Middle East conflict concerns and profit-taking in the AI trade appear to be driving a risk-off rotation into perceived safe-haven China SOE banks, while weighing on higher-beta names such as STAN.
