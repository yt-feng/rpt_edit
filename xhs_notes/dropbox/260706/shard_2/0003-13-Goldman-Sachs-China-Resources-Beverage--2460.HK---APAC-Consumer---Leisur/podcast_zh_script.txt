ZH_A: 最近华润饮料的这份研报很有意思，上半年主动去库存，水业务销售预计下降，但公司却说终端需求更坚挺，这中间到底发生了什么？
ZH_B: 核心在于公司主动做了一次深度的渠道调整，而不是需求崩了。上半年他们花大力气清理渠道库存，部分区域库存下降了30%，所以出货量看起来不好看，但终端零售其实比出货表现要稳得多。
ZH_A: 所以这不是一个坏消息，而是公司在主动排毒？那饮料业务呢，是不是更糟糕？
ZH_B: 对，饮料业务调整幅度更大，因为去年新产品铺得太猛，今年要集中资源聚焦核心品牌，比如只做“至本清润”这一个重点。所以上半年饮料销售下滑会更明显，但这也是为了下半年轻装上阵。
ZH_A: 那下半年怎么看？公司有没有给出明确的增长抓手？
ZH_B: 有三个明确的增长方向。第一个是低线城市渗透，尤其是北方和成熟市场，他们认为还有很大的空白空间。第二个是家庭水渠道，已经进入7个试点城市，下半年要开始规模化。第三个是新兴渠道，比如硬折扣店，他们已经和万辰签了总部对总部的协议。
ZH_A: 硬折扣店这个很关键，但会不会打乱原有的价格体系？
ZH_B: 公司想得很细。他们专门为这个渠道做了520ml的定制规格，零售价定在1.2元一瓶，这样就和主流的555ml批发渠道产品区隔开了，保护了原有的价格体系。不过报告里没有完全展开的是，线上渠道虽然增长更快，但利润更薄，这块怎么平衡，还需要继续观察。
ZH_A: 那成本端呢？PET价格最近涨了，会不会影响利润？
ZH_B: 上半年毛利率基本能持平，因为前四个月锁定了低价PET。但6月开始PET价格走高，如果下半年维持在7000元每吨以上，全年毛利率就会承压。公司也在通过其他包装材料的采购优化来对冲一部分。
ZH_B: 对，按现在的股价算，股息率大概5.5%，而且回购的股票会注销，这对股东价值是实打实的提升。不过报告里还留了一个悬念——饮料业务到底什么时候能走出调整期？公司说下半年重点是执行，但具体到利润拐点，还需要看完整报告里的详细测算。

ZH_A: So this report on China Resources Beverage is really interesting. They are actively destocking in the first half, water sales are expected to decline, but management says retail demand is actually more resilient. What is going on here?
ZH_B: The key is that the company is doing a deep channel cleanup, not that demand is collapsing. In the first half, they aggressively reduced channel inventory, down about 30% in some regions. So shipments look weak, but sell-through at the retail level was much more stable.
ZH_A: So this is not bad news, it is more like a detox. What about the beverage segment? Is it even worse?
ZH_B: Yes, the beverage adjustment is deeper. Last year they launched too many new products, so this year they are focusing resources on just one core brand, Zhiben Qinrun. Beverage sales will decline more in the first half, but this sets the stage for a cleaner second half.
ZH_A: What about the second half? Are there clear growth drivers?
ZH_B: Three clear ones. First, lower-tier city penetration, especially in northern China and mature markets. Second, the family water channel, which is moving from pilot to rollout. Third, emerging channels like value retailers. They just signed an HQ-to-HQ agreement with Wanchen.
ZH_A: Value retailers are key, but won't that disrupt their pricing?
ZH_B: They have been very careful. They created a dedicated 520ml SKU for value retailers at 1.2 yuan per bottle, which is different from the mainstream 555ml SKU for wholesale. That protects the existing price architecture. But the report does not fully answer how they will balance fast-growing but margin-dilutive online sales.
ZH_A: What about costs? PET prices are rising.
ZH_B: First half gross margin should be roughly flat because they locked in low PET prices for the first four months. But from June onward, PET went higher. If it stays above 7,000 yuan per ton in the second half, full year margins will face pressure. They are offsetting some of that with savings on other packaging materials.
ZH_A: There is also a very important point on shareholder returns. They committed to a dividend of 0.37 yuan per share for 2026 to 2028, plus a 530 million Hong Kong dollar buyback.
ZH_B: Exactly. At the current price, that implies a dividend yield of about 5.5 percent, and the buyback shares will be canceled. That is a very strong commitment. But the report leaves one big question open: when exactly will the beverage business turn the corner?
