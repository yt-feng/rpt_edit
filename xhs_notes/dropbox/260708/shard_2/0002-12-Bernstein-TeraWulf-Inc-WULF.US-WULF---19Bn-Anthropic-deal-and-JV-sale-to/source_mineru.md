# WULF: \$19Bn Anthropic deal and JV sale to Fluidstack - our thoughts and model update

TeraWulf (WULF) announced \$19Bn AI contract with Anthropic (link), successfully meeting management's guidance for kentucky tenant by end of Q2'26. WULF also announced sale of Abernathy JV to group of investors led by Fluidstack, with \$530Mn sale proceeds to support future data center campuses (link). We believe this marks a shift in WULF's business model - moving towards full ownership, direct customer relationships and complete operational controls. In this note, we share key takeaways and update our model for the Anthropic deal and JV sale.

We update our model to account for the Abernathy JV sale, and update the Anthropic contract terms at Justified Data Campus with available information. The Anthropic deal has better economics than our modeled assumptions (average annual yield at \$2.4Mn per IT MW vs our assumption of \$1.9 Mn per IT MW). However, the net impact on our CY30E Adjusted EBITDA estimates after adjusting for Abernathy JV sale is marginal (incremental \$43Mn EBITDA impact). We maintain our Outperform rating with PT\$36.

Investment Implications

We rate WULF Outperform (PT \$36).

## DETAILS

## WULF - MOVING TOWARDS DIRECT LEASE MODEL

WULF announced a \$19Bn AI contract with Anthropic, delivering on management's guidance for Kentucky tenant in Q2'26. This marks WULF's third AI deal across growing bluechip clientele - Core42 (private), Fluidstack (Google backstopped), and Anthropic (private). WULF's AI orderbook now stands at \$27Bn contracted revenue against \~839IT MW over 10-20 years long term deals. We reiterate our Outperform on WULF led by its power M&A capabilities, bluechip orderbook and disciplined execution (Exhibit 1).

WULF announced \$19Bn deal with Anthropic: WULF has contracted out 401 IT MW to Anthropic (Exhibit 2) at the Justified Data site (Kentucky). WULF has not yet disclosed details on margin structure and capex, however key terms of the deal include:

\- Contract duration: WULF has signed a 20-year lease agreement with Anthropic at its Justified Data campus in Hawsville, Kentucky. Anthropic also has two 5-year renewal options to extend the total deal duration for another 10 years. This is the longest duration deal signed by emerging AI infra players (former crypto miners) with industry peers still securing 10-15 year base contract terms.

\- Revenue Yield: The total contract value is \~\$19Bn, implying an average ARR of \$950 Mn. Assuming 3% annual rent escalator implies \~10% improvement in base rent (first year rent) vs the previous Fluidstack deal at Lake Mariner. WULF continues to benefit from efficient PUE buildout (Justified Data contract implies a PUE of 1.2 - gross 480 MW and 401 IT MW).

\- Delivery Schedule: WULF expects to deliver the initial capacity to Anthropic in H2'27e, with full delivery expected in early 2028e.

\- Financing: WULF have not yet announced capex guidance or financing arrangement for the project. However, the deal is expected to be supported by investment grade credit, allowing favorable financing terms vs WULF's prior deals with indirect hyperscaler exposure through neocloud tenants and Google credit backstop arrangements.

WULF announced sale of Abernathy JV stake: WULF has also announced sale of 50.1% stake in Abernathy JV - simplifying accounting, and shifting towards an infrastructure business model with full ownership, direct customer relationships and complete operational control.

\- Abernathy JV structure: The JV was established in October 2025 to develop 168 IT MW data center campus in Abernathy, Texas. Fluidstack was both the JV partner and anchor tenant at the Abernathy site. WULF owned 50.1% share of the JV with \$9.5Bn contracted JV revenue over 25 years.

\- \$530Mn JV sale implies \~18% return on WULF's equity stake: WULF invested \$450Mn equity stake in the Abernathy JV. The stake is now being sold to investor group led by Fluidstack for \$530Mn (implying \~18% return), payable in 3 installments (i) \$250 Mn within 14 days of signing the Purchase agreement; (ii) \$150 Mn by Dec 31, 2026; and (iii) remaining \~\$130 Mn by April 30, 2027, subject to certain adjustment. WULF expects to redeploy the capital into fully owned AI infrastructure buildouts.

## We share our key takeaways from WULF's Anthropic deal:

\- Strengthening direct customer relationships: WULF is now shifting to direct customer leases, without neocloud intermediaries (Exhibit 3). This allows emerging AI infrastructure companies like WULF to work directly with end customers, optimize design specifications and benefit from improved credit quality (lower financing cost).

\- Deal economics continue to improve: WULF has not disclosed details on margin structure and capex yet, however deal economics are improving in terms of contract duration and revenue yield. Longer duration contract (20 years vs 10 years for earlier deals) derisk WULF's data center buildout and improves long term revenue visibility. While higher average revenue yield (30% improvement on average annual revenue and 10% improvement on base year rental vs prior Fluidstack deal at Lake Mariner) indicates strong pricing and demand environment for AI infrastructure.

\- Prioritizing balance sheet management and fully-owned data center campuses: WULF's Abernathy JV sale indicates management's intent to build an infrastructure business model with full ownership, direct customer relationships and

complete operational control. The Abernathy JV sale removes complex JV accounting for WULF's books, and allows it to redeploy the sale proceeds (\~\$530Mn) for development of future data center campuses (like Lake Cayuga, Muskie and Chesapeake) and future power pipeline.

\- We continue to like WULF's power pipeline: WULF controls 3.6GW of power assets (Exhibit 6) across key data center markets like New York, Kentucky, and Maryland. This de-risks WULF power pipeline from changing regulatory policies or grid dynamics in a single state and gives WULF an edge to serve hyperscaler customers that are looking for diversified geographic footprint. WULF leverages brownfield sites with existing power and transmission infrastructure to deliver HPC capacity at lower incremental capex vs greenfield buildouts. WULF's power pipeline supports multi-year development runway. Lake Mariner (\~228 MW remaining), Muskie (\~1 GW) and Cayuga (\~400MW) are in advance stages for medium term expansion while the Morgantown site provides long term opportunity to deliver 1GW campus with on site generation. WULF is targeting to deliver 250-500 MW of critical IT load each year by full development of existing power assets.

## FINANCIALS AND VALUATION

Key model changes: We update our model based WULF's \$19Bn Anthropic AI contract and announced sale of Abernathy JV. Our model already accounted for a 384 IT MW deal at Justified data, with \$1.9Mn per IT MW revenue yield. However, the actual contract with Anthropic delivers improved average revenue yield at \$2.4Mn per IT MW and 401 IT MW (implying best-in-class PUE of 1.2). However, we also adjust for sale of Abernathy JV, reducing WULF's share of the JV operating income modeled earlier (\~\$129Mn steady state EBITDA in CY30E). The reduction in EBITDA in FY27E is because of removal of share of Abernathy JV (which was scheduled to begin delivery in CY27E) against addition of the Anthropic contract (full ramp in 2028E).

Our AI revenue buildup for WULF now comprises \~839 IT MW delivered by H1'28 across 3 AI deals (Core42, Fluidstack - Google backstopped, Anthropic). We expect net AI revenue (net of cost pass through) to ramp up from \$209Mn in CY26E to \$1.7Bn by CY30E (Exhibit 4), growing at a CAGR of 70% over the same period. Our model only accounts for WULF's current orderbook (\$27Bn, \$1.7Bn ARR), and a conservative delivery schedule. Overall, we expect WULF to generate \$1.5Bn EBITDA (up 3% vs earlier estimates) by CY30E. We expect WULF's HPC EBITDA to grow at a CAGR of 94% over CY26-30E, growing from \$106Mn in CY26E to \$1.5Bn by CY30E. We expect EBITDA margins to stabilize at 85%.

Valuation: We rate WULF Outperform (PT\$36). We value WULF at 21x EV/ 1-year forward EBITDA on CY30E steady state EBITDA (Exhibit 5). The 21x one year forward EV/EBITDA multiple is based on long term average multiple of infrastructure businesses. Applying 21x multiple on CY30E adjusted EBITDA of \$1.5Bn, we arrive at \$31Bn EV for CY29E. We discount it to arrive at our target EV of \$25Bn (12 month target). After adjusting for \$6.6Bn in net debt, we arrive at our target valuation of \$36 per share.

In our view, any further power development/energization from its incremental 2.6GW pipeline leading to new AI co-lo leases is upside optionality for WULF. Our \$36PT assumes execution on current confirmed order book.

## VALUATION METHODOLOGY

## TeraWulf Inc

We value WULF at 21x one-year forward EV/EBITDA on steady state CY30E EBITDA (given the steep ramp-up). We discount the implied EV to arrive at our target valuation. We sunset the Bitcoin mining segment by CY28E. We rate WULF Outperform (PT\$36).

## RISKS

## TeraWulf Inc

Downside risk to our price target:

1. The AI infrastructure business is capex intensive and execution depends on WULF's ability to secure low cost and favorable financing.

2. Scalability of AI infrastructure business depends on WULF's ability to develop new powered sites, and its ability to procure land, equipment and execute the data center development in time. Any lag in supply chain or execution delays, could impact its ability to scale AI business.

3. WULF's current AI orderbook is concentrated in few customers. WULF needs to demonstrate robustness of its AI infrastructure business by diversifying its customer base over time.
