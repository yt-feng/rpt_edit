EN_A: What does a stable operating environment actually mean? Wasn’t the competition in Korea pretty fierce in recent years?
EN_B: It was, but the report says competition has stabilized. One big reason is the data breaches at SK Telecom and KT. That made operators more cautious. They are not fighting price wars like before. And capex is trending lower.
EN_A: So that means profits should improve?
EN_B: Exactly. Free cash flow is getting better. But the really interesting part is where the money is going. The report finds that operators are shifting capital away from traditional telecom equipment and into data center investments.
EN_A: Data centers. That is the AI optionality you mentioned. Are Korean telcos really serious about AI?
EN_B: Yes, and the scale is huge. The report mentions that SK Group, GS, and Naver are planning to build 8.4 gigawatts of AI data center capacity. That is backed by 550 trillion won in investment. The long-term target is 18.4 gigawatts by 2035.
EN_A: 8.4 gigawatts is a massive number. Why does Korea need that much AI data center capacity?
EN_B: There are two key drivers. First, Korea has one of the highest rates of AI chatbot usage in the world. Enterprise adoption is also accelerating. For example, Samsung Group decided to deploy external AI services across all its business units. That is a huge demand catalyst.
EN_A: And the second driver?
EN_B: Data sovereignty. Industries like memory semiconductors, defense, and financial services have strict rules against sending data outside Korea. That means AI inference capacity has to be built locally. You cannot just rely on data centers overseas.
EN_A: So it is a massive domestic investment cycle driven by real demand. What are the specific stock picks in this report?
EN_B: The top pick is LG Uplus. The analyst expects it to deliver the strongest earnings growth this year, driven by a workforce restructuring done in 2025. The expectation is that management will use the higher earnings to increase share buybacks.
EN_A: What about the other two big names? SK Telecom and KT?
EN_B: For KT, the report is waiting for more clarity on the new management’s strategy around AI and shareholder returns. For SK Telecom, the value of its AI assets is now the key driver for the stock price. The reinstatement of dividends is seen as a positive.

For Samsung SDS, the key re-rating catalysts are AI data centers, the enterprise AI deployment cycle, and cash deployment.
