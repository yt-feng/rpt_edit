EN_A: So it is more of a psychological threat than a real one. What about AI itself? How is it changing the industry?

EN_B: Surprisingly, no. Both software vendors and IT service providers are seeing productivity gains, but they are not seeing margin improvement. They either pass the savings to clients or absorb additional costs. So it is a case of more output, but not more profit.
