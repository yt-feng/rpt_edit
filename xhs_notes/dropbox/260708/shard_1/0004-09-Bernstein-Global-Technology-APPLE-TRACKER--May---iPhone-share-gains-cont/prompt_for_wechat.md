【金字塔原理写作原则】
1. 结论先行：文章开头先回答“这份报告最值得看的判断是什么”，而不是介绍背景。
2. 统领思想：全文只能服务一个主判断，避免变成摘要合集。
3. 纵向回答：每一层都要回答上一层提出的“为什么”或“所以呢”。
4. 横向 MECE：每个一级小节必须彼此独立、共同支撑主判断，避免重叠。
5. Synthesis over summary：不要复述报告段落，要提炼“这些事实合在一起意味着什么”。
6. So what：每个小节末尾必须落到对行业、公司、竞争格局、市场定价或读者观察框架的含义。
7. Action title：所有 `##` 小标题必须是“直接讲述洞察的完整句子”，不能是目录标签。

## 1. IPHONE SALES PERFORMANCE - SELL THROUGH UNITS UP 2% YOY, 1% MOM

Apple's May sell-through units were up 2% YoY and 1% MoM. Nearly all markets delivered positive YoY sell-through revenue growth, with Japan and emerging markets posting the strongest gains. China was the only exception, with sell-through revenue down 15.5% YoY, marking the first YoY contraction since the launch of the iPhone 17 series.

\- US: May iPhone sales up 6.5% YoY, volume up 4.6% YoY, ASP up 1.9% YoY. Apple continued to outperform the broader US smartphone market, expanding its market share by 3% from 50% in April to 53% in May. The YoY growth benefited from easier comps, as consumer demand was pulled forward into April 2025 amid tariff-related concerns, making May 2025 an easy comp.

\- China: May iPhone sales down 15.5% YoY, volume down 19.1% YoY, ASP up 4.4% YoY. This is the first YoY sales decline since the launch of the iPhone 17 series and was largely attributable to the weaker 618 promotions this year. During the 618 campaign in 2025, discounts on the iPhone 16 series ranged from \~\$175 to \$295. The entry-level iPhone 16 Pro fell below the ¥6,000 (\~\$880) threshold required for eligibility under China's national subsidy program, allowing buyers to receive an additional ¥500 (\~\$75) trade-in subsidy. During the 2026 618 promotion, however, iPhone 17 Pro received a price reduction of roughly \$145 but remained above the ¥6,000 threshold, making it ineligible for the national subsidy. As a result, Apple entered this year's promotional period with a meaningfully weaker value proposition than a year ago. While the base iPhone 17 received a more modest discount of around \$30, bringing it below the subsidy threshold and into eligibility, the overall promotional support was still significantly less favorable on a YoY basis. Apple still outperformed the broader China smartphone market, expanding its market share by 2% from 16% in April to 18% in May.

iPhone's May ASP slightly declined by $1.2\%$ YoY, after six months of consecutive ASP growth. The strength of the iPhone 17 cycle contributed to ASP strength over the past six months, with the growth trend inflecting up significantly (Exhibit 4). However, May ASP declined modestly, reversing the positive trend. We believe this is likely due to the iPhone 17e and 16e sales. In May 26, the 17e and 16e had a combined unit sale of 1.9mn, higher than the 1.7mn units in May 25. Accordingly, the e series unit mix as $\%$ of total increased from $10\%$ in May 25 to $11\%$ in May 26. Being much lower ASP than the rest of the iPhone family, the e series likely contributed to the modest decline in ASP.

## 2. HOW IS FQ3'26 SHAPING UP - EARLY READ FROM THE FIRST TWO MONTHS

Units and revenue tracking for the first two months of FQ3 are running modestly below expectations. Combined April and May iPhone sell-in volumes totaled 35.9 million units, which is 67% of the consensus FQ3'26 shipment forecast of 53.3 million units and 64% of our 56.1 million unit estimate. This compares with a historical FQ3 average of 72% and average of all quarters of 65%, indicating a slightly slower start to the quarter than in previous FQ3's.

On revenue, April and May iPhone revenue, calculated using sell-through ASPs and sell-in volumes, reached \$31.4 billion. This represents 58% of both the consensus FQ3'26 revenue expectation and our forecast, below the historical FQ3 average of 69%. Overall, both unit shipments and revenue are tracking somewhat behind typical seasonal patterns heading into the final month of the quarter.
