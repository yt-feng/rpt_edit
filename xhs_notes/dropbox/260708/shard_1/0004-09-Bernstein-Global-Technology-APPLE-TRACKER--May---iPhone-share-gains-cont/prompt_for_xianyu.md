Apple's May sell-through units were up 2% YoY and 1% MoM. Nearly all markets delivered positive YoY sell-through revenue growth, with Japan and emerging markets posting the strongest gains. \- US: May iPhone sales up 6.5% YoY, volume up 4.6% YoY, ASP up 1.9% YoY. Apple continued to outperform the broader US smartphone market, expanding its market share by 3% from 50% in April to 53% in May. The YoY growth benefited from easier comps, as consumer demand was pulled forward into April 2025 amid tariff-related concerns, making May 2025 an easy comp.

\- China: May iPhone sales down 15.5% YoY, volume down 19.1% YoY, ASP up 4.4% YoY. This is the first YoY sales decline since the launch of the iPhone 17 series and was largely attributable to the weaker 618 promotions this year. During the 618 campaign in 2025, discounts on the iPhone 16 series ranged from \~\$175 to \$295. The entry-level iPhone 16 Pro fell below the ¥6,000 (\~\$880) threshold required for eligibility under China's national subsidy program, allowing buyers to receive an additional ¥500 (\~\$75) trade-in subsidy. During the 2026 618 promotion, however, iPhone 17 Pro received a price reduction of roughly \$145 but remained above the ¥6,000 threshold, making it ineligible for the national subsidy. As a result, Apple entered this year's promotional period with a meaningfully weaker value proposition than a year ago. While the base iPhone 17 received a more modest discount of around \$30, bringing it below the subsidy threshold and into eligibility, the overall promotional support was still significantly less favorable on a YoY basis. Apple still outperformed the broader China smartphone market, expanding its market share by 2% from 16% in April to 18% in May.
