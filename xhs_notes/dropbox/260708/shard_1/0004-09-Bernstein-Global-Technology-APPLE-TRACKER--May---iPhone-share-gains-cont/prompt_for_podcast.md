Apple's May sell-through units were up 2% YoY and 1% MoM. Nearly all markets delivered positive YoY sell-through revenue growth, with Japan and emerging markets posting the strongest gains. The entry-level iPhone 16 Pro fell below the ¥6,000 (\~\$880) threshold required for eligibility under China's national subsidy program, allowing buyers to receive an additional ¥500 (\~\$75) trade-in subsidy. During the 2026 618 promotion, however, iPhone 17 Pro received a price reduction of roughly \$145 but remained above the ¥6,000 threshold, making it ineligible for the national subsidy. As a result, Apple entered this year's promotional period with a meaningfully weaker value proposition than a year ago. While the base iPhone 17 received a more modest discount of around \$30, bringing it below the subsidy threshold and into eligibility, the overall promotional support was still significantly less favorable on a YoY basis. Apple still outperformed the broader China smartphone market, expanding its market share by 2% from 16% in April to 18% in May.
