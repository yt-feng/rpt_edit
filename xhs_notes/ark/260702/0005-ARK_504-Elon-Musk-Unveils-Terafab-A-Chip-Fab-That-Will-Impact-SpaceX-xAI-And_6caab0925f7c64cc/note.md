# 木头姐ARK：#504: Elon Musk Unveils Terafab, A Chip Fab That Will Impact SpaceX, xAI, And Tesla, & More

Last week, Elon Musk unveiled his plans for a “Terafab”—a facility capable of producing 1 terawatt (TW) of AI chips per year when complete and ~50x larger than current global available new compute in a single site. The facility could scale to ~100M square feet, ~3x the size of New York’s Central Park, and require ~10GW (Gigawatts) of power, equivalent to ~10 large nuclear reactors, at scale.
