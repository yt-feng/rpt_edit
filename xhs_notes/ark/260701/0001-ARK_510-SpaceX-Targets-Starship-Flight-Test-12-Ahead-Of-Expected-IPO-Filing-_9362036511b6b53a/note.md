# 木头姐ARK：#510: SpaceX Targets Starship Flight Test 12 Ahead Of Expected IPO Filing This Week, & More

This week, SpaceX is targeting its twelfth Starship flight test, debuting its next-generation Starship (V3) and Super Heavy booster as well as upgraded Raptor engines and a redesigned Starbase launch pad. The test comes amid reports that SpaceX could file its prospectus as soon as this week ahead of an Initial Public Offering (IPO) in June.
