Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

KC桌面 collects information needed to operate accounts, search and report access, downloads, service support, and first-party traffic measurement.

Information We Collect

Account records may include your username, provided email address, role, account session, report access, and download activity.

First-party analytics may include a random browser visitor ID, a 30-minute activity session ID, landing and page paths, referring host and path (without its query string), UTM campaign fields, on-site search terms, report or Blog identifiers and titles, recommendation clicks, country and Cloudflare data-center code, device category, language, screen size, navigation type, User-Agent, and a one-way shortened IP hash. When a device-scoped service decision is needed, the server derives a pseudonymous identifier by applying an HMAC to the random browser visitor ID. An IP address is used only server-side for a one-way hash and abuse-rate controls and is not retained as a hardware identifier. KC桌面 does not use hardware fingerprinting for this purpose. Signed-in events may be associated with the account used.

When you use the RAG research assistant, we may retain the question, the generated source-grounded response, cited reports and charts, response status and timing, membership tier, quota decision, and an account or pseudonymous device reference. Administrators may select a useful question and its sanitized answer for the public popular-question library; account, device, conversation-history, and internal storage fields are removed from that public copy.

We do not intentionally collect the search query entered on Google, Bing, ChatGPT, or another referring platform, and we do not receive a user's AI prompt. A referring platform may supply its own UTM tag or referring host.

Newsfeed Access and Topics

General visitors and free accounts have read-only access to built-in Newsfeed topics. They cannot create custom topics or subscribe to Newsfeed email delivery. A valid member whose server-verified qualifying membership period is less than two months may keep up to three custom topics; a valid member whose qualifying period is at least two months may keep up to five. A server-verified super account has no custom-topic limit. Built-in topics do not count toward these limits, and browser-supplied role or quota values are not accepted.

Newsfeed email subscription is available only to valid members and is bound to the signed-in account email. When a member has reached the custom-topic limit, the member may explicitly request an additional topic. The request is sent server-side only to the fixed service address info@kcdesk.com; a browser cannot choose or replace the recipient. The requested topic, account identity, membership tier and quota state, language or region preferences, page path, timestamps, and notification status may be retained in a private operational archive that authorized administrators can review. This policy does not imply that a visual administration screen is available.

The raw custom topic and the raw topic in an over-limit request are not written to public analytics. A custom topic may be processed by configured AI and search providers only as needed to create and refresh that topic. First-party analytics may record only bounded interaction metadata such as action, status, membership tier, quota count or limit, and a pseudonymous request reference.

Access Requests

Registration requires an email address. We may use the information submitted with your registration or access request to activate and support your account.

A report request may include the requested report title and identifier, your reply email, account identity when signed in, page path, submission timestamps, and delivery status. We use these fields to notify the service operator, respond within the stated service window, prevent duplicate submissions, and limit automated abuse.

A RAG research request or course-material request may include the submitted question or selected material, your reply email and account identity, membership tier, quota state, page path, timestamps, and notification status. Course decks are not directly unlocked by the site: eligible members can view a cover and submit a separate request for each deck.

Cookies and Local Storage

The site uses cookies and browser storage to remember login sessions, report access state, private tool unlock state, a random visitor ID, first-seen time, and session attribution where applicable.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use infrastructure, AI, search, and email-delivery providers such as Cloudflare, GitHub, Brevo, and, where configured, Supabase to host, secure, process, store, notify, and deliver the service. Access to raw analytics, report-request records, and private Newsfeed topic-request records is restricted to authorized administrators.

Retention and Requests

Built-in Newsfeed topics are product content rather than account data. Custom topics, preferences, and email-subscription settings are retained while needed to provide the account service or until they are removed, replaced, disabled, or deleted with the account. Derived Newsfeed caches may expire and be rebuilt. Private over-limit request records are retained only while needed to process the request, prevent duplicates or abuse, provide support, or meet legal obligations.

Newsfeed retention and deletion are scoped only to Newsfeed records and caches. They do not delete or shorten retention for saved report PDFs, parsed Charts, chart images or indexes, report-research data, or course materials. PDF and Charts cleanup processes likewise do not process Newsfeed topics, settings, or request archives.

Operational and analytics records are retained only while needed for service operation, security, support, measurement, or legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.
