Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

KC桌面 collects information needed to operate accounts, search and report access, downloads, service support, and first-party traffic measurement.

Information We Collect

Account records may include your username, provided email address, role, account session, report access, and download activity.

First-party analytics may include a random browser visitor ID, a 30-minute activity session ID, landing and page paths, referring host and path (without its query string), UTM campaign fields, on-site search terms, report or Blog identifiers and titles, recommendation clicks, country and Cloudflare data-center code, device category, language, screen size, navigation type, User-Agent, and a one-way shortened IP hash. Signed-in events may be associated with the account used.

When you use the RAG research assistant, we may retain the question, the generated source-grounded response, cited reports and charts, response status and timing, membership tier, quota decision, and an account or pseudonymous device reference. Administrators may select a useful question and its sanitized answer for the public popular-question library; account, device, conversation-history, and internal storage fields are removed from that public copy.

We do not intentionally collect the search query entered on Google, Bing, ChatGPT, or another referring platform, and we do not receive a user's AI prompt. A referring platform may supply its own UTM tag or referring host.

Access Requests

Registration requires an email address. We may use the information submitted with your registration or access request to activate and support your account.

A report request may include the requested report title and identifier, your reply email, account identity when signed in, page path, submission timestamps, and delivery status. We use these fields to notify the service operator, respond within the stated service window, prevent duplicate submissions, and limit automated abuse.

A RAG research request or course-material request may include the submitted question or selected material, your reply email and account identity, membership tier, quota state, page path, timestamps, and notification status. Course decks are not directly unlocked by the site: eligible members can view a cover and submit a separate request for each deck.

Cookies and Local Storage

The site uses cookies and browser storage to remember login sessions, report access state, private tool unlock state, a random visitor ID, first-seen time, and session attribution where applicable.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use infrastructure and email-delivery providers such as Cloudflare, GitHub, Brevo, and, where configured, Supabase to host, secure, process, store, notify, and deliver the service. Access to raw analytics and report-request records is restricted to authorized administrators.

Retention and Requests

Operational and analytics records are retained only while needed for service operation, security, support, measurement, or legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.
